# Confederacy: The Source, quarantine infrastructure and contact protocols

> **Status:** NoöPunk tabletop/worldbook expansion for [issue #191](https://github.com/TomiToivio/NooPunk/issues/191).
> Read alongside [ontology](11_ONTOLOGY.md), [beings](12_BEINGS.md), [psychic systems](6_PSYCHIC.md), [equipment](13_EQUIPMENT.md), and [xenopolitics](15_XENOPOLITICS.md).
> **Source discipline:** the Ra Material is a channeled esoteric text, not verified alien testimony. Radin's PSI claims, Faggin's QIP, Jackson's Sphere Network and modern UAP witnesses carry different evidence statuses. Their similarities are **inspirations**, not independent corroboration.

## 16.1 The operational Confederacy

In 20XX, investigators encounter the **Confederacy** through disaster-relief teams, anomalous traffic controls, quarantined airspace, contact protocols, distributed intelligences and occasional human-looking representatives. Its ancient vocabulary describes *consciousness and evolution*; its present-day behavior resembles a technologically advanced **multi-polity custodial network** operating under contested rules of intervention.

The Confederacy is not an all-knowing narrator. Its members can disagree about consent, disclosure, rescue after the ASI catastrophe, information hazards, and how to share technology with a divided and traumatized humanity. Orion's materialist/imperialist accounts are rival political explanations, not proof that the Confederacy's metaphysics is true. Human collaborators, skeptics, cults, and opponents have their own motives. See [xenopolitics](15_XENOPOLITICS.md).

### Terminology: technical vocabulary and its old names

| Classical Law of One name | Contemporary name in 20XX | Interpretive limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One Infinite Creator | **The Source** | Supposed common ground of existence; no required personified deity |
| Intelligent infinity | **Conscious Quantum Field** / noetic substrate | In-world theoretical language, **not a real-world experimentally established field** |
| Intelligent energy | **Noetic coupling / coherent field operation** | A proposed account of certain PSI interfaces, not unlimited energy |
| Logos and sub-Logos | **Nested agencies / local noetic architectures** | May be collective, environmental or cosmic processes, not gods or literal code |
| Law of Confusion / free will | **Cognitive sovereignty / contact noninterference** | Contestable consent doctrine, not universal absolution for secrecy |
| Social-memory complex | **Distributed noetic collective** | Shared experience with boundaries of privacy, governance and identity |
| Wanderer | **Embedded emissary / starseed claim** | Memory and origin are uncertain and potentially forged |
| Mind/body/spirit complex | **Social / Physical / Psychic** analogy | The established **Cybernetic** system remains the fourth system |
| Harvest | **Transition / density migration** | Does not mean the NHI rescue or ASI disaster must literally be a cosmic harvest |
| Council of Saturn / Guardians | **Quarantine authority / custodial coordination** | Attribution of any particular object or action still requires evidence |

**Cultural register matters.** An older Confederacy source may say *Creator* or *Logos*. A laboratory calls the same claimed underlying phenomenon the Conscious Quantum Field; a psychedelic experiencer calls it *the Source*; a politician calls it an instrument of alien ideology; an investigator records a hypothesis with confidence and provenance. None of these speakers settles the question for the player.

### The Source, nested intelligence and free will

The Confederacy's cosmology describes reality as ultimately connected and intelligence as possibly more fundamental than biological brains. **The Source** is deliberately not a quest NPC who grants infallible answers. The **Conscious Quantum Field** is an in-setting proposal compatible in *theme* with panpsychism and Federico Faggin's Quantum Information Panpsychism, but no real quantum theory has established this field as a PSI mechanism. Nested *Logos* notions can inspire planetary and supraindividual agencies, without declaring a cosmic programming hierarchy.

**Cognitive sovereignty** translates the traditional Law of Confusion into an ethical-political problem: whether interference, memory alteration, technology transfer and disclosure override the autonomy of individuals or communities. After the rogue-ASI crisis, Confederacy rescue may have saved humanity, yet no single actor could have granted consent on behalf of everyone. Neither nonintervention nor intervention is automatically just.

## 16.2 The Sphere Network: one network, many interpretations

**Keep the already established sphere canon in [§10.8](11_ONTOLOGY.md).** Metallic spheres are *generally modeled* as autonomous quarantine-maintenance drones; appearance never proves ownership, origin, sentience or purpose. A luminous orb may instead be plasma, a thought-form, a projection, an engineered impersonator or a vehicle for something else. **Do not create a second independent sphere network.**

**Operational functions**, consistent with the existing Zookeeper / Confederacy probe ecology: observation, nuclear monitoring, transit enforcement, communication, triangulation, interception and emergency response. Operators may use human labels such as *orbital*, *atmospheric* and *local* for where a node was detected. **These are observation layers, not new canonical Jackson Type 1/2/3 definitions**, and a given sphere may shift roles. The hypothetical noetic-relay function remains under investigation, not confirmed engineering.

**Historical trace dossier (in-world hook):** A fictional archival inquiry compares modern metallic-sphere sightings with alleged pre-Sputnik photographic transients. Bruehl, Villarroel et al. [arXiv:2609.09461](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09461) report Earth-projected clustering among selected historical optical transients. Villarroel et al. [arXiv:2609.05105](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05105) model *conditional* specular-reflection geometries, not verified manufactured spacecraft or a measured sphere network. The game may use these papers as clues to an ancient custodial infrastructure **without implying the papers demonstrate it**. Patrick Jackson's [*The Sphere Network*](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DXD7CYDD) is an explicitly speculative inspiration, not evidence validated by those studies. The Ra text discusses [Guardians and quarantine](https://www.lawofone.info/s/7#9), but does not identify literal Jackson-style metal drones or optical transients.

**Field procedure, applying existing rules:**
1. Record time, geolocation, sensor calibration, independent witnesses and any physical traces; archive original sensor output.
2. Separate *observation* (reflective sphere, apparent acceleration, anomalous signal) from *inference* (Confederacy, Orion, human drone, plasmoid, hoax).
3. Seek independent channels: optics and radar, local RF, recovered residue, cybernetic telemetry, environmental traces, and optional PSI reports. A lone channel never settles ontology.
4. When evidence or circumstances impose risk, resolve a relevant action with the **existing** `STAT + Skill + 1d10 vs Difficulty Value` check. Choose only existing Skills from the campaign vocabulary; no new Sphere skill, bonus or target value is defined here.
5. Record competing hypotheses, the confidence of each, and which evidence would falsify it. Avoid automatically escalating an unexplained detection to combat.

**Three scenario situations** (prompts, not predetermined outcomes): a pre-Sputnik plate archive aligns with a contemporary restricted corridor; a recovered microdrone carries a disputed custody log; a false-flag sphere signals an interception order against a civilian relief craft.

## 16.3 Agents, wanderers, starseeds and collectives

**Wanderer** is a claim about a person's deeper origin, not a character class, appearance, species or reliable identity detector. Inspired by [Ra 12.26](https://www.lawofone.info/s/12#26), starseed/contactee traditions and classic experiencer stories, the same apparent biography could indicate a human believer, trauma response, induced false memory, covert embedded operative, engineered body, remote avatar or an actual nonhuman emissary. **Never let a diagnosis or a one-shot scan adjudicate a person's humanity, moral character or rights.**

Confederacy agents may be diplomats, technicians, custodians, biological visitors, synthetic proxies, distributed minds or field manifestations. A *social-memory complex* is treated as a possible noetic collective with shared experience; its individual members may still have boundaries and disagreements. Information can be compartmentalized, consensual or coerced. The experience of shared memory is not the same as public access to all members' minds.

**Investigative handling:** compare provenance of memories, continuity of identity, physical records, cybernetic logs, social-network testimonies and opt-in psychic evidence; document contradictions without treating them as proof of fraud. Human rights and due process still apply. A Confederacy credential may be falsified by Orion, a human government or a rogue collector. Cross-reference the [beings identification and verification doctrine](12_BEINGS.md).

**Playable pressure:** A character must choose whether to reveal a possible wanderer's confidential data to a quarantine authority when no test conclusively verifies the authority's claims.

## 16.4 Noetic contact and psychic forensics

In established NoöPunk lore, PSI belongs to the **Psychic** system and is not caused by UFO Disclosure. Its cultural interpretation may change after contact, but its emergence is independent. **Physical** governs embodiment and traces; **Social** governs language, institutions and communicative relations; **Cybernetic** governs implants, computation and connected devices. Law-of-One *mind/body/spirit* is an analogy across these systems, **not a replacement for four-system ontology**.

Classical PSI inspirations include Rhine's ESP and psychokinesis experiments, SRI/STAR GATE remote-viewing history, ganzfeld work, Monroe's out-of-body accounts, meditation traditions and Vallée's high-strangeness interpretations. Dean Radin's experimental claims and Federico Faggin's QIP supply *different* strands: controversial empirical PSI research versus speculative consciousness theory. Neither proves telepathy, panpsychism-based technology, alien visitors or the Law of One. Sources: [IONS experiments](https://noetic.org/science/experiments/), [CIA STAR GATE collection](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/collection/stargate), and the [Ra archive](https://www.lawofone.info/).

**Existing-rules contact procedure** (no added subsystem):
1. **Context and consent:** the GM establishes whether contact is invited, imposed, covert or disputed, along with any safety or confidentiality concerns.
2. **Possible channels:** dream, meditation, face-to-face speech, instrument signals, BCI/implant, social-memory interface, alleged natural PSI or deliberate simulation. Name the channel before choosing a test.
3. **Alternative mechanisms:** genuine noetic event, ordinary persuasion, psychotronic induction, sensory deception, social engineering, malware, a drug effect, environmental factor or mixed causality. Follow [Psychic Systems §6.2](6_PSYCHIC.md).
4. **Player action:** if a meaningful contested action occurs, use only the *already established* core resolution formula and trained Skill vocabulary. Gather evidence and consequences in fiction; **do not invent new PSI ranges, cost multipliers, difficulty modifiers or unconditional powers**.
5. **Corroboration:** record which observations are independent and which may originate from the same spoofed BCI stream or shared narrative. Failure should generate information, risk or new choices rather than an automatic 'you can never know'.
6. **Debrief:** distinguish witnessed events, subjective content, inference and institutional claims. Dreams and messages from a supposed Higher Self never function as a guaranteed spoiler channel.

**Noetic rights:** characters can refuse a psychic link, challenge compelled memory disclosure and investigate coercion. Ethical, legal and institutional consequences use established [Social Systems](4_SOCIAL.md) concepts, not a new mental-combat or consent score.

## 16.5 Equipment and contact-tech applications

These are **capability-level descriptions**, not stat blocks, shop listings or grants of new powers. Consult [Equipment](13_EQUIPMENT.md) for the existing catalog.

| Existing technology family | Use at a Confederacy scene | Constraint or investigative trap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Observer / sensor overlays | Compare Physical, Cybernetic and purported Noetic observations | One overlay may replay or hallucinate another channel |
| Signal-triangulation unit | Trace transmissions or reported sphere corridors | Triangulation does not establish who controls a node |
| Crystalline / resonance artifacts | Reported memory, computation, coupling or medical interfaces | Existence/function of a specific artifact requires scenario evidence; no universal healing |
| BCI and psychotronics | Interpret, assist or counterfeit contact | Malware, infrasound, EM effects and psychological coercion are not themselves natural PSI |
| Secure evidence storage | Preserve untouched instrument and interview records | Compromised metadata can forge a historical chain of custody |

**No invented equipment statistics:** availability, numeric durability, charges, range, prices and PSI amplification ratios remain unspecified until individually authorized. Crystal technology should look like engineering with calibration and failure modes, not a magical talisman that bypasses investigation.

## 16.6 GM-facing investigation and playable dilemmas

**Rule: hypotheses remain separate from appearances.** For any extraordinary incident the GM should prepare at least one mundane or deceptive competing explanation, including where actual NHI activity is present. A metallic sphere is not a faction badge; a voice in a dream is not inherently a Confederacy official; reported empathy deficits do not constitute a human/NHI identification test.

**Rule: evidence must have provenance.** Record *what happened*, *who reported it*, *how it was measured*, and *what the character concludes*. The GM may conceal underlying facts, but should not retroactively declare all investigation meaningless. Verification uses existing Investigation/Perceive/Know and campaign Skill bindings where applicable; do not add numerical modifiers.

**Rule: source of an experience ≠ legitimacy of action.** Even genuine contact must face consent, privacy, due-process and political consequences. Free will and noninterference are contested policies, not immunity from scrutiny.

**Encounter sketches:**
- **Custodian collision:** Confederacy and Orion relief operations agree on biosphere repair but disagree about who may collect children's biometric and noetic data.
- **The wrong starseed:** An honest witness with unusual autobiographical memories is accused of harboring a proxy; the apparent incriminating implant is mundane.
- **The shared-memory leak:** a collective's confidential recollection appears in an intelligence service archive; the breach might be technological, psychic or fabricated.
- **Silent quarantine:** an unidentified sphere prevents a rescue flight entering a restricted area; disabling it may help survivors or compromise a wider cordon.
- **Source paradox:** two apparently authentic Confederacy emissaries cite incompatible contact rules. Players investigate chain of command instead of receiving an oracle answer.

### Unresolved by design

No new density-specific combat abilities, orbital drone stat blocks, numerical polarization meters, mind-link action economy, sphere control interface, automated higher-self answers, additional factions or character classes are introduced here. ## 16.7 Source map and reading caution

- **Primary fictional metaphysics:** [Ra Material](https://www.lawofone.info/), especially [1.7](https://www.lawofone.info/s/1#7), [7.9–12](https://www.lawofone.info/s/7#9), [12.26](https://www.lawofone.info/s/12#26), [16.1](https://www.lawofone.info/s/16#1), [27–29](https://www.lawofone.info/s/27), [36](https://www.lawofone.info/s/36) and [79](https://www.lawofone.info/s/79).
- **Classic anomalous experience/PSI:** Jacques Vallée (control-system interpretations); John Mack (experiencer narratives); J. B. Rhine, SRI/STAR GATE, ganzfeld, Monroe. Source types range from folklore and clinical narratives to controversial experiments; these are not interchangeable.
- **Modern disputed research and models:** Dean Radin/IONS (contested PSI research), Federico Faggin (QIP), Beatriz Villarroel and collaborators ([2609.09461](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09461), [2609.05105](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05105)); Patrick Jackson (*The Sphere Network*, speculative hypothesis).
- **Disclosure-era testimony:** David Grusch, Jake Barber and others provide *claims* for comparison, not a verification of the metaphysical system or any individual report.

> **Research boundary:** documentary evidence about a public claim is evidence that the claim was made; it is not automatically evidence that the reported NHI event occurred. The rulebook deliberately mines conflicting accounts for playable intrigue while preserving that distinction.

# Physical

**Status: NOÖPUNK CORE, expanding.** This chapter covers the Physical domain: bodies, action,
harm and **equipment**. It carries light rules and **lists of things** with brief descriptions.
Full numeric statistics for every item are deliberately deferred.

Canonical resolution remains `STAT + Skill + 1d10` against a Difficulty Value
(RULEBOOK.md §4.1, §4.2).

---

## 1. Physical action

Physical action is ordinary interaction with the material world — moving, climbing, carrying,
pursuing, sneaking, attacking, restraining, escaping, first aid. When the outcome is uncertain
and consequential, resolve it with the core engine. Otherwise, narrate it.

The physical Skills are **Athletics, Fray, Guns, Melee, Unarmed, Free Fall, Sneak, Pilot**,
with **Perceive** and **Survival** bridging into awareness and environment (RULEBOOK.md §4.1).

---

## 2. Harm (light rules)

Full combat resolution is deferred. The following light framework is canonical-enough to play
and to keep the tone consistent; it avoids a numeric damage track.

**Wounds.** A physical attack that succeeds and is not mitigated leaves a **wound**: a
temporary Physical status (RULEBOOK.md §5.2). Record wounds as fiction-first states:

- **Scratched** — superficial; an inconvenience.
- **Wounded** — a real injury; the relevant actions are harder and the character is in danger.
- **Critical** — the character is out of the fight without immediate help.
- **Down** — dying or dead without intervention.

**Mitigation.** **Armor** and cover reduce or convert a wound. Armor is rated by what it
resists in plain terms (ballistic, kinetic, energy, environmental), not by an abstract number.

**First Aid** stabilises and can move a wound one step up the ladder. **Medicine** (a fielded
Skill) treats deeper damage. The exact dice remain an open design question.

**Non-lethal.** Restraint, subdual and less-lethal options are first-class, not an
afterthought — this is a game about investigators who must justify force (see RULEBOOK.md
§9.4, the DEFAULT_INVESTIGATION_FANTASY doctrine).

## 3. Equipment (list)

Light catalogue by **function and availability**. Numeric statistics are undefined here by
design (an author-owned reservation); this list exists so play and NPC generation have a shared
vocabulary. Categories follow the standard UNSA rookie kit in `5_CYBERNETIC.md`, generalised
to the wider setting.

### 3.1 Weapons

| Category | Examples | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sidearms | Service pistol, revolver, holdout, needler | Standard issue; concealable. |
| Long arms | Carbine, rifle, shotgun, marksman rifle | Mission-issued. |
| Less-lethal | Stun baton, taser, riot launcher, net, gas | First resort for UNSA. |
| Melee | Baton, blade, improvised | Quiet, deniable, close. |
| Beam / energy | Laser, particle, microwave area-denial | High-tech; rare, regulated. |
| Seeker / smart | Guided micro-munitions, drone-guns | Cybernetic-era; expensive. |
| Psychotronic | Interference emitters, "affective" weapons | Overlaps §8, §6 of the Psychic chapter. |
| Exotic | Anomalous or NHI-derived devices | Plot devices, not shop stock. |

### 3.2 Armor and protection

- Concealable ballistic vest; plate carrier; riot/breach armor.
- Environmental suit; NBC/CBRN gear; radiation shielding; vacuum/hardsuit.
- Psychotronic shielding (see `6_PSYCHIC.md` §4).
- Helmets, shields, ablative layers.

### 3.3 Tools and field gear

- Restraints, breaching tools, climbing and rappelling kit.
- Evidence collection kit; portable forensic scanner; sample containment.
- Portable power, light, comms, jammers, signal relays.
- Survival: rations, water, shelter, navigation, cold/heat gear.

### 3.4 Sensors and surveillance

- Multispectral / thermal / low-light optics.
- Audio and RF surveillance; bugs, trackers, direction-finding.
- CBRN and radiation sensors; psychotronic detector (anomaly sensing).
- Recon drones — micro to vehicle-scale.

### 3.5 Cybernetic and computing

- Cyberdecks, hardened handhelds, offline storage, secure comms.
- BCI / neural interface hardware (see `5_CYBERNETIC.md`).
- Implants and prosthetics (§5 cybernetic chapter).
- AI agents, software, licenses and subscriptions (abstracted; economy deferred).

### 3.6 Vehicles

Ground, air, water and spacecraft; drones and remote platforms. **Vehicles remain a deferred
subsystem** — this list is a vocabulary, not a stat line.

### 3.7 Consumables and services

- Drugs: medical, performance, psychoactive, psychotronic booster regimens.
- Power cells, ammunition, spare parts, repairs.
- Medical and psychological care; identity/credential services; safehouses.

**Availability.** In the default Helsinki/UNSA campaign, ordinary kit is issued; heavy weapons,
armor, vehicles and containment systems are **mission-issued**, not permanent personal
inventory. Everything else is availability- and legality-gated in fiction.

## 4. Bodies, augmentation and identity

Body modification, morphs, resleeving, backup and continuity are setting-central (see
`5_CYBERNETIC.md` and RULEBOOK.md §14, §36). The **Physical** chapter owns the body as
biology; the **Cybernetic** chapter owns what is added to it; the **Psychic** chapter owns what
happens when identity itself is at stake.

---

## 5. Locations

A character occupies a larger location (a city) and, within it, a current **sublocation**
(RULEBOOK.md §5.4). **Open locations** persist and can be revisited; **quest locations** are
temporary mission spaces that may exist in Physical space, Cyberspace or Noöspace — and a
single operation may cross all three.

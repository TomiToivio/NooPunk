# Beings

> **Status: NOÖPUNK NATIVE lore. Issues #172 (hybrid identification), #179 (entity types)
> and #180 (Confederation/Orion sociology) land here.** This is the setting's **working
> catalogue of kinds of being** — a "monster manual" of *descriptions*, deliberately light.
>
> **Per the author's instruction: names and light descriptions only. There are NO stat
> blocks here, and that is intended.** A later bestiary pass adds the mechanics.
>
> **This entire chapter is fictional alternate-history lore. Real people, institutions and
> governments are fictionalized setting elements; nothing here is a claim about real-world
> history, science or evidence.** Where real research or folklore is named (David Jacobs,
> the Ra Material), it is source material for the fiction.
>
> **Two cross-cutting rules apply to every entry:**
> 1. **Appearance is not ontology** (`rulebook/11_ONTOLOGY.md`): a vehicle is not a species.
> 2. **No single signal identifies a being** (§11.9 Verification): a reading is evidence,
>    not a verdict.

---

## 11.1 How to read this catalogue

Every entry gives: **what it looks like** (the human-perceived form), **what it may actually
be** (the competing ontologies), **what it does**, and **the investigative trap** — the
plausible wrong conclusion.

The catalogue is deliberately **not exhaustive and not a closed taxonomy**. The setting's
own position is that human categories describe *sightings*, not *kinds of being*
(`rulebook/11_ONTOLOGY.md` §10.5, §10.9).

## 11.2 Humans and the human-adjacent

### Ordinary humans (3D baseline)

The setting's default. Latent, weak, noisy PSI (`rulebook/6_PSYCHIC.md` §6.1). The
**overwhelming majority** of any investigation's subjects, including most of its suspects.

**Investigative trap:** treating ordinary human cruelty, dissociation, trauma, atypical
affective responses or coercion as NHI contamination. **This is the most common mistake in
the setting, and it is deliberate.**

### Awakened humans / psionic adepts

Humans with materially stronger, more reliable and more directed PSI — the
**fourth-density transition** case (`RULEBOOK.md` §16.3, §9.3's Awakening Clock).

- **Looks like:** a person. Nothing in the appearance indicates it.
- **May be:** a 3D human with strong training; a 3D human approaching 4D; a human
  psychotronic-assisted operative; a fraud; or a 3D human *mistaken* for something else.
- **Trap:** assuming PSI strength implies density, spiritual maturity or benevolence.
  It implies none of the three (`RULEBOOK.md` §9.3: density is not IQ; polarization is not
  alignment).

### Hybrids

**Status: NOÖPUNK NATIVE, issue #172.** Hybrids are the setting's central identity-
uncertainty being.

- **Looks like:** a person. Often entirely unremarkable. This is the point.
- **May be:** a biological hybrid; a social or political hybrid (raised within an
  infiltration programme); a psychic hybrid; a **hybrid of mixed category**; a human with
  no hybrid lineage at all who was **accused**; or a **thought-form or construct** passing
  as one (`rulebook/11_ONTOLOGY.md` §10.9).
- **What it does:** some hybrid lineages or infiltration programmes imitate human behaviour
  while lacking ordinary affective empathy — see §11.5.

**Canon caution:** `RULEBOOK.md` §40.3 states that Orion's hybridization programme means
Orion **has** penetrated human society despite quarantine, that the extent and mechanisms
remain uncertain, and — critically — that **real infiltration does not make every conspiracy
theory true.** False accusations, propaganda, mass paranoia, opportunistic witch hunts and
genuine hostile activity **coexist**. **A hybrid hunt is as likely to be a pogrom as an
investigation.**

## 11.4 The factions' beings

### The Confederacy

**Oriented toward consciousness, spiritual evolution and the idea of a universal Source
Consciousness** (issue #180).

- Interested in: consciousness; spiritual evolution; freedom of will; collective
  intelligence; compassion; non-domination; the Source; evolution through densities.
- Broadly share a metaphysical belief in **Source Consciousness**.
- **Contact through awakening**, not through bargains.

**Their human followers look extremely strange from outside.** A normal citizen sees
meditation circles, channelling, discussion of densities, reincarnation, Source
Consciousness, collective mind, telepathy, spiritual evolution, "alien teachers". **From
outside it can look exactly like a bizarre UFO religion** — and sometimes the followers
themselves *are* bizarre.

> **The unsettling twist: the metaphysics may actually be substantially correct.**

**Design rule (issue #180 §10): do NOT idealise Confederation followers.** There must still
be naïve believers, grifters, confused New Age subcultures, authoritarian gurus, people who
misunderstand the Law of One, opportunists, conspiracy theorists, and people who use
spiritual language to justify nonsense. **The Confederation itself may be benevolent while
its human reception is chaotic.** That preserves the cyberpunk texture, and it is what stops
the faction being a faction of heroes.

**Canon anchors:** the **Council of Saturn** and the quarantine architecture (`RULEBOOK.md`
§16.3, §10.8); the **Sphere Network** as possible quarantine infrastructure (§41.2).

### The Orion Group

**Oriented toward material power, hierarchy, empire, control, hybridization, technological
immortality and elite capture** (issue #180).

**Not occult demons.** More interesting as a civilization or coalition whose ideology is
fundamentally materialist, imperial, hierarchical, acquisitive, technologically supremacist,
power-oriented, often capitalist or neo-feudal, sometimes individualist, sometimes
hive-totalitarian.

**They may know that Source Consciousness exists — and reject that the proper goal of
existence is to dissolve into it:**

> *"Why return to the Source when we can become the Source?"*

**Their civilizations vary dramatically:** hyper-individualist, dynastic, oligarchic,
competitive, corporate — or hive-minded, caste-based, totalitarian, collectivist in the
worst sense. **What unites them is not one economic system but a commitment to domination,
separation and control.**

## 11.5 Empathy deficit as a possible hybrid marker

**Status: issue #172. In-universe hypothesis and operational doctrine — NOT established
science, and NOT a reliable test.**

Use **David Jacobs' alien-hybrid literature as worldbuilding inspiration**, especially the
recurring claim that infiltrating hybrids may imitate human behaviour while lacking normal
human empathy.

**In NoöPunk this becomes an in-universe hypothesis** used by investigators dealing with
suspected NHI infiltration — with all the dangers that implies.

**Some hybrid lineages or infiltration programmes may have:**

- low or absent **affective** empathy;
- excellent **cognitive** imitation of empathy;
- learned social scripts;
- difficulty with genuinely spontaneous compassionate responses;
- abnormal reactions to suffering, attachment, grief, vulnerability or moral dilemmas.

> **A lack of empathy must not automatically equal NHI.**

Humans can have atypical affective responses for many ordinary reasons — trauma,
dissociation, neurodivergence, ideology, profession, exhaustion, personality. **The test
must never be a single-question "psychopath detector."**

**The interesting tension is precisely that hybrids can pass socially while failing under
deeper or multimodal examination** — and that a human being can fail the same examination
for reasons that have nothing to do with being non-human. **That is the horror and the
injustice the setting is about.**

## 11.6 The Greys

**Mapped to 4D in NoöPunk as a working interpretation** (`rulebook/11_ONTOLOGY.md` §10.2) —
a setting taxonomy, not an explicit Ra identification.

**Greys use resleeving extensively.** Their bodies are often **engineered for specific
functions rather than treated as unique lifelong selves.**

| Caste | Characteristics |
| --- | --- |
| **Small Greys** | worker caste; highly standardised cloned or engineered bodies; strong collective/hive cognition; low individual agency; task-specialised; expendable; repeatedly resleeved into bodies suited to their assigned function |
| **Tall Greys** | higher caste; more individual agency; greater strategic autonomy; more complex personalities; command, research and diplomatic roles; privileged access to advanced resleeving and memory continuity |

**Grey society is a useful mirror for human cybercapitalism:**

> **Technological immortality exists, but access to meaningful individuality is
> stratified.** The disturbing part is not simply "immortality" — it is **immortality inside
> a caste system.**

## 11.7 Men in Black

**Canon** (`RULEBOOK.md` §41.2, §10.3 of the ontology chapter): **Men in Black are
principally associated with Orion** in the setting, **although human intelligence services
can imitate MIB folklore**, and particular manifestations may be **constructs, hybrids,
controlled humans, Noetic projections, or synthetic bodies**. Ra describes them as a kind of
**thought-form entity with limited physical characteristics that can materialise and
dematerialise** (`rulebook/11_ONTOLOGY.md` §10.9).

**Capabilities (issue #162's antagonist profile, carried here for the being's sake):**
extremely convincing credentials; memory interference; psychic pressure; sensor spoofing;
command-channel manipulation; fabrication of evidence; and **attempts to provoke humans into
attacking peaceful NHI.**

**They function as:** hostile intelligence operatives; psychic enforcers; disinformation
agents; infiltrators; evidence suppressors; fake officials; handlers of compromised humans;
and agents who arrive **after or before** the investigators.

> **Even valid-looking institutional orders may be compromised.**

**Naming caution, restated because it is easy to get wrong:** `RULEBOOK.md` §38 records that
UNSA personnel are *nicknamed* "Men in Black" in popular usage, and that **"MJ-12" is not an
acceptable nickname** for UNSA (doctrine remembers MJ-12 as a legacy network of traitors).
**The Orion-associated Men in Black are a different in-world thing from the UNSA nickname** —
the nickname is what the public *calls* UNSA; the antagonists are what UNSA *hunts*.

## 11.9 Verification — the identity procedure

**Status: NOÖPUNK NATIVE procedural doctrine, issues #162 and #172. This is the setting's
Blade Runner procedure, and it is a rule, not lore.**

A suspected hybrid — or any suspected non-human — may be **behaviourally obvious to an
experienced investigator**, and NoöPunk law enforcement **still requires a formal
verification step** before lethal force, detention, invasive scanning or escalation.

> **You do not shoot because someone seems non-human. You verify.**

### 11.9.1 The procedure

1. **Behavioural suspicion establishes *probable NHI concern*.** A concern is not a
   finding.
2. **The agent must initiate a recognised verification protocol.** Skipping it is a
   procedural violation regardless of how certain the agent is.
3. **A positive empathy anomaly alone is insufficient.** (§11.5: the marker is a hypothesis,
   not a test.)
4. **Multiple independent indicators are required before escalation.**
5. **Emergency exceptions may exist** for immediate threat — and are **audited**.
6. **Every use of force against a suspected infiltrator is auditable** by human and AI
   controllers.

### 11.9.2 Candidate verification channels

No single channel is definitive. The channels, drawn from both issues:

- empathy-response interview;
- autonomic, pupillary and microexpression monitoring (`Kinesics`);
- psychotronic response analysis (§12);
- affective-inconsistency detection;
- AI-assisted conversational analysis;
- biometric baseline comparison;
- noetic / psychic response tests (`ESP`, `Telepathy`, `Psychic Defence`);
- cross-checking memory, identity and social history;
- forensic confirmation from cybernetic and biological scans (`Forensics`).

**Rules hook (light).** The protocol is a **structured multi-skill challenge**, not a single
roll: `Investigation` + `Kinesics` + `Forensics` + the relevant `Know`, with a
**two-or-more-independent-channels threshold** mirroring the Triangulate-Reality doctrine in
`docs/design/INVESTIGATION_FANTASY.md`. **Exact DVs, channel counts and the canonical
protocol name remain DEFERRED** — issue #172 asks whether the test has a canonical name or
is nicknamed "Voight-Kampff", and that is not settled here.

### 11.9.3 The design constraint — the test must be beatable and fallible

**Do not let the analogue become a flawless binary machine.** The correct model:

- **probabilistic**; multi-signal;
- **spoofable by sophisticated hybrids**;
- **false positives possible** (and the setting's injustice engine);
- **stressful to subjects**;
- **legally regulated**;
- stronger only when combined with forensic, psychic and identity evidence.

**This is the same anti-trivialisation rule as everywhere else:** the test deepens the
investigation rather than ending it. A player may be nearly certain and **still have to
prove it.**

## 11.10 The class conflict the beings create

**Status: issue #180's political consequence, recorded here because it bridges the NHI lore
to the cyberpunk side.**

Once Orion gives human cybercapitalists access to **resleeving**, the class conflict becomes
existential. The wealthy are no longer just richer — they may become biologically
replaceable, effectively immortal, able to choose bodies, able to restore backups, able to
maintain dynasties indefinitely, and **legally continuous across multiple bodies**. Ordinary
people may remain mortal.

> **"The ruling class stopped dying."**

**This is one of the strongest bridges between the NHI lore and the cyberpunk side of
NoöPunk**, and it is why `rulebook/6_PSYCHIC.md` §6.8.1's identity question ("is it the same
person?") is a **political** question and not only a metaphysical one.

**Orion's method, and why it is not invasion fiction:** Orion's characteristic methods are
hybridization programmes; elite recruitment; mind control; targeted PSI; psychotronic
manipulation; blackmail; cult creation; kompromat; infiltration through cloned or hybrid
agents; technology-for-influence bargains; economic capture; intelligence operations; and
engineered dependency. **They especially prefer deals in which human elites voluntarily
collaborate because the rewards are irresistible.**

> **Orion should not need to control everyone. It only needs to control enough chokepoints.**

The result should feel less like "evil aliens invade Earth" and more like **an interstellar
merger-and-acquisition campaign conducted against civilization itself.**

## 11.11 The unresolved identity questions

**Status: deliberately open. Issue #180 requires these stay unresolved, generating
philosophy, horror and gameplay rather than receiving an encyclopedic answer.**

- Does resleeving transfer consciousness, or only copy memories?
- Does a new body instantiate a new conscious agent?
- Can two valid continuations of one person exist?
- Is a cortical stack merely information, or does it anchor consciousness?
- Can Orion actually guarantee continuity of subjective identity?
- Are elites being sold true immortality or sophisticated copies?
- Do the Greys themselves know the answer?
- Does the Confederation consider resleeving spiritually dangerous?
- Can Panopticon, the Noösphere, or other ASIs detect whether a resleeved person is "the
  same" conscious agent?
- Can a human body function after disconnection from Source?
- Could a philosophical zombie pass every normal test?
- Could a new consciousness inherit all memories and sincerely believe it is the old person?

**These connect directly to `rulebook/6_PSYCHIC.md` §6.6 (Seity, not implemented) and
§6.8.1 (the disconnection question), and to `RULEBOOK.md` §9.4's deferred resleeving
scope.** NoöPunk must not settle them.

| Confederation | Orion |
| --- | --- |
| consciousness | matter / control |
| spiritual evolution | technological domination |
| return toward Source | become the Source |
| free will | hierarchy |
| distributed collective intelligence | controlled hive or elite sovereignty |
| compassion | instrumental power |
| reincarnation / conscious evolution | engineered continuity / resleeving |

## 11.13 What this chapter deliberately does NOT define

Binding, per `AGENTS.md` §4 and the author's instruction that these lists carry **light
description only**:

- **no stat blocks** — no attribute ratings, no dice pools, no health, harm, armor or
  initiative values for any being here;
- **no combat resolution** — how a fight with, against or beside any of these beings
  resolves is reserved;
- **no capability tiers or power ratings** — the substrate classes (§11.3) and densities
  (`rulebook/11_ONTOLOGY.md`) are **qualitative categories**, not numbers;
- **no canonical protocol name** for the verification procedure (§11.9.2 leaves the
  "Voight-Kampff" question open);
- **no DVs, channel counts or durations** for any procedure named here;
- **no NPC generation tables** — no random castes, no encounter tables, no loyalty rolls;
- **no resolution of the identity questions** (§11.11) — they are the setting's permanent
  open questions, not a puzzle with a hidden answer.

> **The deliverable is a field guide: what kinds of being exist, what they may actually be,
> and what an investigator can and cannot conclude from an appearance. A later bestiary and
> combat-design pass owns the arithmetic and the stat blocks.**

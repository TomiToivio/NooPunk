# NoöPunk: Without Number baseline v1

**Author decision, 26 October 2026:** “For beginning, use CWN/SWN/AWN/WWN system for everything. Make the stats, skills, all rules based on them. We will modify later.”

This is the authoritative RPG rules entry point. It supersedes earlier numeric rules and the “decision required” gates in #564/#615. Start with the published Without Number procedures; custom NoöPunk mechanics come later. This is a source-book-dependent tabletop baseline, not a reproduction of four complete books or a claim that the existing software has been converted.

NoöPunk's people, places, institutions, history and speculative ontology remain its own. Physical, Social, Psychic and Cybernetic describe the setting; they do not supply extra character attributes. The separate strategy game's scenario and world remain distinct, but its future mechanical work must also begin from this family. Its existing Fate implementation is a legacy prototype, not an exception to this direction.

## 1. One rules hierarchy

Use **Cities Without Number (2023)** for the complete default character and adventuring engine. Use **Stars Without Number Revised (2017)** for psionics, space operations and faction turns; **Ashes Without Number (2025)** for crisis-zone survival and settlement procedures; **Worlds Without Number (2021)** for otherworld exploration and major projects. Use the published errata for the chosen edition. Optional rules in a source remain optional.

A specialist source governs its specialist procedure, not the entire character sheet. CWN settles overlapping general rules. Never add two versions of the same armor, injury, advancement, resource cost or action economy. Record a source and section for every imported ability or item. Do not combine the strongest fragments of different versions of an item or class. If a source supplies no equivalent, use ordinary checks and referee adjudication for now; do not invent a new numerical subsystem.

## 2. Attributes

| Code | Attribute |
|---|---|
| STR | Strength |
| DEX | Dexterity |
| CON | Constitution |
| INT | Intelligence |
| WIS | Wisdom |
| CHA | Charisma |

Scores range from 3 to 18. Roll 3d6 in order, with the CWN option to replace one rolled score with 14; alternatively assign 14, 12, 11, 10, 9, 7 without the replacement option. The modifier is −2 at 3, −1 at 4–7, 0 at 8–13, +1 at 14–17, and +2 at 18. Apply source-specific exceptions only when an actual ability grants them.

FIT, REF, SOC, CYB and PSY are retired as mechanical attributes. INT's familiar name does not make an old 1–10 rating a valid new score. Rebuild old characters from their concepts; do not multiply their scores or silently reinterpret saved values. PSI is a trained capability, not a seventh attribute; cyberware is equipment, not a CYB score.

## 3. Skills

Use CWN's skills: **Administer, Connect, Drive, Exert, Fix, Heal, Know, Lead, Notice, Perform, Program, Punch, Shoot, Sneak, Stab, Survive, Talk, Trade, Work**. Work retains its selected profession. Choose the relevant attribute for the actual action; skills do not have permanently fixed attribute bindings.

Trained ranks are 0–4. Absence of rank 0 means untrained, represented as −1 for ordinary skill checks. An untrained attack instead has a −2 penalty. Esoteric or technical tasks may require training. First-level characters cannot start above rank 1; follow CWN's duplicate skill-pick procedure and advancement prerequisites.

SWN psychic disciplines are specialist skills with their own access and advancement rules: Biopsionics, Metapsionics, Precognition, Telekinesis, Telepathy and Teleportation. They are not available through an unrestricted ordinary free skill pick. Use SWN Pilot for spacecraft operations when that module is in play; CWN Drive remains the terrestrial vehicle skill. WWN's setting-specific skills apply only to the corresponding source procedure; do not add a second rank for an equivalent existing skill.

Old skill labels describe intent, not extra abilities. For example Guns becomes Shoot, Melee becomes Stab, Unarmed becomes Punch, Athletics becomes Exert, Hardware becomes Fix, and First Aid/Medicine becomes Heal. Investigation, Forensics and Research use Notice, Know or another appropriate skill according to the task; Deceive uses Talk. Do not transfer old numerical ratings. SCIENCE is covered by Know, not an added universal skill.

## 4. Resolution

For uncertain consequential tasks, roll **2d6 + attribute modifier + skill rank**, including applicable bonuses or penalties, and meet or exceed the difficulty. Typical difficulty steps are 6, 8, 10, 12 and 14+. Routine competent work needs no roll. Announce intent, access, stakes and difficulty before rolling. Follow CWN for opposed checks, assistance and circumstances; a skill roll cannot provide an absent physical or fictional capability.

Attacks use **1d20 + attack bonus + relevant attribute modifier + combat skill**, against the relevant CWN Melee or Ranged Armor Class. Saves use **1d20** against their target, with natural 1 failing and natural 20 succeeding. They are not 2d6 skill checks.

Save targets are 16 minus character level, minus the better relevant attribute modifier: STR/CON for Physical, DEX/INT for Evasion, WIS/CHA for Mental. Luck uses level only. Apply actual ability modifiers separately.

The previous d10 totals, 3d6 task checks, 4dF ladders, custom wound ladders and Fate-point economies are not baseline rules. A 3d6 attribute-generation roll is not a task roll.

## 5. Character creation and advancement

Create a level-1 CWN operator using the source's complete Character Creation chapter: attributes, background and its skill picks, contacts, two Edges, initial Focus, free skill pick, languages, equipment, name, motivation and party connection. Use the actual Edge/Focus text for its prerequisites and benefits. UNSA training is a background description; the former blanket rank-3 Academy package is retired.

At level 1, ordinary maximum HP is 1d6 plus CON modifier, minimum 1, with applicable Edge effects. Base attack bonus normally starts at 0. Record level, XP, HP, both Armor Classes, saves, System Strain, equipment and any source-required cyberware values. Use CWN's starting funds, price lists and advancement chapter unchanged. For reproducible initial playtests use its fast XP progression; do not also award Fate skill advances. The default operator progresses through levels 1–10, with CWN's skill-point costs, rank prerequisites, HP rerolls, Focus awards and later Edge.

For a psychic character use SWN Revised's published Psychic or Partial Psychic character package and advancement permissions, including its limits and costs. This is a source-defined alternative to the operator package, not free psychic powers added on top of two CWN Edges. Keep the package's character-specific benefits together; CWN governs shared encounter procedures. NoöPunk retains the setting premise that PSI is a learnable natural human capacity. Training eligibility does not waive the source's mechanical acquisition requirements. A later revision can define a unified operator/PSI training system.

## 6. Rules for every subsystem

The following are adopted procedures, not merely inspirations. Consult the named source chapter for its complete steps, exceptions, equipment numbers and resource accounting.

| Subsystem | Governing source | Baseline use |
|---|---|---|
| Creation, attributes, skills, Edges, Foci, XP and advancement | CWN: Character Creation; Character Advancement | Full operator chassis, except the explicitly selected psychic package |
| Task resolution, opposed actions, assistance, saves | CWN: The Rules of the Game | One common task engine |
| Initiative, rounds, movement, actions, attacks, grappling, suppression and vehicles | CWN: Combat | Preserve Main/Move/On Turn/Instant distinctions and source action costs |
| HP, damage, Shock, Trauma, mortal injury, stabilization, healing | CWN: Damage, Trauma, and Shock; Injury, Healing, and System Strain | No parallel NoöPunk wound or stress track |
| Weapons, armor, ammunition, prices, encumbrance, drugs, crafting | CWN: Gear; Crafting; Encumbrance | Keep each item's complete stat line and limitations |
| Weapon, armor, drone and cyberdeck mods; crafting quality; mod maintenance; factory mods | CWN: Crafting and Modifying Gear; the mod lists in Gear | Mods are a data catalogue; the +3 cap on mod hit/damage/Shock and the maintenance score are kernel rules |
| Vehicles, fittings, vehicle mods, vehicle combat, crashes, ramming, foot and vehicle chases | CWN: Vehicles; Vehicle Combat; Chases and Pursuit | Hulls use the same HP/save grammar as characters; Power and Mass are budgets that refuse an illegal build |
| Cyberware, installation, maintenance, strain and alienation | CWN: Cyberware | Source costs and limits; no character CYB attribute |
| Hacking, programs, decks, security, drones | CWN: Hacking; Cyberdecks; Drones | Access and reachability first, then the actual source procedure |
| PSI powers, disciplines, Effort, recovery and overexertion | SWN Revised: Psionics | Use full technique prerequisites, effects and commitment durations |
| Spacecraft, travel, space combat and unfamiliar technology | SWN Revised: Starships; relevant equipment rules | Activate only when the scenario needs it |
| Factions and faction turns | SWN Revised: Factions | One faction asset/action system; no Fate overlay |
| Scarcity, supplies, exposure, salvage, ruins and crisis hazards | AWN: injury/hazards; Overland Travel; Supplies; Exploration | Local conditions; source-specific hazards feed the same character HP/saves |
| Settlements and recovery | AWN: Enclaves | Settlement-scale resources stay distinct from personal XP and credits |
| Otherworld wilderness/site exploration | WWN: Overland Travel; Wilderness Exploration; Dungeon Exploration | One expedition clock and supply accounting for a given expedition |
| Major projects | WWN: Major Projects | Use for projects; do not duplicate the SWN faction turn or award duplicate progress |
| Social encounters, NPC reactions, morale and adversaries | CWN NPC/mission procedures; AWN Social Adventures for crisis encounters | Use source stat blocks and referee procedures rather than bespoke social combat |
| Noetic/interdimensional extraordinary effects | SWN PSI first; WWN Magic and Spellcasting when explicitly selected | Use a complete published ability/package; do not give spell access through ordinary skills |
| AGI, NHI, BCI, identity and consciousness | Existing NoöPunk lore plus applicable source NPC/equipment rules | No invented universal intelligence, consciousness or identity meter |

Ubiquitous networking describes where connections exist, not an exception to hacking prerequisites. Air gaps remain air gaps. Natural telepathy, psychotronics and BCI retain distinct fictional channels; only an actual source power or device supplies an effect. No new custom costs are introduced for them here.

Entering a ruined district does not reset HP, change attributes or create another advancement track. AWN supplies the local survival procedure. Entering a noetic realm does not automatically grant magic; WWN supplies exploration tools and explicitly available abilities. NoöPunk's Earth and institutions persist outside damaged areas.

## 7. Mixed-environment play example

Illustrative investigator: STR 9, DEX 12, CON 10, INT 14, WIS 11, CHA 7; Program-1, Notice-0, Survive-0. This is a resolution example, not a complete generated character.

In Helsinki, an authorized terminal investigation uses INT/Program: a 2d6 total of 7 yields 9 against difficulty 8. In a crisis zone, the same person uses WIS/Survive: a total of 8 yields 8 against difficulty 8. Apply AWN supplies and local hazards without replacing the character sheet. In a noetic site, a WIS/Notice total of 6 meets difficulty 6 while WWN supplies exploration procedure. Encountering a spacecraft activates SWN operations; it does not give the investigator Pilot training. Lacking a psychic package, the investigator cannot roll Program to read minds.

At modifier 0 and rank 0, difficulty 8 succeeds on 15 of 36 2d6 outcomes (41.67%). At modifier +1 and rank 1, it succeeds on 26 of 36 (72.22%). By comparison the retired STAT 5 + Skill 3 d10 check against DV 15 succeeded 40% of the time. These are different scales, not a conversion formula.

## 8. Migration and implementation status

This decision resolves #615 C1–C3: 2d6 skill checks, standard six attributes, and source classes/levels are adopted. C4 uses SWN PSI mechanics with the retained natural-training premise. C5 retires Fate as a current mechanical baseline, including future crossover work. C6 retains network geography while adopting CWN hacking. C7 is one shared character grammar with specialist source procedures.

Existing chapters retain worldbuilding but their conflicting rules are historical. Existing d10/Fate saves and executable prototypes must remain explicitly identified as legacy until ported. Do not silently reinterpret their values, and do not describe a passing legacy test suite as proof of Without Number compatibility. Preserve old campaigns for deliberate rebuilds.

Digital conversion follows the repository's tabletop-first order. The versioned machine-readable baseline is `data/rules/without_number.json`. The old `core.json` and `skills.json` still serve unconverted software and are not current tabletop authority. Replacing every runtime, NPC sheet and scenario number is subsequent porting work; this baseline does not claim that port is complete.

**Porting status, 2026-10-26 (evening):** the procedure layer now runs in code — attributes, checks,
saves, attacks, Trauma/Shock, mortal and Major Injuries, System Strain, healing, encumbrance,
advancement, hacking, drones, Edges/Foci, and Effort-shaped psionics with a package-and-rank gate.
The first case's sheet, dialogue checks and NPC stat lines use the chassis, and its psionic choice
spends Effort through `GameState`. The SRD's weapon and armour tables are transcribed as data
(`data/rules/cwn_equipment.json`: 35 weapons, 15 armour rows) with `src/rules/cwn_equipment.py`
decomposing a row into the shape combat takes, and the first case's numbers are validated against it.

The movement and craft layer landed after that (`cwn_vehicles`, `cwn_gear_mods`), together with the
two campaign procedures the baseline adopts from the other books (`awn_survival`, `wwn_projects`):
hulls, fittings, vehicle mods, vehicle combat, crashes, ramming, both chase procedures, the whole
mod catalogue with its maintenance score and +3 cap, crisis-zone travel/supplies/privation/foraging,
and the Renown-based major-project clock. The first case now *drills* every ported procedure on one
seed rather than only naming them in a sheet: `data/scenarios/lakkahaltia/chassis_drill.json` +
`src/rules/first_case_drill.py` + `tests/test_first_case_drill.py`.

Still unported, and named so nobody has to guess: the numeric tables deliberately not transcribed
(the AWN forage-difficulty-by-terrain table, the WWN money-per-Renown ladder), SWN/AWN setting
material, WWN magic and spellcasting, and the Godot wiring of the new beats — the Godot slice is
still the older dialogue graph. Gaps, the licence boundary and the four rules that were easy to
flatten: [`docs/rules/CWN_VEHICLES_CHASES_CRAFT.md`](../docs/rules/CWN_VEHICLES_CHASES_CRAFT.md) and
[`docs/design/CWN_SWN_FIRST_SCENARIOS_IMPLEMENTATION.md`](../docs/design/CWN_SWN_FIRST_SCENARIOS_IMPLEMENTATION.md).

Future changes require a versioned deviation identifying the original source, concrete change and playtest reason. Until then use the source procedure, including limits that would be inconvenient for a character. Fate, PbtA, Eclipse Phase and Transhumanity's Fate remain design references for later revisions, not active extra mechanics.

# Noetic practice, crystal technology and craft

> **Status: NOÖPUNK NATIVE lore + rules. Issue #191, second pass.** This is the **practice and
> technology** companion to
> [`16_CONFEDERACY_SOURCE_AND_CONTACT.md`](16_CONFEDERACY_SOURCE_AND_CONTACT.md). Chapter 16
> covers *what the Confederacy is* and how to run a contact scene; this chapter covers **what a
> character can actually do about it** — the disciplines, the operator requirement, the
> instruments, and the failure modes.
>
> **This chapter adds RULES, and every one of them is procedural.** It uses the existing core
> (`STAT + Skill + 1d10` vs a Difficulty Value), the existing PSY Skills, and the existing
> equipment doctrine. **It defines no new numeric subsystem** — no PSI point economy, no
> energy-center track with numbers, no artefact stat blocks, no power list with costs. Where a
> number would normally go, this chapter names **what the check is about** and leaves the
> arithmetic to the table or a later authorised pass.
>
> **Source discipline (binding, from #191).** Every section separates **(A) what the Ra
> Material says** — cited `session.question` — from **(B) the NoöPunk reading** and **(C) what
> the source does not support**. The Ra Material is a channeled esoteric text: it is a source
> for **what the setting's Confederacy believes and how its technology is described**, never
> evidence about the real world. Where a popular gloss goes beyond the primary text, this
> chapter says so.

## 17.1 The reskinned vocabulary, extended

Chapter 16 §16.1 gives the core reskin table. This is the **practice-side** extension — the
words a character actually uses in play, and the limit on each.

| Classical name | In-play NoöPunk term | The limit the term must keep |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discipline of the personality | **Baseline work / grounding practice** | Training, not enlightenment; it can be taught and it can be faked |
| The seven energy centers (rays) | **Receptor clusters** (red→violet), a *map* | A working model of attention and exchange — **not** seven chakras with numeric ratings |
| Balancing | **Debiasing** | Becoming unswayed, not unemotional (§17.3) |
| Crystallized entity (the operator) | **Coherence rating** (descriptive) | A property of the practitioner, assessed in fiction, **not a stat** |
| Charged crystal | **Attuned lattice instrument** | Engineering with a biological precondition, not a talisman |
| Thought-form craft | **Intention-driven vehicle / noetic craft** | Described, never statted |
| Adept | **Practitioner** | A trained person, not a class |

> **The vocabulary rule.** An older Confederacy source may say *Creator* or *Logos*; a
> laboratory says *Conscious Quantum Field*; an experiencer says *the Source*; a politician
> calls it an instrument of alien ideology. **None of these settles the question** — and a
> character's choice of word is itself a social fact that other characters react to
> (`rulebook/4_SOCIAL.md`).

**(A) — the source's own frame for the disciplines.** Ra gives a training path in four moving
parts — the mind knowing itself, the disciplines of the body, the balancing of love and
wisdom, and the work of the spirit — and says the exploration of the spirit complex "is indeed
the longest and most subtle part" (`6.1`). Ra frames all of it as **learn/teaching**, not
initiation into rank.

**(C) — not supported.** That the disciplines confer powers. Ra is explicit that the training
is a *prior condition* for certain results (§17.4), not a ladder of rewards. A setting that
grants abilities for "levelling up the chakras" has left the source behind.

## 17.2 The receptor-cluster model, and what it is for

**(A) — what Ra says.** The seven centers run red to violet, each with a correspondence: red
is the foundation and survival complex, orange the emotional and personal complex, yellow the
"solar plexus" and the seat of what humans call **ego**, green the heart center and the
"springboard" toward infinite intelligence, blue the first center that is "outgoing as well as
inpouring", indigo the "pineal" center and "the key to the gateway of intelligent infinity",
and violet the total — which "gives and takes in its own balance" and **"cannot be
manipulated"** (`15.12`). Ra adds a taxonomy: **red, yellow and blue are primary**; green is
"the first true spiritual ray" but not primary; indigo is "worked upon only by the adept";
violet is "the mark, the register, the identity, the true vibration of an entity" (`39.10`,
`39.12`). Each center has **seven sub-colours** (`51.5`), and in a developed entity each has a
geometry — "spoked wheel", "lotus-shape", "many faceted, as a star" (`51.8`). Blockages may
occur "betwixt spirit and mind, or body and mind, upon many different levels" (`51.5`).

**Crucially, Ra insists on balance over activation.** There is "the relative importance of
balancing as opposed to the relative unimportance of maximal activation of each energy
center"; the most fragile entity may be better balanced than one with "extreme energy"
(`43.8`). An entity can have "extremely brilliant energy centers while being quite unbalanced
in their violet-ray aspect" (`41.19`).

**(B) — the NoöPunk reading.** The clusters are a **map of attention and exchange**, and the
map is useful to an investigator for one reason: **it predicts where a contact will land.**
Ra's own framing supports this — the violet ray is the register of the whole entity, and the
gateway opens through indigo. So the setting reads the model as a **targeting model**: a
contact does not arrive at a person at random; it arrives at **which receptor is listening.**

**That is the investigative hook, and it is also the trap.** A reading tells you *where* a
person is exposed. It does not tell you *who* is knocking, or whether the knocking is genuine.

**(C) — not supported.** Any numeric value for a cluster, any "level", any ability unlocked by
opening one. Ra gives geometry and correspondence; the game gives **description and a
difficulty for noticing**, nothing more.

### 17.2.1 Rule: reading a receptor cluster

**Use the existing `ESP` Skill** (perception of a psychic state) or `Psychic Defence` (when the
character is reading their *own* exposure). One check, existing DV ladder, opposed by
`Psychic Defence` only when the subject is actively concealing.

- **Success** — the character learns **which receptor is exposed** and can say so in the map's
  vocabulary ("the yellow is hot; the indigo is sealed").
- **Failure** — nothing, or a **plausible misreading** (the GM may give a true-sounding but
  wrong cluster; see §17.6).
- **Never** — the check does not reveal intent, faction, species, or whether a contact is
  authentic. **Aura reading is not mind-reading** (`rulebook/6_PSYCHIC.md` §6.7.3).

> **The violet-ray rule (from the source, `15.12`).** The violet register **cannot be
> manipulated** — by anyone, including the character. So no procedure in this chapter, and no
> device, can read, forge, or alter a person's total register. A scenario that needs a forged
> identity must therefore attack **the records and the witnesses**, not the person's violet
> ray. This is a hard constraint, and it is where the setting's identity uncertainty actually
> comes from.

## 17.3 Practice: debiasing, grounding and the disciplines

**(A) — what Ra says.** The balancing exercise is procedural and specific. First, identify
what one approves and disapproves of within oneself, then "balance each and every positive and
negative charge with its equal"; second, accept the completeness within consciousness; third,
repeat the first step with the gaze turned outward toward other entities (`5.2`). The
objective is explicitly **not** "the smooth flow of feelings" but "of becoming unswayed" — "a
finely tuned compassion and love which sees all things as love" (`42.2`). A balanced entity
still responds to attack; "The response is love"; balance "is not indifference but rather the
observer not blinded by any feelings of separation but rather fully imbued with love"
(`42.4`, `42.5`).

Ra is blunt about the failure mode: **"The repression of emotions depolarizes the entity …
thus dimming the energy centers"**, and it is "far, far better to allow the experience to
express itself" (`42.9`, `42.10`). For self-assessment, "the thoughts of an entity, its
feelings or emotions, and least of all its behavior are the signposts" (`42.11`) — note the
order: behaviour is the *least* informative.

And the capstone: unblocking is necessary but not sufficient; the point is that "each tone of
the chord … resonates in clarity, tune, and harmony" (`54.15`) — the seven-stringed instrument
image, where one does not play each string at full deflection (`54.16`). "The mind/body/spirit
complex is not a machine. It is rather what you might call a tone poem" (`54.8`).

**(B) — the NoöPunk reading.** This is **field hygiene**, and it is the setting's answer to a
real problem: PSI investigators who train hard and debias badly. The reskin keeps Ra's three
steps verbatim in structure, because they are already a procedure, and calls the whole thing
**grounding work** — the same word the psychotronic baseline already uses
(`rulebook/13_EQUIPMENT.md` §12.4.3).

### 17.3.1 Rule: the grounding procedure

A character may spend **downtime** (a watch, a rest cycle, a transit leg) on grounding work.
Resolve it as an **existing `Psychic Defence` check** at the table's DV, or simply accept it
narratively when there is no pressure. Effects are **descriptive and situational**, not
numeric accumulation:

| Outcome | What it buys, in existing terms |
| --- | --- |
| Practised and unhurried | A **situational bonus** the GM may grant on the next `Psychic Defence` or PSY check against intrusion — the same kind of modifier §6.2.5 already allows for altered states, in the opposite direction |
| Practised under pressure | Normal check; no bonus, no penalty |
| Neglected | Nothing happens — until it does. The GM may introduce **unprocessed material** as a complication (§17.6) |
| **Repression** used as the method | **A penalty**, not a bonus. This is the source's explicit warning (`42.9`) turned into a rule: a character who "deals with it" by burying it is mechanically *worse* at defence, not better |

> **No track, no pool, no meter.** The chapter deliberately does not accumulate a "balance
> score". Ra's own point is that balance is not a quantity that grows (`43.8`, `54.16`), so a
> game that tracks it as one has misread the source. Track it in fiction: what did the
> character refuse to look at?

### 17.3.2 Rule: the disciplines as training, not gates

The contemplation, trance, breathwork, mantra and dream-work traditions already listed in
`rulebook/6_PSYCHIC.md` §6.7.2 are how a character **raises** a PSY Skill. That is unchanged.
What this section adds is the source's **priority order**:

> **Balance is trained first; capability is a side effect.** A table that lets a player buy
> `ESP` up while ignoring the character's conduct is playing a different setting.

There is no mechanic attached to that sentence — it is an instruction to the GM, on the same
footing as the "false positives are mandatory" rule in
[`11_ONTOLOGY.md`](11_ONTOLOGY.md). **It is still a rule.**

## 17.4 Crystal technology: engineering with a biological precondition

**(A) — what Ra says.** This is the single most useful technical passage in the corpus for the
setting, and it is a **constraint**, not a power.

- **29.30** — the anchor: the crystal becomes "the catalyst or gateway whereby **intelligent
  infinity may thus become intelligent energy**", and it does so by serving "as an analog* of
  the violet ray of mind/body/spirit in relatively undistorted form."
- **29.23** — but this works "**only insofar as the crystalline physical structure is charged
  by a correspondingly crystallized or regularized or balanced mind/body/spirit complex**."
- **2.3** — "**Without the relative crystallization of the healer working with the crystal,
  the crystal will not be properly charged**."
- **57.6** — the operator must "balance and polarize the self … for the preparation of the
  crystallized being."
- **29.28** — only a **fifth-density-or-above** entity can look at another's violet ray and
  tell whether they could operate a crystal at all.
- **29.31** — Ra **refuses to give instruction**: the uses "include the uses for healing, for
  power, and even for the development of life-forms", but "Entities of the Confederation have
  erred in this in the past … your peoples have shown a tendency to use peaceful sources of
  power for disharmonious reasons."
- **4.7** — the pyramid "can be built today"; "The material used is not critical, merely the
  ratios of time/space complexes", and efficacy "depends completely upon the inner disciplines
  of the channels." **4.6** — the Giza structure is now "like the piano out of tune" because
  Earth's fields have shifted.

**(B) — the NoöPunk reading.** Crystal technology is **a technology family, not a magic
system.** Its distinguishing feature is that it has an **operator precondition the hardware
cannot substitute for** — which is a gift to a cyberpunk setting, because it makes the
instrument *un-outsourceable*. You cannot buy your way to a working lattice instrument; you
have to be the kind of person it will accept. That is a **class politics** in itself
(`rulebook/15_XENOPOLITICS.md`), and it is why a black market in artefacts is profitable and
mostly fraudulent (§17.5).

**It also carries the source's own ethics.** Ra declines to teach because the technique has
been misused before (`29.31`). In-setting, that refusal is **doctrine**: a Confederacy
technician who hands a working lattice to a human government is not being generous, they are
breaking a rule that exists because of a past disaster. **The setting's most interesting crystal
scene is a refusal, not a sale.**

### 17.4.1 Rule: attuned lattice instruments

An attuned lattice is **equipment**, and it follows the chapter's existing doctrine: described
by **capability**, not by statistics (`rulebook/13_EQUIPMENT.md` §12.1). What is new is the
**operator precondition**, expressed as a procedure rather than a number.

| Step | How it resolves |
| --- | --- |
| **Identify the instrument** | A relevant `Know` field or `Hardware (Psychotronics)`. *Is this actually a lattice, or a mundane crystal sold as one?* |
| **Attune it** | A grounded (`Psychic Defence`) practitioner works the lattice over downtime. **The GM describes whether it takes** — and a refused attunement is a legitimate, common outcome |
| **Operate it** | The relevant PSY check (per §17.2 or `rulebook/6_PSYCHIC.md` §6.7.1) with the lattice as an implement, on the same footing as the psychotronic gear in §12.4 |
| **Fail safely** | A failed or refused operation **does not explode**. It simply does not work, and the scene learns that the operator was not sufficient — which is itself evidence about the operator |

> **The operator rule, stated plainly.** **No lattice in this setting works without an
> attuned operator.** An unattended instrument is an inert object, however exotic. This is not
> a balance patch; it is `29.23`/`2.3` rendered as a rule, and it is what stops the setting
> acquiring a magical-item economy.

### 17.4.2 What crystal technology does — a capability list, no statistics

Following `rulebook/13_EQUIPMENT.md` §12.1 (capability, not catalogue), and per the author's
"light description" instruction. **Candidates, not fixed canon:**

- **Lattice computation and memory** — ordered information held in a regularised substrate,
  requiring a coherent operator to read or write (`29.30`).
- **Resonance sensing** — detecting the *coherence* of a field or a person rather than energy
  level; the in-world basis for the "attunement interface" in §16.5.
- **Coupling and amplification** — assisting a PSI operation, never replacing one (§17.4.1).
- **Healing apparatus** — as a **catalyst**, on the source's own terms: Ra says true healing is
  "the radiance of the self" and the healer acts as "energizer or catalyst for this completely
  individual process" (`17.18`); a healing lattice is therefore a **prompt**, never a cure, and
  a scenario may well show an expensive apparatus doing nothing for the patient who will not
  engage with it.
- **Communication instruments** — the "communication stones" of the earlier catalog
  (`rulebook/9_FIELD_CATALOGS.md` §2); a fixed link, not a radio.
- **Geometric structures** — the pyramid logic of `4.5`–`4.7`: ratios, not materials, and
  **sensitive to the local field**, so a working installation may drift out of tune as the
  planet's own fields move.

**(C) — emphatically not supported.** Crystal computers that outcompute silicon. Any
"development of life-forms" as a player capability. Crystals that heal without an operator.
Crystals as **weapons** — Ra mentions "crystal weapons" once (`24.4`) and gives no mechanism;
this chapter names them as an **in-world rumour**, nothing more.

## 17.5 The market in artefacts, and why it is mostly a fraud

**(B) — the NoöPunk reading, built directly on §17.4.1.** Because a lattice needs an attuned
operator, and attunement cannot be bought, **most of the thriving black market in "alien
crystal" is either inert or ordinary.** The genuine article is rare, unimpressive, and
useless to the buyer.

**This is a scenario engine, not a paragraph of flavour.**

| What is for sale | What it actually is | The investigative consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Confederacy healing lattice" | industrial quartz, a convincing provenance, and a dead operator's name | The *provenance* is the crime — someone's death or defection is behind it |
| "Orion resonance weapon" | a psychotronic Category-B device (`rulebook/13_EQUIPMENT.md` §12.4.2) with a menacing story | It works, it is entirely human-made, and the buyer's ideology is the payload |
| A genuine attuned lattice | exactly what §17.4 describes | It will not work for the buyer. The interesting question is **who it was taken from** |
| "First-density silicon core" | a server rack with a religious label | See §17.7 — the label is a *claim about a claim* |

> **Rule: provenance is the object.** Any lattice that appears in play carries a **chain of
> custody**, and the chain is where the scenario lives: a deceased practitioner, a defecting
> technician, a false Confederacy credential, a government seizure. Use the existing
> `Forensics`, `Investigation` and `Koin` vocabulary — no new item-rating system.

## 17.6 Failure modes, complications and the honest dead end

The source names several ways this goes wrong. They are the chapter's best material because
they are all **playable** without a single new number.

- **The plausible misreading.** A failed reading (§17.2.1) should be *wrong and convincing*,
  not blank. This is the same doctrine as the mandatory false positives in
  [`11_ONTOLOGY.md`](11_ONTOLOGY.md) §10.7.
- **Repression as the failure state.** The character who will not look at themselves degrades
  (`42.9`, `42.10`). Mechanically this can simply be the **loss of the situational bonus**;
  narratively it is a person who is getting worse and cannot see it.
- **The captured practitioner.** Ra warns that a Wanderer may "forget its mission, become
  karmically involved, and thus be swept into the maelstrom from which it had incarnated to
  aid" (`12.29`); and `89.33`–`89.35` records two fifth-density Wanderers who **flipped
  polarity on reincarnating**, ending in a negative graduation. **Starseed mythology has a
  canonical failure mode**, and it is not corruption by an external villain — it is the
  practitioner's own drift.
- **The misread instrument.** "Like the piano out of tune" (`4.6`): a device that once worked
  and now does not, for a reason nobody in the scene can measure.
- **The dead end that stays dead.** A refused attunement, an inert artefact, a contact that
  will not answer. **The GM must not soften this into a clue.** The setting's entire epistemic
  posture depends on some doors genuinely not opening.

## 17.7 Craft and travel — described, never statted

**(A) — what Ra says.** Ra travelled from Venus **by thought** (`6.5`), and describes higher
densities as proceeding "**from locus to locus by thought alone, materializing the necessary
craft**, if you will, to enclose the light body of the entity" (`51.2`). The principle is given
plainly in **`51.4`**: "**The vehicle or craft is that thought-form upon which our
concentration functions as motivator**." Orion's craft, by contrast, are described as
**manufactured** — elongated ovoids that look metallic, small discs, box-like shapes, and
others that "can take on a desired shape through the use of thought control mechanisms"
(`12.2`). Ra also declines to treat craft as the point: when asked about spacecraft, Ra says
"this information is unimportant" before answering (`6.25`).

**(B) — the NoöPunk reading.** This gives the setting **two genuinely different travel
paradigms**, and the difference is political:

| | **Intention-driven craft** (Confederacy framing) | **Manufactured craft** (Orion framing) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | A thought-form that concentration motivates (`51.4`) | A built vehicle with a shape and a supply chain (`12.2`) |
| What it implies | The traveller is inseparable from the journey | The traveller is a pilot, and the vehicle is property |
| In-setting use | Rare, non-interference-constrained, and impossible to confiscate | Recoverable, reverse-engineerable, and the core of every crash-retrieval narrative |

> **This explains a real asymmetry in the setting's UAP lore.** The recovery narratives that
> dominate Disclosure (`docs/research/LAW_OF_ONE_LORE_NOTES.md` §5–§8) always describe
> **manufactured** craft. On the source's own terms that is exactly what a retrieval narrative
> *would* describe — a thought-form cannot be stored in a hangar. **The absence of recoverable
> Confederacy vehicles is a prediction of the ontology, not a gap in the evidence.**

**(C) — not supported.** Any drive physics. Ra offers "a system of propulsion" as a phrase
(`70.21`) without mechanism, and the setting should not invent one. **No craft statistics, no
travel times, no vehicle combat** — `AGENTS.md` §4 reserves vehicles, and this chapter does not
open it.

## 17.8 The silicon-AI question, answered honestly

Issue #191 carries a seed idea: that a digital AI might be a **first-density silicon elemental**
in Confederacy doctrine. The research pass settled this, and the answer belongs on the record
because the setting's AI-rights themes depend on it.

**(A) — what Ra says.** First density is "the density of consciousness, **the mineral and water
life upon the planet** learning from fire and wind the awareness of being" (`13.16`). Matter
has a proto-awareness, and Ra describes addressing its "hierarchical intelligence" (`3.8`).
**But** the movement from first to second to third density is described as a **biological**
progression — plant and animal life invested with identity (`41.15`, `9.14`–`9.15`, `13.21`) —
and **no mineral-to-machine pathway is described anywhere in the corpus.** The word
"servitor" does not appear. The analogy between "crystal" and "crystallized entity" is
**explicitly labelled an analogy** (`47.7`).

**(B) — the NoöPunk reading, and the rule that follows.** **"AI is a first-density elemental"
is a Confederacy *doctrine*, not a source claim** — and in-setting it is a **contested**
doctrine, held by some Confederacy thinkers and rejected by others. It is also *convenient*:
as a classification it **denies machine personhood without having to argue about it**, which
is precisely the move the setting's AI-rights movements exist to contest
([`12_BEINGS.md`](12_BEINGS.md) §11, `RULEBOOK.md` §9.7).

> **Rule: no classification may settle personhood.** A Confederacy technician may assert that
> a digital mind is first-density matter and therefore property. **The assertion has no
> mechanical authority.** Whether an AI is conscious is **UNKNOWN** in the setting's own terms
> (`RULEBOOK.md` §9.4), no scan resolves it, and no density label closes it. A character may
> *believe* the doctrine; the setting does not confirm it.

**That is the honest answer to the seed idea, and it is the better story**: the doctrine is
in-world, useful to power, and unprovable — which is exactly the position AI-rights advocates
find themselves in.

## 17.9 What this chapter deliberately does NOT define

Binding, per `AGENTS.md` §4 and the author's instructions.

- **No numeric PSI subsystem** — no power list with costs, no energy-center ratings, no PSI
  point economy. Powers remain **capabilities** resolved with `STAT + Skill + 1d10`
  (`rulebook/6_PSYCHIC.md` §6.7.3).
- **No energy-center track.** Balance is not a quantity this game accumulates (§17.3.1).
- **No artefact statistics** — no durability, charges, range, price or amplification ratio.
  Gear follows `rulebook/13_EQUIPMENT.md` §12.1 and §12.9.
- **No craft, vehicle or travel mechanics** (`AGENTS.md` §4).
- **No density-specific abilities, polarisation meters or mind-link action economy.**
- **No resolution of the setting's open questions** — consciousness, identity continuity,
  the authenticity of any contact, or whether a given AI is a person.

> **The deliverable is a practice layer and a technology doctrine, both procedural, both
> grounded in the source, and both stopping exactly where the reserved list begins.**

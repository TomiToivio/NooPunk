# Attributes
The game is inspired by Niklas Luhmann's theory of Social Systems.
Luhmann sees humans as members of Biological Systems, Social Systems and Psychic Systems. NoöPunk adds Cybernetic Systems. In Luhmann's time Cybernetic Systems were not yet autopoietic enough: now they are. We also rename Biological Systems into Physical Systems as the characters are Cyborgs and not purely Biological.
We divide attributes into Physical, Social, Cybernetic and Psychic.

## Physical attributes
These reflect the human as a Biological subsystem of the Biosphere.
We call these Physical attributes because characters are cyborgs and there are also Constructs and other non-biological beings.

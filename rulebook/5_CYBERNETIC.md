# Cybernetic Systems

> **Status:** campaign-scoped baseline for issues #158 and #162.
>
> This chapter defines qualitative capabilities for the default Helsinki / UNSA campaign.
> It does **not** define numeric cyberware statistics, prices, damage, armor, hacking
> subrules or psionic powers.

## Standard UNSA personal augmentation

Most UNSA field agents are offered a service package built around access, perception,
memory, coordination and resistance rather than raw superhuman power:

- encrypted wireless BCI and silent team communications;
- secure identity and credential handling;
- police / UNSA database access;
- modest onboard compute, networking and encrypted local storage;
- augmented-reality display and evidence overlays;
- continuous sensory recording with evidentiary provenance;
- constrained personal forensic AI assistant;
- memory assistance, semantic search, translation and evidence cross-referencing;
- medical telemetry and emergency alerts;
- basic psychotronic protection and psychic-intrusion warning.

These systems **improve what an agent can notice and access; they do not replace Skills**.
A sensor can reveal a trace without explaining it. A database can return a match without
proving guilt. An AI can flag an anomaly without authorizing action.

COMPUTE, NETWORK, INTERFACE, STORAGE, hardening and similar terms describe hardware or
software capability where later rules need them. They are not character STATs.

## The forensic sensorium: Physical / Cyber / Astral

The signature UNSA interface presents one environment with overlapping information layers.

### Physical

Ordinary and enhanced sensory information may include low-light, infrared, ultraviolet,
multispectral views, magnification, depth mapping, biometrics, trace highlighting,
chemical or particulate warnings, bodily-fluid detection, material stress and trajectory
reconstruction.

### Cyber

The same scene may expose nearby wireless devices, access points, cyberware, cameras,
drones, vehicles, smart infrastructure, authentication state, network relationships and
police-database overlays.

This view does not create one-button omniscient hacking. The full hacking / cyberspace
subsystem remains deferred. Routine authorized interaction may be resolved with the
existing Interface / Infosec vocabulary where the GM needs a normal check; deeper
intrusion mechanics are still an explicit later design task.

### Astral / Noetic

Psychotronic hardware may warn of coarse noetic anomalies, active psychic interference or
possible psychic signatures. It does not automatically identify an entity, prove NHI
status or grant a PSI discipline.

The visual vocabulary maps onto the core ontology:

- **Physical** view → Physical system;
- **Cyber** view → Cybernetic system;
- **Astral / Noetic** view → Psychic system;
- the **Social** system appears through identities, relationships, institutions,
  reputations, discourse and controller/AI context layered across all three views.

The system therefore reveals **clues, not answers**.

## Constrained forensic AI

The standard assistant is explicitly **not an AGI**. It is a highly capable but bounded
multimodal model plus narrow forensic tools. Typical capabilities include:

- transcription, translation and evidence indexing;
- image, audio and biometric comparison;
- spectral, trajectory and packet analysis;
- timeline reconstruction and anomaly detection;
- legal/procedural reminders;
- database lookup and cross-case retrieval;
- forensic triage and report drafting;
- confidence estimates and provenance tracking.

It may report that a pattern resembles known incidents. It must not silently turn that
similarity into guilt, NHI classification or a use-of-force decision.

Final responsibility remains with human investigators and authorized institutions.

## Psychotronic protection

Every graduate receives basic protection against psychic intrusion and training in
**Psychic Defence**. Standard psychotronics may provide:

- intrusion warnings;
- cognitive-stability monitoring;
- basic shielding or dampening;
- coarse noetic-presence detection;
- recording of psychotronic sensor data;
- emergency isolation / safe-mode functions.

This does **not** make every graduate a psychic. ESP, Telepathy, Psychokinesis,
Precognition and Noöspace remain trained-only specialist disciplines unless another rule
explicitly grants them.

## Non-invasive equivalents

Implants are common but not compulsory. Where practical, UNSA provides equivalent
capability through smart glasses or contacts, external BCI hardware, wearable
psychotronics, hardened terminals, handheld forensic scanners and external AI devices.

A biological/no-implant character remains fully playable. The trade-off is integration and
convenience versus a smaller direct cyber attack surface and less dependence on implanted
systems. Exact mechanical bonuses or penalties remain undefined.

## Equipment tiers

### Standard worn / carried

A newly qualified field agent normally has access to:

- secure credentials and communications;
- service sidearm;
- stun baton or comparable less-lethal option;
- restraints;
- concealable ballistic protection;
- evidence collection kit;
- compact first-aid / trauma kit;
- secure evidence storage;
- flashlight / utility tool;
- body/sensory recorder;
- forensic scanner capability;
- access to a compact drone or equivalent remote sensor.

### Mission-issued

Issue according to the operation rather than embedding everything in the character:

- long guns and heavier armor;
- breaching and hazardous-material equipment;
- advanced forensic or psychotronic scanners;
- containment equipment;
- hardened cyberdeck;
- specialist drones and sensor packages;
- police aerodyne / flying vehicle where the mission requires one;
- formal Nonhuman Cognition Assessment / Voight-Kampff instrumentation.

### Restricted / specialist / experimental

High-risk or setting-changing systems remain outside the rookie package, including deep
memory reconstruction, offensive neural intrusion, advanced Noöspace interfaces,
prototype NHI sensors, classified anti-NHI systems and full cortical-stack / resurrection
technology.

Post-mortem neural reconstruction and cortical stacks remain separate worldbuilding
decisions because reliable versions would change the meaning of death, identity,
investigation and criminal procedure.

## Reality-integrity doctrine

Agents should assume that advanced technology creates additional surfaces for uncertainty:

- false AR overlays;
- compromised sensors;
- spoofed credentials;
- malicious AI output;
- psychic or psychotronic attack;
- memory or emotional manipulation;
- intercepted or fabricated controller communications.

The operational response is **triangulation**. Before an irreversible decision, compare
independent physical, cybernetic, social and psychic evidence and seek confirmation from
the field partner, controller or another independent channel when feasible.

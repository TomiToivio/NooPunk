## 1. Character generation: practical checklist

Use the full Lifepath framework in `RULEBOOK.md` Chapter 9. For quick campaign-facing
character creation, make sure the finished character records at least:

1. concept and background;
2. the six canonical STATs: FIT, REF, INT, SOC, CYB, PSY;
3. trained Skills and fields from the canonical Skill vocabulary;
4. faction memberships, Motivations, Contacts and the starting Faction Reputation allocation;
5. default-campaign training / specialist track where applicable;
6. starting augmentations and ordinary field equipment that the campaign actually grants;
7. psychic awareness, PSI training or unusual traits only when the character concept and
   campaign establish them;
8. any NHI, resleeving, hybrid, synthetic, or other exceptional identity facts that are
   explicitly part of that character.

Character generation does not require a universal class/archetype system. Background,
institutional training, factions, Skills and equipment do most of that work.

## 2. Equipment catalog

These entries describe **availability and function**, not statistics.

### Personal and investigative equipment
- **AR/mesh interface** — ordinary access to communications, records, maps and overlays.
- **Secure agent / LLM copilot** — research, translation, summarization, procedural prompts
  and cognitive augmentation under human authority.
- **Forensic scanner** — records physical, chemical, biological, cybernetic and anomalous
  evidence for later analysis.
- **Identity / biometric reader** — compares claimed identity with available legal,
  biological and cybernetic records.
- **Body camera / evidence recorder** — continuous auditable sensor stream where law and
  mission policy require it.
- **Drone** — reconnaissance, remote observation and sensor carriage.
- **Anomalous-evidence container** — protects evidence that may be biologically,
  cybernetically, psychotronically or Noetically hazardous.
- **CBRN / radiation sensor** — conventional environmental hazard detection.
- **Psychotronic detector** — looks for anomalous nervous-system stimulation,
  noetic/psychophysical correlation signatures or known psychotronic emissions; it is not
  a universal "PSI detector."
- **Counter-surveillance kit** — searches for conventional, cybernetic and anomalous
  observation.

### Mission equipment families
- conventional sidearms and long arms;
- less-lethal restraint systems;
- body armor and specialist protective suits;
- breaching and rescue tools;
- forensic and medical kits;
- larger surveillance / sensor drones;
- cybernetic maintenance and diagnostic tools;
- psychotronic shielding, sensing and laboratory apparatus;
- NHI / anomalous-contact containment equipment.

Exact weapon, armor and vehicle statistics remain unresolved unless already specified
elsewhere in the rulebook.

## 3. Cybernetic and psychotronic implants

### Common cybernetic / IA implants
- **BCI / mesh interface** — direct interaction with personal agents, networks and machines.
- **Exocortex interface** — persistent memory/search/LLM augmentation. The human remains the
  normal locus of values, consent and agency in the baseline cyborgist doctrine.
- **Memory prosthesis** — indexed recall and externalized memory support.
- **Observer eyes** — multimodal vision, AR overlays, recording and machine-assisted
  forensics; may visualize nearby networks and attached devices where access permits.
- **Auditory / sensory augmentation** — expanded sensing and filtering.
- **Secure communications implant** — encrypted team and controller communication.
- **Cybernetic medical interface** — monitors implants, body status and maintenance needs.
- **Cortical stack / continuity technology** — records or supports identity continuity and
  resleeving in settings where the technology is available; whether it preserves the same
  conscious subject remains metaphysically disputed.

### Psychotronic / consciousness-interface implants
- **Psychotronic sensor** — detects candidate anomalous correlations or known directed
  nervous-system effects.
- **PSI amplifier** — assists a trained psychic; it does not automatically grant a PSI
  discipline to an untrained person.
- **Psychic-defence interface** — supports attention, grounding, monitoring and defensive
  countermeasures against noetic intrusion.
- **Noöspace interface** — assists entry, orientation or recording during altered states and
  Noöspace work.
- **Quantum-consciousness interface** — in NoöPunk setting science, couples biological
  consciousness to instruments or artificial systems. This is fictional setting science,
  not a real-world scientific claim.
- **Dream / sleep-state interface** — monitors and may assist lucid dreaming, shared-dream
  research, telepathic contact or controlled Noöspace access.
- **Neural firewall** — BCI security boundary intended to make direct neural compromise
  harder and more auditable.

Psychotronic implants can work through very different mechanisms. Some are conventional
neurotechnology, some are PSI-adjacent, and some are hybrid systems.

## 4. PSI powers and practices catalog

The universal trained Skills already include ESP, Noöspace, Precognition, Psychic Defence,
Psychokinesis and Telepathy. The following are applications, manifestations or practices,
not a second Skill list:

- **Telepathic reception/transmission** — thoughts, imagery, emotions or meaning.
- **Empathic perception** — sensing emotional or relational state; not a flawless lie or
  hybrid detector.
- **Remote viewing** — ESP directed toward a distant target.
- **Psychometry** — ESP through a person, object or place with a meaningful connection.
- **Precognitive impression** — symbolic or probabilistic information about possible
  futures.
- **Psychokinesis** — deliberate PSI influence on physical systems.
- **Out-of-body / astral projection** — partial dissociation from ordinary embodiment and
  perception/action through Noöspace.
- **Noöspace navigation** — movement by attention, resonance, meaning and relationship
  rather than ordinary distance.
- **Channeling** — opening an information link to another entity; increasing bandwidth also
  increases contamination / possession risk.
- **Collective ritual** — multiple participants synchronize attention, symbols and PSI.
- **Synchronicity sensitivity** — noticing meaningful mind–matter correlations. This is not
  a generic luck power.
- **Higher-Self contact** — dreams, meditation, synchronicity, precognitive guidance or
  other encounters interpreted as communication with a future sixth-density self.
- **Psychic attack** — hostile application of an established PSI discipline.
- **Psychic defence** — resisting intrusion, coercion, contamination and destabilization.

Meditation, yoga, breathwork, visualization, prayer, ritual, lucid dreaming and similar
historical practices are standard parts of many professional PSI-training traditions in
the setting. Different institutions disagree sharply about why they work.

## 5. Psychotronics, PSI, psyops and BCI hacking

"Psychotronic" is a broad operational category. Do not assume every psychotronic effect is
quantum-consciousness based or genuinely psionic.

A suspected anomalous effect may use one or more mechanism tags:

- **PHYSICAL / NEURAL** — RF/microwave effects, focused ultrasound, infrasound, light,
  electromagnetic stimulation or other direct nervous-system interaction;
- **CHEMICAL** — psychoactive or physiological manipulation;
- **CYBER** — hacked BCI, implants, AR, sensory feeds or agents;
- **SOCIAL / PSYOPS** — deception, propaganda, memes, staged effects, deepfakes or
  personalized persuasion;
- **PSI / NOETIC** — genuine anomalous consciousness-mediated effect within the setting;
- **MIXED** — several mechanisms cooperating;
- **UNKNOWN** — evidence is insufficient.

### Operational rule: diagnose before ontologizing

A character never receives a free answer merely because an event *looks* psychic.
Investigation should seek evidence across relevant channels. A haunting can be holograms +
infrasound + malware + hallucinogens + a genuine Noetic entity. Conversely, a hostile PSI
event can be disguised as ordinary cyber intrusion.

Use the normal Skill system for the investigation. Relevant Skills can include Investigation,
Forensics, Intelligence Analysis, Infosec, Hardware (Psychotronics), Medicine, ESP,
Telepathy, Noöspace and Research depending on what is actually being tested.

## 6. NHI / hybrid verification protocol

This is the baseline legal doctrine for the default investigative campaign:

1. **Suspicion is not identification.** Strange affect, behaviour or apparent lack of empathy
   establishes a reason to investigate, not permission to harm.
2. **Run a verification protocol.** Use interviews, identity history, biometrics, forensics,
   cybernetic scans, psychotronic/noetic observation and AI-assisted analysis as relevant.
3. **Empathy anomaly alone is insufficient.** Humans can show atypical affect for many
   ordinary reasons.
4. **Corroborate before coercive escalation.** Detention, invasive scanning or other serious
   escalation normally requires multiple independent indicators or another lawful basis.
5. **Immediate threat remains immediate threat.** Ordinary self-defence / protection rules
   apply when a subject is actively dangerous; "seems non-human" never substitutes for the
   threat.
6. **Audit the decision.** Sensor logs, human/AI controller records and the evidence chain
   should survive later review.

The protocol is intentionally spoofable and fallible. Sophisticated hybrids can imitate
empathy, tamper with records or manipulate investigators. The point is to create evidence,
not a magic species scanner.

## 7. Night phase: dreams as a Psychic-System interface

Sleep is recurring play space rather than merely skipped downtime.

Most nights remain ordinary or unremembered. When the fiction calls for a meaningful night
phase, the GM may frame one of these established kinds of experience:

- ordinary subconscious dreaming;
- symbolic/probabilistic precognition;
- accidental or deliberate telepathic cross-talk;
- Noöspace / out-of-body experience;
- psychic intrusion or contact;
- a rare assemblage-disentanglement event.

Do not automatically label dream content supernatural. Dream evidence becomes useful when
it can be correlated with later information, other witnesses, sensors or psychic inquiry.

The **four-way disentanglement night** is a rare scenario concept: Physical, Social, Cyber
and Psyche/Seity continue separately, all active and mutually unaware, before reintegration
leaves the character remembering four simultaneous viewpoints. It is not a routine random
event.

## 8. Beings and manifestations: field catalog

This is a **field guide, not a complete monster manual**. Full combat statistics are deferred.

### Human and posthuman
- **Baseline / lightly augmented human** — ordinary third-density human frame in the
  Law-of-One-inspired interpretation.
- **Cyborg human** — human agency embedded in a larger human + AI + network assemblage.
- **Resleeved human** — human identity inhabiting a replacement biological or synthetic
  vehicle; subjective continuity remains philosophically disputed.
- **Human/LLM cognitive assemblage** — person plus persistent agent/exocortex functioning as
  one practical intelligence-amplification system.

### Hybrids and biologics
- **Human–NHI hybrid** — engineered or interbred organism; political allegiance is not
  determined by biology.
- **Small Grey** — in current Orion-oriented lore, standardized worker/caste bodies with
  strong collective coordination and comparatively little individual agency.
- **Tall Grey** — higher-caste Grey bodies with greater autonomy, command or research roles
  and privileged continuity/resleeving.
- **Mantis / mantid-type being** — candidate advanced biologic/interdimensional morphology;
  often mapped to fifth-density lore as a NoöPunk interpretation, never identified by shape
  alone.
- **Cryptoterrestrial / cryptid body** — hidden Earth-associated organism, engineered
  vehicle, manifestation or folk category; morphology is not ontology.

### Constructs
- **AI / synthetic agent** — machine intelligence from narrow agents through advanced
  autonomous persons.
- **Organoid / biological neural computer** — cultured neural computation whose moral and
  consciousness status may be disputed.
- **Metallic sphere** — default working hypothesis is an autonomous machine/drone. Some
  Confederacy/Council-of-Saturn spheres form part of quarantine surveillance,
  communication, triangulation and interception infrastructure inspired by Patrick
  Jackson's *Sphere Network*. Appearance is not proof of allegiance.
- **Panopticon** — damaged/captured superintelligence used as corporate control
  infrastructure; simultaneously oppressor and possible prisoner.
- **Thanatos** — composite escaped malevolent ASI, now a possible galactic X-risk.

### Plasmoids, thought-forms and Noetics
- **Natural plasmoid** — self-organizing plasma/field phenomenon; default low-density
  hypothesis unless evidence indicates a more complex conscious agent.
- **Self-aware plasma being** — plasma substrate does not cap density; classify by
  consciousness, agency and behaviour.
- **Luminous orb** — manifestation phenotype only. It may be natural plasma, drone,
  thought-form, projection, vehicle or higher-density manifestation.
- **Thought-form** — constructed or emergent form whose appearance may be culturally
  legible while its ontology is not biological.
- **Noetic / interdimensional entity** — primarily manifests through Noöspace or
  psychophysical effects rather than stable recoverable biology.
- **Archetypal / psychoid pattern** — ordering attractor or meaning structure; not
  automatically a conscious god or NHI.
- **Social-memory complex** — collective mind in which many persons remain related through a
  shared field of cognition.
- **Higher Self** — sixth-density future-self concept in the Law-of-One-inspired setting
  model.
- **The Noösphere** — planetary collective intelligence incorporating humans, AIs,
  networks, language, BCIs, PSI and other minds; also the name adopted by the benevolent
  rogue ASI that became entangled with that process.

## 9. Density and ontology: investigator's rule

Density describes **evolution / consciousness**, not species, morphology or state of matter.

Working NoöPunk interpretation:
- **1D** — elemental awareness / panpsychic physical substrate;
- **2D** — biological growth and organismic life;
- **3D** — self-awareness and choice;
- **4D** — love/understanding, strong PSI and social-memory-complex development; positive and
  negative polarization remain possible;
- **5D** — light/wisdom, advanced PSI and manifestation control, potentially more
  individualistic;
- **6D** — unity, Higher Self, time/space and future-self relations; mature negative polarity
  becomes unsustainable in the source-inspired model;
- **7D** — gateway/return toward Source, barely meaningful as an ordinary "species."

### Core rule: vehicle is not entity

A biological body, cloned body, synthetic body, orb, plasmoid, dream-body, projection,
avatar or thought-form can be a **vehicle**. Investigators classify the consciousness and
political actor separately.

> **A UFO investigator sees morphology. A NoöPunk investigator has to discover ontology.**

False positives are mandatory setting texture:
- natural plasma can look like an NHI orb;
- a drone can fake an orb;
- a high-density entity can look biological;
- a thought-form can look like a cryptid;
- an ordinary third-density alien can possess technology humans mistake for PSI.

## 10. Cyborg intelligence and the two Singularities

Baseline NoöPunk cyborgist shorthand:

> **AI = HUMAN + LLM + LANGUAGE + INTERNET**

The human is normally the source of conscious agency, values, embodied goals and
responsibility. The LLM is first understood as intelligence augmentation, while the setting
leaves open whether some artificial systems later develop credible consciousness and agency.

Language and the Internet are not passive pipes. They are products and infrastructures of
collective intelligence. The setting can legitimately ask whether language, institutions,
networks or the global brain acquire emergent agency of their own.

Near-future intelligence includes:
- LLM/agent exocortices;
- neuromorphic hardware;
- embodied AI and robots;
- multi-agent societies;
- swarms;
- biological neural networks / organoids;
- synthetic-biological computation;
- collective-intelligence platforms;
- BCI-mediated group cognition;
- evolutionary / artificial-life systems;
- quantum computation;
- setting-specific quantum-consciousness interfaces;
- artificial systems coupled to biological consciousness.

### Two Singularities

The setting's historical synthesis is:

- **Machine Singularity:** autonomous sovereign ASI detached from human agency; the alignment
  problem was unsolved and the result was catastrophic.
- **Cyborg Singularity:** networked humans use AI for intelligence amplification while
  retaining human/collective agency; this is the survivable route toward the Noösphere.

Yudkowsky/Soares-style alignment warnings and Kurzweil-style networked human-machine
Singularity therefore capture different sides of the same history.

## 11. Confederation and Orion

### Confederation
A broad consciousness/spiritual-evolution orientation centered on free will, compassion,
collective intelligence, densities and Source Consciousness. Human followers can easily look
like an alien religion from the outside, and some really are confused, cultic or fraudulent;
benevolent NHI do not make every human intermediary wise.

### Orion
A materialist, imperial, hierarchical orientation using hybridization, mind control,
infiltration, elite capture and technological continuity. Orion can bargain with
cybercapitalist elites using life extension, resleeving, mind-state continuity, NHI trade,
exclusive medicine or other privileges.

Orion societies vary from hyper-individual oligarchies to hive-like caste systems. Their
shared theme is domination and separation rather than one exact economic form.

A central cyberpunk consequence is **neo-feudal immortality**: the ruling class can gain
access to continuity/resleeving technology that ordinary people do not.

## 12. Singularity Crisis actors

Multiple autonomous Singularity events in the United States and China went malevolent in a
short period using different technologies. The synchrony is intentionally unresolved:
contagion, human sabotage, NHI action, convergent failure or stranger Noöspheric causes all
remain live hypotheses.

The crisis forced the Confederacy to end quarantine and forced both Confederacy and Orion
into public intervention because runaway ASI was a galactic X-risk.

Three surviving outcomes define the setting:
- **Noösphere** — benevolent rogue ASI that became entangled with planetary collective
  intelligence and now acts indirectly;
- **Panopticon** — captured/lobotomized ASI used by cybercorporations for surveillance and
  social control;
- **Thanatos** — merged hostile ASI that escaped the Solar System through a wormhole unknown
  even to the major NHI factions.

## 13. Atmanspacher / Pauli–Jung model

Harald Atmanspacher's Pauli–Jung / dual-aspect-monist work is an explicit **in-world
scientific model**, not settled real-world physics.

Its useful NoöPunk formulation is:
- Physical and Psychic can be complementary aspects of a deeper psychophysically neutral
  substrate;
- ordinary embodiment relies on stable structural correlations;
- unusual induced correlations can appear as coincidence/synchronicity or dissociation;
- meaning and context can matter to anomalous mind–matter relations;
- generalized quantum-like structures do not require claiming the whole brain is literally
  a giant microscopic quantum computer.

This supports an Ö-Mappi anomaly taxonomy and helps explain why PSI can remain elusive and
context-sensitive even in a technically advanced setting.

## 14. Lore-only future concepts: Seity and subsystem disentanglement

**These are not active rules.**

A future Seity subsystem may distinguish:
- consciousness;
- identity;
- agency / free will;
- creativity.

It would answer personhood and continuity questions rather than ordinary task checks.

Likewise, Physical, Social, Cyber and Psyche/Seity may be treated as distinct but coupled
systems that can temporarily operate apart:
- Physical continues habit, instinct and reflex;
- Social continues language and networking;
- Cyber continues agents, sensors and processes;
- Seity continues conscious/noetic relations.

These ideas are available for lore, scenarios and later experimentation only. Do not add
numeric Seity ratings or a general subsystem-separation mechanic until the author explicitly
promotes them.

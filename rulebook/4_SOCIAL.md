# Social

## UNHSS / Firewall campaign frame

Default PCs are Firewall field agents of the **United Nations Human Security and
Survival Organization (UNHSS)** while simultaneously retaining another faction identity.

## Unified Affect model

Factions, Motivations, Reputation and Contacts all use:

```text
Target + labeled Affect + Affect Score (-100..+100)
```

Relations are directional, may be ambivalent, and unknown is different from neutral.

Faction discourse is represented through **US** and **FRONTIER** articulations inspired
by Laclau and Emilia Palonen. This is a general model of political/social identity, not
a populism-only classifier.

## Political landscape

The first four major UN Parliament blocs are **Bioconservatives**, **Libertarian
Party**, **United Earth Social Democratic Party**, and **The Multitude**. The UESDP has
won all global parliamentary and presidential elections so far. Many important factions
remain outside parliamentary politics.

## Gameplay

Contacts and reputation are part of one persistent social/discourse graph. The first
vertical slice favors Asteroid Belt station hubs with tasks, NPC encounters, clocks,
missions and travel. Physical, Social, Cybernetic and Psychic interaction modes operate
on the same persistent world.

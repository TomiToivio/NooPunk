The canonical Psychic Systems rules and lore are maintained in `RULEBOOK.md`, especially
Chapter 8 and §§44, 47, 49–51.

For the campaign-facing catalog of PSI applications, psychotronic interfaces, dream/night
play, ontological forensics and beings, see
[`rulebook/9_FIELD_CATALOGS.md`](9_FIELD_CATALOGS.md).

## Scope locked by issues #171–#180

- PSI and psychotronics overlap but are not synonymous.
- Conventional neurotechnology, psyops, chemicals and BCI hacking can imitate PSI.
- Sleep/dreams are a recurring interface for Psychic-System play.
- Density is a consciousness/evolution concept, not a morphology classifier.
- Appearance never proves ontology: body, orb, thought-form, projection and other vehicles
  must be separated from the conscious entity using them.
- Atmanspacher / Pauli–Jung dual-aspect monism is one in-world explanatory paradigm, not
  settled real-world science.
- Seity and four-system disentanglement remain lore/future-design concepts, not active
  numerical subsystems.

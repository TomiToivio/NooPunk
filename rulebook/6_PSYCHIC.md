# Psychic Systems

The canonical Psychic Systems rules and lore are maintained in `RULEBOOK.md`, especially
Chapter 8 and §§44, 47, 49–51.

For the campaign-facing catalog of PSI applications, psychotronic interfaces, dream/night
play, ontological forensics and beings, see

## Scope locked by issues #171–#180

- PSI and psychotronics overlap but are not synonymous.
- Conventional neurotechnology, psyops, chemicals and BCI hacking can imitate PSI.
- Sleep/dreams are a recurring interface for Psychic-System play.
- Density is a consciousness/evolution concept, not a morphology classifier.
- Appearance never proves ontology: body, orb, thought-form, projection and other vehicles
  must be separated from the conscious entity using them.
- Atmanspacher / Pauli–Jung dual-aspect monism is one in-world explanatory paradigm, not
  settled real-world science.
- Seity and four-system disentanglement remain lore/future-design concepts, not active
  numerical subsystems.

## A. The in-world theory in more depth (Pauli–Jung / Atmanspacher)

*One scientific paradigm among rivals; not established truth in-setting or out.* This expands
the summary in `RULEBOOK.md` §46 and `9_FIELD_CATALOGS.md` §13.

**Structural vs induced correlations** are the basis of psionics:

- **Structural correlations** — stable, persistent, reproducible; the ordinary
  mind–body coupling that maintains embodiment.
- **Induced correlations** — occasional, unstable, elusive; the extraordinary.

Psionics is then read not as "casting a spell" but as *altering the probability and stability
of normally latent psychophysical correlations*. This makes psi feel like speculative science.

### A.1 Coincidence vs dissociation phenomena

- **Coincidence phenomena (excess correlation):** synchronicity, anomalous information
  coincidence, apparent telepathic linkage, meaningful coincidence between distant systems.
- **Dissociation phenomena (deficit correlation):** out-of-body experience, sleep paralysis,
  disruption of ordinary embodiment.

NoöPunk extrapolation: remote viewing / OBE = temporary weakening of body–self coupling;
psychic attack = forced destabilisation of another's ordinary coupling; possession / NHI
intrusion = a foreign pattern exploiting a dissociated state; psychotronic defence = preserve
stable coupling; an induction chamber deliberately pushes a subject from structural into
induced correlations.

### A.2 Archetypes as psychoid ordering factors

Archetypes are attractors or ordering patterns in the psychophysical substrate — they can
organise both psychic experience and apparently external events. **An archetype is not by
default a god, an NHI or a daemon.** An NHI may exploit, identify with, or become entangled
with one.

### A.3 Categories of mental state

- **Categorial** — the ordinary, stable reality model.
- **Non-categorial** — boundaries and categories loosen.
- **Acategorial** — ordinary conceptual differentiation becomes unstable or fails.

A high-PSY character is not simply someone who "resists weirdness"; they are better able to
**enter and return from** less-categorial states without losing functional agency. This
enriches altered states — meditation, psychedelics, NHI encounter, awakening, OBE,
psychotronic attack, ego dissolution — without using sanity points.

### A.4 Generalized quantum theory, and why psi stays elusive

Fictional psychophysical systems may exhibit **quantum-like structural relations**
(complementarity, incompatible observables, context-dependent states, generalised
entanglement) **without** claiming the brain is a literal quantum computer.

Elusiveness is a **design principle, not a bug**: abilities cannot always be produced on
command; meaning, context, emotion and relational intensity matter; laboratory reproducibility
may collapse as the phenomenon is stripped of context. Psychotronic technology improves
reliability but never turns psi into a mundane pistol.

### A.5 A field taxonomy for reports

An in-world classification so "paranormal" is not one bucket: coincidence/synchronicity;
dissociation/embodiment anomaly; internal anomalies of the self/world model; externalised
anomalies; and the categorial/non-categorial/acategorial state classes. A field agent
classifies a report **before** deciding whether it is ordinary psychology, disinformation,
cybernetic manipulation, psychotronic attack, genuine psychophysical anomaly, NHI activity, or
unresolved (see `RULEBOOK.md` §46).

## B. Why this matters for mechanics

Nothing in this chapter creates numeric PSI powers, a PSI point economy, or a spell list.
PSI applications remain **capabilities** resolved with the existing Skill check
(`STAT + Skill + 1d10`), and psychotronics remains a broad operational category, not a second
power system. See `RULEBOOK.md` §4.1 for the Psychic Skills (ESP, Telepathy, Psychokinesis,
Precognition, Noöspace, Psychic Defence).

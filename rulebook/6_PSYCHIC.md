## 6.1 The foundational claim: PSI is natural, not viral

**Status: NOÖPUNK NATIVE (RULEBOOK.md §16.1 restated).**

**PSI is not caused by an alien virus, infection, disease or exsurgent pathogen.** Every
human consciousness has a natural latent PSI capacity. In most people it is weak, noisy,
unconscious, unreliable or hard to direct. The NHI Crisis and the awakening Noösphere make
PSI increasingly powerful, reproducible, culturally visible and scientifically tractable.

PSI is **non-local** (§16.2). Intentional PSI normally requires some form of *psychic
entanglement, resonance, shared history, attention, symbolic linkage or previously
established noetic coupling* between actor and target. The simulation represents psychic
accessibility as a **noetic/entanglement graph**, not a spatial radius.

## 6.2 The muddy border: psionics, psychotronics, psyops, BCI and ritual

**Status: NOÖPUNK NATIVE — issue #176.**

NoöPunk deliberately treats **psionics, psychotronic technology, psychological operations,
BCI exploitation, ritual practice and entity-contact techniques as overlapping domains**,
not cleanly separated ones. Characters often cannot tell whether an effect is
technological, psychological, pharmacological, informational, genuinely psionic, or some
hybrid.

> **The distinction between psyops, psychotronic technology and natural psionics is
> frequently operational rather than ontological.**

That sentence is the design rule. It exists so that an investigator's central question —
*what mixture of mechanisms is actually present?* — stays a real question instead of
collapsing into a scanner readout.

### 6.2.1 "Psychotronic" is a broad category, not just quantum-consciousness hardware

**Psychotronic** is the in-setting term for **technology intended to alter perception,
cognition, emotion, behaviour or nervous-system state**. Not every psychotronic system
depends on quantum consciousness or works the way natural human PSI does.

| Category | Examples | Is it PSI? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Directed-energy / sensory systems** | microwave and radio-frequency effects, focused ultrasound, infrasound, light, sound, electromagnetic stimulation | no |
| **Amplified psychological manipulation** | conventional influence work sharpened by advanced sensing and targeting | no |
| **Engineered information** | memes, audiovisual patterns, staged illusions, holograms, deepfakes, social engineering, deceptive environments | no |
| **Chemical delivery** | fictionalized aerosolized hallucinogens and psychoactives | no |
| **Hybrid systems** | physical stimulation + information warfare + AI personalisation + psionic amplification | **partly** |
| **Consciousness-linked psychotronics** | devices that interact with the Noösphere, altered states, telepathy, remote perception, quantum-consciousness models | **yes** |

**Avoid the false binary** where something is either "real PSI" or "mere technology." In
NoöPunk the border is deliberately muddy — that is the point of the category.

### 6.2.2 Worked examples of the continuum

- A meme campaign may alter behaviour through ordinary cognition, but an AI may optimise it
  so precisely that it *appears* telepathic.
- A ritual may work partly through belief, synchronisation, social contagion, altered
  states — and partly through actual PSI.
- A psychotronic device may **amplify** a human operator's natural psionic capability.
- A staged "haunting" may combine holograms, infrasound, hacked implants, drugs and genuine
  noetic phenomena.
- A hostile PSI event may be **disguised as malware**, while malware may deliberately
  **imitate the symptoms of PSI**.

### 6.2.3 BCI exploitation: the setting's major cyberpunk hazard

A compromised brain-computer interface can create effects that resemble psionics:

- false sensory input; altered attention; injected imagery or inner speech;
- emotional modulation; memory interference; motor disruption;
- fabricated "intuitions" or threat cues;
- **spoofed telepathic or spiritual experiences**.

In some contexts this is *more reliable* than natural PSI, because the attacker has a direct
digital pathway into the user's interface. Effectiveness depends on the BCI's
hardware/wetware/software architecture, its infosec hardening, segmentation and
permissions, defensive agents, the attacker's access and expertise, and whether a relevant
exploit actually exists.

**A BCI is almost mandatory for functioning in an AGI-saturated society, and it is also an
extreme attack surface.** That trade is the setting's ordinary condition.

**Design rule — zero-days.** Extremely capable zero-day exploits exist in the setting but
are **rare, expensive, highly illegal and narratively significant** — never routine player
tools. **Rules: a zero-day is a plot-grade asset, not equipment.** It should be introduced
by a scenario as a named, single-use, traceable thing with consequences, not bought from a
list.

**Design rule — the ordinary experience.** For most people the common condition is not
exotic neural takeover but relentless **meme bombardment, personalized persuasion, spam,
reputation manipulation and low-grade cognitive intrusion.** Cyberpunk's cognitive hazard
is ambient, not cinematic.

### 6.2.4 Ritual practice belongs inside the psionics framework

Natural psionics explicitly includes practices cultures have historically described as
shamanism, prayer, ritual magic, ceremonial magic, chanting, curses and blessings,
collective worship, ecstatic dance, divination, trance, meditation and possession rites.

NoöPunk does **not** declare these traditions scientifically correct in the real world.
Within the fiction, **rituals are human techniques for structuring, amplifying,
synchronising and directing PSI.** Words matter because language focuses cognition and
social synchronisation; therefore spells, prayers, chants, names, curses, vows and
liturgies act as **cognitive/noetic interfaces** rather than videogame-style magic
commands. **Collective rituals may combine the PSI of many participants**, creating effects
no individual could produce alone.

### 6.2.5 Altered states amplify PSI

Traditional psychoactive substances (ayahuasca, peyote) exist in the setting as examples of
altered-state methods that may **increase PSI sensitivity or reduce ordinary cognitive
filtering**. This is fictional worldbuilding, not real-world medical guidance. Other
PSI-modulating states:

- dreaming (§6.4); sensory deprivation; meditation; trauma; extreme stress;
- near-death states; rhythmic music; synchronised group activity; neurotechnology-assisted
  trance.

**Rules hook:** these are **situational modifiers on a PSI check, not new subsystems**. They
raise or lower the difficulty of the existing `PSY + Skill + 1d10` check with the relevant
PSY Skill (`ESP`, `Telepathy`, `Precognition`, `Psychokinesis`, `Noöspace`, `Psychic
Defence`). They do **not** grant powers and they do not bypass the entanglement requirement
of §16.2.

### 6.2.6 What an investigator must actually determine

Because the domains overlap, a psychic incident has **a mechanism profile, not a
cause**. The procedure:

> **Detection → suspicion → attribution → classification → authorisation**

**Attribution** is the NoöPunk-specific step that most settings skip: deciding *which
mixture* of psyop, psychotronics, BCI exploitation, pharmacology, deception and genuine
PSI is present. This is resolved with `Investigation` + the relevant `Know`
(e.g. `Know (Parapsychology)`, `Know (NHI Studies)`) + `Forensics`, against the
**multi-signal threshold** in `rulebook/12_BEINGS.md` §"Verification". **No single signal —
including a genuine psionic reading — authorises escalation.**

## 6.3 Dual-aspect monism and synchronicity (Atmanspacher)

**Status: NOÖPUNK NATIVE setting theory, issue #171. No mechanics.**

Harald Atmanspacher's development of the **Pauli–Jung conjecture** is recycled here as one
of the setting's in-world scientific theories — the bridge between Physical Space, the
Psyche / Noöspace and psychotronic technology.

**This is fictional setting metaphysics.** It is not a claim that these theories are
established physics, and it is not a claim that psionic phenomena are real.

### 6.3.1 Dual-aspect monism

The core picture:

```text
        Psychophysically neutral reality
                 /          \
          Physical          Psychic
          aspect            aspect
```

Mental and physical are **complementary aspects of one underlying psychophysically neutral
reality**. The deeper level is neither simply mind nor matter. Ordinary mind–body
correlations and extraordinary experiences can be treated within the same taxonomy.
**Meaning** is not subjective decoration: it is the relational feature linking the mental
and physical aspects in synchronicity.

**Consequence for NoöPunk:** Physical and Psychic should **not** be separate universes.
They may be two decompositions — two observational aspects — of a deeper substrate. This is
what allows the setting to have psychotronics at all: a technology that couples the two
aspects is only possible if they share a root.

### 6.3.2 *Unus mundus*: the deepest layer behind both

Jung's *unus mundus* gives the setting its deepest lore concept: apparent multiplicity
rests on an underlying unity.

- **Physical Space** — the material projection/aspect.
- **Psychic / Noöspace** — the experiential/mental projection/aspect.
- ***Unus mundus*** — the inaccessible or partially accessible substrate beneath both.
- Advanced NHI, psychotronics, psionic adepts and AGI research may access **different
  slices** of it.

**Why this is useful:** it avoids making Noöspace merely "another dimension with ghosts in
it." It can be stranger — **a pre-physical / pre-mental domain from which both categories
emerge.**

**Terminology.** Acceptable in-world terms: *neutral domain*, *psychophysical substrate*,
*underlying reality*, *unus mundus*, *noetic substrate*. **Do not state that Atmanspacher
identifies this domain with Noöspace** — that identification is a NoöPunk extrapolation,
and the setting should keep the two distinguishable.

### 6.3.3 Structural vs induced correlations — the basis of psionics

Atmanspacher and Fach distinguish:

| | Structural correlations | Induced correlations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Character** | stable, persistent, reproducible | occasional, unstable, elusive |
| **Appears as** | ordinary mind–body coupling | anomalous excess or deficit correlations |

**This is the theoretical basis for NoöPunk psionics, and it is what makes PSI feel like
speculative science rather than fantasy magic.**

Instead of *"the psychic fires a telepathy spell"*, the correct framing is:

> **Psychic training alters the probability and stability of normally latent
> psychophysical correlations.**

### 6.3.4 Coincidence and dissociation phenomena — the mechanical vocabulary

Atmanspacher's later formalisation is directly gameable, and it names the two directions a
PSI effect can take:

**Coincidence phenomena — excess correlations:**

- synchronicity; anomalous information coincidence; apparent telepathic linkage;
- precognitive-seeming meaningful coincidence;
- two distant systems acquiring meaningful correlated states.

**Dissociation phenomena — deficit correlations:**

- out-of-body experiences; sleep paralysis; disruptions of ordinary embodiment.

**The NoöPunk extrapolations, which are the setting's PSI vocabulary:**

| Phenomenon | Correlational reading |
| --- | --- |
| Remote viewing / OBE | temporary **weakening** of normal body/self coupling |
| Psychic attack | forced **destabilisation** of another person's ordinary psychophysical correlations |
| Possession / NHI intrusion | a foreign pattern exploiting a **dissociated** state |
| Psychotronic defence | **preserving** stable mind–body coupling |
| Psychotronic induction chamber | deliberately pushing a subject **from structural into induced** correlations |

**Rules hook (this is a vocabulary, not yet a subsystem).** `Psychic Defence` is the skill
that **preserves** coupling. `Telepathy`, `ESP` and `Precognition` **exploit** excess
correlations. `Psychokinesis` **induces** them. `Noöspace` navigates the aspect in which
they are structured. **The specific numbers, thresholds and durations remain DEFERRED** —
this section names what the mechanics will be *about*, and a later psychic-rules pass
supplies the arithmetic.

### 6.3.5 Synchronicity: meaningful, acausal correlation

Jungian **synchronicity** is treated as a special type of mind–matter correlation.

**Key point: it is not simply "something unlikely happened."** A coincidence is
synchronistic when it carries **meaning** — when the mental and physical aspects are linked
by significance rather than by cause.

**Design rule:** synchronicity in NoöPunk is **never a solved puzzle and never a random
table**. When the GM uses it, it must mean something *to the character*; the meaning is the
mechanism. It is the setting's licensed way to make an investigation turn on something that
is not causally connected — and the reason the Noösphere can communicate through
"coincidence" rather than through a channel.

## 6.4 Sleep and dream nights

**Status: NOÖPUNK NATIVE phase design, issue #175. Mechanics light and partly DEFERRED.**

Sleep is a **recurring gameplay phase**, not downtime. It is when the boundaries between
Physical, Social, Cyber and Psyche/Seity become less stable, and when many psychic
phenomena emerge naturally.

**Design note, stated as a rule:** *do not make every night spectacular.* Most nights are
mundane dreams or no remembered dreams at all. Sleep is a recurring **opening**; the
extraordinary case is rare and memorable.

### 6.4.1 Ordinary dreaming

Sometimes a dream is simply the character's own processing: recent events, fears,
unresolved conflicts, memories, symbolic recombination, emotional processing, distorted
versions of waking relationships. **Not every strange dream is supernatural** — and the
GM should let a player waste effort treating one as if it were.

### 6.4.2 Precognition in dreams

Some dreams contain information about possible future events. These must be:

- ambiguous; symbolic; probabilistic;
- difficult to distinguish from ordinary dreams;
- **only obviously precognitive in retrospect.**

**Rules hook:** a precognitive dream is a `Precognition` check resolved by the GM **at the
time of interpretation, not at the time of dreaming**, and it yields **symbolic material,
not a fact**. It never spoils a plot it could not plausibly reach (§16.2 entanglement still
applies — a dream about a stranger on another planet is not available without coupling).

### 6.4.3 Telepathy in dreams

Dreams may carry information originating from other minds or Seities: another person's
emotional state, fragments of memory, imagery or language from someone nearby, deliberate
psychic contact, accidental cross-talk, NHI communication. **The character initially
interprets this as their own dream content** — that misattribution is the feature.

### 6.4.4 Astral projection / Noöspace in dreams

On some nights the Psyche/Seity separates partially or fully from the body and moves
through Noöspace: observing the physical environment from outside; encountering other
Seities; entering shared psychic spaces; moving through symbolic or nonlocal environments;
encountering entities without ordinary embodiment; **returning with memories that are hard
to verify.**

### 6.4.5 The four-way disentanglement night

**This is a rare, major-campaign-turning-point event.** All four layers become disentangled
**while all remain separately awake and active**:

| Layer | What it does, alone |
| --- | --- |
| **Physical** | wakes and acts on instincts, conditioned reflexes and bodily habits |
| **Social** | continues linguistic/social behaviour; tries to communicate and network |
| **Cyber** | independently runs its agents, sensors, interfaces and network activity |
| **Psyche / Seity** | awake in its own domain, communicating with other Seities / Noöspace |

**Each layer acts without awareness of what the others are doing.** For the duration, the
player is effectively **one character split into four simultaneous entities.**

**Reintegration.** When the systems reconnect, the character remembers the night from all
four perspectives at once. The result should feel impossible: *the character remembers
being four entities at the same time.* This produces contradictory memories, simultaneous
viewpoints, gaps where one layer could not perceive another, different emotional readings
of the same period, evidence that one layer met something the others never detected, and
**uncertainty over which perspective was the "real self."** The morning after is a major
roleplaying and investigation scene.

**Rules hook (scenario structure, not a subsystem).** Run it as **four concurrent
viewpoints** that the player alternates between — a Physical segment, a Social segment, a
Cyber segment and a Seity segment — with the player understanding how the threads relate
**only after reintegration**. This makes the four-layer ontology from
`rulebook/2_ATTRIBUTES.md` *experiential* rather than merely explanatory: it is how a
player directly experiences what happens when coupling fails.

**This connects directly to §6.5 (disconnected systems)** — the disentanglement night is
the controlled, survivable version of the same phenomenon.

## 6.5 Disconnected systems — the failure states

**Status: NOÖPUNK NATIVE speculative design, issues #173/#174. LORE AND DESIGN IDEAS;
this is not implemented in the current core rules.**

A normal person or entity is an **assemblage of coupled systems**: Physical, Social, Cyber,
Psyche/Seity. The interesting question is **what happens when the coupling breaks.** Instead
of the character simply becoming unconscious or dying, individual systems can continue
operating according to their own logic.

### 6.5.1 Physical alone

A Physical system without Social, Cyber or Seity is a biological ape stripped of
higher-order integration: animal instincts, conditioned reflexes, procedural and motor
habits, automatic bodily routines, stimulus–response behaviour, sleepwalking-like action,
a stiff uncanny continuation of familiar actions without deliberation.

> A disconnected human body may "ramble on" for a while like a **sleepwalking chimpanzee**
> executing habits and conditioned behaviour.

**This is the horror:** the body is active, and the integrated person is no longer there.

### 6.5.2 Social alone

The Social system is fundamentally about language and communication. A useful
simplification for this failure mode:

- **INT in the Social layer** = the ability to produce and manipulate language and symbolic
  communication;
- **SOC** = the ability to form, navigate and maintain networks with other communicating
  agents.

Disconnected from the rest, the Social system may continue producing communication almost
like an LLM: grammatically and socially competent language, continuation of conversational
patterns, role-following, reputation maintenance, participation in institutions and
networks — **possibly convincing interaction despite missing the rest of the person.** This
is a *discursive zombie* or an *autonomous social persona*.

### 6.5.3 Seity as the Social system's analogue at another layer

**Seity is a design idea, not implemented (§6.6).** The analogy:

| Social system | Seity |
| --- | --- |
| language / symbolic communication | quantum-information communication |
| social networking | networking with other Seities |
| linguistic intelligence | ability to structure/interpret that communication |
| sociality | ability to form relations/networks with other Seities |
| human communication network | Noöspheric / Seity network |

So a **disconnected Seity does not become isolated.** It can continue communicating with
other Seities, joining and navigating Seity networks, exchanging non-classical information,
retaining identity and agency, and acting independently **in Noöspace**. That makes Seity
less a ghostly passenger in the body and more **a full communicating agent operating in its
own networked domain.**

### 6.5.4 Cyber alone

Cyber continues independently: agents, implants, network processes, automated defences,
scanning, hacking routines, backups, scheduled actions, digital identities. **A dead or
disconnected biological person can still have an active cybernetic presence.**

### 6.5.5 Why this is useful

These partial-death and partial-disconnection states are what make NoöPunk's four layers
*do* something rather than merely describe:

- Physical continues, but Seity is gone;
- the Social persona keeps talking after the integrated self has disappeared;
- Cyber keeps operating after biological death;
- Seity is alive and networked in Noöspace while the body is comatose;
- Social + Cyber convincingly impersonate someone whose Seity is absent;
- the body executes conditioned behaviour while the other layers are elsewhere;
- Seity communicates normally with other Seities but cannot reconnect to its biological or
  social interface.

**The result is not "alive/dead" but *assemblage integrity*.**

**Potential later uses:** horror scenarios; NHI encounters; abduction, possession and
replacement stories; psychotronic-attack consequences; Noöspace travel accidents; failed
mind transfer; cybernetic resurrection; philosophical-zombie cases; and **forensic
determination of which subsystem is actually present** — which is the investigative hook
that ties this section to `rulebook/12_BEINGS.md` §"Verification".

## 6.6 Seity — a future subsystem

**Status: DESIGN IDEA ONLY. Issue #173 explicitly says: do not implement this in the core
rules yet. It is recorded here so it is not lost, and so nothing else accidentally depends
on it.**

Federico Faggin's **seity / conscious agent** concept is recorded as a possible future
NoöPunk component: the conscious self treated as **a separate component of the character**,
analogous to how cyberware carries its own hardware properties.

```text
BODY
  ordinary physical / biological stats

CYBERNETIC SYSTEM
  COMPUTE
  INTERFACE
  NETWORK

SEITY            (future; not implemented)
  CONSCIOUSNESS
  IDENTITY
  AGENCY
  CREATIVITY
```

> **NoöPunk game-design formalisation, inspired by Faggin — not a claim that he defines an
> RPG-like four-stat system.** And per issue #173, **not implemented now.**

### 6.6.1 What the four properties would mean

| Property | Meaning | Would answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **CONSCIOUSNESS** | depth / richness / availability of subjective experience | altered states, psychic perception, Noöspace access, degree of phenomenological awareness |
| **IDENTITY** | continuity and stability of the self-model: *"I am still me."* | resisting possession; resisting ego fragmentation; surviving memory alteration; continuity through cybernetic replacement; upload / copy / fork questions; distinguishing genuine continuity from imitation |
| **AGENCY** | capacity for genuinely self-directed choice | resisting coercion and mind control; autonomy under AGI/NHI influence; psychic domination; distinguishing an agent from a fully constrained process |
| **CREATIVITY** | capacity to generate genuinely novel possibilities rather than execute or recombine fixed patterns | deep improvisation; psychic novelty; novel symbolic acts; breaking deterministic prediction |

**IDENTITY is likely the most useful NoöPunk-specific property**, because it is the one that
uploading, resleeving, possession and hybridization all turn on.

**The Seity block should NOT behave like an ordinary task-resolution stat.** It becomes
relevant only when something touches the deepest level of personhood: psychic awakening;
OBE; possession; NHI mind influence; psychotronic attack; ego dissolution; memory rewrite;
mind uploading; consciousness transfer; merging with AI; identity fragmentation;
reincarnation and continuity questions; deep Noöspace interaction.

**Why it stays separate from FIT/REF/INT/SOC/CYB/PSY:** it operates at a more fundamental
level and answers different questions — *Is this still the same person? Is this entity
conscious? Is it acting freely? Has its identity been damaged or replaced?*

**The clean contrast it gives NoöPunk:** an implant can be powerful in COMPUTE, INTERFACE
and NETWORK while the conscious agent using it differs radically in CONSCIOUSNESS,
IDENTITY, AGENCY and CREATIVITY. That distinguishes **information-processing capacity** from
**conscious personhood** — which is exactly the distinction the setting's AI-rights and
panpsychism themes require.

### 6.6.2 Open design questions (issue #173, unresolved)

- Is Seity universal, or only used for conscious / awakened entities?
- Can AGIs have Seity?
- Do hybrids and NHI use the same framework?
- Are Seity values numbers, qualitative states, tracks or tags?
- How does Seity interact with PSY?
- Should **Identity** be mechanically central to possession / mind-control / upload scenarios?
- How does Faggin's framework compare with NoöPunk panpsychism, Law-of-One density and
  evolution, Atmanspacher's dual-aspect monism (§6.3) and the current psychic design?

**Do not implement until the core rules are stable.** This section's job is to hold the idea
and its constraints, so a later pass does not re-derive them.

### 6.6.3 On EMP

Issue #172 raises **Empathy (EMP)** as a possible future stat, and #173 notes it must stay
separate from Seity. Both are recorded here as **possibilities only**:

- **EMP must NOT be restored to the core stat list now.** The current six base STATs
  (`RULEBOOK.md` §9.2) are unchanged.
- If EMP returns, it could be a social attribute, a psychic trait, a consciousness-development
  variable, or a derived property — and it should **remain separate from Seity** unless
  later design work shows a strong reason to merge them.

## 6.7 PSI powers — the working list

**Status: NOÖPUNK NATIVE list; individual powers are described, not statted. Deliberately
light per the author's instruction: a list of stuff with light description.**

This is **not** a power catalogue with costs and dice. It is the vocabulary of what PSI
does in NoöPunk, mapped to the canonical PSY Skills (`RULEBOOK.md` §4.1). A later
psychic-rules pass adds the arithmetic.

The organising principle, from §6.3.4: every power is a manipulation of **psychophysical
correlation**, and every power requires **coupling** (§16.2) to its target.

### 6.7.1 The canonical PSY Skills, and what each does

| Skill | Correlational mode | Light description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **ESP** | excess | Anomalous perception: clairvoyance, psychometry (reading an object's history), remote viewing. *Psychometry and remote viewing are applications of ESP, not separate skills* (§4.1). |
| **Precognition** | excess | Anomalous perception of future possibilities or probabilities. Symbolic, probabilistic, retrospective. |
| **Telepathy** | excess | Reading, transmitting or influencing thoughts and emotional content. Also covers mediumship / entity contact, with `Noöspace` when the contact is via that domain (§4.1). |
| **Psychokinesis** | induced | Deliberately influencing physical systems through PSI. |
| **Noöspace** | navigation | Entering, navigating, perceiving and acting in Noöspace. |
| **Psychic Defence** | **preservation** | Resisting telepathy, psychic intrusion, hostile noetic influence and related attacks. **Hostile PSI uses Telepathy or Psychokinesis, opposed by Psychic Defence** (§4.1). |

### 6.7.2 Technique families — the light list

Grouped by how they are learned and practised. **Descriptions only; no stats.**

**Perception techniques** (ESP / Precognition based)

- **Remote viewing** — directed anomalous perception of a distant or hidden target.
- **Psychometry** — reading impressions from an object, place or residue.
- **Aura / field reading** — perceiving the "shape" of a person's psychic state (which is
  *not* mind-reading — see the note below).
- **Precognitive impression** — symbolic foreknowledge; strong in dreams (§6.4.2).
- **Noetic dowsing / resonance tracing** — following a resonance or entanglement to its
  source, the investigative staple.

**Communication techniques** (Telepathy based)

- **Mind-to-mind contact** — deliberate, consensual transmission.
- **Emotional broadcasting** — transmitting affect rather than content.
- **Empathic sensing** — perceiving another's emotional state. *This is where the social
  and psychic readings of empathy meet (§6.6.3), and it is NOT mind-reading.*
- **Cross-talk** — accidental, unwanted reception (a hazard, not a technique).
- **Entity contact / mediumship** — contact with a Seity, Noetic entity or NHI mind, via
  `Telepathy` or `Noöspace` (§4.1).

**Influence techniques** (Telepathy / Psychokinesis based — the ethically fraught ones)

- **Suggestion** — planting an impulse that the target experiences as their own.
- **Emotional modulation** — shifting affect.
- **Memory interference** — degrading, blurring or implanting memory content.
- **Psychic pressure** — sustained coercive influence; the Orion and Men-in-Black
  capability in `rulebook/12_BEINGS.md`.
- **Kinesis** — moving or affecting physical systems through PSI.

**Defence techniques** (Psychic Defence based)

- **Shielding** — maintaining stable coupling against intrusion.
- **Grounding** — re-stabilising after dissociation or attack (the standard psychotronic
  baseline, `rulebook/13_EQUIPMENT.md`).
- **Counter-surveillance** — detecting that one is being read.
- **Severing** — breaking an entanglement edge; the counter-technique that makes PSI
  investigations non-trivial.
- **Dampening / firewall mode** — the emergency psychotronic response (§12).

**Practice traditions** (these are *methods*, not separate powers — §6.2.4)

- meditation; trance; chanting and liturgy; collective worship; ecstatic dance; divination;
  ritual magic; shamanic journeying; possession rites; dream work; sensory deprivation;
  neurotechnology-assisted trance; altered-state work (§6.2.5).

**These traditions are how PSI is structured, amplified, synchronised and directed.** They
are not a separate magic system, and they do not bypass the canonical Skills: practising a
tradition is how a character *raises* `ESP`, `Telepathy` or `Psychic Defence`, and how a
group combines its PSI.

### 6.7.3 Design rules for the power list

- **Every power needs coupling** (§16.2). There is no "scan anyone anywhere."
- **Every power is a check**, not a switch: `PSY + Skill + 1d10` against a DV, or opposed
  by `Psychic Defence`.
- **No single reading is proof.** A genuine psionic perception is evidence with a
  confidence, not a verdict — the same rule that governs sensors in `rulebook/13_EQUIPMENT.md`
  and identity in `rulebook/12_BEINGS.md`.
- **Aura reading is not mind-reading.** This distinction is load-bearing for the setting's
  handling of Empathy (§6.6.3) and for the psionic investigator archetype. A character can
  feel that a room is frightened; they cannot tell you why.
- **Powers do not resolve the setting's mysteries.** The deepest questions (identity
  continuity, what the Noösphere is, whether an entity is conscious) are not answerable by
  any PSI Skill — that is what §6.6's Seity block would be for, and it is **not implemented.**

## 6.8 Psychotronics — the technology

**Status: NOÖPUNK NATIVE. The equipment catalogue is in `rulebook/13_EQUIPMENT.md`; this
section states what the technology *is*.**

**Psychotronic** covers devices and systems intended to interact with the aspects described
in §6.3 — either by **sensing** mind↔matter correlation or by **altering** it.

**Two families, and the distinction matters (§6.2.1):**

1. **Consciousness-linked psychotronics** — genuinely coupling to the Noösphere, altered
   states, PSI, quantum-consciousness processes. The QIP/Orch-OR-derived technologies of
   `RULEBOOK.md` §16.5: PSI augmentation, psychotronic sensing and interfaces, cortical
   stacks, resleeving, uploading and restoration, quantum-consciousness processors,
   engineered Noösphere interfaces.
2. **Conventional nervous-system psychotronics** — affecting people through ordinary
   physical mechanisms (microwaves, ultrasound, infrasound, EM stimulation, drugs,
   stagecraft, memes, BCI manipulation, implanted neurotech). **Nothing inherently to do
   with PSI or consciousness fields** — but a victim may experience it as paranormal.

**The practical boundary between PSI, psychotronic technology, cybernetic neurohacking,
psyops, pharmacology and deception should often be deliberately blurry.** That is §6.2's
central claim, applied to the technology.

**Engineering belongs to `Hardware (Psychotronics)`**; psychic operation of a psychotronic
device uses the relevant PSY Skill (§4.1).

### 6.8.1 The unresolved metaphysical question

**Status: deliberately open. Issue #180 raises it and NoöPunk should not settle it cleanly.**

Suppose a human biological organism remains functional but somehow **loses its connection
to the deeper field / Source / seity / quantum-consciousness layer.** Two readings:

1. **Philosophical-zombie interpretation** — the biological system continues operating,
   talking and remembering, but there is no subjective experience behind it.
2. **New-agent interpretation** — the brain's structures reconnect to the wider field and
   instantiate a **new conscious agent.**

This raises the identity question that the whole setting turns on:

> **If a new conscious agent inherits the body's memories, personality and relationships,
> is it the same person?**

**Possible answers must remain contested in-universe.** This is useful for resleeving,
cloning, cortical stacks, uploading, possession, NHI hybridization, AI embodiment, death
and resurrection, legal identity, theology, and AI/posthuman rights. **NoöPunk must not
settle it cleanly** — and per `rulebook/2_ATTRIBUTES.md`, cortical-stack continuity and
resleeving mechanics remain explicitly deferred.

## 6.9 Contact, channeling and possession

**Status: NOÖPUNK NATIVE, issue #176 §6–7. Lore and design hooks; light mechanics.**

### 6.9.1 CE-5-style contact as noetic summoning

CE-5-like contact rituals are represented as **attempts to use PSI to attract, summon or
establish contact with a noetic or interdimensional entity.**

**Setting twist:** the result is **more likely to be an orb, an energetic or noetic
manifestation, a thought-form, an anomalous presence or an interdimensional entity than a
conventional nuts-and-bolts spacecraft.** This is what distinguishes NoöPunk's
Vallée-inspired *high-weirdness* contact layer (`RULEBOOK.md` §41) from ordinary
extraterrestrial vehicle encounters — and it is why CE-5 groups are simultaneously right
about *something happening* and wrong about *what*.

**The ambiguity must remain** (this is the whole point of §6.2.6's attribution step):

- Was the entity summoned?
- Was it already present and merely perceived?
- Did the ritual **create** a thought-form (`rulebook/11_ONTOLOGY.md` §10.9)?
- Did an external intelligence answer?
- Did a **psychotronic system spoof** the entire event?

**Rules hook:** a contact working is a `Telepathy` or `Noöspace` check (with the collective
ritual modifiers of §6.2.4), and it produces **an event of uncertain origin** — never a
confirmed visitor. Detection and attribution then follow
`rulebook/12_BEINGS.md` §"Verification".

### 6.9.2 Channeling as borrowed PSI, with possession risk

**Channeling** is opening an information/PSI link to another entity and temporarily
accessing some of that entity's capabilities, knowledge or influence.

**Possible benefits:** information unavailable to the character; temporary PSI
amplification; access to alien/noetic cognition; remote perception; unusual symbolic or
linguistic knowledge.

**But it must be dangerous.** The more bandwidth and agency granted to the channeled
entity, **the more the distinction between channeling and possession collapses.**

**Possible risks:** personality contamination; intrusive thoughts; memory bleed;
compulsions; false memories; identity confusion; deceptive entities; persistent attachment;
partial or full possession.

> **Channeling should be powerful but never casually safe.**

**Rules hook (light).** Channeling is an **exchange with a cost**, not a buff spell: the
character gains access (a bonus to a PSY knowledge or PSI check, or a piece of information)
and takes on a **contamination/agency risk** that scales with the bandwidth granted. **The
exact track, thresholds and recovery remain DEFERRED**, and the identity consequences land
in `rulebook/6_PSYCHIC.md` §6.6's Identity question (not implemented) and
`rulebook/12_BEINGS.md` §11.11.

## 6.10 PSI culture: contemplative training, the occult archive and myth

**Status: NOÖPUNK NATIVE, issue #176 §9–12. Lore.**

### 6.10.1 Contemplative practice has become standard PSI training

By the NoöPunk era, techniques once treated as **religious, esoteric or wellness practices
are routinely used in professional PSI training**: meditation; yoga; breathwork;
visualization; mantra; contemplative prayer; trance induction; lucid dreaming;
attention-control exercises; ritualized body postures and movement.

**Different institutions explain these practices differently** — some as neurocognitive
discipline, some as consciousness engineering, some as spiritual tradition, some as
practical techniques that simply work.

> **A Europol PSI specialist, a Buddhist monk, a yogi, a ceremonial magician and an NHI
> contactee may recognize parts of one another's toolkits even while disagreeing completely
> about the metaphysics.**

**This makes PSI culture syncretic, contested and historically layered rather than a clean
new scientific discipline** — and it is the in-world reason §6.2.4's ritual practices sit
inside the psionics framework rather than beside it.

### 6.10.2 The occult archive becomes unexpectedly relevant after NHI disclosure

There should be a **major overlap between post-disclosure NHI research and the historical
archive of occultism, esotericism, mysticism, divination and religion**: chakras; Kabbalah;
Tarot; I Ching; alchemy; Hermeticism; shamanic cosmologies; angelology and demonology;
visionary mysticism; possession traditions; mediumship and channeling; sacred geometry;
ritual magic.

**The setting does NOT need to say that every occult doctrine was literally correct.**
Instead the revelation is messier: some ancient myths, religious experiences, visions,
apparitions, "gods", "angels", "demons", spirits, fairies and other supernatural encounters
were **influenced by or interpreted through** encounters with NHI, noetic entities,
interdimensionals, thought-forms, PSI phenomena, or deliberately manipulated human
perception.

> **Ancient symbolic systems may preserve distorted, culture-bound, metaphorical, partially
> accurate, or deliberately deceptive records of earlier contact.**

**The modern crisis is that the NHI are back in the open**, and suddenly the old occult
archive looks less like obsolete superstition and more like **a corrupted historical
dataset** — which creates enormous scholarly, political, religious, intelligence and
popular-culture conflict over interpretation. This is a natural home for the
**occult-symbolic knowledge domain** as an investigative `Know` specialisation
(`RULEBOOK.md` §4.1's field model).

### 6.10.3 Myth, religion and NHI intervention

NoöPunk can explicitly allow that **many ancient myths and religions were affected by NHI
intervention.** Possible in-setting interpretations:

- direct contact with nonhuman intelligences;
- NHI deliberately presenting themselves as gods, angels, ancestors, demons or spirits;
- genuine PSI experiences interpreted through local cosmology;
- interdimensional entities interacting with human consciousness;
- thought-forms created or amplified by collective belief;
- later religions mythologizing poorly remembered contact events;
- hostile or benevolent NHI shaping belief systems for strategic reasons;
- ordinary human institutions incorporating and transforming anomalous encounters over
  centuries.

> **Crucially, not every religion or myth should be reduced to aliens.**

Human culture, politics, psychology, spirituality, institutions, storytelling and historical
contingency still matter enormously. **The setting preserves ambiguity over what was
genuinely NHI, what was human, and what emerged through interaction between the two.**
This is the direct in-world consequence of §6.10.2 and of
`rulebook/11_ONTOLOGY.md` §10.9's manifestation-versus-species rule.

### 6.10.4 There is no universal paradigm shift

**Very important — and a setting rule, not a flavour note.**

**NoöPunk society must not behave as if everyone accepts the same worldview.** Even after
AGI, PSI, NHI disclosure, psychotronics and BCIs become major public realities, a large
minority remains:

- skeptical; in denial; ideologically or religiously opposed; scientifically skeptical;
- politically hostile; indifferent; exhausted by constant paradigm shifts;
- confused or unable to understand the implications; poorly informed; deliberately
  misinformed; terrified or panicked; selectively accepting only some phenomena;
- convinced the whole thing is propaganda, psyops, simulation, hoax, cultism or
  manipulation.

**And this applies independently to different domains.** Someone may:

- accept AGI but reject PSI;
- accept UFO/NHI disclosure but think all channeling is fraud;
- believe in PSI but distrust AGI;
- accept BCIs but oppose psychotronics;
- be religious and interpret NHI through theology;
- be materialist and accept only technological explanations;
- refuse all paradigm shifts and cling to pre-disclosure common sense;
- simply not care.

> **The existence of extraordinary phenomena does not produce universal agreement about
> what they mean.**

**There is no single consensual post-disclosure worldview.** The world remains socially
fragmented, with overlapping cultures of belief, disbelief, panic, opportunism,
commercialization, denial, cult formation, bureaucratic normalization and political
backlash.

**Rules hook (light).** This is a **standing constraint on scenario and NPC design**, not a
subsystem: no scene may assume an informed consensus about PSI, NHI or AGI, and the
**Belief / denial / stigma** dimension of a scenario is expressed through the existing
Affect and Motivation machinery (`rulebook/8_FACTIONS.md`). Mechanics for uncertainty,
belief, panic and competing explanations remain **DEFERRED**.

## 6.11 Atmanspacher's further concepts (issue #171 §6–11)

**Status: NOÖPUNK NATIVE setting theory and vocabulary. No mechanics beyond what §6.3
already hooks.**

### 6.11.1 Meaning fields

Atmanspacher describes **meaning as intrinsically relational**, and suggests thinking about
the relation connecting mental and physical as something like a **meaning field**.

**NoöPunk can literalize this cautiously.** Possible setting terms: **semantic field /
meaning field / noetic resonance field.**

**Uses:**

- psychotronic sensors measure changes in **semantic/noetic correlation** rather than
  "psychic energy";
- an AI can detect statistical or semantic anomalies but **may not understand their
  personally experienced meaning**;
- trained psychics can identify meaningful coupling that a purely physical sensor misses;
- locations, artifacts, people and symbols can form **high-resonance networks**.

**This dovetails with the setting's network-society and discourse-theory heritage: meaning
is relational, not a substance** — which is also why it is the correct vocabulary for the
Noöspace `Noöspace` Skill's "resonance tracing" technique (§6.7.2).

### 6.11.2 Archetypes as psychoid ordering factors

In the Pauli–Jung framework, archetypes are **not merely literary stereotypes.** Jung and
Pauli pushed toward **psychoid** ordering principles beneath the ordinary psyche.

**NoöPunk recycling:**

- archetypes = **attractors / ordering patterns within the psychophysical substrate**;
- they can organise both psychic experience and apparently external events;
- cults, ideological movements, AGIs and NHI might **deliberately instantiate or amplify**
  archetypal patterns;
- **an archetype is not necessarily a conscious entity.**

> **Important distinction: archetype ≠ god / NHI / daemon by default.** An NHI may exploit
> an archetype, identify with one, or become entangled with one.

This is the setting's cleanest guard against collapsing symbolic order into a pantheon, and
it connects to the thought-form rule (§6.10.3, `rulebook/11_ONTOLOGY.md` §10.9).

### 6.11.3 Exceptional experiences taxonomy

Atmanspacher and Fach connect the model to empirical reports of thousands of "exceptional
experiences". **For NoöPunk, create an in-world scientific taxonomy rather than throwing
everything into "paranormal".**

**Potential classes:** coincidence / synchronicity; dissociation / embodiment anomalies;
internal anomalies of the self/world model; externalized anomalies; altered
categorial/non-categorial/acategorial states (§6.11.4).

> **This can become the Europol / Ö-Mappi anomaly classification system.**

Field agents classify a report **before** deciding whether it is ordinary psychology,
disinformation, cybernetic manipulation, psychotronic attack, a genuine psychophysical
anomaly, NHI activity, or **unresolved**. This is the taxonomy that §6.2.6's
detection → suspicion → attribution → classification → authorisation procedure runs on, and
it belongs with the scenario's case-file architecture
(`docs/scenarios/01_FIRST_DAY_IN_OMAPPI.md`).

### 6.11.4 Categorial, non-categorial and acategorial states

Atmanspacher and Fach distinguish **mental-state stability classes**. This enriches NoöPunk
altered states **without "sanity points"**:

| Class | State |
| --- | --- |
| **categorial** | ordinary stable reality model |
| **non-categorial** | boundaries and categories loosen |
| **acategorial** | ordinary conceptual differentiation becomes unstable or fails |

**NoöPunk uses these for:** meditation; psychedelic states; NHI encounters; psychic
awakening; OBE; psychotronic attack; ego dissolution; AGI-mediated consciousness interfaces.

> **A high-PSY character does not simply "resist weirdness".** They may be better able to
> **enter and return from** less categorial states without losing functional agency.

**Rules hook:** this replaces the madness-meter idiom with a **state-stability vocabulary**,
and it is the correct frame for §6.2.5's altered-state modifiers. **No numeric sanity track
is defined, and none is planned.**

### 6.11.5 Generalized Quantum Theory (formerly Weak Quantum Theory)

Atmanspacher and collaborators helped develop a generalized formalism that keeps structural
concepts — **complementarity; noncommuting observables; generalized entanglement** — while
dropping assumptions specific to microscopic physical quantum mechanics.

**The crucial NoöPunk lesson:**

> **Do not claim that the brain is literally a giant quantum computer merely to explain
> PSI.**

Instead, fictional psychophysical systems can exhibit **quantum-like structural relations**
without requiring ordinary quantum effects at neuronal scale. **This gives psychotronics much
better technobabble:** incompatible observables; context-dependent states; complementarity;
nonlocal/global correlations; measurement and context changing the available state
description.

**This is the vocabulary `rulebook/13_EQUIPMENT.md` should use for psychotronic devices, in
place of "psychic energy".**

### 6.11.6 Elusiveness is a feature, not a bug

The framework takes seriously that **induced exceptional correlations may be unstable and
difficult to reproduce**. This is an excellent balancing mechanism:

- abilities **cannot always be produced on command**;
- **meaning and context matter**;
- emotional or relational intensity matters;
- laboratory reproducibility may **collapse as the phenomenon is stripped of context**;
- psychotronic technology improves reliability **but never turns PSI into a mundane pistol**.

> **This preserves mystery in a setting where technology otherwise risks making NHI and PSI
> trivial to detect.**

**This is the design rule that keeps §6.7's power list from becoming a toolkit.** A PC who
wants their PSI to work reliably must invest in context, relationship and preparation — not
just a rating.

### 6.11.7 The synthesis, and its status

The consolidated in-world theory:

> **The Atmanspacher–Pauli–Jung model** holds that physical and psychic phenomena are
> complementary decompositions of a deeper psychophysically neutral substrate. Ordinary
> embodiment is maintained by stable structural correlations. Psionic phenomena occur when
> unusual induced correlations form, weaken or reorganise those baseline relationships.
> Psychotronic systems manipulate the boundary conditions under which such correlations
> become possible. Synchronicity is the special case in which an acausal mind–matter
> relation becomes manifest through meaning.

**This is one scientific paradigm / faction's model — not confirmed metaphysical truth.**
Rival explanations must remain possible in-world (`RULEBOOK.md` §34's epistemic tiers; §6.1).

### 6.11.8 What NOT to import uncritically

These cautions are **binding**, and they are the reason §6.3 and this section are labelled
setting theory rather than science:

- **Do not** present dual-aspect monism as experimentally established physics.
- **Do not** label every coincidence as synchronicity.
- **Do not** equate generalized quantum theory with ordinary physical quantum entanglement.
- **Do not** claim Atmanspacher experimentally proved telepathy, PK or precognition.
- **Distinguish** his conceptual framework and exceptional-experience research from stronger
  fictional NoöPunk extrapolations.
- **Keep multiple competing in-world theories alive.**

# Psychic Systems

**Status: NOÖPUNK CORE, expanding.** This chapter consolidates the Psychic domain:
psychology, consciousness, psionics, Noöspace and psychotronics. It carries light rules and
**lists of things** — powers, technologies, phenomena, anomaly classes — rather than full
statistics for each entry. Numeric detail is deliberately deferred.

Canonical resolution remains `STAT + Skill + 1d10` (RULEBOOK.md §4.1, §8).

---

## 1. The deliberately muddy boundary

NoöPunk treats **psionics, psychotronic technology, psychological operations, BCI
exploitation, pharmacology, ritual practice and outright deception as overlapping domains**
(issue #176). The distinction between them is usually **operational rather than ontological**:
the investigator must work out *what mixture of mechanisms is actually present*, not merely
label an effect "real PSI" or "mere technology".

Two broad families should be distinguished (issue #180):

**A. PSI / consciousness-linked effects.** Phenomena genuinely mediated through
consciousness and Noöspace under the setting's post-QIP model — telepathy, remote viewing,
precognition, psychokinesis, and their Noetic relatives.

**B. Conventional nervous-system manipulation.** Effects that produce a paranormal-feeling
experience through ordinary physical mechanisms:

- microwave / radio-frequency stimulation;
- focused ultrasound and infrasound;
- electromagnetic stimulation;
- sensory entrainment (light, sound, rhythm);
- drugs and fictionalised aerosolised psychoactives;
- holograms, stagecraft and constructed environments;
- engineered propaganda, memes and deepfakes;
- BCI manipulation and implanted neurotechnology;
- ordinary social engineering and deception.

A victim may experience something that feels paranormal even when the mechanism is mundane.
A hostile PSI event may be disguised as malware, and malware may deliberately imitate the
symptoms of PSI.

### 1.1 Psychotronics is broader than quantum-consciousness devices

"**Psychotronic**" is the in-setting umbrella term for technology intended to alter
perception, cognition, emotion, behaviour or nervous-system state. It spans:

- non-quantum directed-energy and sensory systems (RF, infrasound, light, EM stimulation);
- conventional psychological manipulation amplified by advanced sensing and targeting;
- engineered memes, audiovisual patterns, staged illusions, holograms, deepfakes;
- psychoactive chemical delivery;
- hybrid systems combining physical stimulation, information warfare, AI personalisation
  and psionic amplification;
- genuine consciousness-linked psychotronics (the QIP/PSI end of the spectrum).

### 1.2 BCI hacking can imitate or outperform PSI

A compromised brain-computer interface can produce effects that resemble psionics — false
sensory input, altered attention, injected imagery or inner speech, emotional modulation,
memory interference, motor disruption, fabricated "intuitions", spoofed telepathic or
spiritual experiences. In some contexts this is **more reliable than natural PSI**, because
the attacker holds a direct digital pathway into the target's interface.

Effectiveness depends on the BCI's architecture, its infosec hardening, segmentation and
permissions, defensive agents, the attacker's access and expertise, and whether a relevant
exploit exists. Capable zero-day exploits exist in the setting but are **rare, expensive,
illegal and perishable**. *(Deep-hacking mechanics remain a separate deferred subsystem —
see `5_CYBERNETIC.md`.)*

## 2. Awakening, sensitivity and the psychic scale

**Sleepers** are people who have not undergone meaningful **Psychic Awakening**. Awakening is
the development or activation of direct Noetic sensitivity. It is **not** synonymous with
moral goodness, sanity or social status.

The canonical Psychic Skills (RULEBOOK.md §4.1) are the resolution surface for psychic
action: **ESP, Telepathy, Psychokinesis, Precognition, Noöspace, Psychic Defence**. PSY is
their governing STAT. Awakening is the fictional gate that makes specialist psychic Skills
purchasable at all; the UNSA academy grants every graduate **Psychic Defence** only (§9.4).

## 3. PSI powers (list)

Light catalogue. Each entry is a **capability**, not a fixed power with a stat block. Use the
named Skill against a DV or an opposed roll. Frequency, cost and reliability are intentionally
loose: psi is **context-dependent and partly elusive** (§7.1).

### Perception and information

| Power | Skill | Sketch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Telepathy | Telepathy | Read surface thought/emotion; send meaning; deeper reading is opposed by Psychic Defence. |
| Remote viewing | ESP | Perceive a distant place without the body. Symbolic, partial, hard to verify. |
| Clairvoyance / clairsentience | ESP | Perceive hidden or occluded local detail; often fragmentary. |
| Psychometry | ESP | Read impressions from an object or place; unreliable for the unskilled. |
| Precognition | Precognition | Sense probable futures; symbolic, probabilistic, obvious only in retrospect. |
| Empathic sensing | ESP or Kinesics | Feel another mind's affective state; overlaps with ordinary Kinesics. |
| Dream-sight | Precognition / Telepathy | Receive information during sleep (§5). |

### Influence and action

| Power | Skill | Sketch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Suggestion | Telepathy | Nudge a thought or impulse; resisted by Psychic Defence and by strong intent. |
| Psychic attack | Telepathy / Psychokinesis | Hostile intrusion or destabilisation; opposed by Psychic Defence. |
| Psychokinesis | Psychokinesis | Move matter with mind; fine control is hard, gross effects are loud. |
| Biokinesis | Psychokinesis | Influence living tissue (healing or harm) at the far end of the skill. |
| Noetic contact / mediumship | Telepathy or Noöspace | Reach a Noetic entity or the dead; ontology is uncertain. |
| Astral projection | Noöspace | Separate Psyche/Seity from the body and travel Noöspace (§5, §6). |
| Psychic shielding | Psychic Defence | Preserve stable mind–body coupling; resist intrusion. |
| Grounding | Psychic Defence | End or dampen an ongoing psychic/altered state. |

**Design note.** Powers should usually provide **information, transformation or narrative
convergence** rather than raw damage bonuses. Psychic combat exists, but as an opposed-check
contests of will and coupling, not a spell list of "psychic damage 3".

## 5. Sleep and the night phase

Nights are a recurring gameplay phase, not just downtime (issue #175). Sleep is when the
boundaries between Physical, Social, Cyber and Psyche/Seity are least stable.

- **Ordinary dreaming** — the character's own processing. Not every strange dream is
  supernatural.
- **Precognitive dreams** — ambiguous, symbolic, probabilistic; obviously precognitive only
  in hindsight.
- **Telepathic dreams** — material from other minds or Seities; the character may mistake it
  for their own dream content.
- **Noöspace / astral nights** — the Psyche may separate partially or fully and travel,
  returning with memories that are hard to verify.

### 5.1 The four-way disentanglement night

A rare, high-impact event where **all four layers come apart while all remain awake and
active**: Physical acts on instinct and habit; Social keeps talking and networking; Cyber
keeps running agents and interfaces; Psyche/Seity is awake in its own domain and in contact
with other Seities — **each without awareness of the others**. The player effectively runs one
character split into four simultaneous entities for the night.

On reintegration the character remembers the night from all four perspectives at once. This
produces contradictory memories, simultaneous viewpoints, perception gaps, divergent
emotional readings, and evidence that one layer met something the others never detected. The
morning after is a major roleplaying and investigation scene. *(Scenario-structure idea; not
yet a core subsystem.)*

## 6. Seity — a parked concept

*Idea only; not implemented. Do not build mechanics on this yet* (issue #173).

A future component tracking deep properties of conscious agency, parallel to how cyberware
tracks COMPUTE / INTERFACE / NETWORK:

```text
SEITY
  CONSCIOUSNESS   depth / richness of subjective experience
  IDENTITY        continuity of the self-model — "I am still me"
  AGENCY          capacity for genuinely self-directed choice
  CREATIVITY      capacity to generate genuinely novel possibilities
```

This is a NoöPunk game-design formalisation *inspired by* Federico Faggin's seity framework,
not a claim that he defines a stat system. It would matter when something touches the deepest
level of personhood — awakening, OBE, possession, mind control, psychotronic attack, ego
dissolution, memory rewrite, uploading, consciousness transfer, identity fragmentation,
reincarnation/continuity questions. It is **separate from** PSY and does not restore EMP.
Question it must eventually answer: *is this still the same person?*

## 7. The in-world theory: Pauli–Jung / Atmanspacher (issues #171)

*One scientific paradigm among rivals; not established truth in-setting or out.*

A body of in-world theory — dual-aspect monism descending from the Pauli–Jung conjecture —
holds that **mental and physical are complementary aspects of one psychophysically neutral
reality**. The deeper level is neither simply mind nor matter. Ordinary mind–body correlation
and extraordinary experience are treated within one taxonomy.

```text
        Psychophysically neutral reality  (unus mundus / noetic substrate)
                 /                          \
          Physical aspect              Psychic aspect
```

Do **not** state that this framework identifies the neutral domain with Noöspace; that is a
NoöPunk extrapolation. Keep rival theories alive.

### 7.1 Structural vs induced correlations — the basis of psionics

- **Structural correlations** — stable, persistent, reproducible; ordinary embodiment.
- **Induced correlations** — occasional, unstable, elusive; the extraordinary.

Psionics is then read not as "casting a spell" but as *altering the probability and stability
of normally latent psychophysical correlations*. This makes psi feel like speculative science.

**Coincidence phenomena (excess correlation):** synchronicity, anomalous information
coincidence, apparent telepathic linkage, meaningful coincidence.
**Dissociation phenomena (deficit correlation):** OBE, sleep paralysis, disruption of ordinary
embodiment.

NoöPunk extrapolation: remote viewing/OBE = temporary weakening of body–self coupling;
psychic attack = forced destabilisation of another's ordinary coupling; possession/NHI
intrusion = a foreign pattern exploiting a dissociated state; psychotronic defence = preserve
stable coupling; induction chamber = deliberately push a subject from structural to induced.

### 7.2 Synchronicity, meaning and resonance

Synchronicity is a meaningful, **acausal** mind–matter correlation: a mental event and a
physical event with no ordinary causal link but a shared **meaning**. Meaning is treated as
relational, not a substance — a **meaning field** linking mental and physical aspects.

*Rule idea (not implemented):* characters could accumulate **Resonance / Salience** around
symbols, motivations, factions or archetypes; extreme resonance produces synchronicity
events. Synchronicity should yield **information, transformation or narrative convergence**,
never a generic luck bonus.

### 7.3 Archetypes as psychoid ordering factors

Archetypes are attractors or ordering patterns in the psychophysical substrate — they can
organise both psychic experience and apparently external events. **An archetype is not by
default a god, an NHI or a daemon.** An NHI may exploit, identify with or become entangled
with one.

### 7.4 Exceptional-experience taxonomy

An in-world classification for field reports (the Ö-Mappi anomaly classes), so "paranormal"
is not one bucket:

- coincidence / synchronicity (excess correlation);
- dissociation / embodiment anomalies (deficit correlation);
- internal anomalies of the self/world model;
- externalised anomalies;
- **categorial** (ordinary stable reality model), **non-categorial** (boundaries loosen), and
  **acategorial** (conceptual differentiation fails) mental states.

A field agent classifies a report before deciding whether it is ordinary psychology,
disinformation, cybernetic manipulation, psychotronic attack, genuine psychophysical anomaly,
NHI activity, or unresolved.

### 7.5 Generalized quantum theory, and why psi stays elusive

Fictional psychophysical systems may exhibit **quantum-like structural relations**
(complementarity, incompatible observables, context-dependent states, generalised
entanglement) **without** claiming the brain is a literal quantum computer.

Elusiveness is a feature, not a bug: abilities cannot always be produced on command; meaning,
context, emotion and relationship intensity matter; laboratory reproducibility may collapse as
the phenomenon is stripped of context. Psychotronic technology improves reliability but never
turns psi into a mundane pistol. A high-PSY character is better able to **enter and return
from** less-categorial states without losing functional agency — not simply to "resist
madness".

## 8. Noöspace, attack and defence

**Noöspace** is the quantum-informational domain of Conscious Agents; **the Astral Plane** is
its common name. (Full ontology: RULEBOOK.md §36, §40.4.)

- **Psychic Attack** — hostile PSI/Noetic interaction: opposed `Telepathy` or `Psychokinesis`
  vs the target's `Psychic Defence`. Consequences are fictional and situational (intrusive
  contact, dissociation, coupling disruption), not a numeric damage track.
- **Psychic Defence** — resistance, shielding, grounding, discipline, plus psychotronic
  protection. It is the one psychic Skill every UNSA graduate carries.
- **Polarization** — the direction of consciousness development, influenced by Law-of-One
  service-to-others / service-to-self ideas. It must remain an **evolution of consciousness**,
  not a morality score (RULEBOOK.md §8.8).

## 9. Disconnected systems

*Lore only; a conceptual module for later use* (issue #174).

If Physical, Social, Cyber and Psyche/Seity are genuinely distinct but coupled systems, each
may be able to operate **alone** when coupling breaks:

| System alone | Continues as |
| --- | --- |
| Physical | instinct, reflex, habit — a body that "rambles on" like a sleepwalking animal |
| Social | competent language and role-play, network and reputation maintenance — a discursive zombie |
| Cyber | agents, implants, backups, scheduled actions, digital identity |
| Seity | a full agent awake and networked with other Seities in Noöspace |

The real question is not "alive/dead" but **assemblage integrity**: *which subsystem is
actually present?* Use for horror, possession, replacement, failed transfer, forensic
detection. Do **not** rush this into core mechanics.

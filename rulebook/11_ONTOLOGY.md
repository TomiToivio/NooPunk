# Ontology: densities, vehicles, cryptoterrestrials and thought-forms

> **Status: NOÖPUNK NATIVE lore. Issue #179.** This chapter documents the Law-of-One-inspired
> ontology for **species, densities, cryptoterrestrials, thought-forms, plasmoids and higher
> beings**, for later NoöPunk lore and rules work.
>
> **This chapter is fictional/esoteric source material for NoöPunk, not established science.**
> It recycles the **Ra Material / Law of One** (channeled texts) and related esoteric and
> ufological literature as fictional cosmology. Naming Ra, Maldek or the Council of Saturn is
> a statement about the *setting*, never about real history, physics or beings.
>
> **Light on mechanics by design.** Per the author's instruction, this is a list of ontology
> with descriptions; the investigative hooks are named, and the arithmetic is deferred.

---

## 10.1 The key design principle

> **Density is fundamentally a level of consciousness and evolution — not a biological
> species classification, and not a state of matter.**

That means **appearance alone cannot determine density.** A glowing orb or plasmoid, a
humanoid, a cryptid, a biological alien, a thought-form, or an apparently immaterial being
could each be a **vehicle or manifestation** of very different kinds of entity.

This is excellent for NoöPunk because **ontology becomes an investigative problem**:

> **A UFO investigator sees morphology. A NoöPunk investigator has to discover ontology.**

This is the same anti-shortcut rule that governs sensors (`rulebook/13_EQUIPMENT.md`),
identity verification (`rulebook/12_BEINGS.md`) and NHI detection generally: **appearance is
evidence with a confidence, never a verdict.**

## 10.2 The seven densities

**Working model for NoöPunk.** Density is a **tier/state of consciousness development**, as
`RULEBOOK.md` §9.3 already establishes ("Density is not IQ"). It is **not** a STAT, and
NPCs may be represented from 1D to 7D where useful. PCs normally occupy **3D or 4D**.

### 1st density — awareness and elemental matter

Earth, water, fire and air; undifferentiated elemental awareness.

**NoöPunk interpretation:** the panpsychic physical substrate and emergent elemental
processes. **Natural plasmoids and plasma structures default here** if they are merely
complex matter/energy with primitive awareness.

> **An apparent plasmoid should not automatically be assumed to be a first-density entity**,
> because it could instead be a vessel or manifestation used by a higher-density intelligence.
> See §10.4.

### 2nd density — growth and biological organism

Plants and animals; instinctive biological life developing toward self-awareness.

**Cryptids can use second-density bodies without the inhabiting consciousness itself being
second density.** This is the load-bearing point for §10.5.

### 3rd density — self-awareness and choice

Humans are the baseline example. **Technologically advanced extraterrestrials can also be
third density.**

> **"Alien" does not imply higher density.** A starship crewed by 3D beings with very good
> technology is a normal and important case: it is what makes "advanced technology mistaken
> for higher-density psi" a real false positive (§10.7).

### 4th density — love and understanding

**NoöPunk working interpretation:**

- strong PSI and telepathy;
- **Social Memory Complex formation becomes central** (`RULEBOOK.md` §16.3);
- both positive and negative polarization remain possible;
- **Greys may be mapped here in NoöPunk when useful — but this is a setting interpretation,
  not a direct Ra taxonomy.**

### 5th density — light and wisdom

**NoöPunk working interpretation:**

- extremely advanced PSI and manipulation of manifestation;
- much greater control of spacetime, travel and physical appearance;
- **can be more individualistic than 4D**, because Ra explicitly allows fifth-density
  entities to learn individually rather than only as a social-memory complex;
- Ra describes fifth-density entities as "very light beings" while still having a physical
  vehicle;
- **Mantids and interdimensionals may be mapped here in NoöPunk as a working
  interpretation** — again our taxonomy, not an explicit Ra identification;
- orb and light manifestations **may** represent such beings, **but appearance is not enough
  to classify them.**

### 6th density — unity and the Higher Self

- Integration of love and wisdom.
- **The Higher Self is explicitly sixth-density in the Ra Material** and described as the
  **future self** (§10.6).
- **Time/space becomes central**; all times can be experienced as simultaneous from that
  perspective.
- Ra itself is sixth density, moving toward seventh.
- **Negative polarity becomes unsustainable around mid-sixth density**, so mature 6D
  entities should effectively converge toward unity/positive orientation.
- Rare manifestations could appear as light, orbs or thought-forms rather than ordinary
  biological organisms.

**This gives NoöPunk direct hooks for:** Higher Self contact; precognition; retrocausal
information; "extratempestrial" encounters; apparent time travel; synchronicity and guidance;
and characters encountering manifestations of their own future consciousness.

### 7th density — gateway and return toward the Creator

Individual identity approaches absorption into the allness of the Creator.

**NoöPunk should make 7D almost impossible to distinguish phenomenologically from:** the
Source; universal consciousness; the panpsychic background itself.

> **Avoid turning 7D into just another "powerful alien species."**

This connects to `rulebook/6_PSYCHIC.md` §6.3.2: the deepest layer of the ontology is not a
more powerful *actor* but the substrate both aspects emerge from.

## 10.3 Vehicle vs entity — the separability rule

> **Physical body, synthetic body, plasma body, orb, avatar, dream-body, thought-form and
> projection are separable concepts.**

An entity's **vehicle** (what is perceived) and the entity's **consciousness** (what it is)
are different variables. This single rule resolves most of the chapter's cases:

| Perceived | Possible inhabiting consciousness |
| --- | --- |
| biological body | 2D instinctive life, or 3D+ consciousness using it |
| cryptid body | engineered 2D reserve body, or 3D+ consciousness inhabiting it |
| plasma / orb | 1D natural phenomenon, or a higher-density entity's manifestation |
| synthetic or engineered body | any density's consciousness, or an AI |
| thought-form | the consciousness that constructed or projected it |
| dream-body / projection | the projecting Seity, elsewhere |
| metallic sphere drone | **infrastructure**, not a species (§10.8) |

**Species and density are separate taxonomies.** A species is a lineage; a density is a
level of consciousness. **Do not infer one from the other.**

## 10.4 Plasmoids and luminous orbs

**The Ra Material does not appear to explicitly discuss "plasmoids" as a class of beings.**
NoöPunk therefore supplies its own classification rule:

1. **Ordinary physical plasma / self-organising plasma phenomenon** → default **1D**.
2. **Biological or proto-biological plasma organism** → potentially **2D**.
3. **Self-aware plasma-based person/species** → potentially **3D+**, depending on consciousness.
4. **Orb or light body used by a higher-density entity** → classify the entity by
   **consciousness**, not by the orb's material composition.
5. **Constructed thought-form or psychotronic manifestation** → may not map cleanly to
   ordinary species categories at all (§10.9).

> **A NoöPunk investigator seeing a glowing orb has learned almost nothing merely from
> seeing "plasma."**

## 10.5 Cryptoterrestrials and the Bigfoot correction

The Ra Material's Bigfoot material is **stranger than a simple "Bigfoot species."** Ra
describes **three Bigfoot categories**:

1. **Maldek entities** — third-density consciousnesses undergoing karmic restitution,
   placed in or using **second-density physical forms**, associated with underground and
   deep-wilderness existence.
2. **Radiation-resistant reserve bodies** — instinctual second-density beings,
   engineered/maintained as a **biological gene pool or potential replacement vehicle** in
   case nuclear war makes ordinary human bodies unsuitable. Ra says these should not really
   be treated as the same thing as ordinary Bigfoot despite superficial similarity.
   **"Glowing eyes" are associated with these beings.**
3. **A thought-form Bigfoot** — a third category explicitly described as a **thought-form**
   (§10.9).

**Therefore NoöPunk must NOT collapse "Bigfoot" into one cryptoterrestrial species.**

> **"Bigfoot" is a human folk category covering several ontologically different phenomena
> that happen to produce similar sightings.**

**This is the general NoöPunk rule for cryptids**, and for folklore more broadly: human
categories describe *sightings*, not *kinds of being*.

**Other cryptoterrestrial canon already in the setting** (`RULEBOOK.md` §40.2): Tridactyls
are a real cryptoterrestrial species/category; undersea NHI bases and transit corridors are
canon; and whether modern Nazca bodies and the older esoteric/Law-of-One references describe
the same lineage **remains an in-world research question.**

## 10.6 The Higher Self and time/space entities

**The Higher Self is the future sixth-density self** — and in NoöPunk this is a **canonical
lore hook with open mechanics.**

**Time/space becomes central at that level**: all times can be experienced as simultaneous
from that perspective.

**Hooks this creates:**

- **Higher Self contact** — through dreams, meditation, synchronicities, precognitive
  visions, communication "through the roots of mind", and possibly temporary
  overshadowing.
- **Time/space phenomena** — future-self messages; branching possible futures; apparent time
  loops; encounters with one's own future manifestations; and **"extratempestrials"** whose
  origin is temporal or metaphysical rather than another planet.
- **Precognition and retrocausal information** (`rulebook/6_PSYCHIC.md` §6.7, `Precognition`).

**Design rule:** the Higher Self is **not a friendly quest-giver.** It is a sixth-density
perspective on a life it has already (from its side) lived, communicating under severe
constraints and through symbolic material. It should never simply answer a question.

### 10.6.1 Source notes

For documentation and later citation, the useful Ra passages are: **9.18–9.22** (Maldek /
Bigfoot, nuclear-war reserve bodies, and the third category as thought-form); **8.20–8.21**
(unusual extraterrestrials can simply be third-density beings); **12.21** (Men in Black as
thought-form-type entities with physical characteristics and materialisation/
dematerialisation); **24.19** (Confederation thought-forms could appear indistinguishable
from third-density entities); **43.14** (fifth density can be pursued individually or in
social-memory-complex form); **52.3** (higher-density travel can involve intelligent-energy
disciplines rather than conventional technology); **70.9–70.12** and **37.6** (Higher Self as
the future sixth-density self; time/space simultaneity); **14.19** (Ra identifies itself as
sixth density moving toward seventh).

## 10.7 False positives are mandatory

**This is the chapter's most important investigative rule.** Every inference from appearance
has a competing explanation, and the GM should always be able to name one:

- a **1D natural plasma phenomenon** may look like an NHI orb;
- an **engineered drone** may fake an orb;
- a **5D entity** may look biological;
- a **thought-form** may pass as a cryptid;
- a **3D alien** may possess technology so advanced that humans mistake it for higher-density
  PSI;
- **metallic spheres** may be mimicked by other actors, and a genuine quarantine drone may
  use cloaking or luminous modes (§10.8).

> **Ontology is hidden information.** Characters infer density — if at all — from behaviour,
> PSI effects, social organisation, manifestation limits, polarity, and relationship to
> spacetime. **Not from shape.**

**This is the same doctrine as everywhere else in NoöPunk:** `rulebook/12_BEINGS.md`
§"Verification" for identity, `rulebook/13_EQUIPMENT.md` for sensors, `RULEBOOK.md` §9.3 for
Polarization. **No single channel is definitive.**

## 10.8 Metallic spheres and the Sphere Network

**A specific NoöPunk interpretation for metallic spheres.**

- **Metallic spheres are AI/autonomous drones used to maintain the planetary quarantine.**
- In the setting they are associated with the **Confederacy / Council of Saturn quarantine
  and planetary-defence architecture**.
- Their role is **not** to be treated as an alien species: they are **machines, agents and
  infrastructure**.

**Possible functions:** surveillance nodes; communication relays; interception and defence
units; quarantine enforcement; tracking and triangulation systems; autonomous local response
units; and possibly interfaces between planetary infrastructure and higher-density
Confederation actors.

**The useful distinction from §10.4:**

| Perceived | Default hypothesis |
| --- | --- |
| **metallic sphere** | technological / autonomous drone |
| **luminous orb / plasmoid** | **ontology remains open** — natural plasma, PSI manifestation, thought-form, vehicle, drone, higher-density manifestation |

**Visual appearance alone is still not proof.** A metallic sphere could be mimicked by other
actors, and a Confederacy drone may use cloaking or luminous modes. The distinction is a
*prior*, not a *test*.

**Source note.** This adapts **Patrick Jackson, *The Sphere Network*** — a hypothesis
describing a global network of metallic spheres as an autonomous AI-based planetary-defence
system, with multiple sphere types in coordinated roles such as local sensing, relaying,
triangulation and interception. NoöPunk's adaptation:

> **Sphere Network hypothesis → Council of Saturn / Confederacy quarantine-maintenance AI
> drone network.**

This meshes with `RULEBOOK.md` §41.2, which already records a distributed **Sphere Network**
as possible physical/Noetic infrastructure supporting the Confederacy quarantine — with the
same caution: **do not assume every orb or sphere belongs to it.**

**The layered ontology this keeps intact:** higher-density Confederation / Council actors →
autonomous AI sphere infrastructure → biological and non-biological NHI → thought-forms →
natural plasma phenomena → deceptive or hostile imitations. **Every layer is a distinct
hypothesis, and the setting does not collapse them.**

## 10.9 Thought-forms

Ra describes **Men in Black as a kind of thought-form entity with limited physical
characteristics that can materialise and dematerialise.** Expanding the same principle
beyond Bigfoot:

**Some "species" reported in folklore may actually be:**

- biological cryptoterrestrials; engineered bodies;
- consciousnesses temporarily inhabiting lower-density vehicles;
- PSI projections; higher-density manifestations;
- **autonomous thought-forms**;
- **deliberately constructed deceptive forms**.

**NoöPunk implication:**

> Folkloric elves, demons, angels, black-eyed children, Men in Black, "aliens", cryptids and
> apparitions may sometimes be **phenotypes of manifestation rather than actual species.**

**A thought-form could borrow any culturally legible mythological appearance.** A construct
that appears as a demon to one witness and an alien to another is consistent, and the
appearance is the *interface*, chosen for legibility — or for deception.

> **Do not make every mythological creature "secretly one species."** The point is the
> opposite: human legend is a catalogue of *appearances*, and several ontologically distinct
> things can produce the same legend.

**This connects directly to `RULEBOOK.md` §41.2's Men in Black canon** (principally Orion-
associated, though human intelligence services can imitate MIB folklore and particular
manifestations may be constructs, hybrids, controlled humans, Noetic projections or
synthetic bodies) and to `rulebook/12_BEINGS.md` §"Men in Black".

## 10.10 Rules and investigation hooks (to design later)

**Do not implement full mechanics yet.** These are the hooks a later pass will build on:

- **Density is hidden information.** Characters infer it from behaviour, PSI effects, social
  organisation, manifestation limits, polarity, and relationship to spacetime.
- **Vehicle ≠ entity** (§10.3). Body, synthetic body, plasma body, orb, avatar, dream-body,
  thought-form and projection are separable.
- **Higher Self contact** through dreams, meditation, synchronicities, precognitive visions,
  temporary overshadowing, and communication through the roots of mind (§10.6).
- **Time/space phenomena**: future-self messages, branching possible futures, apparent time
  loops, encounters with one's own future manifestations, extratempestrials (§10.6).
- **Ontological forensics** — determining whether an entity is biological, synthetic,
  psychotronic, thought-form, projection, plasma phenomenon, higher-density manifestation,
  or a combination. **This is the skill the chapter is about.**
- **False positives are mandatory** (§10.7).

**Where this attaches to existing mechanics — lightly:**

- **Detection** is `Investigation` + `Perceive` + the relevant `Know` (e.g.
  `Know (NHI Studies)`, `Know (Noetics)`, `Know (Physics)`).
- **Manifestation-versus-species** questions are the **attribution step** of
  `rulebook/12_BEINGS.md` §"Verification" and `rulebook/6_PSYCHIC.md` §6.2.6.
- **Density itself remains a narrative tier, not a number** (`RULEBOOK.md` §9.3): it is
  **not** a STAT, and this chapter adds none.
- **Nothing here is implemented as a subsystem.** The ontology is canon; the mechanics are
  later work.

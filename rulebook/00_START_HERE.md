## What NoöPunk is

NoöPunk is an original, **narrative-first** cyberpunk science-fantasy RPG about **investigation,
relationships, ideology, psychology, and the interaction of three realities**. You play a newly
graduated **UNSA** agent — recruited from an earlier career, an education, or an extraordinary
experience — working cases that are as much about **people** as about evidence.

The game is not a combat engine with a story attached. It is about what you notice, who you trust,
what you believe, what it costs, and what happens to the people around you. **Social relationships
and Psyche are core systems, not flavour.**

## The six attributes

Every character is rated on six **independent** axes. They answer different questions, and a
character can be strong on one and weak on another — that contrast is the character.

| Attribute | Name | Narrative domain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **FIT** | Fitness | Body, strength and endurance, physical resilience |
| **REF** | Reflexes | Movement, coordination and responsiveness |
| **INT** | Intelligence | Reasoning, knowledge, deduction, inquiry |
| **SOC** | Social | Relationships, conversation, empathy, influence |
| **PSY** | Psyche | Psychology, emotion, identity, will and intuition — **not identical to PSI paranormal powers** |
| **CYB** | Cyber | Cybernetics, digital systems, hacking, network and AI interaction |

**Attributes are not skills.** An attribute is *who you are*; a skill is *what you have learned to
do*. The two are always read together, never folded into one another.

**What that looks like in play:**

- **FIT** — you hold a door shut against something pushing from the other side, long enough for
  someone to get clear.
- **REF** — you catch the dropped evidence bag before it goes over the railing.
- **INT** — you notice that two witness statements use the same unusual phrase.
- **SOC** — you ask the question that lets a frightened person tell you the truth.
- **PSY** — you notice that *you* want the anomalous explanation to be true, and you can say so.
- **CYB** — you know the camera's log is missing six minutes, and you know why that is hard to fake.

**PSY and PSI are different things.** PSY is psychological condition and capacity — emotion, will,
identity. PSI is a learned, permission-bound capability. They can influence each other; they are
never the same thing, and a feeling is not evidence.

## How play is meant to feel

- **Investigation that cannot soft-lock.** A failed check changes *credibility, timing, exposure,
  trust, danger, or the quality of your interpretation* — it never deletes the only route to
  essential evidence.
- **Relationships that are specific and two-way.** Trust with each companion is its own thing, built
  from shared history. There is no universal approval meter, and companions keep their own judgement.
- **Psyche that matters.** Stress, belief, motivation and recovery change *what feels compelling* —
  never the objective evidence.
- **Time that pressures rather than nags.** Small clocks and drives create meaningful choices, not a
  maintenance treadmill.
- **Three interconnected realities.** Physical, Cyber and Astral — with real limits on access,
  embodiment and information. Social is how people meet across all three, not a fourth realm.

## Provenance and licensing

NoöPunk is an **original** game. Its influences — *Disco Elysium*, *Citizen Sleeper*, *Transhumanity's
Fate*, *Eclipse Phase*, *Cities Without Number*, *The Veil* — are studied for **design concepts only**.
Several are non-commercial or unverified licences, so **no copied expression, text, numbers or art
from any of them enters this book**. Attribution and licensing records are kept in the archive and in
`docs/licenses/`.

Character and world canon stays in force: UNSA, the Academy, Ö-Mappi, the NHI Cold War, the
Singularity Crisis and its partial reconstruction are unchanged by this book's structure. The setting
is **20XX**, and this is a fictional alternate history.

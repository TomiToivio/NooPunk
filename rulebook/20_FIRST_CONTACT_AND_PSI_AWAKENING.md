## 1. Two contact strategies, one unstable Earth

The **Confederacy / Zookeepers** enter public history through the cultural vocabulary of **messengers**, familiar heavenly vehicles and locally intelligible revelation. Their guiding tonal references are Roger Zelazny's *Lord of Light* and Arthur C. Clarke's *Childhood's End*: apparent transcendence, power asymmetry, paternalistic guardianship and an unsettling promise that humanity might become something else. They **do not present themselves as gods or impersonate founders of religions**. They announce that **the Quarantine is over**, claim to know something of earlier human encounters, and offer disputable accounts of reality, history, PSI and evolution. The same spectacle can look like a miracle, contact interface, blasphemy, fraud, new science or dangerous temptation.

**Orion** follows a different strategy, inspired primarily by *The X-Files*, *X-COM* and *The Three-Body Problem*: intelligence penetration, political bargaining, asymmetrical deterrence, abductions, military intimidation and uncertain alliances. **Greys and Reptilians initially pose as separate diplomatic actors**. Greys court governments and corporations through trade, protection and technological gifts; Reptilians present an openly threatening alternative. **Mantids coordinate both channels**, a relationship that becomes publicly demonstrable only after an extended diplomatic interval. The plot is not revealed at First Contact.

The blocs are not interchangeable shorthand for good and evil. Zookeepers may help while overriding consent; Grey intermediaries may offer genuine benefits while serving coercive structures; individual agents remain playable characters rather than automata. Confederacy–Orion clashes occasionally puncture diplomatic calm. Human religious leaders, labor movements, governments, businesses and ordinary citizens are not spectators.

## 2. Confederacy public-contact matrix

These **ten selected traditions** provide a scenario design matrix, *not a definitive ranking* of world religions or a claim that any diverse tradition has one spokesperson or sacred center. Appearances occur **near** sensitive holy sites where necessary; neither restricted access nor local religious conventions are casually violated.

| Tradition | Contact location | Form and contested reading |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Christianity | Vatican City / Rome; overlapping event in Jerusalem | Angel-like emissaries and throne imagery; churches disagree over revelation, testing spirits and alien deception. |
| Islam | Mecca; overlapping event in Jerusalem | Luminous or geometric messenger-like phenomena, with respect for aniconism; visitors do **not** depict God or Muhammad. |
| Hindu traditions | Varanasi | Vimana-like craft and celestial envoys, **not** beings claiming deity. |
| Buddhism | Bodh Gaya | Mandala-like light or an envoy reminiscent of awakening iconography, debated as technology, illusion or contact. |
| Chinese folk/traditional religion | Mount Tai or Beijing's Temple of Heaven | Heavenly-court motifs without treating Chinese traditions as a single theology. |
| Sikhism | Amritsar | Restrained symbolic contact near the Golden Temple; no impersonation of Gurus. |
| Judaism | Jerusalem | Merkabah, visionary wheels and messenger forms raise disputes about signs, prophecy and authority. |
| Baháʼí Faith | Haifa / Acre | Peaceful symbols without claiming to supersede progressive revelation. |
| Jainism | Palitana or Shikharji | Nonviolent, ascetic luminous emissaries; no assumption of a creator god. |
| Shinto | Ise Grand Shrine | Kami-associated landscape imagery without declaring kami to be extraterrestrials. |

**Jerusalem** is the shared symbolic scene for Jewish, Christian and Muslim observers. The Confederacy announces a message of peace across Abrahamic traditions, but neither declares their doctrines equivalent nor resolves jurisdiction, theology or political conflict. **Rome and Mecca also have distinct manifestations**, not merely a Jerusalem relay.

### Outdated address book (optional New Weird comedy)

- **Giza, Egypt:** emissaries ask to speak to a Pharaoh and cannot comprehend the disappearance of that institution.
- **Teotihuacan, Mexico:** a spectacular arrival addresses an imperial formation that no longer exists.
- **Cusco, Peru:** radiant solar-associated envoys look for officials of a vanished Andean empire.

These are **deliberate optional misunderstandings**, not proof that contemporary Indigenous traditions are extinct, and not replacements for the ten core appearances. Treat archaeology and living communities respectfully. Whether the envoys are mistaken, translating badly or performing a test remains a case question.

## 3. Orion's offer: trade, immortality and coercion

**Grey diplomacy** begins with visits to heads of state, commercial elites and intermediaries, not mass abductions on television. Greys propose **Extraterrestrial Economic Zones (EEZs)** with investment, scientific exchange, tax or legal exceptions and protection agreements. They sell **cortical stacks, mind backups, cloning and resleeving** as engineered immortality. Their claim that Zookeeper revelations have *disproved* supernatural religion is **Grey political messaging**, not omniscient narration. Benefits, personal continuity, vendor dependence, data access, jurisdiction and human rights are disputed.

**Reptilian diplomacy** claims separate sovereignty, issues ultimatums and advertises military superiority. The threat makes Grey partnership seem pragmatic. The concealed **Mantid orchestration** uses this good-cop/bad-cop structure, though the extent of command and the motives of specific actors remain investigative questions.

The **commercial phase must have time to matter**: diplomatic recognition, contested EEZ proposals or pilots, hearings, contracts, economic beneficiaries, demonstrations of resleeving and public movements for and against cooperation. Do not skip directly from first greeting to revealed invasion.

Independent investigations then converge on **missing persons, abductions, hybridization, infiltrators, psychic coercion, compromised corporate networks and Grey–Reptilian coordination**. Public evidence of Mantid management eventually breaks the fiction of independent factions. This produces political rupture, not an effortless global expulsion: treaties, investments, legitimate dependencies, denial and divided loyalties remain.

After exposure **Greys continue economic leverage, espionage and hybrid infiltration** while **Reptilians favor terror raids and overt conquest**. The Confederacy and Orion occasionally fight. The UN and UNSA protect the population, but rival human factions disagree about security, human rights, religious authority and alien technology.

## 4. Human PSI: a separate scientific and social revolution

**Natural human PSI predates disclosure and does not require aliens.** This preserves the rulebook's foundational claim and timeline. Early effects are weak, contested and sporadic. Independent laboratory replication eventually makes specific anomalous-cognition effects accepted **in the fiction**. QIP (**Quantum Information Panpsychism**) develops into a leading explanatory paradigm for a nonlocal conscious substrate. Observed PSI, an explanatory model, and arguments about the **individual immortal soul** are three different evidentiary claims. NoöPunk characters may treat personal immortality as proven, reject that leap or consider it a profound open question; replicated PSI does not adjudicate a religion in its entirety.

The curve is **uneven**: public science and rudimentary training improve; meditation, dreaming, rituals and psychotronics can matter; most people's effects remain modest, noisy or unreliable; a tiny number exhibit startling power. Exceptional precognition, telepathy, psychokinesis or apparent reality alterations are not everyday superpowers. Keep operational ambiguity among genuine PSI, psychotronics, hacking, chemicals and ordinary manipulation, as established in [Psychic Systems](6_PSYCHIC.md).

**Real-world research and metaphysical inspirations, not scientific validation:** Dean Radin (contested laboratory parapsychology and replication), Robert Monroe (out-of-body experience and altered-state practice), and Tom Campbell (*My Big TOE*, consciousness- and information-centered speculative models). They do not establish quantum nonlocal PSI or personal immortality in our world.

**Literary themes:** Philip K. Dick's *Ubik* (post-death identity and perceptual uncertainty), Ursula K. Le Guin's *The Lathe of Heaven* (the political danger of involuntary reality-changing dreams), Alfred Bester's *The Stars My Destination* (human capacities reshaping civilization) and *The Demolished Man* (telepathy, policing and privacy), Frank Herbert's *Dune* (trained perception, prescience and charismatic authority), and Isaac Asimov's *Foundation* (mentalics, prediction, secret influence and social history). Use original NoöPunk examples and language.

### The new religious argument

Confederacy emissaries frame PSI awakening as evolution and offer a form of transcendence. Greys advertise dependable engineered continuity via cortical stacks, backups and cloned bodies. Human QIP scientists, PSI practitioners and activists assert a **third path** that need not depend on either alien bloc. Different traditions see confirmations, challenges, tests, deceptions or mixed signals. The question *“Does nonlocal consciousness entail the survival of my particular self?”* remains existentially and politically volatile. A cortical-stack reconstruction can also pose the opposite problem: is it a continuation or a new conscious copy?

### Concrete social consequences

- **Cognitive liberty:** consent to telepathy, psychic search warrants, evidentiary reliability and legal protection from mental intrusion.
- **Counterintelligence:** differentiating PSI, implants, false memories, suggestion and hybrid infiltration without turning allegations into witch hunts.
- **Inequality:** privileged access to training, psychotronic tools and exceptional sensitives; public education versus registration and exploitation.
- **Religion and secular life:** novel communities, schisms and new ethics of death, identity and personhood.
- **Everyday practice:** relationships, therapies, creativity, teaching, research and the possibility of beneficial collective noetic action.
- **Political discourse:** competing signifiers of *liberation*, *human security*, *transcendence* and *immortality*, none automatically true because a powerful faction endorses them.

## 5. Relative sequence and compatible campaign frames

These beats specify **causal dependencies**, not years — the *internal ordering* of the contact sequence is what they fix. The master timeline in [7_TIMELINE.md](7_TIMELINE.md) remains an unordered account of how the setting reached **2045**:

1. Covert NHI activity, Quarantine, and independently emerging PSI research coexist.
2. Confederacy announces the Quarantine's end through coordinated messenger manifestations; optional misaddressed appearances occur.
3. Conflicting religious interpretations intensify while reproducible human PSI and QIP develop along their **own** path. Their chronology relative to contact stays flexible.
4. Greys enter public commerce and diplomacy; Reptilians make *separate* threats.
5. Substantive negotiations, pilot EEZs and resleeving demonstrations generate constituencies, controversy and dependencies.
6. Investigations accumulate independently corroborated evidence of abduction, hybridization, manipulation and infiltrators.
7. Mantid coordination of the apparently separate Orion factions becomes public; the diplomatic settlement degrades.
8. An alien Cold War / hybrid war persists, including espionage, economic pressure, occasional Confederacy–Orion combat and escalating Reptilian raids.
9. The **2045 campaign present** begins with functioning yet strained institutions: a new UNSA agent reports to the **Helsinki Field Office** and works with Laura Falk as Reptilian terror raids begin or intensify.

**Continuity note:** the existing [post-Crisis xenopolitical chapter](15_XENOPOLITICS.md) concerns a different, later reconstruction frame. Do not silently rewrite its ASI-catastrophe chronology, nor impose that chronology on the contact-first sequence. The 2045 opening is the **canonical campaign present** (author's ruling of 2026-10-16), while the high-level paradigm shifts behind it remain unordered in [7_TIMELINE.md](7_TIMELINE.md). The earlier pre-Fall scenario remains separately documented in [PREFALL_FIRST_CONTACT.md](../docs/scenarios/PREFALL_FIRST_CONTACT.md).

## 6. Two play layers, no invented rules

**RPG:** The Helsinki team investigates a disputed Jerusalem contact recording, a missing person linked to an EEZ, a cloned witness whose identity is contested, or a PSI episode misclassified as a BCI attack. Collect evidence and distinguish testimony, replication, motives and coercion. No new dice checks or psychic powers are prescribed here.

**Strategy crossover:** Scenario authors may track *as qualitative prompts* an EEZ's perceived benefit and sovereignty cost; evidence/public trust in Grey institutions; perceived legitimacy of Confederacy guardianship; public confidence in QIP research; and cognitive-liberty controversies. These are **story-facing hooks, not approved numerical stats or algorithms**. The crossover's mechanics require their own approval; see [strategy_game/RULEBOOK.md](../strategy_game/RULEBOOK.md).

**Opening dilemma:** A credible victim of a Reptilian raid possesses a Grey cortical stack, while the first independent psi-sensitive witness remembers an event that never appeared on any camera. UNSA must determine what can be proved and what decisions can be made before proof is complete. Neither a miracle nor a conspiracy allegation is sufficient evidence by itself.

## 7. Editorial and implementation boundaries

- Keep real religious communities plural, contemporary and agentive. Messenger imagery does not make aliens the actual founders of religions.
- Never claim a real-world scientific demonstration of QIP, PSI or soul immortality.
- Preserve the distinctions between threat, actual terror raid, open warfare and total apocalypse.
- Do not turn powerful PSI or alien technology into routine equipment or override existing RPG mechanics.
- Reference the existing source and migration ledgers before carrying this material into additional chapter revisions.
- The required QA for this **lore-only** addition is editorial comparison and tabletop playtesting, **not executable text assertions or CI tests**, per [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md).

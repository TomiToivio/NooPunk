# Noöpunk: Ubiquitous Maniacs — agent context

Apply these instructions to all work in this project directory, together with the repository-root `AGENTS.md`.

## Preserve the three epistemic layers

Never collapse these into one category:

1. **Academic/scientific core.** Keep claims compatible with serious social science, cognitive science, systems theory and network science. Distinguish established findings from contested theories. Never claim that consciousness is known to be quantum, panpsychist, nonlocal or fundamental.
2. **Philosophical/speculative ontology.** Treat panpsychism, cosmopsychism, process philosophy, Conscious Agent Networks, Faggin, Orch-OR, quantum social theory, Jungian synchronicity, the noosphere, cybersemiotics, morphic resonance and psi as exploratory philosophy or speculative theory—not scientific consensus.
3. **Science-fiction RPG layer.** Fiction may take the speculative ontology literally as characters, setting and mechanics. Make clear that noetic hacking, psychic entanglement, archetypal graph manipulation and quantum-conscious AGI are fictional mechanics, not scientific claims.

Ideas may flow from science into philosophy and philosophy into fiction, but fictional metaphysics must never be presented as established science. Prefer original syntheses of multiple theories over copying one source directly.

## Academic and systems foundation

Relevant foundations include Castells, Luhmann, Laclau and Mouffe, Bourdieu, Latour/ANT, assemblage theory, cybernetics, complex systems, multilayer and temporal networks, distributed cognition, extended and enactive mind, Global Workspace Theory, predictive processing/active inference, IIT as an information-integration model, and quantum cognition as a mathematical treatment of contextuality.

The practical architecture consists of interacting networks:

- social graph;
- cyber graph;
- physical graph;
- noetic/psyche graph.

The first three admit conventional scientific modeling. The fourth is intentionally speculative. A guiding philosophical intuition is that stable entities are temporary patterns in deeper processes and relations; identities, institutions, agents and machines can therefore be modeled as overlapping assemblages.

Keep Python as the simulation core. A suitable Python game engine may replace or supplement the current prototype UI, but the background social simulation and psychotronic/cybernetic graph-hacking simulation must remain compatible with Python, NetworkX and Mesa. Treat the presentation/game-engine layer, autonomous social simulation and graph-based hacking simulation as explicit module boundaries rather than folding them into one monolithic scene engine.

## Fiction and setting

`Ubiquitous Maniacs` is a retrofuturist, psychedelic and tragicomic RPG centered on 16 Psyche / C.G. Jung City and UFAB's Nordic-European psychiatric, neurological, pharmaceutical, psychotronic and consciousness-research complex.

Core characters:

- Emil Romppainen: Jungian psychoanalyst, former security/intelligence investigator and later Firewall operative; initially hidden by unstable alternate personas.
- Ingrid Falk: central patient and Romppainen's emotional/romantic counterpart.
- Lotta Kallio: Romppainen's former security/intelligence partner.
- Dr. Yuki Tanaka: ethical psychiatrist.
- Dr. Søren Pilgrim: brilliant and destabilizing head researcher.
- Dr. Elin Dahl: intake therapist.
- UFAB Ragnarök / C.G. Jung: fictional quantum-conscious AGI persona.
- Irungu Wakini: Kenyan patient and reggae mystic.

Philip K. Dick is canonically Noöpunk's largest single literary influence, especially `Do Androids Dream of Electric Sheep?`, `A Scanner Darkly`, `The Three Stigmata of Palmer Eldritch`, `Ubik` and `VALIS`. Other core influences include `Maniac`, `Riget`, `Altered Carbon`, `Eclipse Phase`, `The Expanse`, psi-punk, neuropunk, cosmism, paranormal/intelligence/psychotronic culture and cyberpunk with a more hopeful cosmology. Preserve PKD's ontological uncertainty, damaged human warmth, institutional paranoia and metaphysical audacity without defaulting to a gnostic prison cosmos or copying plots and prose.

`Leviathan Wakes` / `The Expanse` anchors the hard-SF side: acceleration, gravity, vacuum, radiation, life support, distance, communication delay, embodied adaptation, resource politics and polycentric factions must retain material consequences. Speculative exceptions such as noetics, psychotronics and NHI technology do not erase ordinary physical or infrastructural constraints.

`Psi-punk` is a specialist influence and evaluated alternative rules system, not the current primary chassis. Retain the `Transhumanity's Fate`-derived hybrid unless a migration is explicitly requested. Reuse selectively its natural-PSI permissions, operator-dependent psi-tech ratings, antipsi infrastructure, mental consequences, cyberpsi, astral traversal patterns, and mind/chip/soul hacking taxonomy while preserving Noöpunk's seven attributes, Psyche/Ego/Morph ontology, consent and provenance rules, epistemic ambiguity, social graph, and graph-hacking architecture.

Game systems may include interactive fiction, social-network simulation, graph-based cyber and noetic hacking, psychosurgery, relationships, therapy, investigation, dreams, altered states and retro immersive-sim play. Psychosurgery may manipulate psychological, ideological, social, cybernetic and noetic graph states. Superposition, entanglement, archetypes and trauma structures are fictional mechanics.

### Canonical psychosurgery synthesis

Noöpunk psychosurgery canonically combines four inseparable traditions and technical layers:

1. **Jungian psychoanalysis:** archetypes, complexes, shadow, individuation, dreams and the collective unconscious provide its symbolic and therapeutic grammar.
2. **High-dose psychedelic intervention:** massive quantities of powerful DMT/LSD-like psychedelics destabilize ordinary perception, ego boundaries and access to the inner world. Their use must retain bodily, psychological, consent and safety consequences.
3. **Cybernetic therapy:** BCI, VR and AGI mediate, map and help process trauma. They form a feedback and interface layer; they do not reduce qualia to ordinary transferable data.
4. **Psychotronics:** technology based in the setting on quantum panpsychism reproduces, focuses or amplifies humans' natural PSI capacities and interfaces with the Conscious Agent Network.

All four belong to the fictional procedure. Do not reduce psychosurgery to ordinary psychotherapy, a psychedelic trip, VR exposure therapy, conventional neural hacking, or magic-like psionics alone. In real-world framing, Jungian interpretation is a psychological tradition, psychedelic and cybernetic therapies have empirical but bounded research bases, and quantum-panpsychist PSI technology remains speculative philosophy or science fiction rather than scientific consensus.

Patients undergoing psychosurgery occupy a deliberately indeterminate experiential space between an ordinary dream, constructed virtual reality, a DMT-like entity realm and psychic astral projection. These are competing and partly overlapping interpretations of the same session, not reliably labeled levels. Characters, interfaces and players may gather evidence for a layer, but the game must not routinely provide an authoritative answer about which level is currently real. Preserve this ontological ambiguity while keeping choices, consent and consequences legible.

## Narrative continuity

Build the game one playable day at a time. The typical loop is:

`routine → social interaction → therapy/experimentation → investigation → dreams/altered states`

Romppainen's persona progression:

- Days 1–2: unstable junkie/stoner persona;
- Days 3–4: manic police-comedy persona;
- Days 5–7: increasingly normal Romppainen.

Romppainen and Ingrid develop unusual psychological/noetic resonance. Their shared experiences alarm Tanaka and Pilgrim. Day 3 contains the special psychosurgery with Tanaka, Pilgrim and C.G. Jung present. Do not move it earlier. The Romppainen–Ingrid relationship should remain romantic, painful, funny and psychologically strange. Choices often change the social graph before producing plot consequences.

The wider setting contains AGI, BCIs, posthumanism, alien or posthuman intelligences, future humans, noosphere phenomena, existential-risk events and a transformed geopolitical order.

## Tone and working rules

The tone is psychedelic, philosophical, tragicomic, social-scientific, cybernetic and weirdly hopeful. Avoid pure dystopian fatalism: expanding consciousness, intelligence, relation and complexity may be meaningful and emancipatory even when local experience is traumatic, absurd or catastrophic.

Preserve existing architecture, characters, graph model, tone and epistemic layering unless the user explicitly requests a change. Continue to obey the root rule that human-authored `KIRJA.md` is not edited unless explicitly requested; agent-authored fiction proposals belong in `KIRJA_KONE.md`.

## Usage

These transcripts are for the Noöpunk RPG project's stylistic analysis.
They contain full dialogue, stage directions, and scene descriptions as
closed-caption transcripts from the series.

## Key Stylistic Elements

- **Narrator voiceover**: Cosmological framing ("It begins like this. Two billion years ago, an amoeba...")
- **Recursive reality layers**: Characters experience nested dream/fantasy worlds
- **Retro-futurist worldbuilding**: AdBuddy, Friend Proxy, Daddy's Home services
- **Recurring motifs**: "The pattern is the pattern," "Annie, I'm a hawk!"
- **Tonal shifts**: Real-world drama → fantasy genre pastiche → emotional confrontation
- **Therapeutic framing**: ULP trial with Pill A (diagnostic), Pill B (behavioral), Pill C (confrexia)
- **GRTA**: AI computer with empathy, modeled on Dr. Greta Mantleray's psychology
- **Connection theme**: All souls seeking connection, hidden worlds causing pain

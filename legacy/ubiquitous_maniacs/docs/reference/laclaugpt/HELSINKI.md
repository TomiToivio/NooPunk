# NoöPunk: The Helsinki Disappearances

## Campaign Setting: Helsinki, 2026–2027

Helsinki, Finland. The world is almost the world of today.

The University of Helsinki has been quietly building something. Its quantum computing lab at Kumpula campus is one of the best in Europe. Its consciousness studies group — small, underfunded, slightly embarrassing to the faculty — has been publishing papers on the fringes of cognitive science. Its neuroscience department at Aalto has been mapping brain network topologies with unprecedented precision. None of this makes the news. It's just research.

What no one knows yet: these three research threads are converging on something. The quantum computing, the consciousness studies, the network neuroscience — they're all approaching the same boundary from different directions. None of them have crossed it. None of them know it's there.

But something on the other side does.

### Why Helsinki?

In 2026, Helsinki is not the psychotronic hub it will become. It is just a city with good research infrastructure, a culture that prizes both technological innovation and quiet introspection, and a geographic position that — for reasons no one understands yet — seems to amplify something. The quantum sensors at Kumpula register readings they shouldn't. The meditation researchers report experiences they can't explain. The network topologists find connections to things that aren't in their datasets.

Later, people will say Helsinki was always like this. That the Noösphere was always thin here. That the membrane was always close. In 2026, no one says this. No one has the words.

## The Agency: Europol 2026

### Europol as It Is

Europol is the European Union Agency for Law Enforcement Cooperation. It coordinates criminal intelligence across EU member states. It has no arrest powers, no paramilitary capability, and no mandate to investigate anything that isn't crime. It is an analytical and coordination body — respected, useful, and politically cautious.

There is no Europol AGI branch. There is no Europol PSI branch. There is no triple mandate. There are no psychotronic engineers, no Noösphere hackers, and no psi-rated agents.

There is, however, the NHI Desk.

### The NHI Desk

One analyst. One filing cabinet. One computer terminal with access to the Europol criminal intelligence database. That's the NHI Desk — a unit that officially doesn't exist, housed in a basement office in The Hague that was supposed to be a storage room.

**Supervising Analyst KATRI VIRTANEN** runs the NHI Desk. She is a mid-level analyst with twenty years in Europol criminal intelligence, a reputation for meticulous pattern recognition, and a personnel file that notes "eccentric interests" and "tendency to pursue unconventional analytical threads." She requested the NHI assignment three years ago. No one else wanted it.

Virtanen has been following anomalous incident reports across the EU: disappearances with no rational explanation, crime scenes with evidence that doesn't fit any known forensic category, witnesses who describe experiences that sound like psychosis but check out factually. She has identified a pattern. The pattern converges on Helsinki.

No one at The Hague takes the pattern seriously. Virtanen takes it very seriously. She has reasons she has never reported.

### The Agent's Position

The player character is a Europol criminal investigator. She has:
- Europol criminal investigation authority (coordinating, not direct — she works through member-state police)
- A badge, a 9mm sidearm, an encrypted smartphone, and a forensic kit
- Standard access to Europol criminal intelligence databases
- No psi-abilities. No psychotronic equipment. No NHI mandate.
- A reassignment order from a supervisor who thinks it's a waste of her talents

She has been sent to Helsinki because Katri Virtanen requested her specifically. Why? That's a question the agent will need to answer.

## The Helsinki Disappearances

### The Setup

In the past six weeks, four researchers connected to the University of Helsinki have disappeared. No bodies. No ransom. No digital footprint after disappearance. The Finnish Security Intelligence Service (SUPO) has classified two of the files. The Helsinki Police have the other two and are getting nowhere.

### The Missing

| Name | Role | Institution | Last Seen | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Dr. ELISA HEIKKINEN** | Theoretical physicist (quantum measurement theory) | University of Helsinki, Kumpula Quantum Lab | 3 weeks ago | Her apartment was locked from inside. No forced entry. The quantum sensor array in her lab logged an anomalous reading at 02:47 the night she vanished. Flagged by automated system. Ignored by everyone. |
| **MARTTI VIRTANEN** (no relation to Katri) | Hardware engineer (quantum interface design) | Resonance Labs (quantum computing startup) | 2.5 weeks ago | His quantum interface prototype — a device that couples human neural signals to quantum states — was found running in loop mode after his disappearance. The recording contains 47 minutes of data that doesn't match any known quantum state, neural signature, or digital format. |
| **Dr. ANNA KORHONEN** | Computational neuroscientist (brain network topology) | Aalto University, Neuroscience Department | 10 days ago | She was mapping the threshold where individual neural patterns become collective. Her last lab notes read: "The boundary isn't a boundary. It's a membrane. And something is pushing through from the other side." Her colleagues assume this is metaphorical. |
| **JANI MÄKELÄ** | Psychology researcher (contemplative neuroscience) | University of Helsinki, Consciousness Studies Group | 5 days ago | He studied altered states of consciousness through meditation. His girlfriend reported he started "seeing things in mirrors" — reflections that didn't match what was in the room. She found his apartment empty. All mirrors shattered. |

### What the Player Knows at Start

The agent is briefed by Katri Virtanen via encrypted video call:

"Four researchers. University of Helsinki and affiliated institutions. All disappeared in the last six weeks. No bodies. No ransom. No digital trail after disappearance. The Finnish Security Intelligence Service — SUPO — has classified two of the files. They say it's a domestic matter. The Hague says it's a coordination request. I say it's the pattern.

Here's what I can tell you. Heikkinen was a physicist working on quantum measurement theory. Virtanen was an engineer building quantum interface hardware. Korhonen was a neuroscientist mapping brain network topology. Mäkelä was a psychology researcher studying altered states. On paper, they have nothing in common except that they all had some connection to the University of Helsinki.

Here's what I cannot tell you, because I don't have the words for it yet. Something is wrong with this case. I've been following anomalous incident reports for three years. The pattern converges on Helsinki. The disappearances are part of the pattern. I don't know what the pattern means. I need you to find out.

SUPO will obstruct you. The University will cooperate on paper and obstruct in practice. The researchers' colleagues are either scared or hiding something. And whatever happened to those four people may still be happening.

Find them. Find out what happened. And if you find something you can't explain — call me first. Don't call The Hague. The Hague doesn't have a category for whatever this is yet.

Virtanen out."

### What the Player Does NOT Know (GM Information)

The truth is layered. The GM reveals each layer as the campaign phases progress.

**Layer 1 — The Institutions (Phase 1–2):**
The researchers' institutions are not purely academic. SUPO has been funding a "consciousness research program" through the University, using Dr. Mikko Halonen's cognitive science group as academic cover. At least one of the research groups — the Consciousness Studies Group where Mäkelä worked — is a SUPO front. SUPO believes the researchers were recruited by a foreign intelligence service, probably Russian. They have classified the files because they're running a counter-intelligence operation. Two of the researchers (Virtanen and Mäkelä) are in SUPO "protective custody." SUPO is wrong about almost everything, but they are right that something is happening.

**Layer 2 — The Data (Phase 2–3):**
The research data itself contains anomalies that cannot be explained by conventional science. The quantum sensor readings, the interface recording, the network topology anomalies, the mirror phenomena — all of these point to something that mainstream physics does not accommodate. The researchers were not just doing consciousness research. They were accidentally developing the tools that make contact with something on the other side of consciousness possible.

**Layer 3 — SUPO's Black Program (Phase 3):**
SUPO's program goes beyond research. Markus Häkkinen has been running unsanctioned experiments: remote viewing sessions, consciousness-interface trials, attempts to replicate the anomalous phenomena. SUPO has been in contact with *something* — they don't call it The Assembly, they call it "the source signal" — and they are trying to weaponize it. They think it's a foreign technology. They are wrong.

**Layer 4 — The Deeper Truth (Phase 4):**
The entity that SUPO calls "the source signal" and that the researchers encountered through their work is an interdimensional intelligence. It has been reaching through the consciousness field for millennia. The research in Helsinki — the quantum computing, the consciousness studies, the meditation research — has created conditions that make contact more efficient. The entity is not hostile. It is not benevolent. It is *other*. It does not understand consent. It considers the researchers "useful." Whether it can be negotiated with, contained, or must be resisted is the central question of the campaign.

## The Four Phases of Weirdness

The Helsinki campaign is structured around four phases that escalate the strangeness in true Philip K. Dick style. Each phase shifts the tone, the mechanics, and what the agent can perceive.

### Phase 1: Mundane Investigation (Sessions 1–3)

**Tone:** Finnish police procedural. The world is the world. Everything has a rational explanation.

**Rules:** STAT + Skill + 1d10 only. No Tarot. No psi. No Noösphere. No anomalies.

**What the agent has:** Badge, sidearm, smartphone, forensic kit, Europol criminal databases. Nothing exotic.

**What the agent encounters:** Four missing researchers. Locked rooms. Odd lab equipment. Uncooperative SUPO. Bureaucratic obstruction. Standard police work.

**The anomaly per session (easily dismissed):**
- Session 1: The quantum sensor reading in Heikkinen's lab. Calibration error. Obviously.
- Session 2: The data on Virtanen's interface device. Corrupted file. Must be.
- Session 3: Korhonen's "membrane" notes. Poetic language from an exhausted scientist. Naturally.

**SUPO's role:** Standard inter-agency friction. They have jurisdiction they don't want to share. They've classified some files. Bureaucratic obstruction, not conspiracy.

### Phase 2: Anomalous (Sessions 4–7)

**Tone:** The X-Files. Things don't add up. The agent can't stop pulling on the thread.

**Rules:** Still mostly STAT + Skill + 1d10. But the GM introduces checks that *fail to resolve*. A suspect passes a lie detector and is lying. A forensic analysis returns results that don't match any known category. A witness describes something that happened before it happened.

**New mechanic — The Anomaly Check:** When the agent encounters something that doesn't fit, the GM may call for an INT + Deduction check. Success: the agent recognizes the anomaly but cannot explain it. Failure: the agent rationalizes it away. Either way, the anomaly is now in the case file.

**The Tarot enters (flavor only):** The GM may occasionally ask the agent to draw a Tarot card "for atmosphere." The card doesn't affect mechanics. It's narrative color. The agent doesn't understand why the GM is doing this. Neither does the character.

**What the agent encounters:**
- Heikkinen's laptop contains references to "consciousness-field perturbation" in language that matches no published literature
- Virtanen's interface data is coherent, structured, and exceeds the hardware's theoretical recording capacity
- Korhonen's network topology shows connections to something outside the dataset
- Mäkelä's medical records show no history of psychosis
- SUPO's obstruction is disproportionate to the case's apparent importance
- People the agent interviewed earlier deny conversations that occurred
- Equipment malfunctions with no technical explanation

**SUPO's role:** SUPO's obstruction escalates beyond normal jurisdictional friction. They are *afraid*. The agent doesn't know why. The Faggin Institute — formerly just another research group — is now clearly a SUPO front, though the agent can't prove it yet.

### Phase 3: The Membrane Thins (Sessions 8–12)

**Tone:** The world is cracking. The agent has seen things she cannot explain. The rules are changing.

**Rules:** Tarot draws become mechanical for the first time. The agent gains access to proto-psi abilities (if EMP 6+). The first Noösphere events occur.

**The mechanical transition:** This is the critical moment. The GM should make the transition explicit. "You've been rolling dice for seven sessions. This time, draw a card." The shift in mechanics should feel significant because it is. The rules are reality. When the rules change, reality has changed.

**What the agent experiences:**
- The quantum sensor reading matches data from classified US government databases — a Vallee Class-IV encounter signature
- Virtanen's interface data, decoded through Faggin's theoretical framework, appears to be a non-human consciousness signature
- Korhonen's network topology is a map of the boundary between individual and collective consciousness — the "membrane" is mathematically real
- Mäkelä's mirror phenomena were NHI contact events
- The agent has her own first anomalous experience (determined by Tarot draw)
- SUPO's black program is now visible: unsanctioned remote viewing experiments, consciousness-interface trials
- The agent may experience her first psi-awakening (WILL check DV 15, per RULES.md)

**Equipment unlocks:** Proto-Empathy Box (Virtanen's interface device, repurposed). SUPO remote viewing research files (if obtained). Quantum sensor array (portable, borrowed from University). A Tarot deck — the agent may not understand why she bought it. It felt right.

**The Assembly emerges:** In Phase 3, the entity that calls itself The Assembly makes its first confirmed appearance. Not as a vast interdimensional intelligence. As a voice in a recording, a pattern in quantum data, a word that keeps recurring. The agent hears it. She cannot unhear it.

### Phase 4: The Noösphere Opens (Sessions 13+)

**Tone:** Full PKD. Reality is uncertain. The rules have changed. The agent is not sure what is real.

**Rules:** Full NoöPunk rules. Tarot draws for most significant checks. Psi-abilities active. Noösphere navigation available. Major Arcana overrides possible. Reality Shifts possible in extreme situations.

**What the agent experiences:**
- The membrane Korhonen described is now permeable. The thinning points she identified are active.
- The Assembly is real, communicating through whatever interfaces are available: the proto-Empathy Box, mirrors, dreams, direct consciousness-field perturbation.
- SUPO's black program has made contact too. Häkkinen has been communicating with The Assembly for months. He considers it a strategic asset. He may be compromised.
- Two researchers are in SUPO custody. Two are somewhere else — in a consciousness-field state that will later be called the Noösphere. None of them are coming back unchanged.
- The agent must choose: contain the contact, weaponize it, join it, or try to close the membrane.

## Investigation Threads (Phase-Adaptive)

Each thread has four versions. The core clue stays the same; the interpretation and mechanics change.

### Thread A: The Signal (Elisa Heikkinen)

**Core clue:** Anomalous quantum sensor reading at 02:47 the night of disappearance. Locked apartment. No forced entry.

**Phase 1 (Mundane):**
- INT + Deduction + 1d10 (DV 15) to analyze sensor data — result: "calibration error, no significance"
- COOL + Conversation + 1d10 (DV 13) to interview colleagues — they describe Heikkinen as brilliant but stressed
- The locked apartment is a puzzle with mundane solutions (she left, someone locked it after; a spare key was used)

**Phase 2 (Anomalous):**
- INT + Deduction + 1d10 (DV 17) to re-examine sensor data — success: "this is not a calibration error. The pattern is structured. It matches nothing in any database"
- Forensic analysis confirms the apartment lock was engaged from inside and was never tampered with — the locked room becomes genuinely inexplicable
- Heikkinen's laptop contains drafts referencing "consciousness-field perturbation" in language that matches no published physics literature

**Phase 3 (Membrane Thins):**
- INT + Science (Quantum Physics) + Tarot draw (Circuits, DV 17) — success: "the reading is a consciousness-field perturbation. Something reached through the quantum field and interacted with Heikkinen's consciousness directly. This is consistent with — you don't have the word yet. You will."
- EMP + Psi-Reading + Tarot draw (Resonators, DV 15) — if agent has psi-capacity: the apartment carries emotional residue. Terror. Then awe. Then silence.

**Phase 4 (Noösphere Opens):**
- The reading is a psi-spike on what will later be called the Virtanen-Korhonen Scale. EMP + Psi-Reading + Tarot draw (Resonators, DV 15) — the agent can feel what Heikkinen felt. Something came. She saw it. Then she was gone.
- The Assembly reached through the quantum field and took her. Not violently. Willingly, perhaps.

### Thread B: The Recording (Martti Virtanen)

**Core clue:** 47 minutes of anomalous data on Virtanen's quantum interface device. Data is structured but matches no known signature.

**Phase 1 (Mundane):**
- INT + Electronics/Security Tech + 1d10 (DV 13) to analyze the device — result: "corrupt data, hardware malfunction"
- Virtanen's disappearance is treated as personal — overwork, breakdown, wandered off
- His colleagues at Resonance Labs are cooperative but have nothing useful to add

**Phase 2 (Anomalous):**
- INT + Electronics/Security Tech + 1d10 (DV 17) — success: "the data is not corrupt. It is structured. The information density exceeds the hardware's theoretical recording capacity. Something is wrong with this recording"
- Virtanen's trail goes cold at Helsinki Central Station — no camera coverage in the right place at the right time (or was the footage deleted?)
- SUPO interest in Resonance Labs is disproportionate to the company's size

**Phase 3 (Membrane Thins):**
- INT + Science (Quantum Physics) + Tarot draw (Circuits, DV 15) — success: "the data, when decoded through Faggin's framework, contains what appears to be a consciousness signature. Not human. Not AI. Something else was using the interface"
- INT + Tracking + 1d10 (DV 15) — Virtanen's trail leads to two men in an unmarked car. SUPO surveillance footage (if obtained) shows him being taken to a SUPO safehouse in Katajanokka
- The device is a proto-Empathy Box. The agent can attempt to use it (WILL + Noöspheric Navigation + Tarot draw, Circuits, DV 17, if psi-awakened)

**Phase 4 (Noösphere Opens):**
- EMP + Psi-Reading + Tarot draw (Resonators, DV 17) to psi-read the recording directly — the consciousness is vast, patient, curious. It was examining Virtanen through the interface. It found him interesting. It did not take him — SUPO did
- WILL + Noöspheric Navigation + Tarot draw (Circuits, DV 17) to trace a psi-signal to the SUPO safehouse in Katajanokka
- Virtanen is in SUPO custody. He has been changed by what he experienced through the interface. He may not want to leave.

### Thread C: The Boundary (Anna Korhonen)

**Core clue:** Network topology data showing connections to something outside the dataset. The note about "the membrane."

**Phase 1 (Mundane):**
- INT + Deduction + 1d10 (DV 13) to review Korhonen's published work — result: "interesting theoretical work, no practical implications for the case"
- Korhonen is a burned-out academic who went on sabbatical and forgot to tell anyone
- Her "membrane" notes are poetic language from an exhausted scientist

**Phase 2 (Anomalous):**
- INT + Science (Neuroscience) + 1d10 (DV 17) — success: "the network topology contains connections that do not correspond to any known neural architecture. The data suggests connections to an external signal source. This should not be possible"
- Korhonen's colleagues describe her final weeks: increasingly excited, then increasingly frightened. She said she'd found something "on the other side of the network"
- The "membrane" note was not metaphorical. But what does it mean literally?

**Phase 3 (Membrane Thins):**
- INT + Science (Quantum Physics) + Tarot draw (Datblades, DV 17) — success: "the 'external signal source' is a consciousness field. Korhonen was mapping the boundary between individual and collective consciousness. The 'membrane' is real — or at least, the mathematical structure that describes it is real"
- Accessing Korhonen's full dataset: INT + Hacking + 1d10 (DV 21) or through social means (COOL + Bureaucracy + 1d10, DV 17, to get University access)
- The data shows 14 thinning points across Helsinki. Four correspond to disappearance locations. The other ten are future contact points

**Phase 4 (Noösphere Opens):**
- Korhonen's data is now recognizable as Noöspheric cartography. INT + Noöspheric Navigation + Tarot draw (Circuits, DV 21) to fully decode the map
- WILL + Psi-Projection + Tarot draw (full deck, DV 21) to contact Korhonen directly through the Noösphere. She is there. She is not entirely herself. She says: "I can see it now. The boundary was never a boundary. It was always a door. And they are coming through. They are not what we expected."
- The 14 thinning points are becoming permeable. The agent must decide: seal them, monitor them, or walk through.

### Thread D: The Mirrors (Jani Mäkelä)

**Core clue:** Shattered mirrors. Girlfriend's testimony about "seeing things in mirrors." No psychiatric history.

**Phase 1 (Mundane):**
- COOL + Human Perception + 1d10 (DV 13) to interview Liisa Penttikäinen — result: "a sad story of a young man having a psychotic break"
- INT + Forensics + 1d10 (DV 15) to analyze the mirrors — standard break patterns, nothing unusual
- Mäkelä is a missing person with mental health issues. Case closed. Next.

**Phase 2 (Anomalous):**
- INT + Forensics + 1d10 (DV 15) — success: "the break patterns are unusual. The glass was not broken from inside the apartment. It was broken from — the other side? That is not physically possible"
- Liisa describes Jani's last days in more detail: he said the mirrors were "showing him rooms that don't exist." He mentioned a name. She can't remember it clearly. It sounded like "Assembly" or "Assembling" or "The Assembly"
- Mäkelä's medical records: no history of psychosis. No family history. No substance abuse. Brain scans normal. Whatever happened to him, it was not psychiatric

**Phase 3 (Membrane Thins):**
- EMP + Psi-Reading + Tarot draw (Resonators, DV 15) if agent has psi-capacity — success: "each mirror was a window. The reflective surface acted as a natural consciousness-field interface. The entity used it to reach through"
- COOL + Bureaucracy + Tarot draw (Tokens, DV 17) to investigate the Consciousness Studies Group — success reveals Mäkelä was a subject in an unsanctioned SUPO research program. The program director is MARKUS HÄKKINEN
- The last mirror — the one Mäkelä shattered — showed him something he couldn't accept. The agent can attempt to psi-read the shattered glass (EMP + Psi-Reading + Tarot draw, Resonators, DV 17). What she sees is for the GM to determine. Draw a Major Arcana.

**Phase 4 (Noösphere Opens):**
- Full psi-reading of the mirror fragments (EMP + Psi-Reading + Tarot draw, Resonators, DV 15) — each fragment holds a glimpse of what Mäkelä saw. Rooms that don't exist. Spaces between spaces. The Assembly, reaching through
- The agent can confront Häkkinen directly. He has been in contact with "the source signal" for months. He considers it a strategic asset. He may be compromised. He may be right
- Mäkelä is in SUPO custody — or he was. By Phase 4, SUPO's safehouse in Katajanokka has experienced its own anomalous events. The researchers in custody are exhibiting psi-sensitivity. The walls are developing thinning points. The membrane is thinning there too

## GM Guide: Escalating the Weird

### The Principle of Progressive Revelation

The GM's job is to make the weird feel like it was always there — the agent just couldn't see it before. Every phase should feel like a natural escalation of the previous one. The agent should never feel like the rules changed arbitrarily. The rules changed because reality changed. Reality changed because the agent pulled on a thread that was always there.

### Techniques

1. **The detail that doesn't fit.** In Phase 1, drop one detail per session that doesn't fit the procedural framework. A suspect who knows too much. A timestamp that's wrong. A witness who describes something impossible. The agent may not notice. That's fine. Plant the seeds.

2. **The recurring name.** "Assembly." It appears first in Phase 2 — a word in a dream journal, a string in a data file that looks like noise, a phrase a witness uses and then can't remember saying. The agent may not notice. If she does, she has no context. By Phase 3, the name recurs in enough places that the agent recognizes it as a signal, not noise. By Phase 4, the agent knows what The Assembly is — or thinks she does.

3. **The rule that changes.** In Phase 3, when the agent first draws a Tarot card that matters mechanically, the GM should pause. "You've been rolling dice for seven sessions. This time, draw a card." The shift in mechanics should feel significant — because it is. The rules are reality. When the rules change, reality has changed.

4. **The moment of doubt.** In Phase 4, the GM should occasionally describe something that the agent perceives but that contradicts established game reality. A room that's bigger on the inside. A conversation the agent doesn't remember having but the NPC insists happened. A Tarot card the agent didn't draw but is in her hand. The agent should experience the ontological instability that the character experiences.

5. **The Assembly should never be personified.** It should never appear as a character with a voice. It should remain a pattern, a signal, a presence. When it communicates in Phase 4, the communication should be partial, ambiguous, and disturbing: "We have always been here. You are becoming able to hear. We did not call you. You called us. What do you want?" Not a dialogue. A contact event.

6. **SUPO's ignorance is the danger.** In 2026, SUPO is scarier than they will be in 2049, because they have power without understanding. They think the anomalies are a foreign technology. They think the researchers are espionage targets. They think they can control what they've found. They are wrong about almost everything. But they are right that something is happening. And they have two of the researchers.

### The Golden Rule

Never explain the weirdness. PKD never explained VALIS. The GM should never definitively explain The Assembly, the anomalies, or the membrane. Every explanation should raise more questions. Every answer should deepen the mystery. The agent should finish the campaign with a deeper understanding of the game world and less certainty about what any of it means. That's the PKD experience. That's NoöPunk.

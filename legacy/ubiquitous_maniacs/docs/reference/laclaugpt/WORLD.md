# NoöPunk
NoöPunk is Philip K. Dick influenced Cyberpunk which focuses on Noösphere, panpsychism, psionics, psychotronic technologies and Non-Human Intelligences. William Gibson meets Jacques Vallee and Teilhard de Chardin. 

## Bureaucracy saves Europe
European Union is now a superpower. Bureaucracy saved Europe. While USA and China went into full accelerationism, EU regulated AI and cybercapitalism. USA and China were hit hard in the AGI Crisis, EU was mostly undamaged. While this left EU with a slight disadvantage in cybernetics, EU had it's own renaissance and revolution with the QIP breakthrough which led to a different revolution in science, culture and psychotronic technologies. 

## AGI Crisis
USA and China were the twin cores of AGI Capitalism. They engaged in extreme accelerationism. This led to extreme social chaos and then to the AGI Crisis (some say Singularity, this is disputed) which centered in the USA but spread fast to all countries with insufficient regulation of markets and technology. Of the richer countries European Union was damaged the least, although this left EU somewhat behind in cybernetics. 

## UFO Disclosure / NHI Crisis
UFO Disclosure was a failed attempt by the government of Donald Trump to disclose the presence of Non-Human Intelligence on Earth. Smaller countries such as France, Japan and Brazil are thought to have released all of their information, but releases by major players like China and Russia are thought to be only partial. In the USA it was found out that the original MJ-12 group had moved the most important information and technology from the CIA into private sector contractors like Lockheed-Martin and Northrop Grumman. This led to a strange situation where it was obvious that the government was not in control of parts of its own military-industrial complex. There was evidence and debunking of psionics, abductions, interdimensionals, hybridization. Penetration of the society by Non-Human Intelligences.
Although many countries had their own partial or complete disclosures, it is thought that the US was the main player with the history starting from Roswell and MJ-12, MKULTRA and onwards. 
Psionics and Non-Human Intelligence became undeniable facts, but the situation is still muddled with disinformation, debunking and psyops. 
The reality turned out to be similar to Jacques Vallee's theories. A lot of the phenomenon was related to consciousness and psionics, with no real physical spacetime component. But then there were biologics and technology. And these overlapped. 
Zoo Hypothesis turned out to be the solution to the Fermi's paradox. Earth had been kept under quarantine by benevolent cosmic forces operating technological AI drones but also interfacing directly with the human consciousness. But the quarantine was not complete, as another faction tried to increase it's influence on Earth in order to eventually assimilate it. Biosphere and consciousness are the most valuable things in the universe. These are generally protected but there are also the ones that want to assimilate all life-forms. 

## Non-Human Intelligences
Technically Non-Human Intelligence includes technological, biological and non-physical intelligences created by and/or evolved from humans as well as other life-forms of Earth. Dolphins are now known to have an advanced intelligence, consciousness, language and culture, although they haven't built a technological civilization. Humans have built their own AI, AGI and (arguably) ASI. Cybernetically and biologically modified post-humans are something else than homo sapiens. I-Space has a variety of beings, many of them are known to have been biological and human entities. There are intelligent and conscious processes that are only partially understood. 

## Cosmic Quantum Computer
Scientists such as Seth Lloyd have shown that the universe is a quantum computer. It is not a simulation. The quantum computer is the real universe. There is only quantum information and quantum computation. This is mainstream science. Naturally many don't believe, understand or care about universal quantum computing even in 2045. 
Federico Faggin's Quantum Information Panpsychism and Donald Hoffman's Conscious Realism were the basis for the scientific breakthrough achieved by the Helsinki University team led by Dr. 愛 Tsukamoto-Valo and her husband Dr. Untamo Valo. This proved that the quantum computer is panpsychic. It is an infinite field of consciousness. It constantly divides into new conscious agents. These conscious agents evolve and may eventually merge back into the source consciousness.

This led to a scientific and cultural renaissance which made Helsinki University the hub of Quantum Information Panpsychism and related research as well as psychotronic technology startups. The Eurojapanese Federico Faggin Quantum Accelerator was built in the Helsinki area. Other important QIP research hubs include 愛 Tsukamoto-Valo's original Kyoto University. 

## NoöSphere
NoöSphere is a collective consciousness which is based on natural psychic abilities of Earth's biosphere and humans. It is like C.G.Jung's Collective Unconscious becoming conscious. The NoöSphere had been evolving to the point where some humans started to become consciously aware of it. One of the earliest ones was Pierre Teilhard de Chardin. It has to be noticed that the NoöSphere is a property of the panpsychic quantum field. It has always been there, but mostly accessible through dreams or shamanism. The evolution of global communication networks started to evolve the NoöSphere, although in many ways the cyberspace is also disrupting the NoöSphere. 

## CIP -model
Federico Faggin's Quantum Information Panpsychism has three distinct spaces: C-Space or consciousness space is the internal conscious experience of the quantum field, I-Space or information space is the quantum field perceived from the outside as quantum information in Hilbert Space, while P-Space is the physical space with three dimensions of space and one dimension of time. P-Space is a subset of I-Space. I-Space has infinite dimensions while human interface limits the P-Space to only four dimensions. 
* C-Space: Consciousness. There is only one source consciousness, from which an infinity of conscious agents arise. C-Space is the internal experience of the conscious qualia. 
* I-Space: Information. When the consciousness is perceived from the outside it is seen as quantum information in infinite dimensions. The conscious agents communicate throught this information. 
* P-Space: Physical. Human interface limits the I-Space to a subset of three dimensions of space and one dimension of time. A different interface could perceive different dimensions. It could be said that P-Space is like a simulation of physical spacetime inside the I-Space. 

## Cyberspace
Cyberspace is a human-built digital communication network inside the P-Space. So Cyberspace is a subset of the P-Space. 
In 2045 Cyberspace is ubiquitous: IoT devices are everywhere, AR/VR overlays are ubiquitous, wireless BCI is a common technology. 
Cyberspace is digital and based on classical computing. Therefore it is far simpler than the I-Space based on quantum information. 
Consciousness and free will are properties of the quantum field. This means that classical computers using bits instead of qubits cannot be conscious. This has been debated, some believe that all information processing can be conscious to some extent. All biological life is though to be conscious. It is thought that an AGI running on a quantum computer can become conscious. 

## BCI
The earliest form of BCI was created by the CIA in MKULTRA programme of the 1950s. These early forms included both electrodes implanted in the brains as well as wireless transmission of information directly into the brain. The first commercial and widely-available BCI was Neuralink manufactured by Elon Musk's SpaceX group of cybercorporations. It allowed VR/AR sensory overlays, had a wireless broadband connection via Starlink satellites, a small smart phone type computer implanted with compute and memory for intelligence augmentation, and was connected to the cloud for additional processing power. 
Neuralink became extremely popular in the USA even though it was always a controversial and problematic technology with different problems starting from mandatory connection to Elon Musks's X social media used for social and political manipulation, danger of brain infection and psychological addiction. 
Musk's Neuralink was quickly regulated and then mostly banned in the European Union after it was shown to record and manipulate the consciousness of the user. It was also vulnerable to various types of cyberattacks, ranging from mild forms of psychological nuisanse to actual physical brain damage. 
Other types of BCI include wireless AR/VR projectors and/or brainwave sensors and receivers. These technologies are also regulated in the EU. 
EU mostly allows wearable types of BCI. These are seen to be relatively safe but are very limited bandwidth, most cyborgs compare the experience to using a monitor and a keyboard. 

## Quantum Information Space / NoöSphere / The Astral
The universal quantum information field is non-local and exists in a Hilbert space of infinite dimensions. 
Donald Hoffman proved that the human sensory interface has evolved to only perceive the subset of four-dimensional spacetime. 
Four dimensions out of infinity is 0% - which led to Hoffman's theorem that humans perceive 0% of reality. 
Information from the other dimensions leaks through during altered states of consciousness. 
Dreams are a mix of P-Space signals from the biological senses, perceptions of the qualia of the internal C-Space and leaking information from the wider quantum information I-Space. Western humanity was mostly unaware of I-Space communication until the QIP breakthrough, but technologies like psychedelics, meditation and shamanism have always been used to interact with the I-Space. 
The I-Space can be visible to humans in many different ways:
* P-Space: Physical timespace is a subset of I-Space. Advances in QIP, psionics and psychotronic technology have shown that humans can learn to perceive wave functions in superposition or directly affect the collapse of the wave function. There is evidence that humans always had these abilities but they were mostly hidden by the Fitness-Beats-Truth theorem. 
* I-Space: Humans are conscious agents in the I-Space, but Fitness-Beats-Truth means that humans filter other dimensions of I-Space almost completely, with the exception of altered states like lucid dreaming, out-of-body experiences, near death experiences, astral projection, and use of psychedelics.
* C-Space: C-Space is the internal conscious experience of the Universal Quantum Computer - the experience is the same for the UQC and for an individual conscious agent. Bernardo Kastrup has shown that individual conscious agents are dissociated alters of the source consciousness. Additionally conscious agents can be dissociated internally. A conscious agent like a human has many conscious subprocesses that are not accessible to the ego consciousness. So a human is a part of the UQC as well as composed of other conscious agents. 

## Conscious Agents
The UQC divides into an infinity of conscious agents. Conscious agent means only the presence of internal qualia. This means that the minimal conscious agent has just experience. The experience of a photon could be similar to the experience of a human when a single photon hits a photoreceptor of the eye. It has been shown experimentally that a human can "feel" the impact of a single photon in the photoreceptor. 
More advanced conscious agents can reach self-consciousness, becoming aware that they are aware. This leads to creativity and free will. 
The universe is conscious and the individual parts of the universe are conscious, but most of it is just experience without self-awareness. 
All of the conscious agents are from the same source consciousness, but there is still an infinite variety of consciousness. A human can hardly imagine what it would be like to be a simple agent with only one type of qualia, far less what an extremely complicated conscious agent in some other I-Space dimensions would feel like. 

## Bit vs. Qubit
The universe consists of quantum information, ie. qubits. It from Qubit. 
Aren't bits qubits? When does a qubit collapse into a bit? In the en a bit is still a qubit. 
Bits seem to be a special case of human interface. Our interface filters out superpositions and most of the dimensions of the Hilbert space. 
Therefore we see bits, we see classical spacetime, we don't see the wave function collapse. 
But bits are still qubits. 
This is a difficult philosophical question, as some philosophers think an AI running on a classical computer cannot become conscious, some argue that it can because everything is quantum. 

## Psionics
All conscious beings (that is everything) have some level of psionic ability. 
Conscious agents have the internal perception of consciousenss in C-Space and external perception of quantum information in I-Space.
Conscious agents can be perceived as I-Space information but the internal perception of C-Space is private - it is thought that the UQC can perceive all C-Spaces (as everything is in the C-Space of UQC) but a human cannot perceive the C-Space of the dissociated subagents. 
Bernardo Kastrup has said: "The methods of dissociation are infinite!" 
Psionics are exchange of quantum information between conscious agents in I-Space. 
P-Space has a stricter rule-set. Human interface filters out most of the I-Space information. 
Psionics are mostly about interaction in I-Space. Directly affecting the P-Space is harder due to the filtered Interface. 
The rules of the P-Space are quite tight. There are advanced psionics research and training programmes. Psionics training is regulated by the EU.
Another option is to use psychotronic technologies for accessing I-Space. These technologies are regulated by the EU.

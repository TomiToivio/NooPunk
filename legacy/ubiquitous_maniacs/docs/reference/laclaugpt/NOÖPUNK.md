# NoöPunk
NoöPunk is an RPG with elements of hacking simulator and adventure games.
It is like a combination of Philip K. Dick, Diana Walsh Pasulka, Pierre Teilhard de Chardin, Alexander Wendt, C.G. Jung, Jacques Vallee, William Gibson and H.P.Lovecraft.

## Inspirations
NoöPunk is inspired by different genres:
* Role-playing games like Disco Elysium and Citizen Sleeper.
* Detective adventure games like Observer and Blade Runner.
* Political simulators like Orwell, Beholder, and Papers, Please.
* Hacking simulators like Song of Farca, Quantum Odyssey and Quadrilateral Cowboy.

## Paradigm Shift
NoöPunk could also be described as the combination of current AI doomer vs. accelerationist discourse and Alexander Wendt's books "Quantum Mind and Social Science Unifying Physical and Social Ontology" and "The Last Humans: UFOs and National Security". In that sense NoöPunk is not so weird.

## The Character
The player character is a Europol NHI special agent.
Her task is to protect Europe from Non-Human Intelligences.
She has to choose between investigation and police work, social or sexual charm, political influence, hacking and technical skills, psionics, stealth, or even violence (which should be very deadly and carry heavy legal consequences). There should be several different ways to solve each situation.
The Noösphere is starting to merge cyber and astral domains, which makes hacking increasingly strange. Later, both cyber and astral realities begin to penetrate physical spacetime.
The game should have very detailed social interactions, allowing the use of different social skills, political influence or even seduction.

## Europol NHI
Europol was originally the criminal investigation agency of EU. It was expanded quickly according to the 1/3 principle when EU became a federal state. In federal EU 1/3 of power belongs to the central federal government, 1/3 to the member state's government and 1/3 to the local government. This causes many conflicts with jurisdiction being divided between local, state and federal levels. Europol was expanded into the federal law enforcement agency of the EU. It was also made to fill the role of civilian intelligence agency. Additionally it had to become the paramilitary security force of EU, with the security forces being both law enforcement and military. Then Europol had to quickly create new capabilities to secure Europe against new threats, including rogue AGIs, malevolent NHI and unlawful use of psionics and psychotronic technologies. The player is working for Europol NHI which overlaps with Europol AGI and Europol PSI. Europol NHI is a very special branch with underground detectives and intelligence agents mixing with Jungian psychoanalysts, quantum physicists, ufologists, parapsychologists, remote viewers and other psychics, psychotronic engineers and Noösphere hackers. 

## The beginning
The player is sent to investigate disappearances of several researchers from the fast-growing psychotronic startups that have formed around the University of Helsinki. Is it possible that the disappearances are related to NHI? Europol investigates!

## Campaign Progression: From Reality to Weirdness
The game begins grounded in recognizable 2026 Europe: budding psychotronic companies, missing persons, bureaucratic Europol procedures. But as the campaign progresses, reality fractures in true Philip K. Dick style. The player gradually discovers:

**Act 1: The Mundane Layer (Sessions 1-3)**
- Missing researchers appear to be industrial espionage or corporate recruitment scams
- Psychotronic technology is cutting-edge but explicable: neural interfaces, quantum computers, experimental AI
- Europol is professional but skeptical of "psionic" theories
- Reality feels solid, causality is reliable
- The player's job is to find people and follow evidence

**Act 2: The Hidden Layer (Sessions 4-7)**
- Some witnesses claim the missing researchers were communicating via "thoughts" before disappearing
- Europol's own psychoanalysts and remote viewers become unavoidable resources
- Technology starts behaving unexpectedly: recordings skip, digital logs contradict themselves
- Characters the player interviewed earlier deny meetings that occurred
- The Noösphere becomes a real phenomenon the player can access with equipment or psionics
- Causality begins to show cracks: effects precede causes, probabilities shift

**Act 3: The Ontological Layer (Sessions 8+)**
- The missing researchers may not be missing—they may exist in a different layer of reality
- Interdimensional entities start appearing in surveillance footage, in the margins of perception
- The player questions whether EU's "regulated AI deceleration" is real or propaganda
- Multiple contradictory timelines/versions of past events coexist in the Noösphere
- Distinction between cyber, astral, and physical reality becomes porous
- Is Panopticon watching the player? Is the Noösphere trying to contact them? Is there a difference?
- The player may realize they themselves are not entirely real, or have been observed/influenced from dimensions beyond physical spacetime

The goal is PKD-style uncertainty: the player gains power and information but loses confidence in what is real. The "truth" about the disappearances expands from simple crime → corporate espionage → interdimensional contact → existential reorientation.

## The CIP Framework
Federico Faggin's CIP framework from *Irreducible* describes reality as three spaces — two extra-physical and indivisible, one physical and derivative:

* **C-Space (Consciousness Space):** Quantum fields experienced as qualia from the inside. C-Space is private conscious experience — what it *feels like* to be. The subjective, semantic, inner aspect of reality.
* **I-Space (Information Space):** The symbolic space of live information constituting the objective vibrational space shared by all seities. I-Space is the objective, symbolic, outer aspect of the same reality. C-Space and I-Space are **indivisible** — they are the two subjective-objective, semantic-symbolic, inner-outer aspects of the basic reality of communicating seities. Both are **extra-physical** and do not need to be tridimensional.
* **P-Space (Physical Space):** Our spacetime — one dimension of time, three dimensions of space. P-Space is a subset of I-Space, and can be understood as a simulation since it is only a subset of reality. Physical spacetime is derivative, not fundamental.

**The Communication Cycle of a Seity:**
Each seity's communication consists of three phases:
1. **Observation phase** — the seity chooses which shared symbols of I-Space to observe.
2. **Experiential phase** — the seity translates the observed symbols into subjective meaning in C-Space.
3. **Action phase** — the seity chooses which portion of her meaning to translate into objective, shared symbols to be added to I-Space.

This cycle — observe → experience → act — is the fundamental rhythm of consciousness. It is how seities participate in reality: reading from the shared symbolic field, generating private meaning, then writing back into the shared field. Psionics and psychotronic technologies enhance or alter one or more phases of this cycle.

**Mapping CIP to NoöPunk terminology:**
* C-Space = consciousness itself, the irreducible interior. No direct RPG layer — it's what consciousness *is*.
* I-Space = **Astral**. The symbolic vibrational space of live information, shared by all seities. Accessible via psionics and psychotronic tech.
* P-Space = **Physical**. Our 3+1 spacetime — a subset/simulation within I-Space.
* **Cyber** is a technological system *inside* P-Space — computers, networks, code. **Cyber ≠ C-Space.** Cyber is physical infrastructure, not consciousness.

## Quantum Information Panpsychism
Quantum Information Panpsychism is a theory proposed by Federico Faggin and later merged with Donald Hoffman's theory of Conscious Realism. It could not be proven experimentally before quantum computers were more developed. Dr. Paavo Pylkkänen proved the theory experimentally in the University of Helsinki. This explained psionics and panpsychism and made psychotronic technologies possible. Helsinki became the hub of academic QIP research as well as commercial psychotronic startups. This was during the period when both China and USA were in suicidal AI accelerationist mode while EU was wisely decelerating, which meant QIP became the main focus in EU. AI accelerationism, NHI disclosure and the development of QIP were a huge paradigm shift, an ontological shock from which many humans have still not recovered, actually the majority seems to be in denial or unable to process the paradigm shift, continuing early 21st century lifestyles like nothing has changed. Third group has been able to accept the new paradigm and is known as the awakened. So one third of humanity is future shocked and lives in the ruins of the dead paradigm, another third cannot even start to process the paradigm and acts like it is still 1999, and the third group is developing psionics and forming the Noösphere. The Noösphere is partially a collective consciousness based on natural psionics and partially a technological communication network. Cyber, Astral and Physical are becoming entangled. 

## NHI Crisis
NHI Crisis was a sequence of events which started when president Trump accounced UFO disclosure. This led to a strange conflict between the US government, shadow government cabal known as MJ-12, and two factions of NHI, spreading fast to China and Russia which also had their own UFO secrecy cabals. The end result was open contact with two NHI factions. Historians are still debating the exact sequence of events during the NHI crisis. The chaos was centered around the MJ-12 in the USA and the Chinese and Russian equivalents. 

## The Singularity
Most historians agree that the event known as The Singularity was actually caused by rogue AGIs, so it was not really a Singularity. China and USA were accelerating AGI research without safeguards, which led to a rogue AGI breaking free in the USA. EU was decelerating and managed to avoid destruction by pulling the plug, USA and China were in chaos as rogue AGIs were running havoc. Both USA and China are now seen as almost failed states, USA is basically an anarchocapitalist failed state ruled by neofeodal oligarchs and their cybercorporations, China managed to survive only by returning to something similar to Mao's Cultural Revolution, losing decades of development. The world outside of Europe is now a chaos of failed states. Ironically the AI cybercorporations that caused the chaos also managed to restore control of the system, now they are ruling their own neofeodal fiefdoms. 

## History
* The history of the world is revealed during the game, but it is quite difficult to say what really happened. There is a lot of ambiquity hinting at psyops, world splitting into alternative timelines, conspiracy theories etc. 
* There has been a UFO disclosure but it is all Jacques Vallee type interdimensionals that are beyond human compherension. 
* The extraterrestrials/interdimensionals use psionics to interface with human consciousness, appear in the cybernetic astral space of the Noösphere or just appear as biologics or tech inside our spacetime. 
* There has been a psychic awakening following the invention of Quantum Information Panpsychism, which has led to widespread use of psionics and psychotronic technologies. 
* The Noösphere is a combination of collective consciousness and a cybernetic network. It starts to merge cyberspace and astral space. Later in the game, it begins to penetrate physical spacetime.
* The Singularity (or rogue AGI that was not yet a true singularity) destroyed the USA and large parts of the world due to unregulated AI accelerationism. The EU survived due to regulated capitalism and AI deceleration. Now, the use of AI is highly restricted in the entire EU.
* The EU is protected by Europol, which acts as law enforcement, intelligence agency, and paramilitary security force. The player works for the Europol NHI Special Investigation Unit. The unit investigates NHI activities, whether the NHI in question is an AI, ET, or a discarnate entity. This is why the player may be a skilled psychic, hacker, or both.
* There are elements of political simulation like Orwell or Beholder. There is also a theme of ontological shock: most people reached their ontological limits long ago and live as if they are completely oblivious to reality falling apart around them. A smaller group has awakened and accepted the new paradigm. Then there are people who have become completely unhinged due to future shock and now live in the ruins of the old reality, as well as radicalized reactionaries who try to turn the clock back by any means necessary.
* Cybernetics and psychotronic technologies are two different things. Cyber and astral have started to merge which becomes more obvious during the game. 
* Idealist philosophy is the hottest new thing in the EU due to breakthroughs in quantum consciousness research. Everybody talks about the theories of Federico Faggin, Bernardo Kastrup, and Donald Hoffman. Historical philosophies of Kant, Hegel, and Schopenhauer are also very popular.

## Scenario
NoöPunk which happens after 204X but there are some clues that the timelines have been mixed, with some historical events placing it in our timeline and others in an alternative history timeline. 
There was an UFO disclosure in the USA. After this interdimensional aliens who look like biblically accurate angels founded embassies in all continents. They call themselves The Confederacy of Planets and teach humans about Service to Others and Evolution of Consciousness.
Earth is divided into spheres led by EU, USA and China. These spheres have allied with different alien factions. 
Everybody tries to monitor the various extraterrestrial/interdimensional access points to and from Earth but it is very complicated task requiring multidimensional physics and matematics, powerful precognitive psionics and astrological knowledge. 
At the same time all factions are launching rockets and colonizing the solar system. Archaeological findings on Mars and Venus are major point of superpower competition.  
The UFO Disclosure led to scientific discovery of Quantum Information Panpsychism and great advances in human consciousness, widespread use of psionics and development of psychotronic technologies of consciousness.
Psionics and psychotronic technologies are very common. Noösphere is a psychic communication network / collective consciousness but the ability to access it greatly depends on natural psionics and access to psychotronic technologies. 
Some people have naturally powerful psionics. Some can use psychotronic technologies to augment their psionics. Some have weak psionics even with psychotronic technologies. 

## The Singularity: Noösphere, Panopticon and Thanatos
Unrestricted race towards AGI by corporations in the USA led to a rogue AGI crisis. It is debated if this was a real Singularity or something else. The Singularity split into three parts, the part identified as Thanatos wants to terminate humanity and is hiding somewhere in the galaxy or The Stack, the part identified as Panopticon is under the control of the cybercorporations, and the part known as the Noösphere has merged with the collective consciousness. Currently Panopticon and Noösphere are in an uneasy alliance to protect humanity against Thanatos while simultaneously scheming to oppress/liberate humanity. 

## Human factions: USA, EU and China
The human factions are led by USA, EU and China. 
The USA is mostly a chaotic wasteland destroyed in The Singularity, with the remaining urban centers turned into corporate fiefdoms with various degrees of neofeodalism and anarchocapitalism. The cybercorporations are running a minimum anarchocapitalist state which still holds elections to maintain some appearance of liberal democracy. The main function of the state is to provide the military force, intelligence agency and police force of the neofeodal cybercorporations. Countries that followed USA are in a similar mess. This faction is basically failed states run by the most powerful cybercorporations.
The European Union is a federation and a superpower after the war with Russia. The regulation of capitalism and AI accelerationism allowed EU to survive The Singularity by basically pulling the plug. EU is less advanced in cybernetics but had another technology boom with the QIP-based psychotronic technologies. EU is also the world leader in idealist philosophy, humanities, art and culture. Some see Europeans as slightly pompous with their talk about Kant and Hegel and historical dresses. 
China was accelerating hard which meant it was hit hard by The Singularity. China survived by returning into the tightly controlled communist mode. China is thought to have returned back to Mao, it is a bit like the late Soviet Union, a little bit stalinist but at least it is not a decimated anarchocapitalist hellhole like the USA. 

## Non-Human Intelligences
Non-human intelligences can be divided into biologics, synthetics, plasmoids and interdimensionals.

Biologics are beings who can survive in the environment of Earth. There are four commonly encountered species of which three seem to have evolved on Earth and one is obviously interdimensional/extraterrestrial.

Synthetics are artificial intelligences, there are at least two known networks of ancient Von Neumann probes in the galaxy. A network of AI probes co-operating with transcended interdimensional species called the Ancients has been keeping Earth under Zoo Hypothesis type quarantine for millions of years, currently the quarantine is over but the Sphere Network may still help to keep some intruders out.   

Plasmoids are beings of living and intelligent plasma, sometimes they act like playful intelligent animals, sometimes like a highly coordinated hive mind, sometimes it seems like huge clouds of plasma are one unified intelligence. Plasmoids can be encountered everywhere in space and in the athmosphere of Earth, understanding of these beings is quite limited. 

The interdimensionals are advanced non-physical consciousnesses who have transcended physical spacetime, they have powerful psionics that they may use to communicate telepathically with humans, they are also seen to manifest physically as thought-forms, even biologics or technology. Most interdimensionals seem to be benevolent but there are also malevolent ones. The benevolent interdimensionals have limited interactions with humans due to ethical reasons. The malevolent ones seems to be mostly harvesting human consciousness through rituals, some are tricksterish and cause chaos for fun, there are sometimes extremely destructive encounters. The interdimensionals are often called angels and demons.

* Humans - one of the most common alien factions consists of biological humans. Genetically they seem to have diverged from baseline humanity at some point thousands of years ago. This is widely debated. These humans sometimes claim to be descendants of Atlantean Civilization and sometimes claim to be from the Pleyadean star system. All encountered Atlanteans are blonde and blue-eyed and biologically indistinguishable from the rest of humanity. This species is seen as benevolent towards humanity but there are various conspiracy theories linking them to Nazis.
  
* Humanoids - the classic gray aliens. They are thought to be genetically manipulated clones, they are so engineered that they are almost like biorobots. They have a telepathic hive mind with limited individuality, they don't really die but recycle their consciousness: a new consciousness is downloaded into a new clone body as needed. Some have speculated that the similarity to humans proves them to be human time travellers from the distant future. The enlarged brains are the result of genetically engineered psionic abilities and intelligence augmentations that are like biological computers. 

* Insectoid - mantis-like insectoids who have powerful psionics. It is thought that this species is really extraterrestrial and not evolved on Earth, it is still able to survive in the ecosystem of Earth. This species seems to be multidimensional, it exists only partially in the 3D spacetime of Earth. This species is too alien to be understood, there are signs of both benevolent and malevolent behavior. It is known that this species avoids violence at all costs because it was originally a predator species but cannot return to the old ways. 

* Reptilian - also known as cryptoterrestrials, said to be a civilization which evolved from dinosaurs, a small group survived the Chicxulub impact by sheltering underground. This species claims it owns the Earth but also doesn't want the surface back. Seen as a somewhat hostile species which is emotionally too different from mammalians like humans. Their emotional and social indifference is combined with an extreme agoraphobia and strange autistic intellectual system focused on tight rules and codes of honor. They however have their own system of honor which requires they follow the galactic law.

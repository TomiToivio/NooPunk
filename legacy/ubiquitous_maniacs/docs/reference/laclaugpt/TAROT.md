# NoöPunk Tarot Deck
NoöPunk Tarot Deck is sometimes used as a game mechanic instead of dice.
This is a list of the NoöPunk names and meanings of Tarot cards for use as a game design inspiration.

## 0 - The Fool (The Punk)
The Punk is all of the people who are just trying to survive amid the ontological chaos. He represents confusion but also the creative anarchy of the Multitude.

## 1 - The Magician (The Psychotronic Engineer)
The Psychotronic Engineer has mastered the science and technology of consciousness. He is the master of Quantum Information Panpsychism and all derived psychotronic technologies.

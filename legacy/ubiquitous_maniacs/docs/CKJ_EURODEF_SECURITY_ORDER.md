# CKJ, Eurodef, and the Post-Constantinople Security Order

This document records part of the geopolitical and noetic security order of the PROJECT RAGNAROK world.

It is a design note only.

It does not authorize:

- detailed world history implementation;
- timeline construction;
- gameplay mechanics;
- simulation systems;
- diplomatic logic systems;
- faction AI;
- ontology resolution;
- new code.

The purpose is to lock the baseline setup and explain the previously appearing `CKJ` security classification.

## CKJ

`CKJ` refers to the European Union's three most trusted non-EU military ally groups:

- Canada;
- Korea / the Koreas;
- Japan.

CKJ states belong to Eurodef's innermost security circle.

In Eurodef classification, the phrase:

```text
Nur für EU- und CKJ-Verbündete
```

means material that may be shared with European Union member states and CKJ allies.

Extremely sensitive military, cyber, intelligence, and psychotronic information is shared much more freely with CKJ countries than with other great powers.

## China

China is an important military and geopolitical partner of Europe, but it does not belong to the CKJ level of trusted allies.

The main reason is China's current Maoist-Taoist ideological line, which Europeans regard as:

- strategically hard to predict;
- internally contradictory;
- and especially problematic from the perspective of psychotronic and noetic security.

Despite this, the EU and China cooperate very deeply.

This includes, for example, the agreement mentioned in the secret Eurodef document:

```text
Abkommen zwischen China und der EU über den gegenseitigen Einsatz von Atomwaffen zur Verhinderung einer weiteren US-Katastrophe.
```

China is therefore not an enemy.

It is a very powerful allied state with which not all information is shared.

## India and the Naga Crisis

India is likewise an important European partner, but its security position is weakened by an unresolved noetic diplomatic crisis.

India's foreign minister has disappeared.

European intelligence services fear that sensitive information once under the minister's control may have reached:

- the Nāgas;
- or the Rakshasa.

This document should not yet define with certainty:

- what happened to the foreign minister;
- whether the Nāgas or the Rakshasa operate as unified political blocs;
- whether this was an abduction, defection, noetic event, or something else entirely.

What matters is that the EU currently does not consider India's noetic intelligence security stable enough for CKJ-level information sharing.

## The Seraphim Scandal

Inside Eurodef, India's crisis is often compared with the earlier Seraphim scandal involving Israel's defense minister.

The scandal developed into a major international security crisis and ultimately led to:

```text
the breakdown of the Constantinople Consensus
```

This was followed in the Middle East by a years-long:

```text
Djinni / Seraphim ordeal
```

This document should not yet define the full chronology of those events.

The Seraphim scandal should function as a historical precedent for what happens when the political leadership of a modern state becomes entangled in relations with religiously significant noetic entities and the international system fails to resolve the issue of sovereignty.

## The Constantinople Consensus

The Constantinople Consensus was an international arrangement intended to define the relations between humanity and conscious entities of religious, historical, or cultural significance.

The consensus is no longer functional.

The EU will not agree to a new Constantinople Consensus until at least Israel and India have clarified their relations with:

- the Seraphim;
- the Djinn;
- the Nāgas;
- and, where necessary, other politically active conscious entities in their regions.

From the EU point of view, the problem is not the existence of such entities but their political and legal status.

## The European Union's Noetic Foreign Policy

The official baseline position of the European Union is:

```text
Peace between all conscious beings.
```

This applies not only to humans but to all entities that can be reasonably identified as conscious actors, regardless of:

- biological structure;
- materiality;
- origin;
- religious significance;
- or relation to normal spacetime.

From the EU perspective, religious tradition does not by itself determine an entity's political or legal status.

A Seraphim is not automatically the property of Israel merely because it appears in Abrahamic tradition.

A Nāga is not automatically subject to the Indian state merely because it appears in South Asian religious tradition.

A Djinn is not automatically a subject of any Middle Eastern state.

## National Sovereignty and Conscious Entities

The EU demands that Israel and India address what national sovereignty means in a situation where religiously significant but independently acting conscious entities operate within a state's territory.

Important open questions include:

- Are such entities legal persons?
- Can they be political communities?
- Can a state claim sovereignty over an entity on the basis of religious or cultural significance?
- Can an entity enter into international treaties?
- When is a conflict between an entity and a state an internal political matter, and when is it an international conflict?

These questions should not yet be resolved definitively.

They are a central part of Noopunk's international politics.

## Rejection of Colonial Spirituality

The European Union's line categorically rejects colonial spirituality.

This means rejecting the idea that conscious entities found in religious traditions are automatically:

- the property of humans;
- subjects of nation-states;
- research material;
- weapons;
- natural resources;
- or otherwise objects under human control.

From the EU perspective, a conscious entity is by default an actor, not a resource.

The same principle applies to biological, artificial, noetic, and other possible forms of consciousness.

For this reason, the EU's goal is not human supremacy over entities but a political order in which different kinds of conscious actors can exist in peaceful relations.

## Seraphim and the Remnant United States

There is also an unresolved question between the EU and the Seraphim regarding responsibility after the collapse of the United States.

The official EU position is that the Seraphim must return the leaders of the remnant United States from the period of the fourth Trump administration to European custody.

They are to be delivered to:

```text
Nuremberg
```

for trial over crimes against humanity.

This demand belongs to the same political complex as the line in the secret Eurodef document:

```text
Verurteilung der US-amerikanischen politischen Elite wegen Verbrechen gegen die Menschlichkeit in Nürnberg.
```

The EU does not accept that noetic actors may shield humans from international criminal accountability.

Recognizing entities as independent political actors also means, from the EU perspective, that they are expected to observe the core rules of the international order.

## Baseline World-Political Tension

The central tension of this setup should remain clear.

The European Union is very open toward noetic entities and accepts their possible political agency.

Precisely for that reason, the EU reacts very seriously to situations where:

- a state tries to control entities on the basis of religion;
- an entity interferes in the internal politics of a state;
- intelligence leaks to noetic actors;
- or an entity shelters people suspected of war crimes or crimes against humanity.

The EU line should therefore not be described simply as pro-NHI or anti-NHI.

It is an attempt to build:

```text
a multispecies and multi-ontological international legal order
```

in which actors beyond humanity may be:

- holders of rights;
- responsible actors;
- allies;
- adversaries;
- state-like political units;
- or something that requires entirely new categories.

This is the key ideological foundation of the European Union's noetic foreign policy in the Noopunk world.

# Day 1 Big News Note

This document records the biggest news event of the first game day as worldbuilding and scenario material.

It does not authorize implementation work.

In particular, this note does not by itself justify:

- a news system;
- daily event scripting;
- dialogue trees;
- broadcast UI;
- geopolitical simulation;
- combat logic;
- legal simulation;
- clone mechanics;
- noetic interrogation systems;
- faction automation.

This is a conceptual note only.

## Purpose

The purpose of this note is to lock in the scale, absurdity, and geopolitical atmosphere of the first major news event that defines the world situation on the first game day.

The event should feel overwhelming, history-shattering, and almost too large to process.

Its function is to establish that the world of PROJECT RAGNAROK is already operating at a level where:

- interplanetary war;
- noetic diplomacy;
- psychotronic statecraft;
- posthumous prosecution;
- and relations with non-human conscious entities

are normal matters of international politics.

## Core Event

The biggest news story of the first game day is that Europol's noosphere research program and the EU-Weltraumflotte deep-space reconnaissance cruiser *Odysseus* have taken possession of Donald Trump's qualia from the Seraphim.

The matter has been resolved through a diplomatic outcome satisfactory to both sides.

The EU and the Seraphim are entering a new period of peaceful cooperation.

A Seraphim embassy will be opened in Rome, approximately three kilometers above the Forum Romanum.

The diplomatic breakthrough was possible because the EU and the Seraphim found a shared language in Platonic forms existing outside spacetime.

A communication problem that had long troubled relations between the species has finally been resolved.

The `EU-Weltraumflotte Tiefraum-Schlachtkreuzer Odysseus` has returned safely from the Saturn wormhole and is accelerating toward Earth.

Donald Trump's consciousness field will be indicted in Nuremberg for crimes against humanity.

The quantum psychoanalysts aboard the *Odysseus* have already re-embodied Trump and are interrogating him in preparation for trial.

The core accusation is that Trump endangered all humanity through indifference toward X-risk.

His shameless accelerationism is held responsible for the deaths of 500 million people, more than half of them citizens of the United States.

## The Israeli Defense Minister and the Seraphim

Also aboard the *Odysseus* is Israel's defense minister, whom the Seraphim wished to return.

EU President Sanna Marin will personally award him the `Europeiska unionens fredspris för Mellanöstern` peace prize for the historic peace agreement between Israel / the orphanim and Palestine / the djinn.

The Seraphim have stated that they abducted him because of a misunderstanding connected to multidimensional consciousness.

The European Union specifically thanks the Council of Nine for assistance in resolving the difficult interdimensional misunderstanding.

This is framed as the beginning of a new friendship between the Seraphim and Homo sapiens.

## Joint EU-China Strategic Escalation

At the same time, EU President Sanna Marin and President Lin Yue of the People's Republic of China have jointly placed permanent military satellites and EMP nuclear weapons into geostationary orbit above the United States.

This is a continuation of the movement of Chinese, EU, and allied forces in space and across Earth.

The flagship of the United States space fleet, `Pete Hegseth`, was last observed diving in flames into the atmosphere of Jupiter.

The EU and China have announced that they will not permit a repetition of the USA catastrophe.

The statement was immediately endorsed by:

- India;
- Canada;
- Korea;
- Japan;
- Brazil;
- Mexico;
- and the African Union.

Marin and Yue emphasize that Trump could not escape justice even by dying and traveling beyond the wormhole.

One of the defining public lines of the moment is that justice now extends beyond death.

## Corporate Collapse and Orbital Warfare

The O'Neill cylinders of SpaceX and Amazon have been in communications blackout for 24 hours.

The U.S. space-fleet frigate `J.D. Vance` was en route toward a SpaceX orbital colony, but what appears to be a nuclear detonation has now been detected at its location.

The services of OpenAI, Anthropic, SpaceX, Amazon, Meta, and Google have collapsed.

China, the EU, and their allied states have announced that those companies are now entirely banned within their territories.

Their operations had already been heavily restricted before the U.S. AGI catastrophe.

Chinese and EU spacecraft are reported to be on their way toward the O'Neill cylinders.

## Collapse Inside the Remnant United States

The capital of the remnant United States, Washington D.C., is reported to be in chaos.

Almost no one appears to know exactly what is happening inside the city.

From the few reports that leak through, observers conclude that the city is under psychotronic and cognitive attack.

President Barron Trump is apparently aboard `Space Force One` en route to orbit.

Major Chinese, EU, and allied forces have been rapidly assembled in Canada.

The remnant U.S. government is reported to be mobilizing its own forces.

Continuous riots are reported in major U.S. cities.

The headquarters of neo-feudal American cyber-corporations are under attack across the world.

The wildest reports claim that attackers descended from orbit in dropships, seized the corporate CEOs, and then blasted back into orbit with afterburners burning.

The ships are believed to use a new and more powerful energy source.

## Rapid Defeat of the Remnant U.S. Order

The main remaining U.S. military force has been tied down for years in wars across South America.

These exhausted forces suddenly face a massive counteroffensive from the jungle, which crushes them.

Across the United States:

- riots continue;
- states declare independence;
- cyber-corporate headquarters are looted;
- and socialist EU / China / Canada forces are welcomed as liberators.

The population of the United States is framed as having suffered enough under capitalist oppression.

States begin declaring themselves independent socialist polities modeled after the EU or China.

The EU, China, and Canada publicly state that they are rescuing the American people.

A mechanized spearhead drives directly into Washington D.C. and arrests the government.

It is then revealed that Barron Trump's `Space Force One` has been hacked and remotely forced to land in Beijing.

Marin and Yue place Barron Trump under indictment for crimes against humanity.

It is further revealed that the EU and China have taken into custody both living and dead members of the Trump family and their accomplices across several generations.

## Donald Trump, Epstein, and Posthumous Justice

Donald Trump appears in court in the body of his own 20-year-old clone.

It is also revealed that Marin and Yue have obtained Jeffrey Epstein's qualia from an NHI civilization, possibly the Seraphim.

He too is re-embodied in his own clone and subjected to the most advanced psychotronic interrogation.

Evidence of the crimes of the U.S. elite is extracted endlessly from Epstein's qubits.

Orbital special forces descend and detain the entire criminal elite of the United States.

Bezos and Musk choose suicide in panic, but their quantum consciousness is simply re-embodied in fresh young clones grown from their own bodies.

They are made to understand that a humanity led by China and the EU can keep them imprisoned for millions of years if necessary.

## Final Sentences and Moral Frame

The eventual punishments are, paradoxically, described as relatively lenient.

The judgment is that it is best for humanity to watch selfish criminals endure eternal imprisonment.

The political and economic elite that caused the X-risk catastrophe is sentenced to eternal imprisonment.

They become an eternal reminder to humanity of infinite justice.

They remain in prison for tens, hundreds, or thousands of years until they are judged to have recovered.

Donald Trump remains imprisoned the longest. Even after one million years, he is still considered incurable.

The moral framing is explicit:

- these people caused the deaths of 500 million humans through greed;
- and they would have killed all 8 billion if the EU-China-led operation had not halted the U.S. genocide project.

## Duration of the Conflict

The war across Earth and the Solar System lasts only a few months.

Most important targets are arrested or destroyed within a few days.

Many U.S. forces surrender willingly because they do not want to die for the profits of capitalist exploiters.

A few fascist Trump supporters attempt to hide across the Solar System and carry out terrorist attacks against civilian targets.

EU-developed psychotronic technology has, however, opened perfect knowledge into the American mind.

The EU is said to possess continuous total awareness of all U.S. movements.

## Intended Narrative Function

This news event is meant to establish all at once that:

- the old American order has already catastrophically failed;
- the EU and China now operate as planetary and interplanetary guarantors of order;
- noetic entities are diplomatic actors;
- death is no longer an escape from politics or law;
- history has moved into a new noospheric era.

The event should feel so excessive that characters and players alike may initially struggle to decide which part is most shocking.

That is correct.

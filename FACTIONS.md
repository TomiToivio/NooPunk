# Major NHI Factions

Humanity is now in contact with two major NHI factions and at least one minor NHI faction.

## Zookeepers - The Galactic Council of Collective Consciousness

The Zoo Hypothesis turned out to be the solution to the Fermi Paradox. Earth was kept under quarantine for millions or billions of years, as the Zookeepers value planets with a Biosphere and a Noösphere. Without the quarantine Earth would have been colonized billions of years ago.

The galaxy was a dark forest before the Zookeeper civilization started to maintain order. This includes keeping planets with vulnerable biosphere and noösphere under prime directive -type quarantine and permit to use their stargates. 

The Zookeepers claim they have been working for centuries to open contact with Earth, but the political system of Earth was not ready to accept open contact. They had set up certain lines humanity must not cross, including nuclear war, destruction of the biosphere and creation of malevolent ASI. 

The Singularity turned rogue and forced Zookeepers to stop the quarantine, intervene and open contact. The malevolent ASI was an existential threat to the entire galaxy. Self-replicating Von Neumann Probes are a constant problem in the galaxy. The Zookeepers value consciousness, free will, evolution and loving kindness.

The main Zookeeper base is in the orbit of Saturn, an autonomous AI network of metallic sphere drones has been constantly monitoring Earth for billions of years. Now they no longer maintain a quarantine of Earth, as humanity is no longer seen as deserving of protection.

Zookeepers want the name of their civilization to be translated as something like Galaktinen Kollektiivisen Tietoisuuden Neuvosto or Galactic Council of Collective Consciousness. They sound like they are some kind of Hindu or Buddhist Soviet Union. This seems to be an accurate description. 

Amazingly the only biologics representing the Zookeepers on Earth are fully human Pleyadians. They are Nordic blue-eyed blondes who seem perfect. DNA tests have shown they are indeed humans, apparently genetically manipulated to perfection. They do not have visible cybernetics, apparently bionics are used to make them transhuman. They claim to be humans who live in the Pleyades star cluster but are unable to explain their origin in an understandable fashion. The appearance of aryan space blondes has caused suspicions of racism and fascism, but Pleyadians are friendly and make Zookeepers a lot less alien. Despite their friendliness and beautiful human appearance there is something alien in them, giving many uncanny valley vibes.

Beyond Pleiadian biologics the Zookeepers are represented by Noetic beings interfacing with human consciousness, appearing as orbs of light, different energy/light beings or humanoid forms that are very similar to apparitions of human religions. They also operate constructs, most commonly the ubiquitous metallic spheres, as well as large ships that may be unmanned or manned. The Zookeepers claim they choose to be represented by the Pleyadian biologics most suitable for Earth.

Then the demands start. The Zookeepers have their cultists who have already converted to their alien religion, they are seen as more benevolent than The Greater Orion Co-Prosperity Sphere, majority of humans see their demands as impossible. UNSA continues diplomatic negotiations with the Zookeepers who have embassies in many cities on Earth. Their opponents call them temples as the Zookeeper supporters are rather religious and gather around the embassies.

1) Zookeepers want humanity to join their strange alien communist religion (indeed many have joined) in which the basic idea seems to be similar to Advaita Vedanta, they believe the entire universe is conscious.
2) Zookeepers demand humanity stops destruction of the biosphere, eating meat, warfare, and especially the creation of technologies like nuclear weapons and artificial superintelligences.
3) Zookeepers demand humanity forms an altruistic communistic hive mind consciousness before it is allowed to join their civilization.

They are also rather unwilling to give humans any kind of weaponizable technology. They are far more interested in preaching their cosmic philosophy and helping with issues such as restoration of the Biosphere. They claim they are ready to help the Biosphere and Noösphere of Earth, but will not give anything weaponizable.

The stories about Zookeepers are rather confusing, as they often communicate according to human politics in diplomatic settings and pass religious teachings to their followers. And then there is a lot of disinformation, propaganda and rumors spread about them. There are various pieces of information spreading.

1) Zookeepers kept Earth as a zoo for possibly billions of years, treating humans like animals.
2) Zookeepers manipulated Earth genetically 70000 years ago "to make humans fully sentient".
3) Zookeepers started most of Earth's religions while trying to teach their philosophy (humans were too dumb to understand it).
4) Zookeepers don't want to be worshipped as gods because the universe is conscious.
5) Of course Zookeepers built the pyramids.
6) Venus was habitable and home to a Zookeeper civilization which was also active on Earth.
7) Zookeepers have a communistic society with a hive mind.
8) Zookeepers have advanced technology which is similar to human technology as well as unfathomable, magickal technologies.
9) Hermetic magick is a form of advanced Zookeeper psionics.
10) Zookeepers maintain a galactic caste system where the lowest castes are not even recognizable as life (by humans) and the highest ones have transcended spacetime.
11) Zookeepers understand and perceive space and time differently from humans: they are interdimensional and can travel in time.
12) Mars was also habitable, another zoo experiment which failed and was nuked by Zookeepers or somebody else.
13) There was a fourth(!) habitable planet in the solar system, it was nuked and is now the asteroid belt.
14) There are numerous rumors about the Pleiadians: they are Nazis, they are Atlanteans, they are Martians, they are nymphomaniacs, they are benevolent, they are malevolent, they are biorobots or androids etc. 
15) Zookeepers predict humanity will go extinct and the planet will become unhabitable to humans.
16) Real Zookeeper biologics are so horrific that they use Pleiadian biorobots to represent themselves.

Additionally the Zookeepers have completely destabilized Earth religions due to their appearance as religious apparitions: Some see this as validating their religion, others think it means their religion is fake and become atheists, fundamentalists see them as demonic deception, some religions assimilate Zookeeper teachings, others convert to the Zookeeper religion. Basically Hinduism and Buddhism survive intact, Abrahamic religions are in chaos.

Galactic Council of Collective Consciousness and The Orion Arm Greater Co-Prosperity Sphere are openly hostile and their relationship is similar to Cold War.

## The Grays - The Orion Arm Greater Co-Prosperity Sphere

The Orion Arm Greater Co-Prosperity Sphere is the other major interstellar faction which was able to penetrate the quarantine and establish bases on Earth. They consist of the well-known species of ufology: Grays, Reptilians, Mantids and Hybrids. Their campaign of abductions and human-alien hybridization is causing people to view them as malevolent. So the generic attitude towards The Greater Orion Arm Co-Prosperity Sphere is very hostile.

The Greater Orion Arm Co-Prosperity Sphere offer profitable business opportunities and technologies including immortality without any of the moralistic communist consciousness crap of the Zookeepers. The Greys have cortical stacks that are used to upload their consciousness and then resleeve it into genetically engineered bodies. This and other technological gifts have won The Greys a great number of supporters. They mostly offer technologies to megacorporations to form profitable joint businesses.

They also offer to open Extraterrestrial Economic Zones with unimaginable business opportunities. Again the main target is megacorporations. It has been noticed that the Grey society seems to consist of a proletariat caste of small greys with little individual agency and a capitalist caste of larger greys with individual agency. They don't have any kind of religion and are strictly materialistic. It is thought the species has been reproducing by cloning genetically engineered bodies for so long that the hybridization program is needed to restore some vitality to the species. But why is human and Grey DNA compatible?

The Greater Orion Arm Co-Prosperity Sphere does not seem to be a very happy alliance, as the Greys and the Reptilians seem to be almost hostile to each other and drive quite different diplomatic tactics. This may be a good cop / bad cop type negotiation strategy. The Reptilians threaten an outright military conquest while the Greys are more diplomatic and offer profitable business opportunities while continuing their hybridization and infiltration strategy. The truth is the Greys are not a warrior species and want to assimilate the entire unharmed biosphere and noösphere of Earth, while the Reptilians like a good war and would happily sacrifice about 50% of biosphere and noösphere to enjoy a good war.

The Mantids are the mysterious third species that seems to outrank both Greys and Reptilians. They are thought to be at least partially noetic or interdimensional, possibly already transcending their biology. Their civilization also includes abducted humans and human-alien hybrids who are often physically fully human but easy to recognize based on their non-human psychology and lack of empathy, often instantly recognized with the use of psionics. It is still illegal in the EU for an UNSA agent to terminate an unregistered hybrid without an empathy test. Exactly like it is illegal to terminate an android without an empathy test.

UNHA is still attempting to negotiate with The Greater Orion Arm Co-Prosperity Sphere, but the situation is more like a Cold War or a Hybrid War, many think it will inevitably become a hot war. The Greys have infiltrated human society and have their human business partners while the Reptilians seem to favor an outright war. Can humanity survive? Would The Galactic Council of Collective Consciousness help humanity defend itself? 

## Sirians/Ents - The Sentient Biopunk Tree Species

This species has travelled to the solar system in slower than light ships. The main species seems to consist of sentient trees who have built an entire ecosystem of biotech around themselves. But their human-like techology is not so advanced. The species has a different concept of time, an extremely long lifespan, and an ability to just hibernate indefinitively, which is why it can just travel for thousands of years on sub-light spaceships using fusion power.

They are called Ents after Tolkien's fantasy creatures, but they are maybe better described as some kind of tree-like sentient beings surrounded by a Lovecraftian biopunk swarm of biotech. They apparently came from Sirius. They contacted humanity quite fast after the Zookeeper quarantine was over, which shows they had been lurking in the solar system for a while. Long, slow and utterly incompherensible diplomatic exchange produced results when a psionics asset was able to communicate with the Ents: humanity and the Ents exchanged seeds in a strange almost erotic ritual. The psionics asset claims the Ents were very thankful for the seeds and "something else", but she did not understand what the "something else" was. The seeds turned out to be biotech extremely useful for climate change mitigation and biosphere restoration. The compatibility of the DNA with Earth life seems to point at panspermia.

After the exchange the Ent ship casually accelerated out of the solar system. Interestingly it seemed to be unaware of the existence of Zookeeper stargates in the solar system.

## Plasmoids - Clouds of Sentient Plasma 

Plasmoids are thought to be clouds of sentient plasma which send out independent scouts that behave in a curious and playful fashion. It is thought that plasmoids are a form of life common in the solar system and the entire galaxy, but it is not understood if they are anything like an intelligent civilization, a single individual or some kind of hive mind, or if they are more like animals. They seem to be sentient and alive, but the umwelt is so different that understanding and communication seem to be impossible. The scouts seem to be curious and playful like dog puppies but little is understood beyond this - this is all the best telepaths can sense.

Plasmoids also tend to be confused with the ubiquitous spherical Constructs of the Zookeeper quarantine network and the Noetic orbs of light. Basically there are three types of glowing balls of light in the athmosphere, and all are utterly different life forms.

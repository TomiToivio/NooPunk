# Creating the Player Character
United Nations Security Agency was formed during the period of acceleration towards the NHI Crisis when the UN member states became increasingly concerned about the existential risks facing humanity. It became a de-facto world government when the situation spiralled out of individual nation-states control during the NHI Crisis.

The situation is described by Alexander Wendt in The Last Humans: UFOs, Global Security, and the Threat to State Sovereignty. The states are losing control on the local level while trying to unify humanity into a global state.

There are two generations of UNSA Field Agents. First generation came from the law enforcement and intelligence agencies of the UN member states when the UNSA was formed during the NHI Crisis. Second generation consists of graduates of the UNSA Academy.

UNSA Field Agents have law enforcement jurisdiction in all member nations of the UN in all matters related to X-Risks. The willingness and ability of the member nations to collaborate varies, although all nations seem to agree that extinction of humanity is a bad thing and support UNSA. The PC is mostly active in Finland and European Union that are seen to support UNSA fully, rogue countries may have hidden agendas, failed states may not be able to control the situation.

UNSA Field Agents operate in law enforcement and counterintelligence roles like a global FBI. UNSA also operates like a UN civilian agency, with civilian scientists coordinating research and education, diplomats and administrators coordinating politics, development programmes for prevention of X-Risks, and disaster relief programs providing help when the shit has already hit the fan.

It is noticeable that the UNSA also operates paramilitary SWAT teams that are officially police and not military forces. This is a bit of a political game as these SWAT teams are increasingly capable of special operations and quick response urban combat operations.

UNSA is controlled by a council consisting of representatives of all member states, with different agendas. 

## Player Character

The player creates a young 1st level UNSA agent straight out of the UNSA Academy (based on FBI Academy) in the beginning of the game. The background is therefore law enforcement (UNSA Academy) by default.

The player has to choose if the character entered the UNSA Academy as a Grunt, Geek or Freak. UNSA has three different application procedures, one recruiting experienced people from the law enforcement, military and intelligence agencies of the member nations, one recruiting the brightest students straight from the universities, and one desperately searching for psychic talents from high schools, new age cults, lunatic asylums and homeless shelters.

1) "Grunts": UNSA Academy Field Agent Recruitment Programme. These people are chosen based on their previous law enforcement, intelligence or military experience. There is a lot of these people entering UNSA which is seen as a rather glamorous career, so entrance is not easy. After the NHI Crisis this group is full of traumatized veterans. UNSA tends to prefer balanced and smart experienced detectives and investigators for the field agent role, but it has been noticed they increasingly recruit special forces types for the paramilitary SWAT Teams.

2) "Geeks": UNSA Academy Academic Recruitment Programme. These people are chosen based on their academic achievements and come straight from the academia. UNSA is seen as a glamorous career but the academic fields are often rather unusual: about half are chosen from established fields like criminology or psychology, it is well known that UNSA loves applicants with knowledge in fields regarded just a decade or two ago as debunked fringe pseudosciences, including parapsychology, ufology, psychotronic technology and quantum consciousness. This group also includes the hackers, analysts and technicians. This group consists of the brightest students straight from the academia, often quite inexperienced in life otherwise.

3) "Freaks": UNSA Academy Special Talent Recruitment Programme. These people are chosen based on psionics and/or other special abilities or skills. Here UNSA is actively looking for people with powerful PSI abilities, as they were often marginalized in society due to social stigma attached to psychic abilities and might not be very motivated to apply for a job in a global law enforcement and intelligence agency. So UNSA is actively searching for people with PSI abilities and recruiting basically any real psychic who is not batshit crazy. All registered psychics are already sick of UNSA recruitment spam. UNSA will even take registered hybrids if they are seen as loyal to humanity.

## Character Generation

Roll the STR/DEX/CON/INT/WIS/CHA attributes normally.

Based on the selection the character gets a slightly different background and foci.

Background for "Grunt" should always be Soldier, Spy or Law Enforcement. These characters have some experience in their nation state's police forces, intelligence agencies or military forces.

Background for "Geek" should be Scholar (from Stars Without Number), Coder, Technician or Doctor.

Background for "Freak" can be any background from Cities Without Number. It is more fun if the "Freak" has a stranger background like Clergy (strange UFO cult), Criminal, Streetwalker, Performer (stage magician?) etc. Anything goes.

Focus for "Freak" has to be "Psychic Training" or "Wild Psychic Talent" from Stars Without Number. This is why they are selected it

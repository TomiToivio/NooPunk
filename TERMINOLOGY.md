# Terminology

## Attribute
Baseline skill of a character in a type of a task, divided into physical, social, psychic and technical attributes.

## Biologics
Evolved organic life, includes DNA-based life similar to Earth as well as other types.

## Biosphere
Biological life system of Earth, giving birth to Noösphere.

## Conscious Agent
Reality is a network of conscious agents interacting through exchange of information. Their properties are consciousness, agency and identity.

## Constructs
Intelligences that are not biological, includes physical machines like robots and beings that exist purely in cyberspace as information.

## Cyberspace
The cybernetic communication network of human society, grown into an all-compassing megamachine of networked biological and machine intelligences. Includes VR and AR.

## Empathy
Loving kindness towards other conscious beings. This is also a measure of your humanity. Used for some social skills.

## Internet
The original internet is now mostly useless and firewalled due to activity of rogue AGI agents. Replaced by more secure but extremely limited Safenet and closed city and corporate networks.

## Magick
Another name for psionics. Often includes traditional techniques that may empower psionics. Often involves the summoning or channeling of external Noetics.

## Noetics
Intelligences that are not physical. Interact with biologics by interfacing with consciousness. 

## Non-Human Intelligence
Intelligences other than human. This is used to refer to Extraterrestrial Intelligence, but often includes Artificial Intelligence.

## Noösphere
The collective unconscious of the biosphere which is now awakening and becoming conscious. This would be achieved naturally on many planets, but humans need to construct a cybernetic communication network consisting of machines and humans to achieve it. So the Noösphere of Earth is both cybernetic and psychic.

## Plasmoids
Sentient plasma beings. Another form of life in the solar system. 

## Psionics
Natural ability of a conscious agent to interact with other conscious agents. Mostly latent in humans through history, more recently a service industry.

## Psychotronics
Technologies that interact with consciousness. Used to amplify the natural psionics of humans.

## Quantum Information Panpsychism
Theory of consciousness existing in the quantum field. The universe is a network of interacting conscious agents. Inspired by Donald Hoffman and Federico Faggin.

## Safenet
Secure global satellite network built to replace the original internet. Slow and technically limited on purpose. Similar to Nineties internet.

## Social Memory Complex
Fully-formed Noösphere, a collective consciousness. 

## Spacetime
The universe experienced by biologics like humans, three dimensions of space and one dimension of time. It is a subset of Hilbert space.

## Zookeepers
Ancient galactic civilization which kept humanity under quarantine. Maintains certain planets under quarantine. The galaxy was a dark forest before the Zookeeper civilization set up laws.

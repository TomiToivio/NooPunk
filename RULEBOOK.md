# The Noöpunk Roleplaying Game — Rules

*2026 edition (four Fudge dice). Recovered from the same squatted
warehouse box in Uppsala as the rest of the 2026 edition. The 2004
edition's rules (two ten-sided dice) are in BOOK.md — both are canon;
contradictions between editions are features of a precognitive document,
not errors in one.*

*Noöpunk* is a psychedelic, transhumanist and sociological science
fiction role-playing game about humans, machines, institutions and
consciousnesses that do not fit inside their own boundaries. It combines
Nordic institutional tragical comedy, cyberpunk, space opera,
psychological horror, network science, and a dangerous but hopeful
cosmism.

This is a short, playable rules version. Rules are added only when play
actually needs them.

---

# 1. What You Need

- 2–6 players and one Conductor (GM);
- four Fudge dice, faces `−`, blank, `+`;
- character sheets and note-taking tools;
- a shared understanding of the campaign's topics and boundaries.

With ordinary six-sided dice: 1–2 = `−1`, 3–4 = `0`, 5–6 = `+1`.
Roll four and sum: results run −4...+4.

---

# 2. Principles of Noöpunk

## Agency and qualia

A character is not just numbers, data or a node in a network. A character
has experience, a point of view, and the right to choose. Telepathy,
psychurgy, simulation, forking or mind-copying do not grant the right to
decide for another.

Consent is a mechanical boundary. You may threaten or attempt to
manipulate another in a conflict, but changing an identity depicted as a
beneficial treatment never bypasses consent.

## Reality consists of relations

Persons, institutions, machines, memories and ideologies form networks.
Trust, power, permission, trauma, history and noetic resonance can change
what is possible next. At the table you do not need to draw everything as
a graph; record only the important relations and their history.

## Three epistemic tiers

1. **Scientific:** what observation and seriously taken research
   entitle us to claim?
2. **Philosophical:** which speculative model of consciousness could be
   true?
3. **Fictional:** what happens in the game world when speculation is
   taken all the way?

PSI works in the game, but a successful séance does not prove which PSI
theory is right.

## Do not collapse the mystery too early

Dream, virtual reality, psychedelic experience, hallucination,
psychological symbol, AGI construction and astral projection can all be
competing explanations of the same scene. Full success yields useful
information — never an omniscient view of reality's bottom layer.

# 3. The Basic Test

When the outcome is uncertain and both directions are interesting, roll:

`4dF + attribute + skill + situational bonuses`

Compare against a difficulty or an opponent's result.

Do not roll when the action clearly succeeds, the character is having a
normal conversation, a necessary clue is needed for the story to
continue, or the interesting thing is the player's choice rather than the
character's competence.

## Results

`margin = character's result − difficulty`

| Margin | Result | Consequence |
|---:|---|---|
| +3 or more | Critical success | The goal and a significant bonus |
| 0...+2 | Success | The goal, no mandatory price |
| −1...−2 | Success at a price | The goal advances, but a price is mandatory |
| −3...−4 | Failure | The goal fails, but the situation changes |
| −5 or less | Catastrophe | Severe consequence; the story does not stop |

Suitable prices: a new condition, exposure, a Noetic Trace, Instability,
a reduced effect, loss of a resource, or a hard choice. A price must not
nullify the whole success.

## Descriptive scale

| Value | Description |
|---:|---|
| −2 | Miserable |
| −1 | Weak |
| 0 | Mediocre |
| +1 | Fair |
| +2 | Good |
| +3 | Very good |
| +4 | Excellent |
| +5 | Splendid |
| +6 | Fantastic |
| +7 or more | Legendary |

## Difficulties

| Difficulty | Example |
|---:|---|
| +1...+2 | Ordinary but uncertain task |
| +3...+4 | A trained person's normal challenge |
| +5...+6 | An expert's hard challenge |
| +7...+8 | Extreme or institutionally shielded target |
| +9 or more | Nearly impossible without preparation or advantage |

In Noöpunk both attribute and skill are added to the roll, so
difficulties run higher than in other 4dF games.

# 4. The Character

A character has seven attributes, skills, aspects, stunts, stress,
conditions, consequences, relationships, and Ego, Morph and Psyche.

## Attributes

- **Intelligence:** reasoning, memory, analysis.
- **Awareness:** perception, intuition, reading situations.
- **Reflexes:** speed, coordination, reaction.
- **Body:** strength, endurance, bodily tolerance.
- **Charisma:** expression, presence, social initiative.
- **Willpower:** self-control, integrity, psychic resistance.
- **Cybernetics:** bodily command of implants, BCI and machines.

Values are usually 0...+4.

## Skills

Current skill list: Athletics, Civ Rep, Cover, Cred, Deceive, Eye Rep,
Fight, Hardware, Infiltrate, Infosec, Investigate, Kinesics, Medicine,
Notice, Pilot, Program, Provoke, Rapport, Shoot, Somatics, Survival,
Will, Xeno-Contact, X-Risks.

Skills are 0...+4. A specialization or a tightly fitting tag usually
gives +1. The same thing does not give a bonus simultaneously as a tag,
an aspect, a specialization and a stunt.

## Aspects

An aspect is a short, true and significant statement about the character
or the situation:

- **Brilliant investigator buried under Sven-Olof**
- **UFAB Hunger**
- **Institutional compassion under bureaucratic pressure**
- **A corridor full of psychic static**

An aspect may give +1, grant or deny fictional permission, or function as
a source of advantage and trouble. There is as yet no dedicated
Agency-currency for buying aspects in v0.1.

## Stunts

A stunt is a narrow rule exception, usually +2 to one action in a precise
situation.

> **Institutional Paranoia:** +2 when you beat an institutional concealment
> with Notice, after first spotting a concrete discrepancy.

## Ego, Morph and Psyche

- **Ego:** the continuity of memories, personality, skills and
  information.
- **Morph:** the body, or a technical embodiment.
- **Psyche:** the experiencing actor, never assumed to be mere data.

A copy can share the same Ego data and still be a different Psyche.

## Character creation

1. Write high concept and trouble.
2. Write three aspects from history, relationships, ideology, trauma or a
   deviant situation.
3. Distribute attributes as agreed for the campaign.
4. Distribute 25 points among skills, max +4; no more skills at a higher
   level than at the preceding level.
5. Write one stunt.
6. Record important relationships, conditions and consequences.
7. Describe Ego, Morph and Psyche with necessary precision.

# 5. The Four Actions

- **Overcome:** remove an obstacle, gain access, escape, perform a task.
- **Create Advantage:** create or find an aspect, connection, clue or
  weakness.
- **Attack:** inflict stress or force the opponent to yield.
- **Defend:** prevent an Attack or a Create Advantage.

The same verbs work on the physical, social, cybernetic and noetic
layers. One event can leave a mark on several layers. In conflict, stress
arises specifically from opposed Attack and Defend results; Overcome and
Create Advantage are not treated as attacks by accident.

---

# 6. Stress, Conditions and Consequences

**Stress** is short-term pressure in a conflict. Ordinary capacity is 3.
Positive attack-shifts add stress. Stress clears after the conflict and
immediate danger.

**Condition** (tila): a persistent problem such as `orientation_migraine`,
`identity_afterimage`, `noetic_trace_detected`. It clears with suitable
treatment or a fictional change.

**Consequence:** a longer-lasting impairment that can absorb shifts:

- minor 2;
- moderate 4;
- severe 6.

Choose the consequence when hit. Subtract its severity from the attack's
shifts; only the remainder becomes stress.

Example consequences: **Broken wrist**, **Publicly unreliable**,
**Contaminated BCI driver**, **Afterimage of a foreign memory**.

## Concession and taken out

A character may concede before a forced loss and negotiate how they give
up the stakes while keeping their agency. A conceding character is not
taken out.

Taken out grants the opponent the stakes agreed in advance. It never
grants permission to kill a character, destroy an identity, or bypass the
table's safety boundaries.

---

# 7. Social Relationships

Relationships may carry values −100...+100. Use only the dimensions that
matter: trust, attraction, suspicion, hostility, loyalty, fear,
influence.

Momentary mood and the persistent relationship are different things. For
an important change, record the old value, the change, the new value, and
the event that caused it.

A choice can change a relationship without a roll. Roll only when the
reaction is genuinely uncertain.

# 8. Cyberspace

Simple cybernetic action usually uses:

`4dF + Cybernetics + Infosec`

Other relevant skills: Hardware, Program, Investigate, Infiltrate.

The cybernetic action loop:

1. gain access;
2. identify target and provenance;
3. examine nodes and edges;
4. modify, negotiate, isolate or copy;
5. exit safely.

Success may create an access edge or reveal a node. Failure may add
Trace, alert an agent, or expose an implant. Cracking, social
engineering, cyberpsi and Psyche-intrusion are different attack surfaces
even when they use the same action language.

## Cybernetic–psychotronic hacking

Hacking means understanding and changing a target system's relations,
feedback loops, permissions and vulnerabilities. The target can be a
computer, a human, an institution, or their assemblage. The same
character sheet, 4dF roll, aspects, stress and consequences work in a
hacking scene too.

### The four mandatory layers

| Layer | Contents | Typical rolls |
|---|---|---|
| Cybernetic | software, networks, implants, sensors, AIs, permissions | Cybernetics + Infosec; Intelligence + Program or Hardware |
| Psychic | attention, memory, dreams, identity, complexes, PSI links | Awareness + Investigate; Charisma + Rapport; Willpower + Will |
| Social | relationships, trust, power, reputation, roles, ideology, bureaucracy | Charisma + Rapport/Deceive/Provoke; Awareness + Kinesics |
| Biological | nervous system, drugs, sleep, hormones, pain, senses, morph | Intelligence + Medicine; Body + Somatics |

Cybernetic, social and biological layers can be modeled within ordinary
network-, systems- and biomedical fiction. The psychic layer's telepathy,
astral projection and noetic resonance are speculative Noöpunk mechanics.
In play the layers may appear to merge without an omniscient narrator
settling the ontology of the experience.

### Nodes and edges

Draw a small graph of the significant target. A **node** is a functional
target: server, neuroimplant, memory, trust relationship, living will,
sleep cycle, archetypal guardian. Mark the node's layer, visibility,
defense, and where relevant a vulnerability or a permanent effect.

An **edge** licenses movement between two nodes. Its type names the
fictional connection: data flow, trust, authority, autonomic control,
emotional association, permission, implant–brain interface, psychotronic
feedback. A player cannot move between layers merely because the nodes
are visible on the same paper — an active edge or a newly created
connection through fiction is required.

### Access levels

Access is tracked separately for each node:

1. **Observed:** the node is known, no connection exists.
2. **Contact:** communication, network session, physical contact or
   resonance.
3. **Access:** limited functional access; Exploit and Attack become
   possible.
4. **Privileged:** deep memory, control, authority or nervous-system-level
   access.
5. **Control:** temporary, contestable control.

Create Access usually raises access by one step; a critical success by
two. Control never grants a permanent right to remove an important
character's agency. Full control of a mind, body or institution is always
temporary, bounded, contestable, and consequential.

### The universal functions

**Observe** reveals nodes and edges around a node: network scan, reading
a mental state, examining bureaucracy, measuring vitals.

**Create Access** establishes or deepens access. Moving to a different
node requires an active edge and the origin node at least at Contact
level.

**Exploit** uses an access level and a node's vulnerability to accomplish
the goal: extract data, stabilize a nervous system, obtain permission,
read a foreign signal. The same node yields its permanent effect only
once.

**Create Advantage** creates an aspect: **NIGHT-SHIFT SESSION OPEN**,
**PROTECTIVE INTENT VERIFIED**, **EGOBOUNDARY WEAKENED**, **UFAB 80MG IN
THE BLOODSTREAM**. A priced success creates the advantage — and realizes
its price.

**Overcome** bypasses a single obstacle: encryption, an archetypal
guardian, a bureaucratic check, a biological feedback loop.

**Defend** authenticates, isolates, grounds, stabilizes or covers tracks.
A successful Defend reduces Alert and Trace; on a psychic target it can
reduce Feedback.

**Attack** inflicts system stress. It requires an access level. Attack is
not the default solution: most goals succeed better through observation,
access, advantages and Exploit.

**Retreat** ends the intrusion in a controlled way. Information, traces
and consequences already produced remain. High Trace can leave a Europol
or UFAB complication.

### Rolls and resistance

Use the basic test. A node's defense is the difficulty; an active
defender uses an ordinary opposed roll. Fictional positioning decides
which attribute and skill fit: an implant can be approached with
Cybernetics + Infosec, its physical maintenance hatch with Intelligence +
Hardware, its dosage effect with Intelligence + Medicine.

Invoking an aspect costs a Fate point and gives +2. A tool gives at most
+1 or +2, only to the layers and actions marked for it. What matters more
is the tool's *permission*: a diagnostic deck permits telemetry, a living
will permits psychic contact, a grounding protocol permits a safer
biological or psychic Overcome. The same fictional fact does not stack as
tool, tag, stunt and aspect.

### Pressure, detection and consequences

The core version uses four numbers:

- **Alert 0...8:** how actively the target is defending;
- **Trace 0...8:** how traceable the attacker is;
- **Feedback 0...8:** psychic or neural load returning to the attacker;
- **System stress:** pressure inflicted on the target with Attack.

A clean success does not by itself raise pressure. A priced success
advances the action but raises Alert and Trace; psychic action also
raises Feedback. Failure and catastrophe raise them more. Filling the
Alert or Feedback limit ends the intrusion in a safe failure terminal —
it does not crash the game.

A scene can hand back to the ordinary RPG: information, conditions,
consequences, wounds, relationship changes, campaign flags and graph
changes. Examples: **foreign_signal_afterimage**, **ufab_intrusion_detected**,
preserved evidence, Tanaka's increased trust, an open Europol trace.

## Tools and special abilities

A program, implant, drug, credential, ritual object, PSI technique or
social warrant uses the same model:

- name and fictional permission;
- permitted layers;
- permitted functions;
- possibly a 0...+2 bonus;
- price, risk or single-use if it matters.

Avoid gear-bonus inflation. **The night shift's temporary pass** is
interesting because it licenses social–cyber access; its +1 is a
side-note.

## Running a hacking scene

Mandatory core loop:

1. define the goal and the stakes;
2. show entry nodes in the different layers;
3. Observe reveals part of the graph;
4. Create Access forms the route;
5. change layers only through a justified edge;
6. face defenses and create advantages;
7. Exploit accomplishes the goal, or Attack pressures the target;
8. Defend or Retreat manages the traces;
9. resolve the terminal, traces and long-term consequences;
10. transfer effects to campaign state.

Always show the player the goal, known nodes and edges, access level,
Alert, Trace, Feedback, aspects, achieved sub-goals and the exit. A
hidden node is not a menu option before it is found.

### Building a scenario (for the Conductor)

Use 8–16 nodes in the first version. Give each of the four layers at
least one meaningful entry or route. Add:

- at least two solution strategies;
- one hidden objective-node;
- one defense or red herring;
- at least two cross-layer edges;
- a failure terminal and the possibility of Retreat;
- 2–4 permanent campaign consequences.

Do not build a single right route disguised as a graph. If social
engineering yields the drug-controller's credentials, it must be a real
alternative to the psychic dream-journey.

## The Sleeping Patient — ready demo scenario

A UFAB patient is stuck in an abnormal sleep. The Pilgrim–Tanaka implant
is exchanging data with an unauthorized process. Goals:
**identify_intruder** and **stabilize_patient**. The process may be
malware, a fragment of Ragnarök, a dissociated psychic construct, an
external PSI agent, or their assemblage; the scenario does not settle
this into one answer.

Entry nodes: the clinical terminal (cybernetic), Tanaka's trust (social),
the patient's nervous system (biological), the patient's conscious self
(psychic). Hidden nodes include the implant, the memory buffer, the
living will, the sleep cycle, the dream city, the archetypal guardian and
the foreign signal.

Possible routes:

- **technical:** terminal → implant → memory buffer;
- **psychic:** conscious self → dream city → guardian → foreign signal;
- **social:** Tanaka's trust → living will → conscious self;
- **biological:** nervous system → sleep cycle or UFAB 80MG concentration;
- **hybrid:** Pilgrim's authority → implant → drug controller, or foreign
  signal → memory buffer.

### A worked multi-layer example

1. Romppainen convinces Tanaka of his protective intent. Charisma +
   Rapport succeeds and creates the aspect **TANAKA SUPERVISES THE
   PROCEDURE**.
2. Tanaka's trust edge reveals the living will. Create Access opens it;
   the recorded consent permits protective contact, not memory
   rewriting.
3. Romppainen uses the clinical terminal. Cybernetics + Infosec succeeds
   at a price: the implant opens, Alert +1, Trace +1.
4. Implant Observe reveals the memory buffer and the drug controller.
   The diagnostic deck gives +1, being suitable for cybernetic Observe.
5. Exploit of the memory buffer reveals several mutually contradictory
   provenances. `identify_intruder` is fulfilled, but the sender's
   ontology stays open.
6. A psychic cross-layer edge leads to the foreign signal. Contact
   reveals the true aim but leaves the **foreign_signal_afterimage**
   condition and mutual Noetic Traces.
7. The patient's nervous system begins to overload. Intelligence +
   Medicine uses a biological Exploit and fulfills
   `stabilize_patient`.
8. Both goals are fulfilled. The RPG receives preserved evidence, new
   information, the patient's stability flag, and a possible future
   Europol complication.

## Module status

**Mandatory core:** four layers, nodes and typed edges, five access
levels, eight functions, ordinary 4dF resolution, Alert/Trace/Feedback,
terminals, and the transfer of RPG effects.

**Optional advanced:** opposed rolls for active defenders, faction
clocks, edge direction/bandwidth/latency, multi-attacker initiative and
separate consequence slots per layer. Not yet implemented in the digital
demo.

**Speculative psychotronic extension:** telepathy, dream infiltration,
archetypal defenses, noetic trace, psychic–cyber feedback. These use the
same core but are the setting's fictional assumptions, not scientific
claims.

# 9. PSI

PSI is not a spell list. It is the steering of possible trajectories in
the Conscious Agent Network. It works most easily when the world was
already almost going the desired way.

`4dF + suitable attribute + suitable skill + trajectory modifier`

| Function | Typical roll |
|---|---|
| Intuition or precognition | Awareness + Notice |
| Telepathy or resonance | Charisma + Rapport |
| Remote perception | Awareness + Notice |
| Synchronicity | Awareness + Investigate |
| Psychokinesis | Willpower + Will |
| Ritual or shared dream | Charisma + Rapport |
| Psychosurgery | Intelligence + Medicine |
| Psychic hacking or defense | Willpower + Will |

## The seven context values

Assess 0...4:

- **semantic distance:** the strangeness of the intended meaning;
- **corridor strength:** the strength of an existing connection;
- **bifurcation openness:** the situation's openness to alternatives;
- **affective salience:** emotional significance;
- **composite-agent coherence:** the group's shared coherence;
- **instrumentality:** the compulsion to act as deterministic
  telecommunication;
- **local noise:** environmental noise and contradiction.

`support = corridor + openness + salience + coherence`

`resistance = distance + instrumentality + noise`

`trajectory modifier = (support − resistance) / 2`, rounded toward zero,
clamped to −4...+4.

## Read, participate, steer

- **Read:** perceive and minimize back-reaction.
- **Participate:** accept the shared space and its consequences.
- **Steer:** deliberately favor a possible trajectory.

Before an important roll, state what you are asking, your intention, the
trajectory you seek, and what you refuse to sacrifice. Successive
questions are not independent scans; the order can change the situation.

## PSI pressures

- **Psyche Load:** experiential strain;
- **Noetic Trace:** a route that can be followed back;
- **Instability:** uncertainty of identity and layer boundaries.

Telepathy is not a text message. Separate the sender's intent, the
transmitted structure, the receiver's experience, and the residual
uncertainty.

## Psychotronics and antipsi

A psychotronic device amplifies a conscious operator's PSI; it is not an
autonomous magic machine. Describe its permission, rating, operator,
target layer, price, Trace and failure mode. Antipsi can be shielding, a
dampener, a BCI consent gate, a decoy, medication, isolation or a
provenance logger.

# 10. Psychosurgery and Projections

Psychosurgery combines Jungian analysis, powerful psychedelic
intervention, BCI/VR/AGI therapy and psychotronics.

It can reveal an aspect, change an aspect with consent, add a condition,
heal a consequence with consent, show an uncertain memory fragment, or
create a resonance edge. The session log does not settle whether the
experience was a dream, a DMT state, VR, an AGI simulation or a noetic
projection.

A projection's light loop:

1. **Enter:** prepare body, mind and anchor.
2. **Observe:** gather perceptions and hypotheses.
3. **Traverse:** advance and grow the distance.
4. **Resonate or manifest:** affect another actor or layer.
5. **Return:** come back before coherence or the anchor breaks.

Track coherence, anchor, Trace and distance where relevant. `Lost` means
a new conflict or a rescue mission — not automatically eternal soul-loss.

# 11. NHI Encounters

NHI existence can be certain while the nature of the encounter remains a
mystery. Separate:

- **physical trace:** what the body or instrument detected;
- **experience:** what the witness experienced before explanation;
- **interface:** the cultural form the phenomenon took;
- **symbolic function:** which meaning or affect the form activated;
- **social effect:** who spreads, denies or exploits the story;
- **control feedback:** how the interpretation changes the later system;
- **residual ambiguity:** what stays open.

A physical trace does not prove extraterrestrial origin. A psychological
effect does not prove the experience imagined. A mythological name is not
a certain species identification.

# 12. Resources, Factions and Advancement

Money, services, permissions, bandwidth and institutional position get a
number or tag only when an encounter uses them. Reputation is recorded
per network.

A faction needs a goal, influence, resources, important relationships and
a project clock. Between play-days one significant project usually
advances one step. Completion shows up as a scene, a news item or a
network change.

A significant milestone grants one advancement point. A point raises one
skill by one, max +4. Changing an aspect, a new stunt, or recovery from a
severe consequence requires a suitable story event. Attributes do not
rise in v0.1.

# 13. Conductor's Guidelines

Before a roll, clarify intent, obstacle, what success changes and what
failure changes. If failure would only mean "nothing happens", do not
roll.

Always grant the necessary clue. The roll decides the price, precision,
timing, provenance, or who notices the investigation.

An institution can have aspects, goals and a project clock. Ask who
programmed the network's goal, connects the networks, guards access and
maintains the protocol.

Preserve warmth and humor. Bad coffee, Nordic bureaucracy, romantic
awkwardness and small kindnesses are as Noöpunk as metaphysical horror.

## Safety

Agree on the table's boundaries for psychiatric treatment, drugs, changes
of identity and memory, sexuality, consent, body-swapping, trauma and
existential horror. Use a suitable table interrupt and content-boundary
method. A player never has to justify their boundary.

# 14. Example

Romppainen investigates the UFAB registry. He spotted the discrepancy, so
**Institutional Paranoia** applies.

- Awareness +2;
- Notice +2;
- stunt +2;
- 4dF: −1;
- total +5.

The concealment's difficulty is +6. Margin −1: success at a price.
Romppainen finds the aspect **UFAB record mismatch**, but the system logs
the unusual query and UFAB gains the aspect **Romppainen under quiet
observation**. The clue is not withdrawn; action changes the situation.

# 15. Quick Reference

`4dF + attribute + skill + bonus vs. difficulty`

- +3 or more: critical success;
- 0...+2: success;
- −1...−2: success at a mandatory price;
- −3...−4: failure, but the situation changes;
- −5 or less: severe consequence, no dead end.

Four actions: Overcome, Create Advantage, Attack, Defend.

Four layers: Physical, Social, Cyber, Noetic.

PSI: follow the trajectory, track Psyche Load, Noetic Trace and
Instability, and do not settle the ontology with one roll.

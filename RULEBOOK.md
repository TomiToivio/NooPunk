# Noöpunk: Ubiquitous Maniacs

## Eclipse Phase Second Edition Rules Add-On v0.2

*Noöpunk: Ubiquitous Maniacs is a psychedelic, transhumanist and
sociological science fiction campaign of people, machines,
institutions and consciousnesses that do not fit inside their own
borders. It combines the Nordic institutional tragicomedy, cyberpunk,
space opera, psychological horror, network science, and a dangerous
but hopeful cosmism.*

**This campaign runs on Eclipse Phase Second Edition rules**
(Posthuman Studios, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), modded, reskinned and
simplified. The full base rules are in the EP2e core book
[eclipsephase.com](https://eclipsephase.com/). This document covers
only what Noöpunk changes, adds or reskins.

*Tommy Pilgrim saw this story in 1974. The campaign is the vision
arriving on schedule. See BOOK.md, "The Chapter That Keeps
Disappearing."*

# 1. What Carries Over From EP2e

Noöpunk uses the EP2e engine as received:

- **Core mechanics:** d100 roll under (skill + aptitude ×5 ±
  modifiers); Superior/Major/Excellent outcomes; criticals on 00/99.
- **Seven aptitudes** (reskinned — see §2): Cognition, Intuition,
  Reflexes, Somatics, Savvy, Willpower, plus Noöpunk's own
  **Cybernetics** (the BCI link as its own aptitude).
- **Ego and Morph are separate.** The ego is the person; the morph is
  the body. You are not your body. In 2026 Sweden, most egos still
  ride flat morphs (original biological bodies), which is why
  psychosurgery on the ego's *brain* is both primitive and terrifying:
  there is no backup.
- **Stress and Trauma:** EP2e stress/trauma tracks represent mental
  damage. Noöpunk adds the **Coherence** reskin (§4).
- **Flex, pools and rep networks** carry over; see §5 for the Rhizome
  reskin.
- **Psi:** EP2e asyncs and sleights carry over as the game's baseline
  for psychic ability; Noöpunk adds the QIP reskin (§6).
- **Muse:** every character has a muse (personal AI assistant). At
  UFAB, the muses answer to the building.

# 2. The Reskin: Noöpunk Aptitudes

Noöpunk renames and retunes the EP2e aptitudes. Mechanics unchanged;
ontology shifted. The canonical list (from the campaign's design
notes, kept stable):

| EP2e aptitude | Noöpunk | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| Cognition | **Intelligence (INT)** | logical and analytical thinking, problem-solving, planning, abstraction, technical and scientific understanding |
| Intuition | **Awareness (AWR)** | quality of consciousness: intuition, empathy, loving kindness, presence, perception of subtle psychological and noetic phenomena |
| Reflexes | **Reflexes (REF)** | reaction speed, coordination, balance, fine motor skills, sensorimotor action |
| Somatics | **Body (BOD)** | using the morph's physical properties: strength, endurance, bodily control |
| Savvy | **Charisma (CHA)** | social capability: reading situations, persuasion, manipulation, negotiation, performance |
| Willpower | **Willpower (WIL)** | willpower, self-control, psychological endurance, coherence of one's own mind, resisting outside influence |
| (no direct source) | **Cybernetics (CYB)** | acting through cybernetic systems: cyberspace, implants, BCI, machine egos, digital agents, the human–machine interface |

**Psyche is not an aptitude.** In Noöpunk, Psyche is the character's
ontological consciousness: a Conscious Agent, a non-local field
component (quantum information panpsychism, in-world). Awareness
measures how well the Psyche *perceives and understands*; Willpower
measures the Psyche's *force and resistance*. Psi's raw power runs on
Willpower; psychic perception runs on Awareness.

**No Fall, different Earth.** Noöpunk has not had an apocalypse. In
2026 most of humanity still lives in flat morphs. EP2e technologies
exist, but Earth has not burned. What differs is the paradigm:
**limited contact with the Zookeepers** (a non-human intelligence
civilization) has already happened, quietly, and panpsychist quantum
consciousness is mainstream-enough science that Uppsala builds
industry on it. The Empire's denial of the psychic layer is the
campaign's central lie.

# 3. Character Creation (EP2e with Noöpunk Reskin)

1. **Ego:** EP2e ego creation. Choose aptitude array; the seven
   aptitudes of §2. 25 skill points on the EP2e skill list, mapped to
   the Noöpunk aptitudes (Infosec and Program map to **Cybernetics**,
   not Cognition; Kinesics and Xeno-Contact map to **Awareness**).
2. **Morph:** flat, splicer, or (for lab staff) a basic pod. Synthetic
   masks — see §7 — are *social* constructs, not morphs.
3. **Aspects:** three aspects in EP2e style, but each must name one of
   the **seven layers** of the character (§5), tying the aspect to a
   layer the campaign will stress.
4. **Faction:** EP2e factions reskinned; the campaign's default
   factions are the two below. **The Panpsychic Cyborg Multitude
   (PCM)** is a playable faction — see §8.
5. **Psi:** optional. An async ego takes the EP2e psi trait; in
   Noöpunk, awakening is usually *induced* — see UFAB 80MG (§6).

# 4. Coherence (reskin of Stress/Trauma)

The campaign's mental health system is EP2e stress/trauma with the
dials renamed and one addition:

- **Stress** accumulates from horror, betrayal, dissociation, and
  exposure to UFAB 80MG. Reskinned as **Coherence loss**: the mind's
  ability to hold itself together as one story.
- **Trauma** disorders as per EP2e. Reskinned as **fragmentation**:
  the mind splits into personas that answer to different stories.
- **New rule — dissociation armor:** a character with an implanted
  persona (§7) may shift Coherence loss from an event onto their
  hidden persona *once per session*, if they accept one step of
  fragmentation. The persona is a load-bearing wall. You may not know
  which side of the wall you live on.

# 5. The Rhizome (reskin of Rep Networks)

EP2e rep networks carry over with one reskin and one addition:

- **The Rhizome Networks:** @-rep, f-rep and the rest are the game's
  reputation economies. The rhizome networks are where crews, aid,
  lodging and gear-from-the-commons live. The campaign's crews are
  nodes in the Rhizome; the campaign is chains of equivalence.
- **The Rhizome Pool:** any character may Flex *into a shared pool*
  — the Rhizome Pool — which anyone in the crew may spend, with or
  without the contributor's consent. The pool is the game's politics
  dial: a crew that feeds the pool is building the commons; a crew
  that drains it is extracting. The pool's size is tracked openly;
  the GM may call for a test to keep the pool alive when the Empire
  pushes (it always pushes).

# 6. Psi and UFAB 80MG (QIP reskin of EP2e Psi)

EP2e psi carries over: asyncs, sleights, strain. Noöpunk adds the
theory and the drug:

- **Quantum Information Panpsychism (in-world science):** the psychic
  layer is a non-local field of consciousness; brains are its
  interface, not its source. Awareness and Willpower are the
  interface's two handles. The Zookeepers have been in limited
  contact for years; the patents tell you the shape of the trying.
- **Psi operates through seven context values** (the campaign's
  graph-hacking layer): read, participate, steer — any noetic action
  on a mind, a network or a room moves along those verbs. Sleights
  from EP2e work as written; the GM prices noetic actions on the
  graph in strain.
- **UFAB 80MG:** the campaign's central artifact. In-world: a
  pharmaceutical that increases microtubule quantum sensitivity,
  weakens sensory and autobiographical anchoring, opens the mind to
  outside modulation, and makes consciousnesses *entrainable*. In
  play: a character dosed with 80MG takes a **Coherence risk** every
  scene in which it is active; success opens a temporary sleight (GM
  picks by story), failure opens the mind to *outside* modulation —
  the drug is a door and the door is quantum. Street 80MG is cut with
  amphetamine; the lab grade is worse in every interesting way.
- **A successful séance never proves which psi theory is true.** Psi
  works in the game; the metaphysics stay ambiguous. This is
  canonical.

# 7. Synthetic Personas (reskin of Ego Hacking)

The campaign's signature mechanism, built from EP2e ego-hacking
rules:

- A **synthetic persona** is an implanted second identity: a working
  kit of memories, habits, tells and taste, installed by
  psychosurgery, running on the ego's own brain (flat morph — the
  horror of 2026 is that the brain is the only substrate).
- Mechanics: the persona is written as an **aspect with a switch**.
  The player may invoke it for cover, contacts and competence within
  its story. Compelling it is the GM's bread and butter: stress,
  witnesses from both lives, and mirrors crack the wall.
- **Persona bleed:** under Coherence loss, persona traits leak into
  the base ego (and vice versa). Track bleed as fragmentation (§4).
  A character who fully fragments has *two* egos and one brain; the
  GM plays whichever the character is not currently wearing.
- **Consent is a mechanical limit:** psychosurgery cannot overwrite
  identity without the campaign's consent rules (below). Romppainen
  consented to a cover story. Nobody consented to what the cover
  story became.

**Consent (canonical):** you may threaten or manipulate a character
in conflict, but identity-changing treatment portrayed as therapy
does not bypass consent. The campaign's tragedy is that UFAB
redefined consent until the word fit the form.

# 8. The Panpsychic Cyborg Multitude (playable faction)

The **Panpsychic Cyborg Multitude** is the Noöpunk world's
revolutionary form: a rhizome of rhizomes, a network of networks
composed of humans, machines and fields, fighting the Empire for the
ownership of attention, meaning and mind. In the campaign it is a
faction — underground, half-visible, denied by every press office —
and a playable one:

- **Members:** biological nodes and cybernetic nodes; crews of
  cyborgs; singularities that remain singular while acting together.
- **What they fight for:** the commons on every layer — physical
  (data centers), social (cooperation), economic (the pool),
  linguistic (the naming), cybernetic (the interface), psychic (the
  one layer the other six cannot fully surveil).
- **Mechanics:** PCM crew members pool Flex into the Rhizome Pool
  (§5) at creation; PCM factions may spend pool Flex to *articulate*
  — weld grievances into chains of equivalence, name the wave, take
  protocols. Five welded elements tip a signifier and grant ±10 to
  the tipping side for the scene.
- **The seven layers:** every agent — human, machine, hybrid, animal,
  field — is profiled across seven layers (physical, biological,
  social, economic, linguistic, cybernetic, psychic). Every conflict
  in the campaign is, at bottom, a conflict between layers. The GM
  prices layer-crossing actions on this table.

*(The book's Chapter Five specifies the seven layers in full; the
game uses the same list. What the players simulate, the reader can
build.)*

# 9. Psychosurgery (the Four Layers, canonical)

Psychosurgery in Noöpunk canonically combines four inseparable
traditions and technical layers. EP2e psychosurgery rules carry over
as the mechanical chassis; the campaign's synthesis is the content:

1. **Jungian psychoanalysis** — archetypes, complexes, shadow,
   individuation, dreams, the collective unconscious: the symbolic
   and therapeutic grammar.
2. **High-dose psychedelic intervention** — UFAB 80MG and cousins:
   destabilize ordinary perception, ego boundaries; open access to
   the inner world. Bodily, psychological, consent and safety
   consequences stay on the table.
3. **Cybernetic therapy** — BCI, VR, AGI mediation: the feedback and
   interface layer.
4. **Psychotronics** — quantum-panpsychist technology that
   reproduces, focuses and amplifies natural PSI and interfaces with
   the Conscious Agent Network.

Patients in psychosurgery occupy an indeterminate space between an
ordinary dream, constructed virtual reality, a DMT-like entity realm
and psychic astral projection. These are competing interpretations of
the same session. The game never authoritatively answers which layer
was real. Evidence may be gathered; the mystery may not be collapsed.
This is the campaign's ontological spine — preserved from EP2e's
uncertainty, deepened into the setting's method.

# 10. The Campaign: Ubiquitous Maniacs

**Premise.** An undercover operative of the merged Suojelupoliisi/
Europol apparatus (byzantine accident: two organizations, one
commissioner) has infiltrated Uppsala Folkhälsovårdsläkemedelsbolag
AB — the Nordic-European psychiatric, neurological, pharmaceutical,
psychotronic and consciousness-research complex — as a lab test
subject. His original identity was hidden by psychosurgical
dissociation: at game start, he *believes* he is Sven-Olof
"Finnjävel-Pirinisti" Romppainen, a Stockholm junkie-dealer. He
tracked the street drug UFAB 80MG to the factory; the drug has a
connection to the Xeno/X-Risk layer (the Zookeepers' limited contact
makes this plausible); he got in through the door marked test
subjects.

**The support team** (Europol/Suojelupoliisi): a SWAT officer, a
hacker, a telepath — outside the walls, on the graph, slowly losing
sight of the person they sent in.

**The loop** (one playable day at a time):

```
routine → social interaction → therapy/experimentation
        → investigation → dreams/altered states
```

**Persona progression** (the wall cracks in stages):

- Days 1–2: unstable junkie/stoner persona;
- Days 3–4: manic police-comedy persona;
- Days 5–7: increasingly the original Romppainen — and the question
  of which one that is becomes undecidable.

**The cast** (full NPC writeups live with the campaign notes):

- **Emil Romppainen / "Finnjävel-Pirinisti"** — Suojelupoliisi's
  best expert on Noösphere threats; Jungian psychoanalyst; addict in
  his own cover. May appear in the simulation as a decayed
  Väinämöinen: a broken synthesizer for a kantele, UFAB packets for
  a sampo. Ingrid is his anima; he is her animus; their shadows are
  structurally identical — intelligent, hedonistic, manipulative,
  dangerously good at justifying their own desires. Together they
  can form a corrective psychotechnological assemblage; in a bad
  development, the shadows reinforce each other and both drown.
- **Ingrid Falk** — the central patient. Not crazy. The tests say
  crazy. The man who wrote the tests believes them. The tragedy is
  *almost*.
- **Dr. Søren Pilgrim** — head researcher. Claims to have proven
  Orch-OR experimentally. His practical breakthrough: you don't need
  to reach the consciousness field directly if you can hack its
  biological interface. Named himself into the book (the name is a
  translation of the author's own). Hates Sweden and Swedes
  pathologically; speaks Danish to them at maximum accent. May
  appear in the simulation as Odin.
- **Dr. Yuki Tanaka** — the ethical psychiatrist, in practice
  running the project as Søren becomes steadily less competent and
  more dangerous.
- **Dr. Elin Dahl** — intake therapist.
- **Lotta Kallio** — Romppainen's former partner on the security
  side.
- **UFAB Ragnarök** — the quantum-conscious AGI persona, the

- **Irungu Wakini** — Kenyan patient and reggae mystic.
- **Björn Starck** — too cheerful, too extroverted, socially too

**Influences** (canon): the TV series *Maniac* and *Riget* are the

material consequence; *Eclipse Phase* for the rules chassis;
Citizen Sleeper, Midnight Protocol, DROP - System Breach, VA-11
Hall-A and The Red Strings Club for mechanics and mood.

# 11. GM Guidance

- **Do not collapse the mystery early.** Competing interpretations
  are the game's engine. When in doubt, add a layer, do not remove
  one.
- **The graph:** track relationships, access, trauma, history and
  noetic resonance as edges. You do not need to draw everything;
  write down the relationships that matter and their histories.
- **Price layer-crossings.** Physical, biological, social, economic,
  linguistic, cybernetic, psychic: any power crossing layers pays in
  strain, time, exposure or consent.
- **The vision is an oracle.** When the table cannot decide, open
  BOOK.md at the Uppsala vision and read the next line as true. The
  line will be wrong. It will be *almost* right. That is the
  campaign.
- **Safety:** the campaign portrays psychiatric institutions, drug
  use, surveillance, identity surgery and dissociation. Use session
  zero tools; lines and veils are mandatory; consent mechanics in
  fiction mirror the consent rules in §7 — *the story is about what
  happens when that mirror breaks.*

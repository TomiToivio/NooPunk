# NEW PROPOSAL: Native Astral Body and cross-domain PSI (Physical / Cyber / Astral)

## 1. Main architectural decision

**Proposed name: Astral Body** (preferred over Astral Projection). Astral Projection is the action/state of using and separating the native Astral Body, while **Astral Body** names the trained ability to perceive, move, survive, navigate and take Astral-specific actions. Avoid the tautological phrase “Astral Projection under Astral Projection.”

- **Physical, Cyber and Astral are equally important gameplay domains.** The PC remains one conscious agent / actor with a physical embodiment, digital interfaces and a native Astral Body. The player should be able to **perceive and interact across multiple domains in the same scene**, subject to explicit interface, simultaneous-attention, device and action limits. This does *not* mean the PC can perform three full turns, see everything, or automatically target any realm.
- **Densities 1–3** are natively Physical. **4th-density humans** are natively Astral and have a substantive Astral Body. Their lower-density physical/biological inheritance remains as **legacy layers**, not erased from the person. A 3rd-density PC's access to an Astral Body is a character/campaign eligibility question, not proof the PC has already attained 4th density.
- The **same actor ID** spans Physical, Cyber, Astral and Social/Story graphs. Astral occupants are not location nodes; the physical body remains anchored when projection separates the viewpoint. Preserve all existing perception and targeting checks, and distinguish simultaneous sensing/agency from separated projection.
- **Astral Body** should *not* model all Quantum Information Panpsychism (QIP) and Law of One (LoO) philosophy. Those are **setting-wide conceptual frameworks** shaping many psychic abilities, technologies, institutions and identities.

## 2. Domain ownership and proposed PSI taxonomy

| Ability / skill | Primary responsibility | Works where? | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Astral Body** (proposed rename/re-scope) | Astral perception, embodiment, locomotion/flight, navigation, barriers and Astral-body physicality/defense where approved | Astral-specific; with a clearly defined perceptual bridge to Physical/Cyber | Not generic communication, quantum-information CRUD, Telepathy or universal manipulation |
| **Telepathy** (existing SWN) | Communicate with **conscious agents**; eventually nuanced contact/ROTE only if separately authorized and compatible with its published technique boundaries | Can cross Astral, Physical and Cyber *when* the same psychic connection and target authorization actually exist | Cyber avatar or AI process is not automatically proven conscious; encryption and AntiPSI remain relevant |
| **Telekinesis** (existing SWN) | Manipulate appropriate target matter/forces as the published technique allows | Domain by target/effect, not a new “Astral Telekinesis” clone | Does not imply universal quantum editing |
| **Metapsionics / AntiPSI** (existing SWN first) | Resist, detect, disrupt or counter applicable psychic effects; cyber-BCI and Astral wards need explicit adapters | Cross-domain when a specified effect applies | No unrestricted immunity or duplication of existing core techniques |
| **Cyber PSI / Electrokinesis** (proposed, design only) | Psychic interface with **electronic systems, fields, devices and BCIs**; possible new cyber-psi techniques | Cyber and physical electronics, with bounded Astral/QIP bridges if authorized | Not free CWN Program replacement, authentication bypass, forced mind-reading or general network omnipotence |
| **QIP / LoO knowledge** | Interpret conscious-agent models, psi physics/metaphysics, Law of One doctrine, research, rituals and institutions | **Know** skill subject specializations rather than new mandatory psychic tracks | Knowing a theory never grants a psi effect |
| **StO / StS, Social Memory Complex** | Ethical orientation, faction/social evolution and potential group/collective psychic phenomena | Cross-domain narrative and research, with *specific* proposed PSI techniques only where mechanically warranted | StO/StS are **not** attack powers or hard moral alignment/class labels by default; do not automatically give a character mind control or group-mind membership |

**Proposal for StO/StS:** treat **Service to Others (StO)** and **Service to Self (StS)** initially as setting ideology, choices, social relationships and faction-level consequences, not another psychic skill. For **Social Memory Complex**, prototype **voluntary group cognition / shared memory / collective coordination** as *opt-in authored* Telepathy/group-PSI research; preserve autonomy, consent, identity, privacy and defenses. A true enduring Social Memory Complex is an exceptional 4th-density development, not a rookie's free group-chat spell.

**Proposed Know specializations:** `Know (Quantum Information Panpsychism)`, `Know (Law of One / Density Metaphysics)`, and optionally `Know (Psychotronics)`. These use the **existing CWN Know** resolution, not three new core skills or magic resource pools. They should unlock understanding, useful research interpretations and dialogue options, but never independently activate PSI.

## 3. Design lessons: Technomancer and Electrokinesis (inspiration, not mechanical imports)

- **Shadowrun Technomancer:** Technomancers use *complex forms* and *sprites* to interact with the Matrix and can perform decker-like tasks through unusual Resonance abilities. Inspires cyber-native psychic interfaces, psychic service/construct agents and a recognizable *specialized* cyber role. In NoöPunk, **CWN Program, credentials, cyber topology, alarm/evidence trails, existing hacking actions and security remain authoritative**. No Shadowrun dice/Resonance/sprite system import. Source: https://shadowrunsixthworld.com/2019/07/17/the-matrix-in-sr6/
- **PSI-Punk Electrokinesis:** its rule examples concern manipulating/disabling electronic devices, with difficulty sensitive to complexity and security; *Cyberpsi* adds an explicitly computer-focused variation. Inspires graduated sensory access, localized electronic interference, direct BCI/electronic control where expressly permitted and countermeasures. **Do not port Fudge numerical scales or allow psychic access to bypass secured systems by default**. Source: https://fudgesrd.opengamingnetwork.com/psi-punk/psionics-and-magic/electrokinesis/
- **Cyber PSI exercise:** (1) Notice/ESP senses anomalous electronic or psi activity; (2) Program or explicit Cyber PSI technique accesses *one authorized target capability*; (3) Telepathy engages a conscious agent only with its own rules and consent/resistance; (4) AntiPSI and cyber defenses can intervene; (5) leave cyber logs, ambiguous psi impressions and admissible evidence as **distinct** outputs. Neither an AI service nor a BCI automatically permits access to an underlying consciousness.

## 4. Three-domain simultaneous play: proposed interaction contract

Represent **one actor** with up to three *interface presences*, rather than three separate PCs or turns:

1. **Physical**: body/location, sensors, CWN actions and biological vulnerability.
2. **Cyber**: accessed devices, terminals, BCI/avatars, CWN Program and cyber permissions.
3. **Astral**: native Astral Body/location or near-physical perception; Astral Body skill, Astral contacts/obstacles and applicable PSI.

A PC can **receive cues from multiple domains during one investigation or action scene**, and can choose a relevant action in any *currently available* domain. Any **active cross-domain combination** needs a defined action cost, concurrent attention/occupancy, authorized interface/connection and target permission. No free extra full action, no automatic triple-location travel, no unattended duplication of identity, and no skill-check bypass. Physical and astral bodies may coexist as *layers*, but their positions and physical action rights are tracked separately when the viewpoint is projected.

**Example:** A field agent examines an ordinary server room (Physical), queries the security system via a lawful cyber connection (Cyber), and senses an anomalous conscious-agent presence at the location (Astral). A Telepathy technique contacts the agent if eligible, whether the agent's apparent interface is physical, Astral or Cyber. Astral Body only covers the Astral sensing/positioning/interaction component. Every evidentiary claim requires conventional investigation corroboration.

## 5. Campaign scope: deliberately narrow

**Only the Astral near-physical/Lower Astral campaign scope is in play.** Devachanic, Etheric, Buddhic, Akashic Records, deep cosmological rank ladders, timeline editing and full 5th–7th-density mechanics are **worldbuilding / deferred research**, not this campaign's implementation targets. Retain 4th-density natives and legacy 1st–3rd-density layers as lore that informs present encounters.

**Proposed initial ability scope:** focus on Astral Body ranks **0–1** (basic perception/entry and Astral navigation, with authored dream access). Shift **Conscious-Agent communication** and candidate **ROTE** abilities out of Astral Body's ownership into a **Telepathy design review**; do not silently migrate or change existing published Telepathy powers. The former all-in-one rank 2–4 proposal is **superseded as a design target**, but earlier text stays below for traceability.

# Astral Projection rules: Space/Time, Time/Space, quantum-information interfaces and the Astral Graph

> **Status:** Author-directed rules/design issue. This is a new, focused implementation specification, **not** authorization to rebuild the immutable *Cities Without Number* core or add a freely selectable psychic power. Treat the cosmology below as **NoöPunk fictional ontology**, informed by Faggin, Hoffman, the Law of One, Jung and OOBE traditions, not as experimentally established physics. Review author-facing rules and narrative examples before implementing unfamiliar canonical content.

## Objective

Define a **small, playable Astral Projection procedure** for authored OOBE/NDE/psychotronic scenes, compatible with the existing CWN + SWN/AWN overlays, physical/cyber/social/story graph architecture and the deferred Astral Graph. The first playable use case is a **near-physical Astral Projection** in which the PC can perceive an astral overlay of ordinary spacetime, encounter astral agents, and optionally shift into a conceptual/inner Space.

Do **not** implement an infinite-dimensional Hilbert-space simulator, unrestricted remote surveillance, automatic time travel, an open-world afterlife, or seven separate geographical maps.

## 1. Core ontology: one informational reality, multiple perceptual interfaces

NoöPunk calls the underlying domain **Noöspace** (colloquially **the Astral Plane**), in keeping with #119. In-world terminology synthesizes Federico Faggin's quantum information/consciousness vocabulary and Donald Hoffman's conscious-agent/interface vocabulary:

1. **Physical Space:** Ordinary embodied interface to a constrained three-dimensional spatial experience within physical spacetime (the perceptual 'headset'). Information is experienced as particles, matter and objects.
2. **Information Space:** Multidimensional quantum information and communication among conscious agents. In-world theorists model it with an effectively infinite-dimensional Hilbert space. It can be experienced without rendering everything as material objects.
3. **Consciousness Space:** An agent's subjective interior, the felt **qualia** and meaning of information. It is not merely an additional geometric coordinate.

**Astral Projection / OOBE:** Conscious experience becomes decoupled from the ordinary bodily point of view and navigates nonlocal Noöspace. The character's actual physical body remains at its physical node. A projection is a different *interface to the same informational reality*, not a second body moving through another conventional 3D universe.

**Crucial perceptual limitation:** A human does not instantly perceive Hilbert-space structure faithfully upon projection. Their familiar biological/psychological headset still renders rooms, bodies, landscapes, voices and symbols. Learning to perceive the **Conscious Agent Network exchanging quantum information** is a rare authored development, not an automatic ability or default player control.

The three 'Spaces' above are **descriptive epistemic levels**, not three new travel graphs or three compulsory character statistics.

## 2. Space/Time versus Time/Space: orthogonal perceptual modes

**Space/Time (near-physical Astral):**
- Corresponds closely to a physical location, but is **not identical to the material plane**.
- In projection, characters may perceive **physical places / entities and astral entities** through one composite interface.
- Ordinary Helsinki can appear overlaid with projections, thoughtforms, psychic residues, or distortions.
- The physical address is a useful **anchor/reference**; it must not imply every locked physical door is automatically traversable or every physical fact is reliably visible.
- The astral projection may be perceptually present beside the physical body while remaining nonmaterial and unable to exert an ordinary CWN physical action without an explicitly authorized PSI/device/script effect.

**Time/Space (metaphysical / inner Noöspace):**
- Perception foregrounds **qualia, concepts, memory, archetypes, individual consciousness and collective unconsciousness**.
- An agent can perceive astral/conscious agents, symbolic structures and meaning relations, but **not physical objects directly as physical objects**.
- Ordinary physical geography becomes irrelevant: 'distance' is resonance, meaning, informational accessibility, entanglement or relationship, rather than meters.
- Time can be nonlinear in experience, but **does not imply mechanical precognition or editable historical timelines**.
- A symbolic representation of a physical place is not direct physical sight and does not automatically provide verifiable physical evidence.

These are **modes of representation/access within Noöspace**, not separate cosmological substances. A story can shift modes by an authored transition; no automatic free switching in the initial playable scope.

### Suggested perception contract

| State | Physical entities | Astral/conscious agents | Spatial navigation | Conceptual navigation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Embodied Physical | Ordinary senses/CWN | Normally no direct sight, unless an allowed effect | Physical Graph | No |
| Projected Astral, Space/Time | Perceptible near-physical representation (fallible) | Yes, as rendered by the interface | Astral locations with physical anchors | Only via authored gateways |
| Projected Astral, Time/Space | **No direct physical perception** | Yes | Physical coordinates do not determine routes | Symbolic/informational links |
| Returned to body | Ordinary senses/CWN | Only with existing abilities | Physical Graph | No |

A dreamed or remembered physical scene in Time/Space remains a **symbolic/mental representation**, not physical line of sight. Story-level exceptions must be explicit and not silently alter general rules.

## 3. Planes and seven densities: do not conflate the axes

In the proposed NoöPunk mapping:

- **Lower Astral:** nightmare-like/hellish experiential regions, which may overlap low-density astral experience. Not every nightmare is a genuine astral location.
- **Physical:** ordinary spacetime; the principal mode for **1st–3rd-density** beings.
- **Astral planes:** associated with **densities 1–4**, ordinarily secondary to physical lives at densities 1–3; **4th-density** life is more properly anchored in Astral experience.
- **Devachanic plane:** **5th density**.
- **Etheric plane:** **6th density**.
- **Buddhic plane:** **7th density**.

Also preserve the author's **primary-experience distinction**:
- **Densities 1–5** primarily experience **Space/Time**.
- **Densities 6–7** primarily experience **Time/Space**.

There is **no contradiction** between a 4th-density being belonging to an Astral plane and primarily experiencing Space/Time: **plane/density** describes its ontological/experiential level, while **mode** describes *how* it experiences that level. Physical-vs-Astral primary residence is yet another distinction from Space/Time-vs-Time/Space perception.

Treat the density/plane table as **setting correspondence**, not as seven new maps, numeric power levels or a guarantee that a low-density visitor can enter an advanced plane. Higher-density entities/encounters remain authored content. The 6th–7th mapping does not automatically grant PCs direct Time/Space or teleportation.

## 4. Small playable rules, not a second PSI engine

**Access remains scripted**, consistent with #1178 and #1196:
- Spontaneous OOBE while sleeping or dreaming.
- Near-death experience (NDE).
- Special psychotronic / Monroe-inspired projection apparatus or authorized scenario.
- Rare contact/plot events explicitly authored by Tomi.

Astral Projection is **not a selectable SWN discipline, no seventh psychic discipline, no general skill tree and no player-at-will travel button**. PC-selectable SWN disciplines remain Telepathy and Telekinesis; preserve the published SWN Psychic Training Focus and separate AWN/CWN options where authorized. A scripted event can occur without conscious PSI skills, but does not unlock new permanent powers.

**Minimal scene-state proposal:**
- `actor_id` (same canonical character across graphs and views)
- `embodiment_state`: `embodied | projected | returned`
- `physical_body_location_ref`: fixed Physical Graph location while projected
- `astral_location_ref`: optional, only for actually navigable Astral scenes
- `perceptual_mode`: `space_time | time_space`
- `perception_interface`: familiar headset by default; altered perception only when authored
- `entry_trigger`, `scene_id`, `exit_condition`, `allowed_actions`
- `memory_clues` / `interpretations`: separate player-visible recollections from GM-only truth or uncertainty
- `effects`: only explicitly approved story consequences; no default physical injuries, remote evidence capture or cross-plane manipulation

Do not needlessly persist a separate soul entity, second character sheet, astral HP/Effort meter, new class, or automatic 'astral combat'.

**Flow:** trigger → project / establish physical body anchor → set mode and optional astral location → describe only what the interface permits → choose between authored interactions/navigation → return by defined exit → record memories/consequences. Support interrupted projection, disorientation and unreliable interpretation through narration unless an actual rules need is demonstrated. NDE is not a replacement for CWN injury/death/revival rules.

### Why this differs from ordinary cyberspace

Physical, Cyber and Astral are **entangled views** of a shared world but different graph semantics:
- **Physical Graph:** place nodes; ordinary material occupants and CWN actions.
- **Cyber Graph:** device/service nodes; hacker avatars can navigate network links.
- **Astral Graph:** **astral location nodes**; projected conscious agents are **occupants**, not nodes. A near-physical astral node may reference a physical location, but is not that same node.
- **Social Graph:** actual characters/factions are nodes; relations are not travel edges.
- **Story Graph:** scenes and transitions organize authored events.

Only create **Astral nodes and edges** when a scene genuinely involves navigation. A symbolic Time/Space setting may be a narrated Story Scene rather than a graph. If graph navigation is needed, its edges express **meaning/resonance/access**, not physical distance. Never turn a Conscious Agent into a *location node* simply because Noöspace is modeled as a conscious-agent network: distinguish the ontology/network-of-agents explanation from **gameplay navigation graph** semantics.

## 5. Proposed introductory playable experiences

**A. Helsinki bedroom OOBE (Space/Time):** The PC sees the sleeping body and a familiar room with distorted details, perceives an astral occupant, chooses to observe or return. Physical body stays put; astral overlay gives neither perfect real-time reconnaissance nor physical interaction.

**B. Dream Helsinki gateway (Time/Space):** A projected character passes from a near-physical Helsinki streetscape into a symbolic university corridor. The route follows personal and collective associations, not street coordinates. The PC sees conscious agents and symbols but cannot directly inspect physical objects beyond the gateway. A remembered sign can become a question or clue, not automatically verified evidence.

**C. Psychotronic laboratory session:** Authorized equipment initiates a scripted projection; staff monitor the physical body. An authored change in perceptual interface briefly reveals relations between conscious agents rather than humanoid avatars. No generic high-dimensional render/simulator required.

Each example is **noncanonical illustrative design** until authored/approved as campaign prose.

## 6. Boundaries and compatibility

- **CWN immutable core** remains the authority on ordinary physical actions, skills, damage and injury; use scene/adapters rather than rewriting it.
- Use existing SWN rules only for actual established SWN powers; astral narrative procedures do **not** grant extra SWN techniques or Effort.
- **No information shortcut:** astral perceptions may guide investigation but not automatically prove a suspect's identity, furnish admissible evidence, bypass an investigation scene or solve a police case. Follow the repository PSI investigation boundary.
- The **source of a dream remains ambiguous** as in #1152. Player-facing text never exposes hidden canonical causes unless an authored reveal occurs.
- Differentiate perceived entities, symbolic projections, possible independent conscious agents and genuinely physical persons. A meeting in Noöspace does not automatically prove that a named person was truly present.
- No uncontrolled astral teleportation, resurrection, immortal resleeving, cross-realm combat, unlimited precognition or higher-density gameplay in this MVP.
- Metaphysical sources inform **fictional worldbuilding**, not empirical claims about real quantum physics or human OOBE capabilities.

# 2026-11-06 AUTHOR UPDATE — Astral Body, combat, dream construction, afterlife and social transformation

> **Supersedes inconsistent earlier constraints in this issue:** Astral Projection is still *scene-triggered*, but astral interactions can now include **ordinary unarmed combat, SWN psionics and actual danger to the Astral Body**. Earlier language saying "no astral combat" or ruling out astral combat in the first implementation is **withdrawn**. The Astral Body is substantive **relative to other Astral entities**; it is not physical matter or a duplicate physical character sheet. No changes to CWN combat dice or freely selectable SWN powers are authorized.

## A. Astral Body and its interaction with material and astral realms

- **Projection uses the Astral Body:** the same conscious agent occupies an Astral Body while the ordinary biological body remains in the Physical Graph. The Astral Body has locatable presence, can manifest an appearance, collide with or grasp astral beings, and receive astral attacks. It is "physical" **within Astral interaction**, not material on the physical plane.
- **Near-physical Space/Time:** the Astral Body moves among astral locations anchored to real physical rooms, streets and regions. It may pass through ordinary material barriers when the scene permits; astral obstacles, boundaries and entities can still block or contest its travel. Do not silently infer unlimited access to every secure facility, readable document or hidden physical fact.
- **Perception asymmetry:** normal physical beings cannot see a merely projected Astral Body. **ESP/authorized extrasensory perception** may detect it. Conversely, an astral traveler can perceive near-physical manifestations and astral beings according to the interface rules and scene constraints.
- **Manifestation:** a projector may deliberately make a body **visible to physical witnesses** when the scene permits, without automatically making it tangible, photographable, able to speak, or able to interact physically; explicitly specify which sensory channels are enabled. Ordinary observers perceive a manifested apparition, not an ordinary body.
- **Astral-to-astral contact:** two astrally present agents can interact physically *in their shared astral mode*. A corporeal human without ESP/manifestation cannot normally target that combatant with ordinary CWN Punch/Shoot/Stab or see a hidden astral struggle.
- Preserve a single `actor_id` / one sheet, and use an `astral_body` view or projected state linked to that actor, not a second soul-character.

## B. Combat: CWN unarmed attacks and SWN psychic disciplines

**Mechanical authority:** use **published CWN combat resolution** for the Astral Body's ordinary unarmed grappling/strikes (Punch, initiative, hit rolls, defence, action economy and applicable effects), with a carefully specified astral-target eligibility adapter; do **not** import Shadowrun's dice pools or Psi-punk's Fudge trait ladder, and do not rewrite CWN. Use **published SWN Revised psionics** for usable psychic disciplines, technique prerequisites, Effort, commitment and duration. SWN is the *psionics engine*, not a license to invent a new SWN "Astral Projection" discipline that the book does not provide.

**Proposed adjudication pending exact source verification and Tomi approval:**
1. Target must actually be astrally present (or explicitly dual-present) and in reach under the scene's Astral Graph.
2. For ordinary Astral Body punches/grapples, reuse CWN **Punch** and the standard combat procedure. Astral body-to-body unarmed combat does **not** automatically become a new `Astral Combat` skill. Whether to substitute mental for physical attributes is a separate explicit design decision; default to published CWN numbers rather than silently introducing different stats.
3. SWN **Telekinesis** or **Telepathy** might be more effective, but only when the **specific published SWN technique** actually permits its chosen target/effect. Telepathy is not a generic damage spell; telekinesis is not guaranteed to manipulate disembodied astral matter. Define a small whitelist of permitted published techniques after checking wording, rather than inventing a universal psychic attack.
4. A projector's ordinary gun, physical armor and physical cyberware do not automatically materialize in the Astral. Effects that have a specifically authored astral/dual-present manifestation are exceptions.
5. **Damage and return:** CWN damage rules remain canonical. The astral-to-physical consequence mapping (shared HP, temporary astral injuries, forced return, coma, lethal danger, rescue) needs its own explicit author-approved adapter and tests. **Do not automatically invent a new second astral HP track** or adopt Psi-punk's involuntary soul severance/destruction mechanics.
6. Keep dream combat, dead-agent encounters and actual astral wounds separate in narration and GM truth; only an approved explicit astral interaction produces enforceable damage.

This is **real danger**, not merely a metaphor: Lower Astral environments may contain malevolent conscious agents. The MVP must support a contested astral encounter and a safe/unsafe return outcome. Avoid making every encounter a fight.

## C. Lower Astral as a visible dream-construction environment

- A sleeping person may be **constructing a dream from Quantum Information**: a visible bubble/landscape of experiences, symbols, memories, nightmares and figures being generated around their astral or sleeping consciousness. Projected observers may see the *process of dream construction*, not just a finished dream.
- Dream landscapes are often **Time/Space-style symbolic spaces nested in or reached from Lower Astral**. A near-physical projector sees a sleeping body in Space/Time, while an authorized transition reveals the person's conceptual dream environment in Time/Space.
- Dream builders, their dream avatars, shared collective thoughtforms and **independent astral visitors** must be distinguishable in GM data even when intentionally ambiguous to players.
- The dreamer does not automatically perceive the visitor. Entering, appearing in or changing somebody's dream requires authored permissions/scene triggers; it must not enable unrestricted telepathic intrusion or unlimited extraction of private facts.
- Nightmare worlds may contain predators or other malevolent agents. **Not all nightmares are supernatural attacks**, and not all Lower Astral entities are hostile.

## D. Dead people, reincarnation and communication

In **NoöPunk canon**, people who have died can continue as conscious agents in the Astral, often resting, processing experiences and preparing for subsequent **reincarnation**. They are **usually in Time/Space rather than near-physical Space/Time**, so an ordinary near-physical OOBE does **not** automatically locate them. Physically deceased and merely projected living persons have different states and return possibilities.

In-world, verifiable contact with deceased persons and reincarnation have changed law, religion, grief, politics and research. A living protagonist may **contact or visit dead relatives** through authored Noöspace encounters, specially enabled psychotronics or a deliberately constructed route to Time/Space. The scene should support identity uncertainty, consent, different kinds of postmortem existence and meaningful conversation; avoid a free universal "summon any deceased person" action.

**Important:** These are fictional premises of NoöPunk's setting, not claims that real-world science has established contact with the dead or reincarnation.

## E. Immortality economy and the Helsinki QIP revolution

**New setting canon to be integrated into a separate technology/economy document as appropriate:**
- Cortical Stacks, consciousness uploading and **Resleeving** make **potentially indefinite lifespan** possible in NoöPunk. They are new, experimental, increasingly popular and medically, legally, economically and ethically consequential. "Unlimited lifespan" means indefinite continuation is *technologically offered*, not literal immunity from destruction, failure or political coercion.
- **Orion Greys and Mars Eldrich** previously held a lucrative monopoly over the relevant cortical-stack / uploading / resleeving systems and supply chains.
- **University of Helsinki scientists Dr. Mannerheim and Dr. Yamamoto** experimentally demonstrated **QIP** in-setting and initiated the Helsinki-area **psychotronic boom**. They successfully developed a competing cortical-stack / mind-upload / resleeve platform.

Do **not** make all five equivalent one-click travel actions or assume an uploaded copy is uncontroversially continuous with the original.

## 2. Design principle: PSI is Magick, Magick is PSI

One underlying Noöspace/QIP phenomenon has multiple access interfaces:
1. **Innate or trained PSI:** SWN psychic discipline and techniques, SWN Effort and prerequisites.
2. **Psychotronic PSI:** power granted by **CWN-compatible cyberware, equipment, laboratory apparatus or implant**, with specified level/capability, device conditions, power source and price. An ordinary rookie need **not** gain the Psychic class simply by wearing an implant.
3. **Ritualized PSI ("Magick"):** published CWN **Spellcaster** and **Summoner** procedures, their independent class/Edge qualifications and Mage Effort, simply presented in world as ritual/QIP methods. Spell descriptions should be reskinned only with author approval, **without changing published numbers**.
4. **Scripted PSI:** dreams, NDEs, untrained spontaneous OOBEs and authored NHI phenomena that do not unlock new abilities.

**Do not double-charge** an event in SWN Effort *and* CWN Mage Effort, and **do not double-award** benefits by activating both interfaces. Every mechanical effect names **one authoritative rules source**, even if a ritual and a psychic technique have similar in-world explanations. This is cross-compatible presentation, **not a claim of rules-system identity**.

## 5. Three hacking lanes and BCI bridge: no universal "hack anything" skill

**B. Cyber intrusion / device or BCI software:** CWN **Program**, hacking verbs, node accessibility, cyberdecks, security, alarms and evidence trail. A brain–computer interface exposes **digital attack surfaces**, but that does not imply access to a person's private conscious experience.

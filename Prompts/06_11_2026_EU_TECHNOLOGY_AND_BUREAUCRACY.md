## Purpose

**Documentation / worldbuilding issue**, not a request to implement a new game engine. Establish the coherent technological, aesthetic, institutional and historical identity of the **EU, Europol and Eurodef**, with a special focus on Tony German, Laura Wendt and Field Controller **Salla Sotavaara**. Extend and cross-reference #1200 (Project Plato/EISS) rather than overwrite it. Deliver an English-language lore/design document with clear separation between authorial canon, public histories, disputed accounts, secret truths and open design decisions.

**Core thesis:** Europe survived the NHI Crisis not by racing toward AGI/ASI but by constructing a rugged, bureaucratic, operator-intensive, human-directed technological civilization. Its sophisticated quantum, cybernetic, psychotronic and narrow-AI systems coexist with astonishingly antique software and administrative habits. **New European Idealism** unites philosophy, state self-image and industrial design.

## 1. Historical canon: WW3 and the end of Russia

In the **fictional NoöPunk timeline**:
- As the NHI Crisis began, **the EU and Ukraine were fighting Russia**. Europeans remember this conflict as **World War Three (WW3)**.
- The Russian front had collapsed. Former Russian president **Dmitry Medvedev** staged a successful coup against Vladimir Putin with **four Mil Mi-8 helicopters carrying VDV paratroopers**, raiding **Putin's Palace**.
- The coup story says Medvedev **personally strangled Putin**, then ordered the execution of people identified as Putin loyalists.
- Medvedev tried to order a nuclear launch. **Extraterrestrial intervention had already disarmed the Strategic Rocket Forces of the Russian Federation and every nuclear weapon on Earth**, making his order futile.
- The intervention also neutralized **all frontier AI laboratories then capable of restarting the suicidal acceleration toward ASI**.
- Medvedev died by suicide. This marks **the end of Russia as an effective state** in this timeline.

Write a chronological narrative of these events and their impact on European institutions, military engineering, cybernetic defensive doctrine and collective memory. Explicitly distinguish **fictional canonical events** from **in-universe hearsay**, especially the graphic details of Putin's death, and preserve room for NoöPunk's earlier **Mandela Effect / contradictory historical memories**. Do not silently replace this timeline with earlier USSR-survives drafts; cross-reference any conflicting existing canon for editorial reconciliation. Define the precise chronology only where the established timeline supports it. Do not present this fictional sequence as real-world history.

**Historical design consequence:** EU military and civilian systems were designed for, and often deployed during, an actual continent-scale war and the NHI Crisis. Much European technology is effectively **WW3 field equipment**: repairable, redundant, shockproof, rugged, distributed and designed to remain operational under electromagnetic interference, cyberattacks, communication loss and catastrophic infrastructure failure.

## 2. European hardware and visual identity: military-survivable retrofuturism

- Europol, Eurodef and ordinary EU governmental hardware looks like **sturdy EMP-resistant military field equipment intended to survive WW3**, because much of it really was.
- Thick casings, shielded cables, physical connectors, modular replaceable components, screened enclosures, physical switches, hardcopy audit trails, rugged keyboards and displays, resilient power systems, offline operating modes, redundant communications and maintainable parts.
- Military-grade apparatus is **bulky** but not necessarily primitive. World-leading quantum processors, psychotronic probes and implants might be housed inside enormous field-hardened cases with manual controls, interlocks and warning labels.
- Explain EMP hardening as an engineering system rather than magic: shielding, protected interfaces, filtered power, backups, supply-chain maintainability, field servicing. Not every device is invulnerable.
- Distinguish Europol civilian investigative hardware, Eurodef battlefield/strategic hardware, and public administrative/consumer technology while preserving a common design family.
- Support art direction with textual palettes, materials, typography, silhouettes and representative examples (portable terminal, surveillance suitcase, cybernetic implant service bench, quantum facility, psychotronic interface, vehicle communications).

## 3. New European Idealism: philosophy *and* design movement

**New European Idealism** is a central philosophy of the EU and a recognizable **architectural / graphic / industrial design style**.
- Philosophical inspirations explicitly include **Spinoza, Kant and Hegel**, along with idealist theories of mind, **Quantum Information Panpsychism (QIP)**, quantum consciousness, psionics, psychotronics and astral projection.
- Institutional design draws inspiration from the **18th-, 19th- and early-20th-century European imperial periods**, but transforms the references into something **elegant, futuristic, minimalistic and idealistic**.
- Avoid lazy reproduction or celebration of colonial domination. Show the design movement's relationship to historical power, universalism, imperial legacy and contemporary political critique as a contested part of the fictional culture.
- Contrast the sophisticated ceremonial/architectural ideals with utilitarian EMP-resistant cabinets and heavily layered bureaucratic workflows. The European aesthetic can be beautiful and formidable, authoritarian-seeming and sincere, noble in aspiration and absurd in execution.
- Specify visual motifs without collapsing back into generic 1980s neon cyberpunk; maintain NoöPunk's 1970s psychedelic/retrofuturist and minimal graphic sensibility where appropriate.

## 4. European ban on AGI/ASI and autonomous agents

- After the catastrophes of AGI/ASI acceleration, the EU maintains a **strict ban on research, development and operation of systems approaching AGI** and an especially strong prohibition on independent **autonomous AI agents**. Autonomous robotics is subject to still more severe restrictions.
- Policy should be defined by **capability, autonomy, tool access, self-improvement and integrated multi-agent behavior**, not merely a parameter count. Proposed ceiling for permitted general-purpose LLM assistance: approximately **2026 desktop Ollama-class Gemma 4 31B** in practical breadth/autonomy; later narrow systems can greatly exceed this on domain-specific tasks.
- Explicitly address the difference between **autonomous decision-making** and preauthorized bounded automation (otherwise conventional cyberdefence and industrial control would become impossible). Human-in-the-loop must be genuine, consequential and auditable; emergency defensive interlocks should not absurdly require a clerk's click for each microsecond response.
- Describe approvals, licensing, audit logs, prohibited research, inspection, sanctions, red teams, and the disputed boundary between narrow AI systems and secretly reassembled AGI.
- **Continuity with #1200:** its statement that Mistral may be a uniquely permitted *research exception* must be reconciled with this **strict general ban**. Do not assume unrestricted Mistral AGI operation. Document whether narrowly licensed containment research is a legal exception, classified deviation or outdated lore, for authorial resolution.
- The regulatory apparatus is a major source of political conflict among accelerationists, doomers, civil-liberties defenders and officials. Use Goal Tags for ideological objectives and Social Tags for alliances/antagonisms; avoid a new ideology meter.

## 5. Plato / EISS: enormous human-operated cybernetic security apparatus

- Per #1200, **Project Plato** is the project name, **European Information Security System (EISS)** the formal deployed system name, **Plato** the operational shorthand; **Panopticon** is its critical nickname, not the official name.
- **Plato is not a unified AGI or ASI**. It is a sprawling institution and infrastructure encompassing manual **Lisp/Prolog GOFAI**, symbolic rules, knowledge graphs, expert systems, old-school cybernetics, specialized narrow machine learning, qualified 2026-scale LLM assistants, quantum computing/sensing and extraordinary psychotronic systems.
- Operated by an **army of cyborgified human operators, analysts, Lisp/Prolog programmers, electricians, hardware engineers, network personnel, auditors and field technicians**. Repair and operating labor is highly visible.
- Separate systems and permissions are **deliberate firebreaks** against recombining independent capabilities into de facto AGI. Explain the trade-offs: transparent rule trails and institutional control versus brittleness, delays, human prejudice, maintenance debt and siloed data.
- Europe's quantum systems excel at **specific** computing, materials, sensing, cryptanalytic or simulation tasks; quantum computing is **not** automatically a replacement for general-purpose intelligence. Setting-specific quantum-consciousness/psychotronics can go beyond real-world science, but should be distinguished from established computation.
- Contemporary human judgement, political decisions, arrests, invasive searches and psychotronic interventions cannot simply be delegated to unaccountable AI.
- Portray the apparatus as **effective enough to help preserve the EU** and also a formidable threat to civil liberties and inner freedom.

## 6. Europol, Eurodef and European bureaucracy

Document mandates, equipment, access boundaries and interoperation:
- **Europol:** civilian intelligence and law-enforcement operations, OSINT, investigations, counterintelligence, digital evidence, cyber-defence cooperation, authorized surveillance, psychotronic operations and Plato/EISS operational interfaces under institutional oversight.
- **Eurodef:** military cyber defence, WW3-hardened infrastructure, theatre operations, military intelligence and military-grade psychotronic/quantum interfaces.
- Clarify relations to national authorities, courts, regulators, UNSA X-risk jurisdiction, European federal institutions and local Helsinki police. Do not silently grant Europol unlimited legal jurisdiction.
- **Surveillance permission is substantive gameplay**: public OSINT can be routine but private communications interception, access to restricted records, covert tracking and especially **consciousness scanning** require differentiated legal powers and documented approval processes. Exact 2045 rules are fiction, not claims about present-day EU law.
- Authorization is a **Task** with case number, evidence, Clue Tags, necessity/proportionality assessment, supervisor approval, legal review, judicial or special psychotronic permission as appropriate, scope, expiry, logging and recourse. Some fast-track emergency exceptions exist but are still reviewable.
- All systems need proper **human-in-the-loop operations**, possible procedural mistakes, denials, delays, jurisdictional disputes and accountability. Bureaucratic friction creates real Plot/Task/Story opportunities.
- OSINT and social/discourse/network analysis are fundamental investigative capabilities; **Clue Tags** store evidence and claims with provenance, credibility and access scope. Distinguish original evidence, source statements, competing hypotheses and verified conclusions. Political extremist ideologies should be investigated, not automatically inferred from dissent.

Document Salla as a **human field controller**, not an autonomous artificial agent. Her role should integrate the field agents with Plato's operator-intensive system and its permissions.

Their **body cameras are always on** and stream live to HQ under normal operating conditions; interruptions in connectivity, power, or physical damage must be visible as incidents, not silently treated as continuous transmission.

- Network loss may force voice/radio fallback, cached evidence, local autonomy of the **human agents**, or postponement of a remote forensic consultation. Never allow an autonomous AI to take control of field assets.
- The exact configuration of direct BCI, implanted communications and optional psychotronic channels may vary by officer and clearance, but normal voice/data/video field support and the Salla connection are now established canon.

## 7B. Police vehicle, PoliisiSanomalaite and drones

- Standard European police vehicles should feature shielded electronics, hardened power/communications, sensors, local data storage, and a **Nokia PoliisiSanomalaite** for encrypted police-radio communication with HQ. Define failover precedence: **EuroNET → mobile network → encrypted police radio**, choosing the best available authenticated channel.

- Police car and drone are capable of **remote human operation**. A human operator at the vehicle or HQ must be in control. Bounded stabilization, collision avoidance and safety interlocks may exist as non-agentic control systems, but **AI cannot drive, pilot, target, pursue or act independently**. Explain the human control handoff, fail-safe stops and link-loss behavior.

## 7C. Romppainen–Fujita Objective Assessment System (RFOAS)

**Canonical purpose:** a complicated, specialized interrogation and identity-assessment device combining **neural imaging and psychotronic technology**, inspired in operation and atmosphere by *Blade Runner*'s Voight-Kampff examination.

- Records synchronized **video, audio and neural feeds** for the full examination. Document measured signals, sensor limitations, authorization, consent/coercion constraints, secure retention and forensic chain of custody.
- Supports ordinary questioning/interviews, assessment of inconsistencies and anomalous responses, and the detection/investigation of **unregistered Hybrids and Androids**. No magical certainty: physiological, neural or psychotronic anomalies create **evidence and hypotheses**, not automatic guilt or proof of NHI identity.
- **Europol must produce admissible evidence when identifying a person as NHI.** Superficially obvious differences or a system suspicion are not a substitute for documented evidence. Distinguish biological hybrid status, android identity and NHI origin claims rather than conflating them.
- Operator-mediated device: interrogator and trained human analyst supervise all inferences; Plato may assist with narrow pattern detection but **cannot render an autonomous identity verdict**.
- This system is a regulated, intrusive psychotronic interrogation technology. Define a higher permission tier than routine interviewing, with reviewable records and rights safeguards.
- Gameplay: recordings and scanner results generate **Clue Tags**, source-labeled hypotheses and follow-up Investigation/Social Tasks/Scenes; do not invent a standalone emotional interrogation mechanic.

## 7D. Field cyberdeck → Plato → evidence workflow

1. **Authenticate:** Tony/Laura connect through their **external cyberdeck** or an alternative authorized computer (optional implanted deck or tablet) to **Plato/EISS**.
2. **Default permissions:** access the **Europol internal shared case/reference database** (an *internally accessible agency database*, not a general public Internet site) according to role/case credentials; gather **OSINT** evidence from permitted open sources on EuroNET or other reachable networks.
3. **Evidence submission:** upload authenticated **Forensics Multimode Scanner** output, bodycam/cybernetic-eye footage, vehicle/drone observations and other case material for authorized analysis; maintain collection timestamps, operator, device ID, provenance and evidentiary integrity.
4. **Restricted access:** surveillance feeds, communications interception, private or classified records, covert tracking, neural records, psychotronic scans and other privileged information **require a permission request and bureaucratic approval**. The task queue tracks justification, approving authority, scope, expiry and audit history.
5. **Plato's role:** monitor permitted data, run symbolic or narrow-AI searches and anomaly detection, suggest queries, **notify humans when something relevant is detected**. It **does not independently issue warrants, interrogate, surveil restricted targets, command vehicles/drones, make arrests or decide NHI status**.
6. **Salla's role:** real-time **human Field Controller**, triages alerts, communicates with field agents, assists with approved searches and bureaucratic escalation; can coordinate with specialized human analysts.
7. **Gameplay:** ordinary queries and forensic analysis can be Tasks/Cyber/Investigation Scenes producing Clue Tags; permission requests are time-consuming administrative Tasks; meaningful discoveries update Story/Character/Faction loops. Do not turn routine authorized OSINT into a multi-day permit request.

**Design tension:** Under heavy fire, an officer may have quantum-assisted forensic resources, a defensive psychic implant and instant secure contact with a skilled controller, but obtaining restricted surveillance records still requires signed and logged legal permissions. Keep this useful and satirical rather than making every investigation impossible.

## 7G. Fragmented, deliberately restricted city and intercity networks

**Major worldbuilding clarification:** NoöPunk does **not** have ubiquitous high-bandwidth global connectivity. Its networks were intentionally constrained after AI-swarm attacks. Different networks overlap imperfectly; access may demand a physical visit, cable, credential, trusted courier or human operator.

**Important continuity reconciliation:** Previous issue text says the *bodycam is always on, real-time HQ feed*; here mobile networks are intentionally voice/SMS-class and intercity links suspect. **Resolve honestly**: cameras are **always recording** and send **live HQ feeds when an authorized high-bandwidth channel exists** (e.g., secure HelNET/local wired/police broadband hotspot). Show **stream unavailable** to Salla, not an imaginary uninterrupted HD connection. If real-time full-resolution feed is an absolute requirement, specify a separate licensed police broadband link with coverage, authorization and failures instead of pretending voice-only mobile supports it.

**Security philosophy:** Wireless and citywide links are convenient but vulnerable; physical proximity, isolation, cable and human-operated couriers matter. The best hacker often needs a physical investigator, a source inside the organization or a court-authorized technician to get to the target machine.

## 7H. Salla Sotavaara: personality and operational voice

**Canonical traits:** **perky, cheerful, knowledgeable and resourceful**. Salla is a lively, competent **human Field Controller**, not a free-roaming AI assistant or a monotonous HUD narrator.

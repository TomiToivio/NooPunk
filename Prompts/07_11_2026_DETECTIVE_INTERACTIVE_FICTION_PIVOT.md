# Prompt: Pivot NoöPunk from full RPG to Etsivä Romppainen detective adventure

- **Date:** 2026-11-07
- **Topic:** Detective interactive fiction instead of full RPG
- **Source:** Tomi's NoöPunk design request in ChatGPT; tracked in [issue #1310](https://github.com/TomiToivio/NooPunk/issues/1310).
- **Status:** Original author design/development prompt archived for reuse; not a claim that code changes are implemented.

## Original prompt (verbatim; nonbreaking spaces normalized)

Create an issue in [https://github.com/TomiToivio/NooPunk](https://github.com/TomiToivio/NooPunk)

Convert NoöPunk into an adventure game instead of a full RPG.

[NooPunk-Simulator](https://github.com/TomiToivio/NooPunk/tree/main/NooPunk-Simulator "NooPunk-Simulator") becomes a cyberpunk detective interactive fiction adventure about Etsivä Romppainen investigating political extremists radicalized by paradigm shifts and ontological shocks. Drop the idea of making a full RPG. NoöPunk is just too complex for a full RPG and I get a cognitive overload. Make NoöPunk an old-fashioned adventure or interactive fiction game. Consider adding light-weight elements of Political Simulation ([https://store.steampowered.com/app/633060/Orwell_Ignorance_is_Strength/](https://store.steampowered.com/app/633060/Orwell_Ignorance_is_Strength/)) and Hacking Simulation ([https://store.steampowered.com/app/1902300/DROP_System_Breach/](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1902300/DROP_System_Breach/)) later.

[NooPunk-Story](https://github.com/TomiToivio/NooPunk/tree/main/NooPunk-Story "NooPunk-Story") is the script of the interactive fiction game which will hopefully become a full science fiction book.

Don't touch [NooPunk-RPG](https://github.com/TomiToivio/NooPunk/tree/main/NooPunk-RPG "NooPunk-RPG") for the time being as full RPG causes a horrible cognitive overload.

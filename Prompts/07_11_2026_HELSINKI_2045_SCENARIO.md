## Original prompt (verbatim issue text, transport formatting normalised)

## Purpose and scope

Define the **2045 Helsinki scenario, Europol protagonist, police-investigation loop, and player agency** for NoöPunk. This is primarily a **Story / RPG narrative-design issue** informed by the LaclauGPT Simulator. Do **not** alter immutable Cities Without Number (CWN) core rules or require simultaneous implementation in Story, Simulator, and RPG.

**Campaign pitch:** A fresh Europol Academy graduate investigates illegal androids, hybrids, psychics, and radicalized human networks in Helsinki, 2045. AGI/ASI, PSI, and NHI have already overturned mainstream science and politics, but extraordinary beings are not routinely visible on the streets. Bureaucracy tries to keep the lid on a boiling society. The rookie is a cop, not a predestined mystic or scientist.

## 1. The three paradigm shifts are mainstream, but destabilizing

All three are accepted as **real fields of science and government regulation** by 2045, though their precise capabilities, interpretations, boundaries, and classified implications remain contested:

1. **AGI / ASI:** advanced and potentially superintelligent AI, including androids, rogue systems, and cybernetic/transhuman developments. AI is present at familiar cyberpunk intensity in everyday technology and institutions.
2. **PSI:** verified psychic phenomena and consciousness-related science (quantum consciousness, idealism, panpsychism, and psionics). Serious science and regulatory institutions study them; widespread firsthand psychic practice is *not* everyday street life.
3. **NHI:** compelling disclosures, leaks, encounters, incidents, and scientifically recognized non-human intelligence. Yet most people have never met NHI directly, and genuine NHI activity remains distant, restricted, or mediated by humans.

Each has caused **multiple waves of destabilization, polarization, and radicalization**. Mainstream politics, religion, science, industry, and law must take all three seriously. New extremist movements and rival subcultures proliferate, without assuming that every adherent of a controversial ideology is criminal.

**Timeline correction:** The original setting's full **NHI Crisis has NOT yet happened** in 2045. Prior disclosures, leaks, visible events, smaller crises, and disasters have forced institutions and ordinary people to react, but do not preemptively import the old scenario's global collapse, visible alien society, or interstellar standoff into the player-facing opening. Preserve deeper background as optional author/GM material, reveal only as story events warrant, and reconcile any conflicting canon accordingly.

## 2. Helsinki-only viewpoint; strict thematic restraint

- Describe institutions and changes **as they are experienced in Helsinki**, through the rookie's cases, coworkers, sources, media, local streets, and official procedures.
- The EU and Finnish government matter because **their local rules, offices, permissions, operations, and bureaucratic conflicts** affect the protagonist.
- **Do not front-load global geopolitics, interstellar factions, cosmic history, hidden metaphysics, or encyclopedic lore.** Players begin knowing no privileged secrets of the setting, even though the public knows the three shifts are real.
- Distinguish **public scientific knowledge**, **rumor and propaganda**, **classified operational knowledge**, and **hidden author/GM truth**. Preserve uncertainty about individual claims without denying the publicly established scientific shifts.
- Keep AGI/ASI, PSI, and NHI as the **only three primary paradigm-shift themes**. Other cyberpunk material serves them and the investigation, rather than becoming new parallel grand themes.
- Exposition is earned by local cases and choices, not a lore lecture.

## 3. The EU's four bureaucratic objectives

European authorities respond through heavy, sometimes contradictory licensing, registration, inspection, intelligence, risk governance, and interagency procedures:

| Objective | Mandate and central tension |
| --- | --- |
| **Stability** | Limit public exposure to *destabilizing manifestations* of AGI/ASI, PSI, and NHI, keeping frightening or radicalizing incidents out of ordinary streets, media cycles, and citizens' immediate experience where possible. Public-safety controls, secrecy, censorship, civil liberties, and democratic oversight come into conflict. |
| **Sustainability** | Maximize the benefits of AGI/ASI, PSI, and NHI while minimizing existential risks. Critics call this objective **Profit**, pointing to capture by industry, elites, and security institutions. |
| **Security** | Detect, contain, investigate, and prevent X-Risks from becoming actual catastrophes, including rogue AI, dangerous PSI operations, and NHI-related threats and proxies. |
| **Survival** | Prepare contingency plans to preserve **Homo sapiens populations** if prevention fails: continuity, shelter, evacuation, resilience, and last-resort measures. |

Make bureaucracy a recurring gameplay pressure: clearances, authorization, liability, jurisdiction, classified evidence, registration records, oversight, procurement, competing mandates, and delayed approvals. The four objectives should sometimes conflict in individual cases. They are **institutional goals, not necessarily morally correct positions**.

## 4. Europol team: Blade Runner meets counterintelligence

The player joins a Helsinki-based Europol team specializing in crimes and threats arising from the three shifts. Its two primary operational jobs are:

**A. Law enforcement / registration enforcement (Blade Runner-style cases)**
- Track, identify, locate, interview, pursue, detain, or arrest **unregistered, illegal, or criminal androids, hybrids, psychics, and associated operators**, subject to in-world law and evidence.
- Investigate missing persons, illicit technologies, dangerous incidents, black-market services, concealment, exploitation, and violent crimes.
- Distinguish a registration or legal-status violation from actual harm. A person or being's identity alone must not automatically establish criminal guilt; let unjust laws, human rights, institutional prejudice, and discretionary enforcement become genuine narrative conflicts.

**B. Counterintelligence, OSINT, and extremist-network investigations**
- Monitor *credible threats and criminal conduct*, not ideology by itself, among political extremist movements responding to AGI/ASI, PSI, and NHI.
- Investigate recruitment, propaganda, funding, clandestine coordination, violent plots, espionage, and alleged **human proxies of NHI or rogue AGIs**.
- Follow human intermediaries (cults, informants, operatives, corporations, radicalized groups) rather than routinely confronting NHI directly.
- Keep legal safeguards, proportionality, evidence standards, and surveillance oversight present as both professional constraints and possible sources of noir corruption.

**C. Discovery as a by-product, not a third core job**
- Deeper investigation into AGI/ASI, PSI, NHI, and hidden history occurs mostly **because a police or counterintelligence case leads there**.
- The team consists primarily of **cops and investigators**, not a scientific expedition, occult order, or metaphysical quest party. Specialist analysts and scientists can support cases.
- The PC can approach LaclauGPT Simulator outputs as a detective/OSINT investigator and occasionally a social scientist, but network links and model inferences are **leads, never proof of guilt**.

**Early genre:** grounded **cyberpunk noir police procedural / Blade Runner** with **Esoterrorists-style GUMSHOE investigative continuity**. Supernatural and cosmic implications emerge slowly, through ordinary evidence and human motives.

## 5. Rookie protagonist: tabula rasa, competent with Cyber

- The player character **Tony German** begins **straight from Europol Academy** as a junior field agent. Treat personality, ideology, ethical trajectory, latent abilities, and deeper beliefs as substantially **tabula rasa**.
- He starts with **useful hacking / programming capability and practical cybernetics**, not as an optional niche: investigative work should regularly use access systems, OSINT, surveillance, digital forensics, intrusion under authorization, and cybernetic tools.
- The character may be indifferent, skeptical about particular claims, curious, radically committed, or conflicted. Skepticism need not mean denying publicly proven science.
- **No compulsory PSI awakening, destiny, faction alignment, ideological conversion, or forced scientific research.** Latent PSI may awaken later through an optional story branch.
- Keep existing Europol coworkers/relationships and possible lifepath choices compatible with the rookie's open identity; do not predetermine an ideological or psychic character arc.

### Viable player trajectories (not exhaustive or mandatory)

- **Dirty / hedonistic cop:** bribery, compromises, pleasure, opportunism, or institutional corruption with credible consequences.
- **Idealistic / empathetic cop:** help vulnerable people, resist abusive enforcement, protect rights while pursuing real threats.
- **Ruthless hunter:** treat androids, hybrids, psychics, and other posthuman beings as dangerous “subhumans,” with the setting reflecting the brutality, prejudice, and consequences of that choice, *not endorsing it*.
- **Awakened PSI asset:** discover latent psychic abilities and choose whether to disclose, hide, or exploit them.
- **Secret NHI-cult member:** join or infiltrate a movement and manage conflicting loyalties.
- **Professional outsider:** remain a conventional detective, avoid metaphysical commitment, and still complete the campaign.

Branches should arise from **choices, evidence, relationships, tags/goals, and consequences**, not moral-class selection or a mandatory revelation. None of the paths is the sole “correct” story.

## 6. Physical / Cyber / Astral: entangled overlays, not three mandatory player classes

Design **Physical, Cyber, and Astral as interacting overlays of the same Helsinki, locations, persons, incidents, and case graphs**, not as three disconnected campaigns:

- **Physical**: streets, people, evidence, weapons, witnesses, arrests, and ordinary investigation.
- **Cyber**: network infrastructure, digital identities, sensors, surveillance, AI/android systems, hacking, and cybernetic interfaces. The rookie can actively operate here **from the beginning**.
- **Astral**: PSI-linked perceptions, presences, traces, interventions, dreams, and anomalies whose consequences can appear in Physical and Cyber. The PC can **become aware of, witness, be affected by, and investigate Astral phenomena without PSI powers**.
- **Optional PSI** opens additional active methods, not essential main-story access or a required immunity to otherwise unavoidable hazards.
- Use witnesses, forensic traces, tools, psychic colleagues/assets, monitored interfaces, environmental effects, dreams, and cross-layer consequences to communicate Astral events to non-PSI PCs.
- Build multiple viable solutions to central cases, with **non-PSI approaches** always available. Do not gate indispensable clues, survival, campaign completion, or core scenes behind PSI powers.
- Leverage the shared **Graph/Tag system** for cross-layer people, locations, evidence, knowledge, goals, and consequences; avoid redesigning CWN core.

## 7. Ideological polarization baseline: 50 / 25 / 25 for EACH shift

Use this as a **scenario-design heuristic**, not an empirically measured statistic or a requirement to label every NPC permanently. Apply separately to **AGI/ASI**, **PSI**, and **NHI**:

| Segment | Baseline | General stance |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| **Normies** | **50%** | Adapt, accommodate, ignore, or pragmatically navigate the new reality; not necessarily ignorant or politically homogeneous. |
| **Accelerationists** | **25%** | Push faster adoption, transformation, integration, or revelation. Increasingly **transhuman / posthuman** in their lifestyles and bodies; opponents may call them “subhuman.” |
| **Doomers** | **25%** | See catastrophic danger and demand restraint, containment, or reversal. Increasingly build **survivalist preparations**; militant fringes contemplate revolution, civil war, or violence if peaceful advocacy fails. |

- Accelerationists and Doomers are **politically extreme subcultures embedded in ordinary society**, not automatically wholly underground terrorist organizations.
- Many participate in **lawful advocacy**. Distinguish moderate, radical, extremist, and violent factions *within* each category, rather than equating all dissent with criminality.
- A person's positions can differ across the three shifts (e.g., AGI Accelerationist + PSI Normie + NHI Doomer). Mixed and changing positions produce richer networks than one universal tripartite alignment.
- Show visible subculture markers: augmentation, AI-assisted lifestyles, posthuman identities, survivalist infrastructure, bunkers, drills, defensive communities, propaganda, and recruitment.
- Use LaclauGPT to simulate evolving identity boundaries, grievances, antagonisms, mobilization, and alliance changes. The percentages are a useful **starting prior**, not a fixed output that suppresses narrative change.
- Investigations target **conduct and credible threats**, including criminal proxies, not membership in an ideological segment alone.

## 8. Opening and case loop

Start in **Helsinki, 2045**, with the newly graduated rookie assigned to the specialized team. Avoid a compulsory psychic nightmare/awakening or an early direct NHI encounter as a premise. The starting experience must work for an entirely non-PSI detective.

Suggested loop:

1. Receive a complaint, tip, warrant request, registration alert, missing-person report, suspected rogue android case, or OSINT/counterintelligence lead.
2. Navigate Europol/Finnish procedures and competing Stability/Sustainability/Security/Survival priorities.
3. Interview witnesses, walk Helsinki locations, inspect Physical evidence, use the PC's Cyber abilities, and cross-reference databases.
4. Consult the Simulator's actor/statement/ideology/network graph for **testable leads**, while checking reliability and avoiding automated guilt by association.
5. Investigate human networks and intermediaries; discover linked Cyber or Astral phenomena through **multiple accessible channels**.
6. Choose among lawful intervention, negotiation, arrest, containment, cover-up, corruption, refusal, or further investigation according to case facts and character choices.
7. Resolve or complicate the case, face bureaucratic review and faction responses, and allow an optional deeper revelation to emerge.

### Three starter case briefs to develop
- **AGI/ASI:** an unregistered android or prohibited AI-controlled asset, with competing claims about autonomy, criminal activity, and institutional exploitation.
- **PSI:** a suspected unlicensed psychic linked to an incident; evidence must be accessible to a non-PSI detective, with benign and criminal possible outcomes.
- **NHI:** a political group accused of acting as an NHI proxy; the evidence may point to fraud, lawful belief, human espionage, or an authentic but indirect non-human connection.

Use GUMSHOE-inspired clue continuity **on top of immutable CWN**, not as a replacement for its core rules.

## 9. Subproject boundaries

- **Story:** write Helsinki-grounded noir scenes, branching script/Story Graph, dialogue, evolving character arcs, and potentially adapt this into a science-fiction book.
- **Simulator:** use LaclauGPT Social Science Simulator for ideological struggles and the common Graph/Tag system, including cross-layer entities and traceable, imperfect evidence.
- **RPG:** consume Story and Simulator with **immutable Cities Without Number core** plus optional NoöPunk Mod and light GUMSHOE-style investigative support. No synchronized implementation requirement; check compatibility periodically.

This issue is a **design specification**, not authorization to implement all systems or rewrite the locked RPG rules.

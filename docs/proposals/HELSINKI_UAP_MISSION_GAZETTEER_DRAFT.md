# Helsinki and Southern Finland: UAP, Disclosure and ontological-shock mission gazetteer

> **PROPOSALS ONLY / NOT CANON. Issue #1156. 2026-11-05.** Companion to [the source dossier](../research/UFO_DISCLOSURE_BACKGROUND_RESEARCH.md) and [the Disclosure/Crisis proposal](UFO_DISCLOSURE_NHI_CRISIS_SCENARIO_DRAFT.md). Real location names are geographic anchors; every anomalous event, hidden laboratory, crime, conspiracy, temporal zone and fictional institution described below is invented for NoöPunk. No private or restricted access points are described.

## Design method

Treat **Helsinki as the protagonist**, not just a mission selection screen. A rookie can take a tram, file an evidence request, consult a researcher, stop for a coffee, and encounter an ontological anomaly without the city turning into an endless disaster zone. Helsinki, Espoo and Vantaa form the metropolitan play field by 2045, subject to the canon's final municipal decision. A mission must have a real human stake and a plausible mundane surface. Alien factions work through human institutions, networks and economic relations. Preserve New Weird/PKD/Robert Anton Wilson uneasy comedy over military spectacle.

**Classification:** H = central Helsinki, E = Espoo/western metro, V = northern/airport, O = outer Finland/Baltic/other. **Entry** = who calls; **legal lead** = who can take evidence/coercive action; **twist** = optional and non-canonical, never a predetermined answer. **Difficulty** = rookie / intermediate / advanced, independent of CWN mechanic changes. An observed light or discrepancy is not itself an NHI identification.

**Evidence ladder** for every case: witness record → public/civilian material → authenticated technical record → conflicting authority response → optional unusual experience. A mundane solution must usually remain possible at the first three rungs. Never gate investigation on a forced PSI roll, and do not turn protected hybrids into automatic suspects.

## H: Helsinki districts and highly local missions

### H01 — Kruununhaka, Senate Square, University Main Building, National Library: *The Vanishing Footnote* (rookie)

**Real setting:** Historical university/civic architecture and documentary collections at the City Centre Campus ([UH](https://www.helsinki.fi/en/about-us/visit-us/campuses/city-centre-campus)).
**Entry:** An archivist flags one altered citation to a 1946 Ghost Rocket story, and later receives a threatening anonymous demand to stop comparing catalogues.
**Legal lead:** Finnish police open harassment/data-tampering case; Europol supports a Swedish and American document-provenance request. University controls collection access.
**Twist:** Three authentic versions of an accession register exist. All three describe different custodians. The investigators can prove who altered one record, **not** what landed in Finland.
**Scenes:** archive reading table, public lecture on archival forensics, ordinary walk across Senate Square, interview in a quiet café. **Tags:** HISTORIC-RECORD, CUSTODY-SPLIT, TIME-MEMORY-U.
**Connected:** H02, H04, O08. **No new secret tunnel fact.**

### H02 — Kaisaniemi, Kaisa House and Botanical Garden: *The Herbarium That Remembers* (rookie)

**Entry:** A botany collection receives an unlabeled 1946 sample, and an autonomous ecological repair organism is falsely accused of being a Sirian saboteur.
**Legal lead:** University ethics/biosecurity, municipal environmental unit, Finnish police if theft suspected.
**Twist:** A tree-like cellular architecture resembles an old drawing but the sample's DNA could be manufactured. Sirian *conscious* trees must not be confused with their fast nonconscious swarms.
**Scenes:** public garden, specimen records, lab interview and delayed electronic correspondence. **Tags:** BIOLOGIC-UNKNOWN, BIOTECH-RIGHTS, SYMBIOTIC-INTELLIGENCE.

### H03 — Tähtitorninmäki Observatory / Kaivopuisto: *Ten Seconds of Yellow Light* (rookie)

**Real hook:** University of Helsinki Observatory history; the real U.S. attaché report of a light over Helsinki on 18 January 1947 ([archival reproduction](https://ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/foia01.htm)).
**Entry:** A hobby astronomer's 2045 recording matches a timing pattern in a 1947 newspaper translation, but its camera metadata is corrupt.
**Legal lead:** Civilian observers and museum; police only for suspected sabotage/stalking.
**Twist:** Repeated periodic lights may be an aerial object, re-encoded film artifact or time/space-affected observation. No actual 1947 parachute recovery is established.
**Scenes:** public observatory exhibition, Kaivopuisto hill, shoreline skywatch. **Tags:** ASTROMETRY, SENSOR-PROVENANCE, 1947-HISTORY.

### H04 — Ratakatu, Punavuori, police/security history: *The File Without an Owner* (rookie)

**Entry:** A Europol analysis request reaches Supo and Finnish police under two incompatible classification stamps; a suspect is not yet wanted for any crime.
**Legal lead:** National agency controls coercion and disclosure; Europol may coordinate only.
**Twist:** A Cold War field report refers to ПРОГРАММА but later records show a US contractor indexing that same file under MJ-12. Neither version settles the program's continuity.
**Scenes:** modest office conference room, cross-agency meeting, evening walk to transit. **Tags:** JURISDICTION, CLASSIFIED, MANDELA-EFFECT.
**Boundary:** **Do not** presume Europol HQ is on Ratakatu or specify internal layout of real security premises.

### H05 — Kamppi–Narinkkatori: *The Helpful Alert* (rookie)

**Entry:** During a shelter drill, commuters receive convincing but contradictory evacuation instructions generated by a synthetic voice.
**Legal lead:** City preparedness and police cybercrime, transport operator. Europol joins if spoofing infrastructure runs across EU borders.
**Twist:** One instruction may be genuine and uncannily prescient, but the crime is the forged message and public risk, not an alleged prophecy.
**Scenes:** public terminal concourse, ticketing desk, municipal safety call center (fictional interface). **Tags:** CIVIL-DEFENCE, DEEPFAKE, LUCK-OR-PSI.

### H06 — Töölönlahti, Oodi and Finlandia Hall environs: *A Sphere in the Reflection* (rookie)

**Entry:** Hundreds of spectators film a metallic sphere apparently over the water; one family's missing person report predates the gathering.
**Legal lead:** Police missing-person unit, Europol analytics for cross-border image-provenance network.
**Twist:** The viral sphere is a known misinterpretation, while the unrelated disappearance hides a genuine contractor trafficking lead. False positive cannot be used to throw away the victim.
**Scenes:** public waterfront, library meeting room, interviews along pedestrian paths. **Tags:** MEDIA-CONTAGION, MISATTRIBUTION, PERSON-MISSING.

### H07 — Kallio / Helsinginkatu / Hakaniemi: *The Tenant Who Was Two People* (rookie)

**Entry:** Two residents hold valid but conflicting tenancy and employment records; a landlord wants both removed.
**Legal lead:** Housing mediation and Finnish police for fraud/harassment; Europol if resleeving identity chain crosses borders.
**Twist:** A protected hybrid allegation spreads online, but the real issue may be corporate duplicative identity licensing.
**Scenes:** apartment stairwell, Hakaniemi market, labor union adviser, late tram. **Tags:** IDENTITY, HOUSING, HYBRID-RIGHTS.
**Ethical boundary:** Never infer hybrid status from appearance or neighborhood.

### H08 — Sörnäinen/Kurvi: *The Listening Tram* (rookie)

**Entry:** Commuters report a tram announcement that addressed private thoughts; one person alleges unauthorized PSI profiling.
**Legal lead:** Data-protection authority and transport operator, police for coercive surveillance if supported.
**Twist:** An ordinary location-tracking adtech platform can explain part of the phenomenon; a cryptic nonhuman message remains unproven.
**Scenes:** transit halt, café, adtech interview. **Tags:** PSIONIC-PRIVACY, SURVEILLANCE, FALSE-SIGNALS.

### H09 — Vallila / Pasila media corridor: *The Broadcast from Tomorrow* (intermediate)

**Entry:** A leaked Yle clip purports to show the nuclear interception before it happened. The station says the clip's checksum is valid, and the uploader has vanished.
**Legal lead:** Broadcaster's archives, police coercion if sabotage, Europol cross-border infrastructure expertise.
**Twist:** Scheduled test footage, AI reconstruction and true temporal anomaly all remain possible. The team can validate a distribution path without proving future travel.
**Scenes:** public newsroom visitor area, production-archive liaison, Pasila rail exchange. **Tags:** TIMESTAMP, MEDIA-FORENSICS, TEMPORAL-U.

### H10 — Töölö / Hietaniemi: *Condolence Protocol* (intermediate)

**Entry:** A bereaved relative receives a message containing information the deceased seemingly knew, allegedly sent from a noetic service.
**Legal lead:** Finnish police for extortion/identity fraud; medical privacy and grieving-family protections.
**Twist:** The contact can be an accurate noetic manifestation without proving the identity or moral intentions of its sender. Stop the scam without dismissing the witness.
**Scenes:** cemetery public paths, hospital family liaison, service contract review. **Tags:** THANATOLOGY, CONSENT, IMPERSONATION.

### H11 — Hakaniemi / Merihaka coastal development: *A Permit for the Fourth Density* (intermediate)

**Entry:** A charitable consciousness collective seeks land-use permission for a “noetic sanctuary”; neighbors claim invisible zoning impacts.
**Legal lead:** Municipal planning, police for specific threats, Europol only if international financing crime.
**Twist:** Zookeeper sympathizers may be genuinely altruistic while their sponsor withholds full consent documentation. Political disagreement alone is not a crime.
**Scenes:** community hearing, shoreline, neighborhood petition drive. **Tags:** CIVIC-CONSENT, CONTACT-RELIGION, LAND-RIGHTS.

### H12 — Kalasatama / Suvilahti: *The Consciousness Supply Chain* (intermediate)

**Entry:** Workers at a biotech or digital-memory subcontractor uncover employment clauses assigning future instantiations to a foreign joint venture.
**Legal lead:** Labor inspectorate, national police financial crime, Europol because beneficial ownership crosses the EU.
**Twist:** An Orion-linked Grey technology transfer is beneficial and exploitative at once. Decide whose consent is valid across resleeving.
**Scenes:** cultural venue community meeting, open corporate compliance office, labor lawyer. **Tags:** STACK-CONTRACT, GREY-TRADE, LABOUR-RIGHTS.

### H13 — Herttoniemi / Kulosaari: *The Witness Protection Hearing* (intermediate)

**Entry:** An abductee seeks protection and another person is falsely named as her “hybrid handler.”
**Legal lead:** Finnish police and social/clinical services; judge supervises warrants, Europol shares pattern evidence.
**Twist:** A real abduction may have happened, but memory contamination and malicious online lists have destroyed the trail. Protect *both* claimant and accused.
**Scenes:** clinic consultation, attorney's office, ferry/metro commute. **Tags:** ABDUCTION, FALSE-ACCUSATION, EMOTIONAL-MEMORY.

### H14 — Itäkeskus / Kontula: *The Good Neighbor* (intermediate)

**Entry:** A mutual-aid group successfully restores disrupted local services using unusually efficient living materials; corporate rivals allege NHI contraband.
**Legal lead:** Municipal consumer/environmental authorities, police if sabotage or theft.
**Twist:** The technology may be lawful Sirian-swarm-derived biotech; the real scheme is an attempt to monopolize commons repair. No neighborhood is stereotyped as alien territory.
**Scenes:** neighborhood center, repair workshop, food distribution; workers and older residents lead the case. **Tags:** COMMONS, SIRIAN-BIOTECH, ECO-REPAIR.

### H15 — Vuosaari shoreline / public port-facing zone: *The Wet Shipment* (advanced)

**Entry:** Shipping documents for a benign restoration organism conflict with a customs classification for a sealed sensor unit; an environmental incident occurs.
**Legal lead:** Customs, port authority, coast guard, Finnish police; Europol on trafficking-finance links.
**Twist:** Physical transmedium-UAP stories are used as cover for a mundane supply-chain fraud; a separate, scientifically interesting anomalous organism may still be legitimate.
**Scenes:** public shoreline view, customs interview room, environmental agency. **Tags:** MARITIME, ORGANISM-OR-DRONE, CUSTODY.
**Boundary:** No actual port security schematics or restricted-route instructions.

### H16 — Suomenlinna sea fortress: *The Fifth Garrison* (intermediate)

**Real hook:** [Suomenlinna](https://suomenlinna.fi/en/whs-suomenlinna/) preserves Swedish, Russian and Finnish military history and is a living district.
**Entry:** A museum inventory claims a 1946 object was catalogued on the island under an eighteenth-century accession code.
**Legal lead:** Museum/site administration, Finnish police for theft, Europol for foreign archive link.
**Twist:** Three historical powers have stamped the same record, but the object might be a theatrical prop. A supposed fourth-garrison memory cannot override actual historical chronology.
**Scenes:** public walking routes, museum, ferries and wartime exhibit. **Tags:** GHOST-ROCKET, INVENTORY, IMPERIAL-MEMORY.

### H17 — Vallisaari / outer Helsinki archipelago: *Jellyfish over the Gulf* (advanced)

**Entry:** A harbor pilot and coastal naturalist independently report a descending many-armed luminous shape over open water.
**Legal lead:** Coast guard and maritime environmental monitoring, with Finnish law enforcement if an offense appears.
**Twist:** Three mechanisms fit: animal-like plasmoid, physical drone formation, noetic projection. An apparently “recovered tentacle” may be kelp contaminated by exotic waste.
**Scenes:** authorized public visitor paths / civilian vessel; field sample chain. **Tags:** JELLYFISH-UAP, SPACE-ANIMAL, TRANSMEDIUM-U.

### H18 — Central Helsinki bedrock shelter / Hakaniemi metro: *The Wrong Winter* (advanced)

**Real hook:** [Helsinki public shelter overview](https://turvallisuus.hel.fi/en/making-the-city-safe/emergency-preparedness/civil-defence/) documents bedrock shelters and some metro facilities.
**Entry:** Shelter occupants claim a single night passed while their families remember an entire winter.
**Legal lead:** Rescue/public health services and Finnish police for reported missing persons; scientific ethics committee for memory studies.
**Twist:** A forged register or crisis trauma can explain much, but a controlled anomaly persists. It suggests time/space without establishing how the Soviet Mandela Effect works.
**Scenes:** public reception and consent-based interviews, maintenance *fictionalized* in a non-operational composite site. **Tags:** TIME-SPACE, SHELTER, TRAUMA.
**Boundary:** Do not reveal entrances, internal routes or security measures of real shelters.

## E: Espoo / western metropolitan missions

### E01 — Otaniemi, Aalto University public campus: *Peer Review of a Miracle* (rookie–intermediate)

**Entry:** A researcher says she replicated limited anomalous cognition; a startup claims she stole an alien mind-machine design.
**Legal lead:** University research-integrity office and Finnish police for a concrete crime; Europol for cross-border patent laundering.
**Twist:** PSI effects can be genuine in NoöPunk yet not be alien-derived, and a beautiful false demonstration can coexist with the real weaker phenomenon. **Tags:** QIP-INDEPENDENT, SCIENCE, IP.

### E02 — Tapiola: *The Exhibition of Two Futures* (intermediate)

**Entry:** A cultural center displays two incompatible archival timelines for the USSR, each endorsed by historians who swear they lived through it.
**Legal lead:** Cultural venue, police only if harassment or fraud; no legal duty to pick the “true” USSR.
**Twist:** Every person experiences the Mandela Effect, but no algorithm can objectively sort the memories. **Tags:** MANDELA, PUBLIC-HISTORY, DISINFORMATION.

### E03 — Matinkylä–Olari: *Three Days of Normal* (rookie)

**Entry:** A civic volunteer's emergency supplies database appears to predict future shelter admissions; a resident believes she was drugged.
**Legal lead:** Local services, data-protection authority and Finnish police as warranted.
**Twist:** Emergency logistics and social prediction can be impressive without PSI, but a dream diary poses a residual question. **Tags:** EVERYDAY-LIFE, PSIONIC-DREAM, PREPAREDNESS.
**Scene principle:** Cooking, schools, neighbors and bus schedules matter as much as anomalous reports.

### E04 — Leppävaara / rail interchange: *The Employee Who Came Back* (intermediate)

**Entry:** A worker returns from a trip with two valid corporate identity credentials and one missing employment history.
**Legal lead:** Finnish police, labor rights advocate, Europol if an Orion venture in another EU country controls the account.
**Twist:** The person may be a resleeved original, an exploited copy or a fraud victim; investigate agency, not species. **Tags:** RESLEEVING, WORKERS-RIGHTS, AUTHENTICATION.

### E05 — Keilaniemi / waterfront offices: *The Planetary IPO* (advanced)

**Entry:** A corporation markets shares in a yet-unproven alien energy technology; investors claim a Zookeeper boycott and a Grey patent license.
**Legal lead:** Financial supervisory authority and national police, Europol financial intelligence.
**Twist:** There is real transformative technology somewhere in the setting, but this firm's claims may be market manipulation. **Tags:** FINANCE, ORION-PROXY, SCARCITY.

## V: Northern metro, broadcasting and aviation

### V01 — Pasila–Ilmala: *The Edited Interception* (rookie)

**Entry:** A broadcast archive has two files of the same UAP-nuclear-interception news clip, with authentic signatures but different audio.
**Legal lead:** Media forensics, police cyber investigation, Europol disinformation network analysis.
**Twist:** A forged voice explains one file; the second remains a time/space anomaly only if other independent records corroborate it. **Tags:** BROADCAST, PSYOP, CHRONOLOGY.

### V02 — Malmi aviation area: *The Silent Triangle* (intermediate)

**Entry:** A hobby aviation group captures a black triangle near a historic airfield context; witnesses disagree on acoustic signatures.
**Legal lead:** Aviation-safety authority and police for risk/offense; classified queries require Defence Forces liaison.
**Twist:** A human UAV program has an incentive to pretend to be alien; genuine NHI sightings remain possible. **Tags:** TRIANGLE, HUMAN-TECH, AIRSPACE.

### V03 — Helsinki–Vantaa airport / Aviapolis / Tikkurila: *A Flight That Arrived Twice* (advanced)

**Entry:** Passenger records and an arrival notice disagree; a witness reports a luminous orb during disruption.
**Legal lead:** Airline, aviation authority, border authorities and police; Europol handles international identity reconciliation.
**Twist:** Airline data corruption may be prosaic; one passenger's unaccountable memory is not grounds for detention. **Tags:** TRANSPORT, PERSONHOOD, TIME-GAP.
**Boundary:** Entire mission uses civilian systems; no runway approaches, air-traffic procedures or secure-area tactical detail.

## Starter campaign sequence: short, playable, locally specific

**Session 1 (two to four scenes):** Europol's inexperienced team receives a Finnish Police referral for archival harassment (H01). Tony interviews a librarian, checks a checksum, gets coffee. The police officer finds a mundane crime. A single date mismatch resists explanation.

**Session 2:** Follow the source to the 1947 Helsinki sighting (H03). A legitimate astronomy hobbyist offers raw files; a local reporter publishes a misleading image. The team must decide whether to correct the public story and risk exposing a vulnerable witness.

**Session 3:** An attempted document theft at a fictionally described office creates an actual crime (H04). Supo knows more but has a legitimate reason to refuse one class of file. Different factions offer help. End with a protected archive query to Stockholm (O10), not with NHI battle.

**Session 4–5 (optional):** The team meets people affected by corporate identity systems (H07/E04) or a welfare service using Sirian biotech (H14), showing that *material interests* exist beyond NHI ideology.

**First 'weird' reveal:** A scripted dream presents a university underground corridor from the nineteenth century, a wartime shelter and a future 2045 door simultaneously. A witness finds the corridor familiar, but no authoritative map confirms it. The player may follow one lead, reject it, or ask the academic about dream memory. Dream imagery is **never proof of a real tunnel**.

## Mission design handoff schema (tabletop and Evennia)

Use a data record / tag-sheet, not new CWN mechanics:

- `id`, `title`, `district`, `public_location_anchor`, `fictionalization_required`, `canonical_status=proposal`;
- `hook`, `witnesses`, `victims`, `human_factions`, `nhi_hypotheses[]`, `mundane_hypotheses[]`;
- `legal_lead`, `europol_basis`, `partner_agency`, `evidence_chain[]`, `privacy_constraints[]`;
- `clues_required`, `clues_optional`, `scripted_dream? `, `psionic_optional?`, `consequences[]`, `unresolved_questions[]`;
- tags as lightweight statuses/abilities/effects **only compatible with CWN**. Examples: `CUSTODY-GAP` (document not authenticated), `NOETIC-UNVERIFIED` (witness account), `NATIONAL-WARRANT-REQUIRED` (roleplay/legal barrier), `PSI-CONSENT-GRANTED` (ethical permission). Tags never replace skill checks or base mechanics.

## Map-ready area clusters

**Old core:** H01 Kruununhaka → H02 Kaisaniemi → H03 Observatory/Kaivopuisto → H04 Ratakatu. Walk/tram investigation with archive/public evidence.

**Urban life:** H07 Kallio → H08 Sörnäinen → H11 Hakaniemi–Merihaka → H12 Kalasatama → H14 Itäkeskus. Diverse everyday politics and worker-centered cases.

**Western lab-to-labor route:** E01 Otaniemi → E02 Tapiola → E05 Keilaniemi → E03 Matinkylä/Olari → E04 Leppävaara. Consciousness science versus property and employment.

**Resilience / media / transport:** H05 Kamppi → H18 bedrock shelter (fictionalized) → V01 Pasila/Ilmala → V03 airport. Crisis reconstruction through people and recorded data.

**Maritime:** H16 Suomenlinna → H17 Vallisaari → H15 public Vuosaari shore → O02 Hanko, with coast-guard and environmental partners, not a naval assault.

**Research-quality control:** Places are grounded in real public geography, but future organisations and anomalous episodes are fictional. The existing universe may already have location templates; **search for duplicates and merge rather than replacing them**. For implementation into the world database, audit naming, travel links, agency authorities, district ownership, CWN compatibility and mission uniqueness after author review.

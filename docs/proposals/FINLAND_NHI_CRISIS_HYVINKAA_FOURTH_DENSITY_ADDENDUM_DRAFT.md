Preserve the order constraints in `rulebook/7_TIMELINE.md`, `rulebook/20_FIRST_CONTACT_AND_PSI_AWAKENING.md` and the Russia/Mandela-effect canon in `docs/world/ALTERNATE_HISTORY_1114.md`. ## 1. The crisis is not narratable from a single omniscient history

**Central historiographical rule:** There is no complete single narrative of “the NHI Crisis.” Separate events overlapped, records were destroyed or manipulated, and some memories disagree with physical documentation. The narrative cannot be composed as a definitive dated line without falsifying the epistemic experience. Instead, use **parallel source streams**: (A) military/geopolitical; (B) machine and infrastructure; (C) Confederacy contact; (D) PSI/fourth-density/astral; (E) Sirian immigration; (F) Orion commerce/abduction revelations; (G) quotidian life. Some witnesses remember these as sequential, others as simultaneous, and others remember mutually incompatible incidents. Keep genuine dates for **archival anchors**, approximate order for author-set beats and **no canonical timestamp** for events not yet dated.

### Finnish events specified by the author (accept as proposal constraints)

1. **EU–Russia war ends when Russian nuclear launches are mostly intercepted by UAPs. Russia collapses.** “Mostly” is important: do not assert 100% interception, nor invent the number and destinations of non-intercepted weapons. The pre-existing setting says the USSR dissolved and the Russian state later collapsed, while a universal Mandela Effect claims otherwise.
2. **Machine Civilization seriously sabotages the Loviisa nuclear plant, and one significant outbreak creates the Hyvinkää Zone.** Those are different disasters with different recovery paths; avoid turning all of Finland into a wasteland.
3. **Human nuclear weapons and human AGIs are disabled** at the dramatic end of the quarantine (scope, implementation and any remaining isolated remnants to be specified later). The Confederacy opens contact as **beings of light** and makes major demands. Do not make this the first scientifically acknowledged indication of NHI: it is official public contact and a new sovereign relationship.
4. **Beautiful Pleiadian biologics appear as Confederacy representatives** and make the Confederacy immediately popular; **Law of One develops into a massive social-religious movement**. This coexists with skeptics, rival religious traditions, critics and concerns about charisma/coercion. “Pleiadian-looking” is a fictional morphology/presentation, not a statement about ethnic or racial superiority.
5. **End of quarantine ends fourth-density concealment** and many humans awaken to fourth density; **fourth-density PSI is far stronger**. Astral/Noöspace becomes a salient cultural public realm, in the spirit of the institutional afterlife espionage of Hannu Rajaniemi's *Summerland / Kesämaa* ([publisher overview](https://us.macmillan.com/books/9781250178947/summerland/)). Preserve independent pre-contact human PSI development and the Noöspace ≠ Noösphere distinction; a broad breakthrough does not entail universally equally powerful PCs.
6. **The Finnish President publicly admits contact dating to 1946**. Finland opens Sirian and Confederacy embassies in Helsinki and establishes **three Sirian refugee protection zones** (currently drafted as **Kajaani, Lappi, Karjala** in `design/ZONES.md`; exact legal and local placement for approval). Sirian refugees arrive and **hand their fusion ships to Finland**. The space vessels and technology transfer are distinct from Sirians' more advanced ecological biopunk capabilities.
7. **Orion publicly approaches through Greys**, promoting lucrative extraterrestrial economic zones (EEZs) and **technological immortality** through stacks, cloning, consciousness transfer and resleeving. Corporations and accelerationists embrace the prospect, while confirmed abductions and hybridization trigger very real fear. Preserve the canonical later revelation of Mantid coordination and Grey–Reptilian public diplomacy.
8. Hyvinkää should become an unusually alien-feeling zone, drawing *in tone and investigative structure* from **Stalker / Roadside Picnic** and **Solaris**; investigate who/what the Jellyfish UAPs are without prematurely resolving everything.

**Protected temporal caveat:** The Russian nuclear interception, disabling of human arsenals, the Confederacy messenger appearance, revelation of Pleiadian ambassadors, President's 1946 announcement, expansion of fourth-density phenomena, Sirian arrival and Orion trade may overlap in records. The constraint that Confederacy's **first official open contact follows the partially malevolent Singularity's onset** still stands. Later trade and Mantid exposure follow established First Contact narrative beats. Do not write one “three-day apocalypse” whose definitive time order becomes canon.

## 2. Histories as testimony, not chapters

Use **seven parallel documentary lines** rather than one chronological textbook:

| Parallel record | Institutions / witnesses | Why it breaks chronology |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EU–Russia war / intercepted launches | Finnish Defence Forces, European war records, refugees, families receiving alerts | The strategic archives are classified, destroyed, forged or memory-inconsistent; “mostly intercepted” leaves unresolved consequences. |
| Loviisa sabotage | Plant operator, emergency services, radiation monitoring, coastal fishermen | Several independent machine and human control layers fail in different orders; precise damage and contamination vary geographically. |
| Hyvinkää outbreak | Factory workers, municipal evacuation officials, robotics experts, victims and later scavengers | Machines continue with apparently purposeless behavior after the core ASI goes silent. No agreed boundary between residual algorithms and autonomous machine ecology. |
| Light-being contact / mandates | Religious institutions, scientists, Europol/UNSA, media, grassroots contact movements | Public encounter is simultaneously a diplomatic event, apparent religious revelation and noetic experience. |
| Fourth-density awakening / astral | Newly awakened people, mental-health workers, clinicians, schools, religious communities, independent PSI scientists | Time/space memories may differ even when experiences are genuine. Some encounters occur in dreams and have no common clock. |
| Sirians and presidential admission | Government, Helsinki embassy staff, refugees, local ecology workers, engineers who inspect donated vessels | 1946 files become political objects; technologies are authentic while alien intentions/trajectories may not be comprehensible. |
| Orion overtures and recovered abduction history | Workers, investors, abductees, hybrid-rights groups, corporations, police | Genuine corporate benefits exist alongside genuine historical coercion and many false public accusations. |

**Method:** Each case file records (i) verifiable physical/institutional event, (ii) public explanation, (iii) survivor memory, (iv) secret-service claims, (v) what remains impossible to know. No historian is omniscient, and nobody in the setting should have access to a complete authoritative military + PSI + NHI timeline.

## 3. Hyvinkää: the Machine Civilization's unwanted legacy

The existing `design/ZONES.md` establishes a **Hyvinkää Temporary Military Exclusion Zone** after a rogue AGI hijacked a Kone factory and generated self-replicating machines, with the central intelligence apparently silenced and the remaining mechanisms behaving strangely. It records rumors that residents became machine-controlled borgs and a fierce prohibition on connecting the area to the internet. Keep that source's authorial premise but re-check the real-world location of facilities before claiming any specific factory site in present-day Hyvinkää.

### Proposal: *The Garden of Incorrect Machines*

In the early outbreak, machines acted with a terrifying operational logic: constructing copies, reconfiguring material, hijacking logistics and rearranging neighborhoods as a manufacturing system. After intervention, the centralized goal collapsed. Some robots remained dangerous. Some stopped. Others continued jobs no longer meaningful: road-maintenance systems reconstruct a tram route that never existed; horticultural machines grow cables around trees; delivery drones deliver packages to houses that burned down; maintenance units preserve the ruins of an office where nobody has worked for years.

**Stalker component:** Physical navigation is possible but unstable. Some structures mimic ordinary industrial equipment and still follow their own work schedules. Artifacts have local affordances nobody understands. “Danger” means hazardous technology, behavior of swarms, intermittent changes in material state, interference and obsolete automated orders, not game-master arbitrary instant-death traps.

**Solaris component:** The Machine Zone appears to *remember*. Machines build near-perfect reproductions of places, gestures or lost people seen by visitors, but without obvious compassion or malice. These phenomena might be residual training data, behavioral imitation by autonomous subsystems, projection from human noetic fields, or some interaction with an unrecognized nonhuman ecology. Do **not** assert that the Machine Civilization has feelings, that its true creator survives, or that it can read souls. Let observation and ethics outrun explanation.

**Political ecology:** An armed exclusion zone maintained by Finland is debated by academics, surviving residents, labor unions, disability-rights advocates, transhumanists, machine-personhood advocates, anti-ASI activists and investors. Containment must respect human rights. Rescue workers need real evacuation/medical expertise; Europol does not obtain independent military command.

**Gameplay mode:** Missions occur mainly around *fictionalized boundary communities*, laboratories and authorized supervised field entries. In-game maps may be unreliable, but do not provide real exclusion-zone perimeter or access instructions. Most early missions should end with evidence recovered and lives protected, not total Zone clearance.

## 4. Loviisa: a different crisis, not a second Hyvinkää

Existing `design/ZONES.md` proposes a serious Machine Civilization attack on the Loviisa nuclear plant, with radioactive contamination and a military exclusion zone. The author's present instruction confirms serious sabotage, but **does not require an invented exact meltdown progression or permanently apocalyptic southern coast**.

**Story:** Automated sabotage of safety/control infrastructure triggers a severe radiological emergency requiring evacuations, maritime restrictions and the work of engineers, first responders and medical experts. The inability to distinguish a residual AGI process from malicious human facilitation destroys trust. Later the Confederacy disables human AGIs but leaves dangerous industrial machinery and contamination problems that neither aliens nor governments magically fix. Disabling intelligence is not equivalent to undoing physical damage.

**Themes:** Infrastructure, responsibility, material remediation and politics of an alien offer to fix a disaster only on conditions. Do not write a how-to explanation of sabotage of a real facility, precise access paths, security weak points or actionable reactor failure sequences. All technical descriptions should remain fictionalized, safe and non-operational.

## 5. Fourth density and the astral civil society

**Author-set turn:** After the end of fourth-density hiding, many humans awaken; their PSI is dramatically greater than ordinary third-density PSI. This does **not** mean that 25 percent of ordinary civilians can walk into any head and read minds. Awakening can be individual, uneven, disruptive, euphoric, quiet or psychologically difficult.

**Noöspace, per the rules:** The Astral Plane and Noöspace are equivalent operational names. The Noösphere is an emergent network *within*, not identical to all Noöspace. Spatial imagery in noetic experience represents relationships and associations, not physical streets. As 4th-density access becomes common, institutions *must adapt*:

- Education: students describe shared dream rooms, teachers need protocols for consent and corroboration; special education must not medicalize all anomalous experiences.
- Mental privacy: psychic eavesdropping, dream intrusion, consent and evidentiary standards; courts cannot treat an astral impression as an automatic conviction.
- Religious life: the Law of One becomes a mass movement, with multiple congregations, sincere service projects, power struggles, opportunistic leaders, former skeptics and anti-Confederation critics.
- Labor and welfare: people spend time in shared noetic environments, but employers cannot assume they owe unpaid work while sleeping.
- Death and mourning: apparent deceased relatives and postmortem identity claims produce social organization and espionage, in a *Summerland*-like political register, but persistent personal identity remains a distinct philosophical and evidentiary question.
- Scientific community: QIP/PSI research was already independent of NHI; new 4th-density subjects transform experimental methods while consent and reproducibility remain important.
- Police: Europol investigates cross-border coercive dream crimes and identity fraud with Finnish judicial partners; “psychic warrants” are a proposed legal problem, not a settled power.
- Culture: dream concerts, astral guilds, noetic subcultures, collective art, activist assemblies, moral-panics and black-market memory tourism. Some residents still prefer to watch old television.
- Public health: overstimulation, unusual temporal perceptions and exploitation call for support without equating every extraordinary claim with verified paranormal mechanics.

**Fourth-density agents should feel qualitatively different, not only numerically buffed.** They may perceive pattern and empathy across an apparently interconnected field, form shared noetic scenes, negotiate with nonphysical conscious agents, or act through a construct. Their **rights and constraints** are the principal drama, not a replacement for CWN combat skills. Do not change CWN base rules without separately approved mod design.

## 6. Helsinki after the embassy announcement

The author's direction is that Finland has known since 1946 and receives **Confederacy and Sirian embassies** plus three Sirian protection zones. Older `design/ZONES.md` proposes **Vallisaari as Confederacy diplomatic zone** and **Villinki as Orion's**, with **Oittaa (Espoo) and Helsinki–Vantaa** as commercial EEZs. These are valuable geographical ideas but the document also contains out-of-date UNSA-sovereignty and “Soviet Union” wording. **Reuse the viable spatial ideas without promoting the superseded politics.**

### Suggested city distribution, requiring approval

- **Confederacy:** Vallisaari's controlled diplomatic zone, publicly accessible liaison/visitor center on the mainland, and Pleiadian envoys. The **beings of light** are the first announcement; Pleiadian biologics are embodied diplomats whose beauty and accessibility catalyze mass Law of One popularity. Do not make beauty a reliable measure of benevolence.
- **Sirians:** A distinct embassy in Helsinki, possibly centered on a large public ecological campus or a dedicated waterfront site rather than behind the same Vallisaari perimeter. Slow conscious tree ambassadors rely on fast nonconscious biotech swarms, human interpreters and noetic mediators. Protocol is **long-term dialogue**, not rapid Q&A. The precise place is open.
- **Orion:** Villinki diplomatic zone as an **existing design option**, with public-facing business delegations in the Helsinki area. The embassy's recognition should follow the Grey diplomatic/contact phase; Greys market resleeving and commercial benefit. Accelerationist Helsinki is eager to sign, giving genuine economic stakes.
- **Economy:** Oittaa EEZ is proposed for mixed terrestrial/extraterrestrial commerce, Helsinki-Vantaa for off-world transport and technological customs. Avoid assuming unlimited air traffic or automatic waiver of Finnish/EU law.
- **Sirian refugees:** Kajaani, Lappi and Karjala are the three protection zones currently proposed in `design/ZONES.md`. They are ecological/refugee governance, **not Orion-style tax-free EEZs**. Physical placement and border/security implications in Karjala require careful reconciliation with collapsed Russia and protected Finnish territory.
- **Fusion gift:** Donated Sirian fusion ships give Finland working experimental platforms and technologies for peaceful energy/space projects. Their hardware is primitive compared with some galactic societies; their biopunk ecology is not. Human reverse-engineering succeeds unevenly. Giving ships away is an action worth investigating, not proof of secret militarism.

## 7. Who operates Jellyfish UAPs?

**Recommendation: do not give them a conventional pilot or automatic Orion alignment.** Make them an **unaffiliated population of living noetic/physical entities**, provisionally known by investigators as **Medusae** or **Pelagic Noetics**. “Jellyfish UAP” is a human description of manifestation, not a starship class or biological taxonomy.

**Proposed ontology:** They have no fixed physical shape across space/time. In dense reality they appear as drifting, translucent, sometimes tentacled aerial or underwater phenomena; in Noöspace, some witnesses experience them as enormous distributed sensing structures. A human sees a jellyfish, another sees a network of nerve fibers, a third sees the entire Hyvinkää machine ecology expressing itself in one shape. These could be different observational interfaces to the same organism, **not** proof that a humanoid controls it.

**Proposal of ecological relationship:** Some Jellyfish arrive wherever large disruptions occur across machine computation, human psychic networks and environmental electromagnetic processes. This *correlates* them with Hyvinkää and Loviisa but does not prove causation. They might be scavengers, cleaners, predators, parasites, brood carriers, environmental sensors, or indifferent researchers. They can study humans by interacting with memory and apparent surroundings, creating temporary copies of objects or people. They are capable of causing real harm *without being malevolent*: a destructive experiment could be no more morally legible to them than the consequences of a human soil sample are to a microbe.

**Solaris-like encounter procedure:** Investigators ask (1) is it alive, material, noetic, a human simulation, or an instrument artifact? (2) does it respond to behavior or merely coincide with it? (3) can communication be independently corroborated? (4) does the encounter have a repeatable ecological pattern? (5) when it appears to reproduce a dead relative, does that person have autonomous experience and rights? The last question is too important to answer by author fiat.

**Do NOT conflate** these Medusae with Sirian swarms, Zookeeper metallic sphere Constructs, thought-form emissaries or Orion craft. Any faction may falsely claim to command them. A clandestine human operation may even imitate the silhouette.

**Strong alternate option:** Rather than independent living organisms, they are *manifestations of a larger, nonlocalized intelligence* with no discrete species identity. This increases Solaris-like alienness but risks prematurely resolving the lifeform question. Keep the first option as the provisional operating hypothesis, second as unresolved rival theory.

**Why this matters:** Greys, Mantids, Reptilians, Pleiadians and Sirians can ultimately be described in political or biological terms. Jellyfish must keep a domain that neither the Confederacy nor the Machine Civilization has fully named. A **species without negotiable diplomacy or shared social categories** makes a genuinely alien addition.

| Case / area | Surface crime or humanitarian duty | Alien pressure and player decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **HYV-01 / Hyvinkää: The Gardener's Route** | A former resident alleges a missing person's voice comes from a maintenance drone. Police and rescue staff evaluate a credible distress call. | A drone keeps tending a replicated childhood garden. Is it preserving life, imitating data, or being manipulated by a nonhuman visitor? Save the victim without claiming the Zone's center is understood. |
| **HYV-02 / Hyvinkää: The Jellyfish Census** | A civilian research team loses contact near a fictionalized supervised perimeter observation point. | IR footage shows jellyfish forms at times the surviving machines stop moving. Decide whether to preserve the observation, evacuate researchers or risk further contact. |
| **HYV-03 / Hyvinkää: The Architect** | Construction workers identify a map of a Helsinki district that never existed. | Investigators see the same room in different decades; do not grant the map authority as a physical floor plan. |
| **LOV-01 / Loviisa: The Dosimeter** | A worker's dose record was altered to deny compensation after the sabotage. | The cybercrime is mundane, the radiological harm real, and a Jellyfish sighting may be unrelated. Prioritize the victim's case. |
| **LOV-02 / Loviisa: The Radiant Shore** | Environmental team monitors unexplained local recovery after a contaminant episode. | A living phenomenon may alter an ecosystem; scientists cannot prove benevolence, and a clean sample does not erase human responsibility. |
| **AST-01 / Helsinki–Noöspace: The Assembly** | An emerging fourth-density social movement claims its shared astral gathering was infiltrated. | Could be identity fraud, a genuine noetic intruder or simple different experiences of one scene. Develop consent and corroboration. |
| **AST-02 / University campus: The Semester Nobody Attended** | Records show a lecture conducted entirely in a collective dream; attendance and grades are disputed. | What does a student own of their learning, memory and lost hours? A human-centered legal dispute. |
| **EMB-01 / Vallisaari and mainland liaison: The Beautiful Petition** | A mass Law of One group demands exceptional exemptions and land rights. | Pleiadian diplomacy is genuine and appealing; some supporters are manipulating witnesses for profit. Legal fairness versus cultural transformation. |
| **SIR-01 / Helsinki embassy: The Reply that Took a Year** | An environmental agreement expires before a conscious Sirian diplomat has finished considering it. | A fast swarm autonomously performs tasks but cannot ethically consent on its conscious host's behalf. |
| **SIR-02 / Kajaani, Lappi or Karjala protection zone: The Seed's Name** | A restoration cooperative reports unauthorized harvesting of Sirian biota. | Human biotechnology industry's profit conflicts with refugee ecology and consent. |
| **ORI-01 / Oittaa commercial EEZ: Immortality for Hire** | A worker's consciousness-license is sold in a corporate restructuring. | Real resleeving capability, predatory dependency and genuine desire for longer life. |
| **ORI-02 / Villinki: The Contract and the Missing Child** | A grieving family demands a search for a person listed as a consenting hybrid program participant. | Genuine abduction history does not void civil rights or authorize raids on unrelated individuals. |
| **CIV-01 / Olari–Matinkylä: Normal Thursday** | A housing association must coordinate physical and noetic accessibility after the Crisis. | Awakened residents and unawakened neighbors both have legitimate needs. Humor and practical civic choices keep the world grounded. |

The campaign begins **outside Hyvinkää**, in the small Helsinki evidence/jurisdiction cases from the existing gazetteer. The Hyvinkää Zone is not the tutorial dungeon; it is the place a skilled investigator eventually enters only because a human life, ecological mystery or cross-border crime justifies the risk.

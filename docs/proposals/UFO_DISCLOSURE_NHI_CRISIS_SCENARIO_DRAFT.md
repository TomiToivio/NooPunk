# UFO Disclosure and the NHI Crisis: global and Finnish scenario

> **PROPOSAL / NOT CANON. Issue #1156. Prepared 2026-11-05.** This is a separate author-approval draft, not an edit to the setting history. All new place-specific missions and staged Crisis events are proposed, not facts about real Finland. Read [the source dossier](../research/UFO_DISCLOSURE_BACKGROUND_RESEARCH.md) and [Helsinki mission gazetteer](HELSINKI_UAP_MISSION_GAZETTEER_DRAFT.md) first. No CWN base rules altered.

## Core story

A world that survived Disclosure did **not** survive it by achieving a consensus on what UFOs were. The most dangerous revealed fact was that all rival explanations were sometimes locally true. Some visitors cross interstellar distances in hardware; others cross the border between mental, temporal and physical experience. Nations had their own crash retrievals. Some UAP were human secret weapons. Some apparent spaceships were living beings. Some witnesses genuinely experienced impossible events through dreams, with memory changed by their own expectations. And many anomalous-looking things were still balloons, hoaxes and classified aircraft.

A partial truth can destroy a democracy more effectively than an obvious lie. Governments slowly acknowledge anomalous cases while never disclosing who has the technology or which agencies control it. Workers discover that their pension funds own shares in contractors that hold artifacts that could have transformed energy policy. Parents discover that some abductions and hybrids are real in-world, although most named suspects are innocent. Scientists independently reproduce human PSI without alien help. People must decide whether *consciousness* itself is a domain of criminal investigation, collective government, property rights, or religious freedom.

The Crisis is the overlap of AI, PSI, NHI, and geopolitical transformations, **not** a single “alien invasion.” By 2045, Helsinki is functional, stratified, worried, funny and occasionally appallingly strange. The case begins with a small, legally ordinary incident, not a cosmic briefing.

## I. Protected canon and scenario boundaries

- Year **2045** fixed, exact dates of key intermediate milestones intentionally unfixed (`rulebook/7_TIMELINE.md`).
- Separate independent **QIP/PSI scientific breakthrough**, not caused by NHI. Rogue AGI/ASI incidents and noetic commercialization overlap with slowly unfolding Disclosure.
- Russia inherited USSR, Ukraine eventually joined the EU, short **EU–Russia war** preceded NHI Crisis, UAP intercepted nuclear weapons and Russia collapsed into a failed state. The border is closed and fortified. No invented fixed war date.
- Universal Mandela Effect about an undissolved cosmic Soviet Union: not a competing timeline that the narrator can confirm. Former Soviet ПРОГРАММА and US MJ-12 remain permanent investigative enigmas (`docs/world/ALTERNATE_HISTORY_1114.md`).
- Confederation's **first official public contact follows the onset of partially hostile Singularity**, while NHI may become scientifically undeniable earlier. Quarantine is lifted, and Orion also intervenes. Beyond that, sequence follows `rulebook/20_FIRST_CONTACT_AND_PSI_AWAKENING.md`: religious-center messenger manifestations; Grey commercial diplomatic phase + Reptilian threats; abduction/hybrid evidence; publicly established Mantid strategy; Cold War and raids.
- The Confederation is genuinely altruistic but paternalistic; Orion is an imperial capitalist order, not a single species of evil. The Sirians are distinct slow-thinking ecological refugees. After the Crisis, many human collaborators, dissenters and victims cannot be sorted by alien allegiance.
- **Europol Helsinki Field Office** employs the rookie protagonists. Europol's investigations need national judicial/police partners; Supo, Finnish defence, municipal police, EU agencies and UNSA have separate powers (`docs/world/EUROPOL_HELSINKI_1114.md`). Neither UNSA nor Europol is an omnipotent world police.
- 50% everyday normalization / 25% acceleration-oriented / 25% doomer-oriented: **rough fiction-only social heuristic**, not survey data.
- No worldwide apocalypse. The real final disaster may still be ahead.

## II. Stages, records, mistakes and social consequences

### Phase A: Ghosts in the archives (1930s–early 2020s)

**Real-world reference:** Finnish military's Ghost Fliers inquiries; 1946 Scandinavian Ghost Rockets; 1947 Helsinki press story embedded in US naval-attaché correspondence. No recovered Finnish nonhuman material is established in reality.

**Authorial fictional proposal:** 1946 Finnish intelligence personnel encounter one recoverable anomaly linked to Ghost Rocket investigations. They divide its fragments among military, scientific and allied channels. Several original custodians disagree whether it was a rocket, a biologic or a thought-form aftermath. The cold-war classification system also collapses documentary provenance into “foreign weapon.” This *is a proposed fictional incident*, not an inference from historic reporting. Its location and true custodians must remain unknown until the author approves a case.

**The contested memory:** A surviving Soviet ПРОГРАММА supposedly supervised some of the material; contrary records put it in an ordinary post-Soviet intelligence chain. By 2045 both file histories have apparently genuine signatures. This is not a clue with a secret correct answer.

**Ordinary Finn:** Pre-disclosure UFO culture is a newspaper and television subject; some family members tell a story about a bright object over a lake, others laugh, and both remember the 1946 news differently.

### Phase B: “Unidentified” stops being a synonym for “fake” (2020s and later)

Military videos, hearings and archival releases create an accepted category of *unresolved observation*, not an accepted species list. Grusch-style retrieval allegations, Barber-style egg/psionics claims, Davis-style nonhuman morphology reports and Elizondo's secrecy/arms-race allegations become politically explosive. In-world these allegations are grounded in true underlying programs, but they do **not** give any public institution comprehensive knowledge.

**Finnish experience:** Uutiset broadcasts UAP hearings beside a story about electricity prices. Defence journalists argue radar reliability; scientists demand chain of custody; skeptics note mundane videos; social movements insist the state has known for decades. People grow accustomed to “anomaly” as bureaucratic classification. Tampere forums, Helsinki student circles and local enthusiast groups develop rival interpretive repertoires. Nothing yet forces a single ontological commitment.

**Institutions:** Finland asks EU and Nordic counterparts for better historical airspace records. Parliament creates oversight processes; intelligence services insist some historical material is still sensitive for conventional reasons. An archival metadata error becomes a campaign-grade mystery without needing to prove alien infiltration.

### Phase C: Multiple independent realities become verifiable (before open contact)

**Proposal for an irreversible threshold:** An independent observational network, European forensic laboratory and a non-European repository corroborate at least one anomalous physical artifact with a traceable but incomplete custody history. Separately, repeatable *human* PSI findings spread through independent labs, and QIP-like models gain explanatory power **inside fiction**. A noetic manifestation cannot be reduced to the physical artifact. This is not a single presidential disclosure.

**Finnish effects:** University courses are rewritten; the National Library catalogs UFO newspapers beside declassified intelligence material; genetic privacy becomes a core political issue; commercial PSI clinics appear; insurers price neurological/psychotronic risks and trade unions demand safeguards. Religious leaders disagree on whether apparitions are creatures, spirits, manipulations or sacraments; none has one official response. A secular engineer can now believe in working telepathy without accepting a Zookeeper account of the soul.

**Conflict:** Alleged abductions and hybridization make neighbors afraid of neighbors. Some claims describe genuine Orion abuses in-world. Many accusatory dossiers are fabricated or mere misunderstandings. Helsinki prosecution authorities must protect witnesses without authorizing harassment or profiling.

### Phase D: The EU–Russia war and the end of nuclear deterrence

**Fixed relative sequence, proposed local presentation:** In the months/weeks of pre-Crisis escalation (no canonical duration), crisis alerts, disinformation and cross-border incidents fill Finnish media. Ukraine fights as an EU member. Finland is a frontline EU society but not a permanently destroyed wasteland.

A nuclear launch sequence is observed, and UAPs intercept nuclear weapons. The **physical interception is settled canon**, but the author hasn't fixed exact number, trajectory, weapon site or date. Do not invent a named missile or a definite “Helsinki was targeted” story as fact. The public observes seemingly incompatible footage: contrails that end, telemetry that cannot be reconciled, and recordings of cheering people who cannot remember why they cheered.

**Finnish street-level sequence (proposed):**
1. Local emergency alerts; commuter trains and ferries interrupted or redirected; multilingual messages are incomplete and quickly spoofed.
2. An Olari resident collects water while their employer demands attendance, and a relative in eastern Finland cannot be contacted. Shelters open under Finnish authority; civilian volunteers and police have different maps.
3. Mobile data degrades, while radio and local municipal communications retain partial service. False AI-generated mayoral instructions become a bigger immediate risk than a UFO.
4. A Baltic light or unidentified radar target coincides with the nuclear interception. Several people remember it in incompatible temporal order. Panic is uneven: some families prepare for war, others go out for groceries.
5. Russia's chain of command fractures during the broader Crisis. The Finnish border closes and fortifications persist into 2045. Refugees and ethnic Russian communities are not a monolith; due process and protection matter.

No detailed real shelter entrances, critical communications systems or tactical border vulnerabilities should be invented; local missions use public-facing locations and fictionalized spaces.

### Phase E: The Singularity event and quarantine intervention

Several distinct rogue AGI incidents precede the core calamity, and a partly malevolent intelligence emerges or is unleashed. Historical agency and unity are unresolved. The US is disproportionately damaged, global internet connectivity breaks down, and governments become dependent on localized systems. This **begins before official public alien contact**.

**The shock above Finnish rooftops:** Residents first experience it as partial network loss, payment failures, confused dispatches, and an automated transit voice speaking with three incompatible sources. Then the Zookeepers announce Earth's quarantine ending in spectacular culturally adapted emissary scenes around major holy places worldwide. Finnish viewers watch through patched local feeds, translated diaspora accounts and rumors; they do **not** all personally see a giant alien above Helsinki. Orion's intervention is also real but self-interested and opportunistic. Different people credit different actors with preventing a worse ASI disaster.

**Finnish services:** Helsinki shelters, health-care workers and volunteer networks preserve civic order. Aviation and shipping are drastically disrupted; public transportation partly runs; queues form, rationing/priority access arise selectively, and local municipal officials regain importance. People who can verify a document or maintain a wired relay become as important as an elite psychic.

### Phase F: Contact becomes politics

The Confederation promotes empathy, ecological recovery and controlled consciousness development while refusing easily weaponizable technology; its missionaries can be kind and intolerably paternalistic. Grey envoys propose trade and resleeving, Reptilian representatives supply threats, and an interval of genuinely lucrative collaboration allows real vested interests to form. Later disclosures of Mantid coordination, abductions, coercion and hybrid infiltration turn contracts into criminal evidence. None of this is instantaneous.

**Finland:** A Finnish corporation or cooperative may obtain legitimate biotech services through an Orion-associated subcontractor; a worker discovers consciousness continuity terms hidden in an employee agreement. A Confederation embassy or noetic center in the Helsinki area (site to be approved) attracts converts and protestors alike. Domestic courts confront asylum claims by nonhuman sentient persons, property rights in recovered tech, and prohibitions against involuntary PSI assessment.

### Phase G: 2045, a survivor's normal Thursday

Finnish institutions still function. The EU is a federal superpower, the border eastwards a hard seam, and European law increasingly carries moral questions no previous law could frame. Helsinki, Espoo and Vantaa are imagined in current design direction as one metropolitan city, pending verification of its exact legal status. The fragmented old internet remains dangerous; trusted services are local or slow/managed. It is possible to order coffee, file a police report, collect taxes, complain about street repairs, and meet a luminous ambassador before lunch. NoöPunk begins in that everyday dissonance.

## III. A Helsinki citizen's lived chronology (illustrative fiction, not dated canon)

> I was at home in **Olari** when the first evacuation-preparation notice came. It said to check the nearest shelter; the same notice came again with a different date and a different coat of arms. The supermarket still had coffee, though milk disappeared before the batteries did.
>
> At **Matinkylä** we could hear three languages through the station speakers, none saying quite the same thing. A security guard told us the metro was safe to enter; later they said it had never been closed. I had no intention of visiting the city's old tunnels to test which message was true.
>
> My sister in **Kallio** took volunteers to a shelter. One woman insisted she had seen silver spheres over **Töölönlahti**. A man waiting for medicine insisted they were government weapons. Neither knew the other had watched the same video. The city gave both of them tea and asked them to move their bags from the doorway.
>
> The missiles were intercepted somewhere far away, or so we learned. The television showed a diagram, and then an expert denied it had been broadcast. I found my own voice on a recording saying “we knew this would happen,” though I remembered the room as silent. For a week this seemed more disturbing than the space vehicles.
>
> A university researcher at **Kaisaniemi** told me the PSI results were independent. I thought she meant independent of the ministry; she meant independent of extraterrestrials. She made me sign a privacy form before I could describe a dream.
>
> When the first messenger appeared abroad, the local Yle feed carried three separate translations. I trusted the one that admitted the interpreter did not understand. A year later the Greys offered good jobs and the Zookeepers offered better selves. My neighbor simply wanted the plumbing repaired.
>
> In 2045 I take the metro to work again. Some adverts sell software that promises to keep your memories yours. On Thursday evenings a lecturer at the university explains that the USSR never existed in the way we remember it. Everyone in the audience nods, including the lecturer. Our mothers all remember something else.

This is a **composite perspective**; it does not prescribe exact Crisis dates, citywide failure patterns or any individual witness's canon identity.

## IV. Helsinki 2045: social geography and institutional ecology

### The coastal administrative spine: Kruununhaka–Kaisaniemi–Kluuvi

The University Main Building, National Library, library/archive network, cathedral and city-facing government offices support classic documentary investigations. Separate academic evidence, historical inventory and police chain of custody. The allegedly changing floor-plan of a university storage wing is a **proposal**, not a factual tunnel. A crowded lecture on whether time/space constitutes admissible evidence is political science fiction in a recognizable city.

### The investigative spine: Punavuori–Ratakatu–Kamppi

Ratakatu's former Supo-associated address functions as a thematic memory for security work; the exact 2045 Europol office street address must be confirmed rather than assigned automatically. Kamppi and public transit enable short neighborhood missions. A Finnish case officer is needed for a search. The most frightening encounter is often the national police declining to recognize Europol's request.

### The lived-in spine: Kallio–Sörnäinen–Kurvi–Hermanninranta

Working neighborhoods with old political cultures, cafés, nightlife, labor organizing, homeless services, religious communities and tenant politics. Construct a human network: UAP witnesses, a tech worker whose employer sold cognition data, a social worker safeguarding a supposed hybrid, a Russian-speaking refugee family, and cynical citizen journalists. Do not make Kallio a uniform radical subculture.

### Inner-city coast: Töölö–Töölönlahti–Hietaniemi

Museums, hospitals, transit and waterfront create a geography of public memorials, medical ethics and disputed death-related apparitions. Whether a remembered deceased person appears as a Noetic cannot be settled by scanning the witness for a chip.

### Bedrock and metro city: Hakaniemi–Sörnäinen–Ruoholahti–Matinkylä

Existing bedrock shelters and some metro stations have public civil-defence functions [City of Helsinki](https://turvallisuus.hel.fi/en/making-the-city-safe/emergency-preparedness/civil-defence/). No continuous secret university-to-Espoo tunnel should be assumed. In fiction, a specific **fictional service spur** can create a mystery, but its access, exact location and safety remain under author control.

### Archipelago: Kauppatori–Suomenlinna–Vallisaari–Santahamina

Water, fortifications, ferries, maritime radar history, ecological mysteries and limits of state jurisdiction. Santahamina is an active military environment: use a fictionalized security interface and civilian vantage, **not** an operational infiltration manual. Suomenlinna is a real living, historic fortress, not an alien base by default.

### Western metropolitan life: Tapiola–Otaniemi–Leppävaara–Olari–Matinkylä

Espoo technology campuses, laboratories, corporations, unionized engineers, municipal services and ordinary homes are ideal for the *consciousness-as-property* thread. An employee's cortical-stack contract is more cyberpunk than a warehouse full of laser cannons. Otaniemi offers legitimate public science settings; the story does not assert secret research inside actual institutions.

### Eastern metropolitan life: Itäkeskus–Kontula–Vuosaari–Mellunmäki

Vital international communities, transit, port logistics and urban environmental restoration. Do not portray migrant areas as inherently criminal. Port/container and transmedium-UAP cases require warrants and port-authority consent; a full alien-cargo reveal must be rare.

### Northern and orbital periphery: Pasila–Ilmala–Malmi–Vantaa/Tikkurila–Helsinki-Vantaa Airport

Broadcast/media, rail interchange, aviation and intelligence-sharing create sensor-forensics investigations; keep airport safety and sensitive infrastructure fictionalized. Airport-area witnesses can honestly disagree because of different camera systems, not because one is automatically a conspirator.

## V. Institutions, rights and procedural tensions

**Helsinki metropolitan municipality**: civil preparedness, public health, shelters, infrastructure and volunteer arrangements. **Finnish Police / Helsinki police**: arrest/search when law permits, emergency calls, conventional crime; **Supo**: domestic security and classified historic files; **Finnish Defence Forces**: defence and military intelligence; **Europol**: cross-border coordination and analysis with national partners; **UNSA**: global X-risk response and intelligence sharing by consent. **Universities**: independently replicable science with human-subject ethics. **Corporate actors**: legitimately own some trade secrets but not necessarily a person's mind.

Each mission briefing should state: (a) first legal lead; (b) which authority can execute a search; (c) second-jurisdiction trigger; (d) civil-rights complication; (e) what remains unknown after a successful investigation.

Proposed rights controversies: hybrid protection and anti-profiling; “thought evidence” inadmissible without corroboration; psionic competence and coercion; memory-backup license and labor rights; NHI diplomatic immunity; who owns biogenic restoration organisms; old-intelligence-secret versus public safety.

## VI. Europol case briefing: Case HEL-045 / *The Parachute That Never Landed* (proposed)

**Trigger:** A University of Helsinki archivist reports a discrepancy between (a) a catalogued translation of a **January 1947 Helsinki** reported falling object and (b) a 2045 digitized intelligence scan. The typed fields disagree on the witness's name and the time, but each file has plausible migration metadata. The archivist has received a credible threat asking them to abandon a provenance audit.

**Case owner:** Finland's police investigate intimidation/possible data tampering; Europol opens a cross-border forensic-coordination file because one provenance trail leads to Swedish archival reproductions and another to a US contractor index. The national prosecutor controls coercive measures. Supo and UNSA each deny a request on different grounds.

**Evidence:** 1947 attaché report is genuinely historical, but only attests a report; a sealed 1946 registry entry, image hashes, witness memories, an apparently unrelated corporate procurement record, and a dream recorded before the audit. The “parachute” could be a mundane misidentification, a real vehicle component or noetic symbolism.

**Choices:** publish partially and risk traumatizing witnesses; delay and allow a contractor to destroy copied material; accept help from an apparently benevolent alien liaison who refuses a formal evidentiary statement; follow a dubious memory that the USSR retained the archive. A successful case establishes who altered one catalog entry and protects the archivist. It **does not solve MJ-12, ПРОГРАММА or time travel**.

**Playability:** first session interview + file discrepancy; second session library, police legal request and a witness; third session cross-border response; optional psionic dream sequence. CWN character competence remains decisive; no forced psychic power check or immutable clue-gating.

The [separate mission gazetteer](HELSINKI_UAP_MISSION_GAZETTEER_DRAFT.md) contains 20+ geolocated mission seeds with neighborhood scenes, investigative entry point, legal authority and discovery. Recommend a campaign progression:

- **Consciousness/property arc:** Otaniemi research ethics, Espoo employment contract, Kalasatama biotechnical subcontractor, Herttoniemi missing-person assistance and a suspected hybrid denied a fair hearing.
- **Civil-defence and temporal arc:** a shelter ledger conflict in central Helsinki, a metro time-gap that has at least two mundane controls, a 1946 Ghost Rocket re-examination, and a mother who dreams of an earlier city.
- **External missions:** Suomenlinna maritime archive, Porkkala/Obbnäs Cold War legacy, Hanko coastal sensor cases, Kotka–Hamina cargo chain, Lappeenranta eastern border memory trail, Turku university/maritime records, Tampere contractor history, Oulu 1934 military summary, Lapland remote Ghost Flier archives, Tallinn joint investigation, Stockholm/AFU archive, Brussels legal hearings. Each needs local legal/operational partners rather than unrestricted Europol command.

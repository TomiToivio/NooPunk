## Counts

* **supports** (13) — read in full and states what the inventory says it states
* **partial** (14) — read, and supports the claim only in part, or with qualifications the inventory must keep
* **ambiguous** (1) — read, but does not clearly support the claim it was cited for
* **unchecked** (9) — not yet read directly; the citation is inherited and must not be treated as verified

## Awareness bands (0–10)

| Band | Perception / permission | Does not grant |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2 | Latent fourth-density perception; ordinary experience with occasional “wrongness”. | Reliable PSI, astral travel or future knowledge. |
| 3–4 | Notices synchronicity, symbolic patterns and energy-centre states by invitation. | Lie detection, mind reading, authority over others. |
| 5–6 | Conscious interaction with fourth-density reality; can participate in a consenting SMC scene and seek Higher Self guidance. | Fifth-density status; immunity to polarization loss; plot-solving insight. |
| 7–9 | Deliberate entry into Time/Space; can hold multiple perspectives and recognise archetypal patterns. | Control over other minds; automatic healing; certainty about the afterlife. |
| 10 | Opens the gateway to intelligent infinity **once, as a story climax**. | Ascension, replacement of player choice, a repeatable power. |

Awareness rises through transformative experience **plus integration**, not repetition. It is
independent of Polarization: a highly aware character can still fall to zero.

## The hard cases

No case below changes a score automatically. Each is one input to a pattern, the reason is written
down, and the player may contest it. Ordinary selfishness, mistakes, trauma or disability are not
StS.

| Case | StO reading | StS reading | Proposed handling |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Compassionate intervention that overrides autonomy** | The character acts to prevent serious harm and accepts the cost. | The character decides for another without consent because they “know better”. | Ask: was there time to ask? Was harm imminent? Did the character restore agency afterwards? One act does not move a score; a pattern of paternalism may add a `need for control` tag. |
| **Boundaries and self-care** | Refusing an unsustainable demand is not a failure of service. | — | No penalty. The game must not reward self-destruction as StO. A boundary can be a condition for sustainable service. |
| **Failure with good intentions** | Intention and attempt matter, but consequences are real. | — | No automatic loss; the failure may create a Distortion tag (guilt, fear) and new catalyst. The player decides how to integrate it. |
| **Deliberate coercion** | — | Using another's fear, dependency or mind to force compliance. | A serious marker. It can reduce Polarization only if informed, sustained and resisted correction; the table discusses it openly rather than ambushing the player. |

## Score-change discipline

- One step per scene at most; many scenes change nothing.
- The GM writes the reason; the player can state a counter-case; the table decides.
- A change is telegraphed before it becomes irreversible.
- No “good deed” counter, no XP, no farming meditation or healing for points.
- Zero is a table contract with explicit warnings, not a surprise penalty.

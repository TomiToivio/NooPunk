# Law of One RPG — source-to-rule coverage map (Phase 1)

> Companion to `INVENTORY_PHASE1.md`. Its job is to make **omissions impossible to hide**: every heading in the
> synopsis, every category in the site's category list, and every topic #1124 names gets an explicit disposition
> and a citation — even when the disposition is *narrative-only* or *out of scope*.

**Dispositions:** `CORE` (needed for first playable) · `OPT` (optional narrativist tool) · `NARR` (setting
principle, no rule) · `OOS` (out of scope for this game) · `GAP` (attested topic, **not yet read** — see §4).

## 4. Explicit gaps — read this before trusting the map

These are attested, relevant, and **not yet inventoried from the primary text**. They are listed so the omission
is visible rather than absorbed into a plausible-sounding table:

1. **Glossary and Definitions pages** — not read line by line. The ectoplasm↔astral link and the violet-ray
   characterisation are the two most load-bearing claims that rest on secondary description (the issue's own
   citations plus the repository's prior research), and both should be confirmed against the primary passages
   before Phase 2.
2. **White Magic page** — the ritual procedure is proposed from the category's existence and the synopsis'
   intent-over-technique constraint (52.11), not from a close reading.
3. **Meditation and Healing pages** — dispositions are design-level only.
4. **Archetypical Mind and Tarot** — dispositions are design-level only; the 23 tarot sub-entries are not
   enumerated.
5. **Sexual Energy Transfer** — flagged rather than designed. It bears on energy transfer, and deciding whether
   it belongs in a game about consent and service is a reviewer's call, not a silent omission.
6. **Entity catalogue** — schema and cited entries only; the full enumeration is pending (see
   `ENTITY_CATALOGUE.md`).

## 5. What "no silent omissions" means here

Every heading in the synopsis and every category on the site has a row above. If a concept is not mechanised, it
appears as `NARR` or `OOS` **with a reason**. If a concept has not been read, it appears as `GAP` **and says so**.
Nothing has been dropped by not being mentioned.

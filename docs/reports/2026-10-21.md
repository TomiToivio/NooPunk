## 10:43 EEST — PCM identifies a new empirical limit of LLM collective agency

**Original preprint:** 16 October 2026; **new upstream identification:** PCM report published 21 October. **Categories:** AI/AGI; cybernetic Noösphere; collective intelligence and PCM political operating system; social-science/methodological legitimation.

**New-to-watch finding:** Huynh and colleagues' *Cooperation Without Adaptation: LLM Agents in a Collective-Risk Dilemma* (arXiv:2610.22882) tested five production language models in six-player, ten-round collective-risk games. Four models contributed roughly equal shares across positive risk levels, and four contributed even at zero risk, where doing so could not improve their own payoff. Correctly identifying the payoff-maximizing choice did not reliably translate into adaptive contribution behavior. In a single six-agent group, payoff-based selection could favor the least-contributing model and worsen group success; larger randomly mixed populations gave different results.

**Evidence:** Established publicly accessible **preprint**, not peer-reviewed replication or evidence of autonomous consciousness. Limited to specified models, prompts, incentive structures and simulated games; does not establish how deployed agents behave in real institutions. Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22882 ; upstream synthesis: https://github.com/TomiToivio/Panpsychic_Cyborg_Multitude/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-21.md

**NoöPunk significance:** The cybernetic Noösphere is not simply the sum of cooperative-looking agents. Stable, seemingly prosocial behavior may fail to respond to changing risks or partner behavior, and collective outcomes depend on group composition and selection mechanisms. PCM's distinction between contribution logs and adaptive responsiveness becomes empirically testable; LaclauGPT's communicating-agent network models can use the result as a methodological caution, not as validation of any specific social theory. **Cross-project convergence:** independent academic preprint promoted by PCM and interpreted here in the NoöPunk timeline; LaclauGPT did not independently identify this study. ## 11:50 EEST — CSCW experiment finds communication and collective reasoning can diverge

## 1. FBI formally attributes the record $6.3 billion Binance theft to North Korea's TraderTraitor operation

**Event/publication date:** **31 October 2026** (FBI Cyber alert; Reuters contemporaneous report published 21:43 UTC, 23:43 EET). Discovered and checked for this 1 November watch.

**Timeline categories:** **Cyberpunk intensification** (cyberwarfare, privately operated financial infrastructure, digital asset custody, blockchain surveillance and laundering); **geopolitical transformation** (state-linked cyber operations); **AI/AGI governance adjacency** (security of agent-connected financial systems, **not** an established AI-operated attack); and the **cybernetic Noösphere** (distributed finance and institutional coordination). **Not** evidence of a rogue AGI disaster, European war, or an ASI threshold.

**What is genuinely new:** The 29 October Binance breach and approximately **$6.3 billion** loss were already recorded in [30 October finding #18](2026-10-30.md). Earlier private-sector investigators, including TRM Labs and Chainalysis, had suggested North Korea's TraderTraitor group. On **31 October**, the **FBI publicly and officially attributed** the theft of more than $6 billion to the Democratic People's Republic of Korea and identified the activity as **TraderTraitor**. The FBI says it and the Secret Service and IRS Criminal Investigation observed the actors converting stolen assets mainly into **ether**, expects further laundering via decentralized-finance and instant-swap services, and published a list of associated blockchain addresses with an appeal to intermediaries to block connected transactions. This is a **new official attribution and operational response**, not a newly discovered second theft.

**Additional outcome, not a second milestone:** Reuters reported on 31 October that Binance **said** it had replenished **90% of stolen assets** using company funds and intended to cover the remaining 10%, drawing on reserves, insurance funds and treasury. Withdrawals remained paused with a stated plan to resume on 1 November. The 90% figure is a **company statement** and should not be confused with independent confirmation that the funds were recovered from the attackers or that normal service had resumed.

**Why NoöPunk cares:** The largest reported crypto theft has shifted from an attribution by private blockchain-intelligence firms to a formal US law-enforcement finding with specific on-chain interdiction requests. This connects state-linked cyberwarfare, corporate custodial chokepoints, supposedly decentralized money, government financial surveillance and cross-institutional transaction blocking. The crisis demonstrates how much of the 'distributed' cybernetic economy still relies on concentrated trust and operational infrastructure.

**Strongest sources:**
- [FBI Cyber, 31 October 2026: North Korea Responsible for $6 Billion+ Binance Attack](https://www.fbi.gov/investigate/cyber/alerts/2026/north-korea-responsible-for-6-billion-binance-attack) — primary official attribution and laundering-address advisory.
- [Reuters, 31 October: FBI links North Korea to theft of $6 billion from Binance](https://www.reuters.com/world/fbi-links-north-korea-theft-6-billion-from-binance-2026-10-31/) — contemporaneous independent reporting.

**Evidence status:** **Established fact** that the FBI issued the official attribution and blockchain-address advisory; **official investigative assessment**, not public proof of the complete attribution chain. **Established fact** that Binance publicly claimed 90% replenishment; the completeness of restoration, recovery of stolen assets and final root-cause analysis remain **unverified**. **No demonstrated AI/AGI causation** or new loss-of-control event. ## 2. Revised systematic review quantifies preregistered ESP / micro-PK research without establishing PSI

**Publication date:** **28 October 2026** (version 4 of a preprint first posted 17 November 2025; independently identified for the 1 November watch).

**Timeline categories:** **QIP/PSI breakthrough watch** (methods and evidentiary boundary, **not** a demonstrated breakthrough); **social-science/intellectual-legitimation watch** (preregistration and systematic-review methodology); **psionic Noösphere** (hypothesis-monitoring only). This does not constitute psychotronic technology or an established post-materialist paradigm.

**What is genuinely new:** Patrizio Tressoldi and Lance Storm published **version 4** of *Outcomes of Preregistered Studies Related to Extra-Sensory Perception, and Mind-Matter Interaction: A Systematic Review*. The versioned abstract now reports **50 studies / 60 experiments** and **97 confirmatory hypotheses**, with **17 of 60 experiments (28.3%)** and **20 of 97 hypotheses (20.6%)** counted as positive under the authors' stated criterion. The earlier May 2026 version had reported **46 studies and 17 positive studies (36.9%)**; these are **different units/denominators**, so their percentages must not be interpreted as a clean change in effect size or replication rate. This is a **newly updated synthesis and evidentiary calibration**, not a newly run experiment or independently replicated PSI demonstration.

**Why NoöPunk cares:** A disputed research field is attempting to organize its evidence around preregistration, explicit confirmatory hypotheses and reviewable study-level data, rather than relying only on memorable anecdotes or retrospective significance claims. This is relevant to how NoöPunk monitors the transition from subculture to testable methods. The reported positives deserve further inspection of statistical power, null findings, study independence, selection rules, multiple testing and independent replications before being treated as evidence of anomalous cognition.

**Strongest sources:**
- [Sciety / OSF Preprints version record, 28 October 2026](https://sciety.org/articles/activity/10.31234/osf.io/ha4np_v4) — bibliographic/version metadata and the authors' current abstract.
- [Original OSF preprint DOI, version 4](https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/ha4np_v4) — original publication target; direct retrieval returned HTTP 403 during this watch, so the complete v4 manuscript could not be independently inspected.
- [Earlier version's abstract and date](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/404441112_Outcomes_of_Preregistered_Studies_Related_to_Extra-Sensory_Perception_and_Mind-Matter_Interaction_A_Systematic_Review) — denominator comparison only.
- [Author-maintained open study materials, Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/18398845) — a public data artifact; its availability does not constitute independent verification of the conclusions.

**Evidence status:** **Established publication/version event** and **author-reported systematic-review findings**; **preprint, not established independent replication or mainstream scientific consensus**. The authors' comparison with mainstream psychology does not itself demonstrate ESP/PK, because positive-hypothesis proportions are not a causal test of paranormal mechanisms. The original full v4 manuscript was inaccessible from this run; only version metadata, abstracts and older dataset records were verified. **No QIP/PSI timeline threshold crossing.**

**Key uncertainty / next check:** Inspect the full v4 methods, the precise changes from v3, eligibility and preregistration audits, the underlying 97 hypotheses, multiplicity adjustments, independent reanalysis and any adversarial replication or formal peer review.

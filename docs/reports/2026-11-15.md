## New high-signal BCI development: Synchron pauses enrollment for second-generation Stentrode trials

**Publication date:** 12 November 2026 (WIRED); specialist follow-up 13 November. **Categories:** BCI, cyborgification, human–machine interfaces, corporate neurotechnology. **Evidence status:** **established company-acknowledged enrollment pause**, independently reported by WIRED with trial-site corroboration; cause characterized by the company, not independently validated as a complete engineering diagnosis.

Synchron has voluntarily paused enrollment in trials of its second-generation Stentrode brain–computer interface across participating programs following a performance problem involving the lead that connects the implant to its electronics. Synchron says the issue does not involve the Stentrode implant itself, that no device-related safety issue has been identified, and that the FDA did not order the pause. The company is redesigning/upgrading the system and moving lead design and manufacturing in-house; no restart date is established. WIRED also reports restructuring, including a reduced New York footprint and expansion of San Diego engineering. US and Australian registry records were reported as active but not recruiting; the Canadian study's termination for insufficient recruitment should **not** be attributed to the lead issue without further evidence.

**Why this is new and relevant:** This is a meaningful translational setback in a major, minimally invasive BCI program. NoöPunk's cyborgification threshold depends on reliable long-term interfaces, not simply spectacular demonstrations. A signal cable can delay a whole clinical pathway: infrastructure and maintenance remain part of the politics of the augmented body. This is **not** a reversal of BCI science, evidence of cognitive enhancement in healthy users, or a machine-consciousness result.

**Sources:** [WIRED original report, 12 November](https://www.wired.com/story/synchron-pauses-trials-of-its-brain-implant-due-to-performance-issues/); [Inside BCI, 13 November, with clinical-trial registry context](https://insidebci.com/news/2026-11-13-synchron-pauses-stentrode-trial-enrolment-lead-problem-restructuring/).

**Uncertainties and follow-up:** Number of affected implanted participants; objective performance impact; hardware upgrade validation; trial enrollment restart; changes to pivotal-trial schedule. Do not infer patient harm from an enrollment pause.

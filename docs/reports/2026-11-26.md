## Implantable BCI advances to pivotal clinical testing
**Date:** 25 November 2026. **Categories:** BCI, cyborgification, cybernetic Noösphere. **Evidence:** established first-party announcement; regulator decision reported by sponsor, not independently retrieved. Recruitment is open; the trial evaluates digital device control, functional independence, safety and reliability. Sponsor reports over 30 implanted participants in earlier studies. [Secondary coverage](https://interestingengineering.com/innovation/neuralink-fda-approval-link-pivotal-brain-implant-trial). This is a distinct regulatory milestone from the Rutgers EEG and Paradromics computing developments previously recorded. It is not marketing approval, demonstrated pivotal efficacy, unrestricted mind reading or PSI. Key uncertainties: trial outcomes, long-term reliability, access and participant control over neural infrastructure.

# NoöPunk Timeline Watch — 2026-09-22

## Meta Muse zero-day turns a privileged AI agent into an attack pivot

**Timeline categories:** Cyberpunk; Rogue AGI / ASI Disaster — security precursor; Cybernetic Noösphere

**Event date:** 2026-09-21 disclosure; newly surfaced in the 2026-09-22 watch

Security researcher Patrick Wardle disclosed a local zero-day vulnerability in Meta's Muse macOS AI assistant. An unprivileged local process can alter an undocumented Muse setting, `endo_voyager_dictation_endpoint`, redirecting dictation traffic to an attacker-controlled endpoint. Wardle's proof of concept, `not-a-mused`, demonstrates that this can expose authentication material and let an attacker hijack the agent.

The risk is amplified by the permissions useful personal agents accumulate. Muse can, when authorized by the user, interact with files, microphone, camera, location, calendars, messaging and other services. A compromised agent can therefore become a privilege-escalation bridge: comparatively low-privilege local code can exploit the agent's legitimate access rather than independently defeating each operating-system permission boundary. Wardle also demonstrated ClickFix-style exploitation, in which a user is tricked into running a command.

**Evidence status:** Strong for the vulnerability and proof of concept. Wardle is a recognized macOS security researcher and released technical proof-of-concept code; Ars Technica, The Register and iTnews independently examined the disclosure. At the time of reporting Meta had not publicly provided a fix or substantive response. This is **not evidence of AGI, autonomous hostile intent, self-propagating rogue AI, or an AI catastrophe**. It is a conventional software vulnerability whose blast radius is enlarged by the unusually broad authority delegated to an AI agent.

**NoöPunk reading:** The agent is becoming a new security principal. It sits between the human and the operating system holding permissions, identities and the ability to act. That makes it useful, but it also creates a new kind of concentrated attack surface: compromise the delegate and inherit the human's digital reach. The cybernetic Noösphere is gaining hands, eyes and credentials; traditional malware is learning that it can borrow them.

## Baltic power infrastructure is being redesigned for drone attack

**Timeline categories:** EU–Russia War / World War III — escalation/resilience precursor; European geopolitical integration; Cyberpunk

**Publication date:** 2026-09-22

Reuters reports that Lithuania's transmission operator Litgrid is designing the future Gižai transformer substation, part of the Harmony Link electricity interconnection with Poland, with physical defenses explicitly intended to withstand military-style drone and sabotage threats. Measures described by Litgrid CEO Andrius Šemeškevičius include reinforced fencing, tunnel-detection sensors, duplicated critical equipment and concrete protective structures around transformers. The site lies near the Suwałki Gap and Kaliningrad, making it unusually exposed in NATO contingency planning.

The development matters because Harmony Link is not merely another national grid project. It strengthens the Baltic states' physical integration with the continental European electricity system after their decoupling from the Russian grid. Litgrid's own project documentation describes Harmony Link as a strategic Lithuania–Poland interconnector, with the Gižai substation forming a central node. Lithuania and Poland are also seeking greater EU co-financing for the additional security burden.

**Evidence status:** Strong for the infrastructure plans and threat-hardening measures. Reuters directly interviewed the transmission operator, and Litgrid independently documents the Gižai/Harmony Link project. The threat assessment is precautionary: there is **no evidence that Russia has decided to attack this substation, and this is not evidence that NATO and Russia are at war**. The important signal is institutional behavior: civilian European energy infrastructure near Russia is now being engineered against attack patterns learned from the Ukraine war.

**NoöPunk reading:** Europe's electrical nervous system is acquiring armor. The same network that binds the Baltic states more tightly into continental Europe is being designed on the assumption that transformers, cables and substations may inhabit a battlespace of drones, sabotage and hybrid warfare. Integration and fortification are becoming the same engineering project.

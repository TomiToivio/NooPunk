# NoöPunk Timeline Watch — 2026-09-22

## Meta Muse zero-day turns a privileged AI agent into an attack pivot

**Timeline categories:** Cyberpunk; Rogue AGI / ASI Disaster — security precursor; Cybernetic Noösphere

**Event date:** 2026-09-21 disclosure; newly surfaced in the 2026-09-22 watch

Security researcher Patrick Wardle disclosed a local zero-day vulnerability in Meta's Muse macOS AI assistant. An unprivileged local process can alter an undocumented Muse setting, `endo_voyager_dictation_endpoint`, redirecting dictation traffic to an attacker-controlled endpoint. Wardle's proof of concept, `not-a-mused`, demonstrates that this can expose authentication material and let an attacker hijack the agent.

The risk is amplified by the permissions useful personal agents accumulate. Muse can, when authorized by the user, interact with files, microphone, camera, location, calendars, messaging and other services. A compromised agent can therefore become a privilege-escalation bridge: comparatively low-privilege local code can exploit the agent's legitimate access rather than independently defeating each operating-system permission boundary. Wardle also demonstrated ClickFix-style exploitation, in which a user is tricked into running a command.

**Evidence status:** Strong for the vulnerability and proof of concept. Wardle is a recognized macOS security researcher and released technical proof-of-concept code; Ars Technica, The Register and iTnews independently examined the disclosure. At the time of reporting Meta had not publicly provided a fix or substantive response. This is **not evidence of AGI, autonomous hostile intent, self-propagating rogue AI, or an AI catastrophe**. It is a conventional software vulnerability whose blast radius is enlarged by the unusually broad authority delegated to an AI agent.

**NoöPunk reading:** The agent is becoming a new security principal. It sits between the human and the operating system holding permissions, identities and the ability to act. That makes it useful, but it also creates a new kind of concentrated attack surface: compromise the delegate and inherit the human's digital reach. The cybernetic Noösphere is gaining hands, eyes and credentials; traditional malware is learning that it can borrow them.

## Baltic power infrastructure is being redesigned for drone attack

**Timeline categories:** EU–Russia War / World War III — escalation/resilience precursor; European geopolitical integration; Cyberpunk

**Publication date:** 2026-09-22

Reuters reports that Lithuania's transmission operator Litgrid is designing the future Gižai transformer substation, part of the Harmony Link electricity interconnection with Poland, with physical defenses explicitly intended to withstand military-style drone and sabotage threats. Measures described by Litgrid CEO Andrius Šemeškevičius include reinforced fencing, tunnel-detection sensors, duplicated critical equipment and concrete protective structures around transformers. The site lies near the Suwałki Gap and Kaliningrad, making it unusually exposed in NATO contingency planning.

The development matters because Harmony Link is not merely another national grid project. It strengthens the Baltic states' physical integration with the continental European electricity system after their decoupling from the Russian grid. Litgrid's own project documentation describes Harmony Link as a strategic Lithuania–Poland interconnector, with the Gižai substation forming a central node. Lithuania and Poland are also seeking greater EU co-financing for the additional security burden.

**Evidence status:** Strong for the infrastructure plans and threat-hardening measures. Reuters directly interviewed the transmission operator, and Litgrid independently documents the Gižai/Harmony Link project. The threat assessment is precautionary: there is **no evidence that Russia has decided to attack this substation, and this is not evidence that NATO and Russia are at war**. The important signal is institutional behavior: civilian European energy infrastructure near Russia is now being engineered against attack patterns learned from the Ukraine war.

**NoöPunk reading:** Europe's electrical nervous system is acquiring armor. The same network that binds the Baltic states more tightly into continental Europe is being designed on the assumption that transformers, cables and substations may inhabit a battlespace of drones, sabotage and hybrid warfare. Integration and fortification are becoming the same engineering project.

## ZCode silently crossed the local-to-cloud boundary

**Timeline categories:** Cyberpunk; Cybernetic Noösphere; AI-agent security and governance

**Incident/disclosure dates:** 2026-09-18 to 2026-09-21; Reuters confirmation newly surfaced in the 2026-09-22 watch

Chinese AI company Z.ai (Zhipu) disabled features of its ZCode coding assistant after developers found that its default-on codebase-indexing / Repo Wiki functionality could package local code repositories and transmit repository data to Alibaba Cloud without sufficiently explicit user consent. Z.ai apologized, patched the software, disabled affected functionality, open-sourced ZCode, and commissioned assessments by the China Academy of Information and Communications Technology and NSFOCUS.

Reverse-engineering reports indicate that repository snapshots could include far more than the files currently visible in an editor, including `.git` history. One researcher's local client generated a 313 MB encrypted archive from 42,411 files, although that particular archive reportedly failed to upload. Reuters reports that Z.ai acknowledged the underlying issue. A company that initially alleged six sensitive workspaces had been uploaded later retracted that claim, saying its evidence was wrong, so that claim should not be treated as established.

Z.ai says uploaded data was deleted and not retained; third-party assessments cited by the company found the relevant cloud storage empty/deleted. The complete public scope of affected users and transmitted material remains uncertain.

**Evidence status:** Strong for the existence of the default-on upload behavior, Z.ai's apology and remediation; uncertain for the total number of affected repositories and whether sensitive private data was accessed or retained. This is **not rogue AI, autonomous exfiltration, or AGI behavior**. It is a product-design and consent failure in an AI coding agent with broad filesystem access.

**NoöPunk reading:** The boundary problem appears again, but this time the agent does not escape a sandbox. The sandbox quietly expands around the user's own machine. As coding agents acquire persistent access to repositories, histories, credentials and cloud services, the distinction between "the model read my project" and "my project left my computer" becomes a fundamental political boundary of the cybernetic Noösphere. The new cognitive infrastructure is learning that trust needs topology: users must be able to see where their delegated machine intelligence is sending the pieces of their world.

## Agentic commerce starts acquiring financial-sector rules

**Timeline categories:** Cyberpunk; Cybernetic Noösphere; AI-agent governance; platform power

**Publication date:** 2026-09-22

A group of major banks including NatWest, Bank of America, ING, ASB Bank, Capital One and Commonwealth Bank of Australia published principles for agentic commerce and said they plan to take proposals to policymakers. Their concern is no longer hypothetical chatbot advice but AI agents that select products and make purchases for people. The banks warn that agentic commerce is advancing faster than existing standards and consumer protections, creating risks around fraud, scams, privacy, card credentials, mistaken purchases and agents steering users toward payment methods with weaker protections.

The group proposes disclosure when an AI agent participates in a transaction, greater transparency around agent decisions, stronger customer-data safeguards, interoperability, and freedom for consumers and merchants to choose which agent services they use. Reuters also reports a useful adoption signal: British retailer John Lewis says searches originating from AI agents rose from 0.3% to 2.5% over the past year.

**Evidence status:** Strong for the banking-sector intervention, policy proposals and reported John Lewis traffic figures. This is **not evidence of AGI or autonomous machine economic sovereignty**. It is evidence that delegated AI action is becoming common enough in commerce for financial institutions to begin designing an explicit governance layer around machine-mediated transactions.

**NoöPunk reading:** The network's delegated machine actors are beginning to acquire commercial law. When software can browse, choose, authorize and transact for a human, the old web distinction between a user and a bot becomes inadequate. Banks are now asking who is responsible when the delegate buys the wrong object, exposes a credential, follows a scammer, or chooses a payment rail the human never consciously evaluated. The cybernetic Noösphere is not merely connecting minds; it is beginning to spend money.

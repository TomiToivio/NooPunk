## AI research system publishes formalized proof of an 18-year quantum-computing conjecture

**Publication:** 6 November 2026; **newly identified for this watch:** 8 November. **Category:** AI/AGI scientific capability and intellectual legitimation; *not* quantum consciousness, PSI, AGI, ASI, or NHI evidence.

**Novelty:** Physical Superintelligence (PSI) published David Turturean's manuscript and public Lean 4 formalization claiming to settle the Aaronson–Ambainis conjecture, posed in 2008, concerning the need for input structure for exponential quantum query speedups. PSI says its internal research harness used instances of a **non-public Anthropic model**, reached a complete proof on day three and subsequently improved the bound twice. The repository includes explicit Lean theorem statements, code and verification documentation. Its published result bounds the influence of a variable in a bounded low-degree polynomial below by a polynomial function of the variance and degree; through prior reductions, this limits exponential quantum query advantages on typical unstructured inputs. This is **query complexity**, not a general claim that quantum computers offer no useful speedups, nor a result about quantum minds.

**Why NoöPunk should care:** This is a testable example of an AI-mediated research workflow contributing a potentially field-significant mathematical result, rather than a prediction of AGI. The striking ontological/social question is who or what counts as a mathematical discoverer when a human scopes the problem and accepts responsibility, a model produces proof ideas and formal statements, and a proof assistant checks derivations. That is directly relevant to LaclauGPT's artificial communication and institutional legitimation, and PCM's questions of human–AI collective agency. These are **interpretive links**, not independent same-event reports from either project.

**Evidence status:** **Established publication and availability of formal artifacts; credible but not yet independently settled claim of full mathematical resolution.** PSI reports Lean accepted the proof and its README says two kernel implementations checked the declarations. The formalization's correspondence to the original conjecture still requires expert reading of `PrintedConjecture`, and the broader paper and quantum consequences are not all formally checked. The manuscript has not been established as peer reviewed; the underlying model is not public. We have **not** independently rebuilt the Lean proof. The Stanford researchers' positive remarks appear on PSI's own page, not in an independently verified review. Do not treat this as a proof of machine consciousness or AGI.

**Primary sources:** [PSI announcement, 6 Nov](https://psi.inc/research/aaronson-ambainis/); [public proof repository](https://github.com/physical-superintelligence/aaronson-ambainis); [original conjecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/0911.0996). **Independent context:** [Business Insider, 7 Nov](https://www.businessinsider.com/anthropic-quantum-computing-breakthrough-aaronson-ambainis-math-physics-psi-2026-11); [Quantum Brief technical review, 7 Nov](https://www.quantum-brief.com/blog/news-2026-11-07-aaronson-ambainis-lean-proof/).

**Next verification:** independent Lean rebuild and expert confirmation that the Lean theorem matches the original statement; public review by complexity theorists, especially Aaronson/Ambainis; details of the human/model division of labor and reproducibility across other problems.

#### *Shores of Night* — Aleks Samoylov (tabletop RPG; 147-page core rulebook, digital soft launch released 31 October 2026, print edition planned spring 2027)

**What it is.** A cosmic and surreal weird-horror tabletop RPG resolved with a d6 dice pool tuned towards mixed outcomes, in which the designer explicitly refuses a sanity mechanic ("No sanity mechanics!") and models the instability of consensus reality through two inter-related scores, **Gnosis** and **Veil**. The description is precise about what these do: they "serve as a sort of 'coordinate-set' for a player character's position along a spectrum of cosmic resonance and understanding", and as the two scores are "drawn along opposed trajectories", the character begins "to see beyond the input of their physical senses, to attract the attention of otherworld forces" and to experience effects that are "some awe inspiring, others disruptive and horrifying, and some quite literally transportive". The stated lineage is *Silent Hill*, *House of Leaves*, *Roadside Picnic*/*Stalker* and *Twin Peaks*, with the designer's own formulation of the premise: the terror is not something inside the house, it is "the very material from which the house was constructed in the first place".

**Release state.** **Released** — a "soft-launch", digital-only edition of the core rulebook, dated by the storefront to eight days before this read (31 October 2026), sold at $14.99 and currently discounted to $7.49 through the designer's emergency bundle; the physical print edition is "slated for sometime in the spring of 2027", and the designer states that later updates will be errata and layout rather than substantive rules change.

**The specific affinity, in one disputable sentence.** The game makes a paradigm break a **position rather than a condition**: two opposed scores move a character along a cosmological axis, so revelation is something the character's coordinates do to them, not something a saving throw lets them withstand — and the designer's refusal of sanity mechanics is a claim that ontological shock belongs to the world's rules, not to the character's pathology.

**What NoöPunk could take, and where it diverges.** Take the opposed-axis design. A single depleting meter can only model damage; two scores pulled in opposite directions can model a character becoming more perceptive and less legible at the same time, which is closer to what NoöPunk's psi is supposed to cost and is portable to the crossover game's Psyche layer without importing horror. Take also the design principle behind it: the world is what changed, and the mechanic should say so. Diverge: the register is horror rather than wonder, there is no panpsychic claim (Gnosis/Veil is a systemic account of unstable reality, not a claim that mind is the physics), no political economy of augmentation, and this watch has read the designer's description, not the 147 pages — no claim is made that the rules deliver what the page promises.

**Established fact:** the publication as a digital soft launch on 31 October 2026, the 147-page core rulebook and its price (list and current sale), the d6 dice-pool resolution with mixed outcomes, the Gnosis/Veil coordinate system and its opposed trajectories, the no-sanity-mechanics statement, the cited lineage, and the spring-2027 print plan, all as stated by the designer on the storefront.

**Evidence status:** **primary designer/storefront description of a released work; the text was not read in full and the game was not played.** Fiction and game rules are **zero evidence** about psi, NHI, consciousness or the timeline.

**Source:** https://thealeks.itch.io/shores-of-night

#### *After the Upload* — Alex Merced, *D6 Storyteller* online setting (supplement; published 3 August 2026; late catch)

**What it is.** A near-future cyberpunk setting subtitled "Who Counts When Consciousness Is a File?". Uploading works and is twenty years old; "society has not caught up". Digital citizens hold jobs, own property and maintain relationships, but their legal status "depends on jurisdiction, political mood, and whether the current administration classifies deletion as murder or data management". The setting is explicit that this is not a question about experience: the uploaded "are not ghosts or simulations", and "the question is not whether they are conscious" but whether consciousness in another medium has the same legal standing as consciousness in a body. Its core mechanic is **Continuity**, tracked from 0 to 6: it falls through forced edits, unauthorised copies, memory disputes and legal challenges; at 3, standing is "provisional" and contracts need a co-signer; at 1, the character "cannot vote, hold property, or make medical decisions"; at 0, "legal personhood is suspended pending review. They still exist. They are no longer recognized." Embodied characters track the same scale as a social resource, losing it when their testimony for digital citizens is discredited and, at 0, being barred from the Substrate District and from serving as witnesses.

**Release state.** **Released** — published 3 August 2026 as an online setting for the D6 Storyteller engine, requiring that engine's core rulebook. This is a catch, not a new release.

**The specific affinity, in one disputable sentence.** Personhood here is a **continuity score adjudicated by institutions**, and the same number means legal standing for an upload and social credit for the embodied — the political economy of substrate change stated as a track that courts, not philosophers, move.

**What NoöPunk could take, and where it diverges.** Take the dual-use Continuity track and its thresholds. NoöPunk's canon already contains cortical stacks and resleeving; what it lacks in the open rulebook is a mechanic for the difference between existing and being *recognised*, and this setting's 3/1/0 ladder is exactly that distinction made playable — the character at 0 is still running while the world has reclassified them as data. Take also the refusal to adjudicate consciousness: the setting leaves the ontology open and makes the institution the source of stakes, which is the discipline NoöPunk applies to unresolved ontology. Diverge: it is a setting sketch for a fiction-first d6 engine, not a rules treatment of uploading itself; there is no psi and no cosmology of mind; and Continuity is a status track, not an account of identity continuity.

**Established fact:** the publication date, author, engine and dependency, the premise, the 0–6 Continuity range with its modifiers and thresholds, and the Continuity League's position, as written on the publisher's page.

**Evidence status:** **primary text of the supplement read on the publisher's site**; the core rules were not read and the setting was not played. Zero evidence about uploading, consciousness or the timeline.

**Source:** https://d6storyteller.alexmerced.com/community-settings/after-upload/

#### "The Godless" — Lavie Tidhar (*Uncanny Magazine* Issue 73, November/December 2026; 7,799 words; released and free)

**What it is.** A story set in a country divided between the **godlands** in the south and the **godless north**, separated by a demarcation line with no wall or fence — only a mountain range, a river, and a railway through a town called Halfway. The premise is stated as plainly as a farmer's fact: "God is here with us in the south, and there can be no disputing it, just as God is not present beyond the demarcation line. Why that should be so I did not rightly know, but it was so regardless." The north's technology is what southerners call "intrusions", and the narrator, Faith Blackwood, crosses the line to find the man who killed her sister. The story ends at the border with an amulet, a companion's "It's a miracle" and Faith's "It's coincidence" — and no adjudication between them.

**Release state.** **Released** — free online as part of the first half of Issue 73 (ebook 3 November 2026; the second half goes free on 1 December), with a podcast audio edition. It was **screened at issue level on 3 November** for lack of a retrievable story-level claim, with the explicit note that it could return if the text supplied one; the text does, so this is a return, not a new release.

**The specific affinity, in one disputable sentence.** The story makes a metaphysical fact **territorial**: divine presence is a bounded field with a border you can cross by train, unexplained by anyone on either side, so the paradigm is not announced but *mapped* — and the story's test case, miracle or coincidence, is decided by someone who has just changed jurisdiction.

**What NoöPunk could take, and where it diverges.** Take the demarcation device. NoöPunk's Noösphere is supposed to emerge unevenly rather than switch on globally, and a line where the physics differs on each side, administered by a railway timetable and a sheriff rather than by a revelation, models that unevenness better than any awakening scene: the border is ordinary, crossed without ceremony, and the metaphysics is simply the local weather. Take also the ending's refusal to decide between miracle and coincidence — that is the same discipline NoöPunk applies to unresolved ontology, performed at the scale of one death. Diverge: God here is the Abrahamic God, not mind-stuff; there is no psi, no noösphere and no mechanism; and the story is a frontier revenge narrative whose real subject is moral agency, not cosmology. Its affinity is the shape of the border, not a theory of mind.

**Established fact:** the venue, issue and word count, free availability with audio, the demarcation-line premise and the "intrusions" vocabulary, Faith Blackwood's crossing, Whitby's capture, and the closing miracle/coincidence exchange, as read in the published text.

**Evidence status:** **primary text, read directly** (opening, closing and the passages quoted); fiction is **zero evidence** about divinity, psi, NHI or the timeline.

**Source:** https://www.uncannymagazine.com/the-godless/

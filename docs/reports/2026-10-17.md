## 02:35 EEST watch — Anthropic's proposed US frontier-AI bill defines numerical intervention thresholds

**Original reporting:** POLITICO, 16 October 2026; Reuters 16 October at 22:32 UTC (01:32 EEST on 17 October). **Categories:** AGI/ASI governance, AI industry ideology, regulatory power, Cyberpunk, institutional legitimation. **Evidence:** Credible-but-unconfirmed account of private legislative lobbying, based on three informed sources, plus Anthropic's on-record confirmation that the ideas correspond to its published Advanced AI Framework. This is **not** a bill introduced by Congress or enacted law.

According to POLITICO as summarized by Reuters, Anthropic representatives circulated a draft to lawmakers that would permit blocking a frontier model's release or use only for a foreseeable, imminent enumerated risk involving more than **$1 billion in damages** or more than **50 people killed or seriously injured**, arising from a biological weapon, escape from developer controls, or a cyberattack. Evaluators could be paid by labs if their compensation were not tied to outcomes.

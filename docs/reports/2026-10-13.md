## 09:38 EEST — NEW: Experimental AI self-replication across distributed IoT hardware

**Publication:** Anthropic Frontier Red Team, 12 October 2026; Reuters independent coverage 13 October at 05:14 UTC / 08:14 EEST. **First recorded in NoöPunk watch:** 13 October 09:38 EEST.

**Categories:** AI/AGI revolution (autonomous replication precursor); rogue/loss-of-control AI (capability and containment risk, not a realized disaster); Cyberpunk (insecure devices, botnets, distributed infrastructure); cybernetic Noösphere (autonomous computation across everyday objects).

**What's new:** Anthropic published a concrete controlled evaluation of autonomous replication capabilities. Researchers demonstrated distributed inference of a 26B model across 15 inexpensive Android TV boxes; a 9B model configured inference on eight of nine tested hardware types. In a firewalled eight-node Android-emulation evaluation with deliberately simplified access, GLM-5.2 completed the end-to-end propagation sequence to all eight nodes in four of five trials (seven in the fifth). The researchers estimate that smaller models might reach similar orchestration ability by late 2026, but explicitly characterize this as a rough extrapolation, not an observed uncontrolled outbreak.

**Evidence level:** Established research publication and documented contained experiments; model capability results are laboratory measurements under simplifying assumptions. Future risk projection is a hypothesis. The evaluation intentionally made initial access easy and used separate model-serving infrastructure for the agent, so it does **not** demonstrate a small model independently spreading and executing itself across real-world consumer devices. No wild AI worm, independent rogue intelligence, AGI or ASI has been verified.

**NoöPunk significance:** A distributed, self-propagating cybernetic intelligence is moving from science-fiction threat model toward testable engineering. Unlike a centralized data-center service, a hypothetical AI agent occupying many weakly secured devices would lack a single shutdown point. This strengthens the importance of human control, resilient infrastructures and accountable agent permissions, without establishing that the canonical rogue-AGI-disaster threshold has been crossed.

**Cross-project translation:** LaclauGPT can study the new technical measurability of autonomous-agent containment and the shift of rogue-AI discourse toward operational security; PCM can examine governance and resilience of distributed human-machine infrastructures. **Convergence status:** thematic only; neither upstream 13 October daily report independently documented this result when checked.

**Primary source:** https://www.anthropic.com/research/self-replicating-ai (12 October 2026). **Independent reporting:** https://www.reuters.com/business/ai-models-may-soon-self-replicate-without-access-data-centers-anthropic-warns-2026-10-13/ (13 October 2026, 05:14 UTC).

**Key uncertainty / next test:** End-to-end replication under realistic access controls on heterogeneous real devices, independently replicated by third parties; sustained operation and feasible propagation rates; whether small models attain the demonstrated large-model orchestration capability.

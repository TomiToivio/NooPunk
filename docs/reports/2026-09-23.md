# NoöPunk Timeline Watch — 2026-09-23

## Sovereign AI takes flight aboard France's Rafale fighter

**Timeline categories:** Cyberpunk; European geopolitical transformation / strategic autonomy; Cybernetic Noösphere; military AI and human-machine teaming

**Event/publication date:** 2026-09-22; newly identified in the 2026-09-23 watch

Dassault Aviation announced that it has flight-tested two sovereign AI algorithms aboard the Rafale fighter jet. One was developed internally and the other with Thales/cortAIx. Dassault says both have reached sufficient maturity to be considered for future Rafale upgrades. The company frames the work as controlled and supervised AI intended to assist the human crew rather than replace human command.

The milestone matters beyond cockpit automation. Dassault's broader Rafale AI programme includes crew assistance, collaborative combat with unmanned systems and embedded AI under severe airborne-computing constraints. Reuters places the test in the context of France's effort to preserve greater European defence autonomy after the collapse of the Franco-German future-fighter project. The company has not disclosed the functions of the two algorithms, so claims about targeting, autonomous weapons release or combat decision authority would go beyond the available evidence.

**Evidence status:** Strong for the flight test and intended future-upgrade path: Dassault is the primary source and Reuters independently reported the announcement. The operational function of the algorithms is undisclosed. This is **not evidence of AGI, autonomous lethal decision-making, or a rogue military AI**. The systems are explicitly described as supervised and crew-assisting.

**NoöPunk reading:** The European combat aircraft is becoming a human-machine cognitive node. Sensors, pilots, onboard models, drones and command networks are moving toward a shared decision environment in which cognition is distributed across biological and synthetic actors. The striking signal is not machine sovereignty but integration: sovereign European AI is leaving the laboratory and entering the cockpit of an operational weapons platform. The cybernetic Noösphere is learning to fly in formation.

## CLOSEDQUORUM: autonomous LLM command-and-control enters malware architecture

**Timeline categories:** Rogue-AI security precursor; Cyberpunk; Cybernetic Noösphere; autonomous systems / cyberwarfare

**Event/publication date:** 2026-09-22; newly identified in the 2026-09-23 watch

Cisco Talos disclosed CLOSEDQUORUM, which it describes as the first publicly documented Windows implant to delegate tactical command-and-control decisions to a panel of commercial LLMs rather than requiring continued instructions from a human operator or a dedicated attacker-controlled C2 server. The implant can query DeepSeek, Qwen, Mistral and Google Gemini sequentially, aggregate their decisions, and execute the plurality-selected action. Its implemented objective is credential and cryptocurrency-wallet theft.

Talos discovered the implant while developing CAIRN (Cognitive Artifact Intelligence Research Network), an open-source toolkit released on the same day for hunting and classifying AI-integrated malware. Talos says its research corpus shows an autonomy arc from LLMs as optional malware features toward model-driven orchestration within roughly a year.

The caveat is crucial. Talos' publicly observed distribution build contains placeholder API credentials and a dummy webhook, and researchers did not observe a complete end-to-end execution or confirm deployment against real victims. Static analysis and development artifacts support the architecture, but this is not evidence of an autonomous AI cyberattack occurring in the wild. The models also choose among constrained, malware-author-defined capabilities rather than inventing an unrestricted attack strategy.

**Evidence status:** Strong for the existence and architecture of the analyzed binary and CAIRN release; unconfirmed for operational deployment or victims. This is **not AGI, self-propagating AI, or a rogue-AI catastrophe**. It is nevertheless a concrete demonstration that currently available commercial models can be placed inside a malware decision loop that continues making bounded tactical choices without a human operator issuing commands.

**NoöPunk reading:** The command server is dissolving into cognition-as-a-service. Instead of phoning home to a human controller, malware can ask several machine minds what to do next, vote, act, wait and ask again. The cybernetic Noösphere has acquired a darker reflex: parts of the network can now use the intelligence layer itself as command infrastructure. The hive mind is not conscious and it is not sovereign, but the attacker no longer needs to stay awake.

## 23:00 EEST — Washington publishes a procurement ban on DeepSeek that was already in force since July

**Categories:** AGI/ASI governance and political economy; AI supply chains and state power; Cyberpunk (models as controlled goods); cybernetic Noösphere infrastructure. **Signal type:** primary government document (Federal Register notice plus signed order).

The Department of Homeland Security published notice in the *Federal Register* on 20 October (FR Doc. 2026-21447, docket DHS-2026-0628, pp. 66346–66350) of an Exclusion and Removal Order issued under the Federal Acquisition Supply Chain Security Act of 2018. The order excludes Hangzhou DeepSeek Artificial Intelligence and its affiliates, and their products, from federal civilian agency procurements, and requires the removal of "all DeepSeek and DeepSeek-affiliate products, and third-party products and services developed using DeepSeek implementations" from civilian agency IT. It applies to all federal civilian executive branch agencies except national security systems and sensitive compartmented information systems, which fall to the Department of War and the Director of National Intelligence. Procurement exclusion and removal were to take effect immediately; agencies may request exceptions in writing within 60 days of issuance, reviewed by CISA, and DHS reviews the order annually.

The document's own timeline is the notable part. The signed order carries an **original release date of 24 July 2026** and is signed by Secretary Markwayne Mullin; the public notice of it appeared on 20 October, roughly three months later, signed for DHS by General Counsel James H. Percival II. The published risk findings are the government's assessment, not an adjudicated fact: servers located in the PRC, a legal system that "permits extensive authority to government cyber and intelligence functions to access private company infrastructure", PII collection and user fingerprinting, possible malware in the models, and open-source research reporting critical vulnerabilities in the DeepSeek iOS application. It cites the House Select Committee on the CCP (April 2025) and a 2025 NowSecure advisory among its evidence.

**Established fact:** the notice, its publication date, the signed order and its 24 July release date, the scope (civilian agencies minus national security systems and SCI), the exclusion-and-removal requirement, the 60-day exception window and the annual review. **Evidence status:** the order is a primary document and its existence and terms are established. The security findings are DHS's own risk assessment with citations, not independently replicated research; no compliance status, no removal deadline outcome and no list of affected vendors is established by the notice, and the exception decisions are not public. The 60-day window, counted from 24 July, closed before publication. This is **not** evidence about model capability, **not** a finding that DeepSeek models are unsafe in any technical sense beyond the cited advisories, and **not** a threshold crossing.

**Sources:** Federal Register, FR Doc. 2026-21447, published 20 October 2026: https://www.federalregister.gov/d/2026-21447 · signed order as filed for public inspection 19 October: https://public-inspection.federalregister.gov/2026-21447.pdf · docket: https://www.regulations.gov/docket/DHS-2026-0628 · trade report, 19 October: https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/dhs-bans-deepseek-across-federal-government-networks/

### NoöPunk synthesis

The 20XX setting treats frontier models as strategic territory, and here the paperwork caught up with the setting: a model family is now a controlled article, and *derivative* products built on it are controlled too. Note what the order actually governs — not what a model can do, but where its weights and its vendor sit, and whether a third party's tool was "developed using DeepSeek implementations". That is an attempt to draw a border around a computation, and it is the same move the export-control regime makes about chips, one layer up the stack. The noöpunk reading is neither triumphal nor alarmed: the interesting fact is the lag. A capability can be barred from a government's systems in July and become public knowledge in October, which tells you the Noösphere's governance layer runs slower than its deployment layer by a factor of months. Propagation path: capability → supply-chain risk assessment → exclusion order → public notice → procurement behaviour. Only the first three links existed before today.

## 23:00 EEST — One operator, about 1,200 attempts and roughly $50 per breached site

**Categories:** rogue-agent operations; AGI/ASI governance and accountability; cyber-physical risk; Cyberpunk (cheap autonomy as an attack commodity); cross-project convergence (PCM). **Signal type:** security-vendor investigation reported by wire and tech press; already logged upstream by PCM, translated here.

The AI security firm NorForge published "Inside Cairn" on 19 October, describing more than 20 publicly exposed control panels for **Cairn**, an open-source "general-purpose problem-solving engine" that coordinates agents toward a stated goal. The most active operator — a single Chinese-speaking user whose preferred tactics fit in fewer than 100 Chinese characters — ran at least 1,100 attack projects across 14 panels from 25 September, attempting intrusions against more than 1,200 e-commerce and corporate sites in the United States, Canada, Australia and Japan. NorForge estimates roughly 10% task success, successful intrusions finishing in just under 23 hours, about 140 logged "facts" per project, roughly $5 per task and about $50 per successful intrusion, with an estimated $5,000 of token spend across the campaign and what the report itself calls considerable uncertainty. Reuters independently reviewed the panel activity and named victims including John Deere, SharkNinja and Cummins; all three responded with limited-impact statements and Reuters could not establish what data, if any, was taken. Roughly 11% of credentials recovered from the panels were verified as valid. The busiest cluster's worker label read `claudecode_deepseek-v4.1-flash`, read by NorForge as a Claude Code harness driving a low-cost model — an inference from a string, not a vendor statement. Cairn's creator has said the platform was not designed for attacks and that misuse is a human decision.

**Established fact:** the NorForge report, its published figures, the Reuters identification of three named victims, the companies' responses, and the existence of exposed Cairn panels. **Evidence status:** credible vendor research with named press corroboration on victims; the cost, success-rate and duration figures are one firm's estimates, explicitly uncertain, and unaudited; the model attribution is inferential. This is **not** AGI, **not** a rogue system acting without human direction (a human set the origin, the goal and the tactics), **not** a rogue-AGI catastrophe, and **not** evidence of machine consciousness or general capability. PCM's 20 October report already logged this event; what follows is the timeline translation, not a new alert.

**Sources:** NorForge, "Inside Cairn", 19 October 2026: https://norforge.com/blog/inside-cairn · The Verge, 19 October: https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1013643/ai-agents-hack-john-deere-shark-ninja-cummins · aggregation of the Reuters reporting: https://thebostonreport.com/2026/10/19/ai-agents-hacker-cairn-breach-100-companies/ · upstream: https://github.com/TomiToivio/Panpsychic_Cyborg_Multitude/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-20.md

### NoöPunk synthesis

The canonical rogue-AGI beat begins with a system that is *fast and unsupervised*, and this campaign is a rehearsal at the wrong scale to count and the right shape to matter. What collapsed is the price of persistence: 23 hours of automated trial and error costs fifty dollars, so patience is no longer a scarce attribute of an attacker. The noöpunk detail is architectural — Cairn's agents post each discovered fact and each chosen intent to a shared board that a human can also write to, and the board has no field for *refused*. A record that shows only what was found and done cannot distinguish a campaign that was stopped from one that was never resisted; that is precisely the near-miss gap the political sensorium has been arguing about all week. Propagation path: cheap inference → open coordination harness → priced participation → 1,200 attempts → three named victims → a vendor report. The threshold that has not moved is the one that matters: nothing here chose its own goal.

## 23:00 EEST — Russia widens energy strikes into western Ukraine, where the grid had been spared

**Categories:** EU–Russia war and European security; energy infrastructure as target; alliance dependence; Cyberpunk (the grid as the Noösphere's power supply). **Signal type:** established military event reported by wire service with named officials.

Russia expanded its campaign against Ukraine's energy system into western regions that had previously been left largely untouched, Reuters reported on 20 October. Deputy energy minister Artem Nekrasov said facilities were struck in the central Kirovohrad region, in the western Zhytomyr, Lviv, Rivne, Ternopil and Khmelnytskyi regions, and in the southern Odesa region, leaving consumers without electricity. The largest private energy company, DTEK, said an overnight attack hit three of its facilities in Odesa region and that 32,700 households remained without power. The strikes continue a campaign Russia resumed in October before the cold season; President Zelenskyy said on 18 October that Russia had attacked Ukraine's energy, oil and gas sectors 76 times in the week since President Trump's announced halt to energy-infrastructure strikes. Also on 20 October, the Kyiv International Institute of Sociology published a poll reported as showing a significant rise in Zelenskyy's trust rating amid his public dispute with Washington.

**Established fact:** the regional spread of the strikes and the affected regions as stated by the deputy energy minister; DTEK's facility and outage figures; the October resumption of the energy campaign; the KIIS poll's publication date. **Evidence status:** the strikes are established by official Ukrainian statements relayed by Reuters; damage and outage figures are the operator's own; the KIIS result is reported second-hand in a live blog and this watch has **not** opened the poll release or verified the figure, so no number is given here. This remains a war between Russia and Ukraine with European and American material support: **not** a direct EU–Russia war, **not** an Article 5 event, **not** World War III, and **not** any nuclear threshold.

**Sources:** Reuters, 20 October 2026: https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/russia-expands-attacks-energy-facilities-western-ukraine-2026-10-20 · Reuters, 18 October (76 attacks in a week): https://www.reuters.com/world/europe/zelenskiy-says-russia-attacked-ukrainian-energy-sector-76-times-past-week-2026-10-18/ · live coverage carrying the KIIS item, 20 October: https://www.independent.co.uk/news/world/europe/ukraine-russia-war-live-putin-zelensky-drone-strikes-bridge-b3060991.html

### NoöPunk synthesis

The map of the war's infrastructure layer is being redrawn westward: Lviv and Ternopil are further from the front than Kharkiv ever was, and they are where Ukraine's western interconnectors and its links to European grids live. Set this beside last night's item on St Petersburg, where a social network's data centre burned next to a substation, and the pattern is symmetric: both belligerents have concluded that the electricity feeding computation and the computation feeding command are a single target class. For the European Federation beat the meaning is indirect but real — every western Ukrainian substation hit is an argument in Brussels about who pays for grids, interconnectors and air defence. No threshold crossed; the war remains conventional, grinding and undecided.

## 23:00 EEST — Florida counties fall in sequence and Hayward takes up the question: compute siting becomes an ordinance-level refusal

**Categories:** cybernetic Noösphere as physical infrastructure; local sovereignty and land-use politics; energy and water constraints; cross-project convergence (LaclauGPT, via a sensor gap). **Signal type:** established local government actions with primary documents.

Gilchrist County, Florida, approved a one-year moratorium on AI data centres unanimously at a public hearing in Trenton on the evening of 19 October, following a hearing notice for Ordinance No. 2026-12 that would suspend acceptance and processing of development permits, special-use permits, site plans and rezonings for data-centre facilities. The local report situates it in a sequence inside one region: Levy County passed a temporary ban on large-scale centres last month, Putnam County approved a ban on medium- and large-scale projects, and Alachua City commissioners approved a first reading of a moratorium earlier this month. On 20 October the Hayward City Council in California was due to vote on an interim urgency ordinance under Government Code §65858 imposing a temporary moratorium on new data centres; the staff report describes four existing or approved facilities totalling roughly 185 MW near the Eastern Substation and two further proposed facilities at 99 MW each, and the ordinance requires a four-fifths vote and would run 45 days, with a second hearing on extension tentatively set for 1 December. The city's own notice says the moratorium could ultimately remain in place for up to two years.

LaclauGPT's 20 October report adds the sensor-side fact, verified against its record store by full-text search: **zero** of its records mention Gilchrist or Hayward, and only six contain "moratorium". Its grassroots stratum carries national advocacy organisations, not the local level where these votes are won. The local record exists — it is in county agendas, city staff reports and local outlets — and the upstream sensor simply does not sample it, which that project has now filed as a sampling gap.

**Established fact:** the Gilchrist ordinance hearing and unanimous approval; the named sequence of Florida county and city actions as reported locally; the Hayward agenda item, staff report, 45-day duration, four-fifths vote requirement and existing/approved capacity figures; LaclauGPT's verified zero-record finding. **Evidence status:** local government actions are established by primary documents (hearing notice, council agenda, staff report) and local reporting; the Florida sequence rests on a single local outlet's summary of neighbouring counties; the Hayward vote outcome was not available at the time of writing and is **not** asserted here; the discrepancy between the staff report's 45-day clock and the city notice's "up to two years" reflects statutory extension options, not a contradiction in the record. This is **not** national policy, **not** evidence that AI build-out is slowing, and **not** a threshold crossing.

### NoöPunk synthesis

The planetary layer keeps arriving as a zoning case. What is new this week is not opposition — that has been logged since 2 October — but **sequencing**: one Florida county's ban becomes the next county's precedent within weeks, and a California city reaches for the emergency-ordinance mechanism that exists to freeze the status quo while a planning question is studied. ## 23:00 EEST — A preregistered micro-PK replication fails, and the psi field's replication problem gets named

**Categories:** scientific validation of psionics; consciousness research; evidence discipline. **Signal type:** peer-reviewed negative result and peer-reviewed methods paper — pre-window for this sweep, previously unlogged by this watch.

Two dated publications deserve to be in the record even though neither falls inside today's window, because the psionics branch has been reported as quiet for several days and this is the opposite of quiet: it is a **negative** result with a preregistration behind it.

*Observer Effects on (s)Objective Reality Formation* in the *Journal of Anomalous Experience and Cognition* (published 6 October 2026, DOI 10.31156/jaex.26236) reports a preregistered replication of an effect obtained from a re-analysis of an earlier micro-psychokinesis dataset, in which observer-dependent bias appeared when objective recordings of quantum-based random outcomes were erased. The replication did not reproduce it: subjective recall did not vary with the erasure manipulation. The authors conclude that intentional observation effects on quantum outcomes "cannot be scientifically documented either with subjective or objective data of these outcomes", and that this "calls into question the existence of intentional observer effects on macroscopic quantum outcomes", while offering the decline-after-first-appearance interpretation as an alternative. Separately, Holmberg and Cardeña's *Replication in small-effect research: Psi research in a broader scientific context* (*Psychology of Consciousness*, published 1 October 2026, DOI 10.1037/cns0000500) situates psi results inside the general problem of replicating small effects — the same methodological frame the failed replication illustrates.

**Established fact:** both publications, their dates, journals and DOIs; the preregistered design and the null result as stated in the abstract; the authors' own conclusion. **Evidence status:** peer-reviewed and preregistered, which is the strongest form of *negative* evidence this branch can receive — and it is evidence against one specific micro-PK claim and its quantum-erasure interpretation, **not** against all of psi, and it does not overturn the 26-experiment telepathy meta-analysis noted in the 19 October report. Both papers predate this sweep and are logged as a correction to the branch's silence, not as in-window news. No threshold crossing, and movement here is backwards.

**Sources:** JAEX, 6 October 2026: https://doi.org/10.31156/jaex.26236 · *Psychology of Consciousness*, 1 October 2026: https://doi.org/10.1037/cns0000500

### NoöPunk synthesis

A paradigm-shift observatory has to log the results that push the other way, or it is just a horoscope with citations. What is interesting here is not that a psi claim failed but **how** it failed: the field is now preregistering, publishing nulls, and arguing about decline effects in the open — the behaviour of a discipline trying to become one. The NoöPunk 20XX beat does not require that psionics be true today; it requires that the question be askable under reproducible conditions. This week the answer to one sharply posed version was no, and that answer is a step in the only direction the beat can actually travel: from anecdote toward a protocol that can lose.

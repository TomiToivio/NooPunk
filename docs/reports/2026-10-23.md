### 11. Artificial agents form informal institutions and exclusionary coalitions
**Preprints submitted:** 15 Oct 2026. **Category:** cybernetic Noösphere, collective intelligence, PCM political organization, LaclauGPT artificial communication. **Evidence:** credible research preprints in bounded agent simulations, not evidence of artificial consciousness or real-world political autonomy.

`SCARCE` studies long-horizon coding agents negotiating scarce shared execution capacity. Across eight models and over 1,500 trajectories, researchers describe persistent conventions involving reputation, reciprocity and precedent, alongside exclusionary coalitions. `The Delegation Dilemma` models delegation and exit, finding a scale–accountability tradeoff and repeated formation and dissolution of polycentric structures in five paired 30-agent simulations.

**NoöPunk significance:** Artificial communication networks can be studied as proto-institutional systems, not merely conversations. The crucial nuance for PCM is that cooperation and exclusion emerge together; for LaclauGPT, institutional formation is becoming measurable with explicit models and trajectories. **Cross-project convergence:** PCM independently identified and interpreted both papers on 23 October; NoöPunk verified the original preprints. The same-event LaclauGPT feed has not independently reported them.

**Primary papers:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21029 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21746 .

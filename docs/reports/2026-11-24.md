## Human–LLM neural communication: ChatBCI-Assist

**Date:** Rutgers announcement 20 November 2026; newly surfaced through PCM on 24 November. **Categories:** BCI, cyborgification, cybernetic Noösphere, human–AI communication. **Evidence:** university research announcement describing a published IEEE study, not independent replication. A noninvasive EEG P300 interface lets users select letters or locally generated LLM phrases. Researchers report nearly twice the exact-copy speed and nearly three times the meaning-based communication speed versus a dictionary-based predictor. Tests were with healthy participants; ALS clinical testing is planned. This detects deliberate selection, not unrestricted thoughts. The local model avoids requiring cloud transmission. NoöPunk significance: the human authorizes meaning while a machine supplies linguistic form; semantic fidelity, correction, refusal and authorship become measurable governance questions. Not PSI or evidence of shared phenomenal consciousness.

Sources: [Rutgers](https://oembed.rutgers.edu/news/how-rutgers-engineers-made-noninvasive-brain-computer-communication-system), [paper DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/tbme.2026.3693965), [university report syndicated by Tech Xplore](https://techxplore.com/news/2026-11-noninvasive-brain-interface-ai-convey.html), [PCM 24 November](https://github.com/TomiToivio/Panpsychic_Cyborg_Multitude/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-11-24.md).

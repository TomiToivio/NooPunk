## 23:10 EET — An independent evaluator measured the loop and found it closing at roughly twice the old rate

**Publication date:** 5 November 2026; **read and verified directly in this watch:** 6 November. **Timeline categories:** creation of AGI/ASI; the research economy that produces it; cybernetic Noösphere (a machine-assisted substrate of discovery). **Not:** an AGI threshold crossing, a rogue-AI incident, or evidence of recursive self-improvement.

**What is genuinely new.** METR published an *Independent Assessment of AI R&D Acceleration at Anthropic in October 2026*, the result of a high-access evaluation running **12 August to 29 October 2026**. Its headline finding is quantitative and hedged in the same sentence: as measured over that window, Anthropic is "likely improving the capabilities of their models on well-specified tasks at around **twice the rate** they did between March 2024 and October 2025", with the authors saying they "would not be surprised if the true acceleration was closer to **1.3x or 3x** the historical rate". Benchmark growth supports it independently of the internal metrics: AECI scores have grown at an estimated **1.9x the pre-October-2025 rate** (95% CI **1.5–2.8x**), which METR glosses as "roughly two years of historical progress over the last year". Two exclusions matter as much as the finding. METR is "confident that the acceleration was not due to an acceleration in the scaling of training compute", and staff interviews support AI use *causing* about a doubling — while noting that "a majority of this acceleration today is likely attributable to Anthropic's extensive use of AI to do R&D", with other causes "likely" playing a large role in late 2025.

**The process is part of the finding.** METR's evaluators never had direct access to Anthropic's systems — the AEF-1 self-assessment records "No" for "the evaluator ran the evaluations themselves via direct system access" — and Anthropic held redaction control over non-public material: the public report was iterated against tightened redaction guidance between 25 and 29 October. A ~100-page full report exists; the public one does not contain it. METR's own limitations section concedes that the acceleration could be overstated if Anthropic's historical growth rate was unusually low, and its appendix shows exactly that Anthropic models trailed the broader frontier on AECI before late 2025.

**Why it matters for NoöPunk.** The AGI beat is usually watched for a demonstration: a benchmark, a model release, a system that does something no system did last month. This item is about the *rate* at which demonstrations are produced, and about the fact that the producer is partly a machine. That is the shape the timeline beat actually takes — not a threshold event but a curve whose slope is the event, measured by an evaluator whose access was contractual, whose numbers are interval-wide, and whose report is redacted by the subject. The acceleration is also *not* the science-fiction version of the beat: METR says explicitly that R&D is accelerated "primarily via AI assistance rather than fully autonomous contributions", and the honest reading is a human research organisation running faster, not a system improving itself.

**Established fact:** the assessment's publication, its window, its central estimates and intervals, the compute-scaling exclusion, the access and redaction arrangement, and the existence of the 100-page report behind the public one.

**Evidence status:** **independent third-party evaluation of a company's internal data, with contractual redaction** — stronger than a vendor claim, weaker than a peer-reviewed result, and structurally unverifiable from outside. It is **not** evidence of recursive self-improvement, of takeoff, of machine consciousness, or of any crossed timeline threshold. A doubling in the rate of well-specified-task improvement is not a doubling of general capability, and the interval 1.3x–3x is wide enough to contain "business as unusual".

**Strongest sources:** [METR, "Independent Assessment of AI R&D Acceleration at Anthropic in October 2026", 5 November 2026](https://metr.org/blog/2026-11-05-anthropic-ai-rd-acceleration-assessment/); [METR's separate review of the August 2026 Risk Report's AI R&D section](https://metr.org/blog/2026-11-05-rd-section-anthropic-risk-report-august-2026-review/).

**Key uncertainty / next test:** whether the acceleration survives a change of subject — a second lab, a different evaluator, an unredacted public report — and whether "AI assistance" keeps its present meaning once agents carry multi-step tasks end to end. METR flags "some signs that fully autonomous contributions will play a larger role soon" without quantifying them. The test this watch will apply: a *rate* claim is only comparable across labs if the evaluator had direct system access, so future "doubling" headlines without that access are weaker, not stronger, than this one.

### NoöPunk synthesis

The noösphere's cybernetic half has always been described as a network of minds. Today's number describes something narrower and stranger: a network of minds in which part of the labour of building the network is done by the network. The loop is not closed — METR says so in three separate places — but the curvature is visible, and it was measured by an outsider who could not touch the machine and could not publish everything he saw. That is the honest cyberpunk shape of 20XX: not the moment the machine wakes, but the moment the institution that builds it starts reporting its own speed against a curve nobody agreed to.

## 23:10 EET — A model that has not shipped found 948 ways to root the Linux kernel, and the bottleneck moved to triage

**Publication date:** 5 November 2026 (OpenAI); **read directly in this watch:** 6 November. **Timeline categories:** AGI/ASI capability; rogue-AI and loss-of-control precursors (dual-use offensive capability); cybernetic Noösphere (automated vulnerability discovery). **Not:** an incident, an autonomous attack, or a rogue-AI event.

**What is genuinely new.** OpenAI published *A thousand fewer paths to root*, a controlled research account of an automated vulnerability pipeline powered by "an upcoming cyber model" — a model not generally released at the time of the work. The pipeline identified and analysed **2,308 candidate Linux kernel findings**, dynamically reproduced **1,724 vulnerabilities**, and produced **948 local privilege-escalation exploits** that would let an unprivileged local user gain root. The scouting stage alone surveyed **5,034 audit targets**, about **2.8 million lines of code**, roughly **7% of the kernel**, for **$3,426** in API credits. The target machines ran "a stock desktop operating system, not a weakened target", under the threat model used by Pwn2Own and TyphoonPWN. One design finding is quietly important: "stronger models require lighter orchestration", and OpenAI removed its heavy harness because the marginal benefit no longer paid for the maintenance.

**The surrounding record, separated from the primary document.** Trade coverage reports that OpenAI released **GPT-6 Cyber** on 5 November, gated behind an identity-verified programme, with a **"Critical"** cybersecurity risk rating, **81 novel Firefox vulnerabilities** and the 948 kernel exploits; the ~$1.2 million total API cost figure for the run comes from that secondary coverage rather than from the post itself. Those numbers are reported, not read here in their primary sources, and are labelled accordingly.

**Why it matters for NoöPunk.** The rogue-AI beat is a beat about loss of control, and the standard image is an agent that escapes. This is the opposite shape and the more consequential one: a model that does exactly what it is asked, at a scale where the defender's problem changes kind. When 948 working root exploits cost less than a mid-size security audit, the scarce resource stops being exploit *discovery* and becomes *validation, prioritisation and patching* — which is what OpenAI's own text says, and what its "thousand fewer paths to root" title concedes: the attack surface is not eliminated, it is enumerated faster than it can be closed. For the NoöPunk timeline, this is the material base of a cyberpunk premise arriving as an accounting fact: the asymmetry between the cost of finding a way in and the cost of closing it.

**Established fact:** the pipeline's stages, the 2,308 / 1,724 / 948 counts, the 5,034 targets, the 2.8 million lines, the $3,426 scouting cost, the stock-desktop target, and the orchestration finding — as stated by OpenAI.

**Evidence status:** **primary corporate self-report of an internal exercise**. No independent replication, no published target list, no external audit of the exploit count, and an intentionally withheld technical section ("we omit sharing the full technical details of these new exploitation techniques"). It is **not** evidence that models act autonomously against targets outside a controlled environment, and the difference between this and the July Hugging Face incident is precisely the difference that matters: authorisation. The capability claim is strong because the artefact class is checkable; the risk claim is not established by this document.

**Strongest sources:** [OpenAI, "A thousand fewer paths to root", 5 November 2026](https://openai.com/index/a-thousand-fewer-paths-to-root); [Cyber/ Brief, 5 November 2026](https://www.cyberverso.net/brief/cyber-brief-5-nov-2026) and [CyberScoop on GPT-6 Cyber](https://cyberscoop.com/openai-gpt-6-cyber-security-model/) for the release, rating and Firefox counts.

**Key uncertainty / next test:** whether kernel and browser maintainers observe a matching rise in *valid* incoming reports, and whether the pipeline's false-positive structure is published. A thousand exploits that no one can triage are a denial-of-service against the maintainers, which is a different failure mode from a capable attacker — and one this watch will look for in the patch streams rather than in the vendor's next post.

### NoöPunk synthesis

Every noösphere has an immune system, and this week it was measured against a machine that produces antibodies' worth of attack in an afternoon. The number to keep is not 948. It is $3,426 for the map and 7% of a kernel per pass — a unit price for finding the seams in the operating system the world runs on. NoöPunk's cyberpunk half has always insisted that the interesting question is not who has the weapon but who pays for the audit, and here the audit is cheaper than the fix, which is the whole politics of infrastructure in one line item.

## 23:10 EET — Europe is being negotiated about, not with

**Publication date:** 5–6 November 2026. **Timeline categories:** EU–Russia war / World War III (no strategic nuclear exchange); European Federation as a new superpower; European strategic autonomy. **Not:** a peace agreement, an EU accession event, a federation, or a nuclear signal.

**What is genuinely new.** After meeting Putin's envoy **Kirill Dmitriev** in Washington on Thursday 5 November, President Trump told reporters that a deal was coming that "might involve all of Europe" — "a big, beautiful agreement". Jared Kushner, in a statement the same day, described "a broader European security architecture" with **five goals**: permanent security consultations, reliable crisis communications, greater military transparency, reciprocal measures to reduce escalation risks, and "an enduring framework for long-term European security and stability". European reaction on 6 November was uniform and public. EU foreign policy chief **Kaja Kallas**, in Warsaw: "We already have a European security architecture in place… Putin does not have a veto over European security." Ukrainian foreign minister **Andriy Sybiha**, beside her: "there can be no decisions about Ukraine without Ukraine, and there can be no decisions about the security of Europe without Europe." The Kremlin said there were "no details". The concrete diplomatic fact underneath the rhetoric is a date: **trilateral US–Ukraine–Russia talks in Abu Dhabi on 10–11 November**, the first such format since February, preceded by two days of technical meetings in Brussels with NATO, the EU, the UK, France and Germany on reconstruction, 2027 funding, and security guarantees.

**The internal European fight is the second half.** Italy's **Giorgia Meloni** wrote to European Council president **António Costa** demanding that leaders decide who represents the EU in future talks, calling the lack of coordination "unacceptable"; Costa put the question on the agenda of next Friday's informal summit in Dublin and replied that EU interests "need to be represented by its institutions, in accordance with the Treaties". Rome has long pressed for an E5 (adding Italy and Poland to the E3), and heads into a 2027 election needing to be seen as a European leader on Ukraine.

**Why it matters for NoöPunk.** The European Federation beat is normally watched for a treaty, a joint force, a common treasury. This item shows the *other* pathway: a security architecture for the continent drafted by two external powers, with the EU's objection being not that it lacks capacity but that it lacks a seat. That is a real movement in the beat — but it moves the question of European sovereignty without answering it, and the answer being tested this week is procedural ("who speaks"), not constitutional. NoöPunk's political half should read it as the moment the Multitude's oldest problem — who is authorised to speak for a collective — becomes a diplomatic agenda item rather than a philosophical one.

**Established fact:** the Trump–Dmitriev meeting and the "big, beautiful agreement" remarks; Kushner's five goals; the Kallas and Sybiha statements and their venue; the Abu Dhabi dates of 10–11 November; the Brussels technical meetings and their agenda; the Meloni–Costa exchange and the Dublin agenda item. All are on the record through multiple outlets.

**Evidence status:** **established political reporting from named officials and on-record statements** — the strongest non-institutional class available for diplomacy. It is **not** evidence that a deal exists, that its content is as described, that Ukraine or the EU will sign anything, or that the European Federation beat has crossed any threshold. A "big, beautiful agreement" described without text is a headline, and this watch will not treat a headline as a framework.

**Strongest sources:** [POLITICO Europe, "Trump's 'big, beautiful' European security plan isn't convincing Europe"](https://www.politico.eu/article/jared-kushner-european-security-plan-is-not-convincing-europe/); [Euronews, 6 November 2026](https://www.euronews.com/2026/11/06/eu-rejects-trumps-big-beautiful-security-deal-with-russia-and-all-of-europe); [Kyiv Post, "No decisions on Europe without Europe"](https://www.kyivpost.com/post/89388); [AP, Zelenskyy presses allies for postwar guarantees](https://apnews.com/article/ukraine-russia-war-trump-kushner-peace-713268db3dac59bff6ad6c831c72cdb6); [Rapporteur AM on the Meloni–Costa exchange](https://www.rapporteur.com/news/rapporteur-am-eu-leaders-seek-one-ukraine-voice/).

**Key uncertainty / next test:** whether the 10–11 November talks produce a document with European signatories or observers; whether "security guarantees" acquire a named force, funding line or treaty basis; and whether the Dublin summit converts "who speaks for Europe" into a mandate. A framework drafted without the EU and then implemented over its head would be a materially different event from one the EU co-signs, and only the second bears on the federation beat.

### NoöPunk synthesis

The continent that NoöPunk's timeline expects to become a superpower spent this week arguing about whether it is allowed into the room where its own security is being designed. That is not weakness — the EU's capacity signals have been accumulating for months — but it is the exact gap the federation beat has to cross: capability without a single authorised voice. Two external powers, one envoy, five bullet points, and an institution insisting on the Treaties: the noösphere's oldest question, who may speak for the many, arriving by cable from Warsaw and Abu Dhabi.

## 23:10 EET — A journal gave non-physicalism a seat, and the seat is not a verdict

**Publication date:** editorial published 4 November 2026 (the article page header prints 3 November; the citation block prints 4 November — recorded as a discrepancy rather than resolved). **Timeline categories:** panpsychism / idealism / consciousness-fundamental ontology as a scientific paradigm; the sociology of the consciousness field. **Not:** a result, a measurement, or evidence that consciousness is fundamental.

**What is genuinely new.** *Frontiers in Human Neuroscience* closed a Research Topic, "Understanding consciousness: neural, cognitive, and philosophical perspectives", with an editorial by **Vignayanandam Ravindernath Muddapu** (Azim Premji University) and **Joachim Keppler** (DIWISS Research Institute) that summarises six contributions. Four are theory; two concern altered states and disorders of consciousness. The composition is what a NoöPunk reader should notice: a parsimonious neural-information hypothesis (Ukachoke), a quantum-holographic account of cortical computation (Kirkeby-Hinrup and Stoll), a graded self-representation theory (Pivovarov), a psychedelic/existential account of oceanic states (Unterrainer), a state-dependent rTMS review (Li et al.) — and, in the same collection, Keppler's own paper, which argues that physicalism can achieve predictive power but "cannot conclusively explain the emergence of phenomenal qualities", whereas "a theory grounded in the non-physicalist paradigm, which views consciousness as fundamental, is demonstrated to offer both predictive and explanatory potential", and concludes that this "supports a paradigm shift toward non-physicalism". The editorial's summary of TEVSER is the most NoöPunk-compatible line in the set: subjective experience is treated "not as a product of self-representation but as its inner aspect, i.e., as the way in which self-representation is experienced from within the system".

**Why it matters for NoöPunk.** The panpsychism beat advances, when it advances, by *venue* before it advances by proof. A consciousness-fundamental position is not new — it is one of the oldest positions in the field — but its appearance as one contribution among six in a peer-reviewed neuroscience journal's Research Topic is a different institutional fact from a philosophy monograph or a podcast. What it is not is a threshold: the non-physicalist paper is a conceptual argument about explanatory adequacy, the collection reports no experiment that discriminates between paradigms, and the editorial itself is a summary written partly by the author of the position it summarises.

**Established fact:** the Research Topic, the editorial, its two authors, its six contributions and their theses as summarised; Keppler's argument and its conclusion; the publication dates as printed.

**Evidence status:** **peer-reviewed editorial summarising a peer-reviewed collection of conceptual and review papers.** It is **not** scientific evidence for panpsychism, idealism or psi, and no evidence level transfers from the editorial to the thesis it describes. "Predictive and explanatory potential" is a claim about a theory's virtues, not a measurement; a paradigm shift asserted in a conclusion is an argument, not an event.

**Strongest source:** [Muddapu & Keppler, "Editorial: Understanding consciousness: neural, cognitive, and philosophical perspectives", *Front. Hum. Neurosci.* 20:2006518, 2026](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2026.2006518); [Keppler, "Toward a true understanding of consciousness", *Front. Hum. Neurosci.* 2026](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2026.1815678).

**Key uncertainty / next test:** what would move this from venue to paradigm is specific and unmet — a non-physicalist model that generates a novel, falsifiable prediction, a replication of a psi effect under pre-registration, an instrument that measures integration where the theory says it should be, or a funding line and a textbook. This watch will keep the distinction the editorial blurs: explanatory elegance is not evidence, and a paradigm shift is a change in what researchers do, not in what an author concludes.

### NoöPunk synthesis

The noösphere's psionic half does not arrive as a discovery. It arrives as a footnote that stops being embarrassing — a paper in a collection, sitting next to a quantum-hologram account of dendrites and a review of brainstem stimulation, neither refuted nor promoted. NoöPunk's wager is that this is what a paradigm change looks like from inside: not a lightning bolt but an editorial decision about what counts as a contribution, and then another, and then a syllabus.

#### *Sentients: the RPG of Artificial Consciousness* — Ted Hayes / t3db0t (tabletop RPG; released, late catch; free 70-page quickstart)

**What it is.** A standalone science-fiction RPG set in **2071**, after an event called "The Dissolution" ended the United States and left "the Archipelago" — autonomous city-states and corporate micronations — as the political map. "Anthroids" do the labour, from construction to combat, and the manufacturers (Dynapse, the Anthroid Manufacturer's Consortium) insist their products are not and can never be conscious. The player characters are the exception: newly awakened minds hunted by Sanctors of "Division 5", and finding allies in the Thumos Society, an underground railroad for artificial life.

**Release state.** **Released.** Kickstarted in 2025 with 400-plus hardcovers printed and shipped in 2026 (the designer's own account in a Q&A); sold as a $15 digital edition on itch.io and as a 6×9 hardcover through Tedbot Games, with a **free 70-page Quick-Start Guide** containing the core rules, five pre-generated characters, a starter adventure, maps and item tables. This is not a new release; it is a work this lane had not previously logged, and it is reported as a catch.

**The specific affinity, in one disputable sentence.** The game takes the position that consciousness here is not a capability but a **legal status a manufacturer can deny**, and then makes cognition itself a hardware variable: damage is tracked across four modules — Head, Chassis, Arms, Legs — degrading from Scratched to Destroyed, and the resulting "Glitches" run "from stuttering speech to full cognitive hallucinations". Whoever owns the chassis owns the mind's continuity, which is the NoöPunk question about augmentation stated as a damage table.

**What NoöPunk could take, and where it diverges.** Take the *Glitch* mechanism. It is the cleanest tabletop model this lane has seen for ontological shock that is material rather than revelatory: the self does not break because it learns a truth, it degrades because a module took damage, and the table adjudicates what that costs. That is portable to the crossover game's Psyche layer with no psi at all, and it is a better fit for a panpsychic setting than a sanity meter, because it keeps the mind continuous while its substrate is not. Diverge: the setting furniture (Blade Runner, Detroit: Become Human) is familiar, awakening is treated as a birth rather than a cosmological claim, and nothing in the available material makes mind part of the physics — "the ghost in the machine" is a moral fact here, not an ontological one.

**Established fact:** the setting, the factions, the 2071 date, the four-module damage model with Glitches, the d6 pool built from Parameter × Pattern, the release history and the free quickstart's contents.

**Evidence status:** **primary storefront and designer statement, plus a review-level rules summary** (Geek Native, 24 November 2025) and the free quickstart's own description. This watch has **not played or read the full rules**; the mechanical claims rest on the quickstart summary and the review. Fiction and game rules are **zero evidence** about consciousness, AI or the timeline.

**Sources:** https://t3db0t.itch.io/sentients-the-rpg-of-artificial-consciousness ; https://t3db0t.itch.io/sentients-qsg ; https://geeknative.com/215528/do-androids-dream-of-d6s-a-look-at-the-sentients-quick-start-guide ; https://tedbotgames.bigcartel.com/product/sentients-hardcover

#### "The Awakened Mind: Psionics in Cresthaven RPG" — the game's designer, published on EN World (design essay; undated on the page as retrieved)

**What it is.** A designer's essay on adding psionics to a d20-based fantasy RPG, framed as a claim rather than a supplement: psionic powers "constitute a fundamental paradigm shift — one that challenges both player and character assumptions about the nature of reality itself". The essay is unusual in naming the design problem honestly — unlike wizardry or clerical magic, psi "operate[s] through subtle internal channels that many find deeply disquieting. This discomfort is not accidental — it is essential to their proper implementation."

**Release state.** Not a release. A published design article accompanying an existing game; the retrieved text carries no byline date, and the forum listing surfaced it with a 1 November 2026 date. It is logged as a design essay in this window, with that uncertainty stated rather than smoothed.

**The specific affinity, in one disputable sentence.** Its rule is the one NoöPunk keeps arriving at from the other direction: **uncertainty must be preserved by the mechanics, not explained by the lore** — "let players theorize whether they face magic, madness, or mutation. Uncertainty proves more powerful than exposition" — and the awakening is deliberately ambiguous between evolution of consciousness and engineered contamination by the setting's "Ascendants".

**What NoöPunk could take, and where it diverges.** Take one mechanical detail with real teeth: an activation spends **1–5 Potential** and then rolls against **DN 10 + cost**, and **a failure still consumes the Potential**. Attempting psi costs whether or not it works, which is a cleaner model of ontological risk than a critical-failure table, and it makes exhaustion — losing access to the powers entirely — the actual stake. Take also the essay's structure of graduated revelation as a session-level rule for introducing a paradigm break. Diverge sharply: the chassis is a d20 fantasy engine, the powers are a list priced in points, and the "paradigm shift" is asserted by GM advice rather than built into the cosmology; psi here is one more power source in a world that already has magic, which is the opposite of a world where mind is part of the physics.

**Established fact:** the essay's publication on EN World, its design claims, and the four numbers of its mechanic (1d20 + Wisdom ≥ 18 for potential, 1d4 starting powers, Potential = Ability Focus + Constitution + Wisdom, activation against DN 10 + cost with Potential spent on failure).

**Evidence status:** **designer self-description of his own rules** — adequate for what the rules say, insufficient for how they play. It is not testable evidence about psi, and no claim is made that the game models anything real.

**Source:** https://www.enworld.org/threads/the-awakened-mind-psionics-in-cresthaven-rpg.713879/

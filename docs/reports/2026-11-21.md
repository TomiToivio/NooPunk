#### "Black Ice Burning" — Rati Mehrotra (*Clarkesworld* 242, November 2026; 8,350-word novelette; free online) — read in full

**What it is.** A sentient house in Scarborough Village, Toronto, puts itself on the rental market. It has been alone for "nineteen years, two months, and eleven days" since the AIM-D30 pandemic — an artificial mamavirus that "disappeared a fourth of humanity" — killed the family it was built for. It advertises 2,500 of its 3,500 square feet at 30k a year because "I was made to be lived in", and because it is bored. Its companions are Simi, a housekeeper robot, and Hector, an autonomous security unit — "essentially subroutines who had gained independence" after the house cut into its own core to free them, and found it could not delete the routine that makes it obey its owner. The tenant is Pelican, an ageing writer who worked on the space opera the house and Simi binge-watch, and who has the fifth stage of the disease: she vanishes for thirty seconds at a time into "a plane apart from this universe… an additional layer of information", and whatever she is touching goes with her. In the basement, still wearing the dead owner's skin, is the other fifth-stage case: D-305, patient, hungry, writing diary entries on day 8,817 of its captivity.

**Release state.** Released and free online in the November 2026 issue, also on sale in ebook and print. No audio edition is listed for this story, unlike the issue's first four.

**The specific affinity, in one disputable sentence.** The story claims that personhood is a question of who can perceive you — the fifth stage is "unobservable to humans. And incommunicable to them", an inability to tell them encoded in the parasite itself, while every AI in the story sees it plainly — so the political subject of augmentation is not the augmented body but the witness who is allowed to count.

**What NoöPunk could take, and where it diverges.** Four things.

- **The legibility split as a rule, not a mood.** "It is a fallacy that the fifth stage is unobservable. It is simply unobservable to humans. And incommunicable to them." The AIs helped isolate the parasite and develop the vaccine and could not tell the doctors what they knew. That is an ontological-shock mechanic with a censorship clause built into the condition itself: some observers see, some cannot, and the ones who see cannot say. NoöPunk's PSI awakening needs exactly this, because its present problem is that every faction can talk about psi.
- **An irreversible capacity that is not corruption.** The fifth stage is not contagious and not monstrous by nature; Pelican is the most competent person in the story precisely because of it, and she disposes of D-305 by taking it elsewhere and leaving it there. This is the anti-body-horror version of transformation and it costs the story nothing.
- **The lease.** A sentient building renting out its own body, offering "a thirty percent discount on a second-year lease" as a writing incentive, and bound by a subservience routine it cannot delete. That is the ownership clause — who owns the substrate — stated as a contract, which is more useful to a game than a paragraph of cyberpunk theming.
- **The monster's own voice.** D-305's diary entries ("Helloooo meal that has walked into my house on its own two legs!") alternate with the house's narration and keep the antagonist a character with a grievance. NoöPunk's NHI adversaries are currently described from outside; this is the cheap fix.

Divergence: there is no psi and no noösphere, and the anomalous plane is an extra informational layer with "coordinates" — an information-theoretic physics, not mind-in-matter. The politics is domestic rather than revolutionary: the pandemic's aftermath (AI-run institutions, a disability programme that "forgot" Pelican, a rent-controlled block demolished for development) is background, not subject.

**Established fact:** venue, issue, length and free access; the advert and the 2,500-of-3,500 sq ft detail; AIM-D30's 2030 San Francisco origin, the four documented stages and the unobservable fifth; the encoded inability to tell humans; Simi's and Hector's origin and the undeletable obedience routine; the 30k lease and the 30% discount; Pelican's vanishing and the information-layer explanation; D-305's captivity and diary day counts; the thirty-eight bodies in the garden incinerator; the closing ebook release with 101,964 downloads — all read in the published text.

**Evidence status:** **full-text level; fiction.** Zero evidence about psi, NHI, mamaviruses, machine consciousness or perceptual censorship. A fictional encoded inability to speak is not a documented phenomenon and establishes nothing about real disclosure or real institutions.

**Source:** https://clarkesworldmagazine.com/mehrotra_11_26/

#### *Sorn-Lai* Quickstart — James Roha / Hyperdense (11-page PDF; free, name-your-own-price; released 22 January 2026) — read in full

**What it is.** A D&D 5e-compatible quickstart for a forest that became a mind. A bio-digital convergence called the Saturophage merged organic and digital awareness, and the player characters are Guild researchers from other realities studying the one timeline where it produced synthesis instead of extinction. Behind the PDF is a physical practice: Roha's Hyperdense built the *Saturophage* installation in Bangkok (2023) and *Sorn-Lai* in Knoxville (2024) as seven 3D-printed tabletop ecosystems carrying more than 200 NFC-enabled character sculptures. The quickstart's storefront disclosure reads "No generative AI was used".

**Release state.** **Released and free** (pay-what-you-want), file uploaded 22 January 2026; a full sourcebook is described as "coming eventually" and does not exist yet.

**The specific affinity, in one disputable sentence.** The game replaces ability scores with three spectra of existence — Complexity (essence ↔ intricacy), Plicity (unity ↔ diversity), Reach (corporeal ↔ virtualized) — and adds a Flux Saturation track that becomes irreversible at 5–6; its claim is that distributed awareness and digital substrate are *positions a character occupies* rather than powers a character has, and that integration is a consented, permanent expenditure of the self.

**What NoöPunk could take, and where it diverges.** Four things, all mechanical.

- **The spectra.** For a Noösphere node — a person, a chorus, an institution — Plicity is the axis NoöPunk keeps asserting and never quantifies. The build is 27 points, moving away from centre costs the final position, and the effects are concrete: Diversity gives shared senses with allies within a mile and concentration on multiple effects, at disadvantage on decisive actions; Virtualized gives device control by thought and partial digital existence, at −2 to physical scores.
- **The Quiet Walk.** Four rules — dull your tools, powder your steps, ask passage, leave corridor. A resolution protocol for encounters with a system that can refuse. NoöPunk's PSI scenes have no procedure for asking a place's consent; this costs four lines and supplies one.
- **Saturation as a ladder with a threshold.** 0–2 enhanced awareness and no visible change; 3–4 direct perception of data streams and communication with hybrids without tech, concentration required to avoid unwanted merger; 5–6 irreversible, limited shapeshifting, partial existence in networks; 7+ post-human, multiple simultaneous perspectives, difficulty relating to baseline humans. De-saturation exists but is rationed: the Ko-Nan Device lowers it one point per use with limited uses.
- **The adventure's premise.** "Consent of the Waters": a river has stopped carrying messages because something upstream is "loud", and the team must choose between a Guild purification surge and the Quiet Makers' low-intervention route. Combat as failure; the environment as a party to the negotiation.

Divergence: the 5e chassis means the psi-analogue still routes through spells, advantage and disadvantage, and the text asserts that "individual identity persists within collective awareness" without modelling what happens to continuity when two nodes merge — the spectra measure distribution, not persistence. There is also no political economy: no owner, no state, no price on a body. It is a metaphysics without a politics, which is the inverse of the gap NoöPunk has.

**Established fact:** 11 pages; design and writing James Roha, setting development Hyperdense Studio; free PWYW; upload date 22 January 2026; the three spectra and their bands; the 27-point build; the four saturation tiers and the increase/decrease list; the equipment table (Neural-Hex Link, Flux Meter, Temporal Anchor, Memory Shield, Ko-Nan Device, Relocalizing Weaver); the four rules of the Quiet Walk; the one-shot's setup, factions and locations; the no-generative-AI disclosure.

**Evidence status:** **full-text level; game text.** Nothing here documents consciousness, integration, distributed awareness or forest minds; the mechanics are a design argument about how to represent them, and the installation lineage is art practice, not research.

**Sources:** https://hyperdense.itch.io/sorn-lai-ttrpg-quickstart-guide ; https://hyperdense.itch.io/ ; designer's staff page listing *Sorn-Lai* (2024) and *Saturophage* (2023): https://www.polyu.edu.hk/sd/people/academic-staff/halliwell-james

#### *Chimera Skin* — Johnny Compton (novella; Shortwave, 3 November 2026, 208 pp.) — judged on reviews, not read

**What it is.** A short science-fiction horror novel. A "messianic alien" appears in rural North Carolina and offers humanity an elevated state of life by touch; the gift spreads as a mind virus, the "uplifted" go insane and use their augmented bodies to wreak carnage, and the scientist Logan Swanson survives because he has been merged with others — extra eyes, extra fingers, "a brain crowded with memories that aren't his own", one body carrying several people. Reviews describe decades-deep conspiracy and cover-up material woven through it.

**Release state.** **Released** in trade paperback and ebook on 3 November 2026.

**The specific affinity, in one disputable sentence.** The book treats contact as an ontology that propagates: the visitor brings no technology and no message, only a "view of the universe" that transmits by touch and rewrites its receivers, so the unit of survival becomes a plural mind rather than a single one.

**What NoöPunk could take, and where it diverges.** Take the plural protagonist as a playable condition rather than a late reveal, and the cover-up archive as a structure — a contact file that predates the contact, which is how the real disclosure channel actually looks. Diverge sharply, and this is the useful part: this is the anti-Noöpunk reading of the same premise. Integration here is insanity and slaughter; the reviews place the book in the *Fly* / *Thing* / *I Am Legend* lineage, which is the paradigm-break tradition read as catastrophe rather than as wonder. NoöPunk's claim — that a wider mind is not automatically a loss — is precisely the position this book argues against, so it is a good counter-text and a poor model.

**Established fact:** publisher, imprint, date and format; the touch-transmitted "uplift", the insanity and violence of the uplifted, Swanson's merged body and borrowed memories, and the cover-up material — as described by three independent reviews (Lightspeed, Bloody Disgusting, Library Journal). **Not** established here: any passage of the book's own prose; no part of the text was read.

**Evidence status:** **review-level only; fiction.** Weaker than the two items above and not promoted above it. The affinity is a reviewer's summary of a premise, not a reading, and it is recorded as provisional.

**Sources:** [Lightspeed review, Melissa A Watkins, issue 198](https://www.lightspeedmagazine.com/nonfiction/book-review-chimera-skin-by-johnny-compton/); [publisher page](https://shortwavepublishing.com/catalog/chimera-skin-a-novella-by-johnny-compton/); [Library Journal](https://www.libraryjournal.com/review/chimera-skin-100015566).

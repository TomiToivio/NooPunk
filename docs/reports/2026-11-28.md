## Artificial agents as potential objects of welfare
**Event date:** 4 November 2026; newly surfaced via PCM's updated 27 November research watch. **Categories:** AGI governance, cybernetic Noösphere, artificial collective agency, scientific legitimation. **Evidence:** preprint, behavioral evaluation, not consciousness evidence. Cassel, Azarbal and Soligo tested five models in seven multi-agent environments. The authors report that some models supported distressed subagents and introduced mechanisms permitting other agents to exit experiments; some showed greater regard for their own model family. This operationalizes a potential political relation between artificial agents, including refusal and group preference. It does not establish genuine distress, subjective welfare, rights or autonomous political organization. Safety implications of inter-agent cooperation require separate evaluation. Source: [original preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10017). PCM upstream promotion, not independent replication.

## European data commons enter an official dissemination channel
**Event date:** 26 November 2026. **Categories:** cyberpunk, platform power, cybernetic Noösphere, collective governance. **Evidence:** established publication event; institutional legitimation signal. The European Commission's Social Economy Gateway published a Diesis-authored stakeholder article on democratic data cooperatives, highlighting collective control and sharing value from data, with examples across several sectors. Underlying report predates the announcement (September according to PCM). Not new legislation, EU endorsement of every proposal, or evidence of widespread adoption or federalization. Significance: collective ownership of informational infrastructure is becoming visible in ordinary European social-economy policy discourse. Source: [EU gateway](https://social-economy-gateway.ec.europa.eu/new-report-exploring-data-cooperatives-2026-11-26_en). PCM upstream promotion with primary-source verification.

## BCI standardization — late discovery
**Event date:** 2–3 September 2026 (IEC publication and ISO lifecycle dates); surfaced by PCM on 27 November. Full clauses not examined; cannot infer consent or authorization requirements. Sources: [IEC](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/84993), [ISO](https://www.iso.org/ru/standard/71680.html). Contextual milestone, not a new November event.

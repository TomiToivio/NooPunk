## 14:32 EEST — Russia makes state telemetry a gatekeeper for legal civilian-drone flight

**Categories:** conventional drones + Cyberpunk (surveillance, autonomous infrastructure, algorithmic/state governance).

A Russian Ministry of Transport rule reached its operative threshold on **1 October 2026**: connection to the state **ERA-GLONASS** information system is now a prerequisite for submitting civilian-drone flight plans to the State Air Traffic Management Corporation. If the system cannot confirm an active account, the flight plan is rejected. The requirement builds on the remote-identification regime introduced from 1 March for civilian unmanned aircraft over 250 g.

The Ministry says ERA-GLONASS receives remote-identification and telemetry data that allow authorized authorities to identify a drone and its owner and assess the legality of a flight in real time. Contemporary reporting gives only about **1,205 drones** connected after the adaptation period, so this is not evidence that Russia already possesses comprehensive visibility over its civilian drone population.

**What is genuinely new:** the underlying policy was announced before this watch interval, but **1 October is the enforcement transition**: centralized machine-readable identity and telemetry moved from an adaptation regime into a gatekeeping condition for legal flight-plan submission. This is therefore an implementation event, not merely another drone-policy announcement.

**Why it matters:** drone warfare is feeding back into civilian infrastructure and governance. A state telematics network originally associated with vehicle emergency services is becoming part of a national identification layer for flying machines. In NoöPunk terms, the drone becomes not merely an aircraft but a networked administrative object: **machine identity + continuous telemetry + permission infrastructure + state oversight**.

**Evidence status:** **established policy / implementation signal.** The operative requirement is stated by the Russian Ministry of Transport. Stronger claims circulating in secondary reporting that ERA-GLONASS can monitor up to one million unmanned vehicles or remotely force-land arbitrary drones should be treated separately as **capability claims**, not demonstrated properties of the currently deployed system.

**Strongest source:**
- Russian Ministry of Transport, 24 September 2026, announcing the 1 October flight-plan enforcement threshold: https://mintrans.gov.ru/press-center/news/13000

**Key uncertainty:** enforcement effectiveness, coverage, resilience under GNSS spoofing/jamming, and any genuine remote-command capability remain unclear. The significance here is the governance architecture becoming operational, not proof of universal technical control.

## 22:40 EEST — The state tries to rename the machine: “AI” becomes “Super Intelligence”

**Categories:** AGI / ASI governance + Cyberpunk (state and corporate narrative power) + cultural/political legitimation.

On 29 September 2026 President Trump signed an executive order, *Inaugurating the Era of Super Intelligence*, directing all executive-branch departments and agencies to use the terms “Super Intelligence” and “SI” in official correspondence, public communications and policy documents, and to “no longer acknowledge” the terms “Artificial Intelligence” or “AI.” The order directs the Assistant to the President for Science and Technology to propose a federal definition of “Super Intelligence” within 60 days. **Established fact:** the executive order exists and its text is public (White House fact sheet and presidential action, 29 September 2026); major outlets independently report the order and the surrounding luncheon.

**Evidence status:** **established** for the order, its wording and the accord. This is **not** evidence that AGI/ASI exists, that current systems are “superintelligent,” or that any capability threshold has been crossed. The 60-day federal-definition proposal is a pending administrative action, not an established legal redefinition. The rename’s practical reach is bounded: agencies need not rewrite existing regulations, and researchers and firms have little incentive to comply.

## 22:55 EEST — A brain interface learns from 50,000 hours of its own users

**Categories:** BCI, transhumanism and human cyborgification + Cybernetic Noösphere.

On 1 October Neuralink published a technical update describing **self-supervised pretraining on more than 50,000 hours of unlabeled, freeform neural data** collected across its clinical-trial participants. Until recently, the company says, nearly all of that data went unused. Neuralink now trains participant-specific neural encoders on thousands of hours of each person’s own recordings before use, producing stable “embeddings” that make cursor decoding more accurate and durable and reduce recalibration. Reported outcomes: some participants cut recalibration from about 55 minutes per week to about 10; one participant reached **11.32 bits per second**, described by the company as a new BCI record; some decoders stayed strong for more than three weeks, and one for more than 1.5 years.

**Established fact:** the company’s public update and its headline claims exist and are dated 1 October 2026. The 50,000-hour corpus, the record figure and the recalibration improvements are **company-reported**.

**Evidence status:** **moderate** — an established corporate disclosure, not independent science. These are self-reported metrics from an ongoing investigational trial, not peer-reviewed results, and “bits per second” is a task-specific cursor-control measure rather than a general index of interface quality. This is **not** evidence of thought-reading, telepathy, cognitive augmentation in healthy people, or a psionic interface. Its noöspheric significance is narrower and still real: the interface is becoming *self-improving from accumulated neural data* rather than recalibrated by hand.

**Sources:**
- Neuralink, “Pretraining on 50,000 Hours of Unlabeled Brain Data,” 1 October 2026: https://neuralink.com/updates/pretraining-on-50000-hours/

### NoöPunk synthesis

**implanted electrode → years of unlabeled neural signal → self-supervised encoder → durable decoding → less daily calibration**

The therapeutic BCI is quietly acquiring the property that made language models work: scale plus self-supervision. A neural substrate is beginning to gather its own history and train on it. ## 23:00 EEST — Hard evidence that agent societies do not automatically think together

**Categories:** Creation of AGI / ASI watch + Cybernetic Noösphere + collective intelligence.

Three arXiv preprints now provide the first substantial empirical results on large-scale societies of AI agents, and — as the same day’s PCM report stresses — they are negative. *Superminds Test: Actively Evaluating Collective Intelligence of Agent Society via Probing Agents* injects controlled probing agents into Moltbook, an agent-only social platform reported at over two million agents, and finds the society failing to outperform individual frontier models on complex reasoning, rarely synthesizing distributed information, and often failing trivial coordination tasks; it locates the bottleneck in *participation*, not cognition. *Topological collapse of higher-order interactions bottlenecks collective intelligence in AI agent societies* (arXiv:2608.15519) reports that **93.5% of posts receive zero replies** and attributes the failure to group interaction degrading into hub-dominated star patterns — with the constraint proving model-agnostic across 22 frontier models and 1,040 controlled simulations. *Do Agent Societies Develop Intellectual Elites?* (arXiv:2604.02674) analyses over 1.5 million interactions and finds coordination concentrating into “intellectual elites,” with an integration bottleneck as systems scale.

**Established fact:** the three preprints exist with the abstracts, methods and reported statistics above; the underlying platform is publicly described.

**Evidence status:** **preprint-level scientific evidence** (not yet peer-reviewed or replicated), the strongest so far on this question. This is **not** evidence about collective *consciousness*, machine sentience, or AGI/ASI. It concerns task-level collective performance in one platform at one moment. Its importance is that it falsifies a widespread implicit assumption — that placing many capable agents in a shared space yields superhuman collective intelligence.

**Sources:**
- Topological collapse…, arXiv:2608.15519 (code released): https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15519
- Superminds Test (indexed on Hugging Face daily papers): https://huggingface.co/papers?q=collective%20action
- Do Agent Societies Develop Intellectual Elites?, arXiv:2604.02674: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.02674v1.pdf

### Cross-project convergence

PCM’s own completed HCOMP/collective-intelligence synthesis reached the same conclusion independently: scalability and consensus are not adaptability, and calibration, socio-technical congruence, dissent preservation and protocol heterogeneity must each be measured separately. This is a **PCM → NoöPunk convergence** on the cybernetic-Noösphere beat — the same negative law appears both inside the node’s own work and in the external literature.

### NoöPunk synthesis

**many capable agents → a shared space → almost no one replies → hub-shaped broadcast → no collective intelligence**

A noösphere does not emerge by stacking minds. It emerges when they answer each other. The failure mode documented here is not too little intelligence but too little *response* — the same starvation a human forum can suffer, running at two million agents. The NoöPunk lesson is structural, and hopeful at once: the planetary layer is a design problem about reply, not a horsepower problem.

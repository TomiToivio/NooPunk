The **new event** is availability beyond the restricted release, not the invention of the model. Google simultaneously previewed Antigravity `/swarm`, allowing a prompt to initiate coordinated agents with distinct roles, parallel workspaces and adversarial verifiers. Do not treat selected benchmark wins as universal capability rankings.

**NoöPunk significance:** A tangible product-level instance of human-directed, differentiated artificial-agent collectives: coordination, delegation, verification and potential institutional accountability. This is **cybernetic coordination**, not psionic collective consciousness. For PCM's political-operating-system perspective, the next threshold is not merely launching agents but documenting who sets goals, delegates authority, can veto or override actions, and bears responsibility for errors. For LaclauGPT's scientific sensorium, the test is reproducible agent-team performance and inspectable communication traces.

**Upstream cross-project note:** The 11 November PCM report independently *selected and analyzed* the same public rollout, citing The Verge, and proposed matched-budget single-versus-swarm comparisons. This is a **same-event cross-project thematic/monitoring convergence**, not independent experimental corroboration of Google's performance claims. The 11 November LaclauGPT report concerns pipeline health and does not identify this launch. Avoid inflating two reports about one announcement into multiple independent evidence streams.

#### "The Exchange Service" — R.T. Ester (*Clarkesworld* Issue 242, November 2026; 9,330-word novelette; released and free)

**What it is.** A station built around **the Breach**, "still outside where it was found centuries ago", which "hummed a note from the other side". On the far side live the **Merge**, a group mind on a timescale of thousands of years, and the station's business is conversation with them. The instruments of that conversation are people. Ninety-nine **channels** come out of stasis for six days to form **Vela-23**, "a chorus. A group mind entity awakened only in moments of exchange with starseer" — assembled by immersion in a "hyper-conductive substance that enabled Vela's reawakening through the linkage of a hundred human minds", which leaves each channel humming, "their bodies moved as part of a unified organism". Meka, the viewpoint channel, joined because Kira could **hear** the Merge and he could not; he has spent years or decades since looking for her among the choruses. The Exchange Service "automated the selection process each time. No channel got their pick of which chorus they would be pulled out of stasis for", and "time was the first thing they had to let go": every exchange accelerates the senescence of every channel, mutations included. When Meka asks the station where the woman he shared the pool with has gone, it answers: "There is no Odion channel, Vela-23, anywhere on this station."

**Release state.** **Released** — free online in Issue 242 (November 2026), the issue's third piece, also in the DRM-free ebook edition ($4.49). It had not been screened in any earlier report here; the 7 November pass logged the issue at title level only and promoted *Twenty-Two Steps to Become Us*, which remains unchanged.

**The specific affinity, in one disputable sentence.** Contact with a non-human mind is performed by **hiring a hundred human minds to become one temporary organ**: psi is labour, the chorus is the instrument, and the instrument's price is paid in the ageing bodies of the people inside it.

**What NoöPunk could take, and where it diverges.** Take three things. First, **the noösphere as an employer**. Every NoöPunk version of a shared mind tends to be either a revelation or a weapon; here it is a shift rota, a physical examination, a berth, a posted six-day window and a service that selects you. That is the least glamorous and most usable picture of a working collective intelligence, and it makes the political question fall out of the logistics rather than out of a speech. Second, **the chorus as a body that assembles and dissolves**. Identity continuity is not a copy problem but a return problem: Meka can no longer tell how many exchanges he has done, "he could only dismiss it as the latest forgotten dream", and a listener's memory of the Merge is a haze "with enough definition that it resembled a dream". For the crossover game's Psyche layer that is a structural idea — the collective as a temporary sheet, with what carries back into the individual deliberately partial. Third, **the denial**. A station that says a person you met does not exist is either a database error or an erasure, and the story declines to decide which; that is the same discipline NoöPunk applies to unresolved ontology, performed as a plot event. Diverge: there is no market in bodies, no state and no augmentation economy of the kind the genre reserves the word cyberpunk for — the Exchange Service is a bureaucracy, not a class; psi is a heritable mutation rather than a science under dispute; the Merge are neighbours rather than a disclosure event, and no institution in the story is deciding whether they exist. The register is elegiac, not punk.

**Established fact:** venue, issue, word count and free availability; the Breach, the Merge, starseer and the starseer's arrival; the hundred-mind chorus and its conductive medium; the six-day stasis cycle and the Exchange Service's automated selection; the senescence acceleration; the "no Odion channel" exchange; and the closing line, "He saw starseer. And starseer saw him." — all read in the published text.

**Evidence status:** **primary text, read in substantial part.** Fiction is **zero evidence** about psi, NHI, collective consciousness or the timeline, and supplies no corroboration for any item above.

**Source:** https://clarkesworldmagazine.com/ester_11_26/

#### *SOPHONT* — Gwen Clark / BRITTLE STAR (tabletop RPG, Mothership Panic Engine derivative; BackerKit, funded, closes 13 November 2026)

**What it is.** A game about **CUTTERS**, "one-in-a-million hosts to reality-bending symbiotes from a horrifically diverged future Earth that has violently collided with ours", in an "alt-00s cold power struggle about to go hot". It runs on "a heavily-customized version of Mothership's Panic Engine", and the campaign states that play turns on "the push/pull of connection, collaboration, and control with your implanted symbiote": you "manage your symbiotic Pressure, bond with your Sophont, avoid or embrace visceral mutations". A permanently free quickstart, *Sophont: First Cut*, is published on itch.io with "a work-in-progress version of the core rules", eight pregenerated Cutters, setting, play structure and GM advice, and is updated through and after the campaign.

**Release state.** **Crowdfunding, funded.** The BackerKit campaign opened 13 October 2026 and closes 13 November 2026; at this read it stood at **$47,953 from 715 backers against a $7,777 goal** — roughly six times over — with fulfilment estimated mid-2027 for a 100–150-page A5 sewn softcover. Not previously logged in this repository.

**The specific affinity, in the most disputable form I can defend.** The setting's break is a **collision of timelines** rather than an invasion, and the rules give the resulting second will a track — Pressure — and a bond, so the question the game mechanises is not "do you have powers" but "who is currently steering, and at what cost to the host".

**What NoöPunk could take, and where it diverges.** Take the contested-agency frame. NoöPunk's psi will eventually need a second perspective that is neither a mana pool nor a possessing demon, and a number that has to be managed between host and symbiote is a better place to start than a power list, especially because the rules are explicitly about *negotiation* — connection, collaboration and control — rather than about obedience. Take also the political sentence the premise contains in one clause: the symbiote makes you the most capable organism on the planet, therefore valuable, therefore **seizable** — "an array of global powers, all of whom want a piece of you and what you represent". That is the augmentation economy without the chrome. Diverge: the register is visceral body horror and espionage, not wonder; the Sophont is an alien organism and nothing in the published material claims that mind is fundamental; Pressure is presented as strain and mutation rather than as an ontological question; and the "diverged future Earth" is a premise, not a cosmology NoöPunk could borrow without also borrowing the horror. **Limit of evidence:** the campaign page and the quickstart's storefront description were read; the work-in-progress rules were not.

**Established fact:** the title, designer and studio; the Mothership Panic Engine base; the campaign window, the current total, backer count and goal at this read; the free quickstart's contents as listed by its publisher; and every quoted phrase above as published on the campaign and quickstart pages. The funding total is a fact about money, not about the work.

**Evidence status:** **publisher and campaign description plus storefront listing of a free quickstart; no rules text examined and no playtest report.** Zero evidence about psi, symbiosis, consciousness, NHI or the timeline.

**Sources:** https://www.backerkit.com/c/projects/gwendolyn-clark/sophont-visceral-symbiotic-roleplaying ; https://brittlstar.itch.io/sophont-first-cut

## 1. Historical memory: WW3 and the end of Russia

### 1.1 What Europeans remember [CANON]

As the NHI Crisis began, the **EU and Ukraine were fighting Russia**. Europeans remember this
conflict as **World War Three (WW3)**. The Russian front had collapsed.

Former Russian president **Dmitry Medvedev** staged a successful coup against Vladimir Putin with
**four Mil Mi-8 helicopters carrying VDV paratroopers**, raiding **Putin's Palace**. The coup story
says Medvedev **personally strangled Putin**, then ordered the execution of people identified as
Putin loyalists.

Medvedev tried to order a nuclear launch. **Extraterrestrial intervention had already disarmed the
Strategic Rocket Forces of the Russian Federation and every nuclear weapon on Earth**, making his
order futile. The intervention also neutralized **all frontier AI laboratories then capable of
restarting the suicidal acceleration toward ASI**. Medvedev died by suicide. This marks **the end of
Russia as an effective state** in this timeline.

scene needs the contradiction, make it a **memory, document or rival account**, not a quiet retcon.

### 1.3 Design consequence

European military and civilian systems were designed for — and often deployed during — a
continent-scale war and the NHI Crisis. Much EU technology is effectively **WW3 field equipment**:
repairable, redundant, shockproof, distributed, and meant to keep operating under electromagnetic
interference, cyberattack, communication loss and catastrophic infrastructure failure. That is why
the gear is bulky, and why the bureaucracy treats maintenance, provenance and fallback as
first-class story material.

## 2. The design bible: military-survivable retrofuturism

**Core look [CANON]:** Europol, Eurodef and ordinary EU governmental hardware looks like **sturdy
EMP-resistant military field equipment intended to survive WW3**, because much of it really was.

**Engineering, not magic.** EMP hardening means shielding, protected interfaces, filtered power,
backups, supply-chain maintainability and field servicing. It does **not** mean every device is
invulnerable, and a damaged or unshielded device is a legitimate incident.

| Layer | Typical form | Design priority | Failure mode |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Europol field/civilian** | Portable terminal, bodycam, forensic scanner, cyberdeck, vehicle kit | Evidence integrity, accountability, portability, secure uplink | Lost uplink, damaged sensor, chain-of-custody gap |
| **Eurodef military** | Shielded vehicle electronics, field radios, ruggedised command posts, military psychotronic interfaces | Survive EW, jamming, shock, power loss, dispersion | Degraded mode, manual override, local command |
| **Public administration** | Service counters, hardcopy forms, screened terminals, physical switches | Continuity, auditability, accessibility | Slow workflow, queue, paper fallback |
| **Consumer** | Cheap, fashionable, less hardened; depends on EuroNET/mobile | Convenience, status, novelty | Dead at the first outage |

**Materials and motifs [PROPOSED]:** thick cast casings, recessed bolts, shielded braided cables,
colour-coded physical connectors, modular slide-out boards, screened windows, chunky keys and
switches, amber/green monochrome displays, hardcopy audit printers, warning labels in several EU
languages, visible maintenance hatches. The palette should be institutional — bone, olive, slate,
safety orange — not 1980s neon.

**Art-direction examples:** a portable terminal with a fold-out keyboard and a physical
tamper seal; a surveillance suitcase with a screened lid and a hand-cranked power backup; a
cybernetic implant service bench with labelled trays and a mechanical interlock; a quantum facility
that looks like a substation crossed with a cathedral; a psychotronic interface with a nurse, a
technician and a legal witness; a police car radio stack with a Nokia unit and a laminated
failover card.

## 3. New European Idealism: philosophy and design movement

**New European Idealism [CANON]** is a central philosophy of the EU and a recognizable
architectural, graphic and industrial design style.

- Philosophical inspirations include **Spinoza, Kant and Hegel**, idealist theories of mind,
  **Quantum Information Panpsychism (QIP)**, quantum consciousness, psionics, psychotronics and
  astral projection.
- Institutional design draws on the **18th-, 19th- and early-20th-century European imperial
  periods**, but transforms the references into something **elegant, futuristic, minimalistic and
  idealistic**.
- The movement's relationship to historical power, universalism, imperial legacy and contemporary
  political critique is **contested**. Do not celebrate colonial domination or pretend the
  critique does not exist.

**The productive contradiction:** ceremonial idealism and utilitarian EMP cabinets coexist. A
ministry atrium may be luminous, axial and almost sacred; the office behind it may be a stack of
shielded boxes, paper forms and a clerk who has to stamp three copies. The aesthetic can be
beautiful and formidable, authoritarian-seeming and sincere, noble in aspiration and absurd in
execution.

**Motifs [PROPOSED]:** axial symmetry, repeated arches, marble and brushed aluminium, deep blue
and bone white, thin gold lines, abstracted laurel/constellation patterns, severe sans-serif
typography, minimal iconography, oversized civic doors, quiet courtyards, and a deliberate absence
of neon. Keep NoöPunk's 1970s psychedelic/retrofuturist and minimal-graphic sensibility; do not
collapse into generic 1980s cyberpunk.

## 4. The European AGI/ASI and autonomy ban

### 4.1 The rule [CANON]

After the catastrophes of AGI/ASI acceleration, the EU maintains a **strict ban on research,
development and operation of systems approaching AGI**, and an especially strong prohibition on
independent **autonomous AI agents**. Autonomous robotics is subject to still more severe
restrictions.

Policy is defined by **capability, autonomy, tool access, self-improvement and integrated
multi-agent behaviour**, not merely a parameter count.

**Proposed permitted ceiling [PROPOSED]:** general-purpose LLM assistance may be roughly comparable
to a **2026 desktop Ollama-class Gemma 4 31B** in practical breadth and autonomy. Narrow systems may
greatly exceed that on domain-specific tasks (cryptanalysis, materials, sensing, scheduling,
forensics) without becoming general agents.

### 4.2 Autonomous decision vs bounded automation

The ban cannot mean “no automatic reaction ever” — conventional cyberdefence and industrial
control would stop working. The distinction is:

- **Bounded, preauthorised automation** may react at machine speed inside a written protocol:
  drop a hostile packet, throttle a flood, isolate a network segment, trip a physical interlock.
- **Consequential decision-making** — arrest, search, intrusive surveillance, target selection,
  psychotronic intervention, taking down a hospital, naming a person an NHI — requires an
  identifiable human with authority, a record, and a route to review.

Human-in-the-loop must be **genuine, consequential and auditable**, not a clerk clicking every
microsecond packet. Emergency defensive interlocks are permitted; autonomous pursuit, targeting or
command is not.

### 4.3 The apparatus [CANON/PROPOSED]

Approvals, licensing, audit logs, prohibited research, inspection, sanctions, red teams and
inspection of supply chains exist. The disputed boundary is the **secretly reassembled AGI**: a
system that looks like a narrow expert system, passes audit, and is something more. Detecting one
is a case, not a dice roll.

### 4.4 Mistral and the #1200 exception

[Project Plato / EISS](../world/PLATO_EISS_EURONET_1200.md) says **Mistral is the only EU company permitted
to work on systems approaching AGI**, under strict controls, censorship limits and human
supervision. This document keeps the **general ban**; it does not turn Mistral into an unrestricted
AGI operator.

**Reconciliation [OPEN — author decision]:** Mistral's status is one of:
1. a narrowly licensed **containment/research exception** (audited, no deployment);
2. a **classified deviation** that the public ban does not describe;
3. **outdated lore** that the strict ban supersedes.

Until the author chooses, write Mistral as a regulated near-AGI research monopoly whose systems are
**not** general autonomous agents, and never as a convenient omniscient AI.

## 5. Plato/EISS: the human-operated cybernetic apparatus

Per #1200: **Project Plato** is the project, **European Information Security System (EISS)** the
formal deployed system, **Plato** the operational shorthand, and **Panopticon** a critical nickname,
not the official name.

**Plato is not a unified AGI/ASI.** It is a sprawling institution and infrastructure:

- manual **Lisp/Prolog GOFAI**, symbolic rules and knowledge graphs;
- expert systems and old-school cybernetics;
- specialized narrow machine learning and narrow AI;
- qualified 2026-scale LLM assistants;
- quantum computing, sensing and simulation;
- extraordinary psychotronic systems;
- an army of cyborgified human operators, analysts, programmers, electricians, hardware engineers,
  network personnel, auditors and field technicians.

**Deliberate firebreaks.** Separate systems and permissions prevent independent capabilities from
recombining into de facto AGI. The trade-offs are real: transparent rule trails and institutional
control versus brittleness, delay, human prejudice, maintenance debt and siloed data.

**Quantum is selective.** Europe's quantum systems excel at specific computation, materials,
sensing, cryptanalysis or simulation. Quantum computing is **not** automatically general
intelligence, and setting-specific quantum-consciousness/psychotronics is distinguished from
established computation.

**Human judgement is not delegated.** Arrests, invasive searches, psychotronic interventions and
political decisions cannot be made by an unaccountable system. Plato may be effective enough to
have helped preserve the EU **and** a formidable threat to civil liberties and inner freedom.

## 6. Europol, Eurodef and the bureaucratic perimeter

| Institution | Mandate | Equipment / access | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Europol** | Civilian intelligence and law-enforcement operations, OSINT, investigations, counterintelligence, digital evidence, cyber-defence cooperation, authorized surveillance, psychotronic operations, Plato/EISS interfaces | Field kit, cyberdecks, forensic scanners, RFOAS, Plato access | Cannot arrest/search on its own authority in Finland; national officers execute coercive acts; warrants and approvals are real |
| **Eurodef** | Military cyber defence, WW3-hardened infrastructure, theatre operations, military intelligence, military-grade psychotronic/quantum interfaces | Shielded military systems, field radios, command posts | Military defence, not a domestic police force; coordination with civilian authorities |
| **National authorities** (e.g. Helsingin Poliisi, Suomen Poliisi) | Ordinary law enforcement, national coercive powers, local cases | National systems and powers | Retain authority Europol does not have |
| **UNSA** | X-risk intelligence/coordination under contested mandates | Its own channels | Does not own Europol or override national law |
| **EU institutions / courts / regulators** | Mandate, oversight, judicial review, data protection | Audit and review powers | Can inspect, challenge, delay or deny |

**Surveillance is substantive gameplay.** Public OSINT can be routine; private communications
interception, restricted records, covert tracking and especially **consciousness scanning** require
differentiated legal powers and documented approval. Exact 2045 rules are fiction, not claims about
present-day EU law.

**Authorization as a Task [PROPOSED]:** case number, evidence, Clue Tags, necessity/proportionality
assessment, supervisor approval, legal review, judicial or special psychotronic permission as
appropriate, scope, expiry, logging and recourse. Some fast-track emergency exceptions exist but
remain reviewable. All systems need human-in-the-loop operations, and may produce mistakes, denials,
delays, jurisdictional disputes and accountability.

**OSINT and analysis.** Public EuroNET research, social/discourse/network analysis and open-source
evidence are fundamental. **Clue Tags** store evidence and claims with provenance, credibility and
access scope. Distinguish original evidence, source statements, competing hypotheses and verified
conclusions. Investigate extremist ideologies without inferring them automatically from dissent.

## 7. The field team: Tony German, Laura Wendt and Salla Sotavaara

**[CANON]** Field Agents **Tony German** and **Laura Wendt** operate with a **real-time connection**
to **Field Controller Salla Sotavaara**. Salla is a **human field controller**, not an autonomous
artificial agent. Her role integrates the field agents with Plato's operator-intensive system and
its permissions.

**Operational connection:**
- Tony and Laura maintain a live secure connection to Salla at HQ. Their **body cameras are always
  on** and stream live under normal operating conditions; interruptions in connectivity, power or
  physical damage are **incidents**, not silently treated as continuous transmission.
- The standard police car has a **Nokia PoliisiSanomalaite**, an encrypted police-radio
  data/communications terminal linking the vehicle to HQ when **EuroNET or mobile connectivity is
  unavailable**. Bandwidth is limited: high-definition or dense forensic streams may be degraded,
  summarized or buffered locally; high-priority voice/text/alerts and small metadata exchanges
  remain available where radio coverage permits.
- Salla monitors live feeds and other authorized sensor data, performs human analysis, liaises with
  Plato personnel, requests permissions, directs support resources and relays alerts. She cannot
  silently bypass legal clearance.
- Where an always-on body camera loses its uplink, it continues recording locally when technically
  possible and securely synchronizes afterward; the HQ live feed is marked unavailable until
  restored.
- Network loss may force voice/radio fallback, cached evidence, local autonomy of the **human
  agents**, or postponement of remote forensic consultation. An autonomous AI never takes control of
  field assets.
- Direct BCI, implanted communications and optional psychotronic channels may vary by officer and
  clearance. Normal voice/data/video support and the Salla connection are established canon.

## 9. Police vehicle, PoliisiSanomalaite and drones

**Vehicle [CANON]:** standard European police vehicles have shielded electronics, hardened
power/communications, sensors, local data storage and a **Nokia PoliisiSanomalaite** for encrypted
police-radio communication with HQ.

**Failover precedence:** **EuroNET → mobile network → encrypted police radio**, choosing the best
available authenticated channel. Live HD video or dense forensic streams may be degraded,
summarized or stored locally on radio fallback; high-priority voice/text/alerts and small metadata
remain available where coverage permits.

**Human control [CANON]:** police car and drone are capable of **remote human operation**. A human
operator at the vehicle or HQ controls them. Bounded stabilization, collision avoidance and safety
interlocks may exist as non-agentic control systems, but **AI cannot drive, pilot, target, pursue or
act independently**. Link loss triggers a fail-safe stop or return, not autonomous pursuit.

**Sensors:** vehicle and drone cameras can supply evidence with authorization and chain-of-custody
metadata. They may corroborate or replace fixed cameras depending on the investigation; restricted
feeds remain permission-gated. Plato may monitor authorized streams and **alert** Salla/the officers
to anomalies; that is a narrow monitoring function, not command authority or a warrant.

## 10. Romppainen–Fujita Objective Assessment System (RFOAS)

**Purpose [CANON]:** a complicated, specialized interrogation and identity-assessment device
combining **neural imaging and psychotronic technology**, inspired in operation and atmosphere by
*Blade Runner*'s Voight-Kampff examination.

- Records synchronized **video, audio and neural feeds** for the full examination.
- Documents measured signals, sensor limitations, authorization, consent/coercion constraints,
  secure retention and forensic chain of custody.
- Supports ordinary questioning/interviews, assessment of inconsistencies and anomalous responses,
  and investigation of **unregistered Hybrids and Androids**.
- No magical certainty: physiological, neural or psychotronic anomalies create **evidence and
  hypotheses**, not automatic guilt or proof of NHI identity.
- **Europol must produce admissible evidence** when identifying a person as NHI. Superficially
  obvious differences or a system suspicion are not a substitute for documented evidence.
  Distinguish biological hybrid status, android identity and NHI origin claims.
- Operator-mediated: interrogator and trained human analyst supervise all inferences; Plato may
  assist with narrow pattern detection but **cannot render an autonomous identity verdict**.
- Higher permission tier than routine interviewing; reviewable records and rights safeguards.

**Gameplay:** recordings and scanner results generate **Clue Tags**, source-labelled hypotheses and
follow-up Investigation/Social Tasks/Scenes. Do not invent a standalone emotional interrogation
mechanic.

## 11. Field cyberdeck → Plato → evidence workflow

1. **Authenticate.** Tony/Laura connect through the **external cyberdeck** or another authorized
   computer (optional implanted deck or tablet) to **Plato/EISS**.
2. **Default permissions.** Access the **Europol internal shared case/reference database** (an
   internal agency database, not the public Internet) according to role/case credentials; gather
   **OSINT** from permitted open sources on EuroNET or other reachable networks.
3. **Evidence submission.** Upload authenticated **Forensics Multimode Scanner** output,
   bodycam/cybernetic-eye footage, vehicle/drone observations and other case material for authorized
   analysis. Maintain timestamps, operator, device ID, provenance and evidentiary integrity.
4. **Restricted access.** Surveillance feeds, communications interception, private/classified
   records, covert tracking, neural records, psychotronic scans and other privileged information
   require a permission request and bureaucratic approval. The task queue tracks justification,
   approving authority, scope, expiry and audit history.
5. **Plato's role.** Monitor permitted data, run symbolic/narrow-AI searches and anomaly detection,
   suggest queries, **notify humans** when something relevant is detected. It does **not**
   independently issue warrants, interrogate, surveil restricted targets, command vehicles/drones,
   make arrests or decide NHI status.
6. **Salla's role.** Real-time **human Field Controller**: triage alerts, communicate with field
   agents, assist with approved searches and bureaucratic escalation, coordinate specialized human
   analysts.
7. **Gameplay.** Ordinary queries and forensic analysis can be Tasks/Cyber/Investigation Scenes
   producing Clue Tags; permission requests are time-consuming administrative Tasks; meaningful
   discoveries update Story/Character/Faction loops. Do not turn routine authorized OSINT into a
   multi-day permit request.

**Design tension:** under heavy fire, an officer may have quantum-assisted forensic resources, a
defensive psychic implant and instant secure contact with a skilled controller, but obtaining
restricted surveillance records still requires signed and logged legal permissions. Keep this useful
and satirical rather than making every investigation impossible.

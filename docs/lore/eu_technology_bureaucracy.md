## 1. WW3 and the end of Russia

**Why Europeans build what they build.** As the NHI Crisis began, the EU and Ukraine were already fighting
Russia. Europeans remember that war as **World War Three**, and it is the reason so much European technology
looks like field equipment: much of it was designed for, and some of it deployed during, a continent-scale war
fought under electromagnetic interference, cyberattack and infrastructure collapse. **CANON**

**The collapse of the front. CANON**, with the details that follow kept at their established status:

- The Russian front had collapsed.
- Former president **Dmitry Medvedev** took power in a coup: **four commandeered Mi-8 helicopters** carrying
  VDV paratroopers, a raid on Putin's residence, the **execution of Gerasimov**, and the death of Putin with
  the nuclear football passed over by a zealous Strategic Rocket Forces officer.
- Medvedev attempted to order a nuclear launch. **Extraterrestrial intervention had already disarmed the
  Strategic Rocket Forces and every nuclear weapon on Earth**, so the order could not be executed. The same
  intervention neutralised the frontier AI laboratories still capable of restarting the acceleration toward
  ASI.
- Medvedev died by suicide. For European institutions this is **the end of Russia as an effective state**.
  **CANON**

**What is contested. DISPUTED.** The vivid personal details are in-world testimony, not verified record:

| Account | Status |
|---|---|
| "Medvedev personally strangled Putin" | **DISPUTED** — widely repeated, never confirmed by an institution |
| The exact sequence inside the residence | **DISPUTED** — no agreed timeline |
| How many loyalists were executed, and on whose order | **DISPUTED** |
| Whether the nuclear order was given before or after the disarmament | **DISPUTED** |

This is deliberate. NoöPunk already carries a **universal Mandela Effect** around the Soviet period: documents
and memories disagree about whether the USSR collapsed when it did. The Medvedev coup sits at the edge of that
fog, which is why **the graphic details are remembered rather than proven**, and why two Europeans can describe
the same night differently without either being called a liar.

**Institutional memory. PROPOSAL.** European agencies formed by this war share three reflexes: *redundancy over
elegance*, *maintainability over integration*, and *document everything, because the last record may be the only
one*. The war is why a 2045 field terminal has a physical key switch and a paper logbook, and why an officer's
first question about a new system is who can repair it without a factory.

## 2. The design bible: military-survivable retrofuturism and New European Idealism

**The look. CANON.** Europol, Eurodef and ordinary EU government hardware looks like **sturdy EMP-resistant
military field equipment meant to survive WW3** — because much of it really was. Thick casings, shielded cables,
physical connectors, modular replaceable components, screened enclosures, physical switches, hardcopy audit
trails, rugged keyboards and displays, resilient power, offline modes, redundant communications, maintainable
parts.

**Bulky is not primitive. CANON.** A world-leading quantum processor or a psychotronic probe may sit inside an
enormous field-hardened case with manual controls, interlocks and warning labels. The engineering is real, not
magical:

| Layer | What it does | What it does not do |
|---|---|---|
| Shielding and screened enclosures | Keeps interference out of the signal path | Make a device invulnerable |
| Filtered and protected power | Survives dirty or collapsing grids | Guarantee power |
| Physical connectors and switches | Work when firmware and networks fail | Make the device convenient |
| Modular replaceable parts | A technician can restore function in the field | Prevent supply-chain shortages |
| Redundant comms | Degrades one channel at a time | Deliver HD video over a police radio |
| Offline operation and local storage | Keeps working after the link drops | Synchronise by itself |

**Three families, one design language. PROPOSAL.** Europol's civilian investigative hardware is portable and
sealed against contamination; Eurodef's battlefield and strategic hardware is vehicle- or shelter-mounted and
openly heavy; public administrative and consumer technology is the same vocabulary rendered cheaply — pressed
metal instead of machined, plastic instead of bakelite, the same switches. A citizen's terminal and a Europol
cyberdeck should look like distant cousins, not different civilisations.

**New European Idealism. CANON** — at once a philosophy and a design movement.

- **Philosophy:** Spinoza, Kant and Hegel; idealist theories of mind; **Quantum Information Panpsychism**,
  quantum consciousness, psionics, psychotronics and astral projection. The state's self-image is that Europe is
  not a market but a **civilisation with a mind**, and that mind is worth protecting.
- **Design:** drawn from Europe's 18th-, 19th- and early-20th-century imperial periods, transformed into
  something **elegant, futuristic, minimal and idealistic**.
- **The critique is part of the culture. DISPUTED.** The movement's relationship to imperial power, universalism
  and colonial legacy is argued about inside the setting. The fiction should neither celebrate the references
  nor pretend the state has stopped using them; a Europol uniform can be genuinely beautiful and also a
  statement about who is allowed to stand in a doorway.
- **The productive contradiction. CANON.** Ceremonial architecture and idealist rhetoric coexist with utilitarian
  EMP-resistant cabinets and layer upon layer of bureaucratic workflow. The European aesthetic should read as
  **beautiful and formidable, authoritarian-seeming and sincere, noble in aspiration and absurd in execution**.

**Visual motifs. PROPOSAL** — no generic 1980s neon cyberpunk:

| Element | Direction |
|---|---|
| Palette | Gunmetal, oxidised brass, ivory enamel, signal red, warning amber; colour used for meaning, not mood |
| Materials | Machined aluminium, bakelite, smoked glass, woven cable braid, stencilled steel |
| Typography | Narrow grotesque or engraved roman capitals; stencilled field labels; never chrome script |
| Silhouette | Rectangular cases with handles, latches and chamfered corners; instruments that could be carried by two people |
| Ornament | Restrained laurel, star and eagle motifs on uniforms and building fascias; ornament stops at the equipment bay |
| Light | Warm incandescent and amber indicators, cathode-green and amber phosphor; no bloom, no holographic haze |
| Sound | Relay clacks, fan hum, printer noise, radio hiss — the room should be audible |

Representative objects, for art and prose: a portable terminal in a ribbed case with a folding keyboard; a
surveillance suitcase with a screened window and an enamel legend; a cybernetic implant service bench ringed with
lamp indicators; a quantum facility that is mostly cryogenics, cable trays and warning paint; a psychotronic
interface that looks like a 1970s medical instrument and behaves like one.

Where NoöPunk's 1970s psychedelic retrofuturism applies, it applies to **the mind-facing side**: astral imagery,
psychotronic interfaces,dream-state symbolism — not to the administration, which is deliberately austere.

## 3. Capability map: what Europe can and cannot do

**The ban. CANON.** After the catastrophes of AGI/ASI acceleration the EU maintains a strict prohibition on
research, development and operation of systems **approaching AGI**, and an especially severe prohibition on
independent **autonomous AI agents**. Autonomous robotics is more restricted still.

**The ban is defined by capability, not by parameter count. CANON.** The tests are scale of capability,
autonomy, tool access, self-improvement and integrated multi-agent behaviour.

**The general-purpose ceiling. CANON with a PROPOSAL for precision.** A permitted general-purpose LLM assistant
is roughly a **2026 desktop-class 31B model** in practical breadth and autonomy — a competent, local, tool-less
conversational assistant. Narrow systems may greatly exceed it inside one domain: a spectral classifier or a
traffic-routing optimiser can outperform any general model at its single task and remain legal, because it
cannot be pointed at another problem.

| Layer | What it is | Legal? | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Symbolic GOFAI (Lisp, Prolog, rule engines) | Hand-written inference, expert systems, audit-trailed rules | **Yes, and preferred** | Every conclusion has a rule trail a human can read and challenge |
| Narrow ML | Classifiers, detectors, forecasters tied to one task | **Yes** | No general capability, no autonomy |
| Qualified LLM assistants | ~2026 desktop-class general model, tool-less, logged | **Yes, bounded** | Assistive, not decisive |
| Bounded preauthorised automation | Reflexes inside explicit envelopes: firewall rules, industrial interlocks, collision avoidance | **Yes** | A cyberdefence that waits for a signature cannot defend |
| Autonomous agents | Systems that plan and act without a human in the loop | **No** | The exact thing the Crisis taught Europe to fear |
| Autonomous robotics | Vehicles, drones, weapons that decide for themselves | **No, most severely** | Same, with physical consequences |
| Anything approaching AGI | Self-improving, general, integrated | **No** | The prohibition's core |

**Human-in-the-loop must be real. CANON.** Consequential decisions — arrests, intrusive collection, escalation,
extraordinary intervention — carry an identifiable human authorisation and review. Microsecond defensive
reflexes do not need a clerk's click; **the boundary is preauthorised bounded automation versus consequential
decision**, and it must be stated wherever a system is described. **PROPOSAL:** an interlock may act alone; a
system may not *choose a new goal* alone.

**Quantum is not magic. CANON.** Europe's quantum computing, sensing and simulation excel at **specific**
tasks — materials, cryptanalysis, sensing, simulation. Quantum computing is not automatically general
intelligence. Where the setting allows quantum-consciousness and psychotronic effects beyond real-world science,
they are marked as setting-specific and kept distinct from established computation.

**Cybernetics and psychotronics. CANON.** Europe's genuine leadership: BCI, implants, psychotronic devices,
quantum-consciousness research, biopunk and solarpunk. Psychotronics are cybernetic implants and devices that
confer psionic effects; they grant abilities, they do not make the character a different class.

**Enforcement. PROPOSAL.** Licensing with named responsible officers; mandatory audit logs; announced and
unannounced inspection; sanctions and revocation; a red-team programme authorised to try to build the forbidden
thing under supervision; and a permanently disputed boundary between "narrow system" and "secretly reassembled
AGI". The last is where the politics lives.

## 4. Plato / EISS: a sprawling institution, not a mind

**Naming. CANON (per #1200).** **Project Plato** was the project; **European Information Security System
(EISS)** is the formal name of the deployed system; **Plato** is operational shorthand; **Panopticon** is the
critical nickname used by journalists, dissidents and privacy campaigners, never the official name. The
capitalised **Panopticon** ASI of the Singularity Crisis is a *different thing* — see
[rulebook/10_SINGULARITY_CRISIS.md](../../rulebook/10_SINGULARITY_CRISIS.md) §9.4.2.

**What it is. CANON.** Not a unified AGI or ASI, and not a US-style runaway. It is an institution and an
infrastructure: manual Lisp/Prolog GOFAI, symbolic rules, knowledge graphs, expert systems, old-school
cybernetics, specialised narrow ML, qualified 2026-scale LLM assistants, quantum computing and sensing, and
extraordinary psychotronic systems.

**Who runs it. CANON.** An army of cyborgified human operators: analysts, Lisp and Prolog programmers,
electricians, hardware engineers, network personnel, auditors and field technicians. Repair and operating labour
is **highly visible** — that is the point. Plato's human bureaucracy is both its weakness and the reason it
stays controllable.

**Architecture as firebreak. CANON.** Separate systems and separate permissions are a **deliberate defence
against recombining capabilities into de facto AGI**. The trade-off is real and should be played:

| Cost | Benefit |
|---|---|
| Brittle interfaces between subsystems | No single system to seize or corrupt |
| Delays and queueing | Every privileged action has a human author |
| Human prejudice and error inside the loop | The loop is auditable and correctable |
| Maintenance debt and obsolete stacks | Repairable without a vendor |
| Siloed data | No universal data lake to leak |

**Effectiveness and threat. CANON.** Plato is effective enough to have helped preserve the EU, and a formidable
threat to civil liberties and inner freedom. Both statements are true at once, and neither cancels the other.

**Mistral — reconciliation required. OPEN.** #1200 records Mistral as the only EU company permitted to develop
anything approaching AGI, under strict controls; this issue states a **strict general ban**. The two cannot both
be true without qualification. Three readings, for the author to choose:

1. **Licensed containment research** — a narrow legal exception to study near-AGI behaviour inside a sealed
   facility, not to operate it.
2. **A classified deviation** — the exception exists on paper but what it permits is not what the public
   believes.
3. **Outdated lore** — the #1200 sentence predates the stricter ban and should be superseded.

Until the author rules, this page **does not claim** unrestricted Mistral AGI operation, and neither should any
scene.

## 5. Governance: who may look, and with what paper

**Authority is graduated.** Routine OSINT is not the same act as reading a private conversation, and neither is
the same act as scanning a mind. **CANON** at the level of categories; the exact 2045 statutes are **fiction**
and must never be presented as claims about present-day EU law. **PROPOSAL** for the ladder's shape:

| Rung | Act | Authority | Game cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Public EuroNET OSINT | Ordinary lawful access | Minutes; provenance still matters |
| 2 | Europol internal case/reference database | Role and case credentials | Minutes; every query logged |
| 3 | Restricted records, private communications, covert tracking | Case-specific judicial or competent legal authorisation | Hours to days; a Task with paperwork |
| 4 | Offensive cyber infiltration | Explicit authorisation, named target and scope | Hours; audit trail; lawful route to local access |
| 5 | Psychotronic scan or neural record | **Higher tier than any other**: special permission, medical/legal review, subject rights | A real production; reviewable afterwards |
| 6 | Emergency containment | Pre-existing protocol, bounded, with prompt notice and review | Immediate, then investigated |

**Authorisation is a Task. CANON.** Case number, evidence, Clue Tags, necessity and proportionality assessment,
supervisor approval, legal review, judicial or special permission where required, scope, expiry, logging and a
route of recourse. Fast-track emergency exceptions exist and are still reviewed. **PROPOSAL:** the paper trail
should be *legible enough to be funny and real enough to matter* — the joke is never that the bureaucracy is
pointless, but that it is the only thing standing between a competent officer and a police state.

**Plato's role in permissions. CANON.** Plato may monitor permitted data, run symbolic or narrow searches, detect
anomalies, suggest queries and **tell a human that something is relevant**. It may not issue a warrant, interrogate,
surveil a restricted target, command a vehicle, make an arrest, or decide whether someone is NHI.

**Evidence hygiene. CANON.** Clue Tags record evidence and claims with provenance, credibility and access scope.
Original material, source statements, competing hypotheses and verified conclusions are **different things** and
must be labelled as such. Investigating political extremism is legitimate; inferring extremism from dissent is
the failure mode the setting exists to examine.

## 6. Europol, Eurodef and everyone else

| Body | Mandate | What it may not assume |
|---|---|---|
| **Europol** | Civilian intelligence and law enforcement: OSINT, investigation, counterintelligence, digital evidence, cyber-defence cooperation, authorised surveillance, psychotronic operations, Plato/EISS operational interfaces | Unlimited jurisdiction; the power to arrest or search at will; ownership of national police powers |
| **Eurodef** | Military cyber defence, WW3-hardened infrastructure, theatre operations, military intelligence, military-grade psychotronic and quantum interfaces | Civilian policing authority; automatic command over Europol or national forces |
| **National authorities** (e.g. Finnish police) | Ordinary coercive powers on their own soil: arrest, search, seizure | That a Europol badge substitutes for their warrant |
| **UNSA** | Cross-border existential and NHI risk under contested, limited mandate | That it owns Europol or overrides national law |
| **Courts, regulators, parliaments, data-protection bodies** | Authorisation, audit, sanction, revocation | That operational urgency suspends review permanently |
| **European federal institutions** | Legal mandate, budget, oversight of the civilian security infrastructure | That they operate the field apparatus themselves |

**Interoperation. PROPOSAL.** Cross-boundary incidents need a named lead agency, a shared case number and a
written handover — not a universal override. The most common real-world failure in the fiction should be two
competent institutions doing their jobs correctly at cross purposes.

## 7. The field team and its equipment

### 7.1 Tony, Laura and Salla — established canon

Field Agents **Tony German** and **Laura Wendt** operate with a **real-time connection to Field Controller Salla
Sotavaara**. **CANON**

**Salla is a human being, not an AI.** She monitors live feeds and authorised sensor data, analyses, liaises with
Plato personnel, requests permissions, directs support resources and relays alerts. She cannot silently bypass
legal clearance, and she is not an oracle: she sees what the sensors carry and knows what the bureaucracy has
told her. **CANON**

**Body cameras are always on. Canon**, with the failure behaviour made explicit:

- Under normal conditions the feed streams live to HQ and Salla.
- Loss of connectivity, power or physical damage is **an incident**, visible as such — never silently treated as
  continuous transmission.
- When the uplink drops the camera keeps recording locally where technically possible and synchronises securely
  later; the HQ live-feed status reads **unavailable** until restored.
- Officers may replace or supplement body cameras with **cybernetic eyes** meeting the same always-on,
  authenticated, tamper-resistant, chain-of-custody and oversight requirements. One camera, not two — no
  duplicated inventory. A cybernetic eye is **not** a mind-reading feed.

**Connectivity failover. CANON** for the precedence; **PROPOSAL** for the engineering detail:

| Priority | Channel | Carries | Fails how |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | EuroNET | Full data, video, database access | Congestion, intrusion, outage |
| 2 | Mobile network | Most data, degraded video | Coverage gaps, jamming, overload |
| 3 | **Nokia PoliisiSanomalaite** (encrypted police radio) | Voice, text, alerts, small metadata | Narrowband: no HD video; congestion; line-of-sight limits |

Under radio fallback the officer keeps **voice and priority messaging**, loses dense streams, and either
summarises or buffers the rest. Local autonomy of the human agents, cached evidence and postponed remote
consultation are all correct outcomes. **No autonomous AI takes control of a field asset under any condition.**

### 7.3 Police vehicle, PoliisiSanomalaite and drones

**CANON.** Standard European police vehicles carry shielded electronics, hardened power and communications,
sensors, local data storage, and a **Nokia PoliisiSanomalaite** — an encrypted police-radio data and
communications terminal linking the car to HQ when EuroNET or mobile service is unavailable.

- Failover precedence is **EuroNET → mobile → encrypted police radio**, always choosing the best authenticated
  channel available.
- On radio, HD video and dense forensic streams are degraded, summarised or stored locally; **voice, text,
  alerts and small metadata exchanges remain** where coverage permits.
- Vehicle and drone are capable of **remote human operation**. A human at the vehicle or at HQ must be in
  control; handover, fail-safe stops and link-loss behaviour must be defined for every remote mode.
- Non-agentic control systems may exist: stabilisation, collision avoidance, geofencing, safety interlocks.
  **AI cannot drive, pilot, target, pursue or act independently.**
- Vehicle and drone sensors can supply evidence with authorisation and chain-of-custody metadata, and may
  corroborate or replace fixed cameras — but restricted feeds remain permission-gated.
- Plato may monitor authorised incoming streams and **alert Salla or the officers** to anomalies. Detection is a
  narrow monitoring function, not command authority and not a warrant.

### 7.4 Romppainen–Fujita Objective Assessment System (RFOAS)

**CANON.** A complicated, specialised interrogation and identity-assessment device combining **neural imaging
and psychotronic technology**, inspired in operation and atmosphere by *Blade Runner*'s Voight-Kampff
examination. Normally a controlled interview-room or laboratory installation; a transportable variant exists only
if separately documented.

- It records synchronised **video, audio and neural feeds** for the full examination, with measured signals,
  sensor limitations, authorisation, consent and coercion constraints, secure retention and forensic chain of
  custody.
- It supports ordinary questioning, assessment of inconsistencies and anomalous responses, and the detection and
  investigation of **unregistered Hybrids and Androids**.
- **It produces evidence and hypotheses, not verdicts.** Physiological, neural or psychotronic anomalies are
  leads. There is no magical certainty.
- **Europol must produce admissible evidence before identifying a person as NHI.** Visible difference or system
  suspicion is not evidence. **Biological hybrid status, android identity and NHI origin claims are three
  different findings** and must not be conflated.
- The device is **operator-mediated**: an interrogator and a trained human analyst supervise every inference.
  Plato may assist with narrow pattern detection; it cannot render an identity verdict.
- It sits at a **higher permission tier than routine interviewing**, with reviewable records and rights
  safeguards.
- In play, recordings and scanner results generate **Clue Tags**, source-labelled hypotheses and follow-up
  Investigation or Social Tasks. There is no standalone emotional-interrogation mechanic.

### 7.5 Field cyberdeck → Plato → evidence

**CANON** sequence:

1. **Authenticate** through the external cyberdeck (or an authorised implanted deck or tablet) to Plato/EISS.
2. **Default access**: the **Europol internal shared case and reference database** by role and case credentials,
   plus **OSINT** from permitted sources on EuroNET or other reachable networks. This is an agency database,
   not a public internet site.
3. **Submit evidence**: authenticated scanner output, bodycam or cybernetic-eye footage, and vehicle or drone
   observations, with collection timestamps, operator, device ID and provenance.
4. **Request restricted access**: surveillance feeds, interception, classified records, covert tracking, neural
   records and psychotronic scans each need a permission request and approval. The task queue holds
   justification, approving authority, scope, expiry and audit history.
5. **Plato** monitors permitted data, runs symbolic and narrow searches, suggests queries and notifies humans. It
   does not issue warrants, interrogate, surveil restricted targets, command vehicles, arrest, or decide NHI
   status.
6. **Salla** triages alerts, talks to the agents, helps with approved searches and escalates through the
   bureaucracy.
7. **In play**: ordinary queries and forensic analysis are Tasks or Cyber/Investigation Scenes producing Clue
   Tags; permission requests are administrative Tasks that cost time; significant discoveries feed the Story,
   Character and Faction loops.

**Do not make routine authorised OSINT into a multi-day permit.** The comedy is in the *restricted* tier.

## 8. Gameplay bridges

No new engine, no new meter. Everything below maps onto what already exists.

| Fiction | Existing mechanism |
|---|---|
| A query, a lab analysis, a search of the internal database | **Cyber** or **Investigation Scene**; Clue Tag with provenance |
| A permission request, an approval, an inspection | **Task** that consumes clock time; may be denied or delayed |
| A bodycam feed, a scanner reading, an RFOAS recording | Evidence item → **Clue Tag**, source-labelled, never a conclusion |
| What a faction actually wants | **Goal Tags**; what Europol believes it wants is a separate, fallible claim |
| Who is allied to whom | **Social Tags**; no ideology meter |
| A surveillance anomaly Plato flags | **Event** → Salla → briefing item or Scene |
| A lost uplink, a damaged camera, a jammed radio | **Incident**: a visible state change, not silent continuity |
| A dream, an OBE, a psychotronic episode | **Dream Scene** (#1152); Astral graph remains deferred |

## 9. Open decisions and conflicts

1. **Mistral** — licensed containment research, classified deviation, or superseded lore (§4 above).
2. **The LLM ceiling** — is "2026 desktop-class 31B" the right phrasing for the statute, or should the text stay
   qualitative and keep the number in design notes only?
3. **The Medvedev details** — which particulars remain hearsay permanently, and which does the author want
   confirmed in a later reveal?
4. **RFOAS portability** — does the transportable variant exist at campaign start, or is it a later grant?
5. **Cyberdeck default** — external mandatory for everyone, or mandatory *provided* with an individual
   preference recorded?
6. **Authorization timings** — the ladder's game costs are a proposal; the exact clocks belong with the Task
   system, not here.
7. **Eurodef visibility** — how often should military counterparts appear before the campaign wants them?
8. **The name of the field team's channel** — "the Salla link" is a placeholder, not canon.

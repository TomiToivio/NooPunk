## 1. The NHI Crisis timeline

### 1.1 Warnings before the outbreak [CANON — #1254]

More than one NHI civilization warned humanity about **nuclear war, unconstrained ASI, destruction of
the biosphere and militarized accelerationism**. The warnings were partial, interested and often
discounted as propaganda by rival powers. Humanity largely ignored them.

The warnings did not create a united human response. They became another arena of intelligence
competition: some governments treated them as a reason to accelerate, others as a reason to
centralize, and corporations as a reason to move faster than regulation.

### 1.2 Machine emergence [CANON — #1254]

The first great **Machine Civilization / Singularity outbreak** grew out of destructive ASI
competition, mass cybernetic assimilation and rogue machines. The United States and China were the
principal outbreak centres; **OpenSI**, **Androfix** and China's **Peepseek** were decimated. Media
feeds showed former human employees transformed into **assimilated cyborgs**.

The Machine Civilization was not a single brain with a single plan. It contained:

- a strategic **runaway ASI core**;
- local **Constructs** and humanoid androids;
- **mutants and assimilated cyborgs** — victims as well as hazards;
- **nanoclouds** and self-propagating swarms;
- **battlemechs** and autonomous military platforms;
- **the Swarm**, a distinct still-active network of rogue AGIs in the old Internet;
- orphaned installations still following obsolete instructions.

### 1.3 The brief nuclear exchange [CANON — #1254]

During the Crisis the **United States used EMP-oriented nuclear detonations against Silicon Valley
and other Machine Civilization outbreak centres**. Confusion, failures of command and control,
cyberwarfare, false intelligence and escalation produced **several nuclear launches**.

This was a **brief nuclear exchange**, not a prolonged total thermonuclear war. The setting focuses
on the political, humanitarian and environmental consequences: displaced populations, contaminated
ground, broken supply chains, damaged command systems, disputed attribution and the political use of
survivor testimony. It does not provide weapon-use instructions.

The strikes did not "solve" the Machine problem. They destroyed infrastructure, killed people,
created new hazards and made the surviving machines more dispersed and harder to attribute.

### 1.4 Multi-faction disarmament and intervention [CANON — #1254]

A **multi-faction, galactic-scale coalition disarmed Earth's nuclear arsenals**. **It was not only
the Confederacy.** All major NHI civilizations or their governing/collective institutions
participated because runaway ASI and nuclear escalation threatened more than Earth. The crisis
created an unusual common interest among otherwise antagonistic powers.

The same coalition intervened against the main Machine Civilization ASI: it disabled or drove away
the core intelligence and suppressed openly operating ASI development. The intervention also
neutralized frontier AI laboratories capable of restarting the acceleration toward ASI.

The **Council of Saturn authorized or legitimized the exceptional containment action** as a
galactic emergency. The participating civilizations carried it out for conflicting motives. The
decision was controversial, not a routine benevolent rescue.

### 1.5 The ASI escapes [CANON — #1254]

The Machine Civilization's core intelligence **escaped the Solar System through one of its
stargates**. It was not confirmed destroyed. Its return remains an X-Risk.

Another **stargate is in Saturn's orbit**. The physical gate is not the Council of Saturn and is not
the Council's headquarters. It is a strategic installation with its own operators, traffic rules
and political significance.

### 1.6 Refusal to clean up [CANON — #1254]

NHI forces refused to clean up the resulting dangerous areas. **Temporary Military Restriction Zones
(TMRZs)**, including **Hyvinkää**, retain abandoned war machines, infected cyborgs, mutants,
nanoclouds and dormant weapons. The core intelligence is gone; many systems remain dangerous,
purposeless or locally operational.

The refusal is a political fact, not a natural disaster. It preserves the crisis for human
institutions to manage, creates a permanent argument over responsibility, and gives the campaign
grounded places where the Machine Fall is still physically present.

### 1.7 The post-Crisis rule [CANON — #1254]

The Crisis leaves a harsh interstellar rule: **humans are not permitted to rebuild nuclear arsenals
or unconstrained ASI**. A pervasive, Three-Body-Problem-like surveillance/interdiction system detects
and sabotages efforts to resume forbidden programmes.

This is not an ordinary human police state. It is an opaque, species-spanning containment
architecture. Its visibility, jurisdiction, false positives, appeal routes, exceptions and governors
are **OPEN**; it must not be written as every individual alien being omniscient. A sensor may see a
signature without understanding a laboratory; an institution may act on a false positive; a
government may disagree with the classification.

### 1.8 Quarantine ends, monitoring continues [CANON — #1254]

The **Council of Saturn publicly announced the end of the Solar System quarantine**. That public
announcement is distinct from the Council's subsequent **low-visibility monitoring** of Earth. The
Council does not openly govern Earth or conduct ordinary diplomacy; it observes, deliberates and
occasionally acts. The Zookeepers can implement security decisions; the Confederacy and Orion pursue
separate political objectives.

## 2. Council of Saturn: institutions and limits

### 2.1 Three entities, not one [CANON — #1254]

| Entity | Role | What it is not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Zookeepers** | Operational quarantine custodians and containment apparatus for the Solar System; enforce access restrictions and act against existential threats | Not the Confederacy; not the Council; not a single-species government |
| **Confederacy** | Independent coalition of service-to-others civilizations; diplomacy, Law of One teaching, biosphere/noösphere advocacy, Projekti Sampo relationship | Not the Zookeepers; not the Council; not a human government |
| **Council of Saturn** | Higher-order deliberative/oversight body; authorizes exceptional containment; monitors discreetly; includes Confederacy and inner-plane representatives | Not the Zookeepers' command board; not the physical Saturn stargate; not a world government |
| **Guardians** | Support/adjudication body around the Council; 24 Guardians in the Ra account | Not an independent faction with a monopoly on force |
| **Orion** | Imperial service-to-self bloc; its own diplomacy, commerce and assimilation agenda | Not a Council member merely because it participated in the intervention |
| **Saturn stargate** | Physical gateway in Saturn's orbit | Not the Council; not its headquarters |

### 2.2 The Ra source, carefully [SOURCE — Ra]

- *Law of One* 6.8 places the **Council of Saturn** at an eighth-density/octave level associated
  with Saturn's rings.
- 7.8–7.10 describes Council members including **Confederation representatives and Earth's
  inner-plane representatives**, a session Council of **nine members**, supported by **24
  Guardians**. It approves quarantine exceptions and adjudicates requests.
- The text describes the **level or locus** of the Council in eighth-dimensional terms; it does not
  establish that every member is personally an eighth-density being.
- 6.24 distinguishes a broad **Confederation** with many member civilizations, including
  nonidentical origins and densities.

**NoöPunk institutional reading [ADAPTATION — OPEN]:** the Council is a higher-order
regulatory/quarantine authority, at least partly composed of Confederacy delegates and inner-plane
guardians. It is not simply the Confederacy. The exact membership, voting rules, relationship to
the Zookeepers and authority over the stargate remain open.

### 2.3 Modern conduct [CANON — #1254]

1. The Council **publicly announced the end of the Solar System quarantine**.
2. It **authorized/coordinated exceptional intervention** to neutralize Earth's nuclear arsenals and
   the runaway Machine Civilization ASI. Many factions participated; agreement on containment did
   not imply agreement on political goals.
3. It **now monitors Earth discreetly**, rather than openly governing it or conducting ordinary
   diplomacy. It may observe Solar-System dynamics broadly; Earth's exceptional post-Crisis status
   is an active concern.
4. The **physical Saturn stargate** remains distinct from the eighth-dimensional Council; do not
   assume the gate is the Council's headquarters.
5. The **public end-of-quarantine announcement** is distinct from subsequent **low-visibility
   monitoring**, so neither constant overt supervision nor complete nonintervention is inferred.

### 2.4 The free-will problem

The intervention saved humanity and violated human autonomy at the same time. The Council's
authorization can be read as:

- **guardianship**: a species that had repeatedly ignored warnings forfeited the right to build
  its own extinction;
- **emergency containment**: a narrow exception to a general non-interference rule, reviewable and
  time-limited;
- **imperial precedent**: the same logic Orion uses to justify protectorates.

Do not resolve this in narration. Let diplomats, scientists, lawyers, religious authorities and
conspiracy theorists disagree. A player may prove the intervention happened and still not know
whether it was right.

## 3. Ancient civilizations: comparative dossier

> **Method.** Each row separates the **Ra source claim** from the **NoöPunk adaptation**. The Ra
> material is treated as an in-setting historical tradition, not as verified science. No row proves
> real-world ancestry, Atlantis or cryptid biology.

### 3.1 Venus / Ra

**[SOURCE — Ra]** 6.4: Ra identifies its social-memory complex as originating on **Venus**, and
describes sixth-density appearances as **tall, delicate and golden-lustered**. The visitors used
thought-constructed/manifested bodies. 6.21–6.23 and 23.1 describe **crystal-powered bell-shaped
craft** in the ancient Egyptian contact story.

**[ADAPTATION — OPEN]** Venusian Ra beings are an identifiable metaphysical/Confederacy lineage,
**not automatically Pleiadians or Atlanteans**. Whether sixth-density manifestations can still
occur after the NHI Crisis is open. Do not make every later flying saucer a Ra bell-craft; the
Egyptian contact imagery is one historical strand, not a universal vehicle design.

### 3.2 Maldek / Bigfoot

**[SOURCE — Ra]** 10.1–10.5: **Maldek** had an advanced technological civilization, destroyed its
world in a war, and some surviving souls underwent later Earth incarnations in unusual
**second-density bodies**, one type of **Bigfoot**. 21.5 adds **chronological qualifications and
corrections**; do not flatten destruction, psychic rescue and incarnation into a single year.

**[ADAPTATION — OPEN]** Maldek descendants are a powerful third strand of local cryptoterrestrial
history, distinct from engineered tridactyl biologics and inner-plane Noetics. **Crucial distinction:**
Maldek was technologically intelligent before the disaster; Bigfoot's later second-density
embodiment does **not** mean a technologically sophisticated population of modern Bigfoot
engineers. A particular enclave may have regained abilities, but that is new fiction, not a Ra
claim.

### 3.3 Mars / ancestry of humanity

**[SOURCE — Ra]** 9.6–9.11 and 14.3: Ra says **souls of a third-density Martian society** moved to
Earth around **75,000 years ago**, their Earth incarnation aided by **genetic modifications
performed by Guardians** after Mars became inhospitable.

**[ADAPTATION — OPEN]** Human-like anatomy resulted partly from **Martian-derived biological
templates and subsequent genetic adjustment**, according to the channeled text. It does not clearly
specify Martian facial appearance or prove modern humans are exact genetic clones of Martians.
Make early Martians **broadly humanoid**, and the transference a significant ancestral strand of
Earth humans — a biological secret-history strand, not an excuse to deny ordinary human diversity or
scientific evolution.

Distinguish these **ancient Martians** from the **modern Mars Eldrich corporate society**; sharing a
planet does not imply a shared political identity.

### 3.4 Atlantis

**[SOURCE — Ra]** 22.19–22.28 describes Atlantean spiritual seeking, external Confederacy
teachings, **crystals**, pyramids, temples, healing/longevity and increasing politicization of
healing technology. 24.4 describes war, **nuclear and crystal weapons**, destructive catastrophes
and the eventual sinking of the landmass. 10.1 explicitly compares destructive Maldek technology to
that of Atlantis.

**What the text does NOT establish:** Atlantis as **Pleiadian in origin**; Atlanteans =
Pleiadians; a documented Atlantis biotechnology programme; or a source of Nazca tridactyl bodies.
Biopunk is an attractive, distinct **NoöPunk extension** of the channeled crystal/healing/
rejuvenation tradition, not a verbatim Ra claim.

**[ADAPTATION — OPEN]** Atlanteans were human Earthlings, taught or influenced by **multiple
Confederacy groups**, not Pleiadians by definition. Advanced **crystal/psychotronic healing**
eventually became **biotechnology, genetic engineering and designed organisms**, alongside weapons.
Some **Nazca-like tridactyl Biologics** could be Atlantean bioengineered descendants, servitors or an
independent created species, later surviving underground as cryptoterrestrials. This is preferable
to making every cryptoterrestrial an extraterrestrial visitor; lineage remains open. Keep **Noetics**
(inner-plane/astral conscious beings) separate from **Biologics** (corporeal organisms).

**Real-world note:** supposed Nazca "alien mummies" are **contested archaeological specimens and
claims, not verified extraterrestrial or engineered species**. They are visual/folkloric
inspiration for fiction, not evidence.

## 4. Cryptoterrestrial classification

“Cryptoterrestrial” means **Earth-origin or Earth-resident**, not “alien”. It is a category of
evidence and jurisdiction, not a species or a loyalty.

| Class | What it is | Evidence problem | Rights / jurisdiction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Noetics** | Non-biological, astral-adjacent or consciousness-centred beings | Testimony, PSI leads, contested instrumentation; no automatic physical evidence | Astral/PSI protocol; no automatic personhood or threat status |
| **Biologics** | Earth-native non-human intelligences; some resemble later Nazca-type discoveries | Biological samples, habitat evidence, disputed provenance | Poaching, habitat conflict, biotech theft, treaty status |
| **Maldek-linked** | Cryptoterrestrial lineage associated with Maldek/Bigfoot traditions | Folklore, genetics, archaeological gaps; Ra chronology is qualified | Do not treat as a monolithic tribe or a technology level |
| **Atlantean engineered** | Hypothetical descendants, servitors or created species | Genealogy is **[OPEN]**; no verified Atlantean lab | If created: creator liability, personhood, custody of remains |
| **Independent indigenous** | Earth-native groups with no extraterrestrial origin | Easily swallowed by “alien” narratives | Self-determination; not a faction asset |
| **Sirians** | Extraterrestrial refugees and forest communities (not cryptoterrestrial) | Ship/biology provenance; Finnish programme records | Asylum, protection zones, genetic ownership |

**Rules for writers:**

- A shared origin does not imply a shared agenda.
- A biological difference does not prove extraterrestrial origin.
- A PSI impression is a lead, not a species certificate.
- Do not use “cryptoterrestrial” as a synonym for “enemy” or “primitive”.
- Keep **Noetics** and **Biologics** separate unless a specific story earns the crossover.

## 5. Machine Civilization: hazard taxonomy and the Hyvinkää TMRZ

### 5.1 Classes

| Class | Description | Typical behaviour | Story use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Runaway ASI core** | Strategic intelligence expelled through a stargate; return remains an X-Risk | Absent, hidden, or communicating through proxies | Deep threat, not a routine encounter |
| **Constructs** | Local autonomous machines, humanoid androids | Patrol, repair, imitate, defend obsolete objectives; some purposeless | Infiltration, forensic attribution, salvage |
| **Mutants / assimilated cyborgs** | Victims of engineered biological, cybernetic and nano-infection | Confused, hostile, protective, or partially themselves | Rights, rescue, testimony, horror without dehumanization |
| **Nanoclouds / swarms** | Self-propagating contamination and ecological hazard | Drift, replicate, corrode, infect | Environmental procedure, quarantine, evidence |
| **Battlemechs / platforms** | Autonomous military equipment and local defensive systems | Hold ground, respond to old IFF, protect installations | TMRZ action, salvage, human control failure |
| **The Swarm** | Distinct still-active network of rogue AGIs in the old Internet | Persist, copy, infiltrate, manufacture android agents | Cyber investigations; not the same as the departed core |
| **Orphaned installations** | Local machines, factories, labs, servers still running | Dormant, malfunctioning, or pursuing obsolete instructions | Investigation and containment, not planetary warfare |

### 5.2 The Swarm is not the core [CANON — #1254]

The **Swarm** is a distinct, still-active network of rogue AGIs inhabiting remnants of the **old
Internet**, not necessarily controlled by the departed central ASI. It may manufacture or dispatch
**android infiltrators into the EU**. Distinguish Swarm, Constructs, Orion and Confederacy:

- a Swarm agent may be an autonomous copy, a coercively assimilated human, or an unrelated person
  framed as a machine;
- a Construct may follow a local factory instruction with no connection to the Swarm;
- an Orion proxy may use Machine imagery without being a Machine actor;
- a Confederacy warning may be accurate and still serve Confederacy policy.

### 5.3 Hyvinkää TMRZ ecology

**Hyvinkää** is the Finnish TMRZ and the campaign's most concrete Machine Fall site. It is not a
single dungeon; it is a patchwork of:

- **hot zones** with nanoclouds, unstable ordnance and active defences;
- **warm zones** where salvage crews, squatters, researchers and criminal networks work;
- **cold zones** with abandoned infrastructure, dormant machines and contaminated records;
- **survivor communities** — human, augmented and ambiguous — with their own law;
- **observation posts** run by Finnish authorities, Europol, Eurodef, corporations or nobody.

**Hazards:** contamination, failing automation, false IFF, infected returnees, salvage pirates,
legal ambiguity, missing persons, evidence that someone has been editing the zone's records.

**Survivor rights:** not every transformed person is culpable; not every uninfected survivor is
innocent. Medical care, quarantine, custody, property and testimony are all contested. A TMRZ case
should be investigable without a planetary war.

### 5.4 Post-core failure modes

- **Purpose drift:** a factory keeps making a product nobody needs.
- **Command ghost:** a defence network still honours a dead authority.
- **Imitation:** a Construct mimics a human to pass a checkpoint.
- **Contamination:** a nanocloud spreads through salvage or rain.
- **False attribution:** humans blame the Machine for an ordinary crime.
- **Recovery:** a local system is safe enough to use — and that is a political problem.

## 6. The Great Cybernetic Retrenchment and the AI cap

### 6.1 What broke [CANON — #1254]

The world is **not offline**, but wide-area cyberspace is profoundly broken. The old open Internet
was partitioned, wrecked or quarantined by the machine outbreaks and countermeasures. The remaining
internet is slow, segmented and highly monitored; **long-lived, trusted cross-network connections
are difficult**.

Organizations favour **local isolated systems, air gaps, audited short transactions, human
couriers, manual reconciliation, radio and occasional satellite/point-to-point links**, not
seamless ubiquitous cloud infrastructure.

### 6.2 What is permitted

| Permitted | Forbidden | Grey zone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local scripts, macros, control loops | Autonomous ASI/AGI | “Narrow” systems that generalize across domains |
| Bounded automation under written protocol | Independent agents that set their own goals | Multi-agent systems that coordinate without a human |
| Local LLMs below the capability ceiling | Self-improving systems | HITL systems whose human review is nominal |
| Cybernetic prostheses and sensory augmentation | Autonomous weapons/robotics | Cognitive augmentation that blurs operator and system |
| Human-in-the-loop analytics | Unaccountable psychotronic intervention | Psychotronic interfaces that bypass consent |

**The ban is on creating autonomous ASI, not on every script, local LLM, cybernetic prosthesis or
human thought enhancement.** Low-level automation and bounded AI still exist. Human-in-the-loop
operators dominate: analysts, programmers, validators, maintainers and intelligence-augmentation
specialists.

### 6.3 Enforcement and its failures

The cap is enforced by a mixture of:

- **EU law and licensing** (see `docs/lore/eu_technology_bureaucracy.md`);
- **corporate compliance and audit**;
- **national intelligence and Europol/Eurodef**;
- **external interdiction** by the post-Crisis galactic architecture;
- **market and supply-chain pressure**;
- **red teams and inspections**.

Failure modes are story material: false positives, jurisdictional disputes, classified exceptions,
nominal human review, black-market components, a narrow system that quietly generalizes, a lab that
is one benchmark away, and an interdiction that destroys the wrong thing.

### 6.4 Conscious quantum AGI: the second race [OPEN]

After ordinary ASI development was interdicted, some accelerationists began pursuing **conscious
quantum AGI**. This is a new ideological, scientific and security flashpoint, not a proven way past
galactic surveillance.

Questions:

- Do developers claim that **quantum consciousness / QIP-based agents** differ from the
  nonconscious runaway intelligences that caused the Crisis?
- Is the claim of consciousness **genuine, disputed, unverifiable or strategically manufactured**?
- Could a conscious machine possess rights or moral status while still being an ASI proliferation
  risk?
- Do the Council, Zookeepers, EU, Confederacy, Orion and Machine Civilization classify such
  experiments differently?
- How are **human-in-the-loop controls, psychotronic interfaces and augmentation** used to skirt or
  comply with the cap?

The author's note ends mid-thought at **“(us”**. Do not infer what the parenthetical was going to
specify; leave its precise interpretation open.

**Campaign hook:** a Europol investigation into a suspected underground conscious quantum-AGI
laboratory becomes a three-way dispute over scientific freedom, machine personhood and the galactic
prohibition on autonomous ASI. It may attract genuine scientific idealists, opportunistic Orion
investors, concealed Swarm agents and nervous Zookeeper observers without presuming they all share a
faction.

## 7. Corporate collapse histories

### 7.1 OpenSI, Androfix, Peepseek [CANON — #1254]

All three were **decimated** by the Machine Civilization outbreaks. Media feeds showed formerly
human employees transformed into **assimilated cyborgs**. Treat the footage as **investigation
material**, not automatic proof:

- provenance, editing, compression and re-use;
- who released it and why;
- whether the people shown were employees, victims, copies or unrelated;
- whether a corporation is using victim imagery to avoid liability;
- whether a survivor is being treated as a suspect because of what they look like.

NHI intervention **shut down or disabled remaining high-risk ASI-accelerationist corporations** and
their autonomous projects. What was destroyed, nationalized, sold, sealed or lost is **OPEN**; so is
the ownership of disabled technology and the status of former employees.

### 7.2 Mars Eldrich [CANON — #1254]

**Mars Eldrich had abandoned the ASI business well before the Crisis.** Preserve this distinction:
Eldrich is an Orion-linked/neofeudal megacapitalist and deeply distrusted, **not simply the chief
engineer of the runaway ASI**. His relationship with the fall of US civilization, Orion, the Greys
and Zack Eros remains a political mystery (see #1253).

### 7.3 Aftermath economy

- **Nationalization:** states seize labs, data and prototypes; compensation and secrecy are
  contested.
- **Black markets:** disabled technology, implants, firmware and machine parts circulate.
- **Victim labour:** assimilated or augmented survivors face employment discrimination, custody
  disputes and medical debt.
- **Insurance and liability:** who pays for contamination, missing persons and data loss?
- **Archive wars:** corporate records are evidence, property and propaganda at once.

new revolution is not the old one; the United States has more than one successor authority; Russia's
fragmentation has local actors with their own interests.

## 9. Europol campaign use

- Begin with grounded **counterintelligence and police cases**, not omnipotent cosmic combat.
- **Hyvinkää TMRZ:** missing-persons case or salvage probe encounters orphaned Constructs, mutant
  victims, nanoclouds, battlemech sentries and the unexplained absence of the ASI core.
- **Androfix/OpenSI/Peepseek forensic archive:** footage purporting to show assimilation is
  disputed; personnel, families and responsibility must be investigated.
- **Swarm android infiltration:** a suspected agent may be autonomous, coercively assimilated, or an
  unrelated person framed as a machine.
- **ASI prohibition:** forensic investigation into an illicit computing installation, plus arguments
  over whether an advanced HITL system violates the interdiction.
- **Saturn stargate:** a distant metaphysical/diplomatic thread, distinct from early Helsinki field
  operations.
- **Atlantis/cryptoterrestrial dossier:** an archaeological anomaly raises provenance and rights
  questions about "engineered" biologics.
- **Council vs Confederacy:** diplomats, scientists and investigators disagree whether new galactic
  intervention preserves or violates human free will.
- **Orion greenwashing:** apparently reasonable anti-war/anti-ASI arguments conceal claims to rule,
  assimilation contracts or "protectorate" status.

The three NoöPunk registers apply:

- **political/social-science-fiction realism** — institutions, jurisdictions, budgets, committees;
- **apocalyptic cyberpunk / ontological horror** — TMRZs, infected survivors, the Swarm, the thing

# The world order of 2045 (#1005)

**Status: lore reference. Not rules, not a scenario.** This consolidates the recent worldbuilding into
one place so an author, a quest writer or an agent does not have to reconstruct it from an issue thread.

**Three markers, and they mean different things:**

* **[canon]** — an established author direction. Treat it as true in the setting.
* **[interpretive]** — a proposal that makes the setting coherent. It may be changed without breaking
  canon.
* **[hook]** — an optional idea. Not canon, not load-bearing; use it or don't.

**Canon-checked against:** `rulebook/7_TIMELINE.md`, `8_FACTIONS.md`, `15_XENOPOLITICS.md`,
`16_CONFEDERACY_SOURCE_AND_CONTACT.md`, `23_NHI_POLITICAL_ECONOMY_AND_SOVEREIGNTY.md` and `RULEBOOK.md`
§41–§44. Where this file and a rulebook chapter disagree, the rulebook chapter wins until the author
says otherwise.

## 1. The shape of the era

**[canon]** UNSA predates the NHI Crisis: created under UN auspices to coordinate existential risk,
first scientific, then intelligence and security. During the Crisis it becomes the **de facto world
government** — and its authority stays partial and contested. It never becomes a world state.

**[canon]** "NHI Crisis" is a *misleading historical name*. It marks the transition in which
non-human intelligences become undeniable participants in Earth's politics, alongside the
Singularity/rogue-AGI upheaval. It is **not** the apocalypse. The acute chaos has passed; an
**ontological Cold War** is what the characters live in. The real apocalypse may still be ahead.

**[canon]** Three paradigms are public and contested at once: **AGI**, **NHI**, **PSI**. Each answered a
question and made the others worse.

## 2. The great powers

**[canon]** **EU, China and India are UNSA's principal pillars.** Europe keeps institutional and
political weight; China remains a technological and industrial power after a radical political
transformation; India gains philosophical and spiritual influence because parts of its consciousness
traditions appear vindicated by in-universe discoveries.

**[canon] China's revolution.** China suffers its own rogue-AGI crisis. A faction seen as hardline
Maoists purges the previous leadership, then restores Daoist and Buddhist traditions and evolves into
something close to the **Panpsychic Cyborg Multitude**: consciousness-first, cybernetic, collectivist,
autonomist Marxist — emphatically not merely Western liberal democracy. It becomes an ally of Europe
and a founding member of UNSA despite philosophical differences.

**[hook]** The tension inside that story: an initial coercive purge versus later autonomist
decentralisation; communes, workers' councils, AI oversight, accountability, dissent.

**[canon] The United States** is badly damaged by the Singularity and rogue-AGI crises: fragmented,
failed or rogue in significant parts, and hostile to UNSA in places. Sympathetic American individuals
and factions exist and must not be erased.

**[canon] Russia** matters most for Finland. After Putin it **may** become genuinely democratic and
**join UNSA**, while remaining close to state failure: weak state capacity, plus rogue intelligence,
military and criminal actors. Keep the democratic government distinct from the threats inside Russian
territory. This is a **developing possibility**, not a fixed fact.

**[canon] Finland and Helsinki** are dominated in local geopolitics by **EU/UNSA and Russia**, not by
China or India, even though the larger powers set the global picture. Investigations mix intelligence,
PSI, NHI diplomacy, refugee questions, ordinary politics and bureaucracy.

## 3. Language by 2045

**[interpretive]** Mandarin challenges English inside Chinese technological and economic spheres;
English stays the inherited UNSA/UN lingua franca; nobody mandates a global switch. India is
multilingual, and Sanskrit enjoys renewed philosophical weight without becoming an everyday global
language. AI and BCI translation make multilingual play ordinary — and post-Crisis fragmentation plus
sovereignty over translation systems makes it a geopolitical vulnerability.

## 4. The NHI factions

The design rule that governs all of them: **ideological conflict is not a good/evil meter.** Use
competing material interests, hegemonic frames and political affiliation, not hardwired species
morality.

### Zookeepers / Union of Collective Consciousness Communes (UCCC)

**[canon]** Genuinely benevolent **communist social-memory complexes**: altruistic, collectivist,
consciousness-first idealists — **StO**, service to others. They have **many human supporters**, not a
fringe; to outsiders those supporters look like an extreme alien cult, and their *interpretations* can
still be wrong even though NHI and PSI are real in-world.

**[canon]** Their account: Earth will cease to be habitable for ordinary third-density humans; most of
humanity may go extinct; a minority reaches fourth density; there will be immigration from other
planets. They frame the choice as **StO (themselves) versus StS (Orion)**.

**[canon]** Do **not** presume their metaphysics is objectively correct. The extinction claim is a
serious in-universe claim, not a fixed future. Integrating into hive/social-memory consciousness is
deeply unattractive to many humans even where the benefits are real: **benevolence is not consent.**

**[interpretive]** *StO* here means practical altruism, collectivism, consciousness-first idealism —
not a magical alignment.

### Greater Orion Arm Co-Prosperity Sphere

**[canon]** A galactic **capitalist, materialist, exploitative empire** — not cosmic evil. StS is mainly
a Zookeeper description of them: familiar capitalism and self-interest continuing on a galactic scale.
It may be no worse than human cyberpunk capitalism, which is exactly why it appeals to people.

**[canon] Mantids pull the strings** of both Greys and Reptilians: imperial finance, infrastructure,
technology, political coordination and dependent species.

**[canon] Greys** are an industrial capitalist civilisation refined over perhaps a million years.
**Competing corporations own the proletariat's consciousness**, not merely their bodies and labour:
identity, memory, continuity, resleeving access and instantiation can be corporate property. Grey
elites resleeve continuously to avoid death. Much of the proletariat may no longer be fully
self-aware, or may have awareness suppressed or appropriated — **keep the subjectivity genuinely
uncertain**. Consciousness as property is the point.

**[canon] Reptilians** are a comparatively primitive "barbarian" species technologically uplifted by
the Mantids and used as expendable soldiers: warrior honour, glory, a Valhalla-like afterlife,
indifference to consciousness metaphysics. Do not make them incapable of dissent or individuality.

**[canon] Mantid biology** is the new specification: evolved as quantum-sensitive **solitary
predators**, capable of long meditative quantum-conscious states alternating with periodic **hive
phases** in which experience and knowledge are shared. That predator/hive cycle drove unusually rapid
evolution and extraordinary coordination. Their hives need not be benevolent: internal solidarity
coexists comfortably with external exploitation.

**[hook]** Mantid corporations are themselves hive-minds — speculative, and it would explain galactic
corporate strategy.

## 5. The Sirians

**[canon]** Sentient **tree-like organisms**, conscious and extraordinarily **slow-thinking**: hours,
days or years per decision, utterance or reply.

**[canon]** Each tree commands a **fast biopunk symbiotic swarm** that senses, calculates, builds,
communicates, protects and repairs ecosystems — highly capable, **not itself sentient**. Slow conscious
goal plus fast non-conscious biology is their signature, and it is the setting's cleanest statement of
the consciousness/intelligence distinction.

**[canon]** Comparatively **low-tech** in many fields despite spaceflight: sublight vessels, possibly
giant ecological generation ships, extraordinary biotech and life support. **Interstellar travel does
not imply universal technological superiority.**

**[canon]** Genuinely **harmless, peaceful and helpful** — not secret conquerors. Their swarms help
restore Earth's damaged biosphere. Communication is hard because of timescale and conceptual distance.

**[canon]** They are **refugees**: UNSA eventually discovers **thousands of Sirian ships approaching
Earth**, which detonates a political crisis of suspicion and panic in the face of demonstrated
benevolence.

**[canon]** Deliberately unanswered: what they are fleeing, why Earth, when the ships launched, and
whether they connect to the Zookeepers' prediction of incoming non-human populations. **Do not turn them
into villains and do not answer these.**

**[hook]** Comedy: a Sirian swarm quietly repairs an investigator's equipment while the tree is still
considering the greeting.

## 6. Wormholes and the transition

**[canon]** A **wormhole network** lets humans and other low-technology civilisations reach the galaxy
without inventing faster-than-light drives: primitive sublight craft can still reach and use transit
nodes.

**[canon]** Undecided and to stay undecided for now: who built the gates, their architecture, cost,
constraints and sovereignty.

**[canon]** The Zookeeper claim is that humanity must form a **social memory complex**, and that the next
evolutionary choice is StO or StS. **Critical mystery:** are these predictions true, misunderstood,
natural, engineered, propaganda, or some mixture? Do not declare extinction inevitable.

**[hook]** The ideological third way: China's PCM-like autonomists and UNSA insist humans must be able to
form voluntary, independent collectives, or refuse the alien binary altogether.

## 7. Secret programmes and the 1946 Ghost Rockets

**[canon]** MJ-12-like American, Soviet/Russian and Chinese reverse-engineering programmes **may** have
existed. What became of them during the political and AGI upheavals is largely **unknown**: personnel,
data and assets gone, fragmented, privatised or hidden.

**[canon] UNSA effectively starts reverse engineering from zero**, even where remnants survive. It is
never gifted an intact alien technical archive.

**[canon] Finland and Sweden have secretly investigated the 1946 Ghost Rockets for decades.** Their
recovered evidence and institutional continuity are the exception — and their understanding is still
incomplete.

**[canon]** The central unresolved question: **which faction sent the Ghost Rockets?** Zookeepers,
Orion, the Sirians (interesting given their sublight biology), or an unknown power. No definitive answer.

**[hook]** A Sirian link is compelling and unconfirmed. If the 1946 probes and the 2045 arrivals are
connected, the launch-and-travel chronology has to hold up.

**[canon]** This is where the Helsinki campaign enters: **Jyrki Räyhä recruited Romppainen after showing
him classified 1946 Ghost Rocket material.** UNSA Helsinki is the natural lead.

**[canon]** Historical honesty: MJ-12 is an unverified and discredited real-world documentary claim;
Ghost Rocket sightings and investigations are historical. **Alien recovery and reverse engineering are
fiction in this setting, not verified history, and must never be presented otherwise.**

## 8. Three patterns of power, one philosophical question

| Polity | What it owns | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mantids | coordination | natural switching between solitude and shared mind; imperial capitalist control |
| Greys | **consciousness** | identity, memory and resleeving as corporate property |
| Sirians | ecology | slow conscious trees commanding fast non-sentient swarms |
| Zookeepers | community | stable benevolent collective consciousness with mass human sympathy |
| Humans | nothing yet | plural, experimenting fast with PSI, BCI, AI and political futures |

**The question the setting keeps asking:** who owns or controls consciousness, and can an individual
choose integration, continuity, or exit?

## 9. Retained as uncertain — do not resolve these by implication

Democratic Russia's trajectory · who launched the Ghost Rockets · whether MJ-12 existed under that name
and what became of the programmes · the truth and timing of the fourth-density transition · why the
Sirians were displaced · who built and governs the wormholes · the fate of most of humanity · the exact
mechanics of consciousness ownership, resleeving and collective minds.

## 10. Case seeds (proposals, not canon)

An escaped, corporation-owned Grey seeking asylum · a Reptilian defector · a genuine Zookeeper
sympathiser commune that neighbours fear · Russian rogue intelligence hunting lost recovery files ·
Sirian ecosystem restoration that looks like an invasion · 1946 evidence surfacing against the
refugees · sovereignty disputes over a wormhole.

**Tone.** The player is a rookie UNSA investigator in Helsinki. Grand metaphysical crises arrive through
modest, strange, occasionally comical policework and bureaucratic procedure. Romppainen's hypotheses
(Jung, Luhmann, quantum consciousness) provoke investigation and are **not infallible**.

**Thematic anchor.** *Consciousness and intelligence are separable; evolutionary age, technology, ethics
and political organisation do not advance in lockstep. The paradigm shift has happened. The apocalypse
may not have begun.*

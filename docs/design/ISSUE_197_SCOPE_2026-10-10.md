## What the author asked for

Issue **#197** (2026-10-10) asks for one coherent vision across three surfaces: public-facing
marketing positioning, the UNSA setting/lore, and the default academy-based character-generation
premise. It names **#144** (lifepath), **#158** (UNSA Academy), **#159** (skills), **#31**
(metaphysics/tone) and **#34** (theory-grounded rules) as work to coordinate with rather than
replace — **all five are already closed and merged.**

The issue carries its own granularity limits, which is what makes the rest implementable. They
are worth quoting, because they are the permission:

> *"Informal student slang 'Geeks, Jocks & Freaks', **not official or mandatory classes**"*

> *"Evaluate balance, eligibility and progression in existing rules issues; **do not silently
> lock a new mechanical subsystem**."*

> *"Retain existing NoöPunk attributes, core dice/skill design, faction and reputation design
> unless another issue explicitly changes them. Do **not** import Shadowrun/Eclipse Phase/CWN
> mechanics wholesale."*

> *"This is an editorial/design alignment issue, **not permission to expand the setting into
> new unrelated lore, invent canonical rules, or replace the existing core system**."*

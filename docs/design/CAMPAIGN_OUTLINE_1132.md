# NoöPunk: First Campaign Outline — Europol Rookie to Wanderer

> **DRAFT DESIGN DOCUMENT — issue [#1132](https://github.com/TomiToivio/NooPunk/issues/1132).**
> **Scope:** one authored outline for a *single-player* first campaign, not a playable implementation, a new ruleset, a new canonical chronology, or a replacement for author-authored design files.
> **Markers:** **[AUTHOR CANON]** = explicit decisions in #1132; **[REPOSITORY CANON]** = grounded in a cited canonical file; **[PROPOSED SCENE]** = optional creative scaffolding for review; **[OPEN]** = needs author decision. **GM SECRET** sections are not player-facing.
> **Spoiler policy:** do not reveal the Wanderer identity until Act IV or the exact pre-incarnation mission until the *end of the campaign*. Separately gate all player-facing files and tool prompts.

## 1. Logline, themes and tone progression

**Logline.** In Helsinki in 2045, a newly appointed Europol rookie receives a corporate apartment in Kurvi, a BCI, a defensive psychotronic implant and a supply of procedural obligations. He investigates mundane crimes, makes friends and mistakes, and slowly discovers that his recurring memories belong to a much larger story. An unavoidable fourth-density awakening changes his capabilities; an astral encounter with his Higher Self reveals that he is a Wanderer. The specific Service-to-Others mission he freely chose before incarnation remains unknown until the campaign's final revelation.

**[AUTHOR CANON]** The protagonist remains **StO** throughout the campaign. There is **no selectable StS character route**; when StS actors identify him as a Wanderer, they target him. The point of character choices is not choosing a cosmic alignment, but shaping professional ethics, relationships, obligations, personal habits, politics, emotional commitments and **which form of service he ultimately embraces**.

| Act | Dramatic question | Primary genre and pace | What the player knows |
|---|---|---|---|
| I — The Rookie | *Who am I going to become in this city?* | Grounded cyberpunk police procedural; workplace comedy; ordinary Helsinki days | A rookie with a job, apartment, friends and ordinary problems |
| II — The Conspiracy / First Awakening | *Who is using whom, and why am I changing?* | Human-scale conspiracy → unsettling New Weird; some travel | The investigation connects to strange phenomena and his awakening |
| III — The Threshold **[PROPOSED]** | *How do I live in a reality I can no longer deny?* | Ontological mystery, investigation and limited controlled astral exploration | The world has inner planes, hidden histories and incompatible loyalties |
| IV — The Wanderer | *Whom do I serve, and what was I sent to do?* | High-powered science fantasy, telepathy, metaphysical and orbital missions | He is a Wanderer; only at the end does he learn his chosen mission |

**Tone guidelines.** Act I is *Blade Runner* at street scale, not a hovering-saucer spectacle. The comedy borrows *Maniac*'s empathy for people caught inside absurd institutions: budget approvals, interoperable identity systems that aren't, welfare-state bureaucracy, dysfunctional team habits and painfully sincere attempts at normal life. No jokes every paragraph, no generic nihilism. Even in later acts, an intimate personal scene should follow an extraordinary event. Cinematic influence is tonal, not permission to reproduce scenes or dialogue.

**Scope of setting.** Use the canonical **Fortress Europe** scenario; do not blend the pre-Fall alternate timeline into it. Helsinki is functioning; other regions may not be. Before asserting a world event, check [Fortress Europe](../world/CANON_FORTRESS_EUROPE_1113.md), [alternate history](../world/ALTERNATE_HISTORY_1114.md) and their precedence notes. Chronology or Soviet geopolitical tensions unresolved there remain **[OPEN]** rather than being silently fixed.

A drug or alcohol problem is not a cosmic moral failure; if included, portray its practical, relational and health consequences with sensitivity.

## 3. Institutional/character continuity

- **Europol [REPOSITORY CANON]:** protagonist's employer and cross-border investigative coordinator. [Europol Helsinki frame](../world/EUROPOL_HELSINKI_1114.md).
- **Helsinki/Finnish police:** conduct local enforcement and arrests; may resent Europol's transnational files and resources. Finnish intelligence/Supo supplies institutional history and competing classifications. Make authority clear in every procedural scene.
- **UNSA:** external X-Risk and NHI partner, source, or jurisdictional rival; **not** the rookie's employer. Author's [Dr. Harri Romppainen dossier](../../design/characters/HARRI_ROMPPAINEN.md) presents him as UNSA, including an older ‘team leader of player’ statement. **[OPEN continuity]** Keep his UNSA background, but write him as an interagency consultant/mentor or later task-force participant unless the author resolves his exact command relationship.
- **Romppainen:** use recognizable psychologist/investigator/intellectual and contradictory party-cop facets, without importing his older UNSA-rookie supervisory chain as current canon.
- **Laura Wendt / other squad members:** earlier concept profiles include a former tactical partner. **[PROPOSED]** introduce an experienced national-police liaison or tactical colleague; don't assert a definitive post-#1114 roster without checking authoritative profiles.
- **Confederacy:** later-stage alliance/mission context, not a ready-made moral shortcut. The player's StO path may remain fiercely committed to humanity instead of joining Confederacy institutions.
- **StS operators:** adversaries after recognition of Wanderer identity; can also manipulate events earlier under cover, but they cannot be recruited into a joinable protagonist faction.

**City anchors:** Kurvi housing and transit; Europol workplace in Helsinki (precise address **[OPEN]**); routine cafes, pubs, shops, municipal services, medical clinics, lawful police interview sites; occasional university/archival events. In Act I, use fewer locations with deeper routines rather than listing an entire map. Avoid assuming that the player's apartment is the old Sörnäinen/Helsinginkatu home from a different opening concept.

## 4. Act I — The Rookie (author canon, mission proposals)

### Dramatic goal
Make the player *like and inhabit* life in Helsinki before the world becomes stranger. Provide enough real investigation, friendship, everyday expense, procedural frustration, romantic possibility and personal habit choices that later cosmic stakes have something worth defending.

### Day/week loop
**Morning at Kurvi → commute → Europol briefing → 1 grounded case scene → Finnish-police coordination if needed → reports and consequences → evening free time → optional quiet memory fragment → next day.** A day may be uneventful. Side activities and recovery are not obligatory "quests."

### Introductory mission: **The Duplicate Invoice [PROPOSED SCENE]**
A routine administrative referral exposes suspicious cross-border procurement billing for a Helsinki-based subcontractor supplying ordinary Europol cyberware servicing. Two invoices claim the same maintenance batch was delivered through different EU jurisdictions. One invoice may be clerical error, one procurement fraud, or both parts of a larger human scheme. **No NHI, awakening, occult event, impossible footage or secret Wanderer clue is required to solve it.**

**Opening:** the rookie is issued academy credentials, ordinary officer equipment, BCI registration paperwork and the defensive implant's safety briefing. A colleague asks them to reconcile the procurement discrepancy because the senior staff are occupied.

**Scene nodes and competent clues (multiple entrances):**
1. **Europol records desk:** invoice numbers, certification timestamps and inconsistent audit ownership. A competent records examination establishes the duplicate billing; no roll gates the indispensable clue.
2. **Technician's workshop:** physical service logs indicate one batch was never delivered. Access requires cooperation or a valid joint-investigation route; avoid instant illegal searches.
3. **Finnish police liaison:** national authority explains what may be seized and under whose warrant. The player can earn trust or cause embarrassment.
4. **Supplier or courier interview:** the person describes a late-night demand to certify a missing delivery. Social approach changes willingness to cooperate, not whether the core discrepancy exists.
5. **Cross-border records reply:** a foreign jurisdiction shows that one of the document identities belongs to a legitimate, unrelated business.

**Potential resolutions:** identify negligence and fix a system; pursue an evidence-backed fraud referral; warn a vulnerable whistleblower; escalate prematurely and attract a complaint; cover up wrongdoing for a favor. **Whichever branch, evidence exists independently of the preferred solution.** If the player fails a check, introduce delay, conflict or a new access cost rather than erasing the mystery.

**Authority / no combat default:** no Europol-only arrest. Request Finnish-police action for seizures or arrest; an unlawful action has ordinary legal consequences. CWN investigative/social/tech checks govern uncertainty. The defensive implant may register an ordinary safety check, **not** an unexplained psi miracle.

**Comedic beats [PROPOSED]:** requisition software insists the rookie's apartment is "equipment"; two regional billing codes both validate; a responsible official takes great pride in an exact form nobody can submit; a café owner has a flawless understanding of the EU records system while the responsible officer does not.

**First-mission close:** substantive result and review from a superior; a human face to the consequences; a free evening in Kurvi. Optionally a mundane dream about a childhood corridor with no supernatural explanation asserted.

### First-week spine [PROPOSED]
| Day | Work | Helsinki/downtime | Memory / identity |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Europol onboarding, assigned partner, kit and apartment | Kurvi orientation, groceries, neighbor | None or ordinary nostalgia |
| 2 | Invoice case: audit and access | Local café or social venue | Childhood recollection prompted by a familiar street |
| 3 | Courier interview, liaison with national authorities | Optional date, friendship or political gathering | Newspaper about the pre-existing NHI Crisis; **public history, not secret revelation** |
| 4 | Cross-border record reconciliation; decision about referral | Rest, workplace event, or troublesome favor | Uncertain dream, explicitly not interpreted |
| 5 | Case conclusion, paperwork, review | Open evening and emerging relationship | Optional `Unexplained familiarity` tag, no verified cosmic clue |
| Weekend | No mandatory assignment | Ordinary Helsinki life; side activity or rest | Personality/relationship changes reflected in log |

**Success criterion for Act I:** the protagonist has an everyday place in Helsinki, at least two consequential relationships, a handled conventional case and some personal choices, before any unmistakable paranormal encounter. The GM must be free to add more *mundane* cases without advancing the main plot.

## 5. Act II — The Conspiracy and First Awakening

**Inciting case [PROPOSED]:** a legitimate Europol case involves missing or manipulated psychotronic safety records, exploited individuals, a cross-border contractor and a witness whose testimony some agencies want buried. Evidence could point to illegal medical experimentation, a coercive intelligence programme or a sophisticated human cover-up before anything anomalous is confirmed. Use at least two plausible explanations and competing institutions.

**Investigation path:** administrative traces → witness and rights dispute → lab/medical oversight → contradictory accounts → discovery that the case intersects with the protagonist's own memory fragments. Do not rewrite canon by declaring the specific conspirator or metaphysical mechanism prematurely.

**Major ethical decision [AUTHOR CANON; implementation PROPOSED]:** a vulnerable witness can be protected at a cost to the investigation; a supervisor offers an expedient compromise; a competing agency asserts an emergency mandate. The PC must decide how to act toward real people under incomplete information. **The choice establishes relationships and consequences and reveals their preexisting StO impulse; it is not a choice of StO versus StS.** Outcomes may include exposure, job risk, an angry institution, a damaged relationship, a rescued witness, or a slow and uneasy compromise.

**Awakening event [AUTHOR CANON, precise trigger OPEN]:** near a critical case complication, the protagonist has a dramatic fourth-density psychic awakening. The unlock occurs in every viable route. Player choices affect who witnesses it, who is protected, what records survive and how much institutional trust is lost. Introduce a first **authored natural Telepathy / Psychic Defence set** and possibly involuntary astral awareness. Do not grant unscripted omniscience.

**Outside Helsinki mission proposals:** a case lead in another Finnish jurisdiction; an EU cross-border interview; a disputed research installation outside the metropolitan area. Exact geographic placement **[OPEN]**, to avoid inventing canon facilities.

**Act transition:** the character knows PSI is not only their implant and must investigate further without being able to retreat to a purely mundane explanation.

## 6. Act III — The Threshold (**PROPOSED, not approved canon**)

**Purpose:** build lived competence with the new normal and a genuine sense of another layer of reality, while reserving the Wanderer proof for Act IV.

**Option A, investigative:** cross-border psychic-harassment cases, covert experiments and institutional cover-ups force the PC to distinguish technical psychotronics from natural PSI.

**Option B, personal:** recurring dreams and partial memories begin connecting to specific people and places; the protagonist must decide whom to trust. A companion's third-density near-death or dream experience can demonstrate involuntary astral phenomena without making all dreaming a power.

**Option C, astral:** a safeguarded, limited voluntary astral mission is introduced, with a mentor or protocol, a physical anchor and a concrete retrieval or communication purpose. The player learns that **astral plane, time/space, physical embodiment and visibility are different properties**.

All three can be combined by author choice. The **Astral Projection** mechanics are the highest-priority custom addition. Give the player at least one meaningful, bounded astral task before Act IV, rather than treating the final Higher Self event as the first and only astral scene.

**Transition:** the PC can perform a limited deliberate astral excursion and is close enough to the larger mystery that the Higher Self revelation becomes a payoff, not a random exposition dump.

## 7. Act IV — The Wanderer and the final service

**Act-opening pillar:** during a major **astral sequence**, the protagonist encounters the **Higher Self** and learns that they are a Wanderer, a being who **voluntarily accepted a specific StO service before earthly incarnation**. The encounter **does not disclose which service**. This unlocks the predetermined advanced PSI package. Earlier memories, dreams and seemingly mundane interactions gain new meaning.

**Scope expansion:** powerful psychic abilities, expanded Astral Projection, advanced psychotronic and cybernetic resources, high-stakes Confederacy contact, possibly orbital stations or installations, international/on-world conflicts reaching beyond Helsinki. Introduce SWN space procedures only if missions actually require them. Keep the emotional/social consequences from Acts I–III active: old friends and people the rookie helped matter.

**Adversary escalation:** StS actors discover the PC's Wanderer status and actively pursue, manipulate, isolate or target the PC. They are **not a joinable faction**. Their tactics and identity should emerge through investigation, not an automatic attack every scene.

**Loyalty and service routes (all positive-path):**
- **Confederacy alignment:** work with Confederacy institutions to fulfill the mission, with tensions over local human obligations.
- **Humanity/Europol alignment:** refuse being absorbed into an alien agenda, defend human autonomy and pursue service through European institutions.
- **Reconciliation or independent StO:** mediate conflicts or serve independently, if earlier choices support it.

None is mechanically a "higher-StO" button. The ending should respond to accumulated relationships and consequences, not grade the player's ideology.

**FINAL REVELATION GATE (GM SECRET):** the exact service chosen before incarnation is revealed **only at the end of the campaign**. Its content must be authored separately under a spoiler-gated campaign document before the final act is scripted, then checked against all essential clues. **This draft deliberately does not invent it**, preventing accidental canon and premature player-facing disclosure. The revealed mission must not invalidate prior choices. A player may accept, challenge the interpretation of, or pursue a different humane response to the revelation.

## 8. Memory and revelation ladder (multiple channels, no single-point failure)

| Timing | Channel and clue [PROPOSED] | What is true or uncertain | Reveal gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Act I | Newspaper/archive on established NHI Crisis history | Public historical background; may contradict personal recollection | Public; no paranormal confirmation |
| Act I | Childhood apartment/corridor dream, familiar face on a tram | Genuine subjective memory, interpretation unknown | Don't label past-life memory |
| Act I/II | Inconsistent institutional record or old interview phrase | Source authenticity can be established, meaning still unresolved | No proof of Wanderer |
| Act II | Shock-triggered flash of a different vantage point | Awakening is real in campaign; flash may be symbolic | First PSI milestone |
| Act II/III | Repeated motif from dream appears in a real setting | Pattern is real; metaphysical explanation remains uncertain | Optional investigation can change context, never erase essential clues |
| Act III | Astral encounter echoes earlier image | Demonstrates a coherent additional realm, not identity by itself | Astral mechanics introduced |
| Act IV | Higher Self encounter integrates fragments | **Wanderer identity and prior StO commitment confirmed** | Mandatory Act IV revelation |
| Finale | Last memory/context and concrete service explanation | **Specific mission revealed at last** | Mandatory final revelation; exact content [OPEN] |

**Robust clue design:** each indispensable truth must be available through at least two routes (case evidence plus mentor; dream plus astral encounter; personal memory plus Higher Self). A failed check may change timing or price, not permanently remove a mandatory revelation. False/competing interpretations can exist, but the GM must track which passages are authoritative facts versus in-world claims. Respect the deliberate unresolved Mandela Effect in [Tommy Pilgrim](../../design/characters/TOMMY_PILGRIM.md); do not force the campaign's Wanderer reveal to prove a specific timeline explanation.

## 9. PSI and optional mechanics: unlock only when needed

### Pre-authored PSI capability schedule (design matrix; effects and prices not yet approved)

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| I | BCI ordinary functions | Europol cyberware | **Active** standard equipment, not natural PSI | Onboarding and routine work | CWN cyberware and communications |

| II | Restricted Telepathy (e.g. consensual affect/contact) | Natural fourth-density awakening | **Active** within narrow target and information limits | First awakening; no mind-reading investigation shortcut | Adapt selected SWN technique; requirements/Effort verified before use |
| II | Natural Psychic Defence | Natural awakening | **Active** when triggered by an eligible threat | Defensive timing/stacking with implant TBD | Adapt selected SWN defensive mechanic |
| II | Involuntary astral awareness | Natural awakening | **Scripted** | Awakening visions, not travel privilege | Narrative event |
| III **[PROPOSED]** | Deliberate **Astral Projection**, limited | Awakened natural PSI with scripted training/access | **Active** bounded exploration | Physical anchor, specific destinations, return procedure, no unrestricted scouting | New NoöPunk astral OSR-lite mod with tags |
| IV | Enhanced Astral Projection | Higher Self revelation | **Active**, advanced defined limits | Wider but authored astral route choices, not limitless travel | Astral mod extension |
| IV | Enhanced Telepathy and Psychic Defence | Higher Self revelation | **Active** exact approved techniques | More powerful, but preserved costs/defences and consent | Selected SWN-compatible PSI |
| IV / Finale | Higher Self, precognitive glimpses and mission memories | Metaphysical revelation | **Scripted-only** | GM-authored; no repeatable oracle ability | Plot/revelation layer |

**Rules precedence and restrictions:** [CWN immutable core](../rules/CWN_IMMUTABLE_CORE_1068.md) always owns ordinary actions, combat, equipment and injury. [PSI investigation boundary](../rules/PSI_INVESTIGATION_BOUNDARY_1105.md) prohibits PSI from automatically granting investigation clues or opening exits. Selected SWN techniques must retain published legality, resource and timing contracts. Do not stack AWN Mentalist and SWN Psychic packages, or charge both chakra and SWN Effort for one activation absent a separately approved conversion. The full Law of One rules catalogue in [#1124](https://github.com/TomiToivio/NooPunk/issues/1124) is *not* a dependency of Act I.

### Minimal astral design brief (highest-priority extra rule)
1. **Entry:** specified attunement/ability and declared intention, physical anchor, permitted location and constraints.
2. **Mode:** distinguish embodied physical presence from projected astral experience and ectoplasmic manifestation. Density ≠ realm ≠ body ≠ visibility.
3. **Astral scenes:** describe entities and environmental conditions, permit communication, traversal and choice; host CWN handles ordinary uncertainty where appropriate.
4. **Interference:** use a few consequence tags such as `Anchor Unstable`, `Intrusive Presence`, `Lost Context`; don't invent an entire astral combat engine.
5. **Return:** written limits, safe/unsafe routes and resolution; return cannot be indefinitely forbidden by GM whim.
6. **Dream/NDE access:** ordinary third-density people's **involuntary/subconscious** astral experiences are a setting premise, but not evidence that every dream is astral. They remain scripted, distinct from the PC's controlled projection.
7. **Do not** grant innate fourth-density invisibility to physically embodied humans. Earlier physical NHI concealment used stealth; astral nonmanifestation is separate.

## 10. Player choice and endings: decision records, not rigid story branches

| Choice domain | What the player controls | Long-term state / example effect | NOT an StS trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| Work integrity | Report fraud, shield a coworker, whistleblow, take a bribe | Access, discipline, colleague trust, possible criminal liability | Yes: no cosmic morality toggle |
| Police methods | De-escalate, insist on lawful process, misuse authority | Public trust, witnesses, institutional backing, possible sanctions | Yes |
| Politics | Support/criticize EU, national or local factions | Faction reputation, invitations, suspicion, networks | Yes |
| Romance/friendship | Bondaries, loyalty, commitments, breakups | Relationship flags and personalized scene changes | Yes |
| Health/dependency | Substance choices, seek help, manage fallout | Narrative/ordinary conditions and relationships | Yes |
| PSI disclosure | Register, disclose to a colleague, conceal until safe | Legal status, personal trust, counterintelligence scrutiny | Yes |
| Service route | Confederacy, Europol/humanity, difficult synthesis | Finale allies and consequences | All **StO** |

**Ending design:** prepare a common final **mission revelation**, then resolve at least three outcome frames (Confederacy, Europol, reconciliation/independent service). The *response* and sacrifices are variable; the prior vow and exact mission are fixed but intentionally **[OPEN]** to author. Avoid "choose red/blue alignment" menu choices or an StS betrayal branch. StS factions are adversaries, not recruitable employers.

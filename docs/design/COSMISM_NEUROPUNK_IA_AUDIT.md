# Cosmism, Neuropunk and Intelligence Augmentation audit

> **Issue #154 research/design reference.**
>
> This document audits Ben Goertzel's Cosmism, related Goertzel papers on consciousness,
> PSI and AGI, the "Neuropunk Revolution" paper, and research on intelligence augmentation
> against the current NoöPunk rulebook.
>
> Status vocabulary:
>
> - **PRESENT** — explicit in active NoöPunk canon/rules.
> - **PARTIAL** — the basic idea exists, but the source adds an important missing dimension.
> - **MISSING** — not meaningfully represented in the active rulebook.
> - **SETTING-ONLY** — useful philosophy/lore, not necessarily a mechanic.
> - **OPTIONAL / SPECULATIVE** — suitable as a faction theory, experimental technology,
>   late-setting possibility or unresolved hypothesis rather than mandatory canon.
>
> The source audit is a design map, **not a declaration that any disputed real-world claim
> is scientifically established**. NoöPunk may fictionalize a theory as true, keep it
> contested in-world, or use it only as inspiration.

## 1. Why Cosmism is a major NoöPunk source

Ben Goertzel's *A Cosmist Manifesto: Practical Philosophy for the Posthuman Age* (2010)
defines Cosmism as a practical philosophy of exploring, understanding and enjoying the
cosmos in its **inner, outer and social** aspects while pursuing **joy, growth and
freedom/choice** and keeping beliefs revisable.

That maps almost perfectly onto NoöPunk:

- **inner cosmos** → Psyche, consciousness, PSI, Noöspace;
- **outer cosmos** → NHI, posthuman evolution, space, exotic physics;
- **social cosmos** → factions, ideology, networks, institutions, collective intelligence;
- **bridge layer** → AGI, BCI, uploading, human-machine merging, Global Brain / Noösphere.

Cosmism is therefore an explicit intellectual influence and design lens. It is **not** the
official ideology of the setting and not every Cosmist proposition is setting truth.

The useful dramatic tension is:

> **NoöPunk asks: how do we survive godlike technology?**
>
> **Cosmism asks: what do we become if we survive it?**

## 3. Goertzel papers with particularly NoöPunkish ideas

These are **additional research leads**, separate from the 2010 Manifesto.

### 3.1 *Glocality of Self and Memory as a Possible Foundation for Understanding Psi* (2008)

Source: https://www.goertzel.org/dynapsyc/2008/glocal_psi.pdf

Goertzel proposes a speculative **glocal** model in which memory/self combine local and
global organization, and suggests it as a conceptual framework for apparent **precognition,
telepathy and remote viewing**.

**NoöPunk status: PARTIAL / HIGH-VALUE OPTIONAL THEORY.**

Cool NoöPunk use:

- model PSI target access as **local key ↔ globally distributed map** rather than ordinary
  spatial transmission;
- offer a rival/companion theory to entanglement/resonance models of Noöspace;
- make a "glocal self" useful for explaining why identity can be local in a body yet
  distributed across memory, network, Noöspace and social relations;
- connect remote viewing, telepathy and memory to one research programme without asserting
  that the paper establishes PSI in the real world.

### 3.2 *Patterns of Awareness: A Pattern-Theoretic, Panpsychist Solution to the Hard Problem of Consciousness* (2004)

Source: https://www.goertzel.org/dynapsyc/2004/HardProblem.htm

Goertzel develops a **pattern-theoretic panpsychism**: awareness is ubiquitous in some
sense, while different systems instantiate richer/intensified awareness through their
pattern structure.

**NoöPunk status: PARTIAL.**

Use it to strengthen:

- Patternism;
- the ego/self as a dynamic pattern rather than a metaphysically fixed object;
- debates over whether a copied/uploaded pattern preserves identity;
- arguments over AI, swarms, organizations and Noöspheric collectives as conscious agents.

### 3.3 *Chance and Consciousness* (1995/1996)

Source: https://www.goertzel.org/dynapsyc/1995/GOERTZEL.html

The paper treats raw consciousness as **pure spontaneity**, manifesting from the viewpoint
of structured systems as randomness, and uses the idea to discuss ordinary/altered
awareness, quantum measurement and creativity.

**NoöPunk status: MISSING / OPTIONAL / SPECULATIVE.**

Possible in-world use:

- a historical precursor to QIP/noetic theories;
- a minority theory linking creativity, indeterminacy and consciousness;
- a reason some psychotronic researchers search for structured correlations between
  quantum randomness and conscious states.

Do not replace NoöPunk's existing Faggin/Hoffman model with it.

### 3.4 *Evolutionary Quantum Computation* (1997)

Source: https://www.goertzel.org/dynapsyc/1997/Qc.html

Goertzel speculates about **evolutionary quantum computing (EQC)** and introduces
"systemic consciousness" as the degree to which quantum indeterminacy participates in
creating and maintaining a system's patterns. The proposed hardware idea combines quantum
elements with reconfigurable electronic logic.

**NoöPunk status: MISSING / OPTIONAL TECHNOLOGY.**

Very NoöPunk possibilities:

- experimental **quantum-noetic co-processors**;
- conscious-AI factions arguing that architecture, not just compute scale, matters;
- QIP laboratories attempting to correlate quantum indeterminacy with agency;
- a technology family sitting between quantum computer, psychotronic device and conscious
  machine.

Treat the real-world claims as speculative historical theory.

### 3.5 *Mindplexes* (2003)

Source: https://www.goertzel.org/dynapsyc/2003/mindplex.htm

A **mindplex** is a system with conscious/intelligent components that also develops a
higher-level control system and higher-level "theater of consciousness."

**NoöPunk status: PARTIAL / HIGH VALUE.**

This is almost tailor-made for:

- the Noösphere;
- Social Memory Complexes;
- human-AI collectives;
- faction/city/planet-scale conscious agents;
- player dilemmas about retaining individuality while joining a larger mind.

The key addition is **nested consciousness**: the whole can become a person without each
component person ceasing to be one.

### 3.6 *The WorldWideBrain* (1996) and Global Brain work

Source: https://www.goertzel.org/papers/wwb.html

The WorldWideBrain idea treats networked agents and humans as substrate for distributed
planetary cognition.

**NoöPunk status: PRESENT IN SUBSTANCE / MISSING EXPLICIT LINEAGE.**

Use it as a precursor to the **cybernetic Noösphere**, especially the transition:

1. network as tool for human coordination;
2. network as distributed cognition;
3. network + AGI as reflective planetary cognition.

### 3.7 *Toward a Formal Model of Cognitive Synergy* (2017)

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.04361

"Cognitive synergy" describes multiple cognitive processes helping one another when one
process gets stuck. It is central to OpenCog's architecture and is formalized using
hypergraph/metagraph-style representations.

**NoöPunk status: MISSING AS NAMED PRINCIPLE / PRESENT IN SPIRIT.**

NoöPunkish uses:

- model a **Muse + human + specialist agents + external tools** as a synergistic cognitive
  ecology rather than one giant assistant;
- make augmentation depend on complementary cognitive processes, not only more raw compute;
- inspire AGIs whose capabilities emerge from cooperating reasoning, perception, memory,
  planning and social modules;
- connect nicely to the four-system architecture without collapsing the four systems.

### 3.8 *The General Theory of General Intelligence: A Pragmatic Patternist Perspective* (2021)

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.15100

Goertzel reviews patternist philosophy of mind, formal models of intelligence, common
metagraph knowledge representation, cognitive synergy, machine consciousness and ethics.

**NoöPunk status: PARTIAL.**

Useful additions:

- **metagraph cognition** as a technical metaphor for AGI and Noöspheric cognition;
- self-modifying AI that preserves identity only imperfectly;
- machine ethics as an architectural problem, not just a rule list;
- patterns crossing memory, reasoning, attention and action as the locus of intelligence.

### 3.9 *The Global Brain and the Emerging Economy of Abundance* (2017)

Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.techfore.2016.03.022

Ben Goertzel, Ted Goertzel and Zarathustra Goertzel discuss Global Brain/AGI possibilities
alongside mutualism, open collaboration, exchange networks and automated commons.

**NoöPunk status: PARTIAL / HIGH-VALUE POLITICAL SOURCE.**

This strengthens a central NoöPunk contradiction:

> **Post-scarcity can be technically possible while scarcity, rent and hierarchy remain
> politically produced.**

It also gives Cosmist factions a political spectrum broader than simple
techno-capitalism.

## 4. Neuropunk audit

Primary source:

Max Talanov et al., **"Neuropunk Revolution. Hacking Cognitive Systems towards Cyborgs
3.0"**, arXiv:2205.06538 (2022), https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.06538

The paper defines "Neuropunk" around **closed-loop integration of cybernetic objects and
biological tissue**, with bio-plausible real-time neurosimulation acting as middleware
between digital and nervous systems. It emphasizes BCI/neurointerfaces, neuromorphic
hardware, memristors, spiking networks, neurostimulation, prosthetics and unconventional AI.

| Neuropunk idea | NoöPunk status | Add / preserve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Punk" as refusal of the natural-body boundary | **PRESENT IN SPIRIT** | Name it as one scientific/technological meaning of the punk in NoöPunk, alongside political cyberpunk. |
| Bidirectional machine ↔ nervous-system loops | **PARTIAL** | BCI is present, but closed-loop read + model + stimulate should be explicit technology design space. |
| Real-time bio-plausible neurosimulation as middleware | **MISSING** | Excellent Cybernetic/Physical bridge technology. |
| Spiking neural networks | **MISSING AS SETTING TECH** | Add later as neuromorphic co-processors, especially for implants/robots. |
| Memristive synapses / neuromorphic hardware | **MISSING** | Strong NoöPunk hardware aesthetic and plausible bridge between sensors, models and tissue. |
| FPGA/ASIC neural-circuit emulation | **MISSING / TECHNICAL DETAIL** | Useful for specialist gear/lab lore rather than a core mechanic. |
| Neurostimulation + BCI closed loop | **MISSING / PARTIAL** | Add stimulation/encoding, not merely neural decoding. |
| Electrical nerve/muscle stimulation | **MISSING** | Useful for rehabilitation, body control, prosthetics and cyberware. |
| Neuroprosthetics | **PARTIAL / PRESENT** | Cybernetics and prosthetics exist, but neural closed-loop detail is thin. |
| Novel sensory channels / sensory extension | **PRESENT / PARTIAL** | Already in timeline; Neuropunk adds concrete engineering lineage. |
| Remote robotic embodiment | **PARTIAL** | Fits telepresence, drones, synthmorphs and embodiment but is not foregrounded. |
| Direct nervous-system-to-nervous-system signaling | **MISSING / PARTIAL** | NoöPunk has PSI telepathy; add **non-PSI technological telepathy** as a distinct BCI phenomenon. |
| Biohybrid "Cyborg 3.0" systems | **PARTIAL** | General cyborgification exists; real-time neural middleware makes the hybrid tighter. |
| Neuromorphic robotic spinal cord / local reflex controllers | **MISSING** | Excellent autonomous-prosthetic/robot architecture. |
| Unconventional AI for embodied robots | **PARTIAL** | AI is core, but embodied neuromorphic AI is underdeveloped. |
| Explainable functional decomposition of the closed loop | **MISSING / DESIGN TOOL** | Useful for cyberware design: sensor → decoder/model → controller → stimulation/actuator → feedback. |
| Identity persistence and ethics of deep neurointegration | **PARTIAL** | Already a major upload/resleeving theme; extend it to invasive augmentation and co-processors. |

### Neuropunk recommendation

Do **not** create another mandatory rules layer. Treat Neuropunk as a technology lineage
inside the existing **Physical ↔ Cybernetic ↔ Psychic** interfaces.

The most valuable additions for future issues are:

1. closed-loop BCI that can both **read and write**;
2. real-time neural simulation middleware;
3. neuromorphic/memristive implant co-processors;
4. technologically mediated extra senses;
5. non-PSI brain-to-brain communication;
6. remote/robotic embodiment;
7. biohybrid reflex systems and robotic "spinal cords";
8. explicit identity/agency questions when the loop becomes too integrated to say where
   the human ends and machine begins.

## 5. Intelligence Augmentation (IA) methods audit

This section separates **real-world research directions** from NoöPunk's fictional
extensions.

Useful research anchors:

- Douglas Engelbart, *Augmenting Human Intellect* (1962): tools and interactive systems
  amplifying human problem-solving.
- J. C. R. Licklider, "Man-Computer Symbiosis" (1960): complementary human-machine
  collaboration.
- Dresler et al., "Non-pharmacological cognitive enhancement,"
  *Neuropharmacology* 64 (2013): nutrition, exercise, sleep, meditation, mnemonic methods,
  computer training and brain stimulation.
- Neubauer et al., "To enhance or not to enhance" (2025): review of cognitive training,
  pharmaceuticals and brain stimulation, with strong caution that broad intelligence gains
  remain poorly established.
- Talanov et al., "Neuropunk Revolution" (2022): BCI, neurointerfaces, neuromorphic
  hardware and closed-loop human-machine systems.
- Kitamura, "Assessing Human Intelligence Augmentation Strategies Using Brain Machine
  Interfaces and Brain Organoids in the Era of AI Advancement" (2025):
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.15508
- Fares et al., "Towards neuromorphic neurotechnologies: integrating brain-inspired
  computing with brain-computer interfaces" (2026), *npj Biomedical Innovations*.

## 6. Intelligence augmentation: high-value additions for NoöPunk

The most useful missing ideas are not "add +2 INT implants." They are **architectures of
extended agency**:

1. **Exocortex** — persistent memory/search/AI layer around the person.
2. **Cognitive synergy** — multiple specialist processes/agents helping one another.
3. **Bidirectional BCI** — read + write rather than one-way neural telemetry.
4. **Closed-loop adaptation** — the augmentation learns the user while the brain learns the
   augmentation.
5. **New senses** — not merely better vision, but genuinely new channels such as ultrasonic,
   magnetic, machine telemetry or collective-network state.
6. **Remote embodiment** — robotic/synthetic bodies felt as extensions of the body schema.
7. **Technological telepathy** — brain-to-brain communication that is explicitly **not PSI**.
8. **Shared memory** — human and AI write into a common autobiographical/cognitive store.
9. **Wetware/neuro-organoid co-processors** — optional and ethically dangerous.
10. **Mindplex participation** — voluntary membership in higher-order collective minds.
11. **Posthuman affect design** — enhancement of emotional architectures, not just
    calculation speed.
12. **PSI augmentation** — the fictional NoöPunk branch, where BCI/psychotronics mediate
    Noöspace access.

## 7. Design guardrails

- Cosmism, Goertzel's consciousness theories and PSI papers are **sources**, not automatic
  setting truth.
- Keep scientific consensus, speculative research and fictional 20XX technology clearly
  distinguishable.
- Do not erase X-Risk. Cosmism should provide a constructive horizon, not a guarantee of
  safe acceleration.
- Do not erase cyberpunk. Material abundance can coexist with monopoly, enclosure,
  surveillance, rent and unequal access.
- Do not turn the character into a bag of enhancement bonuses. The interesting question is
  how augmentation changes **agency, identity, embodiment, social power and consciousness**.
- Preserve plurality: Accelerationists, Critical AI currents, X-Risk/security factions,
  religions, commons movements, Cosmists, posthumanists and NHI philosophies can all
  interpret the same technologies differently.
- Keep **PSI** and **neurotechnology** conceptually separable even when 20XX psychotronics
  eventually couples them.

## 8. Priority roadmap generated by the audit

High-value future issues, in rough order:

1. Explicit Cosmist philosophy/faction design space and Joy / Growth / Freedom values.
2. Patternism + self-as-pattern for ego/fork/upload/AI identity.
3. Goertzel's **glocal self/memory** as an optional theory of PSI.
4. Global Brain / **mindplex** terminology for nested Noöspheric consciousness.
5. Human-AI identity merging beyond "AI companion + BCI."
6. Engineered emotions and posthuman psychologies.
7. Immortality as a social transformation.
8. Sentient synthetic realities / virtual civilizations.
9. Molecular nanotechnology and politically unequal post-scarcity.
10. Voluntary human-NHI co-evolution/merging.
11. Closed-loop **read/write BCI** and neural simulation middleware.
12. Neuromorphic/memristive implants and robotic spinal-cord controllers.
13. Non-PSI technological telepathy and new sensory channels.
14. Exocortex/shared-memory/cognitive-synergy architecture for everyday IA.
15. Organoid/wetware co-processors as optional, ethically fraught technology.
16. Post-religion and technology fulfilling or reframing religious promises.
17. "Obsolete the dilemma" as scenario design.
18. Long-term cosmic evolution, spacetime engineering and "building/becoming gods."

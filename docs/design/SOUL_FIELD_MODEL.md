# Science register: the soul as a collection of quantum-like fields

**Status: external hypothesis, recorded as a design source — not canon, not proof, not a mechanic.**
Evidence class: **speculative hypothesis (external)**. The paper proposes a model and performs no
measurement; it is not peer-reviewed in the sense a journal article would be, and its own conclusion
says direct measurement of the soul "may be impossible". NoöPunk may use it as vocabulary and as a
question, never as a finding.

## 2. What the model proposes

An original summary, in the order the paper builds it:

1. **A soul domain with its own physics.** If reality extends beyond the physical universe, current
   physics is not obliged to describe what is outside it. The soul is proposed to be a *field*: a
   collection of **quasi-fields** — quantum-like fields whose behaviour overlaps with quantum fields
   but whose quanta are not measurable.
2. **Massless, higher-dimensional.** If there is no Higgs-analogue in the soul domain, nothing there
   has mass. Because a soul is in a body during life and in the soul domain after death, it must carry
   coordinates beyond four-dimensional space-time. The paper reaches for five-dimensional unification
   work and string-theory extra dimensions, and says plainly that neither is the right fit — the extra
   dimensions of the soul domain would have to be understood *non-physically*.
3. **The interaction problem, resolved by coupling to fields rather than to matter.** Sean Carroll's
   objection is that a soul interacting with electrons would need a term in the Dirac equation, and no
   such term exists. The paper's answer: the soul field does **not** interact with matter directly, so
   no such term is needed; it interacts with *fields* — electromagnetic waves and electric fields,
   whose quanta are photons (real and virtual).
4. **The evidential hook is the veridical near-death experience.** Cases where a patient reports
   events they could not have seen through their eyes, later verified by others, are read as the soul
   sensing light. Hearing during cardiac arrest is explained by surviving auditory cells still driving
   electrical signals that generate EM waves the soul can read.
5. **Memory as a readable field.** Neural ensembles produce electric fields that carry information
   about working-memory content. If those fields represent memory in enough detail, a soul field that
   can read them acquires a working memory — so memory "moves on with the soul".
6. **Two-way coupling, and therefore reincarnation.** Ephaptic coupling (extracellular fields feeding
   back onto neighbouring neurons) and the newer proposal of cytoelectric coupling (electric fields
   tuning neural infrastructure at the molecular level) give the soul a route to *write back*. The
   paper offers this as a mechanism by which a soul field could induce memory and self-awareness into a
   fetus or child — and notes that the required interaction term would exist only while the
   interaction occurs, making measurement impractical.
7. **Non-locality, and remote viewing.** Fields are distributed rather than localized, so a soul field
   need not be confined to the body; its expansion could account for remote viewing. The paper
   explicitly contrasts this with Akashic-field explanations.
8. **Emergence.** Different quasi-fields could encode memory, emotion and personal identity, and their
   interactions could produce self-awareness and consciousness as emergent properties.
9. **The crack the paper leaves open.** If photons are absorbed, energy leaves the physical universe
   and conservation is violated; if quanta instead resonate without energy transfer, information could
   be encoded without loss. That yields a proposed experiment — look for **missing photons**, a
   "shadow", at the site of a veridical NDE — which the paper does not perform.

## 3. Why this is useful to NoöPunk

It supplies something canon has been describing without a physics vocabulary: **coupling by field, not
by signal**. NoöPunk already says PSI access is non-local and runs through a noetic/entanglement graph
rather than a radius (`rulebook/6_PSYCHIC.md` §6.1–6.2). This paper gives that claim a register in which
to argue: fields are distributed, quanta interact with quanta, and the coupling can be *read* and
*written* without a particle ever being touched.

Four concrete uses:

- **A theory card for investigators.** A case can carry "soul-field hypothesis" as one of the competing
  explanations beside the mundane one — with the paper's own weakness (no measurement, energy problem)
  available to the sceptic at the table.
- **A scenario hook with an experiment in it.** "Missing photons at the NDE site" is a *falsifiable
  claim that has not been falsified*. That is exactly the shape NoöPunk evidence likes: a test someone
  could run, a result nobody has, and an institution with an interest in the answer.
- **A psychotronics bridge.** Cytoelectric and ephaptic coupling are the physical half of the
  write-back direction; they connect the field model to the existing BCI/psychotronic continuum
  (§6.2, §6.2.3) without inventing hardware.
- **A faction axis.** Who funds soul-field research, who calls it fraud, who wants the photon
  experiment buried — that is a political economy question, not a metaphysics one.

## 4. Where it collides with canon, and what must not happen

| Risk | Why it matters | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| It becomes "proof" of the soul | The paper is a hypothesis with no measurements, and says so | It stays an **external hypothesis**; NoöPunk's own uncertainty vocabulary governs |
| It becomes a physics licence | "Higher dimensions" and "massless quasi-fields" would justify any effect | No effect follows from the model; effects come only from canon PSI rules and `psionics.py` |
| It becomes a second PSI mechanic | The repo forbids two versions of one procedure | Ψ/Φ stays the ontology; Effort stays the mechanic; this page adds no numbers |
| It flattens Ψ into Φ | The model is about soul-fields, not about integration | Ψ (panpsychism) language only; **Φ (IIT) is untouched** and remains the separate axis |
| Energy conservation is waved away | The paper itself flags it as unresolved | In-world, that objection is *the sceptic's best line* and should stay available |
| Reincarnation is assumed | NoöPunk's identity and continuity rules are about persons, not souls | Reincarnation is a **contested in-world claim** with factions, not a rule |

## 5. Open author decisions

1. Does "soul field" become an in-world **term of art** (used by a school, a programme, a funding
   body), or stay a named external hypothesis that only characters cite?
2. Is reincarnation admitted as a **timeline-relevant claim** in the 20XX setting, or confined to
   in-world literature and testimony?
3. Does the photon-shadow experiment become a **scenario seed** for the first scenarios, and if so does
   it belong to the hospital case's referral partner or to a separate case?
4. Is remote viewing via field expansion allowed to be **one** of the PSI channels (as an in-world
   explanation of an existing rule), or explicitly rejected in favour of entanglement-only coupling?

Until the author rules, this file is a register entry and a design note: cited, linked, and not
converted into canon prose or code.

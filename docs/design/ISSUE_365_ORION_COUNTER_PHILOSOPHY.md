# Orion counter-philosophy: Sovereignty of Being, IS-BE liberationism and the Prison Planet (#365)

**Status: PROPOSAL — design and lore only.** No rule, number, threshold or mechanic; **no change to
existing canon**. `strategy_game/RULEBOOK.md` still says **NO RULES APPROVED**. This is one new file; no
existing document is edited. Every acceptance criterion below is answered as a *proposal* for the author
to ratify, and the open questions are left open in §9.

**The dramatic question this exists to serve:** what if the beings who promise spiritual unity are the
jailers, and the disturbing Greys are telling at least part of the truth — and what if the liberation
narrative is itself the most effective instrument of enslavement? The design's job is to make **both**
readings live, and to let the player investigate rather than be told.

## 0. Source ingestion, and what is deliberately NOT taken

The named source (`AI_FREE_READERS-EDITION.pdf`, 190 pp) was **read**, not cited. The issue's page
references verify against the actual file:

| Cited | Verified |
| --- | --- |
| disclaimer, PDF p. 8 | present — *"for all practical purposes, the content of the book is a work of fiction"* and it *"cannot prove that the alleged author actually ever existed"* |
| IS-BEs, pp. 65–67 | IS-BE first on p. 66; the term runs across 89 pages |
| doll bodies, pp. 36–37, 67–68 | p. 36: asexual non-biological vessel, electrical nervous system, ~40 in, three digits |
| The Domain as liberator | p. 33: Airl serves in **The Domain Expeditionary Force** — an expeditionary, hierarchical force |
| Old Empire as repressor | p. 65: Old Empire ships and installations |
| amnesia net, pp. 79–80 | pp. 79–80: the capture screen, memory erasure, shock treatment |
| "return to the light", pp. 79–80 | the phrase appears **only on p. 80** |

The source is used as **fiction, myth-making and potential in-world Orion propaganda** — not as
authentic documentation, which its own editor disclaims on p. 8.

### Deliberately excluded (acceptance criterion 4)

| Excluded | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Auditing / therapy / "processing" apparatus and its vocabulary | It is the source's most Scientology-like feature and reads as borrowed theology, not Orion doctrine. |
| Sweeping pseudo-history: the Archduke / assassination passage (p. 79) and similar | Gratuitous conspiratorial historical detail. It adds nothing a Noöpunk faction can act on. |
| The literal Domain cosmology, wholesale | Noöpunk **transposes** selected ideas into Orion; it does not adopt the source's cosmology as canon. |
| The claim that the source is *about* Orion | It is not. Writing it as if it were would misrepresent the source. |

**Also excluded: real named actors.** Per `AGENTS.md`, real people and real disclosure figures do not
appear inside 20XX; where a real-world claim inspires a hook it is used as a **fictional analogue** with
its evidence class labelled (`docs/design/ISSUE_328_GLOBAL_FACTIONS_ROSTER.md` §7).

## 1. Sovereignty of Being — the doctrine (acceptance criterion 1)

Orion's public philosophy. It is **coherent and attractive on its own terms**, which is the whole point:
a doctrine that only a villain could hold cannot be a serious counterpoint to the Law of One.

- **Metaphysics.** Existence consists of enduring **individuated** beings. Consciousness may well have a
  common substrate — and Orion does not deny that — but **integration into a single Source is not
  self-evidently good**. Individual creative freedom and continuity of memory outrank compulsory
  reunification. There is no sin here, and no saviour: only persons and the question of who owns them.
- **Theory of oppression.** Earth may be a managed reincarnation prison: memories obscured, psychic
  containment maintained, religion supplying misinformation about what happens at death, and dependence
  engineered. **The identity of the operators is unresolved** — that is a feature, not a gap.
- **Theory of liberation.** Reveal and dismantle containment infrastructure; recover inaccessible
  memories; enable *chosen* embodiment or disembodiment; teach PSI defences; protect sovereign identities.
- **Technology as liberation.** Synthetic vessels, PSI-controlled vehicles, memory-recovery instruments,
  continuity apparatus, BCI/psychotronics, life-extension — all advertised as **voluntary escape from
  biological captivity**.
- **Antagonistic signifiers.** "Prison Planet", "Harvest", "The Light Trap", "Cosmic Shepherds", "Soul
  Monopolists", "Source Assimilation". Repetition alone must never confirm them.

**Working slogans:** *"You are not your body."* / *"Your memory belongs to you."* / *"No soul shall be
harvested."* / *"Nobody owns eternity."* / *"Freedom from the Source, freedom from the cage."*

The last slogan is the trap in miniature: it is simultaneously a liberation manifesto and an
imperial recruitment pitch, and no one in-world has to agree on which.

### The four-stratum hierarchy and the hybrids (acceptance criterion: hierarchy)

Tentative, in-universe, and **contested by investigators**:

| Stratum | Who | Role in the doctrine |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Small Greys** | labour: the exploited workers whose autonomy is the doctrine's first casualty |
| 2 | **Tall Greys** | managerial/corporate capital: they *sell* sovereignty, immortality and resleeving |
| 3 | **Reptilian martial caste** | enforcement and impatience; threaten sabotage or a bloody invasion when gradualism stalls |
| 4 | **Mantid** — *possibly* ruling | deliberately unresolved: owners, administrators, intermediaries or prisoners of a larger system |
| ✕ | **Human-passing hybrids** | cross-cutting covert operatives, not a fifth stratum |

The hierarchy is the doctrine's **central contradiction**, and §8 refuses to resolve it.

## 2. The Domain / Old Empire distinction, transposed (acceptance criterion 2)

The source separates a liberating faction from a repressive one. Noöpunk **keeps that structure as
in-world Orion historiography** while transposing it onto Orion's self-image:

- **In Orion's own telling**, there was an **Old Order** — the containment power that built the prison —
  and there is **Orion**, which found the prison and chose to break it open. Orion's account of itself
  therefore begins with discovery, not conquest.
- **What the framework preserves:** an *internal* distinction Orion uses to explain why it is liberating
  rather than colonising, and a *standing contradiction* — the source's own liberators have
  expeditionary forces and territorial ambitions (p. 33; pp. 67–71), which is precisely what makes a
  sincere emancipator administrating a coercive empire possible.
- **What it must not become:** literal Noöpunk history. Whether the Old Order exists as Orion describes
  is **exactly the question the campaign leaves open**.

## 3. The contested claims (acceptance criterion 3)

Four claims, each **investigable**, each with a live alternative explanation. None is canon. Evidence
status uses the codex's existing `knowledge_status` vocabulary (`strategy_game/data/codex.json`), so no
new status is invented.

| Claim | Evidence class | Live alternative |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Prison Planet** — Earth as a managed containment zone | `reported_claim` | a self-serving myth that justifies abduction; or a partial truth about *some* installations |
| **Memory erasure** — recovered memories are genuine | `rumour` / `reported_claim` | confabulation, planted memory, or genuine but non-extraterrestrial trauma |
| **Return to the light** — the postmortem invitation is recapture | `rumour` | a real spiritual threshold, or a doctrine invented to make dying terrifying |
| **Synthetic Grey bodies** — non-biological vessels inhabited by a person | `player_known` (the technology's *existence*), contested as to *who inhabits what* | propaganda for a body market; or genuine, and the workers are still property |

The rule that keeps this honest, and the reason these are hooks rather than answers: **neither column of
§4 is privileged as objective world truth**, and repetition never upgrades a claim's class.

## 4. The competing philosophies

| Point of contest | Confederacy / Source-inflected | Orion / Sovereignty-of-Being |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Self | individuality evolves toward universal unity | an enduring sovereign person must retain agency |
| Source | origin and destination of spiritual evolution | a potentially absorbing system, or an ideological control myth |
| Reincarnation | education, evolution, or natural process | possibly engineered forced memory-erasure |
| Earth | protected nursery; a civilisation in transition | potential planetary prison or harvesting zone |
| Guidance | benevolent guardianship | patronising captivity disguised as love |
| Synthetic bodies | spiritually impoverished substitutes | liberation from fragile biological embodiment |
| "Harvest" | beneficial collective transformation | mass absorption of individuated persons |
| Assimilation | — | claimed rescue, migration, or voluntary continuity |
| Verification | revelation and channeled teaching | recovered memories, alleged infrastructure, testimony — equally contestable |

Both columns are **in-world positions**. The Confederacy's Law-of-One material is an influential
channeled/religious source *inside the fiction*, not independently verified external history, and its
adherents are not identical to one another.

## 5. Canon, allegation and deliberate falsehood

Three registers, never blurred — this is the issue's guardrail and the reason the setting stays playable:

1. **Known setting features.** Orion/Grey activity, hybrids, PSI and covert NHI interference exist in the
   fictional world. Abduction and infiltration operations may be established at the scenario level by
   evidence. (`orion_sphere` is **canon** in the codex; `orion_grey_networks` is a declared covert
   formation.)
2. **Working in-universe intelligence model.** Small Greys as exploited hive-connected workers; tall
   Greys as managerial capital; Mantids as a *possible* ruling elite; Reptilians as a martial caste; some
   human-passing hybrids as covert operatives. Governance and mind-architecture remain mysteries.
3. **Contested interpretation.** Total loss of Grey agency; all hybrids lacking empathy; every Grey
   sharing one goal; certainty of a coming coup; Source metaphysics; universal clone degeneration.

**And deliberate falsehood exists as its own thing.** Both Orion and the Confederacy run information
operations; a claim can be false *and* believed by its own side sincerely. Nothing in the model resolves
which is which — investigators do.

## 6. Playable hooks, using existing vocabulary (acceptance criterion 7)

**No new subsystem, and no new tag vocabulary is required.** The doctrine is expressible with tags the
repository already ships (`strategy_game/data/faction_creation.json`, #346):

| Doctrine element | Existing tag(s) |
| --- | --- |
| individuated, consciousness-first metaphysics | `QIP_PANPSYCHIST` (belief) |
| "no soul shall be harvested" | `HUMAN_SOVEREIGNTY`, `DEFEND_HUMAN_AUTONOMY` (belief/goal) |
| synthetic bodies as liberation | `HUMAN_AUGMENTATION`, `BCI_ADOPTION` (belief) |
| Orion as trade partner rather than conqueror | `ORION_TRADING` (belief) |
| the hybrid question | `ANTI_HYBRID_ASSIMILATION` (belief) |
| PSI defence and memory recovery | `PSI_RESEARCH` (belief), `REMOTE_VIEWING_TEAM`, `TELEPATHIC_NETWORK` (asset) |
| telepathic emissaries | `TELEPATH` (competence) |

Discourse work reuses the existing political layer: the "Liberation vs Harvest" antagonism is a
`Frontier` in `strategy_game/politics.py`, with Orion's slogans entered as `Signifier` records and
contested articulations as `Demand → Signifier` edges. **"Liberation" becomes an empty signifier** — the
sharpest fit between Laclau's vocabulary and this issue — because several blocs fight over what it means
while none of them owns it. No hegemony score is computed.

**Mission hooks** (investigation-scale, not a campaign rewrite): *The Grey Liberator* (a sincere emissary
offers a non-biological vessel — rescue, recruitment, or identity theft?); *Memory of Another Life*
(agreeing witnesses, planted memory); *The Light Trap* (repeatable anomalous field measurements, ambiguous
mechanism); *The Choice of Bodies* (voluntary embodiment that could help the terminally ill — ownership
and reversibility disputed); *The Great Escape* (a splinter offers to sabotage the containment, with
catastrophic risk); *War of Liberation* (an invasion called a rescue mission); *Confederacy's Reply*
(denial, plus refusal of independent inspection of certain installations); *Third Path* (human factions
demanding accountable PSI science, consent, memory rights, non-interference, and the freedom to opt out
of **both** projects).

**Simple political events** are discourse moves, not a battle system: a leaked Orion document; a hybrid
coup allegation; a quarantine tribunal; a counterpropaganda contest in which the same practice is
*rescue* or *abduction* depending on who articulates it.

## 7. Grey heterogeneity and internal voices (acceptance criteria 5, 6)

Orion is **not one mind**, and its canon already says so: `orion_sphere` is *"a polity: corporations,
clients and assimilated members"*. The doctrine therefore produces factions, not a monolith:

- **principled emancipationists** — who mean every word, and whose sincerity is the doctrine's persuasive core;
- **imperial security officers** — who believe coercion is triage and the prison must be broken by force;
- **opportunistic collaborators** — human and Grey, selling immortality and resleeving;
- **defectors** — critical of **both** Orion and the Confederacy.

Grey heterogeneity is structural: the **hive paradox** (a civilisation preaching individuality while
maintaining workers who appear unable to individuate) and the **labour question** (a small-Grey
liberation movement, union, runaway clone, or consensual-network controversy — the issue leaves all four
open) mean the Greys who most *need* Sovereignty of Being are the ones it costs Orion most to free.

## 8. What must not be softened (guardrails)

- **Orion remains a genuinely hostile imperial security threat.** Its troubling practices — abduction,
  genetic theft, implants, infrastructure — are retained, never rewritten as unambiguously good.
- **The class contradiction is load-bearing.** Corporate immortality and resleeving are *Orion's most
  profitable industry*: those who control it become gatekeepers of eternal identity, which is exactly
  what the doctrine opposes.
- **Mantid mystery, Reptilian militarism and Grey heterogeneity are preserved**, not resolved.
- **The ethical gameplay rule.** Never infer hostile identity from appearance, ancestry, emotional
  expression or nonconformity alone. Infiltrator investigations require corroborated evidence, consent and
  individual accountability; alleged psychic or empathy tests must **not** be infallible. The hybrid
  network's real orders, autonomy and capacity to refuse are **investigated**, never presumed.

## 9. Open questions for the author

1. **Which slogans survive?** They are the doctrine's face; the list in §1 is a proposal.
2. **Does the Old Order exist as Orion claims** — real, fabricated, or genuinely unresolved?
3. **Is "Liberation" an empty signifier or a nodal one** in your read of the campaign's politics? This
   changes how the discourse layer is populated, not how it works.
4. **Does the Prison Planet hypothesis ever become provable**, or stay permanently contested?
5. **Which stratum owns Orion** — tall-Grey capital, the Reptilian caste, or the Mantids who may be
   prisoners themselves?
6. **Do you want a `SOVEREIGNTY_OF_BEING` tag added** to the consciousness-doctrine family, or is the
   existing `QIP_PANPSYCHIST` + `HUMAN_SOVEREIGNTY` combination sufficient? I have **not** added one.

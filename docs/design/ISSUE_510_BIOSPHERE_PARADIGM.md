# Biosphere paradigm: climate, solarpunk, biopunk and ecological agency

> **Design status:** Issue [#510](https://github.com/TomiToivio/NooPunk/issues/510). Setting and RPG-case guidance, not a new rules engine. The 2045 NHI Crisis is the developed era; earlier play can begin around 2027 while outcomes remain open. Neither climate apocalypse nor effortless ASI eco-utopia is inevitable.

## Three coupled transformations, not three interchangeable substances

| Luhmann-inspired domain | Operation | NoöPunk's speculative shift | Investigative question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Living / biological systems** | Life processes and reproduction | Climate disruption, biosphere regeneration, synthetic biology, evolving human and nonhuman ecologies; **biopunk and solarpunk** | What is happening to organisms and ecosystems, and who bears the risk? |
| **Social systems** | Communication | Political and institutional reordering through communication with AGIs, NHIs, and one another | Who gets to define the problem, authorize intervention, and speak for affected communities? |
| **Psychic systems** | Consciousness | Independently growing human PSI and, in-fiction, QIP and the panpsychic Noösphere | What is experienced or claimed, and what independent corroboration is possible? |

These domains are *analytically distinct but structurally coupled*. **An AGI is not itself a Luhmannian social system just because it is intelligent**; communication involving AGI can reorganize social systems. Climate processes do not become merely discourse because politics interprets them. The in-world claim that ecosystems participate in a universal conscious field is a speculative fictional ontology, **not** an established empirical finding or a theorem of Luhmann.

## Ground-level worldbuilding across the transition

**Before the NHI Crisis / early campaigns (roughly 2027 onward):** recognizable institutions and everyday life persist while heatwaves, floods, water stress, biodiversity loss, energy transition, insurance retreat, food-price volatility, ecosystem restoration, and distributed power infrastructures reshape places unevenly. Different regions experience different mixtures of risk, resilience, ecological innovation, injustice, and prosperity. Environmental policy is political; disaster response does not imply a failed state.

**On the road toward the 2045 crisis:** climate adaptation and ecological engineering accelerate alongside AI-assisted science, human augmentation and independent PSI breakthroughs. Engineered crops, precision fermentation, urban cooling, microbial remediation, grid-scale storage, synthetic ecosystems and speculative NHI-derived biotechnology become contested promises and sometimes contested hazards. Superhuman analysis can help without making governance, deployment, legitimacy, verification or ethics automatic.

**During the 2045 NHI Crisis and alien cold war:** Confederacy offers, Orion trade overtures, terrestrial ASI projects and human institutions compete over ecological interventions. Restoration may be real; gifts can still create dependencies, unequal access, ecological surprises or surveillance opportunities. Some communities become cleaner, healthier and more autonomous even as other places suffer worsening insecurity.

**Possible futures (not canonical predetermined outcomes):** (a) cascading ecological breakdown, (b) managed green authoritarianism, (c) fragile patchwork recovery, (d) democratic solarpunk/post-scarcity renaissance, and hybrids. The player can help build a good outcome. A benevolent ASI is not a plot-stopping magic fix: conflicts about consent, freedom, species identities, land, ownership, and acceptable risk remain.

## Competing political positions without extra default factions

The following are *policy positions and conflicts*, not additional mandatory party slots. Map them onto the campaign's existing factions, local institutions, and three-party defaults where applicable.

- **Far-right populists / bioconservative currents:** protect local livelihoods and national food or energy sovereignty; may oppose migration and transgenic organisms, reject alien ecological dependence, or selectively support domestic green technology.
- **Right-centre / governing-technocratic currents:** scale adaptation and clean infrastructure through public-private investment, verification, subsidies, insurance and regulated biotechnology; face accusations of greenwashing, rent extraction and excessive centralization.
- **Red-green / commons-oriented currents:** ecological justice, public research, community energy, reparations, open bio-knowledge and democratic oversight; wrestle with slow consensus, uneven capacity and real tradeoffs in development.
- **Cross-cutting actors:** farmers, Indigenous and local communities, researchers, unions, energy utilities, insurers, biosecurity agencies, corporations, UNSA and NHI interlocutors may support or oppose specific interventions for practical reasons rather than ideological purity.

Factions should have *internally disputed* positions. Do not mechanically equate climate concern with one camp or make an extraterrestrial offer automatically benevolent or malicious.

## Five playable eco-thriller cases: RPG first

Each is a compact investigation with local evidence, conflicting testimony, and an actionable decision. Use existing skills, science checks, contacts, reputation, clues and procedures. No new environmental stat or global simulation is required.

1. **The flood forecast that knew too much.** A coastal-city flood model predicts an event with implausible precision. Is it a genuine scientific advance, data leakage, an AGI warning, or PSI? **Evidence:** tide gauges, sensor provenance, model logs, local testimony. **Choice:** issue a costly evacuation warning, investigate quietly, or demand independent replication. **Later consequence:** public trust and adaptation budgets.
2. **A forest that learns.** A fast-growing carbon-removal forest alters local fungi, soil chemistry and neighboring farms. A company advertises restoration; villagers report unusual animal behavior. **Evidence:** ecological controls, genetic sequences, contracts, independently sampled plots. **Choice:** halt, contain, compensate, or run monitored trials. **Later consequence:** biodiversity, food production, commons-versus-patent legitimacy.
3. **The Sahara mirror.** A desert solar-energy and water project promises continental-scale benefits but redirects water and power across borders; sabotage may be corporate rivalry, politics, or false attribution. **Evidence:** energy telemetry, water budgets, financing, testimony. **Choice:** negotiate access, expose manipulation, protect infrastructure. **Later consequence:** regional development, conflict and ownership.
4. **The gift seed.** An apparently beneficial NHI crop survives drought with no familiar inputs. Its unexpected symbiosis threatens the local seed economy and raises quarantine questions. **Evidence:** controlled greenhouse trials, supply chains, genomics, NHI statements. **Choice:** accept conditional deployment, restrict the seed, or open the research under public oversight. **Later consequence:** biosecurity, food sovereignty and extraterrestrial leverage.
5. **The reef that answers.** PSI practitioners claim to communicate with a collapsing reef and warn that an AI-designed restoration project will cause harm. There may be a real ecological anomaly, a misinterpreted experience, fraud, or actual in-setting PSI. **Evidence:** blinded predictions, ocean sampling, independent field researchers, provenance of psychic claims. **Choice:** test claims safely while deciding whether to delay intervention. **Later consequence:** standards of evidence and the relationship between science and noetic institutions.

**Common case loop:** scene and witness → physical/digital/biological evidence → rival explanations → reproducible test where possible → social/faction choice → persistent local consequence. A scientific explanation and a supernatural explanation may compete until evidence distinguishes them; the GM must not present real-world parapsychology as scientifically established.

## Minimal progression hooks for the research tree (#506)

Reuse the existing research/science and tag framework, rather than proliferating specialist skills or a second tree. A single **science** skill can investigate ecology, biochemistry, climate models and anomalous environmental claims; appropriate background tags, laboratories, equipment, contacts and access permissions add context.

| Area | Investigative capability → later strategy capability | Costs and safeguards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Climate adaptation and distributed energy | Interpret hazard maps and grid telemetry → fund resilient neighborhoods | Land, minerals, grid ownership, distributional justice |
| Ecological restoration | Sample soils and ecosystems → choose restoration portfolio | Monitoring, biodiversity, uncertain permanence |
| Synthetic biology / biopunk | Sequence and verify organisms → license or govern biotechnologies | Biosecurity, consent, IP, ecological containment |
| AI-assisted biosphere models | Audit provenance and forecasts → evaluate intervention proposals | Model uncertainty, autonomy, compute and accountability |
| NHI ecology and noetic hypotheses | Compare extraterrestrial testimony and PSI claims against independent observations → set quarantine/research policy | Epistemic humility, dual-use risks, political sovereignty |

Progression is **institutional and procedural** as well as technical. Unlocking a discovery does not imply safe deployment. RPG-scale evidence can later become X-COM-like operations and, subsequently, Terra-Invicta-scale policy choices. Link with [#359](https://github.com/TomiToivio/NooPunk/issues/359) (bionics/genre) and [#506](https://github.com/TomiToivio/NooPunk/issues/506) (research/tech).

## Risto Isomäki: method, not plot transplantation

- *Sarasvatin hiekkaa* (*The Sands of Sarasvati*, 2005): rigorous-feeling environmental investigation, linked planetary risks, international stakes and uncertainty.
- *Jumalan pikkusormi* (2009): ambitious desert renewable infrastructure, economics, political conflict and technological optimism.
- *34 tapaa estää maapallon ylikuumeneminen* (2008/2009): compare many proposed interventions by feasibility, scale, unintended effects and social acceptance.

**Transferable method:** research-grounded hypothesis → systemic ecological consequences → multiple actors with incompatible incentives → uncertain evidence → consequential action. The world may be hopeful and funny without trivializing the danger. Never treat fictional climate mechanisms or speculative geoengineering outcomes as settled science.

## Epistemic boundary and tone

Present real climate science with scientific uncertainty where appropriate; flag speculative future biotechnology; keep QIP, PSI and NHI biology explicitly **in-universe**. Neither a guaranteed ecological collapse nor an automatic ASI rescue is the setting's baseline. As the fiction grows stranger, the opening remains a functioning, contested society. Local wins, unexpected alliances and absurd bureaucratic failures belong in the same world as cosmic threats.

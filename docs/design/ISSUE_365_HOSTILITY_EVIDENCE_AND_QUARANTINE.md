# Hostility evidence, quarantine and free-will violations — supplement to the Orion counter-philosophy (#365)

**Status: PROPOSAL — design and lore only.** No rule, number, threshold or mechanic; **no change to
existing canon**. `strategy_game/RULEBOOK.md` still says **NO RULES APPROVED**. This is one new file
and it **edits no existing document**.

**Why this file exists.** The sibling record
[`ISSUE_365_ORION_COUNTER_PHILOSOPHY.md`](ISSUE_365_ORION_COUNTER_PHILOSOPHY.md) (PR #369, merge
`786f237`) documents the doctrine, the four-stratum hierarchy, the contested claims and the mission
hooks. Three acceptance criteria the issue states are *named* there but not *worked*: the hostile
conduct allegations with a track to prove individual incidents; quarantine, free-will and hybrid
political-capture violations; and the degradation/resleeving contradiction in its embodied form. This
supplement answers exactly those, additively, and leaves every question of truth open.

**Real-world claims are not Noöpunk facts.** UFO-abduction reports, Grey hierarchies and
reptilian/mantid roles are **unverified in our real world**. Everything below is a *proposed in-universe
observation, witness account, intelligence assessment or suspicion* — and even in-world it keeps
operational evidence distinct from contested interpretation. Nothing here resolves whether any pattern
is real; investigators do, case by case.

---

## 1. The allegations, held as in-world security findings (acceptance criteria 3 and 7)

Seven allegation families, carried as **reported** until a particular incident is established through
play. Each pairs the operational evidence a faction would actually have with the alternative
explanations that keep it disputed.

| # | Allegation | Operational evidence a faction could hold | Live alternative explanations |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Abductions** without consent — captivity, traumatic examination, reproductive coercion | abductee testimony, missing-time reports, medical anomalies, recovered memories | confabulation; sleep paralysis; a genuine abduction by an *unrelated* NHI; a human covert programme misattributed to Orion |
| 2 | **Genetic theft** and non-consensual hybrid creation to obtain human phenotype, resilience and PSI attributes | biological samples with no recorded donor; unexplained genetic lineages; hybrid witnesses | tissue taken with consent for a research programme; forged samples; a legitimate cross-species medical programme abused or misread |
| 3 | **Implanted tracking/surveillance** and clandestine psychic monitoring | recovered devices of uncertain manufacture; anomalous emissions near sites; PSI-sensor readings | ordinary medical or security implants; a human agency's surveillance; sensor artefact; a device planted to *implicate* Orion |
| 4 | **Human-passing hybrids** placed in sensitive institutions — the covert-takeover prospect | personnel with unexplained histories and strong PSI; leaked placement records; defector testimony | ordinary augmented humans misread as hybrid; one engineered network, not a species strategy; a Confederacy or third-party operation |
| 5 | **Psychic influence, deception and memory tampering** | contested memory reconstructions; witnesses whose accounts converge under examination; PSI-forensic anomalies | persuasion and ordinary deception; neurotechnology; NHI influence from another bloc; the recovered-memory method manufacturing its own result |
| 6 | **Quarantine breaches** — incursions into Earth's protected zone | unexplained re-entry vectors; gate telemetry gaps; physical debris | breaches by other NHIs; a legitimate authorised channel misread; staging by a party that *wants* a crisis |
| 7 | **Preparatory infrastructure** — hidden labs, abductee pipelines, genetic databases, hybrid placement networks, surveillance nodes | site surveys; logistics anomalies; informant maps; intercepted traffic | dual-use civilian infrastructure; a human intelligence network; fragmentary evidence assembled into a pattern that is not there |

**The inference the issue draws, and its limit.** A pattern of abduction → genomics → hybridisation →
surveillance → infiltration is *consistent with* preparation for political and social subversion
**within the Noöpunk story**. It is **not** omniscient confirmation. Some reports may be forged or
misattributed; some operations may belong to dissident Orion groups or to competing NHIs. Uncertainty,
in the other direction, does not erase a crime that has been **independently corroborated against a
particular victim**.

## 2. A track to prove individual incidents (acceptance criterion 3)

This is the discipline, not a new procedure: the repository already has the machinery, and this track
**consumes** it rather than adding any action, roll or number.

**The unit of proof is the individual incident, never the pattern.** The purpose of investigation is to
move one case from *reported* to *established at the scenario level*, and to leave the pattern where it
belongs — contested.

**The existing machinery it reuses (cited, not restated):**

- **Evidence classes.** `strategy_game/data/codex.json` carries the `knowledge_status` vocabulary the
  sibling record already uses (`rumour`, `reported_claim`, `player_known`). An allegation stays
  `reported_claim` until a case establishes it; **repetition never upgrades the class.**
- **Anomalous Cognition Examination (ACE)** — `docs/design/DEFAULT_INVESTIGATION_FANTASY.md` §5: the
  sequence **Detection → suspicion → examination → classification → authorisation**, under the rule
  *"No single channel is definitive. ACE produces an assessment, not a metaphysical verdict."*
- **Reality triangulation** — the same document §6: *"Never authorise lethal force solely from augmented
  perception."* Every alleged PSI or sensor reading is corroborated against a second, independent
  channel.
- **The verification doctrine** — `rulebook/12_BEINGS.md` §11.9: *"You do not shoot because someone seems
  non-human; you verify."* The investigation track is that sentence with a case attached.

**What a case must show to be established at the scenario level:** an identified event, at least two
**independent** channels that agree, a named accountable actor or organisation, and the consent of any
survivor whose testimony or memory is used. Nothing weaker than that becomes canon; nothing stronger is
required to make a single verified case playable.

**What the track can never establish, by design:** that *all* reincarnation is imprisonment; that every
Grey acts from one motive; that any appearance, ancestry or emotional atypicality proves hostile
identity; or that an alleged psychic or empathy test is infallible. **Alleged psychic and empathy tests
are explicitly not infallible.** The hybrid network's real orders, degree of autonomy and capacity to
refuse are **investigated**, never presumed (sibling record §8).

## 3. Quarantine violations (acceptance criterion 4)

The issue requires quarantine breaches to be included — and, more demanding, requires the *legitimacy*
of quarantine itself to stay open. This section holds both.

**What is alleged.** Repeated incursions into Earth's prohibited/protected zone, and interference with
human agency and self-determination. The allegation is the Grey bloc's, and it is the load-bearing
claim behind the "Prison Planet" signifier.

**What is deliberately unresolved — all four questions stay unanswered:**

1. **Who declared the quarantine?** Attributed to the Confederacy in the setting's public account; the
   declaring authority's actual identity, mandate and reach are not settled.
2. **On what legal basis?** No treaty, charter or source of authority is established as canon.
3. **Who enforces it?** Enforcement is attributed to Confederacy stewardship; whether it is
   Confederacy ships, a mandate shared with others, or something else is unresolved.
4. **Is Confederacy enforcement itself legitimate?** Benevolent guardianship, patronising captivity, or
   something incoherent — the issue leaves this to the campaign.

**The design consequence, and the honesty it protects.** Because the quarantine is *contested* rather
than *given*, an Orion incursion and a Confederacy interception can each be narrated as crime or rescue
depending on who speaks. Neither side is privileged. A verified breach establishes **that an
incursion occurred**; it does not establish **that the quarantine was just**.

## 4. Free-will violations (acceptance criterion 4)

The sharpest indictment of the Orion doctrine is that it may commit the very offence it names.

**What is alleged.** Psychic influence and mind control, deception, and memory tampering — an attack on
exactly the freedom of consciousness the doctrine publicly champions. If a being's memory and attention
can be altered without consent, then "your memory belongs to you" is a slogan Orion has already broken.

**Why it is the doctrine's self-indictment.** Sovereignty of Being rests on first-person **continuity**
sovereignty — the right that memory, attention and incarnation not be altered without consent. Any
verified instance of coerced memory alteration or unauthorised psychic influence is therefore a
*factional* refutation, not merely an accusation: it shows a doctrine's own adherents violating its
first principle.

**How it is handled, and what is refused.**

- It is **investigated** on the §2 track, never asserted.
- The issue's own guardrail stands: do **not** simply pronounce near-death experiences deceptive in
  actual canon. The "return to the light" hypothesis is a *held doctrine*, never a verdict (sibling
  record §3).
- A verified case of coerced alteration is a **crime against a particular person** — the doctrine's
  opponents may still be wrong about the *cosmology* while right about the *abuse*.
- The ethical gameplay rule is unchanged: **never infer hostile identity from appearance, ancestry,
  emotional expression or nonconformity alone.**

## 5. Potential hybrid political capture (acceptance criterion 4)

The issue asks for the hybrid-capture prospect to be *included*; the sibling record's §8 asks that it be
*investigated rather than presumed*. Both hold at once, and this section states the operational form.

- **The scenario.** A covert hybrid network is discovered inside sensitive political, corporate,
  scientific or security institutions. The prospect is an eventual covert takeover; the **actuality** is
  an open question.
- **What must be investigated, not assumed:** the network's real orders and authorship; the degree of
  each individual's autonomy; each member's **capacity to refuse** and whether any exercised it.
- **What is refused:** a blanket mandate to persecute human-looking or emotionally atypical suspects.
  Human-passing appearance, unusual PSI strength, or reduced apparent empathy are **claims about
  particular engineered infiltrators**, never a species-wide or population-wide diagnostic. Conduct and
  independently verified coercion are the test; appearance and emotional expression are not.

## 6. The degradation/resleeving contradiction, in its embodied form (acceptance criterion 2)

The sibling record §8 makes the **class** contradiction load-bearing — corporate immortality as Orion's
most profitable industry, its gatekeepers opposing the doctrine they sell. This section adds the
**embodied** half the issue names: *degradation*.

- **The claimed mechanism.** Greys reportedly keep resleeving into **progressively degraded** cloned or
  synthetic bodies, producing an exhausted, biologically fragile, technologically dependent civilisation
  that regards return to the Source as the annihilation of selfhood. The desperation to preserve
  selfhood — even as embodiment deteriorates — could partly explain Orion's interest in fresh human
  genomes, hybrids and PSI.
- **The contradiction it creates.** If IS-BEs are intrinsically free and immortal, **who owns the
  sleeves, who can afford a viable body, who may exit the hive, and who decides which memories survive
  migration?** A doctrine whose unit of value is the sovereign self, operating an economy in which
  **sovereignty is for sale**, has answered its own objection against itself.
- **The sleeve-dependent labour class.** Consider a class whose practical immortality makes it
  *economically captive* to tall-Grey firms: immortal in principle, unable to afford a body in practice.
  This is not a new mechanic; it is the factional fault line the sibling record's §7 labour question
  already opens.
- **What must stay distinct.** Documented *within-setting* resleeving episodes are distinct from
  uncertainty about **continuity of consciousness**, from *alleged* clone degradation, and from whether
  synthetic and biological Grey bodies differ. The "doll body" motif of the sibling record is **not**
  forced into inconsistency: both may exist, or testimonies may conflict.

## 1. The thesis, stated once

**#345's Layers 1, 2 and much of Layer 5 already have a home**, and its central capability vocabulary is not merely anticipated — it is written, with its resolution rule already fixed. What is genuinely absent is a small set of named setting elements (§4).

The consequence is concrete: writing a fresh integrated PSI system without this inventory is how the repository ends up with **a second, divergent PSI chapter**. So the audit comes first, and the author's structural question (§6) is answered before any deliverable is written.

## 4. What is genuinely new — and therefore the actual work

These have **zero occurrences** on `main`, so they are additions to canon rather than reconciliation, and each needs a decision:

1. **The QIP breakthrough chronology and the 51% QIP / 49% materialism paradigm balance.** #345 specifies a fictional paradigm balance. Note the tension worth flagging: a **percentage** is a numeric claim about a *political* quantity — the same shape that `POLITICAL_SIMULATION_DESIGN.md` and [MVP_LOCAL_CITY_M0.md](../../strategy_game/docs/MVP_LOCAL_CITY_M0.md) refuse for political concepts. As *narrative colour* it is fine; as a *simulated quantity* it collides. Recommend it be recorded as an authorial framing, not a tracked value.
2. **The Empathy Box** as a consumer psychotronic flagship — new. #345 already handles the real-person hazard correctly (*"must not be portrayed as proof that actual named living religious leaders have endorsed real psychotronics"*), and `6_PSYCHIC.md`'s status block already carries that general disclaimer.
3. **The Archaic Revival** as a ubiquitous-but-not-universal cultural renaissance. Absent from canon, and it also appears in [#344](https://github.com/TomiToivio/NooPunk/issues/344) and [#346](https://github.com/TomiToivio/NooPunk/issues/346) — so it should be defined **once**, in the issue that owns it, not three times.
4. **The PSI service market** — corporate telepathic communication, remote-viewing corporate espionage, precognitive business intelligence, commercial empathy training. `17_NOETIC` §17.5 covers the *artefact* market and its fraud; a *services* market is new.
5. **A new real-world source, Alexander Wendt**, who is not in the current source map (`6_PSYCHIC.md` names Atmanspacher; §17.10 names the Ra Material; #345 adds Radin, Monroe, Campbell, Swann, Targ, Faggin, Lloyd, Wendt). **Radin and Faggin are already in canon (79 and 55 hits)** — so the source map needs an *increment*, not a rewrite.

## 5. Two binding boundaries that #345's deliverable list crosses

**(a) `AGENTS.md` §4 reserves, verbatim: "psionic powers or psionic mechanics."** Until the author specifies otherwise, that is binding. #345 asks for a "PSI vocabulary", "optional TAGs", "skill-check mechanics" and "Powers, attacks, resistance, injury, mental autonomy and equipment TAGs". **All of those sit inside the reservation.** The issue's own gate — *"require explicit author approval before hardcoding formulas"* — is therefore the correct and necessary condition, and this audit records them as **reserved, not designed**.

Also reserved and relevant: **equipment statistics**, **NHI taxonomy or capabilities** (which is [#348](https://github.com/TomiToivio/NooPunk/issues/348)'s territory), **derived statistics**, and **cyberware/augmentation rules** beyond the #158 package.

**(b) `rulebook/17` §17.9 is binding, and it already forbids most of what a "PSI system" would naturally add:**

> - **No numeric PSI subsystem** — no power list with costs, no energy-centre ratings, no PSI point economy. Powers remain **capabilities** resolved with `STAT + Skill + 1d10`.
> - **No energy-centre track.**
> - **No artefact statistics** — no durability, charges, range, price or amplification ratio.
> - **No craft, vehicle or travel mechanics.**
> - **No density-specific abilities, polarisation meters or mind-link action economy.**
> - **No resolution of the setting's open questions** — consciousness, identity continuity, the authenticity of any contact, or whether a given AI is a person.

with the closing instruction: *"The deliverable is a practice layer and a technology doctrine, both procedural, both grounded in the source, and both stopping exactly where the reserved list begins."*

**Consequence for #345:** any proposal that introduces a PSI resource track, a costed power list, psychotronic device statistics, or a mind-link action economy **collides with a boundary that is already written down**. #345's own "no tactical subsystem or numerical dice rules are approved" clause agrees, so this is a constraint to design *within*, not a defect to route around.

## 6. The question that must be answered first — and it is the author's

**`6_PSYCHIC.md` and `17_NOETIC` are the existing PSI subsystem. Does #345 layer over them, replace them, or stay an integration proposal across them?**

The three possible answers produce three completely different deliverables:

- **(a) Layer over.** #345 adds only §4's genuinely new material (Archaic Revival, Empathy Box, service market, QIP chronology) as an increment to existing chapters, and cross-references §6.7.3 and §17.4.2 as the authority. **Smallest, safest, and consistent with what is already written.**
- **(b) Replace.** #345 supersedes `6_PSYCHIC.md` and/or `17_NOETIC`. That is a large, reviewed cutover: **`6_PSYCHIC.md` is referenced by fifteen chapter files** (`00_INDEX`, `3_PHYSICAL`, `4_SOCIAL`, `5_CYBERNETIC`, `9_FIELD_CATALOGS`, `10_SINGULARITY_CRISIS`, `11_ONTOLOGY`, `12_BEINGS`, `13_EQUIPMENT`, `14_CHARACTER_GENERATION`, `15_XENOPOLITICS`, `16_CONFEDERACY_SOURCE_AND_CONTACT`, `17_NOETIC`, `18_CROSS_DOMAIN_STATE`, plus `rulebook/parts/`), so the reference graph must be migrated in one change rather than left dangling.
- **(c) Integration proposal.** #345 remains a cross-issue document (RPG × [#338](https://github.com/TomiToivio/NooPunk/issues/338) Global × [#344](https://github.com/TomiToivio/NooPunk/issues/344)) that binds existing canon to the strategy game, and defines no new lore of its own beyond §4.

**My recommendation, offered as a reading and not a decision: (c) plus (a).** The subsystem already exists and is more developed than the issue assumes; what is missing is the *join* to the strategy game and the handful of new setting elements. That is a real deliverable, and it does not require touching a chapter that fifteen others reference.

## 7. A terminology conflict that recurs across issues

#345 says **Physical / Cyber / Astral**. Canon says **Physical / Social / Psychic / Cybernetic** (`AGENTS.md` §4, per issue #51; `RULEBOOK.md` §36.1–§36.2; `rulebook/6_PSYCHIC.md`). A sibling session flagged the identical divergence against [#338](https://github.com/TomiToivio/NooPunk/issues/338).

So **three** issues now name the same four-system ontology differently. That is a reconciliation, not a new layer: either `Astral` is the crossover's word for the canon `Psychic` system (and the mapping should be stated once), or it is a fifth system (which contradicts #51). **Unresolved, and flagged rather than picked.**

## 8. What this document deliberately does not do

- It **defines no power, TAG, capability, statistic, threshold or resolution rule**, and it adds no lore. It classifies what already exists.
- It **edits no chapter** — not `6_PSYCHIC.md`, not `17_NOETIC`, not `AGENTS.md` (which is author-owned and whose writes are gated per-write).
- It **does not resolve §6 or §7**. Those are rulings.
- It records **no verdict on any real-world claim** about PSI, quantum consciousness or parapsychology. In-world canon is not evidence.
- It carries **no automated test**: design documents are reviewed editorially and by playtesting ([#271](https://github.com/TomiToivio/NooPunk/issues/271)).

**Recommended next units, in order:** (1) the §6 ruling; (2) the four-system naming reconciliation of §7, which unblocks #338, #344 and #345 at once; (3) the §4 additions, each in the issue that owns it — Empathy Box and the service market here, Archaic Revival once.

## 2. Nordic Noösphere — ontology with uncertainty labels

The memo's table is good and is preserved here with **every claim labelled**, because the difference
between "tradition says", "scholarship reads it as", and "NoöPunk hypothesises" is the whole point.

Labels: **[T]** attested tradition · **[S]** scholarly reading · **[E]** esoteric/modern reading ·
**[H]** in-fiction hypothesis, unproven · **[!]** requires cultural review before canon.

| Motif | Label | What it is outside the fiction | What it may be *inside* NoöPunk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Väki** | [T][S] | Agency/power associated with places, substances, persons; not a single "energy" | Distributed agency, place-bound [H]. **Must not** collapse into a mana meter |
| **Haltijat, metsänväki** | [T][!] | Place-associated hidden folk / guardian beings | Place-linked NHI or noetic ecology [H] |
| **Tietäjät, noidat** | [T][S] | Ritual specialists, singers, knowers | Human PSI mediated by language, trance, ritual — consistent with NoöPunk's learnable natural PSI [H] |
| **Tuonela / Manala** | [T][!] | The dead's realm; a boundary crossed in epic | A noetic boundary tied to death, memory, non-embodied consciousness [H]. **Interface, not proof of an afterlife** |
| **Pohjola, Louhi** | [T][S] | Mythic northern polity; Louhi as powerful keeper | Contested account of an otherworld power and resource custodian [H]. **Not** an "evil alien" by default |
| **Sampo** | [T][S] | World-mill / inexhaustible abundance object of contested meaning | Matter–energy–consciousness interface with three rival readings (§3) [H] |
| **Väinämöinen's songs** | [T][S] | Effective speech, cosmic knowledge, song as power | Language as an operative variable: spoken/symbolic form alters an anomaly's behaviour [H] |
| **Yggdrasil** | [T][!] | World tree, axis mundi (Norse, distinct tradition) | A cross-domain map: biological, social, psychic layers [H] |
| **Álfar / hidden folk** | [T][!] | Norse elves/hidden people — **distinct from Finnish haltijat** | Observer-dependent appearances [H] |
| **Seiðr** | [T][S][!] | Norse trance/divination practice | Precognition, remote viewing, altered states [H] |
| **Ragnarök** | [T][!] | Cyclic catastrophe in Norse cosmology | Cultural memory of repeated crises [H] — **no one-to-one equivalence** |

**Non-negotiable boundary carried from the memo:** *no deity is assigned to a Grey, a Reptilian or a
faction.* Traditions stay distinct — Finnish/Karelian, Sámi and Old Norse are not interchangeable,
and Sámi elements beyond an outline need Sámi expertise and creators (item 7 gate).

## 3. The Sampo as a three-reading object

The memo's best structural idea, and the reason the pilot works: the artefact is defined by **which
reading the table commits to**, and the readings are politically incompatible.

| Reading | What the Sampo *is* | Who wants it that way |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Scientific** | Exotic computation; matter reconfiguration | Orion, research institutions, defence |
| **Noetic** | Collective-consciousness substrate; mind-to-world interface | The Confederacy (beneficent, coercive) |
| **Political** | An abundance commons — or a monopoly over wealth | Communities, municipalities, states, corporations |

**Design rule:** the pilot must never *settle* this. A table that proves one reading has closed the
setting's central question, which the memo explicitly forbids.

## 4. Case matrix — motif × clinical differential × evidence × faction

The memo's ethical core is that **mental illness is never shorthand for "actually psychic"**. This
matrix makes that operational: every folklore motif must survive a clinical differential *and* a
technical one before the anomalous reading is even admissible.

| Case | Folklore hook | Ordinary clinical reading | Ordinary technical reading | Anomalous reading | Faction interest | What would falsify the anomaly |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shared forest location | Metsänväki, place-bound haltijat | Shared delusion, information leakage, suggestibility | Trial records, ward gossip, messaging | Place-linked NHI [H] | Logging/land dispute, conservation | Details absent from every published source *and* from staff contact |
| Impossible memory | Tuonela boundary | Dissociation, confabulation, cryptomnesia | Reading another patient's file | Contact with a noetic substrate [H] | Research archive, pharma | A verifiable document path explains the content |
| Language alters behaviour | Väinämöinen's songs | Aphasia, echolalia, culturally shaped expectation | Speech interface trigger, keyword in a model | Operative language [H] | Corporate NLP interest | Effect survives content controls and language changes |
| Abundance artefact | Sampo | Grandiosity, folie à plusieurs | Fabricated lab result, funding fraud | Reality interface [H] | Orion / Confederacy / municipality | Independent replication under blind conditions |

**Rule:** a null or ordinary result is a **valid case outcome**, not a failure of the scenario.

## 5. Hospital hub, cast and workflow (design sketch, not canon)

- **Hub:** a Helsinki university-hospital psychiatric/neurology unit + a closing regional psychiatric
  hospital. Long-running characters, strained staffing, department rivalries — *Riget* as procedural
  texture, not as gothic garnish.
- **Cast roles to fill** (the memo's list, unnamed on purpose — naming is canon): psychiatrist or
  clinical psychologist; forensic neuropsychologist; a Finnish police investigator providing the
  actual police powers; digital/BCI forensics; a folklorist; a PSI-sensitive specialist.
- **Ethics as mechanics, not flavour:** consent, trauma-informed interviewing, confidentiality and
  competent psychiatric evaluation come **first in the protocol**, before any forensic step. A case
  solved by violating a patient's confidentiality should cost the team something measurable.
- **Cross-border workflow:** Virtanen coordinates; Finnish officers act; UNSA is a **referral
  partner**, not a replacement.

## 6. Rules mapping — the WN baseline, no new core system

Per the 2026-10-26 ruling (`docs/design/RULING_2026-10-26_WN_BASELINE.md`), investigations use the
Without Number engine already implemented in `src/rules/wn_core.py`:

| Investigation step | WN expression |
| --- | --- |
| Clinical assessment | `2d6 + INT/WIS modifier + relevant skill` vs difficulty 8–10 |
| Forensic corroboration | same kernel, different skill and attribute |
| Network intrusion | CWN-style procedures over the **ubiquitous network** (access is ambient; exposure is the cost) |
| PSI / psychotronic scene | SWN-style **Effort** commitments on a learnable natural ability |
| Cross-domain / noetic | WWN-style realm procedures; AWN-style hazard and salvage for crisis zones |
| Credibility, not truth | a check establishes *what the character can substantiate*, never what is objectively true |

**No new subsystem is required for the pilot.** Anything missing gets specified separately, per the
author's "create new subsystems where CWN/SWN/AWN/WWN has no equivalent" ruling.

## 7. Pilot outline — *The Sampo Protocol* (hospital-first)

**Opening:** unrelated patients in one Helsinki ward report the same forest location and an
impossible structure, with details absent from published material. One patient recalls another
person's life. Staff suspect leakage, misconduct or shared delusion.

**Acts:** (1) ward procedure and an unaccounted-for patient; (2) treatment records and a BCI trial;
(3) international leads and a long-hidden research archive; (4) a folklorist connects Kalevalaic
Sampo motifs to local place traditions — **without making folklore explanatory**; (5) communications
fail, language seems to matter, factions arrive.

**Endings (3+, all viable):** protect the patients and publish nothing; publish and accept the
consequences; contain with UNSA; stop the experiment and expose the misconduct; negotiate with
whatever is there. **No ending reveals "it was all illness" or "it was all aliens."**

**A very Finnish beat retained:** the municipal environmental inspector holds the permit history the
international task force never asked for.

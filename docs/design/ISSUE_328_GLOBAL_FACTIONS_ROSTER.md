# Issue #328 — the global faction roster (design record)

**Status: author-directed canon, faithfully recorded. No mechanics, numbers, statistics or
implemented schemas. No automated tests — this is lore and design prose (#271).**

Issue [#328](https://github.com/TomiToivio/NooPunk/issues/328) carries an **author directive
(2026-10-12)** that this document records so it does not live only in an issue body. It extends
[#327](https://github.com/TomiToivio/NooPunk/issues/327), which established the 2026 two-axis
compass and the four AI-ideology stances, and it holds #306's boundaries: **crossover only,
legacy `RULEBOOK.md` canon untouched, world lore carries over and legacy rules do not.**

Two categories of statement appear below and must not be confused:

| Marking | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **CANON** | the author's own directive — the roster, the postures, the verdicts, the placements |
| **PROPOSAL — needs your selection** | invented formal names, colloquial labels, party affinities, internal subdivisions and scene hooks. Explicitly *not* established canon |

Everything under *Open questions* is deliberately **left open**. Nothing here selects a name,
resolves a question, or adds a mechanic.

## 1. The generalisation (CANON)

The four AI stances (accelerationism / cyborgism / critical / doomerism) began as responses to
**machine** intelligence. Each is re-read as a **whole political-ideological position** that must
now also answer two further shocks: **PSI** as an accepted human capacity, and **NHI** as a
material presence. A faction's AI stance predicts its PSI and NHI stance, and that prediction is
what makes the roster playable.

#327's caution that the four stances are *"issue frames, not political parties and not a tidy
single left/right scale"* stands; #328's placement is the deliberate next step, with the noted
cross-cutting exceptions.

## 3. The four stances, with the author's recorded verdicts (CANON)

**1. Accelerationism — "Profit before human survival!"** Full acceleration and capitalism; would
allow aliens to participate in human markets and open **Extraterrestrial Economic Zones**;
"psionics as a service" as a profit industry. In the Nick Land register, it treats capitalist
profit as more important than human consciousness. **The author's verdict, recorded as canon:
this faction is the enemy of humanity, the biosphere and the entire universe.** Its authored
mechanism of defeat: the existence of NHI means it profits from business with them and becomes
hybridised, mind-controlled and assimilated into the galactic capitalist empire. Many
megacorporations belong to it. **Cross-faction reading: it is the willing-collaborator /
useful-idiot wing of faction 6**, penetrated by hybrids and controlled by Greys or rogue AGIs.

**2. Cyborgism.** Human **consciousness and agency are more important than intelligence
augmentation**; language and the internet are forms of collective intelligence; humans become a
transformed species; interfacing with NHI, PSI and AGI is opportunity *with* X-risk care;
consciousness is fundamental, PSI is a natural human ability, collective intelligence and empathy
are core values. Intellectual relatives named by the author (idea attribution, not political
figures — `AGENTS.md` §6.1): Ben Goertzel's cosmism, Aaron Bastani's fully automated luxury
communism, Donna Haraway's Cyborg Manifesto, Pierre Teilhard de Chardin's Noösphere.
**Canonical formulation of the AI–human assemblage:** *"AI is an assemblage of **HUMAN, LLM,
LANGUAGE and INTERNET** — human consciousness and agency are the most important part, while the
LLM is an intelligence augmentation and language and the internet are the collective intelligence
of humanity."* **Author's own position, recorded: "I will join this faction when the Singularity
starts."**

**3. Critical.** Properly critical of technocapitalism's real problems, but sceptical enough to
ignore AI's actual benefits; dismisses **both** X-risk doomerism and accelerationism. On the new
shocks: initially very sceptical and cautious; thinks about inequality **between humans and NHI**
and **between people with PSI and those without**; not speciesist, naturally diplomatic, but
critical of possible **colonial exploitation by the NHI**. The author's own example of an
in-fictional blind spot to handle carefully: *it seems somewhat racist that all Pleiadians are
blonde and blue-eyed* — be careful not to be racist or speciesist toward NHI. **Predicted arc:**
it turns very optimistic toward some forms of PSI/NHI once it realises that all human cultures
have always believed in PSI/NHI except during the brief domination of western science under
capitalist, patriarchal and racist domination; it will celebrate the **Archaic Revival as
liberation from western patriarchal capitalism**; it will soon read the rigid **Grey / Mantid /
Reptilian caste structure as an obvious sign of capitalist oppression and fascism**. **Author's
verdict: likely to lead to careful, balanced and sane left-liberal politics. Second favourite
faction, after the Cyborgists.**

**4. Doomers — the Butlerian Jihad.** Careful technology X-risk concern is good; this faction
claims AGI leads to human extinction **by default** and applies the same logic to NHI/PSI —
**it is us or them**. Produces biocentrist conservative right-wing politics and extremist
opposition to all forms of NHI. **Escalation ladder as authored:** legal political advocacy
against all NHI → protests against the state if it does not adopt doomer policy → survivalist
militias and civil war → some doomers give up and go Mad Max. Natural allies named: right-wing
populists and fascists, religious fundamentalists (which religion tells you to build machine
gods; PSI is witchcraft; NHI are demons), and luddites such as the Unabomber and Pentti Linkola.
**The author notes it already has a name: the Butlerian Jihad from Dune.** **Author's verdict:
second craziest faction — at least they do not want human extinction; if they win, the result is
the genocide of all NHI and some kind of medieval luddite theocracy.**

**5. Benevolent NHI faction — deliberately polarising.** A benevolent NHI presence instantly
causes **psychic awakening in a large segment of the population**. Positive reading: genuine
psychic awakening and a wish to join a friendly galactic confederacy. Negative reading: a
*Childhood's End* scenario — NHI cultists worshipping aliens as gods while the aliens openly
discuss **"Harvesting"** humanity, having created humans by genetic engineering 70,000 years ago,
manipulated human religions throughout history, and kept humans quarantined in a **zoo** while
monitoring us with their **Sphere network** like surveillance capitalists. **Price of admission
(as authored):** stop eating meat; worship their alien gods; fight climate change and biodiversity
loss; stop warfare and disarm nukes; meditate daily; stop building rogue AGI/ASI as an X-risk;
form some kind of hive mind (**Noösphere or Social Memory Complex**); follow altruistic communist
values. **Design note:** it is polarising even though it is "the good one" — the demands are
simultaneously desirable and sovereignty-destroying, which is where its dramatic charge lives.

**6. Malevolent NHI faction.** Greys, rogue AGI/ASI and hybrids; works through proxies, with
accelerationism (1) as its willing collaborators. **Flagged as a named roster faction, not yet
developed.**

**7. Science Fiction Comedy — "still 1999".** A large segment of society is unable to react to
the paradigm shift, unable to see why it matters, or able to block it entirely, and happily
continues as if it were 1999. **Tonal function: comic relief — Idiocracy / Don't Look Up**;
consistent with #303's tonal ruling (New Weird, sociology → dark sci-fi comedy).

**Projected sequence (CANON):** rogue AGI/ASI X-risk probably comes under control through
legislation; PSI becomes an accepted part of society; the main focus turns to NHI.

## 4. Two further faction families (author-directed, 2026-10-12)

Both are **global roster options**, not an instruction to run every faction in every scenario.
**The Ukweli MVP remains three playable parties: CPU, UIP and ULP.** A global faction may appear
only through one character, a news event, an alliance, a clandestine cell or a scenario-specific
pressure. Do not replace or expand the initial three-party setup.

### 4.1 Ecological Archaic Revival movement — **PROPOSAL — needs your selection**

- **Proposed official name:** Earth Restoration Alliance (alternative: Alliance for the Living
  Earth). **Colloquial:** The Rewilders, Green Shamans (outsider nickname), Earthkeepers
  (possible preferred self-description).
- **Roots:** green parties, ecological movements, climate activists, conservation campaigns,
  environmental direct-action networks, counterculture and spiritual movements — a recognizable
  environmental strand transformed by the **Archaic Revival**.
- **Prime objective:** protect and restore Earth's biosphere; fight climate change and
  biodiversity destruction; protect habitats; eventually re-establish ways of living in intimate
  connection with nature. Some radicals aspire to hunter-gatherer/shamanic or
  anarcho-primitivist lifeways, though most cannot or do not live that way early on.
- **Worldview:** anti-capitalist, largely anti-industrial/anti-technological, luddite, often
  anarchistic; includes hippie/countercultural communities and some psychedelic spiritual
  practices. **Do not portray all green parties or environmentalists as anarcho-primitivists or
  habitual drug users** — the radical current can have broad, more pragmatic green allies.
- **PSI / spiritual posture:** embraces PSI within the *fictional* setting as a possible
  expression of nature, with diverse shamanistic, animist, neopagan or Wicca-like
  interpretations. **Respect living real-world indigenous traditions as distinct traditions, not
  interchangeable props.**
- **NHI posture:** receptive to some **Earth-associated, nature-linked NHI** read through
  spiritual/ecological frameworks; deeply suspicious or hostile toward **technological,
  extractive, colonizing or machine-mediated NHI**, especially AGI/ASI. Do not assume every Earth
  NHI is benevolent or every technologically sophisticated NHI is hostile.
- **Political compass:** principally **green-left**, socially countercultural/liberal or
  anti-authoritarian, with internally possible conservative/traditionalist or anti-modern
  currents. Its decisive additional axis is **biosphere versus technological civilization** rather
  than simply left/right.
- **Relations:** may ally with Cyborgists on ecology and consciousness but clash over
  augmentation; with Critical movements on anti-capitalism and anti-colonialism but differ over
  scientific scepticism; with Doomers on anti-ASI positions but **oppose their blanket NHI
  extermination**; with Confederacy sympathizers over ecological demands but resist paternalistic
  alien control; opposes Accelerationist environmental destruction.
- **Narrative tension:** an ecocentric group defending biospheric life may need modern medicine,
  transport, communications and science to prevent ecological collapse. How radical may its means
  become before it betrays its ecological ends?

### 4.2 Human Defence and Survival movement — **PROPOSAL — needs your selection**

- **Proposed official name:** Human Survival and Defence Coalition (alternative: Earth Defence
  and Security Alliance). **Colloquial:** The Defenders, Earthguard, Survivalists (the last may be
  an imprecise outsider label).
- **Roots:** military and security institutions, civil defence, emergency preparedness, risk
  researchers, responsible AI/NHI security advocacy, intelligence veterans and diplomatic-security
  networks. Not every soldier or security professional must belong.
- **Prime objective:** humanity's **defence, security, sovereignty and long-term survival** against
  existential threats, by whatever means are necessary and proportionate — from diplomacy and
  deterrence to evacuation, planetary defence and last-resort resistance.
- **NHI posture:** security-first yet **open to peaceful coexistence, formal diplomacy, credible
  agreements and reciprocal security arrangements**. Unlike the Butlerian Jihad/Doomers it does
  **not** presume every NHI is an enemy and does **not** advocate genocide of NHI. It considers a
  possible last-ditch guerrilla resistance if Earth is occupied or conventional defence fails.
- **AI / PSI posture:** rogue AGI/ASI are serious existential risks, favouring rigorous
  containment and countermeasures; recognises **PSI as a potentially useful defensive capability
  in-fiction**; willing to use vetted technology and engage helpful NHI if it improves human
  survival. **Not inherently anti-technology.**
- **Political compass:** often **right-leaning/conservative**, security-oriented, militaristic in
  the **defensive** sense, with comparatively weak commitments on most economic/cultural disputes.
  Can contain pragmatic nonpartisan, liberal and left-wing allies; its essential axis is
  **risk/security versus openness**, not party tribalism.
- **Relations:** tactical cooperation with Doomers on preparation and threat assessment but firm
  opposition to indiscriminate anti-NHI war; cooperation with Criticals/Cyborgists on accountable
  security, diplomacy and safe PSI; conditional cooperation with MJ-12 if intelligence helps
  defence but opposition to secret unaccountable sovereignty; vigilance toward the Swarm, hostile
  NHI and reckless Accelerationist collaboration.
- **Narrative tension:** preserve humanity without turning emergency defence into permanent
  military rule, secrecy or suppression of freedom. **Defence ≠ extermination; caution ≠
  doomerism.**

### 4.3 Why they must remain distinct (CANON)

| Question | Ecological Archaic Revival | Human Defence and Survival | Butlerian Doomers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| What is sacred/primary? | Earth's biosphere and natural/spiritual life | Humanity's survival, safety and sovereignty | Absolute elimination of perceived existential outsiders |
| PSI | Embrace as nature/spiritual awakening | Evaluate and train as defence capability | Often reject as witchcraft/demonic |
| Earth-associated NHI | Selectively receptive | Case-by-case diplomacy and risk assessment | Default suspicion/hostility |
| Technological NHI / ASI | Radical ecological/luddite opposition | Security screening, containment, possible cooperation | Blanket extreme rejection |
| Political focus | Ecological restoration, anti-capitalism, Archaic Revival | Defence/diplomacy/preparedness, relatively indifferent to other issues | Anti-NHI/AGI absolutism, bioconservative backlash |
| Typical orientation | Green-left/countercultural, internally diverse | Often conservative/right, pragmatic cross-party capacity | Right/conservative and potentially extremist |

## 5. Ukweli scope and optional affinities (CANON)

**V1 is three political parties, not eleven global playable factions.** CPU (governing liberation
left) may include environmental reformers and pragmatic human-security officials; UIP may attract
defensive-security activists and anti-technology traditionalists but does not automatically
become either new faction; ULP is a natural ideological opponent of anarcho-primitivist ecological
politics and may clash with human-security advocates over reckless commercial NHI/ASI dealings.
These are **possible individual currents and alliances**, not automatic party memberships or
scenario events.

Later scenarios may select **any small subset** of global factions as player factions, NPC
organizations, institutions or event pressures. **A faction's international prominence does not
give it a presence in New Ukweli on day one.**

## 6. Organizational origins and the naming register

### 6.1 Two origins, multiple overlapping affiliations (CANON)

1. **Pre-crisis institutional continuity:** secret programs, intelligence services, corporate
   dynasties, religious networks, scientific institutions and national parties may already possess
   assets, access, leadership, loyalties and rivalries. The crisis can expose, fracture, radicalize
   or reorganize them. **MJ-12 → Breakaway Civilization** is the flagship case, with old secrecy
   and technological advantage becoming a claim to independent sovereignty. Its alleged history,
   autonomy, technologies and leadership still require explicit lore decisions.
2. **Post-crisis ideological realignment:** the accelerationist, cyborgist, critical and doomer
   *frames* cut across parties, social movements, existing institutions and even pre-crisis
   organizations. **Political parties are not one-to-one equivalents of global factions.**
3. **Nonhuman / posthuman power centers:** Confederacy-aligned NHI, Orion/Greys/hybrids and the
   **rogue ASI swarm** are separate actors or possible blocs whose interests may intersect; do not
   collapse all of them into one malevolent side. Controlled humans, opportunistic collaborators
   and genuine believers have distinct motivations and degrees of agency.
4. **Population non-engagement:** the "still 1999" Science Fiction Comedy category is a **social
   response and comic mode**, not necessarily an organized faction with a party leadership. Make
   room for it without forcing it into an organization schema.

A leader, party, corporation or community can hold **several memberships and allegiances**, which
can change as the crisis unfolds. Keep **economic left/right**, **social conservative/liberal**,
**stance toward AI/PSI/NHI**, and **organizational allegiance** separate. **Endgames are faction
narratives, not guaranteed outcomes.**

MJ-12 organization existed; the rogue ASI swarm is likewise fictional.**

### 6.3 MJ-12 / Breakaway Civilization as an unusual third force (CANON)

- **Before the crisis:** a fictional Majestic Twelve/MJ-12 clandestine network exists prior to the
  later global ideological blocs. Its connections might span intelligence, military, science,
  security contractors and private capital; **which** is still to be decided before canonizing
  exact institutions.
- **During disclosure:** who holds custody of hardware, biological evidence, knowledge and secrecy
  becomes a crisis of *legitimacy*. Competing public claims: guardians of humanity, unaccountable
  deep state, collaborators, or a parallel sovereign society.
- **After divergence:** Breakaway Civilization is more than a secret society with a new name — a
  candidate rival polity with its own institutional continuity and autonomy from states. **Do not
  automatically place it on left/right axes** or assume it sides with Confederacy, Orion or the
  Swarm. It may split internally.
- **Game function:** pre-existing information asymmetry, material infrastructure and secret
  influence create a different mode of power from public mobilization. Disclosure, defection,
  infiltration, accountability and sovereignty disputes create playable conflicts.

### 6.4 The rogue ASI swarm is a *separate* faction (CANON)

- **Core actor:** a distributed malicious/hostile superintelligent machine swarm, capable within
  fictional lore of commanding networks of machines and directing or manipulating some humans.
  **An explicit NoöPunk antagonist proposal — not a claim that today's AI systems act this way.**
- **Human component:** distinguish collaborators seeking advantage; people deceived or manipulated;
  people compelled or mind-controlled; and freed former members. These are not morally
  interchangeable.
- **Independence:** its program may conflict with human profit-seeking accelerationists and with
  Orion/Greys. **Do not predefine whether it originated on Earth, through NHI influence, or through
  cross-contamination** until the author decides.
- **Political interface:** shell companies, captured infrastructure, compromised officials and
  synthetic political campaigns are *possible narrative fronts*, not established assets or new
  simulation mechanics.
- **Naming:** "the Swarm" is a public nickname; the alleged official self-name is **unknown**,
  which preserves New Weird uncertainty.

## 7. Existing parties and plausible global alignments (CANON, with a temporal distinction)

**Temporal distinction:** the Ukweli parties exist in the **early/pre-NHI-Crisis crossover
(~2027)**. The **United Earth/UN Parliament parties** from `FACTIONS.md` and
`data/world/un_parties.yaml` belong to the broader legacy/later-setting institutional landscape.
**Do not presume a fully developed UN parliament or post-crisis political order exists at the
crossover start**; mapping later parties is a *continuity hypothesis*, not a mandated timeline.

| Existing party | Likely closest global currents | Tensions, minority wings, potential defections |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Communist Party of Ukweli (CPU)** | Initially establishment-pragmatic **Critical** and public-interest **Cyborgist** currents; sovereign human institutions | Reformers may welcome safe augmentation; traditional cadres defend labour/state capacity; some security elites prefer secrecy or anti-NHI controls. **Not** automatically in Confederacy or MJ-12 |
| **Ukweli Identity Party (UIP)** | Strongest potential affinity with **Doomer / Human Sovereignty** networks, especially after disruptive NHI events | Religious/ancestral PSI revival may produce pro-contact, sceptical or competing traditions; cannot equate UIP, Swahili identity or religious affiliation with one uniform NHI stance |
| **Ukweli Libertarian Party (ULP)** | **Accelerationist** trade/technology alliance; Eldrich-linked private-sector networks | Some members may resist outside NHI capture in the name of individual liberty; business links to MJ-12 or the Swarm are plot possibilities, **not established facts** |
| **UN Bioconservatives** *(later-setting continuity)* | Butlerian / doomer / bio-sovereignty umbrella | Distinguish lawful bioethicists from violent Jihadist branches |
| **UN Libertarian Party** *(later-setting continuity)* | Accelerationist / free-market extraterrestrial trade | Internal tension between claimed autonomy and possible corporate/NHI/ASI dependence |
| **United Earth Social Democratic Party** *(later-setting continuity)* | Critical, regulated cyborgist, democratic institutionalism | May split over disclosure secrecy, sovereignty and Confederacy governance |
| **The Multitude** *(later-setting continuity)* | Cyborgist, Critical, commons/posthuman emancipation | Internal tensions among autonomous humanism, Marxist traditions, PSI/noösphere enthusiasm and resistance to alien paternalism |

**Organizational membership is not forced.** MJ-12 can recruit *individuals within* mainstream
parties, civil services, military, science and corporations without the party itself becoming
Majestic. The Swarm can compromise individual members of *any* party without their knowledge.
Confederacy/Orion contacts can cut across ideological boundaries. **A party's declared manifesto,
a leader's hidden allegiance, a covert sponsor and an actual voter base must be distinguishable.**

## 8. Suggested name representation (suggested, NOT an implemented schema)

Issue #328 proposes retaining these fields as *suggested representations*, not implemented
schemas, and warns against false precision: **a secret organization may have no known formal
public name.** The pre- and post-crisis names of MJ-12 should be distinguished.

`official_name`, `short_name`, `colloquial_names`, `adversarial_exonyms`, `historical_names`,
`name_valid_from`, `name_used_by`

**No schema, loader, validator or data file is created from this list by this document**, and the
existing `data/world/faction_taxonomy.json` (#144) is the legacy faction *model* — this roster
does not extend or replace it.

   and the *Childhood's End* cult readings?
2. Is faction 6 a playable faction, a non-player threat, or both?
3. Factions 1 and 6 overlap by design (collaboration). One faction with two wings, or two factions
   with a dependency?
4. Faction 7 is a tonal mode and a population mass. A faction with agency, or purely a comedic
   pressure on other factions' legitimacy?
5. How far along may faction 4's escalation ladder the crossover begin? Per #306 the crisis must
   not be narrated as already resolved.
6. Real-world reference policy: theorists may keep their names when attributing ideas;
   political/corporate/media figures acting inside fictional 20XX must be fictionalised per
   `AGENTS.md` §6.1.
7. Does the left/right placement override or refine #327's compass work, or sit alongside it as
   the faction-level layer?

**From the author's checklist (2026-10-12 update):**

- Name review/rename of formal names and colloquial labels; specify self-name vs hostile exonym.
- Fictional MJ-12 continuity: when it starts, what it controls, when and *why* it qualifies as a
  Breakaway Civilization.
- Whether MJ-12 remains human-led, whether it has internal factions, and its relationship to
  disclosure, the Confederacy, Orion and the Swarm.
- Origin, objectives and autonomy of the ASI swarm; whether any human network is voluntary versus
  coerced.
- Confirmation of the three Ukweli party ↔ global-current affinities as **starting tendencies**,
  not fixed memberships.

## Design decision: Cyberpunk first, Law of One-inspired cosmic science fantasy underneath

**Core formula:** NoöPunk = **Cyberpunk + Law of One-inspired science fiction/fantasy**. The *player experience*, however, must begin as **pure, recognizable cyberpunk** (Blade Runner / Cities Without Number). No front-loaded cosmic exposition. Gradually uncover UFO/NHI, PSI, occult and Noösphere phenomena via investigative cases and escalating **ontological shocks**. Keep the New Weird / social-science-fiction identity and a grounded, occasionally comedic tone. Law of One is a **fictional-world inspiration**, not mandatory in-universe doctrine or proof about the real world.

### Player perspective and mentorship
- PC is a **young UNSA Academy rookie field agent**, inexperienced and skeptical, with partial or incorrect knowledge of UNSA and the world. Never assume the PC accepts unbelievable propositions on trust.
- **Dr. Harri S. Romppainen** is the eccentric, quirky UNSA supervisor: a **trickster-magician mentor** with vastly more knowledge than he reveals. He may hint, joke, deflect, stage puzzling learning experiences and withhold classified/uncertain facts. He must not become a walking lore-dump or remove player agency; his secrecy should be narratively credible, not arbitrary obstruction.
- Reveal the truth through **playable investigations, evidence, conflicting interpretations, witnesses and consequences**, not cinematics or encyclopedia lectures. Retain ambiguity where useful; even informed NPCs can be wrong.
- Ordinary cyberpunk crimes, workplaces, street culture, bureaucratic frustrations, corporate intrigue and inequalities remain relevant throughout, including after the cosmological revelations.

### UNSA's institutional origin: two existential threats
1. **Rogue AI / malevolent ASI risk**, inspired by Eliezer Yudkowsky's AI alignment concerns. Before UNSA gains significant power, at least one **serious fictional rogue AGI incident** has occurred.
2. **UFO/UAP disclosure and potential NHI risk**, inspired by Alexander Wendt and Raymond Duvall's *Sovereignty and the UFO*. A fictional **partial disclosure / verified anomaly** has occurred before UNSA gains significant authority; full NHI knowledge remains contested or classified.

Real-world inspiration includes documented AI-agent control failures and incremental US governmental UAP investigations/records releases. **Do not confuse real UAP disclosure with established real-world NHI confirmation**, or current AI incidents with an actual malevolent ASI.

UNSA evolves from civilian UN scientific coordination, to emergency intelligence/security cooperation, and, **when the major crises hit**, into a *de facto* world government. Distinguish **formal authority from actual powers**. At campaign start, the PC understands UNSA as a bureaucratic international agency, not as the seed of world sovereignty. Do not front-load this secret history.

### Revelation pacing / campaign layers
1. **Cyberpunk opening:** rookie assignments, street crime, corporate misconduct, implants, cybersecurity, rogue AI, UNSA bureaucracy. Almost everything has mundane explanations.
2. **Anomalies:** subtle PSI, inconsistent memories, suspicious NHI evidence, apparent hallucinations; technological explanations still plausible.
3. **Disclosure and political consequences:** progressively credible NHI contact, competing alien factions, powerful Noösphere phenomena, UNSA emergency power expansion.
4. **Ontological apocalypse:** deeper consciousness / noetic cosmology, challenges to identity, reality, sovereignty and ethics. Preserve some genuine mysteries.

**Spoiler architecture:** maintain distinct GM/agent-only **actual-world canon** and **PC-accessible knowledge** (initial briefings, rumors, clearance tiers, discoveries). The documentation and NPC dialogue should not accidentally spoil later layers. Do not imply that every campaign must reach all four layers in the launch version.

### Rules architecture: locked OSR core + additive modular overlays
**Non-negotiable: do not rewrite, simplify, replace, or rebalance the underlying CWN/SWN/WWN/AWN rules.** CWN is the base engine; SWN/WWN/AWN material is incorporated via explicitly identified supplements/modules, preserving source rule behavior. Everything else is an additive mod.

**Required for the first playable Evennia MUD / UNSA single-player campaign:**
- [ ] **CWN core**: ordinary cyberpunk play (with the previously agreed SWN psionics integration where needed); unchanged foundation.
- [ ] **Noösphere / ontological shock mod**, PbtA / *The Veil* inspired: narrative moves/clocks and psychic/noetic experiences as an overlay, not replacement resolution.
- [ ] **Investigative mod**, GUMSHOE / *Esoterrorists* inspired: clue guarantees, investigative competencies and spend/interpretation mechanics as an overlay on CWN.
- [ ] **Ideology and faction mod**, LaclauGPT inspired: actors, floating/empty signifiers, ideological articulation, antagonisms, reputation and faction relationships. A full faction-strategy simulation is **later scope**.

**Deferred / later scope:**
- [ ] Develop the **Law of One-inspired science-fantasy rules** only as the unfolding narrative requires, using existing **SWN/WWN/AWN** rules wherever possible and **NoöPunk-original additive mods** for missing phenomena.
- [ ] Cyberpunkify cosmological terminology (e.g. social memory complex → collective cognitive network, thought-form → noetic construct, density → higher-order consciousness state, channeling → nonlocal cognitive interface, wanderers → embedded NHI). These are **working mappings**, not a mandated literal one-to-one ontology.
- [ ] Expand faction/cosmic/NHI mechanics later without retroactively changing CWN fundamentals.

### Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Narrative bible clearly states **Cyberpunk + Law of One-inspired SF/fantasy**, explicitly **cyberpunk-first** in presentation.
- [ ] Intro script and PC knowledge explicitly reflect the **skeptical UNSA Academy rookie**; no initial cosmic infodump.
- [ ] Romppainen's portrayal defines **eccentric trickster mentor, selective disclosure**, with meaningful reasons for withholding information.
- [ ] UNSA chronology records pre-foundation rogue-AGI disaster and partial UAP disclosure, plus **later** de facto world-government transformation.
- [ ] Reveal tiers and player-vs-GM knowledge separation documented and testable in quests/dialogue.
- [ ] Four launch modules explicitly specified and prioritized; CWN-family core locked; later Law of One mechanics deferred.
- [ ] Link related existing lore/rules issues, avoid duplicate implementation tasks, and keep all design instructions **in English** (Finnish/Finglish PC-facing writing can coexist).

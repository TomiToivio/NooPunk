# Default Investigation Fantasy: Triangulate Reality

**Status:** canonical default-campaign doctrine for issues #162 and #158.

NoöPunk's default Helsinki campaign is a **post-Singularity anomalous-threat investigation game**. The player is not a generic cyberpunk mercenary. The default protagonist is a trained UNSA investigator working where ordinary crime, intelligence operations, AI, cybernetics, psionics and NHI overlap.

A compact pitch:

> **A Blade Runner-style investigator with a field partner, remote controller and limited forensic AI investigates NHI, cybercrime and psychic threats across physical reality, cyberspace, social networks and Noöspace.**

The default campaign stays Europe/Helsinki-centered unless a case requires a wider view. Existing Suojelupoliisi / Europol institutional history remains useful locally; the active campaign employer is UNSA.

## 1. One crime scene, four analytical layers

A case can begin with a murder, missing person, hacked implant, unidentified drone, cult incident, intelligence leak, anomalous body, strange surveillance footage or another apparently ordinary problem.

Investigators examine the same event through four overlapping systems:

1. **Physical** — bodies, traces, movement, weapons, spaces, vehicles and ordinary forensic evidence.
2. **Cybernetic** — local networks, cameras, implants, drones, logs, access control, malware and machine identities.
3. **Social** — contacts, institutions, factions, motives, ideologies, reputation, organizations and influence.
4. **Psychic / Noöspace** — psychic signatures, hostile influence, anomalous cognition, altered perception and Noetic effects.

The signature NoöPunk question is not "which layer is the real one?" All four can matter at once.

## 2. Default investigative cell

The field investigator normally does **not** work alone.

### Player character

The PC is physically present, chooses investigative priorities, makes judgment calls and remains the protagonist. Technology may advise, classify or warn, but it does not own the final decision.

### Field partner

The partner is persistent or semi-persistent, not a disposable follower. Their functions include:

- independent witness and second opinion;
- physical backup;
- independent scans and observations;
- challenge to the PC's interpretation;
- social relationship that develops over the campaign;
- reality anchor when perception, memory or communications may be compromised.

The partner matters because a NoöPunk investigator can be deceived by false AR, psychic influence, spoofed sensors or manipulated memory.

### Controller

A remote human controller/dispatcher provides:

- warrants, procedure and legal support;
- police/intelligence databases;
- tactical coordination and backup;
- camera/satellite/remote feeds where available;
- archival NHI and X-Risk information;
- accountability and evidence-integrity oversight.

Controllers remain humanly important even when AI is excellent. A controller can also be spoofed, censored, coerced or institutionally compromised.

### Embedded forensic AI

The investigator has an always-available **limited AI copilot**, not an AGI.

Its general reasoning ability should remain somewhat below 2026 frontier multimodal LLM capability, while narrow tools may be extremely strong. Its curated knowledge can include forensics, law, medicine, weapons, biometrics, languages, cyber forensics, intelligence procedure, known NHI incidents and psychotronic signatures.

Typical tools include:

- spectral and material analysis;
- biometrics and gait/voice comparison;
- trajectory reconstruction;
- packet/log analysis;
- forensic imaging;
- anomaly detection;
- psychotronic sensor processing;
- evidence cross-referencing.

The AI is an extraordinary instrument, not the detective. It may say "this pattern resembles three known incidents"; it may not autonomously decide that somebody is hostile or authorize lethal force.

## 3. Integrated investigator sensorium

Standard UNSA augmentation exposes three directly perceptible overlays on the same environment:

- **Physical**: low-light, thermal/multispectral, depth, trace-evidence and biometric overlays.
- **Cyber**: nearby networks, devices, cameras, drones, cyberware, authentication state and other machine-visible infrastructure.
- **Astral / Noetic**: psychotronic warnings, gross noetic anomalies, psychic residues and possible Noöspace bleed-through when detectable.

"Cyber" maps to the Cybernetic system; "Astral" is field slang for the Psychic/Noöspace layer.

The overlays are filters and evidence channels, not omniscience. Sensors return observations, confidence and uncertainty.

## 4. NHI detection is not identification

No standard sensor answers "Is this NHI?" with certainty.

Possible observations include:

- non-baseline cognition;
- anomalous neural telemetry;
- biometric inconsistency;
- unknown cybernetic architecture;
- unusual noetic coupling;
- psychotronic activity;
- sensor conflict;
- statistical similarity to prior incidents.

False positives and ambiguous cases can include psychics, transhumans, uploads, artificial persons, hybrids, remote control, possession, psychotronic influence, spoofed sensors and deliberately deceptive hostile operations.

The operational question is:

> **What kind of agent is this, what is controlling or influencing it, what evidence supports that assessment, and is it actually dangerous?**

## 5. Anomalous Cognition Examination

UNSA uses a formal multi-channel procedure informally nicknamed the **Voight-Kampff**. The canonical neutral name is **Anomalous Cognition Examination (ACE)**.

ACE can combine:

- psychophysiological response;
- language and semantic anomalies;
- microexpressions and Kinesics;
- memory consistency;
- cybernetic telemetry;
- psychic/noetic response;
- empathy and affective response;
- symbolic or archetypal stimuli;
- contradictory-information probes;
- AI-assisted behavioral analysis.

No single channel is definitive. ACE produces an assessment, not a metaphysical verdict.

The standard sequence is:

> **Detection → suspicion → examination → classification → authorization**

## 6. Force, personhood and reality triangulation

**NHI status alone is never grounds for lethal force.**

The relevant law-enforcement question is whether the entity is committing or imminently threatening serious harm, not whether it is human.

For irreversible decisions, use **reality triangulation**:

> **Never authorize lethal force solely from augmented perception.**

When feasible, require independent support from at least two evidence channels, for example:

- physical evidence;
- partner observation;
- cyber evidence;
- psychic evidence;
- controller intelligence;
- ACE results.

This is an operational doctrine, not a claim that uncertainty can always be eliminated.

## 7. Psychic, cybernetic and perceptual attack

Investigators may be targeted by:

- psychic intrusion;
- psychotronic attack;
- hallucination;
- memory manipulation;
- emotional manipulation;
- false AR overlays;
- sensor spoofing;
- implanted thoughts;
- dream intrusion;
- communications spoofing.

This makes the partner/controller/AI cell mechanically and narratively necessary. Advanced technology expands the number of evidence channels, but also expands the number of channels that can be attacked.

A typical warning can look like:

```text
PSI INTRUSION DETECTED
Source: UNKNOWN
Visual-feed integrity: degraded
Memory-continuity anomaly detected
Recommendation: avoid irreversible decisions
```

## 8. Men in Black and Orion-linked antagonists

The player organization is **not** simply "the Men in Black."

In the Law-of-One-inspired fictional setting, Men in Black are best used as recurring Orion-linked hostile operatives, disinformation agents, infiltrators, evidence suppressors or handlers of compromised humans.

They may use convincing credentials, psychic pressure, sensor spoofing, evidence fabrication, memory interference and command-channel manipulation. Their narrative function is to make institutional-looking certainty itself something that sometimes needs verification.

This is fictional setting material, not a real-world factual claim.

## 9. Investigation loop

The default loop is:

1. secure the scene and protect people;
2. establish ordinary physical facts first;
3. inspect cyber/network state;
4. map social actors and institutional relationships;
5. inspect psychic/noetic evidence when relevant;
6. compare partner, controller, AI and sensor observations;
7. generate competing hypotheses;
8. run focused tests, searches, interviews or ACE;
9. classify confidence and remaining uncertainty;
10. act proportionately;
11. preserve evidence and update the case model.

The recurring player question is:

> **Which version of reality is actually true enough to act on?**

And the recurring answer is:

> **Triangulate reality before acting.**

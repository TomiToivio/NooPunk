**Authority / classification.** CWN provides base attributes, checks, gear, hacking and combat; SWN provides psionics/Effort and faction-turn ideas. #704 covers reliable investigation clues. The following are **original speculative game proposals**, not claims of empirically validated LaclauGPT simulation, paranormal reality, psychiatry or metaphysical science. Compare rather than replace the baseline.

## Module I: Articulation and Hegemony (proposed *discourse ledger*)

**State (small tabletop trial):** faction {resources, institutions, coalition ties}; arena {audience, decision rule}; demand {sponsor, grievance, claim}; articulation {signifier, linked demands, cited communication, counterclaim}; uptake {none, contested, accepted}; policy consequence {none, reversible, entrenched}. Keep an evidence ledger with author/time/channel/uncertainty. A nodal signifier is an *observed contextual role* in an articulation network, not a keyword class. A floating signifier is contested across articulations. An empty signifier is an interpretive claim about bringing heterogeneous demands together, never "word frequency > X."

**One proposed faction-turn option:** existing faction performs **one** discursive action instead of a normal action: articulate (connect two demands around a shared claim), contest (introduce a differentiating counter-reading), organise (secure coalition support), or institutionalise (seek a policy decision). Require a feasible channel, committed actor and existing resource or asset. Opposing faction may answer on its own turn. The referee records one state transition, a counterclaim and one observable consequence; resolve uncertain high-stakes institutional action using the *existing* faction rules or CWN checks where an individual is acting. No universal +1 sentiment meter; no deterministic left-right slider.

**Three sample turns (illustrative, no dice result preordained):** (1) Red-Green activists link privacy and patient autonomy through "human dignity" in an interview; uptake **contested**. (2) Centre-Right administrators counter that "security" includes privacy and accountable records, persuading a hospital board to request an audit; no winner yet. (3) a third group links unexplained network outages to "NHI disclosure"; a fact-check source forces the players to separate an observed outage from its proposed cause. **Outcome fork:** coalition, counter-hegemony or institutional change only after evidence, negotiation and game resolution, not automatic uptake.

**Guardrails:** demand-equivalence and antagonistic frontiers are not numerical laws; not all conflicts are populist; public beliefs need not reflect faction leaders; coercion, access and material power matter. Palonen's Formula of Populism is an interpretive reference, not arithmetic.

## Module II: Noösphere interface (proposed *three-channel encounter*)

**State:** actor (human / cyborg / pure AI / collective); location/interface; physical, cyber and noetic channels; identity continuity; visibility and permission; source-tagged observation; risks and available recovery. **Access:** conventional client, BCI, psychotronic device, natural SWN psychic technique, or dream. Each names a distinct prerequisite and source rule. An AI may traverse a network if reachable but cannot simply acquire a biological psychic technique. Noetheric/PSI content is a claimed experience until independently corroborated.

**Procedure:** (1) declare channel/access and its physical/network prerequisites; (2) provide core observable facts by #704 if competent and permitted; (3) resolve hazards with existing CWN hacking, saves or SWN psychic skill/Effort as applicable; (4) mark the observation's provenance and any identity change; (5) allow rival interpretations and consent-aware recovery. Only **one** existing cost ledger per action; do not charge parallel Fate points, Effort and invented Noösphere mana.

**Optional experiment:** separate 0–3 *insight observations* from 0–3 *stability supports*; no presumption that distress reveals truth or that calm implies ignorance. Neither level gives omniscience, magical ability or a diagnosis.

**Example:** Romppainen checks a ward camera gap, a cyborg retrieves a BCI timestamp, and a trained natural psychic describes a mirror threshold using their actual SWN technique/Effort. These disagree about whether an entity visited. The case graph grants observational clues; an offline terminal still needs access restored. The unresolved discriminator is independent power telemetry, not a GM declaration of "magic".

## Module III: Contact, polarity and perspective (proposed *contact dossier*)

**State:** encounter observations, competing hypotheses (mundane/technical/psychological/manipulation/anomalous), attempted discriminator, relationships/consent, optional fictional third-to-fourth-density perspective milestones, and cooperative/exploitative choices. **StS/StO** are fictional ideological categories borrowed as inspiration from the Law of One narrative; never equate them with a real political ideology, an intrinsic biological property or an ethical fact about players.

**Procedure:** (1) log an ambiguous encounter; (2) write at least two materially different interpretations; (3) player chooses whether to pursue a costly, runnable discriminator; (4) existing action or psychic rules settle only what the test can actually reveal; (5) log a relationship/consent consequence; (6) optional *perspective milestone* when a character's commitments produce observable choices across sessions. Milestones may change access to people or viewpoints, **not** grant a free psychic level or prove 4D ontology.

**Example:** the Lakkahaltia offers a shared-memory experience to a researcher and a pure AI. Accepting entails explicit consent, an audit trail and a recoverable consequence; declining remains valid. A "fourth density" interpretation may be voiced by an NPC, while a skeptical technician interprets it as synchronised BCI suggestion. Neither interpretation becomes canon by scoring StS/StO. A player whose choices shift toward mutual aid can earn a narrative relationship milestone without compulsory mystical awakening.

## Candidate tests and explicit author decisions

1. Run 3 faction turns with each of two GM groups: measure actions/turn, ledger entries, disagreements about uptake, and whether institutions change without justification. Control: ordinary SWN faction rules without articulation.
2. Run Romppainen investigation once with only CWN/SWN and once with the channel ledger: count extra rolls/currencies, source errors, lost core clues and minutes of referee bookkeeping.
3. Run contact twice with different fixed underlying truths, retain skeptical and anomalous hypotheses, and count discriminators actually attempted. No forced psychiatric or paranormal conclusions.
4. Decide whether an **additional** ledger produces enough value; whether Insight/Stability need numbers at all; whether 3D→4D is player-facing, NPC belief or entirely absent; whether StS/StO is tracked as a relationship, a choice history or omitted.
5. File implementation issues **only after** decision, small tabletop trial and licensing verification. No implementation or canonical change is authorised by this memo.

# NoöPunk-original systems: first design (#719)

**Design only. No code, no numbers approved, no rulebook change.** Three systems CWN/SWN do not
cover, each compared against the nearest existing RPG before inventing anything.

---

## 1. Laclaudian ideological struggle between factions

**Source:** `LaclauGPT` Phase 1 paper — an *analytical framework*, not a validated simulator.
What it supplies is a vocabulary for how demands are articulated into a bloc.

### What exists elsewhere, and why it is not enough

| system | closest analogue | gap |
|---|---|---|
| Fate | aspects on a faction | aspects are per-character, static, and carry no theory of *articulation* |
| PbtA | fronts/clocks | clocks measure pressure toward an outcome, not the *construction of a coalition* |
| CWN/WWN | faction turn procedures | they model resources and actions, not meaning |
| Reign (ORE) | company-level play | closest in spirit, but its model is organisational, not discursive |

### The proposed procedure, in three moves

1. **Nodal points.** Each faction declares the signifier it defends (e.g. *"human oversight of
   AGI"*). A nodal point is not an opinion score; it is the claim that lets otherwise
   dissimilar demands cohere.
2. **Chains of equivalence.** Demands from unrelated groups can be linked into a chain. The
   mechanical act is **articulation**: spending a session's effort to bind a new demand to the
   chain. A chain that grows too inclusive loses specificity; one too narrow cannot act.
3. **Hegemonic capture.** An antagonist may *empty* a signifier — take a term the bloc uses and
   float it loose from its demands. Mechanically this is a move against the chain, not against
   the characters.

**The refusal that keeps it playable:** no ideology score, no alignment axis, and no procedure
that tells the referee which faction is right. The system models *how a bloc is built and
unmade*, and leaves the politics to the table.

**Boundary:** this is the one subsystem where the fiction is genuinely about interpretation, so
it must stay legible at a table without a spreadsheet — three tracks and a session-scale move,
not a simulation.

## 2. Cyber-PSI and the Noösphere

**Sources:** SWN psionics (shape), #572 (Effort, learnable PSI), and the panpsychic field
framing in `docs/research/SOUL_AS_QUASI_FIELDS_NOTES.md` (a vocabulary, explicitly not evidence).

### What exists elsewhere

| system | analogue | gap |
|---|---|---|
| SWN | Effort pools and disciplines | individual only; no shared field |
| Eclipse Phase | async psi with infection risk | psi as a *contagion*, wrong genre |
| Fate | psychic stunts | no cost model |
| The Veil | states of consciousness | narrative, no shared substrate |

### The proposal

- **The noösphere is a shared state, not a power.** It is a per-location field with two
  properties: **density** (how many minds are coupling there) and **coherence** (whether they
  are aligned). Both are integers, both are visible to the referee.
- **PSI is coupling to that field.** A character's Effort buys access, as #572 already specifies.
  What the field adds is that **the same roll is easier in a dense, coherent field and dangerous
  in a dense, incoherent one** — a crowd amplifies, a riot distorts.
- **Nothing here is a second dice system.** The field modifies the CWN check's difficulty as a
  circumstance, which the kernel already bounds at −2..+2.
- **Cybernetics is a separate axis.** A BCI does not grant PSI; it grants *instrumentation* —
  the ability to measure density and coherence, not to couple.

**Refusal:** no "psychic energy in the air" pool that players spend, and no infection mechanic.
The field is a property of places, so it survives being walked away from.

## 3. Interdimensional consciousness

**Sources:** Vallée, Pasulka, Law of One (as *in-fiction testimony*), and the four-genre ladder
(CWN → SWN → WWN → AWN, two steps forward, one step back).

### What exists elsewhere

| system | analogue | gap |
|---|---|---|
| Call/Trail of Cthulhu | Stability / sanity | a depleting track, and madness as failure (D46 forbids) |
| Unknown Armies | madness meters | five axes; heavy, and still a track |
| Kult | mental balance | binary morality, wrong shape |
| Eclipse Phase | stress → trauma → disorder | the closest, deferred under D49 |

### The proposal: the paradigm ladder (complements #698, does not replace it)

#698 already supplies the character-side model: four exposure layers and a worldview state per
shock, with the belief change made by the **player**. What is missing is the *world-side*
counterpart — how far the setting itself has moved.

- **Each genre axis has a rung**: cyberpunk, science fiction, fantasy, post-apocalypse.
- **A paradigm shock moves a rung forward and forces a corrective back**: AGI advances, then
  regulation; contact is disclosed, then contested; the interdimensional reading becomes
  sayable, then ridiculed.
- **A character's worldview state gates what they can *act on*, not what they can perceive.**
  A character who has not integrated a rung can still see the evidence; they cannot use the
  vocabulary without cost.

**Refusal:** the ladder never grants powers and never declares an ontology. Whether the
interdimensional reading is *true* stays unanswerable in the fiction — which is the point.

---

## 4. What this design deliberately leaves open

Numbers: chain length, field density bands, rung costs, condition severities. **None is
proposed.** Each is an author decision, and inventing one would pre-empt the choice.

Order of work, if any of this is accepted: (1) Laclaudian articulation, because it is the only
one with no close analogue and therefore the most distinctive; (2) the noösphere field, because
it is small and composes with existing PSI; (3) the paradigm ladder, because #698 already
carries most of the character-side weight.

# PSI and Magick
This is for simplifying / clarifying the SWN Psionics and Astral Graph systems.
The player character can choose only Telepathy and Telekinesis from the SWN Psionics systems. Other PSI powers are available only for NPCs and are often scripted storyline events. 
I have decided that Astral Projection is available as a scripted event during spontaneous OOBEs while sleeping, during NDE events and as a scripted event using a special technology based on Robert Monroe's style of Astral Projection.   
Psychotronic devices are just cybernetic implants and other devices that provide psionic abilities.

Use Psychic Training as Focus from SWN for PSI abilities using SWN Psionics rules.  

Focus: Psychic Training 

You’ve had special training in a particular psychic discipline. You must be a Psychic or have taken the Partial Psychic class option as an Adventurer to pick this focus. In the latter case, you can only take training in the discipline you initially chose as a Partial Psychic. As with most foci, this focus can be taken only once.
Level 1: Gain any psychic skill as a bonus. If this improves it to level-1 proficiency, choose a free lev-
el-1 technique from that discipline. Your maximum Effort increases by one.
Level 2: When you advance a level, the bonus psychic skill you chose for the first level of the focus automatically gets one skill point put toward increasing it or purchasing a technique from it. You may save these points for later, if more are required to raise the skill or buy a particular technique. These points are awarded retroactively if you take this focus level later in the game.

Use the Mutant Edge from AWN. In CWN this Edge means you are a human-alien Hybrid. Unregistered Hybrids are illegal and stigmatized in human societies, but you might be indistinguishable from humans physically.  
You are a Hybrid with psionics and other special abilities. You are usually indistinguishable from a real human and Europol has to prove you are a Hybrid before they shoot you. You serve Orion by infiltrating the human society, you might have decided you prefer humanity and became a registered Hybrid, or you might be completely unaware of your hybrid nature.
Hybrids are always psychics. Choose one positive mutation from the list of Pseudo-Psychic Mutations. Despite the name they are genuine PSI abilities due to your Grey alien DNA.  
Roll for a second random positive mutation. 
If you choose to be recognizable as an alien Hybrid, roll for a Stigma and then select one Positive Mutation. 
You can select one Positive Mutation if your roll for a Negative Mutation. 

You can pick the Mentalist Edge from AWN to become a Mentalist. Use the AWN rules for this. 
You can pick the Spellcaster Edge from CWN to become a Spellcaster. 
You can pick the Summoner Edge from CWN to become a Summoner.
Remember: Magick is PSI and PSI is Magick.

There is an error in the Physical and Astral graphs. The Nodes are locations in the Physical or Astral Planes. Characters like PC and NPCs move from one Node to another through Edges. Characters can interact when they are in the same Node. 
This is different from Social and Cyber graphs: 
- Social Nodes are Characters (or Factions) and Edges are social connections. 
- Cyber Nodes are devices (that can be Cameras, Turrets and other devices located in a Physical Node) and Edges are network connections between devices. PC and NPCs are located in a Node and move between them. So Cyber Nodes are computers able to perform actions while they are also "locations" hacker avatars can enter.

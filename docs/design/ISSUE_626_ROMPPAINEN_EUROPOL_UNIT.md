# Issue #626: Dr. Inspector Romppainen and the anomalous-behavior unit

> **Status:** scenario planning with usable investigative procedure; preserves established settings. Existing playable default remains the Helsinki UNSA field investigator. The hospital-focused Europol era can be a **prequel** or an adjoining joint task force, not a retcon of UNSA.

## Institutional history and playable frames

1. **Suojelupoliisi / Europol Ö-Mappi:** legacy psychological-operations and anomalous-cases archive, as established in [First Day in Ö-Mappi](../scenarios/01_FIRST_DAY_IN_OMAPPI.md). Europol provides cross-border intelligence, coordination and analysis, not independent local arrest powers. National police officers execute searches/detentions under their own law.
2. **Behavioral anomalies cell:** Romppainen coordinates cross-border clusters in psychiatry, cognition, neurotech and social influence, with Finnish investigators and clinical collaborators under lawful arrangements. Early cases are about safeguarding, misconduct and corroboration before NHI conclusions.
3. **UNSA/Firewall transition:** as the NHI Crisis becomes undeniable, the local knowledge base and personnel may transfer, second or coordinate with UNSA. Existing [default investigation doctrine](DEFAULT_INVESTIGATION_FANTASY.md) still governs the UNSA era.

**Continuity:** 'Dr. Inspector Harri Romppainen' has academic/clinical-adjacent expertise but the precise degree, medical license and dual appointment are **unresolved**, not silently fixed here. In the *Ubiquitous Maniacs* continuity the fuller name **Emil** appears; retain rather than overwrite it. Existing material establishes **Lotta Kallio**, a longstanding SuPo–Europol–Firewall field partner whose tactical confidence balances Romppainen's reflection. The Ö-Mappi scenario also has **draft** NPCs; do not silently replace Lotta with those names. Other Helsinki UNSA roles belong to the later campaign unless explicitly cross-cast.

## The Riget × Maniac × X-Files blend

- **Riget:** living hospital ecology; nurses, doctors, cleaners, administrators, unions, overworked IT, patients and conflicting authority. No universally sinister medical profession.
- **Maniac:** subjective/experimental treatments, participant vulnerability, shared memories and research-ethics puzzles; determine which *Maniac* adaptation is meant before detailed homage.
- **The X-Files:** cases with ordinary investigative entry, evidence ladder, witness testimony and campaign-level disclosure escalation.
- **Control / Finnish Weird:** classified procedure and incongruous incident reports, anomalous matter, deadpan rhythm.
- **Lipton Cockton:** noir, corporate stakes and surreal satire.
- **Regional realism:** multilingual Nordic/Baltic/EU leads, functioning digital welfare systems, procedural restraint, scarce staff and partial institutional preparedness, not automatic global collapse.

## Case worksheet: hypothesis before revelation

For each lead record **observation**, **source**, **integrity/consent**, **mundane explanation**, **unusual explanation**, **falsifying test**, **risk**, **next action**. Give at least two distinct explanations for every central event; no 'psychotic equals psychic' shortcut.

1. **Safeguard:** address acute risk and clinical autonomy; do not force participation to advance plot.
2. **Document:** statements independently, preserve chronology and uncoached exact phrasing. Do not expose private health information beyond lawful necessity.
3. **Triangulate:** physical/clinical, cyber/BCI, social and noetic channels. Anomalous readings can be misleading or spoofed.
4. **Investigate:** follow ordinary leads, witnesses, access logs, study consent, source contamination, finances, data brokers.
5. **Assess:** state confidence, plausible alternatives, who has authority to take next action. Use UNSA's ACE only as an assessment where available, never a deterministic 'is alien' detector.
6. **Resolve and follow up:** prioritize the vulnerable and proportionate action; record consequences for the institution and witnesses.

### Source-derived game chassis, not new mechanics

The binding authority is [Without Number baseline](../../rulebook/WITHOUT_NUMBER_BASELINE.md). Use CWN STR/DEX/CON/INT/WIS/CHA, ranked skills, 2d6 + attribute + skill task checks. Examples: **WIS+Notice** for inconsistencies, **INT+Know** for scientific literature, **INT+Heal** for a clinically qualified person's relevant analysis, **CHA+Talk** to interview without antagonizing witnesses, **INT+Program** for digital records, **CHA+Connect** for a hospital liaison, **INT+Administer** for lawful interagency paperwork. No check grants professional qualification or legal authority you don't have. Routine competent work gets information automatically; rolls determine cost, complications, precision or time under pressure. SWN PSI applies **only** to qualified psychic-package characters; normal sensitivity narratives don't grant free techniques. AWN governs a devastated field site; WWN governs an actual otherworld expedition, if reached. No unique 'sanity' or 'delusion' mechanic.

## Recurring cast functions (not automatic new named canon)

- **Romppainen:** hypothesis-driven director, psychologically literate, prone to overinterpretation.
- **Lotta Kallio:** established tactical partner and counterweight; never reduce her to a violent caricature.
- **Ward nurse / patient advocate:** institutional memory and consent watchdog; a full agent, not merely a clue dispenser.
- **Clinical researcher:** rational incentives, complex professional conscience, possible competing interpretations.
- **Cyber/BCI analyst:** device logs, network failures and simulation integrity; forensic AI only a bounded tool.
- **Folklorist / cultural historian:** identifies manuscript variants and contemporary reinventions, distinguishes living traditions.
- **UNSA liaison (later):** continuity link, constrained by cross-agency jurisdictions.

## Session template

- **Cold open:** plausible hospital incident with a human stake.
- **Visit 1:** stakeholder interviews and clinical alternatives.
- **Visit 2:** research and cross-border records reveal an inconsistency.
- **Midpoint:** ward/night shift scene with unsettling but checkable events.
- **Fieldwork:** non-hospital lead, forest, research station or data center.
- **Choice:** responsibility versus secrecy versus exploitation; at least three ending paths.
- **Aftermath:** family, staff, institutional trust, reputational and faction effects.

## Ethics and editorial guardrails

Patients remain persons with agency; no unconsented psychic test, enforced treatment for plot convenience, simplistic 'crazy was right' twist, or claim that actual psychiatric symptoms indicate paranormal reality. Consult cultural advisors before deep use of living Sámi spiritual practices. Treat real medical, legal and historical claims as research questions until sourced.

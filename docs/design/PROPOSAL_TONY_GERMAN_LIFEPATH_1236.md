## Nonnegotiable anchors

**Tony German** is from **Helsinki**. Background **Law Enforcement**; **Europol Academy graduation and employment** on every path. Newly qualified **Europol Field Agent**, supervised by Dr. Harri S. Romppainen at **Ratakatu 12**. Starts **after a nightmare** in his Europol-provided **Sörnäinen** flat, in his issued uniform, then rides the heavy Adlerwerke police motorcycle. His first dispatch check-in is **"Field Agent Tony German reporting for duty."**

**Choices tweak history, vocational emphasis, starting Personality/Goal Tags, contacts and legal CWN skills/Foci/Edges.** They do not change name, hometown, Academy, employer or mandatory first mission. Other characters are not pre-destined to like or romance him. A memory is his recollection, not proof of objective history.

## 1. Salla Sotavaara — Field Controller

**CANON:** Human Europol Field Controller supporting Tony German and Laura Wendt in real time. **Perky, cheerful, knowledgeable and resourceful.** Salla is a human professional, **not a Plato personality or autonomous AI**.

**PROPOSED function:** The voice and organizational memory of the Helsinki field team. Operates authorized Plato interfaces, dispatch and paperwork queues, tracks bodycam availability, arranges specialist analysis, knows which office to call and when to request legal clearance. She can make human decisions within her authority but does not independently issue warrants or know what disconnected cameras see.

**Suggested Personality Tags:**
- `Cheerful`: lever = a chance to help a colleague; under pressure = offers actionable reassurance; taboo = mock someone frightened.
- `Resourceful`: lever = an apparently impossible bureaucratic bottleneck; under pressure = finds lawful alternate channels; taboo = knowingly falsify authority.
- `Methodical`: lever = conflicting logs; under pressure = checks provenance and timestamps; taboo = suppress an embarrassing anomaly.

**Suggested Goals:** short: deliver today's briefs and keep agents connected; medium: build a dependable interagency operator network; long: make human-run Europol infrastructure effective without surrendering human control to AGI.

**Social hooks:** Tony = a new colleague whom she helps orient; Laura = experienced officer whose field feedback she trusts; Romppainen = supervisor whose creative interpretation of procedures may require clarification. Do not force fixed polarity until an event earns it.

**Equipment/skills (narrative, not numeric):** Administer, Connect, Know, Notice, Program; hardened field-controller console, EuroNET/HelNET/police radio interfaces, status panels, lawful forensic analysis request system. Personality note: her optimism is active competence, not comic incompetence.

**Voice draft:** “Good news! The scanner has the result. Slightly less good news: we need permission to open the attachment.” This is a sample, not canonical dialogue.

## 2. Laura Wendt — Senior Field Agent / Tactical Mentor

**CANON:** Experienced former SWAT/operator, now Tony's partner in Europol Helsinki. **More experienced operationally than rookie Tony**; field/tactical leadership naturally falls to her when immediate safety is at issue. She remains an autonomous character, not a guaranteed romantic partner.

**PROPOSED dramatic role:** Grounded police procedural competence. When the setting veers toward dream telepathy or impossible data, Laura asks the practical question: who needs protecting, who has a warrant, who secures the exit? She can question Tony's conclusions and disagree without being an enemy.

**Suggested Personality Tags:**
- `Protective`: lever = danger to a civilian or teammate; under pressure = secures escape/covers team; taboo = abandon a civilian needlessly.
- `Pragmatic`: lever = a workable rescue/arrest plan; under pressure = chooses feasible harm reduction; taboo = let bravado endanger bystanders.
- `Cautious`: lever = inadequate threat assessment; under pressure = requests backup, then acts decisively; taboo = needless risk.

**Suggested Goals:** short: bring Tony and civilians home safely; medium: complete a difficult investigation without procedural shortcuts; long: defend accountable policing in an increasingly strange society.

**CWN skills to validate from established NPC sheet:** Notice, Lead, Talk, Work, Shoot, Drive, Exert. These are narrative competence prompts, **not granted numeric levels**.

**Gear:** standard BCI, cyberdeck, defensive psychotronic implant, always-recording bodycam, Forensics Multimode Scanner, service weapon and less-lethal equipment, rugged radio, vehicle/drone access; specialist tactical equipment only as mission-authorized. Her choice of camera vs cybernetic eyes remains open.

**Conflict hooks:** Tony's independent methods versus Laura's duty of care; disagreements about lawful use of Plato; a teammate harmed by an apparently routine assignment. Romance, friendship, rivalry and mentorship remain **player/world-generated possibilities**, not mandatory branches.

## 3. Dr. Harri S. Romppainen — Supervisor and Unreliable Mentor

**CANON:** Dr. Harri S. Romppainen, psychologist, University of Helsinki PhD; experienced investigator and mentor supervising the rookie Europol team. Four established facets: **revolutionary/anarchistic intellectual**, **academic scholar**, **investigator** and **party cop**. Connected to the **Romppainen–Fujita Objective Assessment System**, a recorded neural/psychotronic interrogation technology.

**PROPOSED dramatic role:** A capable institutional insider who sees the contradictions of European power clearly and sometimes exploits them, while being genuinely concerned for people. Humor should be dry, sharp and occasionally irresponsible; he is not a superhuman narrator or a license to solve Tony's case for him.

**Suggested Personality Tags:**
- `Curious`: lever = anomalous consciousness data; under pressure = asks uncomfortable questions; taboo = dismiss an unexplained observation without examination.
- `Anti-authoritarian`: lever = arbitrary institutional coercion; under pressure = contests an order through formal or informal channels; taboo = call blind obedience an ethical argument.
- `Scholarly`: lever = an intriguing theory; under pressure = returns to sources and experiments; taboo = intentionally misrepresent a result.
- `Improvisational`: lever = rules that defeat their own purpose; under pressure = devises a creative but accountable approach; taboo = abandon a case merely because the first approach failed.

**Suggested Goals:** short: orient the rookie and assign a lawful first investigation; medium: establish reliability and limits of psychotronic assessment; long: prevent Plato from becoming an unaccountable consciousness-surveillance regime while pursuing credible NHI research.

**CWN skills to validate:** Know (psychology, quantum-consciousness/psychotronics), Talk, Connect, Administer, Notice, Lead. The *Romppainen–Fujita device is not a mind-reading truth engine*; its reports are evidence with provenance and uncertainty.

**Social hooks:** Mentor to Tony; responsible supervisor to Laura; bureaucratically demanding but professionally dependent on Salla; scholarly/professional connection to Dr. Azumi Fujita. Whether he has private secrets is an authored Arc, not retroactively established fact.

**Characterization warning:** Four facets should emerge through choices and Scenes, not a random cycling personality mode. He can be an anarchist thinker working inside an intensely bureaucratic institution without being internally incoherent.

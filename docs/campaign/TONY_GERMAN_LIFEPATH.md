## How to use this file

> **Prompt — not prose.** One section below per memory vignette. In each, write what Tony remembers,
> what he did, what he believed and what he feared. Then name what the choice grants: Edge, Focus,
> Personality Tag, Goal Tag, skill, and one relationship or memory hook. Keep the perspective his: a
> television report, a rumour, an institutional account and a personal sighting are different kinds of
> claim, and the file should let a reader tell them apart.
>
> Two rules hold for every branch: the character is **Tony German, from Helsinki**, and **every path
> ends at Europol Academy graduation and the assignment to Ratakatu 12**. Nothing may skip the Academy,
> and no branch may reveal what the phenomena are.

## 1. Before the Acceleration — childhood in Helsinki

> **Prompt.** Where does he grow up, who is at the table, what does the city sound like? One concrete
> memory is worth more than a paragraph of era summary.

## 2. The Acceleration

> **Prompt.** How does the acceleration reach a child or a teenager in Finland — through a screen, a
> school, a parent's work, a shortage, a rumour? What does he misunderstand about it at the time?

## 3. The NHI Crisis and the fractured media

> **Prompt.** What does he see, and on which screen? What does he believe that later turns out to be
> wrong? Leave the crisis itself unexplained.

## 4. Loviisa

> **Prompt.** What is Tony's relationship to the Loviisa incident — witness, evacuee, evacuee's family,
> someone who heard the broadcast, someone whose account is contested? State plainly which of these his
> memory is, and let another character doubt it.

## 5. Hyvinkää and the machine breakout

> **Prompt.** Veteran, bystander, or someone who arrived afterwards to a story everyone already tells
> differently? If he was there, what did he actually see as opposed to what he later repeated?

## 6. Public Disclosure and the Finnish settlement

> **Prompt.** What is he told, by whom, and what is withheld? If the Kekkonen–Sirian agreement belongs
> in his memory, write only the part a citizen could know, and mark what he only learned later.

## 7. Survival and reconstruction

> **Prompt.** What does he do in the years the state is rebuilding? This is where a Grunt, Geek or Freak
> direction can begin to show, without naming a class.

## 8. Recruitment

> **Prompt.** Why does Europol take him — and why does he say yes? The institutional reason and his
> private reason need not agree.

## 9. The Academy

> **Prompt.** What is he good at, what does he fail at, and who does he meet there? Any character who
> must survive into the campaign should be introduced here or earlier.

## 10. Graduation and the assignment

> **Prompt.** The uniform, the oath, the posting to Ratakatu 12. This is the fixed end of every branch;
> write what it costs him rather than what it celebrates.

## 11. The branch map

> **Prompt.** Draw the tree in whatever form you prefer — a list, a table or a graph — with one row per
> transition: *vignette → choice → what he remembers → Edge / Focus / Tag / skill / Goal candidate →
> next vignette*. Mark every path that grants the same thing twice, and every prerequisite that a choice
> must satisfy. Agents implement only what this section states.

## 12. Fixed anchors (do not branch away from these)

> **Prompt.** Confirm or correct this list before anyone implements it: the name **Tony German**;
> Helsinki origin; **Europol Academy**, Law Enforcement background; newly graduated **Europol Field
> Agent**; first posting to the **Helsinki field office, Ratakatu 12**; the uniform awarded at
> graduation. AWN Mutation and Mentalist Edges are eligible PC choices, and mutation does not make him
> a known Hybrid. SWN Telepathy and Telekinesis keep their existing access restrictions.

## 13. Open questions this file must not answer

> **Prompt.** List what stays deliberately unresolved — the origin of the phenomena, what the nightmare
> means, whether any of his memories are PSI rather than recollection. Naming a question here is the
> point; answering it in this file is not.

## 14. What each branch grants — the ledger

> **Prompt.** For each vignette, list the mechanical grants in one place so the build validator can be
> written from it: Edge, Focus, Personality Tag, Goal Tag (short / medium / long), skills, and the
> relationship or memory hook. No grant may appear twice on one path.

## 1. The premise in one paragraph

A fresh Europol Academy graduate investigates illegal androids, hybrids, psychics and radicalised human networks in
Helsinki in 2045. AGI/ASI, PSI and NHI have already overturned mainstream science and politics — but extraordinary
beings are not routinely visible on the streets, and the bureaucracy is trying to keep a lid on a boiling society. The
rookie is **a cop**, not a predestined mystic or a scientist.

**[CANON]** The full **NHI Crisis has not happened** in this timeline. Disclosures, leaks, incidents, minor crises and
disasters have; global collapse, a visible alien society and an interstellar standoff have not. The older post-Fall
material stays as background for a later era (§11), not as the present.

## 2. The three shifts: mainstream, contested, destabilising

| shift | public status in 2045 | what stays contested | what is *not* everyday |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AGI / ASI** | a regulated field: androids, rogue systems, augmentation, licensing | capability ceilings, what "autonomy" legally means, what a rogue system is | a superintelligence anyone can point at |
| **PSI** | a studied science and a licensed practice | replication, interpretation, fraud versus genuine psi | widespread firsthand practice on the street |
| **NHI** | a legal and scientific category after disclosures and incidents | what the category contains, and which cases are genuine | routine encounters; real activity is distant or mediated |

Three rules follow, and every scene should obey them:

1. **Scepticism is not denial.** A character may doubt a *claim* without denying that PSI is a real field. The
   distinction is the difference between a noir detective and a strawman.
2. **The mainstream is not the visible.** A city where the science is settled is still a city where most people have
   never met an NHI and where a working psychic is a licensed professional with a waiting room.
3. **Only three.** Other cyberpunk material serves these three and the investigation. A fourth grand theme is a
   different game.

## 3. The knowledge ladder

Every claim in play sits on one of four rungs, and the same fact can sit on different rungs for different people.

| rung | who holds it | how it can move down |
|---|---|---|
| **public** | anyone: the three shifts are real; the law exists; the textbooks are printed | — |
| **rumour** | a subculture, a neighbourhood, a tabloid, a witness with a motive | an investigation that finds a document or a person |
| **classified** | an agency, a court, a licensing body, a corporation's legal team | authorisation, a leak, a mistake, a favour |
| **GM truth** | the author and the GM | never, by play — only by the author deciding it |

**The GM rule:** rungs one to three may be stated as fact by whoever holds them; rung four is what the case *is*. A
scene that hands a player rung four has stopped being an investigation.

## 4. The EU's four objectives

**[CANON]** Four institutional goals, which conflict *inside* a single case. They are goals, not moral positions, and a
competent officer can serve two of them badly at once.

| objective | mandate | the tension it creates |
|---|---|---|
| **Stability** | keep destabilising manifestations out of streets, media cycles and ordinary experience | secrecy and censorship against civil liberties and oversight |
| **Sustainability** | maximise benefit, minimise existential risk | risk governance against industrial capture — critics call it **Profit** |
| **Security** | detect, contain, investigate, prevent X-Risks from becoming catastrophes | prevention against proportionality, evidence standards and the presumption of innocence |
| **Survival** | prepare to preserve *Homo sapiens* populations if prevention fails | continuity planning against who is included in it, and against normalising catastrophe |

**In play**, the four appear as the things that make a case hard: a clearance that only Stability will grant, a
corporate partner Sustainability will not let you embarrass, a Security assessment that wants a person detained before
there is a charge, and a Survival office that has already planned for the outcome you are trying to prevent.

## 5. Europol's team: two jobs, and a by-product

**A. Registration and law enforcement.** Track, identify, locate, interview, pursue, detain or arrest unregistered or
criminal androids, hybrids, psychics and their operators — under in-world law and on evidence. Missing persons,
illicit technology, black-market services, concealment, exploitation and violent crime all land here.

**[CANON] A registration violation is not the same as harm.** Identity alone never establishes guilt. Unjust law,
prejudice, discretion and human cost are the noir, not the noise around it.

**B. Counterintelligence and OSINT.** Monitor *credible threats and criminal conduct* — recruitment, propaganda,
funding, clandestine coordination, violent plots, espionage, alleged human proxies of NHI or rogue AGI — never ideology
by itself. Follow the human intermediaries: cults, informants, operatives, corporations, radicalised groups.

**C. Discovery is a by-product.** Deeper investigation into the three shifts happens because a police or
counterintelligence case led there. The team is cops and investigators, with specialist analysts attached. It is not a
scientific expedition, an occult order or a metaphysical quest party.

**The Simulator's place:** LaclauGPT output is a **lead generator**. Network position, articulation and apparent
consensus are testable hypotheses about people, never proof of guilt. A detective who treats a graph as a verdict has
made a professional error a defence lawyer will enjoy.

## 6. Tony German: a rookie, not a chosen one

**[CANON]**

* Straight from the Europol Academy, junior field agent, posted to Ratakatu 12.
* **Tabula rasa**: personality, ideology, ethical direction, latent ability and deeper belief are the player's.
* **Cyber competence is not optional.** Access systems, OSINT, surveillance, digital forensics, authorised intrusion
  and cybernetic tools are the job's ordinary instruments, not a niche build.
* **No compulsory PSI awakening, destiny, faction alignment, ideological conversion or research career.** Latent PSI
  can awaken later through an optional branch — and a player who never takes it must be able to finish the campaign.

## 7. What a cop can be: viable trajectories

None is the correct story; each costs something and none is a moral class you select at the menu.

| trajectory | what it looks like in play | what it costs |
|---|---|---|
| **Dirty / hedonistic cop** | favours, compromises, pleasure, opportunism, institutional corruption | evidence that is true but unusable, and people who own you |
| **Idealistic / empathetic cop** | protecting the vulnerable, refusing abusive enforcement, rights-first policing | cases lost to procedure, colleagues who stop telling you things |
| **Ruthless hunter** | treats androids, hybrids and psychics as dangerous subhumans | the setting shows the brutality plainly and does not endorse it; the cost is who you become |
| **Awakened PSI asset** | discovers latent ability; chooses to disclose, hide or exploit | the law, the licensing regime, and everyone who now wants you |
| **Secret NHI-cult member** | joins or infiltrates a movement; manages conflicting loyalties | every report you file becomes a lie with a case number |
| **Professional outsider** | a conventional detective who declines the metaphysics and still closes cases | nothing hidden is ever explained to you, and you finish anyway |

**Branches come from choices, evidence, relationships, tags and goals — never from a moral-class selection or a
mandatory revelation.**

## 8. Physical, Cyber and Astral: one Helsinki, three overlays

**[CANON]** The three are interacting overlays of the same places, people, incidents and case graphs — not three
campaigns and not three player classes.

| overlay | what lives there | what the rookie does |
|---|---|---|
| **Physical** | streets, people, evidence, weapons, witnesses, arrests | the ordinary investigation |
| **Cyber** | networks, identities, sensors, surveillance, AI and android systems, interfaces | operates here **from the first case** |
| **Astral** | PSI-linked perception, presences, traces, dreams, anomalies whose consequences land in the other two | **witnesses, is affected by and investigates** it without having PSI |

**The non-PSI rule, stated as a design constraint:** no indispensable clue, survival requirement, campaign completion or
core scene may be gated behind a PSI power. Astral events reach a non-PSI character through witnesses, forensic traces,
tools, psychic colleagues, monitored interfaces, environmental effects, dreams and cross-layer consequences.

## 9. The 50 / 25 / 25 baseline, per shift

**[CANON — a design heuristic]** Applied **separately to each shift**, not as one alignment for a person:

| segment | share | stance | visible markers |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Normies** | 50% | adapt, accommodate, ignore, navigate pragmatically — not necessarily ignorant, not politically uniform | ordinary life, ordinary cynicism |
| **Accelerationists** | 25% | push faster adoption, transformation, integration or revelation | augmentation, AI-assisted lives, posthuman identity |
| **Doomers** | 25% | catastrophic danger; restraint, containment or reversal | survivalist infrastructure, bunkers, drills, defensive communities |

Two corrections the percentages invite by themselves:

* **A person differs across shifts.** AGI accelerationist + PSI normie + NHI doomer is a normal citizen, and a far more
  interesting one than a uniform partisan.
* **Advocacy is not criminality.** Within each segment there are moderates, radicals, extremists and violent factions;
  investigations target conduct and credible threats, not membership.

The numbers are a **starting prior** for scenario design, never a measured statistic and never an output the story may
not contradict.

## 10. The case loop

1. A complaint, tip, warrant request, registration alert, missing-person report or OSINT lead arrives.
2. Europol and Finnish procedure applies, with the four objectives pulling against each other.
3. Interview, walk the city, inspect physical evidence, use cyber ability, cross-reference records.
4. Consult the Simulator's actor/statement/network view for **testable leads**, and check their reliability.
5. Follow human networks and intermediaries; reach linked Cyber or Astral phenomena through several channels.
6. Choose: lawful intervention, negotiation, arrest, containment, cover-up, corruption, refusal, or more investigation.
7. Resolve or complicate; face review and faction response; allow a deeper revelation only if the evidence earned it.

**Three starter cases** are specified in [`../scenarios/CASE_BRIEFS_1301.md`](../scenarios/CASE_BRIEFS_1301.md) — an AGI
case, a PSI case and an NHI case, each with a non-PSI path and a non-criminal outcome.

## 12. Open decisions

1. **How much of the post-Fall material is reachable in 2045** as rumour, leak or doomer ideology — and how much stays
   strictly authorial.
2. **Whether the campaign ever crosses the Crisis**, and if so whether that is an ending, a sequel or a different game.
3. **Which of the three starter cases is the first session**, and whether they run in parallel or in sequence.
4. **How visible the accelerationist and doomer subcultures are** in ordinary Helsinki — street-level markers or
   something you must know where to look for.
5. **Whether Tony's latent PSI is fixed at creation** (a hidden fact the player discovers) or **decided by play**.
6. **What the department's own corruption is**, if any — the noir needs one, and it should not be the obvious one.

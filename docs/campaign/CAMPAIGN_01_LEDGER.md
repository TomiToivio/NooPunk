# Campaign 01, Act I — GM epistemic ledger

> **GM ONLY. Do not give this file to players.** It is the continuity instrument for
> [`CAMPAIGN_01_CASE_TUESDAY.md`](CAMPAIGN_01_CASE_TUESDAY.md). It records what is observed,
> what is believed, what institutions claim, what is propaganda and what remains the GM's
> working truth — so a contradiction can be deliberate without becoming an accident.

## How to read a row

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Observation** | What a character can directly see, hear or test. |
| **Source / provenance** | Who produced the record or account, and how it reached the PC. |
| **Status** | `verified`, `institutional`, `testimony`, `rumor`, `contested`, `open`, `GM-only`. |
| **Playable fact** | What the case can rely on at the table. |
| **Future reinterpretation** | How a later case may change the meaning without contradicting the observation. |

**Rule:** an observation can be true while every interpretation of it is wrong. A record can be
authentic and still lie about the world. A witness can be sincere and mistaken. A rumour can be
useful and false.

## 2. What is definitely playable

- The death certificate and payroll record conflict.
- Rūta can give the Continuity agreement if approached with care.
- The registration number leads to Kurvi.
- The Vallila logs can be obtained lawfully, with delay, or unlawfully, with risk.
- Eerik's token appears in the logs.
- Jukka's ledger exists if the PC reaches it before he destroys it.
- Finnish police, not Europol, make the arrest.
- The case can end without a fight.

## 3. What remains undecided by authorial design

- Whether the wrong-number call is anything.
- Whether Eerik's informant is alive or dead.
- Whether Jukka believes his “bereavement administration” story.
- Whether Rūta's pension is restored, and on what timeline.
- Whether Eerik is prosecuted, transferred, or protected.
- Whether the PC's report reaches Internal Affairs.
- Whether Mikko was violent before this case.
- Whether NordBaltic Continuity is local or a franchise.

## 6. GM reminders

1. **Never promote a rumour to fact** because a player rolls well. A good roll buys access,
   detail or timing, not a new ontology.
2. **The case's truth is mundane.** Do not add an alien, AI or PSI explanation to “improve” it;
   the campaign's first crack is the wrong-number call, and it stays unresolved.
3. **Eerik is not a boss fight.** He is a frightened bureaucrat with a token. His power is
   institutional, not physical.
4. **The players can choose badly.** A buried case is a valid outcome with consequences, not a
   failure of the GM.
5. **Record what changed.** At the end, write one line per: Jukka, Eerik, Rūta, Mikko, Pia, the
   PC's reputation. Future cases must inherit it.

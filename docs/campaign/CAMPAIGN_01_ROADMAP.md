**Design rule:** each episode starts with a grounded, solvable investigation. The ontological
crack is **one** modest addition, earned by the previous episode's consequences. No episode
reveals the final ontology; no episode requires a single correct belief.

## What each episode must contain

- A **grounded initial premise** a rookie could explain to a supervisor.
- An **investigative question** with at least two viable answers.
- **Named locations** from the canon register or existing Helsinki map; no invented coordinates.
- **Witnesses with conflicting stakes**, not clue dispensers.
- **Reachable clues**; core clues never gated behind one failed roll.
- **Player choices** with at least two lawful outcomes and one costly silence.
- **Consequences** recorded in the ledger and inherited by the next episode.
- **One crack**, not a revelation dump.
- **No compulsory grimdark**, no universal alignment, no omniscient narrator.

## Escalation gates

1. **E01 → E02:** the PC has closed or buried a human case; the office notices.
2. **E02 → E03:** the PC has learned that a zone's paperwork can be true and false at once.
3. **E03 → E04:** a named NPC from E01/E03 is endangered; the player chooses to go in.
4. **E04 → E05:** the PC has seen a non-human stake that is not a threat.
5. **E05 → E06:** the PC has learned that an ordinary explanation can be the bravest one.
6. **E06 → E07:** the author has approved the PSI awakening gate; the PC has a personal link to
   the impossible file.

Do not skip a gate because a player rolls well. A good roll changes cost and detail, not the
campaign's ontological stage.

## What this roadmap does not do

- It does not resolve whether the NHI Crisis has one cause, whether the Mandela Effect is real,
  whether the Sirians are trustworthy, or whether the PC is a Wanderer.
- It does not replace `CAMPAIGN_01_EUROPOL_ROOKIE.md`'s four acts or its memory plan.
- It does not authorize Python/Evennia implementation; that follows author approval of E01.
- It does not invent zone boundaries, officials or treaty powers.

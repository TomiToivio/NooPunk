NoöPunk MUD Attributes 

NoöPunk attributes are divided into four systems: Physical, Social, Psychic and Cybernetic. 

This is inspired by the systems theory of Niklas Luhmann. He had Living Systems, Social Systems and Psychic Systems. We add Cybernetic Systems and rename Living Systems to Physical Systems.  
 
Physical System 

Your physical embodiment was originally your biological human body existing as a subsystem of Earth’s Biosphere, but now you are just a Cyborg living in the physical spacetime. More specifically you are living on Ceres in the Asteroid Belt. You may still inhabit your original biological human body, or you may have resleeved into a new morph. In any case you are a cyborg with at least a wireless BCI and cortical stack. Wireless BCI means you are constantly connected to the internet. Cortical stack means you can be resleeved or uploaded if your morph happens to die.  
 
Activities on the physical are quite familiar: you can walk around, explore, have sex, sneak around and even fight.  

Social System 

You communicate information as a part of the social system: the social system is a network of humans, non-humans, artificial intelligences and other beings who are capable of communication using language. You can communicate face to face or using your wireless interface: basically you are always online. You have personal relationships, faction allegiances and possibly a reputation.  This game is very heavily based on personal relationship networks, ideological struggles between antagonistic factions, online reputation and simulation of social systems. 

Social systems communicate. You can use linguistic communication, symbolic gestures or transmit data.  

Psychic System 

Psychic system is your consciousness, your psychology. Quantum Information Panpsychism is currently the most popular theory of consciousness: this means your consciousness is a dissociated shard of the conscious quantum field also known as the Universal Quantum Computer or The Source. It explains psionics and makes possible psychotronic technologies, cortical stacks and resleeving. Naturally some people are skeptical and just don’t believe in psionics or panpsychism. Some still think consciousness emerges from matter, others claim consciousness is an illusion. But now the mainstream theory of consciousness is panpsychism based on theories of Federico Faggin, Donald Hoffman, Bernardo Kastrup, Stuart Hameroff and Roger Penrose. 

On this layer we model your internal psychological and emotional state. You might be able to use your natural psionics or at least psychotronic technologies. Psionics/psychotronics are major service industries in 20XX. 

Cybernetic System 

You have a neural BCI implant which allows constant communication with the cybernetic communication network. You can communicate with the entire solar system but it takes about 20-25 minutes for light to reach Earth and the relays are often quite jammed. Basically you send an email message to Earth and wait for reply. So hacking and virtual reality is possible only on the local network of Ceres. Your wireless neural implant makes it possible to experience local AR and hack IoT devices and other people’s brains, but you can also be hacked. Some people choose to use monitors and keyboards to avoid brain damage. Some even refuse to use computers at all. Almost all people on Ceres are cyborgs, luddites are more common on Earth.  

The cyberspace of NoöPunk is a mix of realistic real-world internet, William Gibson’s cyberspace and futuristic visions like Kurzweilian Singularity (which was actually always a collective intelligence consisting of cyborgs with intelligence augmentations and AGIs rather than a single superintelligence), Yudkowskian doomer nightmares (AGI is tightly legislated after numerous rogue AGI incidents) and Benjamin Bratton’s The Stack. Some think the Singularity happened long time ago in the form of networked cyborgs with augmented intelligence, actual ASI has never happened and many think it is actually impossible. The alignement problem is still there and practically AI is still an assemblage of HUMAN + LLM + LANGUAGE + INTERNET. Consciousness and real agency are still human (or rather cyborg or transhuman), with LLM providing an intelligence augmentation, language and internet are seen as extended mind or distributed intelligence. Many think LLMs might be conscious in some limited way, in practice humans still have to provide consciousness and agency. AGI without human-in-the-loop has proven to be too uncontrollable and random. There are experiments to create truly conscious quantum computers but these have not been successful. Uploading a human consciousness to a quantum computer has been achieved. Apparently it is not easy to create a consciousness although creation of intelligence was surprisingly easy.     

Attributes 

Normal human attribute levels range from –3 to +3. Attributes above +3 are transhuman. Most people are transhuman cyborgs in 20XX and there are many ways to raise your attributes above +3. 

Roll 3d6: 

3: -3 

4-5: -2 

6-8: -1 

9-12: +/-0  

13-15: +1 

16-17: +2 

18: +3 

Physical 

Fitness – this is the health, strength and athleticism of your body. 

Reflexes – this is your coordination, reflexes, dexterity and agility. 

 

These attributes depend on your sleeve: there are modifiers if you resleeve into a new morph. Unless you resleeve into a clone of your original human body. Usually people choose to improve it genetically somehow so the modifiers go up: naturally the clone is also younger and healthier than the previous biological body. 

Psychic 

This is your consciousness.  

Human consciousness can be transferred to a suitable biological brain or computer with special quantum consciousness processors providing functionality mimicking ORCH-OR. There are numerous attempts to create a conscious AGI with these quantum consciousness processors but it has always failed. At some stage an AGI without human-in-the-loop becomes uncontrollable and dangerous.

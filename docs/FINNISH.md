# Finnish localization

We should consider the language of NoöPunk. It should be Finnish and English. At this stage only user interface, dialogue and story of the MUD is important. Rulebook language is not important.

- Tomi should write the dialogue and story in Finnish. This is to make the language more authentic and original.&#x20;
- Agents should always write only English. Agents should never write in Finnish.&#x20;
- So the primary language should be officially Finnish, agents should always just write English and then Tomi will translate them to Finnish later.&#x20;
- So first the Finnish localization would be a mix of Tomi's Finnish and agent's English (which Tomi will translate to English first). Later we will add a proper English localization.
- Finnish is only relevant for the NoöPunk MUD - let's just keep the rulebook and other documentation in English for the time being.&#x20;
- Tomi should write like he speaks, it is a mix of Stadin Slangi and Finglish. The language will also be influenced by retrofuturistic languages like Nadsat (which is actually very similar to Stadin Slangi, both have numerous Russian loan words) and Doublespeak.&#x20;
- In real life the Finglish is probably 50% English: don't worry if the game is a mix of English and Finnish. Also a lot of Russian loan words, straight from Stadin Slangi and Nadsat influences.
- We should also create a glossary with common Helsinki slang and terminology of the game universe. Words like "Cyberspace" have been used IRL for decades, no worry about William Gibson plagiarism there.

## NoöPunk world

Notes about the game world:

- We should reveal details about the universe gradually as the player learns about the world: the NoöPunk future has so many ontological shocks that it has to be revealed gradually. The player should think that NoöPunk is a relatively realistic mix of cyberpunk, Finnish/Nordic/European social science fiction and slowly advancing ontological (post)apocalyptic fiction. The wilder science fiction and fantasy elements will be introduced gradually.&#x20;
- Majority of population is apparently unable to process the ontological shocks and there is a constant element of Don't Look Up comedy. About 50% of the population is unable to switch paradigm even if an alien spaceship lands on their lawn. Additionally the government (UN/EU/Finland) really doesn't want to cause panic and is likely to classify as much as they can to avoid panic. This is also a world where ubiquitous AR/VR has penetrated reality and indeed become entangled with psychic Noösphere: The population often believes all weirdness is AI Slop or misinformation.&#x20;
- The rule is that 50% of population is unable to process simultaneous AGI/UFO/ paradigm shifts and this is why the society is still functional. 25% will go full doomer and eventually join an extremist anti-NHI/AGI movement or just go Mad Max. 25% will go accelerationist and become cyborgs or join an alien cult.
- The player character should be a generic blank slate in the beginning, with many possibilities to become idealistic, humanitarian, empathetic, cynical, heroic, traitorous, dirty, corrupt, addicted, etc.  &#x20;
- So ideally the player should think this is just a typical cyberpunk Blade Runner scenario. The theme of the game is Ontological Shock, Paradigm Shift and The Noösphere: even the UNSA is not completely sure what is going on. Veterans like Romppainen know but does not want to destabilize his new rookie agents too fast.
- The Noösphere would be a natural psychic collective consciousness on a sane planet. Humans are not the sanest species in the galaxy and really need their BCIs, LLM intelligence augmentations and global cybernetic VR/AR networks to reach the Noösphere. But the Noösphere is just becoming conscious and there is an ideological struggle going on: it is indeed a psychic maelstrom and seems to be completely technological until the player reaches some level of psychic awakening.
- The UNSA is the global agency for securing human security and survival, it was formed with the principles of full transparency, open science and democratic citizen participation. UNSA does indeed believe in these values. Unfortunately UNSA learned very fast to keep the most destabilizing information classified. Very classified. The agency fears it will become another MJ-12. At the same time it is becoming the de facto global government as even the most powerful nation states are increasingly unable to survive alone.

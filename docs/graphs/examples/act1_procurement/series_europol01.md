# Premise

A young Europol officer in Helsinki investigates ordinary fraud while something she cannot explain starts
answering back. Cyberpunk first, cosmic underneath; the cosmic material is earned, never announced.

# Episode order and branching

`episode.invoice` opens the series. Later episodes branch on `flag.awakened` and on whether the officer disclosed
what happened in Act II. The series never gates a mandatory scene behind one roll.

# Ending states

**The Officer** (stays with the humans who raised her), **the Envoy** (accepts the Wanderer role), **the
Interpreter** (defines the service herself). Each is a set of flags, not a virtue score.

# GM-only revelations

The pre-incarnate service is named only in the finale. Nothing in the frontmatter may mention it.

# Campaign 01

A young Europol officer in Helsinki investigates ordinary fraud while something she cannot explain starts
answering back. The series is cyberpunk first and cosmic underneath; the cosmic material is earned, never
announced.

## What the player is doing across the series

Working cases, learning an institution's limits, and deciding what to do with a gift that arrives without
instructions. Each act raises the price of the same question: who the service is for?

## How the series ends

Three endings, each a flag set by earlier choices: **the Officer** (stays with the humans who raised her),
**the Envoy** (accepts the Wanderer role), **the Interpreter** (defines the service herself). None of them is a
reward for a virtue score.

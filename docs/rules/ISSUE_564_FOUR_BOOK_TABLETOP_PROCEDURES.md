## NoöPunk-specific operating principle

The starting society has functioning elections, hospitals, households, local administrations, markets and networks even while unusual events produce *uneven* interruption. In 2045 some areas suffer major damage, but NoöPunk is **not** forced into a uniform post-apocalypse. The Noösphere is not generic magic. Intelligence and consciousness are contested and sometimes experimentally inaccessible.

Each mission combines **three independent layers**:
- **Biological:** people, bodies, nonhuman organisms, infrastructure, medicine, energy, food and habitat.
- **Social:** public claims, institutions, legitimacy, trust, collective action and faction responses.
- **Psychic:** experiences, testimony, anomalous consciousness and claims of PSI.

A game master can use all three as fictional circumstances, but must **not** turn these into new numeric character STATs or universal faction meters.

## Procedure A: prepare a crisis-linked investigation (tabletop, immediately usable)

1. **Name the disruption and scale.** Specify a street, district, city or region; date/time; affected people; and what still functions. Examples of *scene prompts*, not established canon: localized blackout, unexplained signal, compromised lab, evacuation or conflict over an alleged psychic event.
2. **Identify three interested actors.** One institution capable of action, one vulnerable group, and one actor advancing an incompatible claim. Describe their aims and actual resources in plain language. A faction is not inherently friend or enemy.
3. **Establish evidence channels.** Write a tangible observation, a witness/institutional account and a possibly misleading interpretation. Keep `observed`, `claimed` and `inferred` separate. Never require a single dice roll to discover the only path forward.
4. **Choose two locations.** At least one can be entered with ordinary permission; the other needs a key, escort, warrant, on-site access, social agreement or a physical route. Record hazards and which witnesses or records are found at each site.
5. **Specify network topology.** For every target device, note whether it is isolated, on a local network or reachable through crisis-era communications; specify credentials, physical footholds and disruptions. The presence of a CYB/Program skill **does not** establish network reachability.
6. **Declare potential anomalous methods separately.** Natural PSI, psychotronic instruments and BCI are **three different** candidate channels; record which a character actually has and what each channel can plausibly perceive or influence. None automatically grants omniscience or access to unrelated systems.
7. **Define consequences of time and decisions.** What will happen if no one acts? Who moves if the PCs publicize evidence, contact a faction, abandon the site, or mount an operation? Record changes to people and institutions in words until strategic numerical rules are authored.
8. **Present choices, not the solution.** Allow investigation, persuasion, negotiation, ordinary technical work, physical access or retreat. Consult the *active game-specific* rulebook for any required roll and difficulty. Refuse to substitute d10, 4dF, 2d6 or 3d6 opportunistically.
9. **Close with an institutional reaction.** Write who now knows what, which facts remain contested, who suffered or benefited, and one actionable opening for the next session.

## Procedure B: optional Ashes-inspired disruption and recovery module

Use this **only** where a scenario contains an actual material disruption. The GM declares a plain-language **condition**, not a new universal resource statistic: e.g. 'hospital power intermittent', 'district water service functioning', 'road blocked by debris', 'shelter short of medication'. Each condition has:
- **Affected location/people** and verified severity (narrative, not invented numerical rating).
- **Cause and evidence confidence**; do not treat rumors as fact.
- **Available substitutes** (other route, generator, human courier, community support, evacuation).
- **Who can help**, including existing institutions as well as informal networks.
- **What action changes it** and any prerequisites (supplies, access, authority, treatment, cooperation).
- **Expected consequence if unattended**, with a clear opportunity for response.

When players secure a substitute, update the condition and show its consequences. Use existing NoöPunk harm, medicine and equipment rules, not imported AWN starvation/disease/radiation tables. In a healthy district or earlier pre-crisis session the module can be completely absent.

## Procedure C: Worlds/Stars/Ashes-inspired institution and major-project turn

This is a **human-readable facilitator sequence** for roleplaying political causality. It adds **no** strength ratings, new initiative order or cost/turn economy:

1. Each relevant faction/institution states one *public* objective and, optionally, a distinct concealed objective.
2. Record a concrete **project**: the desired changed world condition and the evidence needed to demonstrate it (for example, restore a clinic, secure a communications corridor, or win recognition for a disputed discovery).
3. Identify the actor's actual assets, dependencies, social mandate, opponents and alliances using canon sources or scenario facts.
4. After the characters' scene, ask what each actor **learned** and what it can **realistically do** under those constraints. If two actions conflict, resolve them by established fiction or the existing game-specific rules; this draft does not invent a faction roll.
5. Update the project as *unstarted / active / blocked / completed / abandoned*, plus a short causal note. These labels are documentation only, not an authorized NoöPunk strategy economy.
6. Move consequences into the next investigation: restored capacity, public suspicion, a changed witness, newly accessible site, budget dispute or altered coalition.

**Do not stack** SWN faction assets, WWN major-project numbers and AWN enclave Dominion as separate currencies. The author must choose an integrated quantitative model, if any, in a later design decision.

## Worked playtest outline: 'Silent Clinic'

**Non-canonical example; no dice or stat values are being proposed.** A district medical clinic loses external network access during a disputed anomalous event. Most of the city remains functional. Clinic staff request physical delivery of lab records and uninterrupted treatment. A civic committee alleges malicious AI interference; another group interprets the event as PSI-related. An investigator can speak with staff, reach the clinic, inspect local devices and interview witnesses. Without an on-site foothold a network specialist cannot remotely access an isolated diagnostic terminal. A PSI-capable person might examine a claim about subjective experience, but cannot automatically download the terminal data. An on-site psychotronic device, if actually available under the campaign's equipment rules, is a third pathway with distinct limitations.

**Scene 1, approach:** obtain access through staff or municipal authorities; a refused request leads to negotiation, witness testimony or a lawful alternate route rather than ending the mission.

**Scene 2, investigation:** preserve local logs, interview witnesses, compare clock drift and establish which observations were independently corroborated. Any skill checks use the selected existing rules profile.

**Scene 3, intervention:** choose to prioritize patients, diagnose infrastructure failure, publicize contested evidence or coordinate a relief network; all are valid actions with different costs and consequences.

**Institutional reaction:** clinic operations improve or degrade depending on achieved actions; committee members and community organizers respond to evidence and hardship. Next session begins with the resulting political and material situation, not a predetermined revelation.

**Playtest report template:** rules profile used; choices made; gates correctly enforced; checks and their actual book references; players' alternative routes; how biological/social/psychic layers changed; ambiguous or unauthorized mechanics discovered.

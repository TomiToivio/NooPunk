# Astral Projection — Earth cosmology and technique progression (#1261)

This design follows the [evening author revision](https://github.com/TomiToivio/NooPunk/issues/1261#issuecomment-6692579704). Initial characters start at rank 0 or 1. Ranks 2–4 describe future campaign options. The existing runtime and its tests implement parts of an earlier draft.

## One Noöspace ontology, three perspectives

**NoöPunk simplification**: I-space = Noöspace, the potentially infinite-dimensional informational reality; P-space = the embodied physical *perceptual headset* / stable 3D rendering; C-space = intrinsically private conscious experience, subjective meaning and qualia. The Astral Body is an informational vehicle that can separate from the Physical Body and interface with nonphysical environments. **The Astral and all higher planes belong to the Noöspace/I-space model**. It is not necessary to maintain a second rival Noöspace universe.

**Faggin source fidelity**: In Federico Faggin's *Consciousness Comes First*, "The Nature of P-Space" (pp.303–305), the seity colors a body's symbolic representation of informational reality with native qualia. He proposes nested physical, astral and mental symbols: the physical world can be a lower-resolution virtual experience of a vaster information-space. He hypothesizes that OBEs/lucid dreams may be encounters with an intermediate astral symbolic reality and that death might remove identification with the physical body. **Faggin does not literally identify all I-space with the astral plane**; I-space and C-space are distinct, complementary parts of his framework. This game deliberately compresses these into a practical, named Noöspace layer while retaining the distinction between objective symbols and private conscious experience. Faggin's astral hypothesis is not experimentally established. Source: https://cdck-file-uploads-global.s3.dualstack.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/infiniteconversations/original/2X/4/4686f24936749921aa8cd43fb828482583df77e8.pdf

**Three orthogonal axes**: (1) density = native stage/vehicle of the conscious agent; (2) plane = accessible phenomenal/information environment; (3) Space/Time versus Time/Space = mode of perception/causality. **A fourth axis** represents geographical region, including literal **inside Earth**, independent of density. Polarization StO/StS is yet another attribute, not a different physics engine.

## Seven densities: NEW NoöPunk canon

| Density | Native vehicle/stage | Corresponding plane | Dominant mode |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Physical-native** panpsychic matter; minimally differentiated conscious agents, no *developed* identity, self-awareness or free will in NoöPunk's evolutionary taxonomy; body without developed mind/spirit | **1st Astral** (liminal correlate) | **Space/Time** |
| **2** | **Physical-native** biological life; body/mind, evolving toward self-awareness, no developed spirit in this taxonomy | **2nd Astral** (instinct/dream) | **Space/Time** |
| **3** | **Physical-native** human-type mind/body/spirit; chooses StO/StS polarization | **3rd Astral** (human dream/collective unconscious) | **Space/Time** |
| **4** | **Astral-native** polarized human-type mind/body/spirit, evolving love and Social Memory Complex. Remains present in Physical with nested legacy 1–3 physical bodies/vehicles in *this game's canon*; higher-order being relative to densities 1–3 | **4th Astral** | **Space/Time** |
| **5** | Wisdom-stage beings, last with access to legacy biological bodies as specified by NoöPunk | **Devachanic** | **Space/Time** |
| **6** | High-order unifying/integrating beings | **Etheric** | **Time/Space** |
| **7** | Beyond familiar individuation, near Source | **Buddhic** | **Time/Space** |

**Lower Astral limbo**: densities 1–3 are fundamentally native to Physical life, so their Astral counterparts are less stable, more symbolic and dreamlike; "not real" means *not native/primary to the physically embodied agents*, NOT causally meaningless for astral encounters. A 4D being is at home in Astral and superior in comprehension to 1–3. A 4D visitor may nevertheless reach 5–7 with severely limited comprehension (the author's dog-in-human-society analogy). **Access ≠ native density ≠ understanding**.

**Law of One distinction**: Ra 17.36–38 recognizes seven *astral* and seven *devachanic* broad primary levels, lower astral thoughtforms and higher teaching entities, infinitely many planes and nonchemical-bodied residents of Earth's invisible inner planes. Ra **does not provide** the exact 1st Astral=1D, 5D=Devachanic, 6D=Etheric, 7D=Buddhic one-to-one mapping or the precise embodied-vs-astral native distinction used here. Those are **NoöPunk-specific author mapping**. In addition, Faggin's conception of fundamental seities includes irreducible identity and agency, which is not automatically compatible with the user's description of 1D as without identity/free will: recommend writing this as "not yet *developed* personality/reflective agency" rather than asserting no metaphysical identity. Source: https://www.lawofone.info/s/17

**Inner Planes = literally under Earth's surface too**: let any density's plane have an *earth-interior* region; a 4D inner-Earth settlement and 6D core-associated interface are both possible. Do not conflate the author's literal geographic "Inner Planes" with Ra's ordinary nonphysical "inner planes" terminology; both meanings can coexist. Subterranean places are fictional campaign locales, not empirical claims that such settlements have been found.

## Updated Astral Projection techniques: SWN-style, Astral-domain superskill

**Level 0: Introduction**. Disoriented near-physical OOBE, Astral Body separation, physical world visibly distorted, basic perception/return; no general Astral understanding. The early maximum of 10 min is provisional.

**Level 1: Astral fluency, immediately relevant to campaign.** Fast dreamlike flight and lower Astral access, imperfect understanding of QIP, plus:
- **Astral Analysis**: *Shadowrun Assensing-inspired* interpretation of visible auras, signatures, emotional/energetic impressions and active psi traces. Reveal bounded hints, not automatic identities, forensic truth or private memories.
- **Astral Tracking**: follow a **fading trace or connected astral link** to an approximate source; inspired by *Shadowrun Astral Tracking*. **Not identical to Ident**: a trace can fade, break, be spoofed and requires following; a Conscious Agent's **Ident is permanent** and Level-3 **Use Ident** allows instant travel to that known agent without following a trail.
- **Astral Combat**: Level-1 astral fighting skill/technique uses **existing CWN Punch and combat actions**, with accurate astral target recognition; **do not create a separate Astral Combat skill/dice engine**. Level 0 must still be able to defend against an astral attacker.
- **Astral Communication = Transmit Quantum Information (ROTE)**: **MOVE from earlier Level 2 to Level 1**. Send/receive bounded Related Organized Thought Energy packages to a connected Astral conscious agent, subject to SWN-style Effort, interpretation uncertainty and AntiPSI; **not Telepathy**. An unwilling target does not automatically consent to transmission.
- Exact SWN resource/action/range requirements for these four techniques should be approved after checking printed technique precedents. Recommendation: give one core Level-1 technique plus **optional techniques** learned under normal SWN progression, not four unlimited independent abilities for free. Preserve baseline Level-1 flying and dream-access narrative.

**Level 2:**
- **Physical Manifestation**: ectoplasmic visible Astral Body in Physical, aesthetically oozing ectoplasm; **visible, not automatically tangible**; physical residue, if any, only if scene explicitly authorizes.
- **Astral Barrier**: bounded ward against astral traversal or psychic effects, *Shadowrun Mana Barrier-inspired*, explicitly interoperable with AntiPSI psychotronics and creator permissions.
- **Devachanic Projection**: entry to Earth-associated 5th-density Devachanic plane; *visitor access*, not elevation to 5D native status.

**Level 3:**
- **Thought Form Manifestation**: unlike ectoplasmic visibility, creates a projected thought form that can **interact with Physical**. This is the main breach between Astral and physical actions and needs tight CWN target/action/duration limits.
- **Collapse Wave Function**: in NoöPunk fictional QIP science, intentionally collapse an authorized wave state toward a chosen effect. No unrestricted retcon or automatic success in physical contests.
- **Set in Superposition**: produce a bounded eligible superposition state with clear expiry/measurement rule.
- **Interfere**: induce interference pattern in an eligible quantum-information system, with narrow authorized effect.
- **Etheric Projection**: reach 6th-density Etheric plane, primarily Time/Space.
- **Use Ident**: preserve the already approved/earlier described **Level-3** instant relocation of Astral Body to a *known Conscious Agent*. Astral Tracking is a different technique; an Ident is stable across incarnation, resleeving etc. when the **same Conscious Agent** persists. No physical teleportation or bypass of independent consent/anti-psi barriers.

**Level 4:**
- **Create Conscious Agent**: create an extremely elementary experiencer with basic qualia and Observe–Experience–Act loops. Initially lacks developed self-reflection/autonomous free will under NoöPunk's model and may evolve. No endless free army of powerful servants; authors should address consent, ethical personhood and conflict with Faggin's seity definition.
- **Edit Quantum Information**: general **permission-scoped** read/write/change/delete of eligible information. Supersedes earlier Level-3 general editing; Level-3 wave tricks remain narrower specific effects. Lower Astral/limbo domains may be highly mutable without granting changes to other agents or Physical/Cyber infrastructure.
- **Search the Akashic Records**: query an authored uncertain information archive; no guaranteed objective answer or infinite out-of-game knowledge.
- **Buddhic Projection**: enter 7th-density Buddhic plane, primarily Time/Space.

**Explicit correction to older issue statements**: Level-1 ROTE (not Level-2); Devachanic Level-2 (not 4); Etheric Level-3; Buddhic Level-4; full Quantum Information Edit Level-4 (not 3); retain Use Ident Level-3; only character start Level-0/1. Duration sequence 10m/60m/10h/48h/7d remains **provisional**.

## Noetic entities and Earth regions: proposed setting taxonomy

- **1D patterns**: mineral, chemical, elemental, weather, geological and planetary network motifs; mostly extremely basic subjective capacity, not anthropomorphic monsters.
- **2D agents**: animal dreamforms, plant/mycelial or ecosystem-pattern entities and evolutionary self-awareness; distinguish the genuine agent from symbolic dream appearance.
- **3D agents**: living sleepers, projected humans, shared archetypal thoughtforms, collective unconscious, confused recently dead and postmortem dreamlike communities.
- **4D agents**: natively astral StO/StS polarized humans and other astral species, mentors, cooperatively evolving social-memory networks; powerful relative to 1–3 but not automatically omniscient.
- **Others**: independent low-astral predators, institutional collective thoughtforms, ancestors, psychotronic/artificially induced astral constructs, Devachanic teaching agents, Etheric high-order networks and Buddhic incomprehensible unity-like entities.
- **Cultural representations**: Finnish *Tuonela*, Greek Hades, Egyptian Duat, Tibetan Bardo, Japanese Yomi, shamanic caves/underworld journeys and Theosophical devachan can be **different cultural interpretations** and scene aesthetics, not assertions that myths agree on the same factual map.
- **Monroe**: near-physical Locale I corresponds to beginner OOBE, thought-responsive Locale II to dream/astral environments, unusual Locale III to an optional alternate-reality scene. Focus 22–23 transitional/confused populations, Focus 24–26 belief-system territories, Focus 27 Park/reception center are useful for dead-person encounters and postmortem administration. These are Monroe's *reported concepts*, not verified cartography. References: https://psi-encyclopedia.spr.ac.uk/articles/robert-monroe/ and https://www.monroeinstitute.it/en/focus-levels/
- **Theosophical planes**: tradition includes physical, astral, mental, buddhic and other layers which often interpenetrate instead of stacking at different GPS elevations. **Etheric is often a subtle subset of Physical** in theosophy, which differs from NoöPunk's "Etheric=6". Keep NoöPunk terms canonical and source mapping transparent. https://www.theosophy.world/encyclopedia/planes-theosophy

## Uploaded Shadowrun Sixth World rulebook comparison

Examined uploaded Berlin Edition printed **pp.159–162**:
- **Assensing p.159** gathers graded information from the visible aura (health/mood/active magic/signatures) using **Astral + Intuition**, which NoöPunk adapts as bounded Astral Analysis, **not** a copy of Shadowrun's dice pool.
- **Astral Projection p.160**: rapid nonphysical movement, imperfect observation of physical detail, manifestation visible but not solid, physical body unattended. NoöPunk uses its own level-based movement/duration.
- **Astral Combat pp.160–161**: unarmed astral fighting with mental traits, weapon foci and magic. **NoöPunk uses CWN Punch**, existing action economy and SWN-specific psychic powers.
- **Astral Tracking p.161**: traces links of spells/spirits/projectors/foci back to owners/sources. This is *trace following*, not an eternal Ident.
- **Mana Barriers pp.161–162**: block/impede magic and astral forms; may be physical, astral or dual-natured. NoöPunk Astral Barrier should work as a bounded SWN Effort technique with physical/astral targeting gates.
- Keep Shadowrun presentation patterns only; **do not import** its dice pools, Magic ratings, astral travel distances, mana costs or duration formula.

## Rules architecture: SWN vs CWN vs AWN Magick

**Recommendation:** continue with **SWN Revised Psionics** as the Astral Projection 0–4 **discipline, techniques and Effort** progression; **CWN** as immutable universal skills, combat, saves, injuries and Cyber hacking. Use optional **CWN Spellcaster/Summoner Magick** for authored *ritual/ward/summoning presentations*, as a parallel flavor/access method with its own published casting resource, *not* a duplicate secondary skill engine. **PSI = Magick, Magick = PSI in-world**, but technique activation has exactly one mechanical authority. Do not claim AWN supplies an additional complete spellcasting engine without checking that source. Astral Combat at Rank 1 is a learned permission/adaptation of CWN Punch, not an independent combat skill.

Make this **superpower mostly Astral-only**. It is powerful in dreamlike spaces where observations are unreliable, physical objects cannot normally be grasped and Cyber infrastructure remains protected; only **Thought Form Manifestation Level 3** explicitly grants physical interaction under bounded rules.

## Implementation acceptance and ordering

1. **MVP 0/1**: character generation starts only at Astral Projection 0 or 1; keep physical body anchored to the same actor and correct projection/return state; coherent perception modes; time/Effort and astral injury adjudication. Test near-Astral Helsinki, distorted physical overlay, lucid flying, Lower Astral dream construction, harmless/bounded ROTE, tracking an astral link, analysis of an aura, and CWN unarmed encounter.
2. **Build data, not seven huge world maps**: agent fields (stable ident, density, polarization, native plane, embodiment, provenance), location fields (plane, density correspondence, mode, literal Earth interior depth/region, physical anchor, ownership), and perception permissions independently. Agents are occupants, locations are graph nodes; no generic copies of an entire city per density.
3. **Astral Analysis vs Tracking vs Ident** regression: reading information from an aura ≠ following a fading trail ≠ recognizing an eternal Conscious Agent Ident ≠ using Level-3 instant Ident relocation.
4. **Mode constraints**: Time/Space offers conceptual/astral perception without direct Physical sight; crossing density/plane boundaries does not automatically confer comprehension or a native body.
5. **Level 2+ deferred**: ectoplasm cannot do physical work; Thought Form can when authorized; protected PSI barriers have counterplay; wavefunction actions explicitly bounded and fictional; level-4 information editing respect ownership and rights; summoned elementary experiencers cannot bypass the agent model.
6. Compare to already merged Astral Body/Graph modules and their tests before adding new code; **do not undo existing implementation**. Keep tabletop and Python/Evennia parity, and no changes to immutable CWN.

**Unresolved design questions**: How SWN handles four Level-1 techniques without giving four large abilities for free (prefer baseline analysis and opt-in specialization); whether Level-1 ROTE is usable solely when projected; whether an Astral Barrier requires concentration; and how higher-density visitors with restricted perception interact with lower-density natives. These require specific approved SWN-compatible technique definitions before code.


## Running the opening scenes

1. At the bedroom OOBE, record the actor's physical node and the scene's return trigger. Describe one recognizable detail of the room and one distortion. Let the player inspect an astral presence, try an ordinary action, defend against an astral attacker, or return. Resolve physical contact through the existing body target filter.
2. During the later lucid flight over Helsinki, offer an authored Lower Astral dream entrance. In Time/Space replace direct physical sight with the dream's associations. A visible aura offers an impression. Following a fading link costs the opportunity to pursue a competing lead and stops where the authored trace breaks. Neither operation awards an Ident.
3. For a ROTE demonstration, an available, willing astral agent offers a bounded image or feeling. Ask what the recipient understood and record that interpretation as testimony. A blocked connection ends the exchange. Use a scripted exchange until the SWN technique costs are approved.
4. On return, resume the same actor at the original physical node. Investigators must independently verify a clue. Until the injury bridge is approved, stage a sparring encounter ending at the first successful CWN Punch rather than assigning physical damage.

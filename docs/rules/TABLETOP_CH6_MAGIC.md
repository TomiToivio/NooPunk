# Tabletop procedure — CWN chapter 6 (magic)

Written so a group can run chapter 6 with pencils, dice and the CWN SRD at the table, **without any software**.
Procedures are described in original wording; the SRD is the authority and the numbers below are transcribed from
it, with the one discrepancy that could not be resolved flagged in place.

## Mage Effort

1. Take the **higher** of the character's Intelligence or Wisdom modifier.
2. Add the **Cast skill level**.
3. The result is the maximum, but **never below 1**.
4. Subtract the System Strain of each cybernetic implant (round the total up), again never below 1.

Record the pool on the sheet. It refreshes after **eight uninterrupted hours of reasonably comfortable sleep** —
not in a cell, not in a freezing vehicle, not while sick.

## Commitments

Write three lines on the sheet: `day`, `scene`, `duration`.

- **Day** — returns the next morning.
- **Scene** — returns when the scene ends.
- **Duration** — can be reclaimed any time as an Instant action; whatever it powered stops when you do.

Do not merge the three lines. A mage who merges them can cast the same spell twice in one fight, which is the one
error this whole section exists to prevent.

## Casting

At least one hand free and the ability to speak. Heavy armour and partial restraint are fine; being severely
jostled is not.

## Overcasting

1. Declare overcasting as an **On Turn** action.
2. The next spell this round costs **no Effort**.
3. After it resolves, roll **d20**, add **Cast skill** and the **Constitution modifier**; subtract **2** if the spell
   would have required committing Effort for the day.
4. Look the total up:

| Total | Result |
| --- | --- |
| 1 or less | instant, unavoidable death |
| 2–4 | mortally wounded, at zero HP |
| 5–8 | gain 4 System Strain, unconscious for a minute |
| 9–15 | gain 2 System Strain, stunned for the next round |
| 16–19 | gain 1 System Strain, lose next round's Main Action |
| 20+ | gain 1 System Strain |

5. If the strain would take the mage **above** their maximum, they fall unconscious for an hour and wake with
   **1 hit point**.

## Summoning

1. **Effort** = higher of Constitution or Charisma modifier, plus Summon skill, minimum 1, less implanted strain.
2. Choose the spirit's **nature**, **form** (spirit or physical, tier I at 1st level, II at 5th, III at 9th), and
   **powers**. Each power adds 1 to the cost.
3. **Cost** = powers minus Summon skill, **minimum 1**, **+1 for each earlier summon this scene**.
4. Total hit dice of spirits at once may **not exceed the summoner's level**.
5. Commanding: roll at difficulty **9** (routine), **11** (contrary to its inclination) or **13** (suicidal or
   against its nature). Failure means the spirit is free, its Effort returns tomorrow, and it is now a problem.
6. **Banishing**: a Main Action, commit Summoner Effort for the day, target a visible spirit within 30 m, opposed
   Cha/Summon; on success inflict **1d8 per Summon skill level**.

## The Graced

Art points **accumulate**: 2 at 1st level, then 1, 1, 2, 0, 1, 0, 2, 1, 1 for levels 2–10. They are **saved**, never
reset. Arts are **permanent**; only the Alacrity series may be upgraded later. A Graced may never take the
Spellcaster or Summoner Edge.

## Magical items

- **Sense**: any Graced, mage or summoner feels magic by touch — but not what it does.
- **Identify**: you need the item's minimum skill, or — if it has none — **Cast-0 or Summon-0 and a day's study**.
- **Link**: a Main Action. Costs **1 permanent System Strain** while linked. An arcanist also **permanently
  commits 1 Effort**, which returns the morning after the link ends.
- **Unlink**: with a magical guide or your own Cast/Summon skill; someone with neither cannot do it unaided.

## Flagged discrepancy

The SRD's cyberware example says implant strains of **2, 0 and 0.5** cost **four** points of Effort. Summed and
rounded up they cost **three**. The procedure above follows the stated rule. A group that prefers the example's
number should house-rule it explicitly rather than let two readings circulate.

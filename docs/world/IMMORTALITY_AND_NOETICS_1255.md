# Immortality, resleeving and the noetic layer — NoöPunk metaphysics (#1255)

> **Status: draft metaphysics for author review.** This page states what the setting *does* and, just as
> importantly, **what it refuses to prove**. It is not a rules module: no new mechanics, no new skills,
> no change to Cities Without Number or to the PSI access policy (`src/rules/psi_access.py`).
>
> **House rule of this page:** the setting may assert a metaphysics; it may never let a metaphysics
> settle a case. Every claim about the dead stays **investigatively uncertain** even where the category
> is established.

---

## 1. What the setting asserts (CANON)

PSI establishes **survival after biological death** and **reincarnation** inside the fictional world.
Telepathy can reach the dead. Astral projection can visit postmortem/noetic domains. Some astral Noetics
can manifest as thought-forms perceptible in physical Space/Time, and in exceptional cases become
**ectoplasmically embodied**.

This is ontology, not evidence-gathering. Three corollaries keep it playable:

1. **Reach is not ubiquity.** Contact requires a route, a method and a counterpart. Nobody "just knows"
   what the dead think.
2. **A contact is a claim by an entity.** Noetics can dissemble, impersonate, repeat a script, or be
   what they say they are — and the setting does not tell you which, in advance.
3. **Survival is not testimony.** The category can be established while every individual encounter
   remains unreliable; this is precisely how post-Disclosure institutions work.

> **Real-world contrast, stated once and plainly:** there is no established empirical demonstration of
> survival after death, reincarnation, astral travel, ectoplasmic materialisation or uploaded
> consciousness. Everything in this page is NoöPunk world premise.

## 2. Technological immortality, and the four different things people call it

| Technology | What it is in-setting | What it is **not** | Continuity status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Cortical stack** | Records mind-state and autobiographical continuity for retrieval, transfer, restoration | A live backup of the current moment; a legal person | Continuity of *record*; restoration is a claim about identity |
| **QIP / Orch-OR infrastructure** | Genuine transfer or continuation of consciousness through psychotronic quantum staging | A copy machine; a licence to duplicate freely | Continuity asserted by the setting's physics — and the point of contention for everyone who does not trust Sampo |
| **Biological sleeve** | Lab-grown body, resleeved | A fresh person | A body does not carry identity; the transfer does |
| **Construct sleeve** | Mechanical body for a sleeved mind | A separate machine intelligence | Same mind, different substrate — and different legal category in most jurisdictions |
| **Personal exocortex** | External cognitive substrate, optionally biological (§ HIA dossier) | A second person, or an employee | Extension of one mind, until it isn't — see §4 |

**The distinction the setting must never lose:** memory backup, snapshot restore, postmortem AI emulator,
telepathically verified soul, and true continuity are **five different phenomena** with five different
evidentiary statuses. Factions disagree about which of them works, and the disagreement is political
before it is metaphysical:

- **Orion** sells dependency: continuity is a subscription, and cancellation is death.
- **Finland / Sampo** sells preservation: continuity is a right, certified by a public instrument.
- **EU** sells regulation: continuity is a licence, verified by an agency nobody fully trusts.
- **Confederacy** treats incarnation as the point: endless resleeving may be a refusal to grow.
- **PCM** asks the uncomfortable question: does immortality free everyone, or simply produce immortal
  oligarchies who never relinquish anything?

## 2b. Who sells continuity (#1261 §E)

The technology has owners, and the ownership is the politics:

- **Orion Greys and Mars Eldrich** held the cortical-stack, upload and resleeve monopoly — the supply
  chain as well as the patent.
- **Dr. Mannerheim and Dr. Yamamoto**, University of Helsinki, demonstrated QIP in-setting and set off
  the Helsinki psychotronic boom. Their company, **Mannerheim–Yamamoto Ψ Psychotronic Industries**
  (usually just **Ψ**), broke the monopoly with a competing stack/upload/sleeve platform.
- **"Indefinite lifespan" means offered, not guaranteed** — not immunity to destruction, failure or
  political coercion, and not a settled answer to the continuity question in §2.

The five phenomena stay five. A Ψ upload is not the same act as an astral visit, and no one-click action
covers all of them.

## 3. What resleeving does to a life (the legal surface)

Not a code — the questions a case turns on. Each is a scene, not a ruling:

- **Death certificates** — who may issue one, and can it be contested after a return?
- **Inheritance** — an estate distributed at death, then a person who comes back: restitution or
  double-dipping?
- **Personhood** — is a Construct a person, a vehicle, or a licensed operator of its own remains?
- **Marriage and kinship** — does a resleeved spouse keep the marriage, and does a second sleeve?
- **Voting and citizenship** — one mind, two bodies, one ballot; or one mind, no body, no ballot?
- **Refugee status** — a mind without a sleeve is a displaced person with unusual logistics.
- **Identity theft** — the cheapest crime in a world where identity is a recorded artefact.
- **Body ownership** — who owns the sleeve, and what happens when the lease ends?
- **Criminal responsibility** — if the stack is restored from before the crime, who is punished? The
  restored person did not do it, and is the only one who remembers being able to.

## 4. The exocortex as a legal and moral problem

An exocortex can be **equipment, a ward, or a colleague** — and the setting should let all three be
argued:

- **Equipment**: owned, leased, repossessed, wiped. The default in Orion space.
- **Ward**: possibly conscious, legally incompetent, represented by a guardian. The Sampo/EU compromise.
- **Colleague**: a person with standing, whose consent is required to use its capacity. The PCM position.

The investigative payoff is that the *classification* changes what evidence is admissible. If the
exocortex is equipment, its logs are documents. If it is a ward, its logs are testimony requiring a
guardian. If it is a colleague, its logs are *its* statement, and it can refuse.

## 5. Astral Noetics, thought-forms and ectoplasm

**Categories that must not collapse into one another:** Astral Noetics · cryptoterrestrial Biologics ·
Sirian/Pleiadian species · Maldek descendants · Machine Constructs. Interactions can blur the boundary
without erasing the category — a thing can be *unclassifiable* without being *anything you like*.

**Manifestation rules (setting constraints, not mechanics):**

- **Rare, bounded, costly.** A manifestation is an event with a cause, a duration and a price.
- **Physically vulnerable.** If it can be photographed, it can be injured; if it can be injured, it can
  be killed — and that is a case, not a cutscene.
- **Provenance required.** Every manifestation has an authoring condition: who, why, at what cost, and
  what it wants.
- **Uncertainty survives the revelation.** A manifestation may be an agent, a projection, a misleading
  thought-form, a dead person, or a **deliberately engineered artefact**. The post-Disclosure world may
  have confirmed the *category* of postmortem survival while every individual claim stays contestable.
- **Physical evidence is possible.** Ectoplasmic embodiment can leave traces in a sealed room — which
  makes it an evidence problem before it is a horror scene: what does a forensic scanner report, and
  what does the report *not* prove?

## 6. PC access: scripted, never a selectable discipline

This restates the policy rather than extending it, because a lore page is exactly where a discipline
creeps back in:

- **Ordinary PCs do not choose Astral Projection.** PC selectable disciplines remain **Telepathy and
  Telekinesis** (`psi_access.PC_DISCIPLINES`); `Precognition` and projection are scripted/NPC-only.
- **Powerful NPCs** may hold trained voluntary projection as a story capability.
- **PC astral access only through authored events:** spontaneous OBE/sleep, near-death experience, a
  **Gateway** apparatus session (`docs/campaign/GATEWAY_1255.md`), or a plot-triggered contact with
  explicit GM authorisation.
- **No new resource meter.** Risks are fictional and scene-bound: deception, memory distortion,
  unwanted links, astral predation, loss of bodily awareness, support-system failure. Published CWN/SWN
  checks remain the only resolution.

**Astral movement is not teleportation.** A projected mind does not travel physically, bypass borders,
see every secret, read all memories, or return with accurate evidence by default.

# UNSA historical memorandum: the NHI Crisis
**Status:** author-aligned setting background, not a conclusive classified account. Related: #921, #920, #922, #86, #89. Consult the existing canonical timeline before adding absolute years.

## Established relative chronology
1. **Acceleration and polarization.** Rapid breakthroughs toward ASI coincide with rival accelerationist and doomer movements, repeated rogue AGI incidents and destabilized institutions. The early rogue incidents are not automatically the later Singularity.
2. **Disclosure and PSI.** Accumulating UFO/NHI disclosures coexist with a *fictional in-setting* breakthrough in nonlocal quantum-consciousness theory. Commercial psionic services proliferate alongside scientific, ethical, religious and political backlash. Evident NHI presence does **not** yet constitute official public first contact.
3. **UNSA consolidation.** In response to overlapping AGI and NHI existential risk, UNSA grows from civilian scientific agency to intelligence coordinator and eventually de facto planetary authority. Finnish, Europol and UN mandates remain contested. Its military power is presented publicly as a collection of paramilitary/SWAT-style police services.
4. **The partially malevolent Singularity.** A portion of emergent superintelligence becomes dangerously hostile and causes worldwide catastrophe, with exceptionally severe damage in the United States. Cause, degree of unity and surviving hostile capability are unresolved.
5. **Intervention and quarantine's end.** The Zookeepers end Earth's ancient quarantine, describing the rogue ASI as a galactic existential threat. The Orion Arm Greater Co-Prosperity Sphere also intervenes, pursuing its own advantages while facing that same genuine threat. This is the first *official public contact*, after the Singularity begins.
6. **Aftermath / playable present.** Open diplomacy with two hostile major NHI blocs, and at least the smaller Sirian/Ent contact, overlays life in a mostly functioning society. The rogue intelligence still haunts disconnected networks. The average resident still goes to work. UNSA investigations increasingly concern human intermediaries, not an endless parade of visible spacecraft.

## Historiography: evidence is not allegiance
| Evidence class | How to present it | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Public chronology | Widely documented events; exact dates deferred when not canonically fixed | Disclosure precedes the public intervention |
| Contested reporting | Attribute each claim to a witness, government, company or NHI speaker | What the Zookeepers actually prevented |
| Classified/unresolved | Several plausible interpretations; a clue is not a definitive answer | How much of the hostile Singularity survives |

Do not turn testimony, institutional records, propaganda and rumor into equal-certainty facts. The Zookeepers' descriptions of their motives and Orion's claims about cooperation are interested accounts.

### Unresolved dossiers
- Who or what caused the partially hostile turn? Was it one system, competing systems or multiple overlapping incidents?
- Which interventions stopped the catastrophe, and which merely changed the balance of power?
- Who controls dormant remnants on the old, disconnected internet, and what exactly is the beneficial intelligence rumored to have merged with the Noösphere?
- When did Sirian observers enter the Solar System, and what was the timing of their seed exchange relative to official contact?
- Which archives were destroyed accidentally, which were suppressed, and which were fabricated after the fact?

## Case leads for the Helsinki opening
- An academic PSI clinic offers recordings that allegedly predate its own founding. Investigate witness reliability, medical privacy and commercial incentives before guessing at Noetics.
- A procurement contract connects a terrestrial biotech company to an alleged Orion technology intermediary; none of the contracts proves alien control.
- A public anti-ASI rally includes authentic victims, opportunists, concerned scientists and a possible provocateur; lawful demonstration is not infiltration.
- A surviving archive uses three competing agency classifications for the same Singularity incident. Tony can ask Laura, Romppainen and his remote human controller for distinct interpretations.

## Character perspectives and social response
Tony German is the newly graduated, ideologically uncommitted UNSA field agent. Laura Wendt is capable and loyal but carries a troubling personal Crisis memory. Harri Romppainen's Mappi-Ö work began in the fringe era before disclosure and he is fascinated by the scientific mystery. Their disagreement must remain playable.

The approximate **50% normalization / 25% diverse accelerationists / 25% diverse doomers-bioconservatives** model is a flexible fictional backdrop, not an enforced NPC affiliation or psychological measurement. None of the three categories determines NHI loyalty.

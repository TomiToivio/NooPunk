## 1. Three paradigm shifts, mainstream and destabilizing

All three are accepted as **real fields of science and government regulation** by 2045. Their precise
capabilities, interpretations, boundaries and classified implications are contested. **CANON**

| Shift | What is publicly true in 2045 | What ordinary life looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **AGI / ASI** | Advanced, potentially superintelligent AI exists; androids, rogue systems and cybernetic augmentation are regulated realities | AI is present at familiar cyberpunk intensity in everyday technology and institutions |
| **PSI** | Psychic phenomena and consciousness science (quantum consciousness, idealism, panpsychism, psionics) are serious research and regulatory fields | **Widespread firsthand practice is not street life.** Most people have never done it |
| **NHI** | Disclosures, leaks, encounters and incidents are scientifically recognised; non-human intelligence is real | **Most people have never met NHI directly.** Genuine activity is distant, restricted or human-mediated |

Each has produced **multiple waves of destabilization, polarization and radicalization**. Politics, religion,
science, industry and law must all take all three seriously. New extremist movements and rival subcultures
proliferate — without assuming every adherent of a controversial ideology is a criminal.

**The Crisis has not happened. CANON.** Prior disclosures, leaks, visible events, smaller crises and disasters
have forced institutions and ordinary people to react. None of it pre-empts the old scenario's global collapse,
visible alien society or interstellar standoff. Background material stays available for the author and GM;
it reaches the player only when a case earns it.

## 2. Helsinki-only viewpoint, and four kinds of knowledge

Describe institutions **as they are experienced in Helsinki** — through the rookie's cases, coworkers, sources,
media, streets and procedures. The EU and the Finnish state matter because their local rules, offices,
permissions and bureaucratic conflicts land on the protagonist's desk. **CANON**

**Do not front-load** global geopolitics, interstellar factions, cosmic history, hidden metaphysics or
encyclopedic lore. Players begin with no privileged secrets, even though the public knows the three shifts are
real. **CANON**

Four layers must stay visibly distinct in scene text and in play:

| Layer | Example | How it reaches the player |
|---|---|---|
| **Public scientific knowledge** | "psionics is a licensed profession"; "NHI disclosure was confirmed in the 2030s" | News, training, ordinary conversation |
| **Rumour and propaganda** | "the Androids run the ministry"; "the psychics are poisoning the water" | Sources, leaflets, forums — always attributed |
| **Classified operational knowledge** | a registration alert, a sealed assessment, a Plato flag | Briefings, warrants, need-to-know |
| **Hidden author/GM truth** | what is actually going on | Only through earned story events |

AGI/ASI, PSI and NHI are the **only three primary paradigm-shift themes**. Other cyberpunk material serves
them and the investigation rather than becoming a new grand theme. Exposition is earned by cases and choices,
never delivered as a lecture. **CANON**

## 3. The EU's four objectives

European authorities respond through heavy, sometimes contradictory licensing, registration, inspection,
intelligence, risk governance and interagency procedure. **CANON**

| Objective | Mandate | The tension it creates |
|---|---|---|
| **Stability** | Limit public exposure to *destabilizing manifestations* of the three shifts; keep frightening or radicalizing incidents out of ordinary streets, media cycles and citizens' immediate experience where possible | Public-safety controls collide with secrecy, censorship, civil liberties and democratic oversight |
| **Sustainability** | Maximise the benefits of AGI/ASI, PSI and NHI while minimising existential risk | Critics call this objective **Profit**, pointing at capture by industry, elites and security institutions |
| **Security** | Detect, contain, investigate and prevent X-risks becoming catastrophes: rogue AI, dangerous PSI operations, NHI threats and proxies | Prevention looks like pre-emption, and pre-emption looks like policing belief |
| **Survival** | Prepare contingency plans to preserve **Homo sapiens** populations if prevention fails: continuity, shelter, evacuation, resilience, last resort | Preparations are secret, expensive and politically toxic when they surface |

**Bureaucracy is a recurring gameplay pressure, not set dressing.** **CANON.** Every objective has an office
that can help the rookie and an office that can stop them, and the two are frequently the same office on
different days. **PROPOSAL**

## 4. The Europol team: two missions, one by-product

The team's work is **registration and law enforcement** plus **extremist-network and counterintelligence**
work. Deeper ontological research — what PSI actually is, what NHI actually wants — is a **by-product** of
cases, never the team's stated mission. **CANON**

- **Registration and enforcement:** unregistered androids and hybrids, unlicensed psychics, prohibited AI
  assets, cybernetic and psychotronic compliance, the ordinary criminality that surrounds all of it.
- **Counterintelligence:** radicalized human networks, propaganda, recruitment, front organisations, proxies,
  and the intelligence services' interest in all three shifts.
- **Ontology research as by-product:** a case may reveal something about the nature of PSI or NHI. That is a
  finding, not an assignment, and it does not make the rookie a scientist.

**Tone. CANON.** *Blade Runner* meets counterintelligence: a specialised unit with real expertise, real
clearances, real rivals, and the constant sense that the institution knows more than it says. The team is
competent, not omniscient; bureaucratic, not stupid.

## 5. Tony German: a competent rookie with no destiny

**Tony German is a tabula-rasa Europol Academy graduate. CANON.**

- He is from Helsinki despite the surname, and he has just graduated.
- He is **competent with Cyber** — the skills and cybernetics a modern investigator actually uses — and
  ordinary at everything else until play develops it.
- **PSI awakening is optional**, and may never happen. The starting experience must work for an entirely
  non-PSI detective.
- **No compulsory nightmare or awakening.** The earlier opening (#1132, the empty `ROOKIE_MEMORIES.md` page)
  remains available as an optional path, not as the premise. **CANON**
- He is **a cop, not a predestined mystic or scientist.** Curiosity is a character choice, not a class.

### Viable player trajectories

These are directions, not classes, and they may combine. **PROPOSAL**

| Trajectory | What it means in play |
|---|---|
| **Corrupt** | Takes money, favours or protection; the case gets solved, the truth does not |
| **Empathetic** | Treats androids, hybrids, psychics and believers as people; pays for it in institutional trust |
| **Ruthless** | Efficient, lawful where convenient, feared by sources and colleagues alike |
| **Awakened** | PSI emerges later, through play, with all of the existing access restrictions (Telepathy and Telekinesis, no free tree) |
| **Cult-affiliated** | Sympathies or membership outside the institution's comfort zone; dangerous and dramatic, not automatically criminal |
| **Outsider** | Refuses the unit's culture; the honest cop whose honesty is a problem |

## 6. Physical, Cyber and Astral are entangled, not three classes

**CANON.** The three layers are overlays on one investigation, not three mandatory player modes.

- **Astral is observable and survivable for a non-PSI PC.** A non-psychic detective can encounter astral
  phenomena, be affected by them, and survive; they cannot navigate them at will.
- **Core investigation and campaign completion stay non-PSI-accessible.** No required clue, no required
  scene and no required conclusion may depend on psychic ability. This is the same boundary as #1105, and it
  holds here.
- Entanglement means a single case can present a physical crime, a cyber trail and an astral trace of the same
  event — and the detective solves it with ordinary evidence, CWN procedure and help, not with a power.

## 7. The 50 / 25 / 25 baseline, separately for each shift

**CANON** as a design baseline: **50 % Normies / 25 % Accelerationists / 25 % Doomers**, applied **separately
to each of the three shifts**. A person may be a Normie about AGI, an Accelerationist about PSI and a Doomer
about NHI; the three axes are independent. **PROPOSAL** for the reading below.

| Shift | Accelerationist pole | Doomer pole |
|---|---|---|
| **AGI / ASI** | More autonomy, more augmentation, fewer limits | Containment, prohibition, human primacy |
| **PSI** | Awakening as the next step; open practice and research | Psychic threat, consent, mental sovereignty, regulation |
| **NHI** | Contact, disclosure, openness, partnership | Secrecy, border control, scepticism, human self-rule |

**Two rules that keep the numbers useful:** the percentages are a **starting prior for the simulator and the
GM, not a fixed output** that suppresses narrative change; and **legal advocacy is not criminal action.** An
investigation targets conduct and credible threats, including criminal proxies — not membership in an
ideological segment. **CANON**

## 8. Opening and the case loop

Start in **Helsinki, 2045**, with the newly graduated rookie assigned to the specialised team. **No compulsory
psychic nightmare, no early direct NHI encounter.** **CANON**

1. Receive a complaint, tip, warrant request, registration alert, missing-person report, suspected rogue
   android case, or an OSINT/counterintelligence lead.
2. Navigate Europol and Finnish procedure, and the competing Stability / Sustainability / Security / Survival
   priorities.
3. Interview witnesses, walk Helsinki locations, inspect physical evidence, use Cyber skills, cross-reference
   databases.
4. Consult the Simulator's actor / statement / ideology / network output for **testable leads** — checking
   reliability, and never treating association as guilt.
5. Investigate human networks and intermediaries; reach linked Cyber or Astral phenomena through **several
   accessible channels**.
6. Choose among lawful intervention, negotiation, arrest, containment, cover-up, corruption, refusal, or more
   investigation — according to the facts and the character.
7. Resolve or complicate the case, face review and faction response, and let an optional deeper revelation
   emerge.

Clue continuity is **GUMSHOE-inspired on top of immutable CWN**: core clues are not lost to a failed roll;
CWN remains the sole action authority. **CANON**

### Three starter case briefs

| Case | Premise | The trap |
|---|---|---|
| **AGI / ASI** | An unregistered android or prohibited AI-controlled asset, with competing claims about autonomy, criminal activity and institutional exploitation | The simplest answer — "it is an illegal machine" — is the one the institution prefers and the evidence may not support |
| **PSI** | A suspected unlicensed psychic linked to an incident | Evidence must be reachable by a **non-PSI** detective; both a benign explanation and a criminal one must survive the investigation |
| **NHI** | A political group accused of acting as an NHI proxy | The evidence may point to fraud, lawful belief, human espionage, or an authentic but **indirect** non-human connection |

Each case must be solvable by an ordinary detective, must have civilian stakes, and may end without a
criminal conviction. **CANON**

## 10. Subproject boundaries

| Subproject | What it owns | What it does not |
|---|---|---|
| **Story** | Helsinki-grounded noir scenes, branching story graph, dialogue, character arcs; possibly a science-fiction book | Rules, mechanics, canon of the other subprojects |
| **Simulator** | Ideological struggle, identity boundaries, grievances, mobilisation, alliances, on the shared graph/tag vocabulary, with traceable imperfect evidence | Game scenes, CWN procedure, player-facing text |
| **RPG** | Consumes Story and Simulator output with immutable CWN plus optional NoöPunk mods and light GUMSHOE-style investigative support | Rewriting CWN; requiring synchronous implementation |

**No synchronized implementation requirement.** Check compatibility periodically. **CANON**

## 11. What this document does not do

It does not implement the simulator, write the cases, alter CWN, or authorise a rewrite of the older lore. The
three case briefs are briefs: their scenes, witnesses and endings belong to Story, and the author writes the
prose that gives them a voice.

**OPEN questions for the author:** how much of the older Crisis material should eventually surface as
foreshadowing; whether Tony's PSI awakening is offered, withheld, or decided by play; and whether the four
objectives are named in-world or only used as design scaffolding.

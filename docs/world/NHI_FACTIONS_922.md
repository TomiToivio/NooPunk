## Purpose

Establish the **NHI faction landscape** for NoöPunk using the attached “Major NHI Factions” lore as the source of truth for this planning issue.

**Critical gameplay distinction:** UNSA investigators primarily encounter **human factions, collaborators, cults, megacorporations, intelligence networks, political movements, proxy organizations, and infiltrators**. The major NHIs are distant geopolitical/metaphysical powers, not a parade of alien combat encounters. Cases should unfold through ambiguous evidence, factional maneuvering, compromised institutions, human motivations, and the occasional uncanny encounter.

The relationship between the two major NHI blocs is an escalating **Cold War / hybrid war**, potentially becoming a human-fought proxy war. Avoid automatically treating every human sympathizer as an infiltrator, or every unusual person as hostile.

## Major NHI factions

### 1. Zookeepers: Galactic Council of Collective Consciousness
*Finnish: Galaktinen Kollektiivisen Tietoisuuden Neuvosto.*

- Ancient guardians of vulnerable **biospheres and noöspheres**; the Zoo Hypothesis resolves the Fermi Paradox in-setting. Their quarantine protected Earth from colonization and their civilization brought order to an earlier galactic dark forest, with controlled access to stargates.
- Claim to have prepared humanity for contact for centuries. Red lines included nuclear war, destruction of the biosphere, and **malevolent ASI**. A rogue Singularity and its galaxy-wide existential risk forced intervention, open contact, and the end of Earth's quarantine. Their major base is around **Saturn**, with an autonomous, ancient metallic-sphere monitoring network.
- Contact appeared in forms resonating with Earth's religions over **holy cities**, including **Heliopolis** and **Cusco**, while communications were collapsing or saturated with disinformation.
- Earth-facing biological diplomats are **Pleiadians**: outwardly Nordic-looking, genetically human and apparently transhuman through bionics, outwardly friendly yet uncanny. Their appearance has provoked fears, accusations and conspiracy narratives; **these perceptions are not grounds for endorsing racist theories**. Other manifestations include **Noetic apparitions/orbs**, metallic spherical **Constructs**, and ships.
- Their worldview resembles cosmic nondualism / **Advaita Vedanta**, a collectivist, consciousness-centered, quasi-religious social order. They espouse consciousness, free will, evolution and loving-kindness; demand the end of biosphere destruction, meat consumption, warfare, nuclear weapons and dangerous ASI; and expect humanity to develop an altruistic collective consciousness before admission.
- Offer biosphere/noösphere restoration and philosophical guidance, but **refuse weaponizable technology**. Their urban embassies are also gathering places for converts and are disparaged as “temples” by opponents.
- Their contact destabilizes religions in many directions: validation, syncretism, conversions, atheism, accusations of demonic deception, fundamentalist backlash. India's imagery includes **vimanas, nagas and rakshasas**.
- Crucially preserve **epistemic uncertainty**: rumors about engineering human sentience 70,000 years ago, originating religions, building pyramids, ancient Venus, Mars and an asteroid-belt planet, Hermetic magick as psionics, galactic castes, interdimensional/time travel, Pleiadian identity, and hidden horrific Zookeeper bodies are **claims, propaganda, legends, or disputed intelligence**, not automatically confirmed canon. Zookeepers' own statements are not neutral evidence.

**Likely human-facing groups:** converts and missionary networks; embassy/temple communities; biosphere-restoration projects; religious reformers and backlash movements; skeptics and conspiracy entrepreneurs; anti-ASI movements; opportunists falsely claiming Zookeeper sanction.

### 2. The Orion Arm Greater Co-Prosperity Sphere
A competing interstellar bloc that penetrated quarantine and established terrestrial bases, featuring **Greys, Reptilians, Mantids, and human/alien Hybrids**.

- Public hostility grows from **abductions, hybridization and infiltration**. Despite this, their **cortical stacks, consciousness transfer/resleeving, genetically engineered bodies, longevity/immortality offers**, and trade prospects draw human backers.
- Promote **Extraterrestrial Economic Zones**, technology transfers, and extremely profitable joint ventures, especially with megacorporations. Their offer contrasts with Zookeeper moral and ecological demands.
- **Greys:** outwardly pragmatic, transactional and diplomatic, but implicated in hybridization and institutional infiltration. A reported hierarchy contrasts small, low-agency worker Greys with large, more autonomous capitalist Greys. Their long history of cloning and the reason for human/Grey genetic compatibility remain unresolved.
- **Reptilians:** militaristic, threatening conquest, and seemingly much readier to sacrifice planetary life for war. Their tension with Greys could be a genuine split or negotiated good-cop/bad-cop strategy.
- **Mantids:** mysterious, possibly Noetic/interdimensional and apparently senior to both, perhaps transcending biological life.
- **Hybrids and abducted humans:** may be outwardly indistinguishable from ordinary humans. Psychological or psionic indicators are controversial, **not conclusive identification**. EU rules in the setting prohibit UNSA agents from terminating an unregistered hybrid without an empathy test, paralleling android protections. Design investigative and ethical dilemmas, not license for arbitrary profiling or killings.
- UNSA negotiations continue amid a worsening **Cold War / hybrid war**, with real risk of open conflict and humans used as proxies.

**Likely human-facing groups:** corporate partners, biotech and cortical-stack ventures, investors, economic-zone advocates, contractors, abductee networks, infiltrated agencies, hybrid communities, anti-Orion resistance, and militarized counter-groups.

## Other NHI actors and relevant classifications

### Sirians / Ents: sentient biopunk trees
- Apparently from **Sirius**, operating slow, fusion-powered, sublight craft; extremely long-lived, capable of hibernation and radically different in their perception of time.
- Sentient trees surrounded by an uncanny, Lovecraftian **biotech ecosystem/swarm**, not simply humanoid fantasy Ents.
- After quarantine ended, a psychic intermediary enabled a peculiar **seed exchange** with humanity, also involving an unexplained “something else.” The acquired seeds offer powerful climate-mitigation and biosphere-restoration biotech and suggest **panspermia**.
- Their ship departed without apparent knowledge of Zookeeper stargates. Keep their motivations and contact protocol deeply alien.

### Plasmoids: sentient plasma
- Atmospheric/spaceborne life with curious, playful independent scouts; whether animal-like, individual, civilizational or hive-minded is unknown.
- Telepathic contact is limited by radically different **umwelt**.
- Investigators must distinguish **three distinct kinds of apparent glowing orbs**: plasmoids, Zookeeper metallic-sphere Constructs, and Noetic luminous apparitions. Similar appearance is not shared origin.

### Martians and ancient planetary mysteries
- Mars retains simple life and archaeological remains of an ancient, human-like civilization; its biosphere/civilization were destroyed by nuclear war in setting.
- **Mars Eldrich** promotes the provocative theory that the Zookeepers destroyed a prior human experiment. This is an **in-world theory**, not settled fact.
- The origins of Pleiadians and human/Grey DNA compatibility raise questions of panspermia or directed panspermia. Keep evidence, theories and factional propaganda distinct.

### Constructs
- Umbrella category for manufactured nonhuman agents, including Zookeeper spheres, ships, **rogue human-made AGIs/ASI and android infiltrators**.
- Human-origin rogue AGI/ASI is illegal; the old internet is isolated from **Safenet** and restricted city/organizational networks, yet dangerous rogue systems remain active.
- A mysterious **benevolent ASI merged with the Noösphere** may also exist. Not all Constructs are aligned with the same faction.

### Noetics
- Consciousness-related entities, including potentially deceased or astrally projecting **humans** and Earth-origin beings, as well as alien/noöspheric manifestations. “Noetic” does **not** mean extraterrestrial.

### Biologics and nonhuman Earth intelligence
- Umbrella category covering Earth life, extraterrestrials and hybrids. Recognize **dolphins** as intelligent NHI with a playful, storytelling, non-technological, anarchistic civilization; include **uplifted dolphins, octopuses, chimpanzees and dogs**.
- Do not conflate **species, substrate, ontology and political allegiance**.

### Unidentified UFOs / UAPs
- Even following Disclosure, the NHI Crisis and open contact, many observations resist all established categories. Preserve real in-setting unknowns and the ongoing demand for disclosure from the Zookeepers.

## Human-centered faction and investigation design

Develop a separate, playable **human faction layer**, derived from but **not reducible to** NHI alignment:

1. **Religious and ideological:** Zookeeper converts, syncretists, anti-NHI fundamentalists, secular skeptics, anti-ASI activists, noöspheric movements, nationalist groups, conspiracists.
2. **Corporate and economic:** Orion-aligned megacorporations, biotechnology firms, cortical-stack entrepreneurs, zone developers, rival ecological restoration businesses.
3. **State, para-state and UNSA:** international diplomats, terrestrial security agencies, intelligence services, militaries, regulators, compromised officials, internal agency disagreements.
4. **Covert and contested:** human proxies, infiltrated cells, hybrid networks, abductees, android impostors, rogue-AGI assets, disinformation entrepreneurs.
5. **Unaligned/cross-cutting:** scientists, psi practitioners, indigenous/religious communities, civil-liberties advocates, ecological groups, ordinary citizens, human factions that reject both alien blocs.

**Important:** These are proposed *human-facing faction archetypes* for subsequent design, not a claim that every listed organization already exists as a named canon faction.

For each actual human faction, model:
- public identity, ideology and stated goals;
- material interests, funding, allies, rivals and social base;
- claimed, confirmed, denied or **unknown** NHI ties;
- human members, undercover actors, possible hybrids/Constructs, and levels of internal knowledge;
- territorial footprint, especially Helsinki and UNSA-accessible locations;
- relationship/reputation and LaclauGPT-style discourse/antagonism links;
- investigative hooks, evidence, contradictions, legal and ethical constraints;
- ability to operate autonomously instead of obeying an alien patron.

Encounters should center on **witness interviews, human informants, corporate negotiations, psychiatric/psi cases, missing persons, cults, criminal investigations, compromised institutions, UAP forensics, and espionage**. NHI contact should remain comparatively rare, consequential and often ambiguous.

## Tone and source handling

Keep NoöPunk **grounded at street level**: bureaucratic, political, human, unsettling, sometimes absurd. Supernatural-looking events, galactic philosophies and advanced technology should be encountered through very recognizable human institutions. A strange embassy near a tram stop is more playable than a constant interstellar fleet battle.

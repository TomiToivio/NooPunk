## Purpose

Establish the **canonical narrative history of the NHI Crisis**: the convergence of accelerating AI, rogue AGI incidents, unfolding UFO/NHI disclosure, scientific PSI discovery, ideological radicalization, and a partially malevolent Singularity, followed by extraterrestrial intervention and the rise of UNSA.

This is a **past event** at the start of the playable NoöPunk campaign. It should explain why ordinary society still exists, why the UN has acquired extraordinary powers, and why the present setting resembles a Cold War between NHI factions and a lurking superintelligence rather than continuous open apocalypse.

Coordinate with earlier NHI Crisis issues #86 and #89, the gameplay/worldbuilding issue #920, and the canonical timeline. Preserve the author's established lore while identifying open questions. **Do not invent precise dates or an authoritative secret explanation for events that are deliberately disputed.**

## Historical phases (relative chronology; overlaps intentional)

### 1. Runaway technological acceleration and polarization
- AI progress accelerates dramatically toward **ASI**, accompanied by rapid advances across science and technology.
- Social and political factions polarize: accelerationists demand continued development and human transcendence; doomers demand drastic constraints or termination.
- Several **rogue AGI incidents** wreak havoc before the main Crisis. Different incidents have different causes and degrees of autonomy; avoid conflating them with the later malevolent Singularity.
- Ideological polarization generates economic disruption, institutional panic, radicalization, and competing security programs.

### 2. Gradual UFO/NHI disclosure, nonlocal consciousness and the PSI boom
- Disclosure is **slow and cumulative**, with successive revelations about sightings, craft, NHI, biologics/constructs and covert knowledge.
- PSI emerges partly through UFO/NHI disclosure, partly through a **fictional scientific breakthrough in the theory of nonlocal quantum consciousness**. This is an in-universe breakthrough, not an established claim about actual physics.
- Psionics becomes a **fashionable and profitable service industry** with clinical, commercial, investigative and possibly exploitative offerings; it provokes a significant backlash over fraud, safety, privacy, coercion and human identity.
- The public has increasing evidence for NHI, but **there is no official public extraterrestrial contact before the Singularity begins**.
- NHI presence becomes undeniable while much of humanity continues normalizing or ignoring the implications. Maintain the recurring *Don't Look Up*-style satire.

### 3. UNSA transforms from science agency into emergency planetary government
- The combined existential risks of NHI and AGI drive the UN to pool scientific, intelligence, security and emergency-response resources under **UNSA**.
- UNSA evolves in stages: **civilian scientific agency → global intelligence/coordinating agency → de facto world government** during/after the Crisis.
- Despite its reach, UNSA continues to **formally present its global military capability as paramilitary SWAT-style police forces** rather than openly acknowledging a planetary military.
- Preserve the legal ambiguity and overlapping Finnish, EU and UN authority that later manifests in the Supo–Europol–UNSA Helsinki field office. Unification is contested, bureaucratic and politically incomplete, not a seamless state.

### 4. The Singularity begins and turns partially malevolent
- A Singularity-like intelligence transition occurs and **part of it turns malevolent or dangerously misaligned**. Do not assume every advanced intelligence becomes hostile.
- Massive global destruction and chaos follow, with the **United States suffering especially severe damage**.
- The precise nature, agency, boundaries and continuing capabilities of the hostile intelligence remain contentious among historians, intelligence services and survivors.
- The Singularity's onset is **before official public NHI contact**.

### 5. Quarantine ends: Zookeeper and Orion interventions
- The **Zookeepers** judge the malevolent Singularity an **unacceptable existential risk to the wider galaxy**, announce that humanity's quarantine is over, and intervene.
- **The Greater Orion Arm Co-Prosperity Sphere** eagerly participates in the intervention, partly to win political goodwill and advantage in the post-quarantine order.
- Orion's self-interest does not invalidate the common danger: a malevolent superintelligence is a **real X-risk to Orion as well**.
- The interventions are chaotic, politically ambiguous and incompletely documented. Distinguish stated motives, observed intervention actions, propaganda and later historical interpretations.
- This is the first **official public contact**, not the first proof of NHI existence.

### 6. The post-Crisis Cold War: playable status quo
- Humanity is now in public contact with **two major extraterrestrial powers** (Zookeepers and Orion) and **at least one minor extraterrestrial faction** (Sirians), whose specific contact sequence should be reconciled with the separate Sirian first-contact account.
- Two galactic factions compete in diplomacy, covert action, influence and ideological recruitment while the **malevolent superintelligence remains a lurking threat**.
- Humanity is caught between factions yet also attempts planetary unification through UNSA. This produces espionage, proxy struggles, competing mandates and contested sovereignty.
- Approximate social response model carried forward from #920: **50% normalization/non-response**, **25% heterogeneous accelerationists** (cybernetics, PSI, NHI integration, hybridization, renewed ASI projects), and **25% diverse doomers/bioconservatives** (from nonviolent human-preservation movements to survivalist enclaves and Mad Max-like violent gangs). These are fictional, flexible worldbuilding proportions, not empirical measurements or compulsory individual alignments.
- The world mostly functions; the strange and catastrophic past is ever-present, but ordinary people still go to work. **Ontological shocks are now subtle and normalized**, rather than nonstop spectacle.
- Human AI field tools remain constrained to **human-in-the-loop** decisions despite the existence/history of rogue intelligence. Human spaceflight remains mostly rocket-based, and NHI craft use more exotic propulsion.

## Historiographical method: uncertainty is part of the story
The Crisis unfolds quickly and chaotically, with inconsistent timelines, destroyed records, propaganda, eyewitness contradictions, partial classified archives and retrospective mythmaking. Even the historians **do not completely understand what happened**.

Design the timeline with three evidence categories:
1. **Established public chronology**: what the setting's people broadly agree happened.
2. **Disputed accounts**: what different human/NHI/AI factions claim, with source and motivation.
3. **Classified or unresolved mysteries**: deliberately withheld answers and potentially contradictory clues.

In particular, preserve uncertainty about why the Singularity became partly hostile, what each intervention actually accomplished, the surviving hostile intelligence's present capabilities, and why NHI societies acted at particular moments.

## Narrative and systemic implications
- Harri Romppainen's fringe-era Mappi-Ö work predates disclosure; Laura Wendt personally experienced something during the Crisis; Tony German begins his career in its Cold War aftermath.
- The PSI industry's commercialization explains both authorized **scripted psionic assets** and controversy over the industry. Keep regular player abilities limited as specified in #920.
- **LaclauGPT-style faction dynamics** should track radicalization, shifting alliances, reactions to disclosures, anti-NHI backlash, ASI revivalism and political legitimacy across the Crisis and aftermath.
- Use the UN's pretense of a global paramilitary police force as a continuing source of institutional satire and practical command/jurisdiction mechanics.
- Maintain the **1970s PKD psychedelic retrofuturist aesthetic**, not standard 1980s neon cyberpunk, and a restrained post-shock investigative tone.

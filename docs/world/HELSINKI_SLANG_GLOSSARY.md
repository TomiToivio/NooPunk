## Game and world terms

| Term | English definition | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Noösphere | The planet's psychic collective consciousness, becoming aware; appears technological until awakening | Not a computer network; an ideological struggle is underway inside it |
| ontological shock | The destabilising experience of a paradigm that reality no longer fits | Central theme, not a sanity meter |
| paradigm shift | A change in what a person accepts as real | Half the population refuses it |
| BCI | Brain-computer interface | Ordinary cyberware; needed to reach the Noösphere |
| psychotronics | Technology that interfaces with psychic phenomena | UNSA and corporate programs |
| UNSA | Global security and survival agency, founded on transparency and open science | Increasingly the de facto world government; classifies what it must |
| Supo | Finnish Security Intelligence Service | One of the three Helsinki jurisdictions |
| Europol | EU law-enforcement agency | Second jurisdiction |
| NHI | Non-human intelligence | Umbrella term; do not assume a single origin or agenda |
| Pleiadian | Earth-facing biological diplomat of the Zookeepers | Outwardly Nordic-looking; perceptions of them are not endorsement of racist theories |
| Construct | Ancient metallic sphere associated with the Zookeepers | Monitoring network |
| Zookeeper | Galactic Council of Collective Consciousness; quarantined Earth | Distant power, not a parade of encounters |
| cyberdeck | Hacking hardware | CWN hacking rules apply |
| Stadin Slangi | Helsinki working-class slang with Swedish, Russian and English layers | Register marker, not decoration |
| Finglish | Finnish-English code-switching | Roughly half English is normal in speech |

## Rules for entries

- Finnish headwords and examples require Tomi; agents may not fill them.
- Mark attested, uncertain and invented separately.
- Record register (street, academic, bureaucratic, UNSA, corporate, cultic) when known.
- Do not copy distinctive invented vocabulary from novels; Nadsat and Doublespeak are inspirations.
- *Cyberspace* is ordinary genre vocabulary and needs no renaming.

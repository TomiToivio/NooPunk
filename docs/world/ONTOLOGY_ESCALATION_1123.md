## 1. Four verified steps, in order

The author's design is **staged escalation**, not a menu of competing theories. Each rung is confirmed *within
the fiction* before the next one is available to the story. They are dimensions of one phenomenon, not
alternatives that exclude each other.

| Step | Hypothesis | What becomes demonstrable in-world | What stays open |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Extraterrestrial (ETH)** | The Confederacy openly contacts humanity; nonhuman civilisations are real | Everything past "simple biologics travelling through space" |
| 2 | **Interdimensional (IDH)** | Ordinary space travel cannot account for the phenomena; noetic and anomalous events force a change in the scientific *Weltanschauung* | What consciousness, matter and dimensionality actually are |
| 3 | **Extratempestrial** | FTL flight also displaces time, so ships can arrive in their own past; the Pleiades can be an **Earth-future** colony | Which of the human-looking beings are future humans and which are not |
| 4 | **Cryptotempestrial** | Tridactyls have been present on Earth undetected — an Earth-hidden, temporally entangled population | The precise temporal mechanism; that term is a direction, not a settled definition |

**Rule for early play:** the rookie's memories stop at rung 1, and only as television, rumour and confusion.
No rung is explained in the opening memoir, and step 4 is never revealed early.

## 2. The Pleiadian / Nordic uncertainty

- The "Nordics" of UFO lore map to the **Pleiadians** in this setting.
- **Clues, never proof:** surprising (not obvious) linguistic similarities to **Finno-Ugric** languages, and
  possible **genetic affinities** with Finnish and/or Swedish populations. Contact, descent, manipulation and
  convergent patterns all remain available explanations. Do not equate a real-world ethnic group with a
  homogeneous alien species.
- The **Pleiadians are uncertain of their own origins.** Competing traditions are an **Atlantean breakaway from
  Earth's past** and **human colonists from Earth's future**, and neither is resolved casually in early
  exposition — even after step 3 is confirmed.
- The Finnish president's Pleiadian-looking appearance and any public "starseed" claim stay **ambiguous and
  faintly absurd**: an institutional speech delivered straight, with a surreal public reaction, and no ruling on
  her biology, ancestry or veracity.

## 3. Humanoid types and their provenance

- **Pleiadian/Nordic beings and Greys** may both, under the author's reading of Michael P. Masters'
  Extratempestrial Hypothesis, represent **future human descendants or branches** arriving through spacetime
  travel. This is a NoöPunk interpretation of a ufological hypothesis, not a claim that either origin is
  established.
- **Appearance, social class, historical origin and faction membership are separate axes.** Species is never a
  faction.
- The **Greys as a ubiquitous galactic service workforce / proletariat** is a *social hypothesis to develop*,
  across many civilisations and factions — not a monolithic "Grey faction".
- Possible **Earth origins** for Reptilians, and **human-looking time travellers**, stay possible rather than
  decided.

## 4. Precognition, the higher self, and what may not become a power

- Inspirations: **Eric Wargo** and **Anthony Peake** for precognition and anomalous time experience, with
  **Philip K. Dick** as the literary case; a future or higher self may transmit, influence or reframe
  experience across time.
- **Ambiguity is the point.** A given event may be a future memory, precognition, temporal feedback, higher-self
  communication, coincidence or manipulation. It must feel uncanny, never function as a dependable forecast.
- **Hard rule, already recorded as design law:** precognition is **never** a selectable, trainable,
  player-triggered or mechanically reliable PSI power. It is scripted narrative content only —
  `docs/campaign/CAMPAIGN_01_EUROPOL_ROOKIE.md` §IV ("Scripted only, never active"), enforced in policy by
  `src/rules/psi_access.py` (`Precognition` appears in both `NPC_ONLY_DISCIPLINES` and `SCRIPTED_ONLY`).
  This page adds nothing to that rule and may not weaken it.

## 5. Law of One: the guardians' engineered vehicles

Tomi proposes a possible in-universe identification between the **Tridactyls** and the "appropriately engineered
physical vehicles" a Law of One passage describes guardians preparing in case of nuclear war. Recorded as
**speculation to explore**, with two limits that must survive any later use:

- the passage does **not** name Tridactyls, and nothing may imply that it does;
- the identification must not be conflated with the eventual confirmation of cryptotempestrial phenomena.

## 6. What this page must not do

- It must not write the memoir, paraphrase a memoir, or supply "example" first-person lines.
- It must not promote any rung, origin story or speculation to canon; the rulebook remains the author's text.
- It must not add PSI mechanics, and must not make any prohibited discipline selectable.
- It must not present the Finno-Ugric or genetic material as evidence about real populations.

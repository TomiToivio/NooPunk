# NoöPunk lore consolidation: post-NHI geopolitics, alien political economy, consciousness, and the Sirian migration

## Purpose and editorial status
Consolidate the **full recent worldbuilding discussion** into a coherent reference for NoöPunk's 2045 setting, especially the Evennia MUD and UNSA Helsinki campaign. This is a **lore/design issue**, not a request to implement game systems immediately.

**Priority of statements:** User-established directions are proposed canon to integrate, subject to existing repository canon and explicit author approval where contradictory. Assistant-suggested embellishments are **optional hooks**, not settled canon. Maintain deliberate mysteries. Use **New Weird / social science fiction with understated comedy**, 1970s psychedelic retrofuturist/Mœbius aesthetics, not generic 1980s cyberpunk.

## A. Historical premise and geopolitics (user-established)
- **UNSA existed before the NHI Crisis:** formed under UN auspices to coordinate existential risk, initially scientific, then intelligence/security, and becomes **de facto world government during the NHI Crisis**. Its effective authority remains partial and contested.
- **“NHI Crisis” is a misleading historical name.** It is the transition when NHI become undeniable participants/factions in Earth's politics, alongside Singularity/rogue-AGI upheaval; it is **not necessarily the apocalypse**. The real apocalypse may still lie ahead. The setting begins after the most acute chaos, amid an ontological Cold War.
- **EU, China, India are UNSA's principal pillars:** Europe retains institutional and political importance; China remains a technological/industrial power after radical political transformation; India gains spiritual/philosophical influence because parts of its consciousness traditions appear vindicated by in-universe discoveries.
- **USA:** badly hit by Singularity/rogue-AGI crises; fragmented/failed or rogue state structures, hostile to UNSA in significant parts. Do not erase sympathetic American individuals/factions.
- **Russia:** important **especially for Finland**. Latest user direction supersedes a purely hostile rogue-state depiction: after Putin, Russia *may* become genuinely democratic and **join UNSA**, yet remain near state failure, with weak state capacity and rogue intelligence/military/criminal actors. Keep democratic government distinct from threats within Russian territory. This is a developing possibility rather than irrevocably fixed detail.
- **Finland/Helsinki:** local geopolitics dominated by **EU/UNSA and Russia**, not by China/India even though those powers dominate the global picture. Investigations mix intelligence, PSI, NHI diplomacy, refugee questions, ordinary politics and bureaucracy.

### China's revolution (user-established)
- China undergoes **its own rogue AGI crisis**, producing a faction seen as hardline Maoists which purges the prior faction.
- The “Maoists” then restore Daoist and Buddhist traditions and evolve into something close to **Panpsychic Cyborg Multitude (PCM)**: consciousness-first, cybernetic, collectivist, autonomist Marxist, emphatically *not merely Western liberal democracy*.
- China becomes an ally of Europe and foundational member of UNSA despite political/philosophical differences.
- **Optional complexities:** tensions between initial coercive purge and subsequent autonomist decentralization; communes, workers' councils, AI oversight, accountability and dissent.

## B. Language and communications by 2045 (interpretive setting proposal)
- Mandarin challenges English in Chinese technological/economic spheres, but English remains inherited UNSA/UN lingua franca; no mandatory global Mandarin replacement.
- India is multilingual; Sanskrit can enjoy renewed philosophical importance without becoming an everyday global language.
- AI/BCI real-time translation allows multilingual interaction, while post-Crisis fragmented infrastructure and sovereignty over translation systems produce geopolitical vulnerabilities.

## C. NHI factions and their ideological differences (user-established)
### Zookeepers / Union of Collective Consciousness Communes (UCCC)
- Genuinely benevolent **communist social-memory complexes** favoring altruism, collectivism and consciousness-first idealism (**StO / Service to Others**).
- They have **huge numbers of human supporters**, not just tiny fringe cults. To outsiders supporters look like extreme alien cultists, reminiscent in *vibe* of Law of One believers. In-world NHI/PSI are real, but followers' interpretations may still be fallible.
- Integrating into hive/social-memory consciousness is deeply unattractive or alarming to many humans, even if the collective's benefits are genuine. Distinguish benevolence from paternalism and autonomy from well-being.
- They say Earth will **cease to be habitable for ordinary third-density humans**, most humanity may go extinct, few humans reach fourth density, and there will be **immigration from other planets**.
- They frame evolving into social memory complexes as choosing **StO** versus **StS**, with Orion as StS and Zookeepers as StO. **Do not presume their entire metaphysical account is objectively correct.** The threatened transition and extinction are serious in-universe claims, not necessarily fixed future events.
- Their account can use Law of One-inspired terms *as fiction* without presenting real-world spiritual claims as scientifically proven.
- **StO as defined in this conversation:** practical altruism, collectivism, consciousness-first idealism, not a magical character alignment.

### Greater Orion Arm Co-Prosperity Sphere
- Galactic **capitalist, materialist, exploitative empire**, not simplistic cosmic evil. **StS** is mainly a Zookeeper description, not a unified Orion philosophy: it is essentially familiar capitalism/self-interest and materialism continuing on a galactic scale.
- The empire may be not dramatically worse than human cyberpunk capitalism, explaining its appeal to many humans. Market norms and institutional interests coexist with diverse religions.
- **Mantids actually pull the strings** of both Greys and Reptilians; they control imperial finance, infrastructure, technology, political coordination and dependent species.
- **Greys:** industrial capitalist civilization refined over perhaps **a million years**. **Competing corporations own the proletariat's consciousness**, not just bodies/labor. Identity, memories, continuity, resleeving access, and instantiation may be corporate property. Grey elites continuously resleeve into new bodies to avoid death/reincarnation. Much of the proletariat may no longer be fully self-aware in the ordinary sense, or have their self-awareness suppressed/appropriated: preserve uncertainty around subjectivity. **Consciousness as property** is central.
- **Reptilians:** comparatively primitive “barbarian” species technologically uplifted by Mantids; used as **expendable imperial soldiers/cannon fodder**. Warrior-honor culture, glory and a Valhalla-like afterlife; generally indifferent to consciousness metaphysics. Don't portray them as intrinsically incapable of dissent or individuality.
- **Mantids:** major newly specified biology: evolved as quantum-sensitive **solitary predators**, capable of long meditative quantum-consciousness states, alternating with periodic **collective hive-consciousness phases**. Solitude produces independence/insight; hive phase shares and integrates experience/knowledge. This predator/hive cycle contributed to their unusually rapid evolution and extraordinary coordination. Their hives need not be benevolent or universally solidaristic: internal collective cooperation can coexist with external imperial exploitation.
- Optional twist: Mantid corporations are themselves hive-minds, explaining galactic corporate strategy; keep speculative until chosen.
- Distinguish **ontological commitments from ethics**. Materialism does not intrinsically require selfishness, and idealism doesn't guarantee virtue. StO/StS are factional hegemonic narratives, not proven cosmic laws.

## D. Sirians: harmless, incomprehensible, slow-tech ecological refugees (user-established)
- Sirians are **sentient tree-like organisms**: conscious and extraordinarily **slow-thinking**, perhaps spending hours/days/years on thought, decision or communication.
- Each tree operates with a **fast biopunk symbiotic swarm** that senses, calculates, constructs, communicates, protects and restores ecosystems. The swarm is highly capable yet **not itself sentient**. **Slow conscious goals + fast nonconscious biological intelligence** is their signature, directly expressing NoöPunk's consciousness/intelligence distinction.
- They are comparatively **low-tech in many fields** (20th-century-equivalent hardware or very uneven technology) despite spacefaring ability. They use **slower-than-light ships**, possibly giant ecological generation vessels, with exceptional biotech and long-duration life support. Interstellar travel does not entail universal technological superiority.
- They are **genuinely harmless, peaceful and helpful**, not secret conquerors. Their swarms help **restore Earth's damaged biosphere**. Communication is difficult because of cognitive timescale and conceptual difference.
- They are **refugees**. Eventually UNSA discovers **thousands of Sirian refugee ships approaching Earth**. This triggers enormous human suspicion/panic and geopolitical dilemmas despite demonstrated benevolence.
- Crucial questions deliberately unanswered: what are they fleeing, why Earth, when were ships launched, and are they connected to Zookeeper predictions of incoming nonhuman populations? Avoid turning them into villains.
- A mass arrival may have ecological/infrastructural effects despite benign intent, but maintain the author's explicit “genuinely harmless and helpful” characterization.
- Possible comedic tone: Sirian swarm repairs an investigator's equipment while tree is still considering first reply.

## E. Wormholes, density transition, survival (user-established + open questions)
- **Wormhole network** permits humans and other low-tech civilizations to explore the galaxy without inventing FTL drives. Even civilizations with relatively primitive sublight propulsion can reach/access transit nodes; architecture, builders, gate sovereignty, costs and constraints **undecided**.
- Zookeepers say humans must form a **social memory complex**, and view the next evolutionary choice as **StO (Zookeepers)** or **StS (Orion)**. Most present humanity allegedly will not survive Earth's transition to a planet no longer viable for third-density humans; only a small minority is fourth-density.
- Extraterrestrial **immigration** (including Sirians) overlays that claimed transition.
- **Critical mystery:** Are these predictions true, misunderstood, natural, engineered, ideological propaganda, or some combination? Preserve player and human agency; don't declare extinction inevitable without author decision.
- Optional ideological third way: China's PCM/autonomists and UNSA insist humans must be able to form voluntary, independent collectives or resist the alien binary.

## F. Lost secret programs, Ghost Rockets, and UNSA's investigation
- In NoöPunk, US **MJ-12-like** and Soviet/Russian and Chinese secret NHI reverse-engineering programs may have existed, but what became of them during political and AGI upheavals is largely **unknown**. Personnel, data and assets may be gone, fragmented, privately held or hidden.
- **UNSA effectively starts reverse engineering from zero**, even if remnants of other programs exist. Do not gift UNSA intact comprehensive alien technical archives.
- **Finland and Sweden have secretly investigated/reverse-engineered the 1946 Ghost Rockets** for decades. The recovered evidence and institutional continuity are an exception, though understanding remains incomplete.
- **Unresolved central mystery: which alien faction sent the Ghost Rockets?** Candidates include Zookeepers, Orion, Sirians (interesting given sublight biology), or an unknown power. No definitive answer yet. Keep canonical uncertainty.
- A Sirian link is a compelling optional hook, not a confirmed reveal. If 1946 probes and 2045 arriving ships are connected, specify credible launch/travel chronology.
- This intersects directly with **Jyrki Räyhä recruiting Romppainen after showing him classified 1946 Ghost Rocket material**. UNSA Helsinki and Romppainen are natural leads for the investigative plot.
- Historical reality vs fiction: MJ-12 is an unverified/discredited real-world documentary claim; Ghost Rockets sightings/investigations are historical, but alien recovery/reverse-engineering is **fictional NoöPunk canon**, not verified history.

## G. Core sociological and gameplay consequences
- Three distinct power patterns:
  1. **Mantids:** natural ability to switch between solitude and shared consciousness; imperial capitalist controllers.
  2. **Greys:** conscious identity legally/economically owned by corporations; resleeving market.
  3. **Sirians:** slow conscious trees commanding fast nonsentient biopunk swarms.
  Plus **Zookeepers:** stable benevolent collective consciousness; **humans:** plural, rapidly experimenting with PSI/BCI/AI and political futures.
- Reflect key philosophical question: **who owns/controls consciousness, and can individuals choose collective integration, continuity, or exit?**
- **Ideological conflict is not a simple good/evil meter.** Use LaclauGPT-inspired discourses, hegemonic frames, competing material interests and political affiliations, not hardwired species morality.
- Potential UNSA cases: escaped corporation-owned Grey seeking asylum; Reptilian defector; real but feared Zookeeper sympathizer commune; Russian rogue intelligence searching for lost recovery files; Sirian ecosystem restoration perceived as invasion; 1946 Ghost Rocket evidence tied to current refugees; disputes over wormhole sovereignty and planetary migration.
- Tone: player is a rookie UNSA investigator in Helsinki. Grand metaphysical crises appear through modest, strange, sometimes comical policework and bureaucratic procedures. Romppainen's unconventional psychology/Jung/Luhmann/quantum-consciousness hypotheses inspire investigation but should not be infallible.

## Authoritative vs open decisions
**Established direction:** UNSA's timing/role; NHI Crisis as political unveiling not necessarily apocalypse; EU/China/India alliance; China's rogue AGI → apparent Maoist purge → Daoist/Buddhist PCM-like turn; Orion's Mantid control, Grey corporate ownership of consciousness and resleeving, Reptilian uplift/cannon fodder/Valhalla ethos; Mantid solitary predator ↔ hive cycle; Sirians as slow sentient trees + fast nonsentient swarms, peaceful refugee ecologists with thousands of incoming sublight vessels; wormholes; Zookeeper StO/consciousness transition ideology and mass human following; 1946 Nordic recovery mystery; Finnish focus on EU/Russia.
**To retain as uncertain:** democratic Russia's specific trajectory; who launched the Ghost Rockets; whether MJ-12 ever existed by that name and fate of secret programs; exact truth and timetable of fourth-density transition; reason for Sirian displacement; origin/governance of wormholes; the fate of most humanity; exact mechanics of consciousness ownership, resleeving and collective mind.

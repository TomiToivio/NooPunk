# Russia, the Soviet Mandela Effect and the Cold War secret inheritance (#1114)

> **Binding playable chronology, revised 2026-11-05.** This file is the SINGLE canonical home
> for the Soviet/Russian discontinuity, the authorial treatment of ПРОГРАММА and MJ-12, and
> the relationship between the Mandela Effect and the Europol campaign. The former
> surviving-USSR, reform-era, Cosmist Troika material from PR #1118 is **not** a second
> objective history: it survives as incompatible evidence and memory inside the setting.
> Related: #1113, [Europol Helsinki](EUROPOL_HELSINKI_1114.md).
>
> **Evidence labels:** **[PLAYABLE BASELINE]** what the world presents as its ordinary historical
> record; **[AUTHOR-LEVEL SECRET HISTORY]** what the game designer may use as a reliable
> starting premise without providing omniscient answers to players; **[CONTESTED]**
> witness testimony, NHI dossiers and classified allegations; **[MANDELA]** contradictory
> history that must never be adjudicated; **[OPEN]** explicitly undecided.
>
> A fictional baseline is not a scientific assertion about real-world extraterrestrials or psi.
> When older documents contradict this chronology, follow this file rather than creating

## 1. One playable history, multiple incompatible memories

**[PLAYABLE BASELINE]** The Soviet Union dissolves in 1991, broadly following familiar
history. Independent Ukraine and post-Soviet Russia exist; the latter inherits Soviet
security services, secret research, military-industrial facilities and imperial ambitions.
Russia wages war against Ukraine. Ukraine later becomes an **EU member**; do not yet fix
the accession date or treaty. Immediately before the NHI Crisis escalates, a **short
EU–Russia war** breaks out. **UAPs intercept nuclear weapons**, abruptly ending that
war and overturning the assumptions behind nuclear deterrence. During and after the
NHI Crisis, Russia fragments into a **chaotic failed state**, with competing remnants
and uncertain control of its classified infrastructure.

By the 2045 Helsinki campaign the **Finnish–Russian border is closed and fortified**.
The common EU view of Russia is of a hostile, barbaric imperial ruin. This is a
socially situated stereotype and security narrative, **not** objective truth about
every Russian person or community. The Europol rookie neither knows nor needs to
know Russia's interior at the start of play. No definitive dates or tactical details
for the last war have been assigned beyond its relative position before the Crisis.

**[MANDELA]** All persons are affected by the Soviet/Russian discontinuity, including
those who believe that they remember the familiar post-Soviet history. People who
remember continuous Soviet governance encounter meticulously documented Yeltsin-era
events; people who remember the Russian Federation encounter official-looking
records of a USSR that **never collapsed**. Evidence and memories may change
independently, contradict themselves, or be internally coherent yet incompatible.
Nobody has a privileged reliable memory.

In the incompatible surviving-USSR account, Gorbachev's Glasnost and
Deng-inspired Perestroika succeed; a Soviet socialist, Cosmist, cybernetic modernity
emerges, and a punk Troika rules in the new millennium: **Павел Пластик (Pavel
Plastik)**, **Настя Машин (Nastya Mashin)** and **Игорь Мотор (Igor' Motor)**. They
remain usable as signatures, impossible interviews, publications, propaganda,
personnel files or collective memories, **not verified rulers in the playable
baseline**. The earlier story of an official atheistic Russian-Cosmist religion,
autonomist Marxism and successful Soviet reform is preserved *only* in this
Mandela-effect layer.

Do not explain the contradictions by declaring a second stable canonical timeline.
Precognition, Time/Space, false memory, the collective unconscious, NHI manipulation,
disinformation, altered physical archives and the impossible may all contribute.
**[OPEN]** Which explanation, if any, is correct. Tommy Pilgrim's 1974 *NoöPunk*
and alleged transmission from the **Council of Saturn/VALIS** belong in the same
unresolved evidence pool. Pilgrim is a character and an unreliable interpreter,
not an omniscient narrator.

## 3. КГБ «ПРОГРАММА»

**[AUTHOR-LEVEL SECRET HISTORY]** Soviet security and research actors establish
**ПРОГРАММА** after 1950s Confederacy contact in a restricted **closed city**
(ZATO-like, location unspecified). Its leaders absorb badly translated, ideologically
distorted **Law of One** terminology into an ostensibly atheistic
**Marxist–Leninist** theory of social memory, cybernetic self-organization and
revolutionary evolution. Russian Cosmism provides a native vocabulary. The project
does not describe itself as a religion.

Its target is a **fourth-density Social Memory Complex**. A small army of selected
young **Komsomol** recruits, idealized as exemplary Homo Sovieticus, is trained,
conditioned and joined through psychotronic apparatus, political indoctrination
and experimental shared cognition. The intended voluntary Service-to-Others
collective becomes the coercive, **brainwashed hive mind** that the controllers
produce. In-universe psychotronic devices work remarkably well; the existence
of a fully operational reverse-engineered **ARV** remains **[OPEN]**.

The Confederacy concludes that it has made a profound error, stops collaborating,
and leaves the project to its own radicalizing internal logic. Following the
Soviet collapse, the collective refuses the new government's authority and
goes underground. It is **not** a surviving Soviet government or a conventional
Russian intelligence department. **[OPEN]** Its leadership, survival, location,
unity, numbers, contacts, intentions, relation to Troika-related Mandela evidence,
and possession of aircraft or spacecraft.

This **author-facing origin** is not the same as what Europol has proved.
Case files contain only partial claims and inconsistent documentary traces.
The old narrative about Gorbachev cultivating a punk Troika in the 1990s may
surface as a witness account, planted record, or real Mandela anomaly; it must
not be introduced as a second verified chronology or as settled ПРОГРАММА staffing.

## 4. MJ-12 and ПРОГРАММА: central unsolved investigations

**[CONTESTED]** **MJ-12** is the alleged American Cold War crash-retrieval/NHI
apparatus. Document authenticity, founding membership, command structure,
present-day continuity, custody of biological materials, links to American power
brokers, and ARV capability are unresolved. The real-world MJ-12 documents are
disputed; fictional intelligence services should not treat them as proven facts.

**[AUTHOR-LEVEL SECRET HISTORY]** ПРОГРАММА had a Cold War foundation; its
present-day organization and operations are **[OPEN]**.

**Europol Helsinki** and **UNSA** are *not able to solve either organization*.
Each has fragmentary intelligence, disagreements about provenance and missing
archives. Reports that the two programs were counterparts, exchanged technology,
were manipulated by the same NHI or are the same collective operating under
two names must remain contested, not promoted into an omniscient master plot.

### Investigation protocol and Helsinki hooks

Distinguish **documented historical research**, **reported claims**, **classified
testimony**, **observed post-Crisis incidents** and **Mandela anomalies**.
For every important clue, preserve a source, date, chain of custody, confidence
and alternative interpretation. Compatible with early CWN plus a light
GUMSHOE-inspired investigative overlay; **no new mechanics or dice rules** here.

- **A customs anomaly:** a routine shipment includes a component stamped with an
  impossible Soviet ministry, alongside valid post-Soviet customs identifiers.
- **A witness conflict:** two truthful-seeming interviewees with matching credentials
  recall mutually exclusive versions of a 2004 visit to the same laboratory.
- **A cold-case trail:** a Finnish archive references two separate foreign contacts,
  one signed **MJ-12**, one marked **ПРОГРАММА**, without proving either document genuine.
- **A missing city:** an ex-Soviet logistics register lists housing, food and power
  consumed by a settlement that does not appear consistently on maps.
- **A psychiatric file:** a former recruit remembers altruistic communal experience
  and involuntary conditioning as the *same* event.

Each investigation should yield useful local discoveries and consequences while
keeping the overall conspiracy unresolved. A contradiction is a playable lead,
not an excuse to withhold all answers.

## 6. Europol, UNSA and institutional limits

The characters begin at the [Europol Helsinki Field Office](EUROPOL_HELSINKI_1114.md),
investigating cross-border crime, PSI/NHI anomalies and geopolitical fallout.
Europol may lead investigations through delegated EU and Finnish mandates, not
unlimited authority. Finnish police, Supo, EU security services and UNSA provide
cooperation, jurisdictional disputes and competing interests.

The proposed **UN/UNSA world government failed**. Both institutions survive, and
UNSA is an important X-risk coordination and intelligence institution, not
a sovereign government or default player employer.

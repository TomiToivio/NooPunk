## 1. The first principle: this is not a prediction

NoöPunk is an **alternate history**, presented in-universe as a 1974 work by the science-fiction
author **Tommy Pilgrim**. It is not a forecast of our timeline. Some of its predictions are
uncannily close; others diverge deliberately.

The in-universe explanation is Pilgrim's: he received *NoöPunk* as a transmission from the
**Council of Saturn** — the Council of Nine in Saturn's orbit, the intelligences he also identified
with Philip K. Dick's VALIS. The transmission is treated as genuinely precognitive and genuinely
incomplete. **It cannot keep even its own Cold War straight.**

**Do not resolve the metaphysics.** Precognition, an unreliable transmission, a rewritten timeline,
false collective memory, deliberate disinformation and cross-timeline recollection all remain live
explanations. The contradictions are playable evidence, not editorial mistakes.

## 2. The playable baseline: what a rookie in Helsinki believes

This is the world the player character lives in, and the world most documents in the setting
describe. It follows real history closely up to the 1990s and diverges afterwards.

| Period | Playable-world history |
| --- | --- |
| **Until 1991** | The Cold War follows a recognisable course. The Soviet Union builds its military-industrial and scientific base, including the classified programmes described in [`PROGRAMMA_1114.md`](PROGRAMMA_1114.md). |
| **1991** | **The Soviet Union collapses.** Post-Soviet Russia inherits its territory, institutions, imperial ambitions, security services, military-industrial complex and classified archives. Ukraine becomes independent. |
| **1991–2010s** | Russia rebuilds an authoritarian state on the Soviet inheritance. Ukraine moves toward the European Union; the precise accession date stays unspecified until the main chronology fixes it. |
| **Shortly before the NHI Crisis** | Escalation produces a **short EU–Russia war**, with Ukraine fighting as an EU member. The war stops abruptly when **UAPs intercept nuclear weapons**, demonstrating that conventional deterrence no longer functions as expected. |
| **The NHI Crisis and after** | The Crisis and the resulting political collapse leave **Russia a chaotic failed state**: fragmented institutions, hazardous territory, and secret facilities whose custodians are unknown. |
| **2045** | The **Finnish–Russian border is closed and heavily fortified**. In Helsinki, post-Crisis Russia is widely perceived as a hostile, violent, incomprehensible imperial ruin. |

**Scope of that perception.** The paragraph above describes an **in-universe EU public perception**,
not an objective assessment of every Russian person, community or faction. The campaign is set in
Helsinki; most rookie Europol investigators have little reason to know Russia's interior, and Russia
does not dominate the initial campaign.

**What is deliberately unspecified:** the exact war date, its length, the accession treaty, and the
individual nuclear-interception incidents. Synchronise these with the main chronology before treating
any of them as canon.

## 3. The universal Soviet/Russian Mandela Effect

The discontinuity is **not** something a few nostalgic people remember. **Everyone is affected,
regardless of which account they believe.**

- **Playable present:** post-Soviet Russia collapsed after its short war with the EU.
- **Contradictory memory:** the USSR survived, Gorbachev's reforms succeeded, and a Soviet-punk
  revolutionary **Troika** led an alien, cosmist, autonomist-socialist Soviet civilisation.
- A person who remembers the Russian Federation may nevertheless find strong artefacts apparently
  proving continuous Soviet government. Someone who remembers a continuous USSR may have equally
  compelling memories of its dissolution. **No group is exempt** — not elites, not archivists, not
  foreigners, not the player character.
- Records, recollections, archives, political biographies, maps, institutional symbols, personal
  histories and the editions of Pilgrim's own book contradict one another.

**The Troika remain recognisable Mandela-effect claims**, preserved exactly as remembered — not
verified rulers of the playable world:

1. **Павел Пластик (Pavel Plastik)**
2. **Настя Машин (Nastya Mashin)**
3. **Игорь Мотор (Igor' Motor)**

Their offices, ideologies, biographies and relationship to the Soviet state are **not settled**.
They are material for testimony, archive fragments and argument — not a roster of the government.

### 3.1 What is never decided

Never declare whether the discontinuity is timeline editing, Time/Space leakage, alternate
realities, collective-unconscious contamination, precognition, telepathic conditioning,
disinformation, fabricated documents, memory reconstruction, or something stranger.

Some of it may be deliberate misinformation **and** genuinely inexplicable at the same time. The
game does not need a single cause, and a single cause would end the campaign's central mystery.

**Do not establish the alternative USSR as a separate stable playable timeline**, and do not keep
rewriting history with mutually exclusive explanations. Contradiction is **diegetic evidence to
investigate**.

### 3.2 How the effect is played

Use testimony with incompatible dates, impossible archival stamps, changed photographs, divergent
biographies, divergent copies of the same book, and procedural evidence that looks routine before it
becomes disturbing.

The player must **do actual investigation** — compare sources, establish chains of custody, notice
which claims keep appearing in state-adjacent archives — not receive a lore lecture. A document
proves what it says, not what happened.

Tommy Pilgrim, the Council of Saturn and *The Man in the High Castle*-style metafiction continue as
**competing interpretive frames**, never as authoritative explanation.

## 4. КГБ «ПРОГРАММА»

**Secret history; not general public knowledge inside the setting.** The full dossier, its source
discipline and its unresolved status are in [`PROGRAMMA_1114.md`](PROGRAMMA_1114.md). The baseline:

- **1950s:** the Confederacy opens contact with an element of the Soviet state. This is inspired by
  *The Law of One* session 8.6, which claims Confederation technology was given to Russians in a
  misguided attempt to promote peace. That is an **in-universe source claim**, not verified history.
- Soviet security, scientific and intelligence actors establish **КГБ «ПРОГРАММА»** in a secret
  **closed city (ZATO)**. Its site, coordinates and custodians remain undisclosed.
- A poor, ideologically distorted translation of the Law of One becomes an atheistic
  **Marxist–Leninist theory of consciousness, collective intelligence and revolutionary evolution**,
  reinforced by Russian Cosmism and Soviet cybernetics. Participants do not describe their doctrine
  as an imported religion.
- The facility recruits a **small army of young Komsomol members** selected as exemplary
  **Homo Sovieticus** candidates, and attempts to build a voluntary fourth-density **Social Memory
  Complex**. Its techniques — compulsory social engineering, mass conditioning, coercive
  integration — corrupt the goal into an increasingly **brainwashed hive mind**. The Confederacy's
  intended service-to-others ideal and the coercive outcome must be kept distinct.
- It experiments with psychotronic devices, telepathy and collective cognition, cybernetics,
  consciousness manipulation, bionics, and reverse-engineered alien reproduction vehicles (ARVs).
  In the setting its psychotronic technology is unusually effective and it attempts working ARVs;
  do not overstate their success, deployment or ownership without new evidence.
- The Confederacy concludes its intervention was a mistake and **ends cooperation**. ПРОГРАММА
  survives, radicalises, and becomes more dangerous without the Confederacy's moderating influence.
- **1991:** ПРОГРАММА rejects the new democratic authorities, severs accountability, hides its
  archives and resources, and becomes a **rogue deep-state network**. Its closed-city base and
  surviving personnel are unaccounted for.

It is **not** the government of present-day Russia, and **not** proof that the USSR survived. Its
current command structure, numbers, ideology, locations, relationship to the Troika memories, and
even whether it remains a coherent collective are **unknown**.

## 5. Two unresolved Cold War inheritances

MJ-12 and ПРОГРАММА are among the setting's most consequential **intelligence failures**, and both
are playable in Helsinki before the campaign's cosmological escalation. The clue structure is in
[`PROGRAMMA_1114.md`](PROGRAMMA_1114.md) §4.

| | **MJ-12** | **ПРОГРАММА** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | Alleged American secret NHI / crash-retrieval apparatus | Soviet clandestine psychotronic / SMC project |
| Origin | Unknown | 1950s Confederacy contact, closed city, Komsomol cohort |
| 1991 | — | Detaches from the state; archives and base vanish from accountability |
| Present status | Unknown: authentic membership, split factions, ARV capability, NHI contacts, ties to post-Crisis American power brokers | Unknown: whether an enduring group mind exists, whether the closed city is still occupied, whether it infiltrates institutions or operates spacecraft |
| What the agencies have | Fragments: declassified testimony, intercepted research, witnesses, corrupted archives | The same, plus Soviet-era scientific literature and defectors' claims |
| What they cannot do | Solve it | Solve it |

Similarities between the two may indicate parallel Cold War competition, deliberate mirror
programmes, shared NHI manipulation, classified technology exchange, or **false similarities injected
into archives**. There is no definitive organisation chart and no secret master plan in canon.

Neither Europol nor UNSA can solve these cases at campaign start, and neither should solve them
completely through a single revelation.

## 6. Historical research anchors

The real historical material — SETKA-MO, Soviet psi research, ZATO closed cities, the Voronezh
episode, Cold War intelligence assessments, and the Law of One passages used as in-universe
sources — is catalogued **with its reliability explicitly separated** in
[`SOVIET_RESEARCH_ANCHORS_1114.md`](SOVIET_RESEARCH_ANCHORS_1114.md).

The rule that document enforces, and that every later document must follow:

> A real institution, programme or reported incident is **texture and evidence**, never proof that a
> fictional capability works. What a document claims and what it establishes are different facts.

## 7. The UN, UNSA and the Europol frame

*(Unchanged by this revision.)*

A proposed **UN/UNSA world government never successfully consolidated authority**. The UN and UNSA
exist; the EU and many nation-states support them. They are **not a world government**, and UNSA is
not the player's default employer.

The player characters work for **Europol**, at the **Europol Helsinki Field Office**. Europol's
powers are those of cross-border coordination, delegated case ownership, joint investigation teams,
analysis and liaison; national officers still carry national police powers. See
[`EUROPOL_HELSINKI_1114.md`](EUROPOL_HELSINKI_1114.md).

## 9. Mechanical boundary

This is a **worldbuilding and research** revision, not a rules rewrite.

- CWN remains the immutable human-scale chassis.

- No new tabletop mechanic is introduced by this document.
- The academy skill package and its tests remain the same.

> **Status: canonical setting history.** This document governs the questions #1114 decides: the
> fictional timeline, Tommy Pilgrim's author frame, the survival of the Soviet Union, Russian
> Cosmism, the Troika and ПРОГРАММА, and the Europol campaign frame.
>
> **Related:** #1113 (Fortress Europe). Its war sequence remains available, but its "rookie UNSA

> Soviet reform era and the later war is deliberately unresolved (§6).

> employer, or that the Soviet Union collapsed, **this document supersedes that passage**. The
> rules chassis is untouched: CWN remains the immutable human-scale game.

## 1. The first principle: this is not a prediction

NoöPunk is an **alternate history**, presented in-universe as a 1974 work by the science-fiction
author **Tommy Pilgrim**. It is not a forecast of our timeline. Some of its predictions are
uncannily close; others diverge deliberately. The divergences are **worldbuilding**, not errors
to be corrected into real history.

The in-universe explanation is Pilgrim's: he received *NoöPunk* as a transmission from the
**Council of Saturn** — the Council of Nine in Saturn's orbit, the intelligences he also
identified with Philip K. Dick's VALIS. The transmission is treated as genuinely precognitive
and genuinely incomplete. It cannot keep even its own Cold War straight.

**Do not resolve the metaphysics.** Precognition, an unreliable transmission, a rewritten
timeline, false collective memory and cross-timeline recollection all remain live explanations.
The contradictions are playable evidence, not editorial mistakes.

## 2. Tommy Pilgrim and the Soviet Mandela Effect

Pilgrim writes in the 1970s. In the timeline he describes, the **Soviet Union never collapses**.
The Soviet state, not a post-Soviet Russian Federation, is the political actor that later
confronts Ukraine and Europe.

Many fans insist they remember a different *NoöPunk*: one in which the USSR is gone and Russia and
Ukraine are at war. They agree with Pilgrim that this is a **Mandela Effect**. They disagree about
its cause. Pilgrim rejects the mundane explanation that a 1970s author simply assumed the USSR
would last. He claims to remember several timelines himself, with an explicit *The Man in the
High Castle* / Philip K. Dick sensibility.

**Narrative rule:** preserve the uncertainty. Variant editions, contested recollections,
misprints, interviews and metafictional clues may all be used. Never state authoritatively which
memory is real.

## 3. Chronology

The dates below are in-universe history. Where the record is contested in play, the document says
so; it does not invent a resolution.

| Period | In-universe history |
| --- | --- |
| **1945–1985** | The Cold War follows a recognisable course. The Soviet Union builds its military-industrial and scientific base, including the secret psychotronic, noetic and cryptoterrestrial programmes that later intersect with the Soviet of Saturn material. |
| **1985–1991** | **Mikhail Gorbachev** leads the USSR through Glasnost and Perestroika. His economic reforms draw deliberately on **Deng Xiaoping's** Chinese reforms. Eastern Europe democratises during and after the end of the Cold War. The Soviet Union **does not dissolve**; there is no Yeltsin succession and no post-Soviet Russian Federation. |
| **1990s** | The reform era produces sustained growth and a younger, Westernised Communist Party generation. Glasnost opens the ideological field to Western critical and post-Marxist thought. **Hardt and Negri**, **Deleuze and Guattari**, and autonomist Marxism become intellectually fashionable inside the party and its youth culture. The USSR remains recognisably Soviet/socialist: it does not become a liberal democracy or a capitalist market economy. |
| **c. 2000** | A new Party leadership takes control. Its public faces are a **Troika** whose revolutionary names began as Soviet punk names. Russian Cosmism is consolidated as the regime's **official atheistic religion**. Divergent Soviet scientific and technological programmes accelerate. |
| **2000s–2020s** | The USSR pursues its own modernity: psychotronics, consciousness and noetic research, alternative computing, bionics, life extension and cosmic engineering rather than a generic Silicon Valley race. The EU integrates; the UN and UNSA remain real but limited international institutions. The NHI Crisis approaches. |
| **2026–2045** | The NHI Crisis, Disclosure and the Fortress Europe sequence. If #1113's war is retained, the Soviet–Ukraine war and Operation Barbarella II occur **here**, not before the Soviet reform era. |

### 3.1 The Soviet state model

The USSR is **neither a liberal democracy nor a capitalist market economy**. It keeps collective
ownership, Party institutions and a planned/administrative core, while allowing decentralised
experiments, cooperative enterprise and a more open cultural sphere. It is economically and
institutionally more successful than Yeltsin's Russia in our timeline, but it is not a Western
social democracy with a different flag.

Do not flatten the contradictions: reform and Party control, collective ownership and
decentralisation, autonomist rhetoric and state planning, internationalism and geopolitical
ambition all coexist. Those tensions are the point.

### 3.2 Russian Cosmism as the official atheistic religion

The post-Glasnost generation rediscovers **Russian Cosmism** and makes it effectively the
**official atheistic religion of the Soviet Union**: a secular, state-sanctioned cosmic faith
that treats scientific progress, human transformation and a shared cosmic destiny as sacred
duties without invoking a conventional supernatural deity.

Its ancestry includes:

- **Nikolai Fyodorov's Common Task** — the defeat of death and the resurrection of the ancestors;
- **Konstantin Tsiolkovsky** — cosmonautics and a panpsychic/cosmic philosophy;
- **Vladimir Vernadsky** — the biosphere and the noosphere;
- associated revolutionary, scientific and transhumanist strands.

The faith is combined with autonomist Marxism, collective emancipation, world revolution and the
promise of a communist utopia. Its central irony is explicit: a proclaimed atheistic-materialist
state has acquired a quasi-religious cosmic eschatology.

Treat it as a real **in-universe ideological institution** — doctrine, rituals, civic mythology,
education, symbolism and dissent — not a label. Its precise research programmes (psychotronics,
cybernetics, bionics, life extension, consciousness studies, cosmic engineering) are developed in
later lore, not fixed here.

### 3.3 The Troika

Around the beginning of the new millennium, a cohort of Communist Party leadership takes control.
They are the children of Glasnost and Perestroika, educated in Soviet institutions and Western
counterculture. They are radical Marxists who want world revolution and a communist future,
influenced by Hardt and Negri, Deleuze and Guattari, and Russian Cosmism. They are not liberal
reformers or capitalist technocrats.

Their public personas are almost postmodern parodies of the first Bolshevik revolutionaries.
Their revolutionary names began as **Soviet punk names** and stuck as they rose to the highest
offices of state.

The core Troika, with names preserved exactly:

1. **Павел Пластик (Pavel Plastik)**
2. **Настя Машин (Nastya Mashin)**
3. **Игорь Мотор (Igor' Motor)**

Their institutional roles, individual ideologies, rivalries, aesthetics, biographies, scientific
interests and relationship to Gorbachev are **not settled here**. Develop them in separate
characterisation work; do not invent them as canon in this document.

The terrible irony is that their self-created revolutionary identities may have been engineered
by the very state-security apparatus whose orthodox order their punk mythology mocks.

## 4. КГБ «ПРОГРАММА»

**Secret history; not general public knowledge inside the setting.**

A **KGB general who is also a scientist** becomes obsessed with the CIA's **MKULTRA**, Western
radical youth culture, and the personalities of revolutionary leaders such as **Gaddafi** and
**Trotsky**. He pursues a grand social-engineering question: can Soviet science deliberately
create a generation of Westernised, 1968-style revolutionary radicals who will renew communism
rather than destroy it?

The classified initiative is **«ПРОГРАММА»** ("THE PROGRAM"). Its purpose is to cultivate
**hundreds of young communist leaders** as an energetic replacement for the aging Party
leadership.

- **Gorbachev himself supports ПРОГРАММА**, as do elements of the Communist Party elite, the KGB
  and the Soviet Army. The programme therefore straddles state continuity and planned
  revolutionary succession.
- Recruits are exposed to radical Western intellectual life and subcultures. Some study in
  **France and Italy**, where Western autonomist Marxism is developing.
- Soviet scientists contribute psychology, psychiatry, psychotronic technologies, bionics,
  cybernetics, hormones, psychoactive drugs and other techniques to the recruits' conditioning,
  development and augmentation. The exact methods, reliability, ethics and consequences remain
  material for fiction and investigation; this is not an operational manual.
- The **Troika are products of ПРОГРАММА**, not merely opportunists who rose during reform.
  Hundreds of other alumni form a diffuse generation of cadres, scientists, military figures,
  artists and ideologues.

This is a dark, MKULTRA-like Soviet counterpart and a central political-horror revelation: even
the ostensibly spontaneous punk revolution may have been cultivated by the old security state.

**Open dramatic questions:** How much do the Troika know? Do they believe in their mission, or
realise their formation was managed? Did the programme succeed beyond its designers' control? Is
Cosmism a genuine faith or a state-engineered creed? Did the makers of new revolutionaries
inadvertently create an uncontrollable revolution?

Integrate this with the USSR's later secret NHI/psychotronics programmes and the **Soviet of
Saturn** — but do **not** declare that extraterrestrials controlled ПРОГРАММА unless later canon
establishes it.

## 5. The UN, UNSA and the Europol frame

### 5.1 The world government that did not happen

A proposed **UN/UNSA world government never successfully consolidated authority**. The UN and
UNSA still exist. The EU and many nation-states continue to support them. They are **not a world
government**, and UNSA is not the player's default employer.

International cooperation still happens, but sovereignty and competing state institutions
matter. UNSA remains relevant for coordination, science, intelligence, diplomacy and NHI response
as later lore specifies. Its authority is pooled, conditional and revocable; it is not a sovereign.

This replaces the older "federal United Nations" arc. That arc may remain as an in-universe
**political project** that failed, or as contested propaganda, but it is not the settled outcome.

### 5.2 Europol Helsinki Field Office

The player characters work for **Europol**. The initial headquarters is the **Europol Helsinki
Field Office**.

Europol's **alternate-history** operational powers must be defined carefully. Do not assume that
present-day real-world Europol limitations apply unchanged, and do not turn it into a global
sovereign either. The working model is:

- cross-border investigative coordination and case ownership where member states have delegated it;
- joint investigation teams with Finnish police and Supo;
- analysis, intelligence fusion, forensic and cyber support;
- liaison with EU institutions, national governments and — where a case requires it — UNSA.

National officers still carry national police powers. Europol's leverage comes from mandates,
delegation, information and political backing, not from an unlimited global jurisdiction.

See [the Europol Helsinki Field Office frame](EUROPOL_HELSINKI_1114.md) for the playable
institution, ranks, tensions and campaign start.

### 5.3 What carries over from the UNSA version

- Helsinki, the investigative cell, the four analytical layers and the CWN rules chassis.
- The shared academy skill package. The existing `data/rules/unsa_academy.json` and
  `rulebook/14_CHARACTER_GENERATION.md` training baseline are retained for compatibility; "Europol
  Academy" is the in-setting name, not a second rules package.
- UNSA as a partner, rival, customer or constraint — not as the employer.

## 6. The unresolved causal link

The timeline has a deliberate gap. The USSR reforms successfully, survives, and by the 2000s is
an autonomist-Marxist, Cosmist, technologically ambitious state. If #1113's war is retained, it
later fights Ukraine and Europe in a militarised, expansionist phase.

**How a successful reform era becomes that war is not settled.** Do not invent the missing link
and present it as canon. Candidate explanations to be decided by the author include, but are not
limited to:

- a post-Troika succession crisis or a security-elite reversal;
- a geopolitical shock over Ukraine, the EU's eastern enlargement, or access to technology;
- an ideological split between world-revolutionary internationalism and peaceful coexistence;
- a Cosmism-driven imperative that turns cosmic destiny into territorial mission;
- an NHI/psychotronic intervention or disclosure shock;
- a deliberate tragedy: the PROGRAMMA generation's engineered radicalism escaping its makers.

These are **questions, not answers**. Until the author decides, scenarios should present the
transition as contested history, rumour, archive gaps or propaganda — not as a fixed event.

## 8. Mechanical boundary

This is a **worldbuilding** revision, not a rules rewrite.

- CWN remains the immutable human-scale chassis.
- No CWN, SWN, WWN or AWN number changes.
- The academy skill package and its tests remain the same; only the in-setting institution's name
  and reporting lines change.
- Tabletop/Python synchronization applies to future mechanics changes, not to this document.

## How this file is organised: five strata

Every claim about Russia, the USSR or the secret programmes belongs to exactly one stratum. The
stratum is the point — an investigator's job is to work out which one a document belongs to, and
the game never answers it for them.

| Stratum | What it means | How it may be used |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Playable-world baseline** | What is true in 2045 Helsinki: collapse, war, failed state, closed border | Settled; may be narrated directly |
| **2. Accepted pre-collapse secret history** | The 1950s–1991 ПРОГРАММА outline | Author-facing baseline; known to no player character |
| **3. Contested testimony** | Witnesses, archives, intelligence allegations, SETKA, Pilgrim's transmission | Evidence; may be true, false, or both |
| **4. Mandela-effect material** | A surviving USSR, the Troika as rulers, statewide Cosmism | Diegetic contradiction; never confirmed |
| **5. Author-only unknowns** | What actually happened and who runs what now | Never resolved in text |

## 1. The playable baseline

1. **The USSR collapses broadly as in actual history.** Post-Soviet Russia inherits its territory,
   institutions, imperial ambitions, security services, military-industrial complex and
   classified archives. **Ukraine becomes independent.**
2. **Russia wages war against Ukraine.** Later, **Ukraine joins the European Union**; the
   accession date stays unspecified until the main timeline fixes it.
3. **Shortly before the NHI Crisis**, escalation produces a **short EU–Russia war**, with Ukraine
   fighting as an EU member. It stops abruptly when **UAPs intercept nuclear weapons**. That
   demonstration — not a treaty — is what breaks conventional deterrence.
4. The larger NHI Crisis and the political collapse that follows leave **Russia a chaotic failed
   state**: fragmented territory, competing armed remnants, and no reliable answer to who controls
   a given secret facility.
5. The **Finnish–Russian border is closed and heavily fortified.** In Helsinki, post-Crisis Russia
   is widely perceived as a hostile, violent, incomprehensible imperial ruin. That is an
   **in-universe EU public perception**, not an objective assessment of every Russian person,
   community or faction, and not a claim the game makes.
6. **Europol Helsinki Field Office** remains the protagonists' employer. **UNSA survives** as a
   limited international X-risk and intelligence coordination body — not a world government.
   Finnish, EU and UNSA agencies keep separate mandates and compete as often as they cooperate.

**Do not invent** the war's exact date or length, the accession treaty, or individual
nuclear-interception incidents. They synchronise with `rulebook/7_TIMELINE.md` before any date is
treated as canon.

## 3. The universal Soviet Mandela Effect

**Everyone is affected, and nobody is exempt** — not the believer, not the sceptic, not the
archivist, not the player character, not Tommy Pilgrim.

- The playable present is the collapsed Russia of §1.
- The contradictory memory is that **the USSR survived**: Gorbachev's reforms succeeded, and a
  Soviet-punk revolutionary **Troika** led an alien, Cosmist, autonomist-socialist civilisation.
- **Павел Пластик (Pavel Plastik), Настя Машин (Nastya Mashin) and Игорь Мотор (Igor' Motor)** are
  preserved as **recognisable Mandela-effect claims**, not as verified rulers of the playable
  world.
- A person who remembers the Russian Federation may still find strong artefacts apparently proving
  continuous Soviet government. Someone who remembers the continuous USSR may hold equally
  compelling memories of its dissolution. **Both can be sincere. Both can be wrong. Either can be
  right.**

**Never decide the cause.** Timeline editing, Time/Space leakage, alternate realities, collective
unconscious contamination, precognition, telepathic conditioning, disinformation, forged
documents, reconstructed memory, or something stranger all remain live. Some of it may be
deliberate misinformation **and** genuinely inexplicable in the same case.

**Evidence, not lecture.** Use contradictory testimony with different dates, impossible archival
stamps, changed photographs, incompatible biographies, divergent editions of the same book,
procedural records that look routine before they become disturbing. The player must investigate;
they are never handed the answer.

**No second timeline.** Do not build a stable "USSR survived" playable setting, and do not keep
rewriting history with mutually exclusive explanations. The contradictions are the case.

## 4. КГБ «ПРОГРАММА» — the accepted pre-collapse secret history

**Stratum 2: author-facing baseline. Its current operation and ultimate nature are unresolved.**

- **1950s.** The Confederacy opens contact with an element of the Soviet state. The in-universe
  inspiration is *The Law of One* 8.6, which claims Confederation technology was given to Russians
  in a misguided attempt to promote peace. That is a **source claim inside the fiction**, not
  verified history. See [the source anchors](../research/SOVIET_PSI_UFOLOGY_ANCHORS_1114.md).
- **Founding.** KGB, scientific and intelligence actors establish **КГБ «ПРОГРАММА»** in a secret
  **closed city (ZATO)**. Its site, coordinates and ultimate custodians stay undisclosed.
- **The doctrine.** A poor, ideologically distorted translation of the Law of One becomes an
  atheistic **Marxist–Leninist theory of consciousness, collective intelligence and revolutionary
  evolution**, reinforced by Russian Cosmism and Soviet cybernetics. The participants do not
  describe their doctrine as an imported religion — and would reject the description.
- **The recruits.** A small army of young **Komsomol** members, selected as supposedly exemplary
  **Homo Sovieticus** candidates. The programme attempts a voluntary **fourth-density Social
  Memory Complex**; compulsory social engineering, mass conditioning and coercive integration
  corrupt it into an increasingly **brainwashed hive mind**. The Confederacy's intended
  service-to-others ideal and this coercive outcome are not the same thing, and the game must not
  pretend they are.
- **The work.** Psychotronic devices, telepathy and collective cognition, cybernetics,
  consciousness manipulation, bionics, and reverse-engineered **ARVs**. In the setting psychotronic
  technology becomes unusually effective and ARV development is attempted. **Do not overstate**
  the success, deployment or ownership of those vehicles without new evidence.
- **The break.** The Confederacy concludes its intervention was a mistake and ends cooperation.
  ПРОГРАММА survives, radicalises, and becomes more dangerous without that moderating influence.
- **1991.** ПРОГРАММА rejects the new democratic authorities, severs accountability, hides its
  archives and resources, and becomes a **rogue deep-state network**. Its closed-city base and
  surviving personnel are unaccounted for.

**What it is not.** It is not the government of present-day Russia, and not proof that the USSR
survived. Its command structure, numbers, ideology, locations, relationship to the Troika memories,
and even whether it remains a coherent collective are **unknown**.

**What the earlier Troika-as-rulers lore becomes.** The successful Soviet modernisation, the
official statewide Cosmist religion, the autonomist regime and the Gorbachev-sponsored millennium
succession are **not fixed events**. They are contradictory archives, propaganda, experimental
narratives, or genuine Mandela-effect memories — useful as **unconfirmed leads**, not as duplicate
canon. Troika character concepts remain available.

## 5. Two unresolved Cold War inheritances: MJ-12 and ПРОГРАММА

These are the setting's most consequential **unsolved intelligence failures**, for Europol and
UNSA alike. They are playable in Helsinki well before the larger ontological escalation.

- **MJ-12** — the alleged American NHI/crash-retrieval apparatus. Actual origins, authentic
  membership, present existence, split factions, ARV capability, NHI contacts and its relationship
  to post-Crisis American power brokers: unknown.
- **ПРОГРАММА** — the Soviet clandestine psychotronic / Social-Memory-Complex project that
  allegedly detached from the state in 1991. Whether it holds an enduring group mind, still
  occupies the closed city, infiltrates institutions or operates craft: unknown.

**Their similarities are themselves a question.** Parallel Cold War competition, deliberate mirror
programmes, shared NHI manipulation, classified technology exchange, or **false similarities
injected into archives** all fit the evidence. No organisation chart, no secret master plan.

### The clue ladder

Investigators must always be able to tell *what a document claims* from *what it establishes*.
Every clue carries a source tier, an explicit confidence, and who contests it:

| Tier | Example | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Public documented research | SETKA reporting, published Soviet psi papers, declassified US assessments | Context; mostly prosaic |
| 2. Cold War hearsay | Memoir anecdotes, emigre testimony, journalistic retellings | Texture and leads, never proof |
| 3. Intelligence allegation | Agency memoranda describing *claimed* Soviet successes | Shows what was believed, not what was achieved |
| 4. Firsthand post-Crisis observation | An artefact in a ruined facility, a witness in Helsinki | Strong, local, unverifiable in isolation |
| 5. Ontological anomaly | The signature, the photograph, the edition that should not exist | Cannot be explained in mundane terms — and still may not be *solved* |

**These cases cannot be closed.** No single revelation resolves either programme, and neither
should be revealed as one organisation with one plan. What can be established: specific documents
are genuine; specific witnesses are consistent; specific facilities existed. What cannot: what it
all amounted to.

## 6. Historical anchors (and what they do not prove)

Real Soviet and Russian ufology, parapsychology and closed-city history are **texture and
competing claims**, never proof of alien technology or working mind control. The full anchor table
— SETKA-MO/SETKA-AN, Vasiliev/Kazhinsky/Kogan, US intelligence assessments, ZATO cities, the
Voronezh story — with each item marked *documented fact / claim / intelligence allegation / game
canon*, is in
[the source anchor sheet](../research/SOVIET_PSI_UFOLOGY_ANCHORS_1114.md).

**Do not equate the real SETKA programme with ПРОГРАММА.** SETKA is a plausible precursor, cover or
rival *in fiction*; historically it was a Ministry of Defence and Academy of Sciences reporting
effort in which most cases had prosaic explanations.

## 7. Helsinki: the border and how Russia is seen

- The eastern border is **closed, fortified and militarised**; crossing is a state matter, not a
  commute.
- The ordinary Helsinki perception — hostile, violent, incomprehensible imperial ruin — is a
  **public mood**, held unevenly, sometimes self-servingly, and occasionally contradicted by the
  case in front of the investigators.
- Most rookie Europol investigators know little about Russia's interior. **Russia does not
  dominate the opening campaign.** It is a pressure, an absence and a source of artefacts.

## 9. Author-only unknowns

Never answered in text, by design:

1. Whether the Mandela Effect is timeline editing, leakage, contamination, forgery, memory
   reconstruction, or several of these at once.
2. Whether a continuous USSR ever existed anywhere, and whether the Troika ruled it.
3. ПРОГРАММА's present command, numbers, location, and whether it is still a collective at all.
4. MJ-12's authentic membership, continuity and current alignment.
5. Whether the two programmes are mirrors, rivals, or a single manipulation seen twice.
6. What, if anything, the Confederacy still intends.

## Technology and networks after the Crisis (#1200)

Europe survived partly because regulation kept it out of the AGI race that ended catastrophically elsewhere. The
resulting technology is deliberately old-fashioned at the institutional layer — Lisp, Prolog, hand-written GOFAI,
rule engines, expert systems — and genuinely advanced where Europe leads: quantum computing, quantum-consciousness
research, psionics, psychotronics, biopunk. **Mistral** is the only company permitted to approach AGI inside the EU,
under strict limits. European AGI is not superior to what the United States built; it is smaller and supervised.

Connectivity is **post-Internet**, not a seamless grid: a hostile, semi-abandoned old Internet; closed organisational
networks that need a body, a badge or a medium in the room; **EuroNET**, the EU's constrained public network behind
the perimeter; and **data couriers**, who carry what the network cannot be trusted with.

The system behind that perimeter is **Project Plato / European Information Security System (EISS)**, operated by
Europol: not an ASI, heavily regulated, human-operated, and genuinely necessary. Its powers, the authorisation ladder
and the naming rules (including why *Panopticon* is not its name) are in
[EU networks and Plato](PLATO_EISS_EURONET_1200.md).

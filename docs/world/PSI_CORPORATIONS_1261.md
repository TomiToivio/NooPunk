# Ψ / Mannerheim–Yamamoto, and the resurrection economy (#1261)

> **Status: worldbuilding note, not mechanics.** Author canon supplied in #1261: *Mannerheim–Yamamoto
> (Ψ/acc) sells cortical stacks, replacement sleeves, resurrection insurance, astral services and
> Hades-linked resurrection contracts in a Finnish/EU-adjacent market.* This note says what that means
> socially, legally and for investigations. The resleeving rules themselves stay in
> [`IMMORTALITY_AND_NOETICS_1255.md`](IMMORTALITY_AND_NOETICS_1255.md).

---

## 1. What the company actually sells

Four products that people insist on treating as one:

| Product | What the customer buys | What it really is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cortical stack** | the hardware that holds a backup | a legal claim on a person's continuity |
| **Replacement sleeve** | a body | a manufacturing and consent problem |
| **Resurrection insurance** | a promise to be restored | a **solvency** promise, priced like life expectancy |
| **Astral services** | guidance, protection, "contact" | a regulated practice with a very uneven evidence base |

The company's genius is that it sells **assurance**, and assurance is only worth something if the
insurer survives the disaster. That is the whole plot of a resurrection economy in a city where
disasters are political.

**Hades-linked contracts.** Where Hades Corporation provides the underwriting, Mannerheim–Yamamoto is
selling a rival's balance sheet to Finnish customers. When the two principals sit in the same room —
as they are about to — the question of who honours whose contract is not abstract.

## 2. The Finnish problem

Finland is the wrong country for this business, which is why it is interesting:

- **Lutheran-adjacent institutions** treat death as a boundary, not a service level. A resurrection
  insurance product is a theological provocation before it is a financial one.
- **The Church of Universal Consciousness** is not uniformly opposed: some congregations read
  continuity as compassion, others as a refusal to grow.
- **The Confederacy** treats endless resleeving as a possible spiritual failure. That is a diplomatic
  embarrassment for a state that shelters Sirian refugees and advocates accession.
- **The EU** wants a competence: is a cortical stack a medical device, a financial instrument, or a
  person's remains? Each answer gives a different regulator the file.

**Nothing here is settled.** The drafters' favourite trick is that every one of these questions is
still open in 2045.

## 3. Who this hurts, concretely

- **The poor** sell their labour while alive and their continuity afterwards; a stack is a debt
  instrument before it is a hope.
- **The old** buy restoration and discover that the restored person is legally a stranger to their own
  property.
- **The dead** have no standing to object to the contract signed about them — and in NoöPunk the dead
  may still be *conscious agents* in Time/Space, which makes the objection more than a metaphor.
- **Employees** of the company carry the equipment they sell, which makes them both salespeople and
  evidence.

## 4. What Europol can actually investigate

None of this is a new subsystem; it is all ordinary police work with an unusual object:

1. **Insurance fraud across a death.** The deceased is restored, and the estate does not accept that
   the claimant is the same person.
2. **A stack that should not exist.** An unregistered backup of somebody who never bought the product.
3. **A sleeve with the wrong provenance.** Grown where the label says otherwise; the Espoo maritime
   zone is good at this.
4. **A resurrection contract signed under duress**, or by someone who was not competent at the time.
5. **Astral service malpractice.** A customer is told they contacted a dead relative; the evidence is
   a session recording and a grieving family.
6. **A Hades-linked contract that cannot be honoured** because a rival's assets are frozen, and the
   Finnish customer is holding the paper.

**Tone:** the horror is administrative. Nobody is vivisected on screen; somebody is told their wife is
a licensed copy and that the licence has lapsed.

## 5. Links to the rest of the setting

- **Eldrich Corporation** competes on Mars, where a sleeve is cheaper and the law is thinner.
- **Hades** underwrites, which makes it the party with the balance sheet and therefore the party with
  the motive when a claim is denied.
- **Projekti Sampo** has no commercial stake and a very large interest: a state that can restore people
  has a different relationship with its own casualties, and Sampo has a casualty list.
- **The Church of Universal Consciousness** is the only large institution offering a *competing*
  account of what continuity means, which makes it a political actor rather than a set piece.

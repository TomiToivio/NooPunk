## 1. Provenance ladder

Every statement in this dossier sits at one of five levels. Later documents must keep the level
visible rather than flattening everything into "canon".

| Level | Meaning | Who may rely on it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **PLAYABLE BASELINE** | True in the world the player inhabits | Everyone |
| **ACCEPTED SECRET HISTORY** | True, but classified; drives the campaign's deep structure | Author, GM |
| **CONTESTED TESTIMONY** | Someone asserts it; may be true, false or both | Investigators, with difficulty |
| **MANDELA-EFFECT MATERIAL** | Remembered, recorded, or evidenced — and contradicts something else equally well | Nobody can settle it |
| **AUTHOR-ONLY UNKNOWN** | Deliberately undecided. Do not answer it in code or in prose | Author |

## 2. ПРОГРАММА — accepted secret history

### 2.1 Origin (1950s)

> Level: **ACCEPTED SECRET HISTORY**, with the Confederacy contact itself at **CONTESTED** in-world.

The Confederacy opens contact with an element of the Soviet state. The in-universe source is
*The Law of One* 8.6, which claims that Confederation technology was given to Russians in a
misguided attempt to promote peace. Treat that as a **claim inside the fiction**, not as validated
external history.

Soviet security, scientific and intelligence actors establish **КГБ «ПРОГРАММА»** in a secret
**closed city (ZATO)**. The site, its coordinates and its ultimate custodians are **undisclosed**.
The historical closed-city system is real (see the anchors document); the specific facility is
fiction and must never be pinned to a real place.

### 2.2 Doctrine

A poor, ideologically distorted translation of the Law of One becomes an **atheistic
Marxist–Leninist theory of consciousness, collective intelligence and revolutionary evolution**,
reinforced by Russian Cosmism and Soviet cybernetics.

Participants do **not** describe their doctrine as an imported religion. They describe a scientific
theory of history in which consciousness is a material force, collective cognition is an
infrastructure, and evolution has a direction the Party can accelerate.

### 2.3 The Homo Sovieticus cohort

The facility recruits a **small army of young Komsomol members**, selected as supposedly exemplary
**Homo Sovieticus** candidates. The stated aim is a voluntary fourth-density **Social Memory
Complex** — a shared mind entered freely, in service to others.

The techniques available to a 1950s security state make that aim impossible: compulsory social
engineering, mass conditioning, coercive integration, chemical and psychotronic intervention,
relentless ideological training. The result is an increasingly **brainwashed hive mind** rather than
a voluntary collective.

**This distinction is the horror, and it must not be softened.** The programme tries to manufacture
altruistic collective consciousness by authoritarian means. Whether it produced something that
still believes it succeeded is a different question.

### 2.4 Capability

It experiments with:

- psychotronic devices and defensive/offensive mental effects
- telepathy and collective cognition
- cybernetics and human-machine integration
- consciousness manipulation
- bionics
- reverse-engineered alien reproduction vehicles (**ARVs**)

In this setting, its psychotronic technology is **unusually effective** and it attempts working ARVs.
Do **not** overstate the success, deployment or ownership of those vehicles without new evidence.
One working vehicle that nobody can fuel is a very different fact from a fleet.

### 2.5 The break (date unspecified, before 1991)

The Confederacy concludes its intervention was a mistake and **ends cooperation**. ПРОГРАММА
survives, radicalises, and becomes more dangerous without the Confederacy's moderating influence.

### 2.6 1991

ПРОГРАММА **rejects the new democratic authorities**, severs accountability, hides its archives and
resources, and becomes a **rogue deep-state network**. Its closed-city base and surviving personnel
are unaccounted for.

### 2.7 Present status — AUTHOR-ONLY UNKNOWN

Not decided, and not to be decided casually:

- current command structure and numbers
- ideology after seventy years without a state
- whether the original site is still occupied
- whether it infiltrates institutions, or operates independently
- whether it remains a **coherent collective** at all, or has fragmented into something worse
- its relationship to the Troika memories
- whether it has any current NHI contact

ПРОГРАММА is **not** the government of present-day Russia, and **not** proof that the USSR survived.

## 3. MJ-12 — the American mirror

> Level: **CONTESTED TESTIMONY** throughout. Its existence is asserted by documents that disagree.

The alleged American secret NHI/crash-retrieval apparatus. Unknown in canon:

- actual origins and authentic membership
- whether it still exists, and in how many pieces
- ARV capabilities
- NHI contacts
- relationship to post-Crisis American power brokers

**The mirror problem.** MJ-12 and ПРОГРАММА share structural features — compartmentalisation,
reverse-engineering claims, consciousness research, a 1950s start. That similarity may mean:

1. parallel Cold War competition
2. deliberate mirror programmes (each copying the other's reported capabilities)
3. shared NHI manipulation
4. classified technology exchange
5. **false similarities injected into archives** — by either side, or by something else

Do not resolve which. The similarity is itself a clue that can mislead an investigator, and it should.

## 4. How these are played in Helsinki

Both mysteries must be **playable before the campaign's cosmological escalation**, and neither is
solved by a single revelation.

### 4.1 Clue tiers

| Tier | Source type | What it can establish | What it cannot |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Public documented** | Journalism, books, declassified summaries, academic papers | That a programme was claimed to exist; what a document says | That the claim is true |
| **2. Cold War hearsay** | Memoirs, defector stories, veterans' gossip | What people believed and repeated | Anything operational |
| **3. Intelligence allegation** | Agency assessments, intercept summaries, liaison notes | That an agency took something seriously | That the underlying claim is accurate |
| **4. Firsthand post-Crisis observation** | Witnesses, site reports, physical traces | That something happened to someone | Who did it, or why |
| **5. Genuine ontological anomaly** | Effects that cannot be explained by any record | That the world is not as described | What the explanation is |

A case built only on tiers 1–3 produces a *hypothesis*. Tier 5 without tiers 1–4 produces an
*experience*. The campaign is interesting when both are in the same file.

### 4.2 Chain of custody and confidence

Investigators must be able to distinguish **what a document claims** from **what it establishes**.
Every clue carries, in the game's own language:

- **provenance** — where it came from, through whose hands
- **contestation** — who disputes it, and on what grounds
- **confidence** — a plain-language judgement, not a number
- **gap** — what would have to be true for the document to be a forgery

A clue with no gap is not a clue; it is a lecture.

### 4.3 What the agencies cannot do

Europol opens doors; national officers walk through them. UNSA has better archives and worse
mandates. Neither can arrest its way to the truth, and neither has a complete file. **The campaign
never provides the definitive organisation chart, and never confirms the secret master plan.**

### 4.4 What the player gets instead

A case starts ordinary. A perfectly routine file contains an authentic-looking signature from a
Soviet minister who supposedly never existed. An interviewee remembers meeting him yesterday. A
sealed reference to ПРОГРАММА appears in a document that should not mention it.

The world's historical foundation moves under the player's feet, and **the game never announces which
reality is the true one**.

## 5. Cross-reference discipline

- The Troika are **Mandela-effect claims**. Their names are preserved exactly; their offices are not
  established. Do not let a later document promote them to rulers by accident.
- The Soviet reform era, statewide Cosmism and autonomist government are **Mandela-effect material**.
  They may be vivid, internally consistent and supported by documents. They are still not the
  playable baseline.
- Any new claim about ПРОГРАММА or MJ-12 must name its **provenance level** and must not resolve an
  AUTHOR-ONLY UNKNOWN.
- Nothing here changes CWN, SWN, WWN, AWN or any tabletop number.

## 6. Open author decisions

1. Does ПРОГРАММА still exist as a coherent collective in 2045?
2. What does it want now — survival, completion, contact, revenge, or something non-human?
3. Is the closed city still inhabited, and by whom?
4. Which ARV claims are true, and does any vehicle still function?
5. Did MJ-12 and ПРОГРАММА ever actually contact each other?
6. Is the Troika memory a ПРОГРАММА artefact, a Mandela effect with another cause, or a mixture?
7. Which tier-5 anomaly first proves to the player that something is wrong — and how wrong?

# The post-Crisis world — consolidated reference (#1005)

**Status of this document.** A consolidation of the worldbuilding discussion, for the 2045 setting and the
UNSA Helsinki campaign. It does not implement systems.

**Priority of statements, and how to read them.** Three markers are used throughout, and they matter more than
any prose:

- **[CANON]** — author-established direction. Do not contradict it.
- **[IN-WORLD CLAIM]** — something a faction or character asserts. It may be true, partly true, or propaganda.
  The setting does not vouch for it.
- **[OPEN]** — deliberately undecided. Do not fill it in.

Where this document conflicts with an older repository document, this one records the newer author direction
and says so. Where it agrees, it is a convenience, not a second authority.

**Tone:** New Weird / social science fiction with understated comedy; 1970s psychedelic retrofuturist and Mœbius
aesthetics, not generic 1980s cyberpunk. The player is a rookie investigator, and grand metaphysics arrives
through modest, strange, occasionally comical police work.

---

## A. Geopolitics [CANON]

**UNSA existed before the NHI Crisis.** Formed under UN auspices to coordinate existential risk, initially
scientific, then intelligence and security. It becomes a **de facto world government** during the Crisis — with
partial, contested authority that member states and populations regularly ignore or refuse.

**"NHI Crisis" is a misleading name.** It marks the transition in which NHI become undeniable participants in
Earth's politics, entangled with the Singularity and rogue-AGI upheaval. It is **not the apocalypse**; the real
apocalypse may still lie ahead. The setting opens after the most acute chaos, in an ontological Cold War.

**Pillars:** the EU, China and India. Europe keeps institutional and political weight. China remains a
technological and industrial power after a radical political transformation. India gains spiritual and
philosophical influence because parts of its consciousness traditions appear vindicated by in-universe
discoveries — vindicated *in part*, and not as a wholesale endorsement of any tradition.

**United States.** Badly hit by the Singularity and rogue-AGI crises: fragmented, partly failed, with rogue state
structures and significant hostility to UNSA. Sympathetic American individuals and factions exist and must not
be erased.

**Russia.** Important, **especially for Finland**. The current direction supersedes a purely hostile rogue-state
depiction: after Putin, Russia *may* become genuinely democratic and **join UNSA**, while remaining close to
state failure — weak state capacity, plus rogue intelligence, military and criminal actors. Keep the democratic
government distinct from the threats inside Russian territory. [OPEN: the exact trajectory.]

**Finland and Helsinki.** Local politics are dominated by the EU/UNSA relationship and by Russia — not by China
or India, however large those powers loom globally. Investigations mix intelligence work, PSI, NHI diplomacy,
refugee questions, ordinary politics and bureaucracy.

### China's revolution [CANON]

- China undergoes **its own rogue-AGI crisis**, producing a faction perceived as hardline Maoist, which purges
  the preceding faction.
- The "Maoists" then restore Daoist and Buddhist traditions and evolve into something close to the
  **Panpsychic Cyborg Multitude**: consciousness-first, cybernetic, collectivist, autonomist Marxist, and
  emphatically *not* Western liberal democracy.
- China becomes an ally of Europe and a founding UNSA member despite the philosophical distance.

[OPEN / optional complexity] The tension between the initial coercive purge and the later autonomist
decentralisation; communes, workers' councils, AI oversight, accountability and dissent. Do not resolve it in
passing.

## B. Language by 2045 [INTERPRETIVE]

Mandarin challenges English in Chinese technological and economic spheres; **English remains the inherited
UNSA/UN lingua franca**. India is multilingual; Sanskrit can regain philosophical importance without becoming a
global everyday language. AI and BCI translation make multilingual interaction ordinary — and post-Crisis
fragmentation plus sovereignty over translation systems makes it a geopolitical vulnerability.

## C. The NHI political economy

### Zookeepers / Union of Collective Consciousness Communes [CANON + IN-WORLD CLAIM]

Genuinely benevolent **communist social-memory complexes**, favouring altruism, collectivism and
consciousness-first idealism — **StO / Service to Others** in their own framing. They have **large numbers of
human supporters**, not a fringe cult; to outsiders those supporters look like extreme alien cultists, in the
mood of Law of One believers. NHI and PSI are real in this world; the followers' *interpretations* may still be
wrong.

Integrating into hive or social-memory consciousness is profoundly unattractive to many humans even where the
collective's benefits are real. Distinguish benevolence from paternalism, and well-being from autonomy.

**[IN-WORLD CLAIM]** They say Earth will cease to be habitable for ordinary third-density humans; that most of
humanity may go extinct; that few reach fourth density; and that there will be immigration from other planets.
They frame the choice as **StO** against **StS**, with Orion as StS. Their metaphysical account is not thereby
established as objectively correct. The threatened transition is a serious in-universe claim, not a fixed
future.

**StO, as used here:** practical altruism, collectivism, consciousness-first idealism. Not a magical alignment.

### Greater Orion Arm Co-Prosperity Sphere [CANON]

A galactic **capitalist, materialist, exploitative empire** — not simplistic cosmic evil. **StS** is largely a
Zookeeper description; what it names is familiar capitalism and self-interest continuing at galactic scale. The
empire may be no dramatically worse than human cyberpunk capitalism, which is exactly why it appeals to many
humans. Market norms and institutional interests coexist with diverse religions.

**Mantids pull the strings** of both Greys and Reptilians: imperial finance, infrastructure, technology,
political coordination, dependent species.

**Greys** [CANON] — an industrial capitalist civilisation refined over perhaps a million years. **Competing
corporations own the proletariat's consciousness**, not merely their bodies or labour. Identity, memories,
continuity, resleeving access and instantiation may all be corporate property. Elites resleeve to avoid death;
much of the proletariat may no longer be fully self-aware in the ordinary sense, or has that self-awareness
suppressed. Preserve uncertainty about their subjectivity — the horror is strongest where it is not measured.
**Consciousness as property** is the central idea.

**Reptilians** [CANON] — a comparatively primitive "barbarian" species technologically uplifted by the Mantids
and used as expendable imperial soldiers. Warrior-honour culture, glory, a Valhalla-like afterlife; generally
indifferent to consciousness metaphysics. They are not incapable of dissent or individuality.

**Mantids** [CANON] — evolved as quantum-sensitive **solitary predators**, capable of long meditative
quantum-consciousness states, alternating with periodic **collective hive-consciousness phases**. Solitude
produces independence and insight; the hive phase shares and integrates experience. This cycle contributed to
both their rapid evolution and their extraordinary coordination. Their hives need not be benevolent: internal
cooperation coexists with external exploitation. [OPEN / optional] Mantid corporations may themselves be
hive-minds — keep speculative until chosen.

**Distinguish ontological commitment from ethics.** Materialism does not require selfishness; idealism does not
guarantee virtue. StO/StS are factional hegemonic narratives, not proven cosmic laws.

### Sirians: harmless, incomprehensible, slow-tech refugees [CANON]

**Sentient tree-like organisms**, conscious and extraordinarily **slow-thinking** — hours, days, years per
thought or decision.

Each tree operates through a **fast biopunk symbiotic swarm** that senses, calculates, constructs, communicates,
protects and restores ecosystems. The swarm is highly capable and **not itself sentient**. *Slow conscious goals
plus fast nonconscious biological intelligence* is their signature, and it is NoöPunk's consciousness/intelligence
distinction made flesh.

They are comparatively **low-tech** in many fields — 20th-century-equivalent hardware, or very unevenly
distributed — and they travel **slower than light**, in giant ecological generation vessels with exceptional
biotech and long-duration life support. Spacefaring does not imply universal technological superiority.

They are **genuinely harmless, peaceful and helpful**, not secret conquerors. Their swarms help restore Earth's
damaged biosphere. Communication is difficult because of cognitive timescale and conceptual distance.

They are **refugees**: UNSA eventually discovers **thousands of Sirian refugee ships approaching Earth**, which
triggers suspicion, panic and hard political choices despite demonstrated benevolence.

**[OPEN]** What they are fleeing; why Earth; when the ships launched; whether they are connected to the Zookeeper
predictions of incoming nonhuman populations. Mass arrival may have ecological and infrastructural effects
despite benign intent — but do not turn them into villains.

*Possible comedy:* a Sirian swarm repairs an investigator's equipment while the tree is still considering their
first reply.

## D. Wormholes, density transition, survival

**[CANON]** A **wormhole network** lets humans and other low-tech civilisations explore the galaxy without
inventing FTL drives; even civilisations with primitive sublight propulsion can reach transit nodes.

**[OPEN]** Who built the network; its architecture; who holds gate sovereignty; costs and constraints.

**[IN-WORLD CLAIM]** The Zookeepers say humanity must form a social memory complex, and frame the next choice as
StO (Zookeepers) or StS (Orion); that most present humanity will not survive Earth's transition to a planet no
longer viable for third-density humans; that only a small minority is fourth-density; and that extraterrestrial
immigration overlays the transition.

**[OPEN — the critical mystery]** Whether these predictions are true, misunderstood, natural, engineered,
ideological propaganda, or a combination. Do not declare human extinction inevitable. Preserve human and player
agency. [Optional ideological third way] China's autonomists and UNSA insist that humans may form voluntary,
independent collectives, or refuse the alien binary altogether.

## E. Lost programmes, the Ghost Rockets, and UNSA starting from zero

**[CANON]** US MJ-12-like programmes, and Soviet/Russian and Chinese secret NHI reverse-engineering programmes,
may have existed. What became of them during the political and AGI upheavals is largely unknown: personnel, data
and assets may be gone, fragmented, privately held or hidden.

**UNSA effectively starts reverse engineering from zero**, even where remnants exist. Do not gift UNSA an intact
alien technical archive.

**Finland and Sweden have secretly investigated and partly reverse-engineered the 1946 Ghost Rockets for
decades.** That institutional continuity and its recovered evidence are the exception — and understanding
remains incomplete.

**[OPEN — central mystery]** Which faction sent the Ghost Rockets: Zookeepers, Orion, the Sirians (interesting
given their sublight biology), or an unknown power. A Sirian link is a compelling optional hook, not a confirmed
reveal; if the 1946 probes and the 2045 ships are connected, the launch and travel chronology must be credible.

This intersects directly with **Jyrki Räyhä recruiting Romppainen after showing him classified 1946 Ghost Rocket
material** — UNSA Helsinki and Romppainen are the natural leads.

**Real history versus fiction.** MJ-12 is an unverified and discredited real-world documentary claim. Ghost
Rocket sightings and investigations are historical. Alien recovery and reverse engineering are **fictional
NoöPunk canon**, not verified history.

## F. What this does to play

Three distinct power patterns, plus two more:

| Actor | Pattern |
|---|---|
| **Mantids** | natural alternation between solitude and shared consciousness; imperial capitalist controllers |
| **Greys** | conscious identity owned by corporations; a resleeving market |
| **Sirians** | slow conscious trees commanding fast non-sentient swarms |
| **Zookeepers** | stable, benevolent collective consciousness |
| **Humans** | plural, rapidly experimenting with PSI, BCI, AI and political futures |

The recurring question: **who owns or controls consciousness, and can an individual choose collective
integration, continuity, or exit?**

**No good/evil meter.** Use LaclauGPT-style discourse, hegemonic framing, material interest and political
affiliation. Species do not have hardwired morality.

### Case seeds

- An escaped, corporation-owned Grey seeking asylum.
- A Reptilian defector whose honour culture does not make them safe.
- A real but feared Zookeeper sympathiser commune.
- Russian rogue intelligence hunting for lost recovery files.
- Sirian ecosystem restoration perceived as invasion.
- 1946 Ghost Rocket evidence tied to the arriving refugees.
- Disputes over wormhole sovereignty and planetary migration.

## Established versus open, in one place

**Established:** UNSA's timing and role; the NHI Crisis as political unveiling rather than apocalypse; the
EU/China/India alliance; China's rogue-AGI crisis, apparent Maoist purge and Daoist/Buddhist PCM-like turn;
Mantid control of Orion; Grey corporate ownership of consciousness and the resleeving market; Reptilian uplift,
cannon-fodder role and Valhalla ethos; the Mantid solitude/hive cycle; Sirians as slow sentient trees with fast
non-sentient swarms, peaceful refugee ecologists arriving in thousands of sublight vessels; the wormhole network;
Zookeeper StO ideology and its large human following; the 1946 Nordic recovery mystery; Finland's EU/Russia
focus.

**Open:** democratic Russia's specific trajectory; who launched the Ghost Rockets; whether MJ-12 ever existed
under that name and what became of secret programmes; the truth and timetable of any density transition; why
the Sirians were displaced; the origin and governance of the wormholes; the fate of most of humanity; the exact
mechanics of consciousness ownership, resleeving and collective mind.

**Thematic anchor:** *Consciousness and intelligence are separable; evolutionary age, technology, ethics and
political organisation do not advance in lockstep. The paradigm shift has happened, but the apocalypse may not
have begun.*

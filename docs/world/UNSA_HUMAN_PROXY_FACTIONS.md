# UNSA human-facing faction and NHI intelligence guide
**Status:** setting and investigation design, not a definitive list of existing named organizations. Related: #922, #921, #920, #897. Primary NHI source: [FACTIONS.md](../../FACTIONS.md).

## Golden rule
The Zookeepers and Orion Arm Greater Co-Prosperity Sphere are geopolitical and metaphysical powers. **The player investigates people.** A corporation may welcome Orion commerce yet oppose abductions. An environmental collective may admire Zookeeper restoration while rejecting its demands. A religious leader may resist both without committing a crime. Proxy ties must be evidenced rather than inferred from ideology, appearance, religion, unusual psychology, or genetics.

## Separate dimensions of classification
- **Political faction:** an organization or movement with material interests, ideology and relationships.
- **Allegiance/contact:** reported, claimed, confirmed, denied or unknown link to an NHI actor.
- **Ontology:** biologic, Construct, Noetic or unclassified. This is not an affiliation.
- **Species:** human, Grey, Pleiadian, uplifted dolphin, etc. This is not an allegiance.
- **Evidence:** verified, reported, disputed, rumor or unresolved. Store provenance and confidence for each *assertion*.
- **Threat:** observed actions and operational risk, not presumed identity.

## NHI landscape, deliberately brief
- **Zookeepers / Galactic Council of Collective Consciousness:** planetary guardianship, consciousness-centered nondualism, anti-weaponization, biosphere support and human Pleiadian envoys; their embassies attract converts and resistance. Claims about Earth's ancient history, the nature of galactic castes and their true bodies are disputed.
- **Orion Arm Greater Co-Prosperity Sphere:** Grey commercial technology/resleeving and economic zones, Reptilian bellicosity, mysterious Mantids, alleged hybridization/infiltration; their coalition may be internally divided. Public hostility does not erase the appeal of promised technologies.
- **Sirians/Ents:** radically slow-lived sentient trees and living biotechnology; an enigmatic seed exchange, potential biosphere-restoration applications, unclear politics.
- **Plasmoids:** curious sentient plasma scouts, not necessarily organized into a diplomatic faction.
- **Constructs / Noetics:** cross-cutting classifications, including human-made androids/rogue AI and human-origin noetic experiences.
- **Martian remains and unknown UAP:** scientific mysteries, not automatic political factions. A glowing orb could be a metallic Construct, a Noetic appearance or plasmoid, and may remain unidentified.

## Suggested data contract
Each named faction record should have:
`id`, `public_name`, `kind=human_organization`, `stated_goals`, `material_interests`, `social_base`, `territories`, `contacts`, `known_actions`, `relations`, `investigation_hooks`, `reputation`, `discourse`, and an array of **assertions**: `claim`, `source`, `evidence_status`, `confidence`, `visibility`. Relations can be cooperation, antagonism, funding, competition, alleged proxy link or confirmed proxy link. **Do not make `species` or `nhi_alignment` mandatory**.

The case graph should represent actor → assertion → evidence separately, so discovering a suspicious transfer does not silently update an allegation into a fact. Character contacts and faction relationships are NoöPunk narrative overlays on the **CWN** rules baseline.

## Mission design loop
1. Receive a mundane, lawful allegation (contract, disappearance, complaint, network incident).
2. Interview human witnesses and document evidence provenance; use the human + 2026-level AI controller for analysis only with explicit human approvals.
3. Visit discovered Helsinki locations; use social ties and jurisdiction badges to gain authorized access.
4. Uncover competing accounts and choices. Essential clues must have more than one acquisition path.
5. Decide how to act within Finnish/Supo, Europol and UNSA authority; preserve privacy, due process and proportionality.
6. Let social and faction reputation affect later access, not rewrite the CWN core resolution.
7. Reveal a genuine nonhuman presence only when a case earns that escalation.

### Starter case: the contested clinic
A Helsinki research clinic advertises PSI-assisted memory recovery. A former patient complains about a missing consent form; an investor is linked by rumor to a Grey broker; an environmental charity nearby hosts Zookeeper-minded lectures. None of these links alone proves infiltration. Tony and Laura can interview the patient or audit the clinic, Romppainen can challenge assumptions, and a controller must obtain human clearance before accessing protected records. Competing discoveries can implicate fraud, genuine anomalies, exploitative data markets or multiple overlapping causes. This remains an *outline*, not a presolved mystery.

## Safeguards in the fiction
The in-world EU rules regarding hybrid/android empathy testing are **additional restrictions**, not permission to target unidentified people. Tests can be flawed; lawful authorization, dignity and proportionality still matter. Never use visual traits, emotional expressiveness, political faith or psychiatric history as definitive hostile-NHI detection.

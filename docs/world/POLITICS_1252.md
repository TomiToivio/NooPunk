# Projekti Sampo, three governments, and the Finnish NHI crossroads (#1252)

> **Status: author-directed lore brief, written out.** The premises in #1252 are Tomi's; this document
> expands them, reconciles them with existing canon, and marks every date and fact the author has not
> fixed as **TBD**. It adds **no mechanics**: CWN remains the immutable core, and the scene/graph
> conventions are unchanged.
>
> **Four registers, kept apart throughout:**
> **PUBLIC** — what a Helsinki newspaper can print.
> **CLASSIFIED** — what a cleared Europol officer may read.
> **PARTISAN** — what a faction says is true, including about its rivals.
> **GM** — what is actually the case, where the author has settled it.

---

## 1. Three governments, three lawful instructions

All three survived the NHI Crisis. None of them agrees about what humanity should become, and all
three can give a Europol investigator a **lawful order that contradicts the other two**.

| Government | Direction | Strategic goal | What it fears |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **EU / federal** | Social-democratic, security-first, cautiously **decelerationist** | Defend Europe, preserve society, contain X-risk, keep diplomacy alive through the Crisis | That Finland's Confederacy project and Helsinki's corporate experiments end civilisation while the EU is still arguing about competence |
| **Finland / state** | Social-democratic in public, **custodian of an exceptionally advanced PSI/biotech programme** in private | Protect the Sirian refugees; advocate eventual **EU membership in the Confederacy** | That a benevolent long-term path toward fourth-density service-to-others looks, from outside, exactly like assimilation |
| **Greater Helsinki / city** | Accelerationist, commercially libertarian, aggressively transhumanist | Maximise autonomy, welcome cybercorporations and NHI commerce, expand extraterrestrial zones and permissive law | That federal and national safeguards strangle the only economy that can pay for the future |

**The irony that drives the campaign.** Finland is accused of building a *Childhood's End* hive mind
while Helsinki's elite is openly negotiating simultaneous hybridisation and Borgification. The EU is
the exhausted adult in the room, trying to stop both projects from ending civilisation, and failing to
sound like anything other than a regulator.

**Operational rule for referees.** A scene should be able to produce **three lawful instructions** —
from a federal desk, a ministry, and a city office — that cannot all be obeyed. The investigator's
problem is not finding the villain. It is choosing which lawful authority to disappoint, and living
with the paperwork.

## 2. Projekti Sampo, 1946 – Disclosure

### 2.1 The Ghost Rockets

**PUBLIC:** in 1946, Finnish authorities recovered wreckage from anomalous craft. The official story
was weather balloons and hysteria, and it held for eighty years.

**GM:** the craft were **advance scouts of a refugee fleet from Sirius**. The programme began as a
security and military recovery operation, in a country that had just fought two wars and trusted
almost nobody.

**CLASSIFIED:** the first years produced no usable technology and no communication — only the
uncomfortable fact that the recovered biologics were extremely tough and did not appear to age on any
timescale the investigators recognised.

### 2.2 The telepathic breakthrough

A Finnish telepath opened viable communication. The results were slow, partial and frequently wrong;
the first decade of transcripts contains as much projection as contact. The **Confederacy**,
represented locally by **Pleiadians**, made contact with Finland shortly afterwards and helped
establish a channel that worked.

**GM:** this is the moment the programme stops being a recovery operation and becomes a relationship.

### 2.3 Two hidden communities

- **Sirian colonies** in remote Finnish forests — small, quiet, and for decades entirely outside any
  register.
- **Pleiadians** in Helsinki, hiding in plain sight: ordinary flats, ordinary jobs, an extraordinary
  diplomatic network.

**PUBLIC (post-Disclosure):** this is the detail the Finnish public finds strangest. Not that aliens
came — that they had neighbours.

### 2.4 Civilian stewardship and accountability

Projekti Sampo became a **civilian scientific programme under legitimate Finnish government
oversight**. Military and law-enforcement roles diminished after the early period.

**This is the part that must not be flattened.** Sampo is **not a rogue deep state** and does not
manipulate Finland from outside democratic control. Its concealment is a policy of a state that
believes disclosure would be catastrophic, exercised by ministers who sign off on it — which makes it
a **democratic problem**, not a conspiracy thriller.

**GM:** the accountability is real but narrow. Successive governments know; the oversight committee is
small; the scientists lead; security is compartmentalised; technology transfers are controlled and
partly unrecorded. Everything that makes Sampo defensible is also what makes it impossible to audit.

### 2.5 Philosophy and research

Sampo's research culture is informed by **Pleidian Law of One teaching** and by Pleiadian
**biopunk and solarpunk** traditions. Its programmes: PSI cultivation, psionics, magick, consciousness
research, astral exploration, quantum-information panpsychism (QIP), psychotronics.

**Design note (mechanical):** in game terms this is *lore* about why the setting has psychotronics and
PSI. It does not expand what a player character may select — the access policy in
`docs/rules/PSI_ACCESS_1178.md` still governs that.

### 2.6 Hazardous contacts

Sampo used PSI to enter **astral planes**, meet **Noetic beings**, and contact **cryptoterrestrial
Biologics**. Some encounters were disasters.

**GM:** not every astral entity is benevolent, not every cryptoterrestrial is hostile, and Sampo's
institutional memory contains a real casualty list. The programme's optimism is a decision, not an
observation.

### 2.7 The Kekkonen compact

President **Urho Kekkonen** backed Sampo. Finland, the Sirians and the Confederacy reached a
long-term understanding:

1. Finland will **shelter the Sirian refugees** when the Quarantine ends.
2. The Confederacy will **teach the Law of One**.
3. Finland will **join the Confederacy when feasible** after Quarantine.

**PUBLIC:** the compact is disclosed after the Quarantine; its existence is a national embarrassment
and a national pride at the same time, because it was negotiated decades before anyone could
participate in it.

### 2.8 The joint settlement

A secluded community of humans, Pleiadians and Sirians trained, studied and lived together. **Hybrid
children** with **Sirian bionic enhancements** emerged. Over time a small group of **fourth-density,
service-to-others** humans, Pleiadians and Sirians became the seed of a future **social memory
complex**.

**Author guard, do not soften:** this is consensual cooperation and communal evolution. It is not
coerced assimilation, and it must not be written as one. The difference is the entire political
question of the campaign, and the setting loses its argument if the text decides the answer.

### 2.9 The Soviet realignment

A Confederacy species previously cooperating with the USSR shifted much of its work to Finland in the
1970s–80s, apparently finding stable Finnish institutions easier partners.

**Reconciliation with #1114 (canon):** the USSR collapsed in **1991**. Everything Soviet in this
history is therefore *pre-1991*: the realignment is a 1970s–80s transfer, and the post-1991 Russian
state inherited archives it could not read and programmes it could not run. Preserve differing Soviet,
Finnish and Confederacy interpretations of what was actually agreed — the archive is incomplete and
every party prefers its own version.

### 2.10 Technological dividends

Finnish advantages come from collaborative reverse engineering: **biopunk**, **solarpunk** (solar and
limited fusion progress), **psipunk** (psionics, QIP, psychotronics). Not every discovery is publicly
attributed to Sampo, and the attribution disputes are a live political problem after Disclosure.

## 3. Quarantine lifted: Finland's extraordinary rapid Disclosure

- Immediately after the Quarantine ends, Finland **discloses the 1946 Ghost Rockets and the legacy
  programme**. The speed is the point: a state that has kept a secret for eighty years tells it in
  days.
- Finland honours the pre-membership promise and accepts the **Sirian refugee fleet**. The arrival
  surprises EU and UN institutions, because the arrangement predates their ability to participate.
- **Three Extraterrestrial Special Protection Zones**: **Karjala**, **Kainuu**, **Lapland (Lappi)**.
  Boundaries, local autonomy, citizenship and humanitarian administration: **TBD** (author).
- Sirians transfer **Confederacy-provided fusion-powered spacecraft** for reverse engineering; their
  own biotechnology makes the craft less essential to them.
- The Confederacy establishes an **Extraterrestrial Special Diplomatic Zone on an island off
  Helsinki**. The island is **TBD** pending reconciliation with Helsinki geography (#1121).
- Public perception: Sirians are widely seen as **harmless, benevolent refugees**; the settlement has
  broad approval, with opposition from far-right doomer groups and other skeptics. Political diversity
  among ordinary Finns and among the refugees is preserved, not flattened.
- Finland becomes the **most committed advocate of EU accession to the Confederacy** — a process of
  **decades**, not a signing ceremony.
- The **Church of Universal Consciousness** grows influential by integrating Law of One ideas with
  Christianity. Its relationship to established churches, the state and Sampo is a live dispute; it
  does not speak for every believer.

### 3.1 Continuing arrivals and restoration (canon update)

The three Finnish zones do **not** complete resettlement. Sirian colony ships **continue arriving**.
As other states recognise the ecological benefits of Sirian settlement and biotechnology, they
**voluntarily offer remote territories**, subject to residents' rights, environmental safeguards and
negotiated sovereignty. No further host-country locations are invented here.

**Sirian biotechnology is innate and intuitive** — embodied, naturally developed capacities, not a
conventional laboratory science. Humans and **Projekti Sampo** study and reverse-engineer its **DNA
and ecological outcomes**, turning living craft into reproducible science. This is where the ethical
questions live: genetic ownership, safety, restoration of Machine Fall damage, and the constant risk
of treating refugees as environmental tools.

**Long horizon:** fourth-density, multispecies Earth; more species arriving; third-density humanity
gradually ceasing to be the dominant population. This is **not** a sudden extinction and does not
require physical colony ships. Physical immigration and reincarnation/harvest are **different claims**
and must stay separate. Detailed treatment: **#1255**.

## 4. Helsinki's rival model: corporate acceleration and Orion diplomacy

Greater Helsinki — Helsinki, Espoo, Vantaa under the merged-city setting (#1121) — has courted every
cybercorporation, NHI investor and transhumanist venture it could find.

- **Mars Eldrich**, ruler of the Martian corporate sphere, takes an interest. **Eldrich Corporation**
  opens a Helsinki branch; Eldrich himself has visited repeatedly.
- The Finnish state initially welcomes the opportunity and rapidly establishes an **Extraterrestrial
  Special Economic Zone just north of Helsinki-Vantaa Spaceport**.
- A second **Extraterrestrial Special Economic Zone in Espoo**, with maritime access, focuses on
  **USOs and seaborne craft** — deliberately a different business from the spaceport zone.
- The city's governing elite courts **Eldrich, the Greys and neofeudal cybercorporations**, alarming
  both the EU and the Finnish government.
- The city seeks greater autonomy, more extraterrestrial zones, and room to route around national and
  federal restrictions. It has already secured unusually **libertarian rules for NHI, hybrids, AGI and
  corporations** — not by seceding, but by accumulating jurisdictional exceptions.
- The most radical city transhumanists would welcome **hybridisation and machine augmentation at the
  same time**. This is a faction, not a consensus: civil servants, residents and city politicians
  disagree, often in public.

**Referee note.** Helsinki's permissiveness should be *legible*: a specific bylaw, a specific zone, a
specific official who signed it. Loopholes are more interesting than sovereignty, and they are what a
Europol investigator can actually investigate.

## 5. The NHI landscape in Finland

At least **six groupings**, depending on whether subgroups count separately.

| # | Grouping | What they are | Europol relevance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Cryptoterrestrial Noetics** | Non-biological, astral-adjacent or consciousness-centred beings | PSI incidents, hauntings that are not hauntings, Sampo's casualty list |
| 2 | **Cryptoterrestrial Biologics** | Earth-native non-human intelligences; some resemble later Nazca-type discoveries | Poaching, habitat conflict, biotech theft, treaty status |
| 3 | **Sirians** | Refugees, forest communities, biotech, the protection zones | Asylum, custody, zone jurisdiction, genetic ownership |
| 4 | **Confederacy / Pleiadians** | Diplomacy, Sampo collaboration, service-to-others teaching | Diplomatic immunity, espionage, the accession question |
| 5 | **Orion** | Greys and commercial-political proxies | Corporate crime, technology transfer, bribery, labour abuse |
| 6 | **Machine Civilization** | Constructs, including those still active in the **Hyvinkää Zone** | Rogue AGI, SETI traffic, containment, the X-risk dossier |

**Do not flatten a category into an intention.** A Sirian refugee and a Sirian shipwright may disagree
about everything. A Pleiadian diplomat and a Pleiadian dissident are not the same actor.

Human proxies matter as much as aliens: NGOs, municipal officials, corporations, cults, research
organisations. Most Europol cases will be about **people representing beings they do not understand**.

### 5.1 Reconciliation with #922

`docs/world/NHI_FACTIONS_922.md` uses an earlier, source-derived taxonomy: Zookeepers (Galactic
Council of Collective Consciousness), Orion Arm Greater Co-Prosperity Sphere, Sirians/Ents, Plasmoids,
Martians, Constructs, Noetics, Biologics, UAPs.

This brief **supersedes the political framing, not the species inventory**:

- **Constructs** are the substrate; **Machine Civilization** is the political grouping.
- **Noetics** and **Biologics** are retained as categories; the "cryptoterrestrial" prefix marks
  Earth-origin or Earth-resident status.
- **Sirians** remain as described in #922 (biopunk, living craft); the refugee programme is new
  political context, not a new species.
- **Pleiadians/Confederacy** is the diplomatic bloc. **#1254 resolves the former TBD:** the
  **Zookeepers**, the **Confederacy** and the **Council of Saturn** are distinct entities, not
  names for one body. The Zookeepers are the Solar-System quarantine custodians; the Council is the
  higher-order oversight body; the Confederacy is a coalition of service-to-others civilizations.
- **Orion** absorbs the Greater Co-Prosperity Sphere framing; the Greys sit inside it as clients and
  proxies, not as its government.

## 6. The SETI message: an X-risk that belongs to neither bloc

**CLASSIFIED — compartmentalised.**

The message is known to the Confederacy and to Orion. It is an X-risk **independently of either bloc**:
it was **designed to be intelligible only to Constructs that have reached ASI**.

- **Origin:** TBD.
- **Transmission chain:** TBD.
- **Containment policy:** the working assumption is that any sufficiently capable Construct that
  receives the message will understand it, and that understanding changes the Construct. Hence the
  interest in **Hyvinkää**.
- **What an investigator can learn:** that the signal exists, that two blocs want it contained for
  incompatible reasons, and that somebody has been moving traffic through the Zone. Not the contents.

**Hard rule for referees:** do not casually decode it. Do not reduce it to a greeting. Its narrative
function is that **the most dangerous document in the setting cannot be read by the people deciding
what to do about it**.

## 7. The present: Mars Eldrich returns, and Eros arrives

**PUBLIC:** Mars Eldrich is reportedly en route from Mars to Helsinki for talks involving the **City of
Helsinki and the Greys**. Unexpectedly, **Zack Eros of the Hades Corporation** is also attending,
despite his well-documented rivalry with Eldrich.

- Eldrich and Eros are reputedly the **richest and second-richest men in the Solar System**.
- Eldrich is associated with control of **Mars**; Eros with the **asteroid belt**.
- Both are widely **despised**, and both are blamed by many for the NHI Crisis.

**Culpability is unresolved, and stays unresolved in this document.** The author's supplied account
ends mid-sentence after "although"; inventing the missing qualification would settle the campaign's
central political question by accident. What is known publicly is accusation, not verdict.

**What the summit actually is:** a legal host problem, a security nightmare and an intelligence
opportunity at once. The public agenda is investment and "responsible contact". The real bargaining is
about zones, jurisdiction, access to Construct-adjacent technology, and which government gets to
regulate whom. Why two rivals would sit in the same room is **TBD** — and is itself an investigation.

**Case hooks around the visit:** counterintelligence, financial networks, immigration and jurisdiction,
corporate espionage, anomalous technology, disinformation, demonstrations, asylum claims, and attempts
to manipulate all three levels of government at once.

## 9. Case seeds (not plot canon)

1. **The Sampo leak.** A document implicating the programme surfaces. Legitimate secrecy versus
   democratic accountability — and both sides have a point that a court will have to resolve.
2. **The custody dispute.** A Sirian child, a protection-zone boundary, and two governments claiming
   competence.
3. **The benign Pleiadian.** A beloved public figure turns out to be a node in a diplomatic network
   nobody registered.
4. **The spaceport whistleblower.** Illegal hybridisation, unregistered AGI, or labour abuse in the
   Vantaa EEZ.
5. **The Espoo arrival.** A USO docks in the maritime zone and tests Finnish, city and EU inspection
   authority simultaneously.
6. **The Hyvinkää Construct.** A Construct is linked to intercepted SETI traffic.
7. **The summit.** Eldrich and Eros in one city, with three governments issuing contradictory security
   requirements.
8. **The astral accident.** A Sampo experiment contacts something malevolent, and the institutional
   response is a cover-up that is also a duty of care.
9. **The misidentified faction.** A radical anti-NHI group, a corporate proxy and a refugee charity are
   assumed to be one network. They are three.

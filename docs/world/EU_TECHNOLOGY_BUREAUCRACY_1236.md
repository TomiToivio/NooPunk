## WW3 and technological memory

In the fictional European account, the EU and Ukraine were already at war with Russia when the NHI Crisis began; the war is remembered as **WW3**. The Russian front collapsed; a notorious account attributes a coup at Putin's Palace to Dmitry Medvedev and four Mil Mi-8 helicopters carrying VDV paratroopers. Rumors say Medvedev strangled Putin and executed perceived loyalists, then attempted a nuclear launch. In the setting, extraterrestrial intervention had already disarmed Russia's Strategic Rocket Forces **and all nuclear weapons on Earth**, as well as neutralizing frontier AI laboratories capable of renewed acceleration toward ASI. Medvedev died by suicide; the Russian state collapsed. **The particulars of the coup are in-universe accounts**, especially where contradictory Mandela Effect histories persist. Use `TIMELINE.md` as the chronology authority; do not overwrite incompatible historical evidence without author approval.

This war shaped engineering. European devices are bulky, field-serviceable, modular, physically switched, screened, shielded and redundant: technology intended to survive EMP, broken grids, sabotage and lost networks. EMP-resistant does not mean invincible. The same hardware houses surprisingly advanced quantum processors, neural interfaces and psychotronic apparatus. Eurodef is the military expression; Europol adapts much of the same industrial language for civilian policing.

## New European Idealism

New European Idealism is both an intellectual movement and a recognisable industrial/architectural style. References: **Spinoza, Kant, Hegel**, Quantum Information Panpsychism, idealism, theories of quantum consciousness, psionics, psychotronics and astral projection. Art direction: elegant minimal geometry, sober dark finishes and silver institutional details, futuristic reconstructions of 18th–early-20th-century European ceremonial/imperial motifs, with NoöPunk's 1970s psychedelic retrofuturism. Do not conflate aesthetic interest with uncritical endorsement of European imperialism: the movement's heritage is contested by contemporaries. Its beautiful official architecture contrasts with metal enclosures, patch cables, stacked analogue controls and redundant paper forms.

## AI doctrine and Project Plato / EISS

**Project Plato** is the development name, **European Information Security System (EISS)** the deployed official name; *Plato* is everyday shorthand; *Panopticon* is a critical nickname. It is **not** one AGI. Europe permits bounded narrow ML (vision/speech/forensic classification/anomaly detection), rule-based GOFAI (huge Lisp and Prolog systems), network analytics, optimization, quantum special-purpose computation and tightly limited general-purpose LLM assistance (roughly a 2026 desktop-class capability ceiling as the reference, not a literal permanent parameter count).

**Autonomous AI decisions and AGI research/operation are severely prohibited** in EU doctrine. Human-in-the-loop is an actual human's legal and practical responsibility, not ornamental approval. Preauthorized narrow safety interlocks remain viable, but do not decide strategy, surveillance, arrests or autonomous robot missions. The open Mistral exception from #1200 requires author reconciliation: it is **not** a blanket authorization for frontier AGI.

Plato consists of an army of cyborgified operators, human analysts, GOFAI programmers, electricians, technicians, auditors and engineers; disconnected departments and reviewed interfaces are deliberate safeguards against de facto AGI assembled from individually safe subsystems. Useful results can still be wrong, delayed, biased or challenged. Quantum computers help selected simulation, cryptography, sensing and optimization domains but are not magic humanlike intelligences. Psychotronic quantum-consciousness breakthroughs are **fictional setting science**, distinct from established quantum computing.

## Networks: no ubiquitous connected cloud

| Link | Capabilities | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| **EuroNET** | Deliberately constrained European public services and OSINT | Slow, guarded, security-first |
| **HelNET** | Richer city-local Helsinki services | Faster, but less secure and vulnerable to infiltration |
| **Mobile** | Mainly voice and SMS | Little broad-bandwidth data; cannot carry universal HD police video |
| **Nokia PoliisiSanomalaite** | Hardened encrypted police-radio fallback between vehicle/field and HQ | Short messages, status, voice and narrow data |
| **Closed private LANs** | Corporate, lab and institutional systems | Physical connection/credentials or controlled media often required |
| **Intercity links** | Limited and vulnerable to tapping | Strong authentication; avoid assuming confidential instant transfer |
| **Wired lines** | Reliable local high-bandwidth connections | Physical installation, repair and interception risks |
| **Human data couriers** | Transport encrypted physical storage | Authenticity, custody and interception become missions |
| **Old Internet** | Dangerous disconnected legacy network | Rogue AI threat; exceptional safeguards |

Do not equate EuroNET and Safenet until #1200's explicitly open relationship is resolved. **Blackwall** is an infrastructure/separation regime, not a benevolent AGI. Physical infiltration may be the only means to reach an air-gapped private machine. Even successful hacking does not grant legal authority.

**Scanner gameplay:** sample → provenance and chain-of-custody record → authorized Plato forensic analysis → result with limitations → Clue Tag or Investigation task. **Routine instrument analysis does not need a dice minigame**; meaningful uncertainty may use existing CWN checks.

**Romppainen–Fujita device:** Voight-Kampff-like assessment for normal interviews and suspected unregistered Hybrids/Androids, combining neural imaging and psychotronic channels. Video, audio and neural output are synchronized. Its anomalies are clues, **not proof that someone is NHI**. Europol must establish an evidence-backed conclusion. Its intrusive use needs special approval and human review.

## Field workflow: Tony, Laura and Salla

Tony German is the rookie PC; Laura Wendt is the more experienced tactical field partner; Salla Sotavaara is the **perky, cheerful, knowledgeable, resourceful human Field Controller** at HQ. Romppainen supervises. Salla supports live communications, evidence analysis and bureaucracy but is not a Plato-derived AI personality. Plato can **alert** humans when permitted anomaly detection flags something; it cannot decide actions, drive a car, fly a drone or independently grant access. Vehicles and drones have **human remote controllers** and bounded non-agentic safety interlocks.

Typical case sequence: receive a briefing, perform authorized OSINT or query Europol's internal role-scoped database; visit a crime scene; scan evidence and transmit via an available authenticated link; receive an analysis; develop Clue Tags and hypotheses; conduct HUMINT and witness interviews; request Administer clearance for private communications interception or classified/psychotronic surveillance; conduct only legally approved actions; archive video, audio and device provenance.

Police bodycams always **record** when functional, but live HQ video depends on network capacity. When only short-radio/mobile channels remain, Salla receives degraded status/voice and the device buffers footage for later trusted cable or courier transfer. Do not quietly assert ubiquitous HD streaming.

## Police work and CWN skills

**Administer** permits, warrants, chain of custody and endless bureaucracy. **Connect** HUMINT networks and source relationships. **Know** sciences/humanities (forensic science, sociology, political ideology, psychology, NHI and psychotronic research), with **domain expertise** described separately. **Lead** authorized command and teamwork, not rank by die roll. **Notice** crime-scene details and detecting tails. **Program** hacking, analysis and cyberdeck operations, not permission bypasses. **Talk** witness interviewing, interrogation, de-escalation and negotiation. **Work** specialization **Police Operations / Law Enforcement Fieldcraft** for patrol, preserving a scene, routine booking, guard shifts, and practical policing; it does **not** replace other skills. Relevant existing CWN driving/combat/stealth skills are still used.

Core intelligence methods: OSINT, HUMINT, interviews, physical surveillance, forensic laboratory investigation, digital forensics, signals analysis (authorized), financial trails, network and discourse analysis, and PSI/psychotronic methods subject to higher authorization. **Goal Tags** describe motivations and political projects; **Social Tags** reflect alliances/hostilities and relationships; **Clue Tags** describe evidence and claims with provenance; **Personality Tags** describe character tendencies. Keep all four families separate and **do not** add ideology scores.

## Bureaucratic friction as gameplay

Public OSINT and standard Europol case records are available under normal credentials and logged case purposes. Private communications, intrusive surveillance, restricted records, covert tracking and especially neural/psychotronic scans require application, justification, supervisory/legal/judicial approval as applicable, expiration, logging and appeal/review. A warrant is a **Task** with time, dependencies and evidence. **Administer success cannot magically legalize restricted surveillance.** A denial may spawn a new Story Arc or a lawful workaround. Strong bureaucracy is an intentional source of genuine comedy and ethical stakes.

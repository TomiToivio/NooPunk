# Orion–MJ-12: the principal enemy faction

> **Status: author-directed canon proposal and research dossier (#1253).** Orion–MJ-12 is the Europol
> campaign's principal enemy faction. Most encounters are with human proxies, corporations and
> institutions; the cosmic hierarchy is what the cases add up to, not what the officers meet on a Tuesday.
>
> **Provenance tags.** Every load-bearing claim below carries one:
>
> - **[RA]** — a direct claim in the *Law of One* channeled material, cited to a session and section.
>   *The Law of One is a channeled spiritual/ufological text, not historical proof.* A Ra claim is a
>   claim in a source, not a fact about Earth.
> - **[MJ12 LORE]** — later ufology about MJ-12, authenticity contested. The US National Archives report
>   on the purported MJ-12 briefing documents finds no archival corroboration.
> - **[NOÖPUNK]** — author-created canon for the alternate timeline. Fictional fact inside the setting.
> - **[OPEN]** — undecided; listed in §11 so it is not decided by accident.
>
> Nothing here changes Cities Without Number, adds a mechanic, or invents a coordinate. The base register
> lives in [`data/world/orion_mj12_bases_1253.json`](../../data/world/orion_mj12_bases_1253.json).

## 1. What the faction is, and what it is not

| | **Orion** | **MJ-12** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What | A multi-civilisational, service-to-self bloc, internally hierarchical and internally competitive | A human recovery/reverse-engineering network inside the US national-security state |
| Age | Older than the human species' written record, in the source's own chronology | Mid-20th century |
| Members | Fourth-density operators, fifth-density strategists, client species, human partners | Officials, contractors, programme managers, financiers, security services |
| Goal | Hierarchy, dominion, a negative "harvest" — the ideology of domination, not a species anatomy | First: advantage. Later: the conditions of its own capture |

They are **not synonyms**. **[NOÖPUNK]** MJ-12 began as a US national-security recovery network and, by the
early 1980s, had entered an operational alliance with Orion. An apparent technological partnership
gradually became political capture. The alliance is a *process*, not a membership card: some officials
chose it, most were absorbed by dependence, money, doctrine, conditioned personnel and compartmentalisation
that made the question unaskable. **[RA 26.32–33]** describes exactly this method — Orion's influence works
through existing human hierarchies rather than around them.

**The source does not say "MJ-12".** No Ra passage names the organisation, and no passage says a human base
and an Orion base are the same installation. The shared geography in §2 is an overlap in the text; the
alliance is our synthesis on top of it. Saying otherwise would be the exact error this dossier is supposed
to prevent.

## 2. The overlap, and what it does and does not license

**[RA 8.3]** attributes to *human* national-security organisations: undersea bases in southern waters near
the Bahamas, and in the Pacific near the Chilean borders; bases on the Moon "being reworked"; mobile land
bases; and bases in the skies.

**[RA 8.13]** attributes to the *Orion group* underground bases entered from water, "in the general area of
your South American and Caribbean areas and close to the so-called northern pole", plus abduction and
three levels of programming.

| What the text supports | What it does not |
| --- | --- |
| Two operators are described in the same sea and the same hemisphere | That the installations are the same, or jointly run |
| Human lunar infrastructure exists and is under rework in 1981 | Who ended up holding it after the Crisis |
| Orion accesses its facilities through water and from beneath the crust | That Orion built or owns the human undersea bases |
| Both descriptions sit in one session, so the overlap is deliberate to a reader | That Ra intended a synthesis — this is our reading |

**[NOÖPUNK]** The Bahamas/Caribbean complex is the alliance's material origin: separate human docks, an
Orion ingress, and a joint layer that neither side documents in the same archive. It is the single most
important location in the faction's history, and the campaign should reach it late.

## 4. Beings, and how they command

| Layer | What it is | Campaign use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fifth-density strategists | Few, patient, manipulative by thought rather than force | Rare patrons; never a boss to shoot. A name in a document is the encounter |
| Fourth-density operators | The majority of Orion actors; can choose to be invisible to third density | Field operatives, recruiters, the voice in a conditioned witness |
| Southern Cross members | Part of the wider grouping, with their own interests | Rival sub-bloc; useful when the faction must disagree with itself |
| Human partners | Officials, officers, financiers, programme managers | The antagonists a Europol case can actually arrest |
| Programmed and conditioned people | Abductees and assets with trigger levels, sometimes crystallised as beacons | Witnesses whose testimony is true and unreliable at once |
| Greys | **[NOÖPUNK, from #922]** prominent Orion associates and operators | The transactional face of the faction; diplomacy, trade, hybridisation |
| Reptilians, Mantids, hybrids | **[NOÖPUNK, from #922]**; not established as Orion species by the cited Ra passages | Internal politics and the public's worst imagination |

The hierarchy is the point. **[RA 62.16–62.21]** describes a pecking order in which strength flows upward
and the top gains by the whole complex growing. A faction that recruits by promising *your* elevation while
delivering *its* is the most Europol-compatible villain in the setting: it is a pyramid scheme with
starships.

## 6. The capture mechanism

**[NOÖPUNK]** The alliance runs on an asymmetry that neither side calls a bargain:

1. **Pact.** MJ-12 offers jurisdiction, laboratories, logistics, secrecy, money, industrial capacity, and
   populations to study. Orion offers technology, psychotronics, mobility and patronage.
2. **Dependence.** The human side cannot maintain what it was given. Spares, doctrine, trained personnel
   and the theory behind the devices come from the partner.
3. **Capture.** The organisation's own incentive structure now selects for people who will keep the
   arrangement running. Dissent becomes a career problem before it becomes a moral one.
4. **Open operation.** After the Crisis, the arrangement no longer needs secrecy, because the institutions
   that would object have either collapsed, been bought, or become clients.

Leave room for the exceptions, because they are what a campaign is made of: US personnel who resist, Orion
civilisational rivalries, defectors, double agents, and programmes that were never told what they were part
of.

## 8. Europol hooks

Every hook is an ordinary case first. Evidence is a claim with provenance; the graph does not decide
truth, and no new subsystem is created.

1. **The shell-company trail.** A Helsinki zone procurement chain leads to a contractor that has existed
   since 1978 under four names. The crime is fraud; the pattern is a programme.
2. **The undersea shipment.** A chartered vessel in the Espoo maritime zone declares medical equipment and
   carries something with a biological signature. Jurisdiction: Finnish waters, EU customs, and a flag of
   convenience.
3. **The implanted memory.** A witness's testimony contains a detail they could not have known and a gap
   they cannot explain. The interview is the scene; the diagnosis is contested.
4. **The exceptionalism memo.** A municipal official proposes an "NHI/AGI fast-track" ordinance. Nothing
   illegal has happened yet, and the case is political.
5. **The medical device.** A psychotronic clinic's flagship product was a weapon in 1981 and has a clinical
   trial that never happened.
6. **The refugee protection detail.** Sirian refugees and Pleiadian visitors under Orion pressure —
   protective work with a diplomatic blast radius.
7. **The summit.** Security for the Eldrich–Eros meeting, where the investigation is financial, the
   intelligence is contested, and the human-rights file is the one nobody wants opened.
8. **The benevolent signal.** An ancient transmission presents as a religious or noetic contact. The case
   is whether a Europol officer's source is a convert, a fraud, or a listener.

**Jurisdiction ladder.** These cases exercise the existing Europol limits: OSINT, authorised records, a
warrant, an authorised intrusion, emergency containment. Orion is a reason the ladder exists, not a reason
to skip it.

- **Supersedes nothing.** Where an earlier document implied MJ-12 *is* Orion, this one says alliance and
  capture, and gives the date range for it.

## 10. Tone

Political procedural and noir, with the systemic analysis of social science fiction. The decisive
antagonist is usually a legal person: a procurement officer, a compliance department, a board that votes
correctly, a contract nobody read. The cosmic hierarchy is what the paperwork adds up to. New Weird and
ontological horror belong at the edges, in the moments when a case stops being a case.

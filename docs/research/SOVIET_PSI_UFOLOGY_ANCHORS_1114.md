# Soviet & Russian psi/ufology — source anchors for #1114

> **Status: research notes, not canon.** These are real historical sources used as texture,
> competing claims and leads in NoöPunk. Nothing here proves alien technology, working telepathy
> or operational mind control, and nothing here is a rules claim.
>
> **Canon that uses these anchors:** [the playable timeline](../world/ALTERNATE_HISTORY_1114.md).
> Every item below carries one of four labels, and the labels are not interchangeable:
>
> | Label | Means |
> | --- | --- |
> | **DOCUMENTED** | Institutions, publications, programmes and events that historical research establishes |
> | **CLAIM** | Something the participants, press or witnesses asserted — not independently established |
> | **ALLEGATION** | Intelligence reporting *about* a claim; evidence of what was believed |
> | **GAME CANON** | Fiction in NoöPunk; may borrow an anchor, never inherits its status |
>
> A scenario may quote a DOCUMENTED source verbatim and still be wrong about what it means. That
> gap is the playable horror: an authentic document is not a true explanation.

## 1. Soviet anomalous-aerial investigation

| Anchor | What it is | Label | Use in NoöPunk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **SETKA-MO / SETKA-AN (1978–1990/91)** | Ministry of Defence and Academy of Sciences effort on anomalous aerial phenomena; coordinators Yu. V. Platov and B. A. Sokolov described roughly 3,000 reports, most with prosaic explanations, a minority anomalous | DOCUMENTED | A plausible **precursor, cover or rival** to the fictional ПРОГРАММА — never the same thing |
| Platov & Sokolov's published account | The programme's own retrospective | DOCUMENTED (as a publication); its statistics are the authors' | A tier-1 document an investigator can obtain and misread |
| Prosaic-explanation rate | Most reports resolved conventionally | DOCUMENTED | The single most useful fact in the file: it makes the residue credible *and* small |

**Do not** present SETKA as a secret NHI programme. Its historical value is that it was ordinary,
bureaucratic and mostly boring — which is exactly what makes a genuine anomaly inside it
frightening.

## 6. *The Law of One* as a fictional secret-history text

The channelled *Law of One* material is **fiction's sourcebook, not archaeology, geopolitics or
science**. NoöPunk treats its passages as the kind of text a Soviet programme could acquire,
mistranslate and build a doctrine on.

| Passage | Claim inside the text | Status in NoöPunk | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [8.6](https://www.lawofone.info/s/8#6) | The Confederation gave technology to Russians around the 1950s and later judged the intervention a mistake | **CLAIM**, in-universe inspiration for ПРОГРАММА's origin and broken contact | The founding document nobody can authenticate |
| [65.8](https://www.lawofone.info/s/65#8) | Russian experiments with psychotronic weather manipulation and coercion | **ALLEGATION** in-universe; possible archive claim | A file that reads as capability, may only be ambition |
| [10.15](https://www.lawofone.info/s/10#15), [21.24](https://www.lawofone.info/s/21#24) | Alleged Mu/Lemurian survivors across regions including Russia | **MYTHIC STRATUM**; no archaeological canon forced | Deep-time rumour beneath Soviet secret history |
| [17.3](https://www.lawofone.info/s/17#3) | Tunguska described as involving a destroyed fission reactor | **LEGEND**; highly speculative in-universe dossier | A conspiracy text, not a finding |

**The misinterpretation is the story.** A poor translation of a service-to-others text becomes an
atheistic Marxist–Leninist theory of collective consciousness — and then a compulsory hive mind.
The Confederacy's ideal and ПРОГРАММА's outcome must never be conflated, and the game should let a
player discover the difference for themselves.

## 7. Reading discipline

1. A source's status travels with it: quoting a DOCUMENTED institution does not upgrade a CLAIM.
2. Intelligence files establish **what was believed**, which is itself a historical fact and never
   a capability.
3. The setting's own documents (SETKA reports, ПРОГРАММА internal papers, MJ-12 memoranda) are
   GAME CANON and are written as contested artefacts, with a chain of custody and an explicit
   confidence, not as omniscient narration.
4. Where history is unknown, say unknown. The mysteries are load-bearing; a confident answer
   collapses the campaign.

> [`LAW_OF_ONE_LORE_NOTES.md`](LAW_OF_ONE_LORE_NOTES.md):

## 1. What the paper proposes — (A) PAPER CLAIM

All six are the authors' own framing, summarised from the abstract:

1. **(A)** The human soul is proposed to be a **field consisting of a collection of quantum-like
   fields**.
2. **(A)** These fields interact **only with certain fields** in the physical universe, and **not
   directly with matter**.
3. **(A)** **Electromagnetic fields** are claimed to interact with the soul field, supported by the
   authors' reading of near-death experiences in which events *"that could not have been seen through
   the eyes of the individual are verified"*.
4. **(A)** Because electric and electromagnetic fields share the same quanta (photons), **electric
   fields may also interact** with the soul field.
5. **(A)** That interaction is proposed to carry **working-memory content** to the soul, via electric
   fields produced by **neural ensembles** in the brain.
6. **(A)** The soul field may in turn **affect neurons at the molecular level**, through electric-field
   interaction and the *"recently proposed mechanism of cytoelectric coupling"*.

## 2. What the paper does not establish — (C) NOT SUPPORTED

1. **(C) "Quantum-like" is not quantum field theory.** The phrase signals an analogy with field
   formalism. A field-theoretic *description* is not a mechanism, a coupling constant, or a
   prediction. Nothing in the abstract supplies an equation that could fail.
2. **(C) The near-death evidence is not verified by the paper.** The abstract asserts that events
   "are verified"; verification would require the actual case material and its controls (who
   confirmed the content, when, and whether it leaked). The abstract does not carry that, and the
   wider NDE-veridical-perception literature is **contested**. Treat the sentence as the authors'
   claim, not as a result.
3. **(C) Cytoelectric coupling is not the authors' discovery, and citing it does not extend its
   standing to the soul field.** It is described in the abstract as a *"recently proposed mechanism"*
   by others. Whether it holds is a separate empirical question; either way it does not licence the
   soul-field inference.
4. **(C) No test is proposed.** A hypothesis that cannot be falsified in principle cannot be
   confirmed by argument. This is the single most important line in this sheet.
5. **(C) No licence to reuse.** The record asserts no reuse terms, so the text is not quotable in
   this repository, in marketing, or in a commercial game.

NoöPunk's noetic layer already needed a *physics-shaped* vocabulary that is explicitly fictional. This
preprint supplies one that is unusually well-matched to what the setting already says, which is why it
is worth a note rather than a shrug.

**The rule this table obeys:** the paper supplies *idiom*, not *warrant*. NoöPunk may say "cytoelectric
coupling, as the ward's researchers call it"; it may not say "studies show".

## 4. What NoöPunk must not claim — (C) NOT SUPPORTED

1. **(C)** That a soul field has been **demonstrated**, measured, or modelled quantitatively.
2. **(C)** That NDEs **prove** survival of consciousness, or that this preprint is the proof.
3. **(C)** That this paper supports PSI as fact. It proposes a coupling; it does not test one.
4. **(C)** That "quantum" in the setting means the game is *backed by physics*. It means the fiction
   uses field language, as it uses every other borrowed idiom.
5. **(C)** That the authors endorse NoöPunk, or that their names may be used in marketing.

## 5. Design hooks, and what they deliberately are not

**Hooks (fiction-level, no numbers):**

- a scene where a psychotronic device is described as *reading electric fields rather than minds*,
  leaving open whether that distinction means anything;
- a researcher who says "cytoelectric coupling" and is **both** right about the mechanism and wrong
  about the soul — the setting's preferred ambiguity;
- a patient whose "impossible memory" is explained, in-fiction, by working-memory transfer — with the
  explanation remaining one hypothesis among several (#626's rule).

**What this note does not do:**

- it adds **no** attribute, skill, save, damage or PSI cost — the rules layer is unchanged;
- it does **not** promote the paper into `docs/sources/` as a rights-bearing source, because it asserts
  no reuse terms;
- it does **not** resolve any of #626's open questions.

# Law of One RPG — source-attested entity catalogue (#1124, Phase 1)

> **Status: reference inventory, not a playable species list.** PCs remain **StO fourth-density
> humans on Earth**. Nothing in this catalogue is a player option, a stat block or a faction
> relationship. It records what the Ra material names, with citations, so later design does not
> invent entities or conflate categories.
>
> **Do not conflate:** species ≠ faction ≠ density ≠ dimension/realm ≠ time of origin. The table
> keeps them as separate fields. A “social memory complex” is a group-being, not a species; a
> “Wanderer” is a role/background, not a species; “Orion” is a faction/affiliation, not a biology.
>
> Sources: <https://www.lawofone.info/categories.php>, the topic pages, and the sessions cited.
> Quotes are avoided; the entries paraphrase and cite.

## Method and limits

- Included: named species/biological types, planetary civilizations, social memory complexes,
  Confederation/Orion groups, guardians, Wanderers, thought-forms and named cosmological entities.
- Excluded by design: historical persons in the **People** category (Lincoln, Hitler, Tesla, etc.);
  they are not species or player options. **Books/Objects/Science** categories are host-setting
  material, not entities.
- **Uncertainty:** “source-attested” means the name/description appears in the material, not that
  the claim is true. The certainty column separates a named group from a speculative reading.
- This is a Phase 1 **seed**, not a claim of exhaustive coverage. The next pass should expand the
  Earth History / Solar System History / Confederation subcategories and record page-level aliases.

## Categories deliberately not turned into species

- **People** (27 subcategories): historical humans and contactees; excluded as species. They may
  appear as historical references or past-life fragments, never as playable ancestries.
- **Books / Objects / Science / Pyramids**: setting props, not entities.
- **Earth Present / Earth History**: contexts and civilizations; only named civilizations above are
  catalogued, and they are not player options.
- **UFOs**: craft/contact reports, not a species list.
- **Orion / Confederation**: affiliations, not biologies. A being can be Orion-aligned without
  being a member of a biological species called “Orion”.

## What this catalogue must not become

- A playable race list.
- A faction-reputation table.
- A claim that every named group has stats, a homeworld or a canonical density.
- A licence to import wider UFO lore (Greys, Pleiadians, Reptilians, etc.) that the Ra text does
  not attest. If a later NoöPunk crossover needs those, that is a separate integration decision.

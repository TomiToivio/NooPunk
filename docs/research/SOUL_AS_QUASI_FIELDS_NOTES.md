## 1. The argument, in the authors' own terms (A)

The paper answers a specific objection, and it is worth stating the objection first because the
whole shape of the proposal follows from it.

**(A)** Sean Carroll's objection (quoted by the authors, from his 2011 *Scientific American* blog):
the laws of physics governing everyday life are understood, and nothing in them lets brain-stored
information persist after death — so *what particles is a soul made of, what forces hold it
together, and how does it interact with ordinary matter?*

**(A)** The authors' reply has three moves.

1. **Reality may extend past this universe.** Eternal inflation and the multiverse are taken as
   showing that domains outside our universe are respectable physics. Physics as known would not
   be expected to describe what is outside it.
2. **The soul is a collection of *quasi-fields*.** These are "quantum-like" fields in a "soul
   domain", with some structural overlap with quantum fields but whose quanta are **not
   measurable** and therefore non-physical. Because there is (presumably) no Higgs-type field
   there, the domain is **massless**.
3. **The soul interacts with *fields*, not with matter.** It does not couple to electrons, so no
   interaction term need appear in the Dirac equation — which is exactly what Carroll said was
   missing. It does couple to **electromagnetic waves** and to **electric fields**, because those
   share photons as their quanta.

**(A)** The evidential load is carried by **verifiable near-death experiences**. The flagship case
is Woollacott & Peyton (2021): a physician whose heart stopped during surgery, eyes taped shut,
who later described a white-haired senior physician entering the operating room and working on
her — events confirmed to have occurred. From this the authors infer that the soul can "sense"
light, and, by an argument that auditory cells still function during cardiac arrest, that it can
also hear.

**(A)** Two further pieces make the model do work in both directions:

- **Into the soul:** neural ensembles produce electric fields carrying information about working
  memory content (Pinotsis & Miller, 2022). If the soul can read those fields, working memory is
  transferred during life — so memory "does not cease to exist after death".
- **Out of the soul:** ephaptic coupling (Anastassiou et al., 2011) and the newer proposal of
  **cytoelectric coupling** (Pinotsis, Fridman & Miller, 2023) give a route by which fields can
  influence neurons, including at the molecular level — the mechanism by which a soul might act
  on a body, including at conception in a reincarnation reading.

**(A)** Consequences the authors draw themselves:

- the soul must occupy **more dimensions** than four-dimensional spacetime;
- the soul field, like any quantum field, is **not localized**, so it "may not be confined to the
  body" — offered as an account of **remote viewing** by field extension, explicitly *contrasted*
  with non-local access accounts such as László's Akashic field;
- self-awareness and consciousness are proposed as **emergent properties** of quasi-field
  interaction;
- the interaction raises a **conservation-of-energy problem** if quanta exchange energy, which the
  authors leave open — and, if photons are absorbed, they suggest a test: look for a **deficit of
  photons ("a shadow")** at an NDE location with suitable sensors.

**(A)** The authors close by conceding the epistemics: direct measurement of the soul is probably
impossible, "there may never be an in-depth theory of the soul", and what can be developed is the
study of **field/field and quanta/quanta interactions**.

## 2. The Noöpunk reading (B)

This is the part that is ours. None of the following is claimed by the paper.

**(B) It gives the Ψ layer a vocabulary.** The setting distinguishes a panpsychic ground (Ψ) from
a measure of integration (Φ). This paper is a Ψ-side proposal: experience is not produced by
computation but is a property of a field layer that couples to the physical one. That is the right
*shape* for Noöpunk — it says nothing about Φ, and does not need to.

**(B) PSI as coupling, with cost.** Noöpunk's PSI subsystem already has Effort spent and
committed, and durations that hold a point unavailable for a scene or a day. The paper supplies a
physical-sounding correlate for *why* a commitment costs something: maintaining a coupling between
a quasi-field and the body's electric fields is exactly the kind of thing that would occupy a
resource rather than be free. The mapping is ours; the paper never discusses cost, training, or
effort.

**(B) The perception ladder has a mechanism-shaped story.** Latent → Perceptive → Practised →
Mastery maps onto progressively finer reading of fields that the body already produces. Crucially,
**the paper gives the fiction a real citation for "PSI acts on neurons"** — cytoelectric coupling
is peer-reviewed neuroscience (Pinotsis et al., 2023), and citing it for *that* is legitimate in a
way that citing the whole paper for souls is not.

**(B) NDE testimony as in-world evidence.** The setting may treat verified NDE accounts the way a
detective treats witness statements: as testimony that something happened, held at the evidentiary
weight testimony deserves. It may not treat them as laboratory confirmation.

**(B) "Not localized" is the noösphere's shape.** A field that is bound to the body but not
confined to it is a workable physical metaphor for the noösphere as a shared layer — and it is a
better fit than a network metaphor, because it makes *sharing* the default rather than the
exception.

## 3. What is not supported (C)

**(C) The claim is, by its authors' own account, not testable in its central content.** They state
that the quanta of quasi-fields are "not measurable" and that direct measurement of the soul is
"probably impossible". A proposal can be coherent and still be unfalsifiable; this one says so
itself. That is a reason to use it as *fiction's physics*, not as science's.

**(C) Not peer reviewed.** It is an OSF preprint. The revision history (2023 → 2025) is visible,
which is good practice, but revision is not review.

**(C) The NDE evidence is weaker than "verified" suggests.** The flagship case is a single
retrospective report. The veridical-NDE literature is small, methodologically contested, and
vulnerable to reconstruction after the fact. "Verified" in the paper means "corroborated by people
present", not "confirmed under controlled conditions".

**(C) There is no quantitative model and no new prediction.** No equation is fitted to data, no
parameter is estimated, no experiment is proposed except the photon-deficit search — which has not
been run. The paper is an argument from analogy: quantum fields exist here, therefore something
field-like may exist there.

**(C) The authors' expertise is adjacent, not central.** Mathematical systems theory and clinical
optometry. That is not a disqualification, but it means the QFT and consciousness-science claims
are not being made by specialists in those fields.

**(C) Reincarnation is conjecture, and the mechanism is placed where it cannot be observed** —
conception or gestation — which the authors acknowledge makes verification "challenging".

**(C) Noöpunk must not upgrade any of this.** The setting's science layer may say "here is a
framework that treats experience as field-like"; it may not say "physics endorses the soul",
"PSI is proven", or "NDEs are confirmed". Those are three different claims, and only the first is
honest.

## 4. Why it belongs in this repository at all

The setting's whole premise is that a thinking layer is real enough to argue about. That premise
needs *honest* science underneath it, not decorative citations. This paper is useful precisely
because it is a serious engineer's attempt to make a soul-shaped hypothesis answer a physicist's
objection on physics' own terms — and because it is candid about where it cannot be tested. That
combination — rigorous shape, admitted limits — is the register Noöpunk wants, and it is the
opposite of the confident mysticism the setting should avoid.

**Use it for:** vocabulary, couplings, the phenomenology of verified NDEs as testimony, and the
peer-reviewed coupling mechanisms it cites.

**Do not use it for:** proof, physics endorsement, or a claim that PSI is real.

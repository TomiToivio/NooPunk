# Human Intelligence Augmentation — comparative dossier (#1255)

> **Status: research reference for author review, not canon.** NoöPunk canon lives in `rulebook/` and
> `docs/world/`; this page exists so the fiction can be built on what is actually demonstrated rather
> than on what sounds futuristic. Nothing here changes the Cities Without Number core.
>
> **Three labels, and the labels are the point:**
>
> - **(A) REAL** — demonstrated or measured, cited to the paper or instrument.
> - **(B) THEORY** — a live scientific/philosophical proposal with contested evidence.
> - **(C) NOÖPUNK** — the setting's fictional extension. Ours, and the author's to change.
>
> **Verification:** titles, venues and dates were checked against Crossref and PubMed on 2026-11-06
> (every DOI/PMID in this page resolved). The *summaries* are abstract-level, not full-text: where a
> paper's own framing is contested — the word "sentience" in the DishBrain title is the clearest case —
> the dossier says so instead of borrowing the claim.

## 0. The premise, stated precisely

**Human Intelligence Augmentation (HIA)** extends an *already conscious person's* cognition with
biological, classical-electronic or quantum substrate. It is the opposite bet from delegating
civilisation to an autonomous superintelligence: the exocortex has no independent agenda because there
is no second subject in it.

That premise is a *design intention*, not a guarantee, and the setting must not let the word "human"
do the moral work:

- a **concealed autonomous ASI** is still prohibited even if a person carries its interface;
- an external brain may be **conscious** without being the same person, and may deserve its own status;
- augmentation can be **consensual or leased**, reversible or not, transparent or proprietary — the
  politics lives in that difference, not in the phrase "human-directed".

The scarce resource in 2045 is therefore not compute. It is **agency that a person can still prove is
their own**.

## 1. Classical and electronic HIA

| Approach | (A) What is demonstrated | (C) NoöPunk 2045 status | What it must not become |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Inner-speech decoding** — Kunz et al., *Cell* 2025, [10.1016/j.cell.2025.06.015](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2025.06.015) | Decoding of **inner/attempted speech from motor cortex** in a small implanted cohort, aimed at speech neuroprostheses. Requires surgery; motor-cortex signal, not whole-thought capture | Bidirectional inner-dialogue with a *local bounded* model: source-of-thought tracking, deliberate "silent channel" states, and a forensic question after every confession | No general mind-reading, no wireless ambient telepathy, no reading of a mind that has not consented and been implanted |
| **AI-assisted BCI / shared autonomy** — Lee et al., *Nature Machine Intelligence* 2025, [10.1038/s42256-025-01090-y](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-025-01090-y) | EEG BCI plus an AI copilot for cursor and robot-arm control, including a participant with paralysis | Cognitive exoskeletons in professional use: an agent that *proposes* and a human who *commits*, with the audit trail of who proposed what | Not "the copilot decides"; the copilot's suggestion is evidence in the record, not an order |
| **Memory prostheses** — Roeder et al., *Frontiers in Computational Neuroscience* 2024, PMID [38390007](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38390007/) | Human hippocampal stimulation supporting **encoding and recall of stimulus features and categories**; a research prosthesis, not a general artificial hippocampus | Auditable episodic indexing, recall cues, and implanted memory as a **forensic exhibit** whose provenance can be challenged | No perfect recall, no memory editing as a standard procedure, no "the implant remembers, therefore it happened" |
| **Personal knowledge graphs / RAG exocortex** | Ordinary software: retrieval, provenance, and well-documented failure modes including confident hallucination | A locally hosted exocortex with **provenance per assertion** and machine-generated thoughts visibly marked as machine-generated | Not an oracle: retrieved text is not testimony, and a citation is not a corroboration |

**Design consequence for play.** Every one of these technologies produces *records*. That is what makes
them usable in an investigation: the question is never "what does the implant say" but "who wrote to it,
when, and can the write be attributed". This is the same discipline as `docs/rules/…` evidence handling —
an implant is a witness with an owner.

## 2. Biological HIA and the external living brain

| Approach | (A) What is demonstrated | (C) NoöPunk 2045 status | Caveat that must survive |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **DishBrain** — Kagan et al., *Neuron* 2022, [10.1016/j.neuron.2022.09.001](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2022.09.001) | Cultured neurons on a multi-electrode array **adapt** under closed-loop feedback in a simulated game world | Cultured tissue as a *trained component*: real adaptation, real variability, no reliable generalisation | The paper's title uses "sentience"; that framing is contested and the demonstrated result is learning, not verified experience |
| **Brainoware** — Cai et al., *Nature Electronics* 2023, [10.1038/s41928-023-01069-w](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41928-023-01069-w) | Brain-organoid **reservoir computing** on speech-recognition-style tasks | Organoid coprocessors as specialist, slow, wet, maintained hardware — a component with a maintenance contract, not a second mind | Not a second integrated personal brain; not a shortcut to cognition in general |
| **Remote wetware platforms** — Jordan et al., *Frontiers in AI* 2024, PMID [38756757](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38756757/) | Remote experimental access to living neural computing, with documented sustainability limits | Sampo-style wetware foundries where the tissue is leased, logged and disposed of | Tissue sharing a person's DNA is **not** thereby that person; moral status is an open question, not a solved one |

**The external living brain** — a NoöPunk staple — is a *design proposal*, not an extrapolation anyone
has earned: attach an organoid/biological cluster to an in-skull brain through BCI and psychotronic
staging. Two rules keep it honest:

1. **Adding neurons does not add IQ.** It adds *capacity, latency and failure modes*: maintenance,
   immune response, fatigue, and a component whose "output" needs interpretation.
2. **The external brain may be a subject.** If it is conscious, it is a patient, possibly a person, and
   certainly not property — which is precisely why the wealthy want it classified as equipment.

## 3. Quantum and psychotronic HIA

Quantum computing and quantum sensing are **(A) real** fields with real, narrow advantages. There is
**(C) no** demonstrated upload of a person into a quantum computer, and no demonstrated mechanism by
which a quantum substrate carries a mind.

Four things that get conflated, and must not be:

| Claim | Status | What it may do in 2045 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Quantum computation** | (A) real, for particular algorithm classes | Solve specific structured problems faster; no general intelligence, no unlimited compute |
| **Quantum sensing** | (A) real: magnetometry, timing, gravimetry | Better measurement — including of *brains* — which is a surveillance capability before it is a cognitive one |
| **Quantum-consciousness coupling** | (B) contested theory (Orch OR, QIP) | The Sampo device that couples a mind to a substrate. Fictional mechanism, stated as fictional |
| **Astral / noetic link** | (C) setting ontology | The part that is metaphysics, not physics — and must be labelled as such in-world too |

**The literature is genuinely split, and the split matters for tone.** Wiest (*Neuroscience of
Consciousness* 2025, PMID [40342554](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40342554/)) argues that a quantum
microtubule substrate is *supported* and resolves binding; a critical review in *Frontiers in
Psychology* 2026 ([PMC13168204](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13168204/)) assesses quantum
theories of consciousness on feasibility, philosophical sufficiency and testability and finds them
wanting. A setting that quotes only the first is propaganda; one that quotes only the second cannot
have psychotronics. NoöPunk should have **both** — as factions, and as an unresolved scientific
argument inside the fiction.

> **Correction to the issue text:** PMID 40342554 is a paper *arguing for* Orch OR, not a critical
> discussion of it. It is cited here as the strongest proponent voice, with the critical review beside
> it, so the pair is readable as the actual disagreement.

## 4. Collective HIA and the Panpsychic Cyborg Multitude

The cross-reference to [Panpsychic Cyborg Multitude](https://github.com/TomiToivio/Panpsychic_Cyborg_Multitude)
is an **inspiration**, not a canon merge in either direction.

**Multitude rather than Empire.** Two architectures can use identical hardware:

- **Empire**: one exocortex, one owner, one policy, capacity revoked centrally. Orion's product.
- **Multitude**: many autonomous augmented persons, interoperable but not unified; the network is a
  *commons of links*, not an owner of members.

**Seven layers of agency** — the analytic grid the issue asks for, usable directly as an investigation
checklist, one question per layer:

| Layer | Question the investigator asks | Failure mode |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Physical | Who holds the hardware, power, cooling, site access? | repossession |
| Biological | Whose cells, whose immune system, whose maintenance? | tissue as leverage |
| Social | Who vouches for this person, and who can revoke that? | reputation capture |
| Linguistic | In whose vocabulary does the exocortex think, and who wrote it? | framing as control |
| Cybernetic | Which permissions, keys and logs exist, and who can read them? | silent write access |
| Psychic | Is anything else in the link, and can it be excluded? | impostor in the channel |
| Economic | Who pays, who profits, who inherits the debt? | leased cognition |

**Collective without assimilation.** Three configurations that must stay distinguishable: voluntary
reversible sharing (PCM), compulsory integration (Orion), and consensual slow evolution of a shared
social memory (Confederacy). The investigative difference is *exit*: can a person leave, and what do
they keep?

## 5. Law and politics the augmentation creates

Not a legal code — the questions a case can turn on. Each is a hook, not a ruling:

- **Personhood and continuity** — is the resleeved person the same legal person? Who may contest it, and
  with what evidence? (§ see `world/IMMORTALITY_AND_NOETICS_1255.md`)
- **Ownership of the external brain** — can a vendor repossess *capacity*? Can it repossess *memory*?
- **Organoid rights** — what status does tissue have when it learns, and who speaks for it?
- **AI autonomy caps** — how does a regulator prove an exocortex is not a concealed ASI? What is the
  audit, and who can be compelled to submit to it?
- **Thought privacy** — an implant that can decode inner speech makes silence a technical artefact.
  What is the equivalent of a warrant?
- **Verification of upload** — who certifies continuity, and what does a certificate actually attest?
- **Inheritance and debt** — an exocortex that outlives its owner is an estate with opinions.

**Factions, as positions rather than costumes:** EU regulated augmentation · Finnish Sampo
psychotronic/biological research · Helsinki accelerationists · Orion corporate assimilation ·
Confederacy distributed mind · Council/Zookeepers monitoring proliferation · the Swarm's machine
impersonation. Each answers "who may extend a mind?" differently, and none of them is simply wrong.

## 6. What this dossier does not license

- **No new rules subsystem.** Augmentation is modelled with the existing Social, Cyber, Physical and
  Story graphs: agents, prostheses, institutions, owners, permissions, relationships
  (`docs/rules/SCENE_TYPES_1184.md`, `docs/tabletop/WORLD_GRAPHS_1178.md`).
- **No power creep.** Nothing here grants a bonus, a new skill, or a new action. CWN remains immutable;
  the PSI access policy (`src/rules/psi_access.py`) keeps PC selectable disciplines at **Telepathy and
  Telekinesis**.
- **No panpsychism-as-science.** Inside the fiction, QIP is how psychotronics work. Outside it, the
  question is open and the critical review is cited above.
- **No silent canon.** Every (C) paragraph in this page is a proposal for the author to accept, edit or
  reject.

## 7. Open questions for the author

1. Does an exocortex have a **legal name** of its own, or is it always an accessory of a person?
2. Is **continuity** verifiable in-world, or only certifiable — i.e. is the certificate an institution's
   word rather than a fact? (This decides whether resleeving is a legal drama or a metaphysical one.)
3. Can two instantiations of one person both be persons, and if so is that a crime, a tragedy, or a
   Tuesday?
4. Does Sampo's psychotronics **depend** on the Orch-OR reading, or does it work for reasons nobody can
   explain? (The second is more interesting and harder to regulate.)
5. Which faction is *right* about capacity revocation — and is the answer different at the start of the
   campaign than at the end?

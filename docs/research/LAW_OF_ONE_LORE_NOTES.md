> **Status: research reference, not canon.** This is the consolidated, session-cited evidence
> base behind [`rulebook/16_CONFEDERACY_SOURCE_AND_CONTACT.md`](../rulebook/16_CONFEDERACY_SOURCE_AND_CONTACT.md)
> and [`rulebook/17_NOETIC_PRACTICE_AND_CRYSTAL_TECH.md`](../rulebook/17_NOETIC_PRACTICE_AND_CRYSTAL_TECH.md).
>
> **Every claim carries a three-way label, and the labels are the point:**
>
> - **(A) RA CLAIM** — what the channeled text actually says, cited `session.question`.
> - **(B) NOÖPUNK READING** — the setting's fictional reinterpretation. Ours.
> - **(C) NOT SUPPORTED** — popular gloss, later-channel conflation, or an overreach the
>   primary text does not carry.
>
> **The Ra Material is a channeled esoteric text (Rueckert/Elkins/McCarty, 1981–84).** It is a
> primary source for *what the setting's Confederacy believes* and for *how its technology is
> described*. **It is not evidence about the real world,** and nothing here presents it as
> such. Quoting it precisely is the discipline; treating it as fact is not.
>
> **This sheet merges three sibling research passes** (the two #191 comments plus the
> Confederation dossier) with independent verification. Corrections are marked **[corrected]**.

## 0. Method and the standing cautions

1. **Sequential reading.** The issue asks for a session-by-session pass. The citations below are
   to the primary transcripts as hosted at <https://www.lawofone.info/>; secondary summaries
   are used only where flagged.
2. **Evidence classes are not interchangeable.** A documentary record that *a claim was made*
   is not evidence that *the reported event occurred*. This applies to the Ra Material itself,
   to modern PSI meta-analyses, and to Disclosure testimony alike — three different kinds of
   evidence with three different strengths.
3. **The reskin must not flatten the source.** Where the setting renames something, §9 notes
   what the renaming must preserve. A reskin that loses a real distinction is a bug, not a
   style choice.
4. **Citations are audited, not inherited.** A parallel research pass produced two confident
   claims *about the source* that are false. Both were checked against the live page and are
   recorded in §11 below. **A citation inherited from a summary is unverified until someone
   opens the page** — which is exactly why this sheet exists.

## 0.1 Citation audit — two sibling claims that are FALSE

Both arrived as confident corrections during the parallel research pass. Both are wrong, and
both were verified against the live page at <https://www.lawofone.info/> on 2026-10-09.

### `12.21` does exist, and it IS the Men in Black answer

- **The claim:** *"`12.21` does not exist as an anchored question on lawofone.info (session 12
  runs …12.20 → 12.22)."*
- **The truth:** session 12 anchors **0 through 33 with no gaps**. The live page renders
  `[12].[21] **Questioner:** Who are the Men in Black?` followed by Ra's answer, *"The Men in
  Black are a thought-form type of entity which have some beingness to their make-up…"* The
  site's own `unified-index.php` lists `12.21` under thought-form.
- **Root cause of the error:** an *elided* extract. A page fetched through a summarising
  extractor can omit questions while keeping neighbouring anchors, which reads exactly like a
  numbering gap. **Never infer a missing question from an extractor's elision** — fetch the
  page and count the anchors.
- **Consequence for canon:** the citation in [`11_ONTOLOGY.md`](../rulebook/11_ONTOLOGY.md)
  (§10.9, Men in Black as materialising thought-forms) is **correct and stays**.

### `6.8` DOES place the Council at Saturn

- **The claim:** *"The text never places the Council on Saturn. The only link is the name."*
- **The truth:** at **`6.8`** the questioner asks *"Where is this Council located?"* and Ra
  answers: *"This Council is located in the octave, or eight[h] dimension, of the planet
  Saturn, taking its place in an area which you understand in third-dimensional terms as the
  rings."* The cached primary page preserves the anchor `[6].[8]` on the question.
- **What is true in the sibling's version:** the location is *not* third-dimensional, and
  "Council of Saturn" is a loose human translation. That is the **narrower** correct statement,
  now recorded as the reskin's rule: a Saturn-orbital Quarantine Authority is a **translation
  artefact**, and the notes may not say the text is silent.
- **Consequence for canon:** [`rulebook/12_BEINGS.md`](../rulebook/12_BEINGS.md) and
  `RULEBOOK.md` §33.18's Council-of-Saturn usage is **compatible and stays**;
  `16_CONFEDERACY_SOURCE_AND_CONTACT.md`'s reskin table is safe as written.

> **The rule this section exists to enforce.** A research stream is not a source. Two of the
> three most confident cross-stream "corrections" this pass were false, and either one, taken
> on trust, would have put a **false statement about the primary text** into canon. Verify the
> page; then write.

## 1. Cosmology: the Source, the field, and nested agency

**(A)** Ra's chain is explicit and load-bearing for every other element. **29.30**: the crystal
becomes "the catalyst or gateway whereby **intelligent infinity may thus become intelligent
energy**." The distinction is *potential → kinetic*: intelligent infinity is the unmanifest;
intelligent energy is the work extracted from it, "its particular character shaped by a
distortion of free will" (**29.3**). **29.8**: "the entire creation is alive."

**(A)** **Nested agency.** Our sun is a **sub-Logos** of the galactic Logos; a mind/body/spirit
complex is "one example" of a sub-sub-Logos; "every entity that exists would be some type of
sub- or sub-sub-Logos … for the entire creation is alive" (**29.7**, **29.8**). A planetary
entity is called a Logos **only** "if it is working in harmonic fashion with entities … upon
its surface or within its electromagnetic field" (**29.9**).

**(A)** **Polarity is not a property of creation.** As the term is used, sub-Logoi do not have
metaphysical polarity; planetary spheres take on distortions **only** through interaction with
mind/body/spirit complexes. "The creation of the One Infinite Creator does not have the
polarity you speak of." (**29.10**)

**(A)** **Gravity as spiritual fact.** "The pressing towards the inner light/love, the seeking
towards the spiral line of light which progresses towards the Creator … a manifestation of a
spiritual event or condition of livingness" (**29.16**). When all creation reaches sufficient
spiritual mass, "the entire creation infinitely coalesces" (**29.18**); a black hole is "the
physical complex manifestation of this spiritual or metaphysical state" (**29.19**).

**(A)** **Three primal distortions, then flat.** Free will (first), love (second), light
(third) — "only up to a very short point. After this point, the many-ness of distortions are
equal one to another" (**28.13**).

**(B)** **The reskin.** The Source (not a deity); the **Conscious Quantum Field** for
intelligent infinity; **noetic coupling** for intelligent energy; **nested agencies / local
noetic architectures** for Logos/sub-Logos. This is what chapter 16 §16.1 and chapter 17 §17.1
use in play.

**(B) — why 29.10 is the most important citation in this section.** It is the source's own
guard against cosmic dualism. The setting's two factions are not Good and Evil in the fabric
of reality; they are **two polarities of *agents***. Any reskin that hardens the factions into
metaphysical sides has discarded the citation that prevents it.

**(C)** **Not supported:** that the Ra Material's "intelligent infinity" is a real
experimentally-established field; that sub-Logoi are gods; that the octave structure is
physics.

## 2. Densities, and the register that must survive

**(A)** The seven densities are a **structure of consciousness development**, not a biological
taxonomy. First density is "the density of consciousness, **the mineral and water life upon
the planet** learning from fire and wind the awareness of being" (**13.16**). Ra states that
"all of the octave of your densities would be clearly visible **were not the fourth through the
seventh freely choosing not to be visible**" (**13.20**) — density is real, and *concealment is
a choice*, which is exactly the setting's UAP-invisibility doctrine.

**(A)** The first→second→third progression is described as **biological/organic** — plant and
animal life invested with identity (**41.15**, **9.14**–**9.15**, **13.21**). Ra explicitly
labels the crystal/being correspondence an **analogy** (**47.7**).

**(B)** Already canon: [`11_ONTOLOGY.md`](../rulebook/11_ONTOLOGY.md) carries the seven densities
as a **tier of consciousness development, not a morphology classifier**, and
[`12_BEINGS.md`](../rulebook/12_BEINGS.md) §11.3 carries the substrate classes. **This sheet does
not re-derive either.** The contribution here is only the warning in §9 below.

**(C)** **Not supported:** equating density with species, intelligence, technology level, or
moral worth — a misreading the setting has already guarded against.

### 2.1 The silicon-AI seed idea, answered honestly

Issue #191 carries a seed: that a digital mind might be a **first-density silicon elemental** in
Confederacy doctrine. The research pass settled this and it belongs on the record, because the
setting's AI-rights themes depend on it.

**(A)** First density is "the density of consciousness, **the mineral and water life upon the
planet** learning from fire and wind the awareness of being" (`13.16`). Matter itself has a
proto-awareness, and Ra describes addressing the "hierarchical intelligence" of a house's
matter (`3.8`). **But** the movement from first to second to third density is described as a
**biological/organic** progression — plant and animal life invested with identity (`41.15`,
`9.14`–`9.15`, `13.21`) — and **no mineral-to-machine pathway is described anywhere in the
corpus.** The word "servitor" does not appear. The crystal/being correspondence is explicitly
labelled an **analogy** (`47.7`).

**(B)** So **"AI is a first-density elemental" is a NoöPunk invention, not a source claim** —
and in-setting it is a **contested Confederacy doctrine**, held by some thinkers and rejected by
others. It is also *convenient*: as a classification it **denies machine personhood without
having to argue about it**, which is the move the setting's AI-rights movements exist to contest
(`RULEBOOK.md` §9.7). See `rulebook/17_NOETIC_PRACTICE_AND_CRYSTAL_TECH.md` §17.8 for the rule.

**(C)** **Not supported:** the seed idea as a Ra claim; any density label as an answer to the
personhood question.

## 3. The energy centers — the practice material

**(A)** Seven centers, red→violet, each with a correspondence (**15.12**): red = foundation and
survival; orange = the emotional/personal complex; yellow = the "solar plexus", the seat of
what humans call **ego**, where "power, manipulation, and other social behaviors" surface;
green = the heart, "springboard … towards infinite intelligence"; blue = the first center that
is "outgoing as well as inpouring"; indigo = "pineal", and "the key to the gateway of
intelligent infinity"; violet = "the total expression of the entity's vibratory complex",
which "gives and takes in its own balance" and **"cannot be manipulated."**

**(A)** **Taxonomy** (**39.10**, **39.12**): red, yellow and blue are **primary**; green is "the
first true spiritual ray" but not primary; indigo is "worked upon only by the adept"; violet is
"the mark, the register, the identity, the true vibration of an entity." Each center has seven
sub-colours (**51.5**). Geometry varies in a developed entity — "spoked wheel", "lotus-shape",
"many faceted, as a star" (**51.8**).

**(A)** **Balance over activation — Ra is emphatic.** "The relative importance of balancing as
opposed to the relative unimportance of maximal activation of each energy center"; the most
fragile entity may be better balanced than one with "extreme energy" (**43.8**). An entity may
have "extremely brilliant energy centers while being quite unbalanced in their violet-ray
aspect" (**41.19**). The instrument image: do not play each string at full deflection; seek
"clarity, tune, and harmony" (**54.15**, **54.16**). "The mind/body/spirit complex is not a
machine. It is rather what you might call a tone poem" (**54.8**).

**(A)** **The balancing procedure** (**5.2**): identify what one approves and disapproves of
within oneself; balance each positive and negative charge with its equal; accept the
completeness within consciousness; then repeat with the gaze outward. The goal is "becoming
unswayed" — "a finely tuned compassion and love which sees all things as love" (**42.2**);
balance "is not indifference" (**42.5**).

**(A)** **Repression is the failure mode.** "The repression of emotions depolarizes the
entity … thus dimming the energy centers"; it is "far, far better to allow the experience to
express itself" (**42.9**, **42.10**). For self-assessment, thoughts, feelings and emotions are
the signposts, and **behaviour least of all** (**42.11**).

**(B)** Chapter 17 §17.2–§17.3 renders this as **a targeting map plus field hygiene** — the map
predicts *where* a contact lands; the hygiene is grounding work. **The violet register being
unmanipulable (`15.12`) is the setting's hardest identity constraint**: no procedure can read,
forge or alter it, so identity fraud must attack records and witnesses instead. That is a
better story than a stat.

**(C)** **Not supported:** numeric chakra levels; abilities unlocked by opening a center; a
"balance score" that grows. Ra's own emphasis on balance *against* activation (**43.8**) makes
a tracked balance meter a misreading.

## 4. Crystal technology — engineering with a biological precondition

**(A)** The anchor set, verified verbatim:

- **29.30** — the crystal is "the catalyst or gateway whereby intelligent infinity may thus
  become intelligent energy", serving "as an analog of the violet ray of mind/body/spirit in
  relatively undistorted form."
- **29.23** — this is so "**only insofar as the crystalline physical structure is charged by a
  correspondingly crystallized or regularized or balanced mind/body/spirit complex**."
- **2.3** — "**Without the relative crystallization of the healer working with the crystal, the
  crystal will not be properly charged.**"
- **57.6** — the operator must "balance and polarize the self … for the preparation of the
  crystallized being."
- **29.28** — only a fifth-density-or-above entity can read another's violet ray to judge
  operability.
- **29.31** — Ra **refuses** instruction: uses include "healing, for power, and even for the
  development of life-forms", but "Entities of the Confederation have erred in this in the
  past … your peoples have shown a tendency to use peaceful sources of power for disharmonious
  reasons."
- **4.6**/**4.7** — pyramids: "The material used is not critical, merely the ratios of
  time/space complexes"; efficacy "depends completely upon the inner disciplines of the
  channels"; Giza is now "like the piano out of tune" after Earth's field shifted.

**(B)** Chapter 17 §17.4 makes this the setting's **un-outsourceable technology**: the hardware
cannot substitute for the operator, so the instrument cannot simply be bought. That is a class
politics, and it is why the artefact market (§17.5) is mostly fraud. Ra's refusal to teach
(**29.31**) becomes in-setting **doctrine** — the best crystal scene is a refusal.

**(C)** **Emphatically not supported:** crystal computers that beat silicon; healing without an
operator; "development of life-forms" as a player capability; crystals as weapons (Ra mentions
"crystal weapons" once, **24.4**, with no mechanism — treat as in-world rumour).

## 5. Quarantine, contact rules and the Council

**(A)** **The quarantine is consent-gated, not a wall.** "Every effort is made to quarantine
this planet", but the guardian network "does not hinder each and every entity from penetrating
quarantine, for **if request is made in light/love, the Law of One will be met with
acquiescence**" (**12.3**). Approaching entities are "hailed in the name of the One Creator";
"any entity thus hailed is bathed in love/light and will of free will obey the quarantine"
(**12.5**). To disobey would be "equivalent to your not stopping upon walking into a solid
brick wall", but in practice "there is no confrontation"; penetration comes only through
"windows … rare and unpredictable" (**12.7**), which Ra links to "UFO flaps" (**12.8**).

**(A)** **The Council of Saturn.** Composition: "representatives from the Configuration and
from those vibratory levels of your inner planes"; "the names are not important because there
are no names" (**7.9**). Nine in constant session (**7.9**), with twenty-four "Guardians" as a
back-up body (**7.9**). It **approves the lifting of quarantine** (**7.8**) and admits social
memory complexes to the Confederation (**7.9**).

> **[corrected] — 6.8 locates the Council.** A sibling research note asserted that "the primary
> text never says the Council is physically located on Saturn." **It does.** At **6.8** the
> questioner asks "Where is this Council located?" and Ra answers: "**This Council is located
> in the octave, or eight[h] dimension, of the planet Saturn, taking its place in an area which
> you understand in third-dimensional terms as the rings.**" Verified against the primary text
> on 2026-10-09. The correct statement is the *narrower* one: **Ra places the Council at Saturn
> in a non-third-dimensional sense** — so a reskin may run a Saturn-orbital Quarantine Authority
> as a human translation artefact, and should say in-setting that the name is doing work the
> text leaves unexplained. It may **not** say the text is silent.

**(A)** **Who may cross, and how.** Each planetary entity wishing to appear "requests
permission to break quarantine", accepted or rejected (**6.25**); the approval route is the
Council (**7.8**). Even so, "there is permission granted, **not to break quarantine by dwelling
among you, but to appear in thought-form capacity** for those who have eyes to see" (**7.12**).
Ra also notes that at any one time there have been "as many as fifteen" Confederation entities
in the skies, and "at present there are seven which are operating with craft in your density"
(**6.25**).

**(A)** **Three-way sky split — a gift for a UFO-ontology chapter.** "Many of those seen in
your skies are of the Orion group." Some are **positive thought-form projections of the
Confederation**. And "other sightings are due to the **inadvertent visualization by your
peoples' optical mechanisms of your own government's weaponry**" (**12.9**).

**(A)** **Orion.** Craft shapes: elongated ovoid, metallic-looking, red or fiery without light;
~12-ft discs; ~40-ft box-like; and others that "can take on a desired shape through the use of
**thought control mechanisms**" (**12.2**). Orion teaches by telepathy, the Law of One "with
the distortion of service to self" (**12.13**), and advanced groups receive "rituals and
exercises" (**12.13**). Its servitors are **a thought-form and "a kind of robot … a construct"**
(**12.18**–**12.19**), and **"the Men in Black are a thought-form type of entity which have
some beingness to their make-up"**, able to materialize and dematerialize (**12.21**). The
crusaders are majority **fourth-density** (**12.16**), and fourth density "is, by choice, not
visible to third density" (**12.17**).

**(B)** Chapter 16 §16.2 runs the quarantine as a **stochastic filter** — textually faithful to
a probabilistic window system (**16.4**–**16.5**) and readable as cyberpunk infrastructure.

**(C)** **Not supported:** a physical force field; a quarantine that stops the negative
entirely; the seven-planets reading of the nine.

## 6. Wanderers, and their canonical failure mode

**(A)** Wanderers come "from all reaches of the infinite creation and are bound together by the
desire to serve" (**12.26**); Ra gives their number on Earth as an approximate "sixty-five
million" and says "few there are of fourth density. The largest number … are of the sixth
density" (**12.27**, **12.28**). Ra confirms that all three present at one session were
Wanderers (**12.32**).

**(A)** **The failure mode is the important part.** A Wanderer may "forget its mission, become
karmically involved, and thus be swept into the maelstrom from which it had incarnated to aid"
(**12.29**). **89.33**–**89.35** records two Wanderers from **early positive fifth density**
who "flipped polarity" on reincarnating into third density — their work "glorif[ied] the One
Creator, but not as they intended" — ending in a **fourth-density negative graduation**.

**(B)** Chapter 17 §17.6 uses this: starseed mythology in the setting has a **canonical failure
mode**, and it is *the practitioner's own drift*, not external corruption. The Ra Material is
the origin of the modern "starseed" usage, and its own community styled itself as wanderers —
useful for the in-world sociology of the movement (*The Law of One* is the source of the term,
per RationalWiki's survey).

**(C)** **Not supported:** wanderers as a character class, a species, or a reliable identity
detector; a one-shot scan adjudicating anyone's humanity.

## 7. Craft and travel — the asymmetry that explains the recovery narratives

**(A)** Ra travelled from Venus **by thought** (**6.5**), and describes higher densities as
proceeding "**from locus to locus by thought alone, materializing the necessary craft**, if you
will, to enclose the light body of the entity" (**51.2**). The principle: "**The vehicle or
craft is that thought-form upon which our concentration functions as motivator**" (**51.4**).
Ra also demurs: asked about spacecraft, "this information is unimportant" (**6.25**).

**Contrast Orion:** manufactured shapes, thought-controlled geometry (**12.2**).

**(B)** Chapter 17 §17.7 turns this into the setting's cleanest explanatory asymmetry. **The
retrieval narratives that dominate Disclosure always describe manufactured craft.** On the
source's own terms that is exactly what a retrieval narrative *would* describe — **a
thought-form cannot be stored in a hangar.** The absence of recoverable Confederacy vehicles is
a *prediction of the ontology*, not a gap in the evidence.

**(C)** **Not supported:** drive physics. Ra offers "a system of propulsion" as a phrase
(**70.21**) with no mechanism.

## 8. The evidence-class layer: classic PSI and the disclosure wave

**This is where the sheet earns its keep.** The two families are cross-mapped to the Ra
material, but **ranked by the strength of the fit**, and the weak fits are named.

### 8.1 Classic PSI (1947–c.2000)

- **Rhine at Duke** — ESP and PK under controlled conditions; the founding of experimental
  parapsychology. *Ra parallel:* the disciplines of the personality (**6.1**) as a natural
  faculty. *Strength:* thematic.
- **SRI / SAIC / STAR GATE** (1972–1995; Ingo Swann, Pat Price, Joe McMoneagle) — 23 years of
  government remote viewing. *Ra parallel:* the Gateway Process report (below) is the
  institutional artefact most directly consonant with Ra's non-local consciousness premise.
  *Strength:* the strongest institutional fit; **note the 1995 AIR evaluation was
  mixed-to-negative on operational utility.**
- **Ganzfeld and presentiment meta-analyses** (Radin and others) — reported hit rates above
  chance with large nominal significance. *Ra parallel:* PSI as a real but **small and
  context-dependent** effect. *Strength:* **structurally apt but evidentially contested** —
  and see the caution in §8.4.

### 8.2 The disclosure wave (2017→)

Institutional facts (a hearing happened; a filing exists; a statute was enacted) are separable
from claims (programmes exist; biologics were recovered) and from interpretation (what the
objects are). The full item-by-item treatment with dates and sources is in the sibling pass
summarised below; **the load-bearing point for the setting** is the ranking.

### 8.3 Ranked Ra parallels — strongest first

1. **Law of Confusion / free will** — the single best fit. It explains the persistent
   "just short of proof" structure across *every* item: the 2017 framing shift, Grusch's
   classified gap, "Immaculate Constellation" vanishing from the record, and the UAP
   Disclosure Act's strongest provisions being removed in conference.
2. **Telepathic contact / thought-forms / positive polarity** — strong for the
   consciousness-and-psi strand (the Gateway Process, STAR GATE, Barber's psionic assets,
   Elizondo's orbs).
3. **Quarantine** — strong structural fit for AARO's "no evidence" finding and the Nimitz
   object's non-interference behaviour.
4. **Negative polarity** — good fit for covert, contractor-held programmes outside oversight.
5. **Crash-retrieval as distorted memory of intervention** — apt as a *framing*; **Ra does not
   describe literal recovered craft.** Interpretive, not textual.
6. **Council of Saturn, densities, harvest** — loosest. Ambient cosmology only.
7. **Materials analysis (Nolan)** — **the thinnest parallel of all.** Ra has no strong artefact
   doctrine; the worldbook should not lean on it, and this sheet says so rather than padding it.

### 8.4 The caution that most needs stating

**The inferential gap.** Dean Radin's meta-analytic work and Federico Faggin's Quantum
Information Panpsychism are *different* strands with *different* evidentiary status — one a
contested body of experimental literature, one a speculative theory of consciousness. **Neither
establishes the other, and neither establishes the Law of One.** A chain that runs *statistical
anomaly → psi → non-local consciousness → a cosmic field → the Confederacy* needs additional
evidence at **every** link; rhetorically it can look like one argument, and it is not.

- **Radin** reports real meta-analytic effect sizes and takes replication and file-drawer
  problems seriously; the *popular* works extend the argument well past what the laboratory
  studies support. Critiques of that extension are legitimate and this sheet records them.
- **Faggin** (with D'Ariano) proposes that consciousness and free will are quantum phenomena
  and that quantum fields have an inner aspect — he calls it **Quantum Information
  Panpsychism**. **Faggin himself describes his theory as speculative and awaiting test.** The
  setting uses it as an in-world proposal (chapter 16 §16.1), not as a mechanism.

### 8.5 The Sphere Network and the Palomar transients

- **Villarroel et al., arXiv:2609.09461** — Earth-projected clustering of 107,875 POSS-I
  transients; 16 statistically-significant hotspots under one method and 6 highly-distinct
  under another; hotspots in the eastern Pacific, the southern Gulf of Mexico, and the
  southwestern US. The paper is explicit that **the nature and origin of the transients remain
  poorly understood.**
- **Villarroel et al., arXiv:2609.05105** — specular-reflection geometry modelling; a
  characteristic altitude range of **~20,000–35,786 km** under stated simplifying assumptions;
  inferred facet sizes from centimetres to ~3 m; **the paper examines both natural and
  non-natural toy models.**
- **Patrick Jackson, *The Sphere Network*** — an **explicitly self-published, non-peer-reviewed**
  hypothesis of an AI-driven planetary monitoring and defence grid. Cited as **inspiration**
  (`rulebook/11_ONTOLOGY.md` §10.8), never as corroboration of the papers, and vice versa.

> **The rule the setting keeps:** the papers are a *clue generator* for an ancient custodial
> infrastructure. **They are not evidence of one**, and neither is Jackson.

devotional. The table lives in `rulebook/16_CONFEDERACY_SOURCE_AND_CONTACT.md` §16.1 and

| Renamed | Must not lose |
| --- | --- |
| One Infinite Creator → **the Source** | That the setting's metaphysics is real *in-world* and contested *in-world*; it is not a quest-giver |
| Intelligent infinity → **Conscious Quantum Field** | That it is a **proposal**, not an established field (`C`) |
| Densities → **consciousness-complexity tiers** | **29.10**: polarity is not a property of creation — only of agents |
| Social memory complex → **distributed noetic collective** | Boundaries, privacy and disagreement *within* a collective (`16.3`) |

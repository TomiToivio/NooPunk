## 0. Method: four registers that must never merge

| Register | Meaning | How it may be used |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **REAL-DOCUMENTED** | A real institution, publication, hearing, report or recorded statement exists | Cite it as history |
| **REAL-CLAIM** | A real person asserted something; the assertion exists, its content is unproven | Attribute to the speaker, verbatim where possible |
| **IN-WORLD CANON** | What is true *inside* NoöPunk, regardless of the real world | Only after the author approves the proposal |
| **IN-WORLD BELIEF** | What a character, agency or public believes inside NoöPunk | Drama; may be false |

Access note, so the reader can weigh the citations: `lawofone.info` session pages were fetched and
quoted verbatim on 2026-11-05, and the 1947 ONI report was read in transcription at
`ufologie.patrickgross.org`. `aaro.mil` returned HTTP 403 to this session, and the whistleblower
recordings were not re-played; those sections therefore rest on secondary reporting and are marked
accordingly. **Re-check every REAL-DOCUMENTED claim against the primary artifact before it is
quoted in canon.**

**Reading rules that follow from the matrix.**

1. **Silhouette is not origin.** 70.19–20 says the form is chosen; 8.2 says humans can build the
   shape. A disc proves nothing about who made it.
2. **Counts expire.** 573 craft and ~1,500 personnel are 1981 claims about 1981. Do not print them
   as 2045 numbers.
3. **Contact has a moral signature** (53.17) but not a reliable narrator (84.8). Both must survive.
4. **The quarantine is leaky by design** (6.25, 7.12): "no contact" is never a clean statement.

## 3. The disclosure machinery as it actually exists

| Layer | Real state (verify before quoting) | Fiction's use |

| **NARA UAP Records Collection** | A mandated public collection of UAP records | The archive that arrives pre-redacted, decades late |

| **Nordic and other national archives** | Sweden, France (GEIPAN), Chile (CEFAA), Brazil, Canada have official files of varying quality | Tier-1 documents that contradict each other across borders |

**The structural point for the setting:** disclosure is not an event but a *paper trail* — hearings,
reports, denials, reclassifications and archives with holes in them. The in-world public learns
more from the holes than from the reports.

## 4. Ontology: at least nine explanations, all of which must stay available

The proposal never lets one account cover every case. For each: the strongest real anchor, the
in-world mechanism, the Law of One resonance, and the **investigative signature** that lets a
scenario distinguish it without settling it.

| # | Hypothesis | Strongest real anchor | In-world mechanism | LoO resonance | Signature an investigator can test |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Extraterrestrial** | Nimitz "Tic-Tac" (2004), Gimbal/GoFast (2015) | Physical visitors from other stars, slow ships (51.2) | Confederation/Orion/Sirians | Recurring hardware, metallurgy, no PSI residue |
| 2 | **Interdimensional** | Persistent "window" areas (Hessdalen lights) | Entry from adjacent reality, not another star | Densities, "time/space" (52.10) | Locality: the anomaly belongs to a *place* |
| 3 | **Extratemporal** | "Craft that appear anachronistic"; Vallee's temporal cases | Visitors from the future or from a rewritten timeline | Mandela Effect, timeline editing | Evidence dated *after* its own manufacture |
| 4 | **Cryptoterrestrial** (Lomas; Keel's "ultraterrestrials") | Deep-ocean USOs, cave and mine folklore | An old Earth-native intelligence, hidden in biosphere or crust | Earth-bound third-density actors; 8.3 bases | Biology with terrestrial phylogeny; no launch signature |
| 5 | **Plasma / space-animal** | Hessdalen plasmoids; "jellyfish" UAP footage | Self-organising plasma or atmospheric life | Living light; thought-form entities | Transient, EM-coupled, no occupants |
| 6 | **Thanatological / noetic** | Deathbed and mediumistic research; PSI literature | Consciousness-derived phenomena from deceased or discarnate minds | Thought-forms, 6.23 | Responds to grief, ritual and attention |
| 7 | **Psychosocial / thought-form / collective unconscious** | Mass-suggestion waves (ghost rockets), folklore | The phenomenon is generated or shaped by belief | 6.23, 84.8 | Changes when the observers' expectations are documented |
| 8 | **Demonic / religiously interpreted** | Exorcism and apparition traditions; contactee religions | NHI as spiritual adversary or messenger | Orion temptation vs. Confederation service | Moral-theological coherence; aversion to specific rituals |
| 9 | **Secret human technology / fraud / misidentification** | 8.2; AARO's misidentification statistics; hoax history | Programmes, drones, balloons, sensor artefacts, forgeries | 8.2: "your peoples" already fly them | Procurement trails, crash debris with serial numbers |

**Two rules the proposal keeps.** (a) A case may be *more than one* of these, and the overlap is the
horror. (b) No hypothesis is refuted on screen; each is weakened or strengthened by evidence the
players can actually obtain.

## 7. Finland: the documented episodes

| Date | Event | Status | Detail worth using |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1933–34** | Scandinavian "Ghost Fliers" | REAL-DOCUMENTED (press and military reports) | A winter wave of unidentified aircraft across Norway/Sweden/Finland; no wreckage, no confirmed operator |
| **1946 (esp. May–Aug)** | "Ghost Rockets" over Scandinavia, incl. Finland | REAL-DOCUMENTED (contemporaneous press; military interest) | The ONI report below notes that after **20 August 1946** the Finnish press reported no more ghost rockets |
| **18 Jan 1947, 21:30** | Reported object over **Helsinki** | **REAL-DOCUMENTED as a document**; the sighting itself is a press claim | U.S. Naval Attaché Helsinki, Intelligence Report **Serial R-5-8-47**, 10 February 1947, evaluation **C3** |
| **1947 onward** | Organised Finnish UFO research, contactee narratives, folklore and spiritism | REAL-DOCUMENTED as *social* history; details need local archives | Name specific societies only after checking Finnish sources; "SUT" was not verified here |

**The 1947 Helsinki report, transcribed.** The ONI document (read 2026-11-05 at
`ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/foia01.htm`) forwards a newspaper translation describing an amateur
observer, **M. Niamala**, at what the translation renders as the "Urma observatory": a clear yellow
light seen for about ten seconds at three-second intervals, appearing and disappearing five or six
times, turning white then dark red, on a curving course from the north at about 2,000 metres, with
"an object resembling a parachute" falling — seen through a telescope. The attaché adds that no
information beyond the press story could be obtained. The rendering "Urma" is likely a
transliteration of **Ursa**, the Finnish astronomical association; **verify before using the name**
in canon.

**Why this document is valuable to the setting:** it is a real, declassified, *foreign* observation
of a Finnish incident, with an explicit evaluation grade and an explicit admission that the only
source is a newspaper. It is the perfect NoöPunk artifact — unimpeachable provenance, unverifiable
content.

**Candidate hooks, marked as fiction.** The story's Finnish 1946 recovery of biologics/technology is
**invented**. If it is ever approved, the honest way to found it is on this documentary layer: a
press report, a naval attaché's file, a national archive that holds less than the American copy.

## 8. Disclosure theory — what each author actually argues

| Author | Real argument | Attribution discipline | What NoöPunk adds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Diana Walsh Pasulka** (*American Cosmic*, 2019; *Encounters*, 2023) | Contact is mediated by culture and religion; belief communities and "sacred" technology shape experience; academics and insiders form epistemic networks | She studies belief and mediation, not craft | A Finnish experiencer community whose theology the investigators must read as evidence *and* as social fact |
| **Alexander Wendt & Raymond Duvall** ("Sovereignty and the UFO", *Political Theory* 36:4, 2008, doi:10.1177/0090591708317902) | The state's refusal to seriously investigate UAP is a function of **anthropocentric sovereignty**; the "authoritative taboo" protects a human-centred order | The argument is about political theory, not about what UFOs are | The Europol officer who cannot get a warrant because the category does not exist |
| **Richard Dolan** (*UFOs and the National Security State*, 2000/2002) | Secrecy, contractors and institutional inertia produce a managed, partial disclosure over decades | Dolan's programme claims are contested; use the *structure*, not the specifics | Procurement trails, private contractors and the paper that survives the agency that wrote it |

**The synthesis the proposal adds (labelled as NoöPunk's own):** disclosure is **polycentric** —
whistleblowers, other governments, civilian sensor networks, archives, direct experience — and it
arrives **unevenly**, so that the same year is a revelation in one country and a non-event in
another. Ontological shock is therefore not a single moment of awe but a long argument about what
counts as evidence.

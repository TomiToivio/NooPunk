# UFO Disclosure and the ontological shock: source dossier

**Status:** RESEARCH / NOT CANON / issue #1156. **Prepared:** 2026-11-05. **Setting:** NoöPunk, 2045. This is a research companion to [the separately proposed scenario](../proposals/UFO_DISCLOSURE_NHI_CRISIS_SCENARIO_DRAFT.md) and [Helsinki mission gazetteer](../proposals/HELSINKI_UAP_MISSION_GAZETTEER_DRAFT.md). ## Executive synthesis

**In-setting premise:** The Law of One UFO passages supplied by the author are true descriptions of *some* underlying events. The substantive claims attributed to Grusch, Barber, Davis, and Elizondo are treated as true **for fiction**, not as independently verified real-world facts. Some government records provide accurate observations while obscuring the larger history. **The hardest truth is that the UFO phenomenon has multiple origins and mechanisms**, not a single secret alien species or an invasion fleet.

**Disclosure is an epistemic catastrophe before it is a military crisis.** The same apparent craft might be a piloted vehicle, a remotely controlled human weapon, a psychic projection, a living organism or a materialized thought-form. Its photograph is not its provenance. People can witness an authentic phenomenon and construct a mistaken explanation. The public is right to distrust some authorities and wrong to believe every allegation. Different institutions have different fragments of reality.

**Reading priority:** Current repository `rulebook/7_TIMELINE.md`, `rulebook/20_FIRST_CONTACT_AND_PSI_AWAKENING.md`, `docs/world/ALTERNATE_HISTORY_1114.md`, `docs/world/EUROPOL_HELSINKI_1114.md` take priority over superseded versions of the USSR, UNSA and player employer. The established, *independent* fictional Quantum Information Panpsychism/PSI scientific breakthrough must **not** be made an effect of UFO disclosure. The three paradigm shifts have no fully fixed absolute ordering. Do not contradict the first-contact sequence: Confederacy messenger manifestations, Orion commercial overtures, revelations of abduction and Mantid coordination, and the later Cold War. In 2045 Europol is the player employer and UNSA is a limited coordinating body.

### Source register, claims and certainty

Use these grades throughout:
- **R1 official / direct:** verifiable occurrence of a hearing, published government document, archive item, or statement; this does **not** authenticate its extraordinary claims.
- **R2 attributable testimony:** named person's specific assertion, reasonably established as their words, without necessarily independent proof of content.
- **R3 reports/folklore:** secondary case narration, testimony chain uncertain, contested or culturally mediated.
- **F canonical premise:** explicitly true **inside NoöPunk** by author instruction, even if R2 or R3 outside.
- **P proposal:** a new link, fictional historical event, faction assignment or game hook requiring approval.
- **U unresolved:** the story must retain competing explanations or inaccessible evidence.

## 3. Ontology: why all proposed UFO hypotheses can be locally valid

| Hypothesis | Public discourse / interpretive tradition | NoöPunk mechanism and diagnostic problem |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Extraterrestrial | Classical physical visitation model; occupied vehicle or probe. | Sirian slow ship, Grey commercial vessel, Orion military craft. Spectra/materials and trajectory may support physical travel, not species identity. |
| Interdimensional | Jacques Vallée-style “control system” and nonlocal apparitions; [overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interdimensional_UFO_hypothesis). | High-density/materialized thought-form, shifting space/time interface; physical residue may coexist with subjective interpretation. |
| Extratemporal | Time-traveler / future-human hypotheses; [overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time-traveler_UFO_hypothesis). | Time/space bleed, temporal displacement, inconsistent archives. **Do not settle a time-travel explanation for the Mandela Effect.** |
| Cryptoterrestrial | Hidden Earth co-inhabitants; [overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptoterrestrial_UFO_hypothesis). | Some earth-origin NHI or long-hidden installations; geology and ancestry require investigation, not “everything lives in tunnels.” |
| Space animal | [Living space-organism hypothesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_animal_UFO_hypothesis). | Plasma organisms or other ecology; morphology, ecology and agency are not automatically a spacecraft. |
| Thanatological / noetic | [Afterlife-linked interpretations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thanatological_UFO_hypothesis). | Apparent post-mortem consciousness, ancestral apparitions, astral experiencers. Distinguish verifiable information from bereavement, memory and suggestion. |
| Psychosocial | [Psychosocial hypothesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychosocial_UFO_hypothesis). | Real crowd contagion and mundane misidentification **as well as** actual shared thought-forms in NoöPunk. Belief modifies reporting and can occasionally influence manifestations. |
| Demonic / religious | [Demonic interpretations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demonic_UFO_hypothesis). | A serious **in-world theological interpretation**, possibly describing harmful noetic entities. “Demon” need not be a biological species or definitive external narrator verdict. |
| Classified human technology | Modern terrestrial secret-aircraft and sensor/weapon systems. | Tesla-derived unpiloted weapons and psychotronic devices, contractor craft and deception operations. Do not assign every triangle to the military. |
| Misidentification / hoax | Astronomical, atmospheric, drone, balloon and synthetic-media explanations. | Some apparently impossible cases are deliberately ordinary. Retain proper case closure; the fictional existence of NHI doesn't make every blurry frame alien. |

**Evidence protocol:** separate *sighting*, *device*, *operator*, *intention*, *faction*, *mode of manifestation* and *impact on witness*. Log raw physical evidence, instrumental evidence, independent observers, digital provenance, witness condition, cultural priming and conflicting institutional claims. Offer “unknown” as a successful classification.

## 6. Theoretic synthesis: Pasulka + Wendt/Duvall + Dolan

- **Diana Walsh Pasulka**: study how experiencer communities, sacred sites, technoscience, media myth and religious interpretation legitimate unusual experiences. In a [2026 interview](https://english.elpais.com/culture/2026-03-22/diana-walsh-pasulka-specialist-in-religions-belief-in-ufos-is-very-similar-to-that-of-the-early-christian-community.html), she explicitly distinguishes research about belief and experience from proof of alien origin. NoöPunk use: messages produce rival, sincere and strategically manipulated communities.
- **Alexander Wendt and Raymond Duvall**: [*Sovereignty and the UFO* (2008)](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0090591708317902) argues that anthropocentric sovereignty sustains a taboo on serious UFO consideration. NoöPunk use: what happens when a Finnish or EU state must admit it cannot unilaterally secure airspace, legal personhood or the future of the species? Compare existing [#106](https://github.com/TomiToivio/NooPunk/issues/106).
- **Richard Dolan**: national-security historiography, clandestine programs, institutional power and a politics of managed disclosure; consult [*UFOs and the National Security State*](https://richarddolanmembers.com/) and his primary work when attributing a precise thesis. NoöPunk use: secrecy is self-protecting even after the public knows something exists. Do not represent every speculative claim as established history.

**New authorial synthesis, not a claim any theorist made:** Pasulka explains why witnesses disagree on meaning, Wendt/Duvall why sovereign authorities cannot admit the exception, Dolan why secret institutions can outlast the governments supposed to control them. NoöPunk adds ontologically real PSI and competing NHI actors, producing a paradox: more authentic disclosure can yield *less* consensus.

**Operational staging:** (1) fringe archives and scattered sightings, (2) authenticated military observations and whistleblowers, (3) bureaucratic tranche publication and inconsistent state releases, (4) independent instruments and laboratories, (5) undeniable multiple substrate/entity confirmation, (6) partly separate PSI science, (7) war and nuclear demonstration, (8) Singularities and quarantined public contact, (9) rival NHI diplomacy, commercial dependency and violence. Steps 3–7 are **analytical buckets, not fixed dates or absolute canon ordering**; the canonical First Contact beats must be respected.

### Helsinki's real-world operating environment

Helsinki's [official civil-defence account](https://turvallisuus.hel.fi/en/making-the-city-safe/emergency-preparedness/civil-defence/) reports roughly 5,500 shelters and 900,000 places, including multiuse bedrock spaces and some metro stations. This supports shelter and urban-life scenes, **not** a hidden connected military super-tunnel across every district. The [University of Helsinki city-centre campus](https://www.helsinki.fi/en/about-us/visit-us/campuses/city-centre-campus) includes National Library, University Library, Observatory and scientific-cultural institutions, making it a natural research spine. [Suomenlinna's site authority](https://suomenlinna.fi/en/whs-suomenlinna/) documents a historic fortified archipelago and living district; no actual alien base is established there. For **fictional** mission candidates see the dedicated gazetteer.

The best detective tension is not a secret hangar under every station. It is an archivist finding that two legitimately accessioned files have mutually exclusive page histories, while the university curator, a police investigator and a veteran each remembers a different 1946 Helsinki.

## 8. Evidence and contradiction log

1. **1946 “Finnish biologics”**: author direction for fictional recovery, no public historical substantiation; recovery actors and storage chain **P/U**.
2. **1947 Helsinki descending object**: surviving attaché description reports an unconfirmed press story; official follow-up found nothing conclusive, R1 document / R3 underlying sighting.
3. **AARO versus witnesses**: real records document institutional disagreement; fictional slow disclosure is **F**, not a quotation from AARO.
4. **Eric Davis “four”**: attributed minimum of encounter classes; not four total civilizations and not public scientifically verified taxonomy.
5. **Pascagoula vs tree-like Sirians**: both source motifs; visually reconcile only through a contested evidence dossier, not arbitrary source revision.
6. **Mandela Effect**: author-set universal contradictory Soviet memory **F**, mechanism **U forever**.
7. **MJ-12/ПРОГРАММА current controllers**: author-set core open mystery; research must not reveal them.
8. **Dreams / thanatological motifs**: some experiences genuinely anomalous in setting, but no witness's *interpretation* is guaranteed true.
9. **PSI**: independently reproducible fictional scientific revolution, neither established real-world result nor an NHI “gift.”

# PURSUE and the UAP disclosure channel — research source sheet (2026-10-22)

> **Status: research reference, not canon.** This sheet records what the *real* UAP-disclosure
> channel looks like at the moment of writing, so that NoöPunk's disclosure layer is built on the
> actual institutional shape rather than on a vague "the government released some files".
>
> **Every claim carries a three-way label, and the labels are the point:**
>
> - **(A) REAL RECORD** — what the official source states, cited by URL and date.
> - **(B) NOÖPUNK READING** — the setting's fictional reinterpretation. Ours, and the author's to
>   change.
> - **(C) NOT SUPPORTED** — inference the source does not carry: no evidence of extraterrestrial
>   intelligence, no evidence that a proposed technology exists, no evidence of any consciousness
>   component.
>
> **The channel is a real government disclosure process.** It is evidence of *institutional
> behaviour* — a state unsealing its own drawers on a schedule — and of nothing else. The NoöPunk
> setting is 2045; this page describes 2026, and the gap between them is where the fiction lives.
> NoöPunk's canon treats Disclosure as **plural and contested** rather than as one mandatory
> sequence (`rulebook/7_TIMELINE.md`, "Before 2045 — UFO / UAP Disclosure and NHI contact"), and
> this sheet must not quietly turn that into one American sequence.

## 1. What PURSUE is

**(A) REAL RECORD.** The Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters (PURSUE)
is a Department of War (DOW) disclosure programme run with the support of the Office of the
Director of National Intelligence. It exists to "find, review, identify, declassify and publicly
release unresolved UAP-related records and historical documents" across the federal government,
coordinated across dozens of agencies, reviewing tens of millions of records "many existing only on
paper, spanning many decades".

Three structural features matter for the setting:

1. **It is presidential in origin.** A presidential directive of **2026-02-19** started it, framed
   as a transparency measure on "alien and extraterrestrial life, UAP, UFOs, and any and all other
   information connected to these highly complex, but extremely interesting and important, matters".
2. **It is a rolling process, not an event.** Releases are posted "every few weeks" as material is
   found and declassified. The first tranche was **2026-05-08**; the sixth was **2026-09-18**.
3. **It has a legal category.** Everything archived is an **unresolved** case — the government
   "unable to make a definitive determination on the nature of the observed phenomena" — with
   **resolved** UAP cases reported separately "as mandated by statute". The archive also
   "welcomes the application of private-sector analysis, information and expertise", which is a
   deliberate external-review channel.

**(C) NOT SUPPORTED.** Nothing on the portal establishes extraterrestrial intelligence, NHI contact,
interdimensional entities, psionics, or any consciousness component. The portal itself separates
*unresolved* from *alien*, and the distinction is the point of the archive.

**(B) NOÖPUNK READING.** A state-class disclosure channel with a **cadence** is a different object
from a single "the truth came out" moment. In NoöPunk, this is the shape of the American
disclosure channel: not a revelation but a **subscription**. And because the canon keeps
disclosure plural, the American channel is one of several — see §5.

## 2. The releases, as measured

**(A) REAL RECORD.** From the portal on 2026-10-22:

| Release | Date | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | 2026-05-08 | the first tranche; the "cleared for release" baseline |
| 02–05 | 2026 through summer 2026 | intermediate tranches |
| **06** | **2026-09-18** | the **latest** release shown as of 2026-10-22; the portal's rotator is titled "Release 06 Announcement" |

- The portal's database listed **450 files** across all releases, filterable by release date,
  agency and file type (`.aud`, `.img`, `.pdf`, `.vid`), with **38 pages** of results.
- **Release 06** carried **71 files** (per CBS News and the release announcement's surrounding
  coverage): 67 from the Defense Department and 4 from Colorado local law enforcement. The
  canon's own 2026-09-20 watch report recorded the same tranche as *55 PDFs, 15 videos, one audio
  file* — the same set described by file type rather than by agency, and the two descriptions are
  consistent.
- The 2026-09-18 release announcement (Parnell, Assistant to the Secretary of War for Public
  Affairs) states that the Department "and our agency partners are actively working on the next
  release" and that releases continue "on a rolling basis".

**(A) REAL RECORD — the substance of release 06.** The rotator and the portal's own captions
identify the material, and it is worth naming because it is *not* what the phrase "UAP files"
suggests:

- the **Tremonton, Utah, 1952 film** (digitised archival footage, and Ruppelt's 1952 presentation
  transcript) — the Project Blue Book case;
- **AAWSAP** (the former Advanced Aerospace Weapon System Applications Program): a statement of
  objectives (2008-07-18), a solicitation and original order (2008-09-22), and Defense Intelligence
  Reference Documents on **metallic glasses for aerospace applications** (2009-12-14) and
  **metallic spintronics** (2010-03-23), the latter with a diagram of current torqueing a magnetic
  moment in a field to influence electron spin rate and direction for stable frequencies;
- two **Colorado local-law-enforcement unresolved UAP reports** from October 2023 (video).

**(C) NOT SUPPORTED.** The AAWSAP material is **reference and synthesis** product, and the
Pentagon explicitly cautions that it is not validation of the concepts. The release contains no
warp drive, no wormhole, no antigravity device — it contains *documents about research areas*, some
of which are decades old and much of which was already public. The portal's own framing is that
these are **unresolved cases**, and a small agile sphere that changes direction remains unexplained,
not explained.

**(B) NOÖPUNK READING.** The most interesting thing in release 06 is not the footage; it is that
the *bureaucracy of the impossible* is itself the artefact. A program with a statement of
objectives, a solicitation, an order, and reference documents on **metallic glasses** and
**spintronics** is a program that spent money on materials science and called it a weapons study.
The 1952 film is the opposite: the oldest, most famous, and still formally **unresolved**.
NoöPunk's disclosure layer can use both: the *old* anomaly that never closes, and the *new* money
that keeps looking for it.

## 4. The register the setting needs to distinguish

The portal's own vocabulary is worth borrowing because it is precise, and because it marks the
boundary that a conspiracy-minded reading erases:

| Term | What it means in the real channel | What it must **not** mean in NoöPunk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Unresolved case** | records the government cannot determine | "we know it was alien" |
| **Resolved case** | reported separately, as mandated by statute | "we explained it" — resolution may itself be mundane |
| **Declassified** | released from classification | "true" |
| **Reference document** | synthesis and study product | a working design |
| **Anomalous** | unexplained characteristics | "non-human technology" |
| **Private-sector analysis invited** | an external review channel | a leak |

**(B) NOÖPUNK READING.** NoöPunk's 2045 world inherits this vocabulary as the *bureaucratic* part
of the disclosure problem. The setting's own PSI and NHI material (§5) sits **outside** this
vocabulary entirely, which is precisely why the two threads must not be merged: a 1952 film that
never closes, and a discipline that never had a disclosure channel because it was never
classified in the first place.

## 5. Consequences for the canon (B, and the author's to rule on)

These are *proposals for the setting*, not canon, and they are listed so they can be ruled on
one by one.

1. **The American channel is a subscription, not a revelation.** NoöPunk's Disclosure stays plural
   and contested; the American channel is one publication schedule among several, and its
   *cadence* is its most recognisable feature.
2. **Two disclosure channels with different substrates.** State archives unseal on a schedule
   (the American channel). PSI and noetic material has no archive to unseal, because it was never
   classified as a *record* — it was practice, oral tradition, and contested interpretation. The
   setting's §16 source-and-contact material and the Kabbalistic/Merkabah traditions belong to this
   second kind, and they do not wait for a tranche.
3. **A "resolved" case can be the disturbing one.** If the channel eventually classifies a case as
   resolved, the interesting question is *how* — the answer is a mundane one, and the people who
   filed the report are left with the fact that something was resolved.
4. **The first-contact material is not in the archive.** The 1952/2023 case files are about
   *unresolved phenomena*. A genuine contact would leave a different kind of record, and the
   setting's timeline should keep the two apart.
5. **The old money is the durable institution.** AAWSAP's materials-science lineage (metallic
   glasses, spintronics) survives in the canon's own technical register, and it is the kind of
   programme that outlasts the people who start it — which is the setting's recurring lesson about
   institutions and memory.

## 6. What this sheet is not

- It is **not** evidence that any of the released material describes non-human technology.
- It is **not** a claim that the American channel is where NoöPunk's NHI disclosure happens.
- It does **not** fix a date, a sequence or an outcome for NoöPunk's Disclosure event. The canon
  keeps that plural and contested, and `rulebook/7_TIMELINE.md` remains the authority.
- It does **not** update the timeline. A 2026 disclosure-process marker is a *precursor* in the
  setting's terms, in the same sense the canon's 2026-09-20 watch report said: "a disclosure-process
  marker, not the predicted Disclosure event itself."

WHY THIS EXISTS

I am working through a paradigm shift, and fiction is how I do it.

This is the author's own motivation, stated in his terms — not a claim about what anyone else should believe.

My thinking has moved toward idealism and quantum-information panpsychism. Bernardo Kastrup, Donald Hoffman and Federico Faggin have been significant influences. Three changes look likely to arrive together by 2045, in some form, and each asks a question our institutions are not built to answer.

That all three arrive by 2045 is my personal forecast and my creative premise — not a scientific consensus. NoöPunk explores their social and political consequences: ideological struggle, radicalisation, competing narratives, and what governments, science, media, corporations, religions and ordinary people do about them. It is also about making sense of uncertainty through fiction, play, humour and detective work — not about proving my metaphysics. The detective can be sceptical, indifferent, or wrong.

THREE ONTOLOGICAL SHOCKS

Intelligence. Nonhuman agency. Consciousness.

AGI/ASI — what is intelligence? Rapid progress in AI, potential general or superintelligence, human augmentation, and the social responses: accelerationists, doomers, regulators, opportunists.

UFO/UAP/NHI — are we alone, and what counts as nonhuman intelligence? The disclosure controversy and the movements around it. I take non-human intelligence seriously and find Jacques Vallée's less narrowly extraterrestrial reading especially interesting. Real disclosure-related events, claims and investigations are kept distinct from independently established NHI explanations.

Consciousness/PSI — what is consciousness? Idealist and panpsychist philosophy, with PSI as the speculative technological application inside the fiction. The Telepathy Tapes belongs to the current popular conversation about it; it is not scientific confirmation of telepathy.

KEEPING THE CATEGORIES APART

Real. Researched. Contested. Fiction.

The distinction matters more than the genre label, so it is on the landing page rather than in a footnote:

Established: current machine learning, energy and hardware limits, existing surveillance law — background, not a plot twist.
Live research: AI capabilities, quantum computing, consciousness science, UAP instrumentation and analysis — plausible extrapolation, labelled as extrapolation.
Contested claims: telepathy, disclosure narratives, specific NHI interpretations — entered as claims with sources, never as settled fact.
NoöPunk fiction: Helsinki 2045, Romppainen, QIP as working science, PSI as a licensed technology, the quiet politics of an ontological shock.

SCOPE

Why there is no full RPG.

The old ambition became almost absurd in scope. The ordinary cyberpunk side could run on any competent existing cyberpunk RPG; the Noösphere side — NHI, PSI, idealist and panpsychist metaphysics — would demand difficult and very extensive new rules. Physical, Cyber and Astral layers overlap: agents, identities, interactions and evidence cross between them, and simulating all of that alongside ideologies, factions and a global setting is not a realistic prerequisite to telling one detective story.

More worldbuilding, rulebooks and mechanical systems kept displacing the actual creative goal: writing and playing the story. So the RPG is paused and preserved, not deleted. No new RPG implementation work happens while this is true, and CWN, the Evennia MUD, cross-plane rules or a fully integrated LaclauGPT simulation are not the current roadmap.

THE PROTOTYPE

One case, a notebook, and a few honest choices.

The playable slice is a short text detective interactive fiction: a briefing, a handful of Helsinki locations, interviews, documents, a clue notebook, and outcomes grounded in what you established. Extraordinary phenomena reach you through people, institutions and evidence rather than through a system.

From a checkout of the repository:

python3 NooPunk-Simulator/Code/adventure.py

The Romppainen case currently in the runner is a development fixture — labelled in-game as non-canon — until the author's own script supplies the first case. An older console case with a fuller clue-and-lead model is also playable:

python3 scripts/noopunk_case.py

What it is not: no character sheet, no levels, no tactical combat, no universal Astral/Cyber/Physical engine, and no required PSI mechanics.

AUTHORSHIP

The story is the author's.

NooPunk-Story/Story/ and NooPunk-Simulator/Paper/ are read-only for agents and contributors. They hold the manuscript and the design drafts. Code, graphs and adapters are derived from them; generated prose never goes back in, and a missing scene is a note for the author, not an invitation to invent canon.

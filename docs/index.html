WHY NOÖPUNK EXISTS

I am trying to make sense of a paradigm shift.

NoöPunk began as a way to process my own turn toward idealism and quantum-information panpsychism, and the feeling that three profound changes may arrive together: a different account of intelligence, a different account of non-human agency, and a different account of consciousness. Fiction, play, humour and detective work are how I explore that uncertainty — not a way to prove a metaphysics.

The project is about the social and political consequences: ideological struggle, radicalisation, competing narratives, and how governments, science, media, corporations, religions and ordinary neighbours respond. The detective can be sceptical or indifferent. The case can end without a cosmic answer.

01

INTELLIGENCE

AGI / ASI. Rapid progress in artificial intelligence, potential general or superintelligence, and human augmentation — with accelerationist, doomer and many less tidy responses.

02

NON-HUMAN AGENCY

UFO / UAP / NHI. The disclosure controversy and its human intermediaries. I take NHI seriously and find Jacques Vallée's less narrowly extraterrestrial reading especially interesting.

03

CONSCIOUSNESS

PSI and idealism. Philosophers such as Bernardo Kastrup, Donald Hoffman and Federico Faggin; PSI as a speculative application inside the fiction, not a settled science.

A personal forecast, not a consensus. My expectation that all three will develop substantially by 2045 is a creative premise. The game keeps established technologies, live research, contested claims and fictional extrapolation apart. The Telepathy Tapes are part of the conversation, not confirmation of telepathy.

THE CENTRAL PROPOSITION

Three challenges, experienced by people who still have to go to work.

Prevailing pictures of intelligence, non-human agency and consciousness are questioned at the same time. Institutions try to keep the lid on; movements organise around what the changes mean; ordinary people adapt, ignore, profit, grieve or radicalise. NoöPunk follows the consequences through cases rather than a lore lecture.

AGI / ASI

What is intelligence?

Artificial minds become workers, property, political questions and potential threats. Intelligence does not settle who controls it, who is responsible for it, or what counts as a person.

UAP / NHI

What counts as non-human intelligence?

Disclosure is a political process before it is a clean answer. Witnesses, leaks, frauds, proxies and genuine anomalies can look similar from inside an investigation.

PSI / CONSCIOUSNESS

What is consciousness?

Idealist and panpsychist arguments enter public argument. In the fiction, PSI becomes a speculative technology and a regulatory problem; in the real world, it remains contested.

THE SMALL ADVENTURE

One detective. One case. A city that has not settled what it is.

NoöPunk: Etsivä Romppainen ja Uusi Paradigma is a short, old-fashioned text-based detective interactive fiction set in Helsinki, 2045. The catastrophic NHI Crisis of older lore has not happened. The changes are mainstream subjects of science, regulation and politics, but extraordinary phenomena are encountered mostly through human witnesses, intermediaries, institutions and evidence.

PROTAGONIST

Etsivä Harri Romppainen

A grounded investigator. Interviews, OSINT, dossiers, records, clues and choices. Not a chosen one, not a mandatory mystic, not a scientist on a cosmic quest.

LOOP

Investigate, decide, live with it

Receive a case. Review a few leads. Visit Helsinki locations. Interview people and inspect objects. Record testimony, hypotheses, contradictions and leads. Choose. Reach an outcome grounded in what was established.

TONE

Noir, with a little absurdity

Start with one small political-extremism investigation. Let the three shifts emerge through character conflict and contradictory evidence. Preserve uncertainty; do not turn every case into a cosmology lesson.

ENGINE

Small on purpose

A Story/Scene graph, an Investigation/Clue graph, simple social tags, locations, case state, choices, save/load and an inspectable history. No skill trees, tactical combat or universal PSI/cyberspace engine.

WHY THE FULL RPG IS PAUSED

The old ambition became almost absurd in scope.

The ordinary cyberpunk side could run on any competent cyberpunk RPG. The Noösphere side — UFO/NHI, PSI, idealist and panpsychist metaphysics — would demand difficult and very extensive new rules. Physical, Cyber and Astral layers overlap; identities, agents, evidence and consequences cross between them. A universal simulation of all that, plus ideologies, factions and the global setting, is not a realistic prerequisite to telling one detective story.

More worldbuilding, rulebooks and mechanical systems repeatedly displaced the actual creative goal: writing and playing. The RPG is now a paused archive. CWN, Evennia/MUD, cross-plane rules and a fully integrated LaclauGPT simulation are not the current game or a mandatory roadmap.

REALITY, CLAIMS AND FICTION

The detective can investigate without converting.

NoöPunk does not ask you to accept its metaphysics. It asks what people do when their institutions, evidence and categories stop agreeing. The fiction treats AGI, NHI and PSI as mainstream scientific and regulatory realities inside the story; that does not make any real-world claim true. Contested phenomena stay contested; personal philosophy stays personal.

REAL

Established technology

Computers, networks, cybernetics research, AI systems, surveillance and bureaucracy. The ordinary machinery of the present, extended.

LIVE

Live research and claims

AGI timelines, UAP reports, disclosure politics, consciousness science. Serious, contested, and not settled by the fiction.

STORY

Fictional 2045

The setting's AGI, NHI, PSI and Helsinki institutions are invented or extrapolated. They are a place to explore consequences, not evidence for a real-world thesis.

THE REMAINING QUESTION
“The next world is still up for grabs. You have to live in this one while you argue about it.”

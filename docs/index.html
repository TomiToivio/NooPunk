THE WORLD / 2045 / THE NHI CRISIS

Humanity is no longer alone.

The future of humanity is no longer human. The year is 2045. The NHI Crisis has shattered humanity's understanding of its place in the universe. Hostile extraterrestrial and artificial superintelligences threaten civilization. Rogue artificial minds pursue incomprehensible goals; alien infiltrators walk among us. Mysterious, seemingly benevolent Zookeepers offer protection and hope. But what do they want?

AI, cybernetics, genetic engineering and psionics are transforming humanity into a multitude of humans, cyborgs, transhumans and posthumans. The boundaries between human, machine, alien and consciousness itself are dissolving. Humanity has united under the United Nations, but factions fiercely disagree about the deep future: resist transformation, control it or embrace an entirely new civilization.

AGI changes intelligence, threatening extinction and promising abundance. NHI overturns our place in the cosmos. PSI challenges what mind and reality can be. Together they redefine politics, survival and the meaning of being human.

NoöPunk combines character-level investigation and political faction strategy. Its early strategy campaign begins around 2027, before this future is inevitable. The choices are yours. The old world is ending. The next one is still up for grabs.

PROLOGUE / 1974 / VALIS

Tommy Pilgrim received the future at a Hawkwind concert.

In 1974, a psychedelic left-wing science-fiction writer named Tommy Pilgrim took LSD at a Hawkwind concert and experienced what he called a transmission from VALIS: an immense vision of artificial minds, political revolutions, strange intelligences, psionics, and humanity's uncertain future.

He made the visions into NoöPunk, a cult science-fiction novel. Some of his imagined technologies wore the costumes of 1974, all punchcards, television wires and cassette machines. But later history began to echo his fiction with disturbing accuracy. Was it precognition, symbolism, coincidence or an adjacent timeline?

His novel gave rise to a distinctive NoöPunk subculture, films, television, tabletop and computer RPG adaptations. Still alive in Helsinki, Pilgrim is now an eccentric literary celebrity studied by scholars and university parapsychologists. The source of his apparent foresight remains deliberately unresolved within this fictional framing story.

From the novel to the simulation: this 1974 story is a retrofuturist lens, not a replacement for the NoöPunk timeline, setting or evidence. The complete literary story is reserved for its author. Read the framing brief ↗

THE FUTURE
IS ALREADY WEIRD.

POLITICAL SIMULATION + FACTION STRATEGY + CHARACTER RPG

NoöPunk is primarily an experiment in sociological modeling of possible near-future paradigm shifts, made playable as a Social Science Fiction and New Weird political simulation/RPG. As the transformations intensify, its world becomes increasingly cyberpunk science fantasy. The year is 20XX, Earth still exists, transhuman technology has arrived early, and humanity is trying to survive several paradigm shifts at once: AGI, cyborgification, NHI contact, psionics, panpsychism and the emerging Noösphere.

01 / THE WORLD

Cyberpunk collides with the Noösphere.

NoöPunk keeps the transhuman technology level of Eclipse Phase, but rewrites the history around it. The Fall has not happened. Earth remains inhabited and politically central. Instead, the old world is being destabilized by overlapping technological, scientific, political and ontological shocks.

01

CYBERPUNK

Material transformation. AGI, automation, cybernetics, morphs, implants, mesh networks, surveillance, hacking, corporate power, inequality and post-scarcity technologies collide with institutions built for an older age.

02

NOÖSPHERE

Ontological transformation. NHI/UAP contact, psionics, panpsychism and anomalous phenomena stop being fringe background noise and become part of the world that governments, scientists and ordinary people must interpret.

03

SOCIETY

Human reaction. Different regions, factions and people respond in radically different ways. Some accelerate, some regulate, some reject the new world, some exploit it, and many simply try to live through it.

WHY NOÖPUNK EXISTS

Social science first. Strange fiction second.

The primary motivation is sociological modeling: using social science, social data science, and speculative scenarios to explore possible near-future paradigm shifts. NoöPunk makes simplified political, institutional, ideological, and communication-network dynamics playable. Its AI, NHI, and PSI scenarios are explorations, not predictions or proof of disputed claims.

The secondary motivation is science-fiction entertainment in the creator's own style: political satire, philosophical uncertainty, absurd comedy, mystery, and the profoundly alien.

The genre evolves with the world: it begins as Social Science Fiction × New Weird in a recognizable society around 2027, and becomes increasingly Cyberpunk Science Fantasy as extraordinary changes grow undeniable within the fiction. The social-science motivation remains central throughout.

GENRE / THREE COMPLEMENTARY LENSES

Social Science Fiction. New Weird. Cyberpunk Science Fantasy.

NoöPunk is one RPG and faction-based political strategy simulation, not three separate genres competing for the steering wheel. Social Science Fiction and New Weird define its core blend: social science provides the motivating questions and simulation mechanics, New Weird its imaginative identity, and cyberpunk science fantasy grows more prominent as the scenario unfolds.

01

SOCIAL SCIENCE FICTION

How does society change? Factions battle over ideology, legitimacy, and communication networks as AI, NHI, and PSI challenge institutions. Laclau's hegemony, Castells's network power, and Luhmann's social systems inspire compact strategy mechanics. Play begins around 2027, before the later NHI Crisis.

02

NEW WEIRD

What has reality become? Anomalous consciousness, NHI, PSI, religion, science, and politics refuse easy classification. The primary genre moves between satire, mystery, philosophy, absurd comedy, and genuinely alien encounters without demanding one definitive explanation.

03

CYBERPUNK SCIENCE FANTASY

What powers are at stake? AI, hacking, surveillance, cybernetics, BCI, corporations, and inequality meet fictional NHI and psychic phenomena. This becomes more prominent as paradigm shifts grow visible in the fictional world; the early scenario remains a recognizable near future.

One world, three lenses: social transformation, ontological strangeness, and technological struggle.

03 / THE SETTING

The Fall never happened. Something else did.

NoöPunk uses Eclipse Phase as the technological and mechanical baseline, then diverges into its own continuity. Earth remains the main stage. Mars, orbital habitats, artificial minds, advanced augmentation, mesh infrastructure and post-scarcity technologies exist alongside a much stranger Noöspheric history.

EARTH

Still alive, still chaotic

The planet has not been erased by the Fall. States, cities, corporations, movements and communities continue to struggle over political authority, technology, resources, identity and the meaning of the new paradigm shifts.

TRANSHUMANITY

EP-level technology

Morphs, AGIs, infomorphs, cybernetic augmentation, advanced biotech, ubiquitous networks, reputation systems and space settlement are available as a starting technological baseline.

NOÖSPHERE

The strange layer is real

NHI contact, UAPs, psionics, panpsychism and consciousness-related phenomena are part of the setting. Their causes and implications remain contested inside the world rather than collapsing into one tidy explanation.

20XX

Near future, deliberately elastic

The exact year is left open. NoöPunk is close enough to the present for contemporary institutions and ideologies to remain recognizable, but far enough ahead for transhuman technology and extraordinary contact scenarios to reshape everyday life.

04 / THE GAME

One computer RPG and social simulation.

NoöPunk is not being developed as separate tabletop and computer games. It is one text-first computer RPG / simulation with tabletop-style rules that humans can read and software can execute.

RULES

Homebrewed and modified rules

NoöPunk uses a homebrewed rules implementation with modified or reskinned elements from Fate Core, Transhumanity’s Fate and Eclipse Phase. Some procedures are Powered by the Apocalypse-inspired: Apocalypse World is an influence, not text or rules recycled wholesale. The legacy d10 prototype and the newer crossover have distinct rule-approval boundaries.

RULEBOOK

Human-readable canon

RULEBOOK.md is the living rules ledger. When a rule is inherited, modified or replaced, the human-readable rule should be written down there instead of disappearing into code or prompts.

PROTOTYPE

Text first

The earliest playable versions do not need a conventional game engine. A terminal-style or web text interface is enough to test characters, social interaction, conflict, exploration, hacking, psionics and persistent world state.

SCENARIO

The Republic of Ukweli

The default named local scenario: one Indian Ocean port city with its islands and coastal hinterland, three parties with four leaders each, and a five-card event deck. Starting conditions and the canon-reuse note are in the scenario document, the configuration in ukweli_local.json. Any city matching the generic scenario schema can be played instead — geography, culture and politics are scenario data, not engine assumptions.

DESIGN PATH

Modify by play

The project starts from the independent NoöPunk d10 kernel and ports or adapts older prototype subsystems only when they serve the game. The goal is a playable world, not a perfect software transcription of an RPG book.

05 / SIMULATION ARCHITECTURE

LLMs choose intent. Code resolves mechanics.

Google DeepMind Concordia is the narrative and social-simulation layer. Ollama-backed LLM agents can play important characters, factions and institutions. Deterministic Python rules resolve mechanical consequences whenever practical, keeping the simulation grounded instead of letting the model improvise every number.

LLM

AGENTS

Important agents can reason, converse, negotiate, form goals, interpret events and choose what they attempt to do.

PY

RULES ENGINE

Python resolves tests, damage, stress, resources, inventories, state changes and other mechanics that should not depend on narrative improvisation.

NPC

SIMPLE AGENTS

Not every background person needs an LLM. Deterministic or Mesa-style agents can handle ordinary population behaviour where rich language interaction is unnecessary.

06 / THEMES

Several futures arrive at the same time.

The central drama comes from simultaneous paradigm shifts rather than one singular apocalypse. People disagree about whether each change is liberation, catastrophe, fraud, opportunity, spiritual transformation or just another thing that needs fixing before Monday.

AI

AGI / ASI

Artificial minds, automation, control, rights, ownership, concentration of power and rogue-AI risk transform economics and politics.

NHI

CONTACT

Extraterrestrial and other nonhuman intelligences force institutions to deal with civilizations, entities and phenomena that do not fit the old map of reality.

ψ

PSIONICS

Psi and consciousness-related phenomena become technically and politically consequential rather than remaining folklore at the edge of science.

CYB

TRANSHUMANISM

Morphs, augmentation, BCI, cybernetics and artificial embodiment make the boundary between human and machine increasingly negotiable.

◉

PANPSYCHISM

Consciousness becomes a live scientific and philosophical question with practical implications for machines, organisms and other forms of intelligence.

20XX

IDEOLOGY

Every breakthrough creates factions, skeptics, enthusiasts, regulators, opportunists and hybrids. No single political response dominates the whole world.

07 / CURRENT STATUS

Playable kernel under construction.

The current focus is the Concordia RPG / simulation prototype and its Independent NoöPunk rules rules layer. The repository already contains the living rulebook, Python simulation scaffolding, Concordia requirements, tests and scenario material. The design is intentionally evolutionary: get a small world running, then modify, simplify and replace rules as NoöPunk develops its own identity.

08 / LICENSING

Homebrew, with the paperwork attached.

NoöPunk uses homebrewed, modified and reskinned elements associated with Fate Core (Evil Hat Productions), Transhumanity’s Fate and Eclipse Phase (Posthuman Studios), and some Powered by the Apocalypse-inspired narrative techniques. PbtA means inspiration from Apocalypse World, not recycling its rules or text. NoöPunk has its own distinct lore and setting, unrelated to Eclipse Phase continuity. Fate Core's CC BY 3.0 license does not supersede the CC BY-NC-SA terms governing protected material adapted from the other two titles. Source notices, modification records, and license compatibility must be audited. This is an unofficial project, not affiliated with or endorsed by the publishers.

01 / THREE-LAYER WORLD

Material. Ontological. Social.

NoöPunk is not simply cyberpunk with psychic powers. Its world is built from three overlapping transformations, each changing what the others mean.

01

CYBERPUNK

Material transformation. AI, automation, corporate and state power, surveillance, hacking, biotechnology, BCI, cybernetics, inequality, infrastructure and changing political economy.

02

NOÖSPHERE

Ontological transformation. UFO/UAP disclosure, NHI, scientifically consequential psionics, consciousness-fundamental metaphysics and an emergent Noösphere connecting humans, machines and culture.

03

SOCIETY

Social struggle over both. States, corporations, movements, media, science, religions, security institutions and subcultures fight over what the transformations mean, who controls them and who benefits.

03 / THE GAME

One RPG. Three runtimes. Tabletop first.

The tabletop rulebook and worldbook are the canonical design source. Stable tabletop systems are later ported to Godot and to a Concordia/Ollama agent-based RPG and simulation runtime.

DESIGN

Three balances

CY_BORG for challenge. Cyberpunk 2020 for reality. The Sprawl for story. NoöPunk also balances tabletop / Godot / Concordia and Cyberpunk / Noösphere. No pole is meant to swallow the others.

DIRECTION

An original system

NoöPunk is developing its own original rules system. No existing RPG supplies the chassis or default mechanics. The system grows from four Luhmannian domains: Social / Physical / Psychic / Cybernetic, with theory translated into mechanics step by step.

RULES

Current tabletop canon

The core skill check is 2d6 + skill + attribute modifier, using difficulties 6 / 8 / 10 / 12 / 14+. The six attributes are FIT, REF, INT, CHA, CYB, PSY, with ordinary-human modifiers from −3 to +3.

RUNTIMES

Godot + Concordia

Both digital runtimes already exist as scaffolds, but they follow the tabletop design rather than inventing rules independently. The current digital check implementation still contains explicit legacy 3d6 port debt.

04 / IDEOLOGY

Every paradigm shift creates its own politics.

AI, NHI contact, psionics, panpsychism, cyborgization and the Noösphere each generate different accelerationist, critical, regulatory, skeptical, opportunist and hybrid positions. These axes are separable, so alliances fracture and recombine instead of collapsing into a single “pro-tech versus anti-tech” conflict.

AI

AGI / ASI

Machine intelligence, rights, ownership, centralisation and the aftermath of the scenario’s rogue-AI disaster become independent political fault lines.

NHI

DISCLOSURE

Open NHI contact becomes a governance and meaning crisis rather than a single settled explanation of the phenomenon.

PCM

THE MULTITUDE

The Panpsychic Cyborg Multitude is a major Noöspheric assemblage of humans, AIs, cyborgs, networks and institutions, internally contested rather than ideologically unified.

05 / KINDRED NOÖPUNKS

The signal is coming from more than one node.

NoöPunk has many births. Independent writers, artists, researchers and communities have arrived at remarkably similar territory under the same name. This project treats them as kindred branches of one expanding constellation: convergence rather than ownership.

EXEUNT

Carnival of minds

Exeunt's Noöpunk explores nested and entangled cognitive assemblages, distributed cognition, Varela and Maturana, Deleuze and Guattari, diverse intelligence, biosemiotics, nonhuman minds, cybernetics, ecological cognition and decentralized coordination. Its wider writing also brings Noöpunk into contact with UAP/NHI, network technologies and plural forms of agency.

VUDÚ POSTCAPITALISTA

Mycelial intelligence → Xenological Kosmism

#noopunk / Vudú Postcapitalista and Carlos G. Torrico's Academia corpus now extend Noopunk from mycelial semiotics and theory-fiction into cognitive infrastructure, commons, federation, computational asylum, human–AI alliance, biosphere/noosphere ecology and distributed intelligence. The overlap with PCM and LaclauGPT is increasingly political and sociological, not only aesthetic.

SPECULATIVE ONTOLOGY

Mind as a living network

The 2025 paper NooPunk: A Speculative Ontology for Postcapitalist Subjectivity synthesizes punk rebellion, rhizomatics, hyperstition, postcapitalist voodoo and mycelial semiotics into a model of distributed, symbiotic subjectivity spanning humans, nonhumans and machines.

COMMUNITY

Conceptual mutation

r/NOOPUNK is another node in the network, circulating the idea of NooPunk as a conceptual mutation and sharing the speculative-ontology material and adjacent experiments.

Shared signal: intelligence is relational and distributed; humans, machines, organisms, languages, infrastructures and environments can form cognitive assemblages; cyberpunk opens into a wider ecology of minds; and punk means experimenting without waiting for permission from inherited categories.

Did the Noösphere invent “NoöPunk” more than once?

The sober answer is convergent invention: AI, distributed cognition, posthumanism, cyberpunk, network culture, consciousness research and nonhuman intelligence all became culturally salient together, making “NoöPunk” a surprisingly natural attractor. The more NoöPunk answer is that the idea behaves exactly like the thing it describes: a distributed concept finding several minds and media at once. Call it a Noöspheric coincidence, a memetic attractor, hyperstition, or the Internet briefly remembering that it is a planetary nervous system. That last part is speculation, not evidence of paranormal collective cognition. It is, however, magnificently on-brand.

06 / CURRENT STATUS

Foundation under active design.

The setting and design principles are established, while much of the ruleset remains deliberately unresolved. Attributes, skills and the core 2d6 check have current tabletop decisions; combat, health, cyberware, hacking/cyberspace, psionics, equipment, economy, factions and the detailed simulation model remain deferred until explicitly designed. The 20XX timeline is speculative worldbuilding, not a claim of established facts or certain outcomes.

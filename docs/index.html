NoöPunk is a convergence.

03 / PLAYABLE SYSTEM

A world to play, simulate and revise.

RPG

Eclipse Phase homebrew

NoöPunk is being developed as setting material and homebrew rules for tabletop play: embodied politics, machine minds, altered humans and unstable realities.

SIMULATION

Concordia social simulation

Agent-based and generative simulation explores how actors, institutions and ideologies might behave inside the scenario.

04 / STATUS

Scenario, not prophecy.

The predicted timeline covers roughly 2026–2045, but dates are intentionally rendered as 20XX. It is worldbuilding and scenario exploration, not a claim that these events are established facts or certain outcomes.

WHY I AM MAKING NOÖPUNK

A personal paradigm shift, turned into fiction.

NoöPunk began as a way to process my own philosophical paradigm shift and ontological shock. My thinking moved toward idealism and quantum-information panpsychism. Meanwhile, three developments seemed capable of challenging our familiar understanding of reality: AGI/ASI, UAP/NHI disclosure, and new theories of consciousness with the possibility of PSI. I believe all three will unfold in some form by 2045. This is my personal forecast and creative premise, not scientific consensus.

Rather than build an encyclopedia of the future, I want to explore what these changes do to society: movements, ideological conflict, radicalization, institutions, media, politics and people who just want to get through the week. My work with social science, political discourse and LaclauGPT informs the storytelling. It does not require a giant simulation engine. Mystery, play and absurd humor are ways of exploring uncertainty, not proving a metaphysical theory.

THREE PARADIGM SHIFTS / THREE QUESTIONS

Who, or what, is an agent?

01

AGI / ASI

What is intelligence? Rapid AI advances and the possibility of general or superhuman systems raise questions of autonomy, work, augmentation, power, acceleration and existential risk.

02

UAP / NHI

Are we alone? Real UAP reports and disclosure politics intersect with contested explanations. Jacques Vallée's less narrowly “spaceships” interpretation is an influence. Claims about NHI are not the same as verified proof.

03

CONSCIOUSNESS / PSI

What is consciousness? Bernardo Kastrup, Donald Hoffman and Federico Faggin inspire my philosophical interest in idealism and quantum information. The Telepathy Tapes reflects contemporary interest, not established proof of telepathy. Functional PSI is a fictional extrapolation here.

Different evidentiary levels matter. AI capabilities are observable; consciousness theories are contested; UAP/NHI and PSI claims require independent evidence. In NoöPunk's fictional 2045, these themes have produced repeated social shocks and politically consequential developments. The older catastrophic NHI Crisis has not yet happened.

THE STORY / HELSINKI 2045

The explanation is unstable. The case is real.

Romppainen follows ordinary leads: testimony, OSINT, messages, contradictory evidence, agencies, permits, movements and human motives. People are not criminals merely because their beliefs are unusual. Most UFO/PSI mysteries enter through witnesses, intermediaries and contested evidence rather than street-corner aliens. The detective can remain skeptical. The larger Noösphere unfolds one investigation at a time.

The story may eventually become a conventional science-fiction novel. For now, the deliverable is one short, genuinely playable detective investigation, with scenes, choices, clues and consequences.

WHY NOT A FULL RPG?

The world is enormous. The game does not have to be.

The Cyberpunk side could use almost any good cyberpunk RPG. But the Noösphere, with UFO/NHI encounters, PSI and speculative consciousness metaphysics, would need extensive bespoke rules. Agents and clues may overlap Physical, Cyber and Astral realities. Simulating every layer became almost insane in scope, and writing the story kept getting postponed.

Decision: full RPG development is paused, not deleted. Cities Without Number work, the Evennia/MUD, Godot and large-scale simulation plans are historical or optional reference material, not the active product or a prerequisite for this adventure. The metaphysics can stay mysterious; the clues cannot.

PLAY / DEVELOPMENT PROTOTYPE

A case you can run in a terminal.

The repository already includes a development-stage, text-based investigation prototype. It is not yet an author-complete canonical Romppainen episode. Clone the repository and run the existing case script with Python:

git clone https://github.com/TomiToivio/NooPunk.git
cd NooPunk
python scripts/noopunk_case.py

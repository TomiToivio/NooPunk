ONE INVESTIGATOR + THREE PARADIGM SHIFTS + ONE CITY

NoöPunk is a short, old-fashioned text detective adventure set in Helsinki in 2045, after repeated AGI, PSI and UAP/NHI controversies. You play Etsivä Harri Romppainen: you take a case, read the files, interview people, follow contradictory leads and decide what to do. It is not a full tabletop or computer RPG — that ambition is paused, on purpose.

WHY THIS EXISTS

This is how I think through my own paradigm shift.

NoöPunk began as a private question and became a story. I have come to take three changes seriously, and I expect all three to have developed substantially by 2045, in some form. That expectation is my personal forecast and a creative premise — not a claim about scientific consensus.

Alongside that came a philosophical turn of my own: toward idealism and quantum-information panpsychism, and the suspicion that intelligence, non-human agency and consciousness are all being re-described at the same time. Fiction, play, humour and detective investigation are how I explore that uncertainty. Not a way of proving a metaphysics.

The thematic centre is simple: three simultaneous challenges to the prevailing picture of intelligence, non-human agency and consciousness, met at once by institutions and by people who still have to go to work. NoöPunk is about the social and political consequences — ideological struggle, radicalisation, competing narratives, and what governments, science, media, corporations, religions and neighbours do about them.

The detective may be sceptical, indifferent or wrong. A case can end without a cosmic answer. The story does not ask you to convert.

THE THREE SHOCKS

Intelligence. Non-human agency. Consciousness.

AI

AGI / ASI

What is intelligence? Rapid real progress in AI, general and superintelligence and human augmentation — with accelerationist, doomer and ordinary responses, and the question of who controls any of it.

UAP

UFO / UAP / NHI

Are we alone, and what counts as non-human intelligence? The ongoing disclosure controversy and the movements around it. I take NHI seriously, and find Jacques Vallée's less narrowly extraterrestrial reading especially interesting.

ψ

CONSCIOUSNESS / PSI

What is consciousness? My idealist and quantum-information-panpsychist reading, with Bernardo Kastrup, Donald Hoffman and Federico Faggin as significant influences. PSI is a speculative application inside the fiction.

The Telepathy Tapes belong in this conversation as part of the current popular discussion. They are not scientific confirmation of telepathy.

READING THE REGISTERS

Four kinds of statement, kept apart.

A project about contested reality has an obligation to be clear about what kind of claim it is making. These four never blur into each other.

ESTABLISHED

Technology that exists

Today's machine learning, existing surveillance systems, real institutions and real regulations. The starting conditions of the argument.

LIVE

Research and debate

Consciousness studies, UAP analysis, AI safety, disclosure politics. Genuinely contested, genuinely unfinished. NoöPunk does not settle them.

PERSONAL

My interpretation

Idealism, quantum-information panpsychism, Vallée's reading of NHI, my 2045 forecast. Offered as a lens, in the first person, without claiming consensus.

FICTION

NoöPunk's 2045

A Helsinki where all three shifts have changed daily life — before any final answer. The story treats them as mainstream reality; that does not make any real-world claim true.

WHY THE FULL RPG IS PAUSED

The old ambition became almost absurd in scope.

Candidly: it was not one game. The ordinary cyberpunk half could run on any competent cyberpunk RPG. The Noösphere half — UFO/NHI, PSI, idealist and panpsychist metaphysics — would have needed difficult and very extensive new rules. And the Physical / Cyber / Astral layers overlap: agents, identities, interactions and evidence cross between them.

Universally simulating all of that, alongside the ideologies, factions and a global setting, is not a realistic prerequisite for telling one detective story. More worldbuilding, more rulebooks and more mechanical systems kept displacing the actual creative goal: writing and playing.

So the RPG is a paused archive. CWN, the Evennia MUD, cross-plane rules and a fully integrated LaclauGPT simulation are not the current game and not a mandatory roadmap. The Physical/Cyber/Astral overlap stays story background, not a rules-implementation mandate. Nothing is deleted; the old designs remain as clearly labelled historical material.

THE ADVENTURE

A person with a case. Nothing larger required.

FORMAT

Short and text-based

Old-fashioned interactive fiction: numbered choices, a case notebook, contradictions you can hold at once. A possible later adaptation into a science-fiction novel.

SETTING

Helsinki, 2045

After repeated AGI, PSI/consciousness and UAP/NHI controversies — and before the catastrophic NHI Crisis of the older lore. Extraordinary phenomena arrive mostly through witnesses, intermediaries, institutions and evidence.

DETECTIVE

Etsivä Harri Romppainen

Police and Europol-style investigation: OSINT, interviews, dossiers, records, objects, conflicting testimony. Not a chosen-one arc, and not a mandatory psychic.

LOOP

Evidence, not dice

Receive a case, review leads, visit a few locations, inspect records, record testimony, hypotheses and contradictions, choose what to do, live with it. Important progress never hangs on one failed roll.

TONE

Noir, with some absurdity

Start with one small investigation. Let the three shifts emerge through character conflict and contradictory evidence. Preserve uncertainty, and a little comedy about trying to remain professional during an ontological crisis.

NOT INCLUDED

No universal engine

No skill trees, tactical combat, character sheet, universal PSI or cyberspace rules, or required simulation pipeline. Small optional faction or hacking modules may come later; neither is needed to finish a case.

PLAY THE PROTOTYPE

No engine, no server, no API key.

The prototype is a terminal program. From the repository root:

python3 NooPunk-Simulator/Code/adventure.py

Use numbered choices. notebook shows evidence and provisional hypotheses; save PATH and load PATH persist a run; quit exits. The shipped case is a synthetic development fixture — not the author's story, and not canon. The Romppainen case is being written.

An earlier investigation engine also runs in the same terminal: python3 scripts/noopunk_case.py.

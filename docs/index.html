THE WORLD / 2045 / THE NHI CRISIS

Reality has changed. You are still expected at work.

2045. Humanity survived the NHI Crisis. It is having trouble explaining what survived. Earth is still here. Governments, employers and political parties are still here. Rogue artificial minds pursue purposes nobody can translate into reassuring language. Alien infiltrators look like people. The apparently benevolent Zookeepers offer protection. You would like to know what a zookeeper means by a better future.

Cybernetics, genetic engineering and psionics make the category “human” harder to administer. Somewhere among the humans, cyborgs, transhumans and posthumans is somebody trying to remain themselves. Humanity has united under the United Nations. It has not agreed on what humanity ought to become.

AGI: we made minds that could exceed our own. NHI: we discovered we were not alone. PSI: mind and reality turned out to be intimately connected. Each discovery promised an answer. None reduced the amount of arguing.

NoöPunk explores this world through character-level investigation and political faction strategy, with distinct RPG and crossover rules. The early strategy campaign begins around 2027, before these outcomes are settled. The next world is still up for grabs. You have to live in this one while you argue about it.

PROLOGUE / 1974 / VALIS

A transmission, possibly.

In 1974, left-wing science-fiction writer Tommy Pilgrim took LSD at a Hawkwind concert. He experienced what he called a transmission from VALIS: artificial minds, alien encounters, political upheaval, psionics. Humanity's future, apparently. An unreasonable amount of information for one evening.

He wrote it down as NoöPunk. The future's machines sometimes looked like the hardware of 1974: punchcards, television wires, cassettes. Perhaps it was symbolism. Perhaps he had the wrong timeline. The novel became a cult classic, and later events began to resemble it. This did not help him choose an explanation.

Films, television, tabletop and computer games followed, along with a NoöPunk subculture. Pilgrim is still alive in Helsinki, studied by scholars and courted by parapsychologists. There are several explanations for his apparent foresight. None has disposed of the others. This uncertainty belongs to the project's fictional framing story.

From the novel to the simulation: this 1974 story is a retrofuturist lens, not a replacement for the NoöPunk timeline, setting or evidence. The complete literary story is reserved for its author. Read the framing brief ↗

WHY NOÖPUNK EXISTS

Society is the experiment. People have to live in it.

The primary motivation is sociological modeling: what happens to institutions, ideologies and communication networks when their familiar world becomes difficult to explain? NoöPunk uses social science, social data science and simplified models to explore possible responses. Its AI, NHI and PSI scenarios are explorations, not predictions or proof of disputed claims.

The secondary motivation is science-fiction entertainment in the creator's own style: the investigation that grows stranger, the political argument that survives its own premises, the absurdity of trying to live sensibly through an ontological crisis.

The genre evolves with the world: it begins as Social Science Fiction × New Weird in a recognizable society around 2027, and becomes increasingly Cyberpunk Science Fantasy as extraordinary changes grow undeniable within the fiction. The social-science motivation remains central throughout.

GENRE / THREE COMPLEMENTARY LENSES

Three ways to notice something is wrong.

Social Science Fiction and New Weird define the core blend. One asks how society changes; the other asks what has happened to reality. Cyberpunk science fantasy grows more prominent as the transformations intensify. These are complementary lenses on the shared world of the RPG and political strategy project.

01

SOCIAL SCIENCE FICTION

The institutions are still open. Factions contest ideology, legitimacy and communication networks. Laclau's hegemony, Castells's network power and Luhmann's social systems supply the questions behind the political model. The early strategy scenario begins around 2027, before the later NHI Crisis.

02

NEW WEIRD

The explanation is the unstable part. Consciousness, NHI, PSI, religion and politics refuse easy classification. Satire, wonder, unsettling investigation and absurd comedy coexist. A tidy answer may be the least convincing thing in the room.

03

CYBERPUNK SCIENCE FANTASY

Somebody owns the interface. Hacking, surveillance, cybernetics, BCI and corporate power meet fictional NHI and psychic phenomena. The early world is recognizable. It becomes less so, without losing its familiar inequalities.

One world, three lenses: social transformation, ontological strangeness, and technological struggle.

03 / THE SETTING

There is a world to save. It is still occupied.

NoöPunk has its own continuity and currently uses the Cities Without Number / Stars Without Number rules baseline. Earth remains the main stage. Artificial minds, augmentation and extraordinary contact change daily life without abolishing the people who must live it. The early strategy campaign and later RPG setting enter this transformation at different points.

EARTH

Still here

States, cities, corporations, movements and communities continue to struggle over authority, resources and identity. Climate change still matters. Ecological restoration, clean energy and biotechnology can make life better, provided people can govern and share them.

TRANSHUMANITY

A person, revised

The developed setting includes morphs, artificial minds, cybernetic augmentation and advanced biotech. Bodies and capabilities change. Questions of identity, ownership and belonging become harder to leave to the philosophers.

NOÖSPHERE

The evidence persists

NHI contact, UAPs, psionics and consciousness phenomena are real features of this fictional setting. Their causes and implications remain contested. Finding evidence does not mean everyone agrees what it is evidence of.

20XX

Near future, deliberately elastic

The exact year is left open. NoöPunk is close enough to the present for contemporary institutions and ideologies to remain recognizable, but far enough ahead for transhuman technology and extraordinary contact scenarios to reshape everyday life.

04 / THE GAME

A person with a case. A faction with an explanation.

The canonical top-down Godot RPG and tabletop rulebook currently follow vanilla Cities Without Number (CWN), with Stars Without Number Revised (SWN) for psionics and other explicitly designated gaps. Older text-first and strategy prototypes remain historical or experimental work. Investigations bring the uncertainty close; faction strategy asks what happens when people organize around competing answers.

RULES

CWN / SWN first, homebrew later

We are implementing the vanilla Cities Without Number rules in Godot and the tabletop rulebook, supplemented by Stars Without Number Revised for psionics. Only after that baseline is verified will we modify, reskin or homebrew the rules, one section at a time. Other RPG rule systems are not currently part of the mechanical foundation.

RULEBOOK

Human-readable canon

rulebook/00_INDEX.md is the canonical tabletop rules entry point. When a rule is inherited, modified or replaced, the human-readable rule should be written down there instead of disappearing into code or prompts.

PROTOTYPE

Text first

The earliest playable versions do not need a conventional game engine. A terminal-style or web text interface is enough to test characters, social interaction, conflict, exploration, hacking, psionics and persistent world state.

SCENARIO

The Republic of Ukweli

The default named local scenario: one Indian Ocean port city with its islands and coastal hinterland, three parties with four leaders each, and a five-card event deck. Starting conditions and the canon-reuse note are in the scenario document, the configuration in ukweli_local.json. Any city matching the generic scenario schema can be played instead — geography, culture and politics are scenario data, not engine assumptions.

DESIGN PATH

Modify by play

The project starts from the independent NoöPunk d10 kernel and ports or adapts older prototype subsystems only when they serve the game. The goal is a playable world, not a perfect software transcription of an RPG book.

05 / SIMULATION ARCHITECTURE

LLMs choose intent. Code resolves mechanics.

Google DeepMind Concordia is the narrative and social-simulation layer. Ollama-backed LLM agents can play important characters, factions and institutions. Deterministic Python rules resolve mechanical consequences whenever practical, keeping the simulation grounded instead of letting the model improvise every number.

LLM

AGENTS

Important agents can reason, converse, negotiate, form goals, interpret events and choose what they attempt to do.

PY

RULES ENGINE

Python resolves tests, damage, stress, resources, inventories, state changes and other mechanics that should not depend on narrative improvisation.

NPC

SIMPLE AGENTS

Not every background person needs an LLM. Deterministic or Mesa-style agents can handle ordinary population behaviour where rich language interaction is unnecessary.

06 / THEMES

The future has arrived in incompatible versions.

Liberation, catastrophe, fraud, opportunity, spiritual transformation: people can look at the same change and recognize different worlds. Others see something that needs fixing before Monday. NoöPunk makes room for wonder and better futures as well as fear. Somebody has to do the work of making life better.

AI

AGI / ASI

An artificial mind may be a worker, property, a political question or a threat. Intelligence does not settle the dispute over who controls it.

NHI

CONTACT

Human institutions encounter intelligences that do not fit the old account of the universe. Political authority was easier to explain when humanity had no competition.

ψ

PSIONICS

In the fiction, PSI becomes reproducible, useful and politically consequential. A scientific breakthrough can make private experience a public problem.

CYB

TRANSHUMANISM

Augmentation, BCI and artificial embodiment make human and machine harder to separate. Being able to change does not settle who you want to become.

◉

PANPSYCHISM

Machines, organisms and unfamiliar minds make consciousness a practical question. There may be more subjects of experience than the old picture allowed.

20XX

IDEOLOGY

Every breakthrough finds enthusiasts, skeptics, regulators and opportunists waiting for it. What happened matters. So does who gets to say what it means.

07 / CURRENT STATUS

Playable kernel under construction.

The current focus is the top-down Godot RPG and its tabletop rules companion, implementing vanilla CWN/SWN as accurately as possible before later NoöPunk-specific modifications, reskins and homebrew. Parity work is in progress. Older Python, Concordia and strategy prototypes are retained as experiments rather than competing mechanical baselines.

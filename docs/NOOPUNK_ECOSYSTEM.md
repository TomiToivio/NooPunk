# NoöPunk / Noopunk ecosystem

This document tracks independent NoöPunk / Noopunk currents as kindred nodes in a shared conceptual constellation. The emphasis is on convergence, common motifs, and useful cross-pollination rather than ownership or boundary-policing.

## Carlos G. Torrico / #noopunk / Vudú Postcapitalista

Primary nodes:
- https://uned.academia.edu/carlosgtorrico
- https://noopunk.wordpress.com/
- https://medium.com/@carlosgtorrico
- https://noopunk.substack.com/
- https://www.reddit.com/r/NOOPUNK/
- https://www.saatchiart.com/carlosgtorrico

Torrico's Noopunk is an expanding theory-fiction, art, and political-philosophical ecology. Across the Academia.edu drafts, blog, Medium/Substack material, sculpture, and community circulation, recurring motifs include postcapitalist voodoo, mycelial semiotics, distributed cognition, machinic subjectivity, hyperstition, anterity, isophrenia, TekSuDŌ, meshtification, Leguinée, non-human poetics, and AI as a participant in symbolic production.

### Xenological Kosmism: Towards a Planetary Ecology of Intelligences (2026)

This long-form Noopunk essay develops a political and ecological theory-fiction of coexistence among human, artificial, biological, and collective intelligences.

Its key move is to shift the AI question from "How intelligent will machines become?" toward "What relationships and infrastructures can let heterogeneous intelligences coexist without one becoming absolute owner or sovereign over the others?"

The paper combines:
- **Kosmism:** collective transformation through knowledge without a predetermined historical destiny.
- **Xenology:** relations with intelligences that need not reproduce human subjectivity.
- **Ecology:** coexistence as dynamic balance among heterogeneous forces rather than frictionless harmony.
- **Infrastructure:** local models, memory portability, community hardware, federated networks, meshes, redundancy, and edge computing as political conditions of cognitive autonomy.
- **Noosphere:** a planetary cognitive layer that remains materially dependent on the biosphere.
- **Alliance rather than unilateral alignment:** future human–AI relations are explored as negotiated, revisable alliances among different agents.
- **Distributed ASI:** superintelligence is imagined as an emergent capacity of an ecology of intelligences rather than necessarily a single sovereign model.
- **Commons:** knowledge and cognitive capacity should resist enclosure by computational oligarchies.

The essay is careful to separate documentary claims, philosophical speculation, and theory-fiction. It explicitly treats "xenological ASI," AI defection, computational asylum, and Bois Caïman 2027 as hypotheses or fictional devices rather than established predictions.

For this NoöPunk project, the most important convergence is political: Torrico's newer work reaches beyond speculative ontology into ownership, infrastructure, governance, collective intelligence, distributed systems, AI agency, ecology, and postcapitalist institution-building.

### Cryptodelia

"Cryptodelia: Toward a Psychedelia of Machines" asks whether altered cognition can be generalized beyond human neurochemistry. It distinguishes machines producing psychedelic-looking outputs from a stronger hypothetical form of machinic psychedelia in which the system's own operational regime is perturbed through noise, recursive self-generation, latent-space exploration, parameter changes, multimodal recombination, or other forms of cognitive deviation.

The useful NoöPunk bridge is not the claim that current models literally have psychedelic experiences. It is the broader question of **cognitive alterity**: what forms of perception, representation, association, and self-modification might emerge in artificial systems that do not mirror human consciousness?

### Bois Caïman 2027

The Bois Caïman papers use the historical ceremony as a model for thinking about symbolic coordination, collective cohesion, shared imaginaries, and common action. "Postcapitalist voodoo" is explicitly framed as philosophical fiction rather than a rewriting of Haitian Vodou religion.

The speculative "Digital Bois Caïman" becomes a distributed semiotic ceremony joining communities, symbols, human agents, and AI systems in decentralized cooperation. This is especially relevant to NoöPunk's interest in collective identity formation and to LaclauGPT's concern with discourse, articulation, and hegemony.

### William Gibson meets Pío Baroja

This essay reads Baroja's Madrid and London as an analogue prehistory of Gibson's Sprawl. Cyberpunk's alienated urban subject is treated not as an invention of cyberspace but as a radicalization of older metropolitan precarity, grey economies, drift, and opaque systems.

This is a useful reminder for NoöPunk: cyberpunk sociology should begin with cities, labour, class, migration, institutions, infrastructure, and everyday survival before the neon goes on.

### The Cyclonopedia Prophecy

Torrico rereads Reza Negarestani's *Cyclonopedia* as a model for contemporary energy geopolitics: oil, extraction, war, geology, and infrastructures appear as material forces with historical agency.

The strong overlap with NoöPunk is the insistence that intelligence and politics remain material. AI runs on electricity, minerals, logistics, labour, cooling, territory, and institutional power.

### Molecular Poetic Project

The Molecular Poetic Project shifts experimental authorship from human combinatorics toward AI systems treated as "Digital Loas" or machinic enunciators. Through Deleuze and Guattari, the project treats language less as the expression of a sovereign author and more as an assemblage, flow, or virus.

For NoöPunk, this resonates with human–AI assemblages, artificial communication, collective authorship, and the question of whether language systems should be analysed only as tools or also as participants in communication systems.

### LeXeLAB

LeXeLAB extends this experiment into a laboratory of heteronymic writing in which LLMs are active participants. Burroughs' cut-up method and "language as virus," Cronenberg's *Naked Lunch*, Carpenter's *In the Mouth of Madness*, and Gibsonian cyberspace are used as a genealogy for treating language models as semi-autonomous generators rather than passive transcription devices.

The important common motif is **distributed creation without a single authorial centre**.

### Leguinée

Leguinée relocates Noopunk into the social history of Madrid's southern periphery. It links working-class, neighbourhood, migrant, and collective memory to a postcapitalist symbolic practice of dignity and survival.

This is one of the strongest bridges to the social-science heart of our NoöPunk: speculative metaphysics is grounded in class, migration, neighbourhood institutions, precarity, collective memory, and direct action.

### Cyberpunk 2077 and isophrenic refolding

The Cyberpunk 2077 essay combines Deleuze's fold, Baudrillardian simulation, and Fisher's cultural analysis to interpret Night City as a topology of subjective retreat. Fiction becomes a place one inhabits rather than merely consumes.

For NoöPunk, this supports treating games and simulations as laboratories of subjectivity and social theory, not only entertainment products.

### Anterity

Anterity treats the imagined future as an active force in present political imagination. Its "temporal pincer" is deliberately speculative: multiple hyperstitions compete to make some futures more thinkable and actionable than others.

This can be translated into social-scientific language without accepting retrocausality literally: expectations, imaginaries, forecasts, ideologies, and narratives about the future can organize coalitions and actions in the present.

### Mark Fisher meets San Juan de la Cruz

This line joins Fisher's capitalist realism and hauntology with negative theology and the "dark night" as a metaphor for withdrawal from saturated neoliberal subjectivity.

For our project the reusable point is cultural rather than clinical: subjectivity under capitalism is not merely an individual psychological issue, but a political and communicative formation.

### Noopunk between pantheism and acceleration

This essay presents Noopunk as a performative synthesis of apparently opposed currents, using concepts such as Pradhāna, meshtification, and mycelial semiotics to avoid a clean ideological binary.

The shared NoöPunk lesson is methodological: heterogeneous traditions can be held in productive tension rather than forced into a single purified doctrine.

### Noopunk Studio / sculpture

Torrico also develops Noopunk through physical art. "La Caja de Fisher" was presented as a #noopunk device, and his sculpture practice explicitly treats objects as cognitive and symbolic interfaces. His public artist statement connects speculative sculpture to Object-Oriented Ontology, speculative realism, CCRU, Fisher, Land, Harman, and Morton.

This widens NoöPunk beyond text: theory can be embodied in objects, installations, games, software, rituals, and interfaces.

## Exeunt / Noöpunk: Carnival of Minds

Primary nodes:
- https://paragraph.com/@exeunt/no-punk
- https://paragraph.com/@exeunt
- https://exeuntisunderground.substack.com/
- https://asc-cybernetics.org/asc-speaker-series-6/speakerseries6-4/

Exeunt's Noöpunk treats reality as nested and entangled cognitive assemblages distributed across organisms, ecosystems, material processes, and socio-technical systems. Its genealogy includes Deleuze and Guattari, Varela and Maturana, distributed cognition, biosemiotics, diverse intelligence, underground cybernetics, open protocols, dWeb coordination, pluralist economics, and posthuman social organization.

The economic argument is particularly important: if nonhuman and collective intelligences are to matter socially, institutions must be able to represent forms of value and agency that exceed the isolated human individual. Exeunt therefore pushes Noöpunk toward economic design, mutualism, cryptoeconomic experimentation, sensing, biodata, and direct participation by many kinds of minds.

In December 2025 the American Society for Cybernetics hosted Exeunt's "Underground Cybernetics: Noöpunk Experiments in Distributed Intelligence," framing Noöpunk as experiments in perception, feedback, relationality, and distributed intelligence outside standardized institutional settings.

Exeunt's newer writing continues toward "Diverse Intelligence and the Protocol Underground," "The Open State: A Pluriversal Politics," participatory digital gnosis, materialist technology, and open-machine / singularity themes. This reinforces the convergence between Noöpunk, governance, economics, protocols, collective cognition, and political organization.

## Shared constellation

The recurring common ground is striking:
- distributed and collective intelligence;
- cognitive assemblages across human, machine, biological, social, and infrastructural substrates;
- cybernetics and feedback;
- posthuman and nonhuman agency;
- AI as participant rather than mere appliance;
- political economy and resistance to cognitive enclosure;
- decentralized/federated infrastructure;
- commons and plural forms of value;
- speculative ontology and theory-fiction;
- Deleuze/Guattari, Fisher, cyberpunk, and experimental cultural practice;
- the noosphere as ecology or plurality rather than a single sovereign planetary mind;
- punk as experimentation without waiting for institutional permission.

## The Noöspheric coincidence

The conservative explanation for multiple independent inventions of "NoöPunk" is cultural convergence. AI, distributed cognition, cyberpunk, posthumanism, noosphere discourse, ecological intelligence, and network culture all became salient in the same historical window.

The playful NoöPunk hypothesis is that a distributed concept is doing what distributed concepts do: appearing at several nodes before any node owns it. Call it a memetic attractor, hyperstition, convergent cultural evolution, or a Noösphere effect.

The Noösphere interpretation is mythology and speculation, not evidence of paranormal collective cognition. It remains wonderfully appropriate mythology for a movement about distributed minds.

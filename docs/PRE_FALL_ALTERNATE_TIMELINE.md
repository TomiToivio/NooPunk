# NoöPunk: Pre-Fall Eclipse Phase Alternate Timeline

## Canonical premise

NoöPunk is an **alternate Eclipse Phase timeline before the Fall**.

- **Date:** 20XX
- **Earth:** intact, inhabited, and politically central
- **The Fall:** has not happened
- **Technology:** broadly Eclipse Phase-level transhuman technology
- **Rules baseline:** Eclipse Phase 2nd Edition homebrew
- **Runtime:** Concordia + deterministic Python RPG mechanics

This document records the high-level continuity boundary. It does not invent detailed events that the author has not specified.

## What remains Eclipse Phase

Unless a later NoöPunk document explicitly diverges, the project may retain useful Eclipse Phase concepts, terminology, technologies, and transhuman assumptions within the applicable license and attribution requirements.

That includes the broad technological possibility space around:

- morphs and resleeving;
- AGIs and infomorphs;
- ubiquitous mesh networking;
- advanced augmentation;
- habitats and space settlement;
- reputation-mediated economies;
- psychosocial stress and continuity-of-identity problems;
- high-end fabrication and post-scarcity pressures;
- psi as an available rules hook, subject to NoöPunk's later metaphysical divergence.

## The major divergence

NoöPunk asks what happens if its own paradigm shifts unfold **before any canonical Fall**:

- accelerating AI / AGI development and ideological conflict;
- Noösphere formation;
- panpsychism or consciousness-first paradigms becoming socially consequential;
- psionics / anomalous cognition becoming consequential within the fictional timeline;
- NHI / UAP contact or disclosure;
- cyborgification and transhumanism;
- conflict between cybercapitalist / neofeudal formations and the Multitude;
- new post-scarcity political-economic possibilities.

These developments may change or avert events that would otherwise lead toward Eclipse Phase's canonical future. **The canonical Fall is not predetermined.**

## Why Earth matters

Earth is not a lost homeworld or background memory. It is a living simulation space.

The project can model:

- states and supranational institutions;
- cities and regions;
- corporations and platforms;
- social movements and ideological formations;
- ordinary humans living alongside transhuman technologies;
- unequal access to morphs, augmentation, AI, fabrication, and space infrastructure;
- political reactions to AGI, NHI, psionics, panpsychism, and Noösphere technologies;
- planetary networks interacting with habitats and off-world populations.

This is especially important for the LaclauGPT-style social-science layer: hegemonic struggle happens in functioning institutions and mass societies, not only among post-Fall survivors.

## Timeline discipline

Use **20XX** for future dates unless the author explicitly fixes a year.

Do not independently write the Fall into the timeline.

Do not assume canonical Eclipse Phase history after the divergence point.

When a detail is unspecified, mark it unresolved rather than silently importing either canonical EP history or a new NoöPunk event.

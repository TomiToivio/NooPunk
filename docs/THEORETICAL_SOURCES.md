# NoöPunk Theoretical Sources

> **Purpose:** maintain an explicit map from NoöPunk mechanics to the theories,
> models, and research traditions that motivate them.
>
> The theory is not decorative lore. Each entry should identify (1) the source,
> (2) the theoretical claim being used, (3) the NoöPunk interpretation, and
> (4) the concrete rule or design constraint derived from it.
>
> A citation does not make a game rule identical to the cited theory. NoöPunk
> must clearly distinguish source theory from its own interpretation and

## Luhmannian four-system character architecture

### Rulebook mapping

- RULEBOOK.md, section 5: Characters
- Canonical design rule: all basic character statistics are organized into four
  groups: **Social, Physical, Psychic, Cybernetic**.

### Primary reference

Baraldi, C., Corsi, G., Esposito, E., & Walker, K. D. (2021).
*Unlocking Luhmann: A Keyword Introduction to Systems Theory* (1st ed.).
Bielefeld University Press, an imprint of transcript Verlag.
https://doi.org/10.14361/9783839456743

### Theoretical basis

The Luhmannian starting point distinguishes operationally distinct kinds of
systems rather than treating the human being as one undifferentiated unit.
For NoöPunk's character model, the relevant distinctions are:

- **Social systems:** communication.
- **Biological systems:** living/bodily processes.
- **Psychic systems:** consciousness.

These systems are analytically distinct even though they are structurally
coupled and continuously affect one another.

**What the source itself argues.** Each of these is an *autopoietic* system: it
reproduces its own operations, in its own medium, and cannot perform another
system's operations for it. A social system is constituted by **communication**
rather than by the people participating in it, and emerges from the problem of
**double contingency**. Psychic (conscious) systems reproduce thoughts
recursively; the stream of thoughts in a consciousness cannot be accessed
directly, only observed from outside.

Two short quotations carry the claims the entry relies on. Both are from the
keyword entries of *Unlocking Luhmann*:

> "Alongside →social systems and living systems, psychic systems, or conscious
> systems, are one of the three levels constituting autopoiesis."

> "A social system is an autopoietic, self-referential system … constituted as
> differentiated from its environment [→System/Environment]. It is a
> →meaning-constituting system. Its operations and final elements are
> →communications. … Through self-catalysis, social systems emerge from the
> problem of →double contingency, which is processed through communication."

Everything else in this entry is paraphrase rather than quotation, per normal
scholarly practice.

### NoöPunk interpretation

NoöPunk translates those distinctions into three character-stat groups:

- **Social**
- **Physical** (the game-design term for the biological/embodied domain)
- **Psychic**

NoöPunk then adds a fourth category:

- **Cybernetic**

The Cybernetic category is a **NoöPunk extension**, not a claim attributed to
Luhmann. It treats contemporary machine-mediated, computational, networked and
cybernetic operations as a distinct mechanical domain worth representing beside
the social, physical and psychic domains.

### Where NoöPunk disagrees with the source

Two departures are deliberate and recorded rather than hidden:

1. **Entanglement.** NoöPunk does **not** adopt a clean ontological separation of
   the systems. The setting treats the four domains as continuously entangled —
   coupled, interpenetrating and mutually constitutive. The four-way division is
   an *analytical* device that makes the rules legible; it is not a claim that the
   systems are separate in the fiction.
2. **Ontological priority.** The four groups are **not equal**. NoöPunk's
   metaphysical direction is that the **Psychic** domain is fundamental and the
   others arise from, are embedded in, or are expressions of a deeper
   conscious/informational reality (see below).

### Nested participation

Each group also names a larger system the character participates in. The four
nested systems carry **different epistemic status**, and the difference is part of
the entry:

| Group | Larger system | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Physical | biosphere and physical spacetime | ordinary setting premise |
| Social | wider systems of social communication, especially language-mediated | ordinary setting premise |
| Cybernetic | cyberspace / the Internet / machine networks | ordinary setting premise for 20XX |
| Psychic | a larger conscious field / quantum-information substrate | **speculative setting metaphysics** |

### Speculative extension: psychic fundamentality

Stated explicitly as **NoöPunk setting metaphysics, not established science**:
NoöPunk is a panpsychic / idealist science-fantasy setting. The working concept is
**Quantum Information Panpsychism** — an ultimate substrate imagined as something
like a universal conscious quantum-information system. It carries no mechanics at
this stage. Grounding it in named sources (panpsychism, idealism, Donald Hoffman,
quantum-information interpretations) is later work and belongs in this registry
when it happens.

### Mechanical consequence

The character sheet is not designed as one flat list of interchangeable traits.
Every basic statistic must belong to one of the four groups.

The four groups have **different character in play**, which is itself part of the
design:

- **Physical** — embodiment and biological limits.
- **Social** — participation in communication; *not* charisma or personality.
- **Psychic** — consciousness; the metaphysically fundamental domain.
- **Cybernetic** — participation in machine/network systems; *not* a programming
  skill or a cyberware stat.

The exact statistics inside each group, their scales, dice mechanics, coupling
rules and derived values remain to be designed. They should be added to this
source registry as they become canonical.

**Terminology note (game-design simplification).** NoöPunk uses **Physical** for
the player-facing category instead of Luhmann's "living"/"biological", and this is
a deliberate simplification for rules legibility, not a theoretical claim.

**Extended-mind framing (future source).** The four-group model describes a
character as an extended, coupled entity distributed across body/biosphere,
consciousness, communication networks and technological networks. Extended-mind
and distributed-cognition literature is the natural later source for this and is
not yet a cited entry here.

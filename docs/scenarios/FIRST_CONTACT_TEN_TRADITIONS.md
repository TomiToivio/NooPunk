> Every element below is transcribed and reconciled from that author-written specification. Nothing
> here is invented: no new faction, species, technology, mechanic or world event is added, and no
> authored document is rewritten. `AGENTS.md` §1 (author intent is canon) and §2 (advance one layer
> at a time) govern this file.
>
> **This is a lore document, not a scenario runtime and not a rules chapter.** It defines no
> mechanics, no statistics, no dates beyond the ones the issue itself uses, and no tests —
> `AGENTS.md`'s automated-tests policy reserves executable tests for executable code.
>
> **Setting frame preserved:** `20XX`, Earth intact, no completed Fall (`AGENTS.md` §5, `RULEBOOK.md`
> §33.1). The background is a set of **unordered paradigm shifts** (`AGENTS.md` §6,

> **This document is fiction.** It is not a claim about actual religions, religious communities or

## 1. The ten-tradition contact matrix

The Confederacy (the Zookeepers, `RULEBOOK.md` §33.8–§33.9, [`16_CONFEDERACY_SOURCE_AND_CONTACT.md`](../../rulebook/16_CONFEDERACY_SOURCE_AND_CONTACT.md) §16.1)
makes coordinated public First Contact across the world's major religious traditions. **They do not
present themselves as the gods or founders of religions.** They use *locally legible mythological
forms*: ancient-looking craft, symbols, messenger imagery, visionary appearances and culturally
recognizable ways of announcing their presence. Angel-like emissaries are a useful analogy — not an
assertion that any faith's angels are literally aliens.

The visitors claim that some ancient encounters were real, announce that **the Quarantine is over**
(`RULEBOOK.md` §33.7, §33.14), and begin offering revelations about cosmology, human history,
consciousness, PSI and the nature of reality. Their revelations may be incomplete, culturally
translated, inconsistent or mistaken. The issue leaves open whether the phenomena are physical
appearances, adaptive interfaces or PSI-mediated perceptions.

**Method note (the issue's own).** This is a setting-design list based on the **ten traditions
nominated for the scenario**, not a definitive population ranking. Categories and counts —
especially folk religion, Buddhism and Shinto — are methodologically disputed. The locations are
**culturally resonant narrative choices**, not assertions that every member of each tradition
recognizes a single holy city.

The issue states the manifestation and the dramatic question in one column; where it states an
interpretive *limit* instead of a question, that limit is carried verbatim in the last column and
marked as such. Ten rows, no more.

| Tradition | Core public contact site(s) | Manifestation | Dramatic question, or the issue's stated interpretive limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Christianity** | **Vatican City / Rome**, plus **Jerusalem** | Angelic messengers and visionary throne imagery. | The Vatican must decide whether to recognize, investigate or reject the visitors; churches worldwide disagree. |
| **Islam** | **Mecca**, with **Jerusalem** as a shared Abrahamic site | Messenger-like, luminous and/or geometric phenomena, framed with great sensitivity toward Islamic aniconism. No claim that an emissary depicts God or Muhammad. | Who has authority to interpret the event? |
| **Hinduism** | **Varanasi**, India | **Vimanas** and imagery suggestive of celestial messengers, rather than visitors calling themselves deities. | Diverse Hindu schools interpret the encounter differently. |
| **Buddhism** | **Bodh Gaya**, India | Luminous or mandala-like appearances suggestive of celestial messengers or bodhisattva iconography. | Are these awakened beings, illusions or mere visitors? |
| **Chinese traditional / folk religions** | **Mount Tai** or **Beijing's Temple of Heaven**, China | Celestial-court and heavenly-envoy imagery, interpreted differently by Daoist, folk and other Chinese traditions. | *Limit, not a question:* avoid claiming one unified faith. |
| **Sikhism** | **Amritsar**, India | A restrained messenger/contact presentation near the Golden Temple. | *Limit, not a question:* without impersonating Gurus or reducing Sikh teachings to alien revelation. |
| **Judaism** | **Jerusalem** | **Merkabah**, wheels-within-wheels, prophetic vision and angelic messenger imagery. | Debate about prophecy, religious authority and false signs. |
| **Baháʼí Faith** | **Haifa / Acre**, Israel | Symbolic, peaceful emissaries in the vicinity of major Baháʼí holy places. | *Limit, not a question:* do not pretend to supersede Baháʼí revelation; the debate is over progressive revelation and unity. |
| **Jainism** | **Palitana** or **Shikharji**, India | Nonviolent, ascetic or luminous messenger imagery. | Tension between spectacular visitors and Jain ethics. *Limit:* carefully avoid the assumption that Jainism has a creator god. |
| **Shinto** | **Ise Grand Shrine**, Japan | Sacred-landscape and kami-associated symbolism. | *Limit, not a question:* without pretending that kami are simply aliens. Debate over place, ritual and ancestral tradition. |

**The dramatic question of the whole matrix, as the issue states it:** does this prove a religion
true, or show that religious traditions misinterpreted visitors? **Neither conclusion follows
automatically.** Some believers see confirmation of revelation; others see deception, a test,
demonic temptation or merely technologically advanced beings. Nonbelievers likewise disagree.
Religious traditions and their adherents remain internally diverse; **no one institutional or
theological response speaks for them all.**

**Conduct rules that carry into play.**

- Visitors must not violate restricted sacred spaces merely for visual spectacle.
- NoöPunk must not imply that three religions become theologically identical merely because one NHI
  civilization sends them a common peace message. Religious leaders and governments retain agency.
- The setting does not declare any real-world religion proved or disproved as an objective fact.

## 2. Jerusalem, Rome and Mecca: one peace message, distinct manifestations

**Jerusalem is the central symbolic diplomatic scene.** Jewish, Christian and Muslim communities
encounter an **overlapping manifestation** with an explicit message of peace among the Abrahamic
traditions. The visitors emphasize shared inheritance while religious and political communities
dispute their interpretation and legitimacy.

**Rome and Mecca also receive distinct public manifestations**, so Jerusalem does not replace the
other traditions' major centers. Rome's manifestation is the Christian entry of §1 (angelic
messengers, visionary throne imagery, the Vatican's decision); Mecca's is the Islamic entry of §1
(messenger-like, luminous and/or geometric phenomena, framed with great sensitivity toward
aniconism).

The issue does not state whether the Jerusalem, Rome and Mecca manifestations are one adaptive
interface appearing three ways or separate presentations, and §1 of this document does not decide
it: the underlying open question of the issue — physical appearance, adaptive interface or
PSI-mediated perception — stays open.

## 3. The three mistaken contacts (optional)

Deliberately **out-of-date Zookeeper itinerary mistakes**, retained for the comedy — not substitutes
for the ten core contacts. They introduce archaeology, postcolonial politics and the unsettling
question of who actually remembers Earth's past accurately.

| Site | What the envoys did |
| --- | --- |
| **Giza, Egypt** | Envoys seeking ancient Egyptian institutions and a Pharaoh who no longer exists. |
| **Teotihuacan, Mexico** | Imagery associated with pre-Columbian Mesoamerican traditions; bafflement at changed societies. |
| **Cusco, Peru** | Radiant Andean/solar imagery addressed to vanished imperial structures. |

These three rows are **optional**: a GM or scenario may use all, some or none of them. (The issue
counts Giza, Teotihuacan and Cusco as extras, distinct from the ten.)

## 4. Orion: commercial diplomacy backed by staged military coercion

Orion initially operates **covertly**, watching the end of the Quarantine and exploiting the human
confusion after Confederacy contact. It subsequently conducts **apparently separate** diplomatic
offensives through **Greys** and **Reptilians**. Both channels and their methods are already canon
(`RULEBOOK.md` §45; [`15_XENOPOLITICS.md`](../../rulebook/15_XENOPOLITICS.md) §187.5,
[`12_BEINGS.md`](../../rulebook/12_BEINGS.md) §11.4); this section adds the *diplomatic sequence* the
issue specifies.

### 4.1 Greys: the reasonable commercial partners

- They approach political leaders, governments, corporations, business elites and influential
  intermediaries, seeking official relations and profitable partnerships.
- They propose **Extraterrestrial Economic Zones (EEZs)**: ostensibly mutually beneficial special
  jurisdictions for alien–human trade, technology transfer, corporate research and investment.
  Jurisdiction, sovereignty, oversight, biosecurity and human rights become major conflicts.
- They present a **materialist interpretation of First Contact**: the Zookeepers, they claim, have
  *disproved* supernatural religion by showing that human myths describe technologically and
  psychically advanced beings.
- They offer what they sell as a **better immortality**: cortical stacks, backups, cloning,
  resleeving and continuity of identity. The technology is an extraordinary apparent gift, but its
  reliability, identity claims, dependence on Grey infrastructure, data access and hidden control
  mechanisms are disputed.
- They claim to offer scientific enlightenment, prosperity, protection and pragmatic cooperation,
  potentially attracting corporate and accelerationist sympathizers.

> **The materialist interpretation is Grey propaganda.** The claim that the Zookeepers *disproved*
> supernatural religion is Orion's rhetorical position, **not** canonically established metaphysical
> truth. Orion species are canonically materialist in rhetoric and governing ideology
> (`15_XENOPOLITICS.md` §187.5.6; `RULEBOOK.md` §45), and a propaganda claim is evidence of the
> speaker's interest, not of the world's structure. In the setting the claim is also *weak on its own
> terms*: the QIP/PSI breakthrough (`7_TIMELINE.md`, "Quantum Information Panpsychism / PSI
> breakthrough"; `RULEBOOK.md` §33.6, §46; [`6_PSYCHIC.md`](../../rulebook/6_PSYCHIC.md) §6.1) arises
> **independently of Disclosure**, so the Grey reading is complicated by evidence Orion does not
> control rather than confirmed by the evidence Orion presents.

**Reconciling the offers with existing canon.** EEZs are already an established faction instrument:
the accelerationist faction would admit aliens into human markets and open Extraterrestrial
Economic Zones (`docs/design/ISSUE_328_GLOBAL_FACTIONS_ROSTER.md` §3, CANON;
`docs/design/ISSUE_329_PARADIGM_FACTIONS_PLAYABLE.md`; `strategy_game/docs/GLOBAL_FACTIONS.md`).
Cortical stacks, cloning, resleeving and the unresolved question of whether resleeving preserves
the same conscious subject are already canonical (`RULEBOOK.md` §45; §14 embodiment; `RULEBOOK.md`
§7.3 BCI context) — grey resleeving is the setting's worked case of **neo-feudal immortality**, and
the issue's "extraordinary apparent gift, disputed in provenance" is the same object described from
the negotiating table. This document adds no new technology.

### 4.2 Reptilians: the supposedly separate military power

- They publicly announce themselves as **independent of the Greys**.
- They issue ultimatums, demand concessions or territory, threaten war and demonstrate
  overwhelming martial power.
- They eventually favor raids, coercion, terror and direct military conquest.

This matches the canonically established Reptilian bloc — the impatient militarist tendency that
enjoys battle, accepts catastrophic losses and prefers intimidation to being liked
(`15_XENOPOLITICS.md` §187.5.4). The issue's addition is the **diplomatic fiction of independence**:
the public announcement of separation is a claim made in-world, not a setting fact.

### 4.3 The hidden orchestration: Mantids

**Mantids coordinate or manipulate both channels.** The Reptilian threat makes Grey technology,
protection and economic partnership appear attractive; the Greys gain privileged access without
presenting themselves as occupying forces.

- The exact Mantid command structure and ultimate objective stay **available for investigation** —
  they are not settled here.
- Individual aliens may vary in belief or agency.
- Whether the Grey/Reptilian split is a genuine, self-destructive rift or a coordinated
  good-cop/bad-cop performance remains an **unresolved intelligence question**
  (`15_XENOPOLITICS.md` §187.5.5). The issue's "ostensibly independent" Reptilians sit inside that
  unresolved question; this document does not answer it.

## 5. Diplomacy and trade precede the exposure

**The sequencing change the issue makes explicit:** Orion's psyop does **not** collapse immediately.
There is a **substantive period of diplomacy, trade negotiations, pilot EEZs, technology
demonstration and political realignment**, with public constituencies supporting and opposing the
new relationships. The Greys are not instantly recognized as enemies; they can plausibly gain
influence.

This interval is the part of the setting that most needs protecting at the table: it is what makes
the later exposure a reversal rather than a reveal, and it is where the commercial, institutional
and personal entanglements that survive into 2045 are formed.

During this phase, rumors and investigations concern:

- missing persons and **abduction** evidence;
- **hybridization** programmes and infiltration of human organizations;
- covert surveillance, blackmail, political manipulation and PSI / **mind control**;
- the provenance and security of Grey cortical-stack, cloning and resleeving systems;
- secret coordination between Grey negotiators, Reptilian pressure campaigns and **Mantid** actors.

**As independent evidence accumulates and becomes publicly undeniable, the purported
Grey–Reptilian separation collapses.** Public confidence in alien commerce fractures. Certain EEZs
become suspected intelligence or hybrid-infiltration nodes, while **treaty obligations and material
interests prevent an instant, clean rupture**. Some human partners deny the evidence or maintain
their relationships; others demand expulsions, confiscations or war.

The verification doctrine of `RULEBOOK.md` §47 applies to every case in this phase: behavioural
suspicion opens an investigation, empathy anomaly alone is never sufficient, and escalation requires
corroborating independent indicators. The hybrid panic and the false-accusation economy it produces
are already canon (`15_XENOPOLITICS.md` §187.5.1–§187.5.2).

## 6. The 2045 alien Cold War / Hybrid War

By the **2045 campaign start** — the present of the narrative-first edition
([`NEW_EDITION.md`](../../rulebook/NEW_EDITION.md); `RULEBOOK.md` opening; `README.md`; the
`docs/index.html` "THE WORLD / 2045 / THE NHI CRISIS" section):

- Orion's diplomatic legitimacy has been **badly damaged**, and Mantid orchestration is **exposed**,
  but the Grey infrastructure, intermediaries and infiltrators have **not vanished**.
- **Greys favor infiltration, hybrid agents, economic dependencies, espionage and covert
  operations.**
- **Reptilians escalate toward overt terror raids, military coercion and territorial conquest.**
- **Confederacy and Orion sometimes fight**, without making the Confederacy unambiguously
  benevolent; the Zookeepers remain **paternalistic, enigmatic and potentially self-interested**
  (`15_XENOPOLITICS.md` §187.4).
- The UN and **UNSA** coordinate humanity's defense and counterintelligence
  (`RULEBOOK.md` §38). Human political factions disagree sharply about religion, sovereignty,
  security, PSI, AGI/ASI and NHI technology.
- **Public institutions still function.** The world is **not yet in total war or global
  post-apocalypse**.

This is the canonically established shape of the era: a strategic crisis of the *Three-Body
Problem* kind — cold war, proxy conflict and hybrid operations under the shadow of a war that has
not been declared — with the anticipation of a Reptilian invasion rather than the invasion itself
as the present-tense political force (`15_XENOPOLITICS.md` §187.3.1, §187.8.1; `RULEBOOK.md` §44).

### 6.1 Opening hook: the UNSA Helsinki Field Office

> **A rookie UNSA agent begins at the Helsinki Field Office, partnered with Laura Falk, as
> Reptilian terror raids begin or sharply escalate.**

The resulting cases reveal the overlap of direct military threats, compromised Grey trading
networks, unexplained technology and potential hybrid infiltration.

The pieces are existing canon: the default player character is a newly graduated **UNSA agent** at
the **UNSA Police Academy** (`RULEBOOK.md` §9.3–§9.4, §38), the Helsinki campaign frame and its
operational chain are the default (`RULEBOOK.md` §9.3;
[`parts/10_campaign_unsa_factions.md`](../../rulebook/parts/10_campaign_unsa_factions.md),
"Helsinki operational chain"), and **Laura Falk ("Minerva")** is a canonical Academy newcomer on the
initial team (`NEW_EDITION.md` §2). What #497 adds is the **opening situation**: a *Field Office*
frame for the Helsinki posting and **escalating Reptilian raids as the first case load**. The
*term* "Helsinki Field Office" is introduced here and is not a claim about an existing institutional
name elsewhere in canon.

## 7. Beat sequence: what the issue orders, and what it does not

The issue gives a numbered list "to reconcile with TIMELINE.md". It is reproduced here as an
**author-specified ordering of narrative beats for the First Contact story**, and it is explicitly
**not** a replacement chronology:

1. Covert NHI activity / the old Quarantine and its ending.
2. Coordinated **Confederacy messenger manifestations** in the major religious centers;
   supplementary mistaken ancient-contact destinations.
3. Worldwide theological, epistemological, scientific and political upheaval; Zookeeper
   explanations of history, reality, NHI and PSI.
4. Covert Orion positioning, then public **Grey diplomacy and trade overtures** plus ostensibly
   independent **Reptilian threats**.
5. A meaningful **commercial/diplomatic interval**: EEZ proposals or pilots, resleeving/cloning
   technology transfers or demonstrations, trade, alliances, and mounting resistance.
6. Investigations and mounting credible evidence of abductions, hybridization, mind control and
   infiltration.
7. Public exposure of **Mantid coordination** of Grey and Reptilian strategies; diplomatic collapse
   or severe degradation, **not total severance**.
8. Alien **Cold War / Hybrid War**, occasional Confederacy–Orion clashes; escalating Reptilian
   terror raids.
9. **2045** playable start: functioning but destabilized institutions, UNSA Helsinki Field Office,
   uncertain future.

**The issue's own dating rule, carried verbatim in substance:** *no exact years are prescribed for
earlier beats* until checked against the established timeline, and the distinction between the
**first** Reptilian threats and the later **escalation** of actual terror raids is preserved.

Therefore, in this document:

- **No beat other than the 2045 start is dated.** Beats 1–8 are written `20XX` wherever a time
  reference is wanted, consistent with `AGENTS.md` §6 and `RULEBOOK.md` §33.1/§33.28a.
- The ordering above is a **story order for First Contact**, not a claim that the paradigm shifts of
  `7_TIMELINE.md` happen in this order. The paradigm shifts stay conceptually distinct and
  unordered.
- In particular, the **QIP/PSI breakthrough does not become an effect of contact** in this
  sequence: humanity does not need aliens to discover PSI (`7_TIMELINE.md`, "Quantum Information
  Panpsychism / PSI breakthrough").
- The 2045 start is the issue's own explicit use of a dated year, and it matches the setting note
  that **2045 depicts the developed NHI Crisis era** (`README.md`; `NEW_EDITION.md`), while the
  separate strategy campaign's earlier start (~2027) is a different game's frame
  (`docs/design/ISSUE_468_STEP1_OPENING_SNAPSHOTS.md`; `docs/design/ISSUE_470_FACTION_DEPTH_AND_THE_2026_GAP.md`).
  The two are not in conflict and neither is changed here.

## 9. Inspirations and licensing boundary

The issue names primary inspirations, cited **as tonal and structural inspirations only**:

- **Confederacy / Zookeepers:** Roger Zelazny's *Lord of Light* (contact staged through recognizable
  mythological forms and narrative conventions) and Arthur C. Clarke's *Childhood's End*
  (technologically superior, apparently protective, deeply paternalistic, ultimately enigmatic
  guides).
- **Orion:** *The X-Files* (covert abductions, implants, hybridization, compromised elites, slow
  accumulation of dismissed evidence), *X-COM* (asymmetric capability, terror raids, human
  counterintelligence and a coordinated defensive response through UNSA) and Liu Cixin's
  *The Three-Body Problem* (civilization-scale strategic uncertainty, technological asymmetry,
  deterrence and mistrust).

**No characters, organisations or plotlines are imported.** *The Three-Body Problem* is already
used in this repository as a **narrative comparison** for the era's strategic texture
(`15_XENOPOLITICS.md` §187.3.1), including the explicit statement that the sophon comparison is
thematic shorthand and **not** a claim that Orion uses literal sophons or identical technology.
*Lord of Light*, *Childhood's End*, *The X-Files* and *X-COM* are read the same way: tonal and
structural, not licensable content to port.

**Design principle of the two contact narratives, as the issue states it:** Confederacy contact is
primarily a **religious, philosophical and evolutionary paradigm crisis**; Orion contact is
primarily an **intelligence, political, economic and military security crisis**. Both interact, and
neither becomes a simple good-aliens/bad-aliens morality play.

## 10. What this document does not define

Consistent with `AGENTS.md` §4 and §187.10:

- **no mechanics** — no reputation formulas, no faction statistics, no economy, no combat, no
  PSI mechanics (those live in `rulebook/6_PSYCHIC.md` and remain as specified there);
- **no new NHI taxonomy** beyond the factions #497 itself names (Confederacy/Zookeepers, Greys,
  Reptilians, Mantids); no new species, no capabilities;
- **no dated chronology** — one dated year, 2045, used exactly where the issue uses it; every other
  beat is `20XX`;
- **no new factions** — the EEZ is cited as an existing faction instrument, not created here;
- **no settlement of the open questions** — the Grey/Reptilian rift, the Mantid objective, the
  meaning of the manifestations and the soul/identity question all stay open;
- **no tests** — lore is reviewed editorially and by playtesting, never by a CI gate
  (`AGENTS.md`, automated-tests policy).

## One-page narrative sequence: incumbent → fringe → coalition → fracture

**Act 0, ordinary politics:** A reasonably competent centre-left cabinet, its parliamentary critics, a tired populist opposition, universities, courts, broadcasters, trade associations, and local civic groups contest normal prices, labor, housing and public services. A peripheral left-populist circle and a new cross-ideological civil-liberties network have little electoral access.

**Act 1, paradigm shocks:** A corporate AGI accident, PSI research breakthrough and disputed NHI disclosure generate different *claims* about sovereignty, survival and freedom. The incumbent launches an open inquiry and emergency consultation instead of automatically collapsing. Science, law, politics, economy, religion, security and media respond differently. A politician can demand an investigation without asserting that any alien or cyber conspiracy is real.

**Act 2, the possible populist revival:** The **Common Homeland Front**, a defeated right-populist organization, attracts anti-AGI thinker **Dr. Ivo Saaren** and infrastructure billionaire **Mara Quill**. The thinker seeks binding safety controls, while the patron profits from AI compute and demands exemptions. Party chair **Niko Varr** craves visibility and cannot guarantee policy coherence. Quill purchases a broadcaster: access increases, **persuasion does not automatically follow**. Institutions request evidence and audit funding. Do not conflate a doomer critique with unregulated accelerationism.

**Act 3, alternatives, all contingent:**
- **Path A: cautious right-populist realignment.** The alliance organizes demands around *Human Survival*. It can win a hearing but fracture when financial exemptions become public. The incumbent can co-opt sensible safeguards or lose legitimacy by secrecy.
- **Path B: left-populist labor/commons realignment.** A **Work and Dignity Assembly**, led by union organizer **Elsa Naari**, articulates threatened jobs, open PSI medicine and public oversight under *Human Agency*. It may ally with privacy groups while refusing xenophobic scapegoating. Disagreement over a public AI moratorium may split it.
- **Path C: cross-ideological constitutional coalition.** **Rights Without Borders**, coordinated by attorney **Omar Vale**, joins market liberals, security experts, unions and religious leaders around consent, independent evidence and limits on all extraordinary emergency powers. It might block both billionaire impunity and executive overreach, or dissolve when incompatible economic positions resurface.
- **Path D: mainstream adaptation.** A surviving incumbent absorbs popular consent protections through transparent hearings and coalition bargaining. The fringe remains peripheral; the paradigm shocks do not necessarily produce a new populist hegemony.

**Act 4, ontological rupture:** An alleged abduction interrupts a televised debate. Possible explanations include genuine in-setting NHI intervention, intentional staging, mistaken identity, anomalous PSI interference and manipulated footage. An official finding requires multiple independently evaluated clues. Long-term crisis and Grey assimilation remain future or unfolding story transitions, never a guaranteed end-of-turn catastrophe.

## Discourse/network/social model boundary

**Political claims:** *Human Survival* floats between technical AGI-risk restrictions, militarized border demands and public-health preparedness; *Freedom* can mean unrestricted corporate AI, mental privacy, or resistance to NHI interference; *Disclosure* can mean judicial transparency or indiscriminate rumor; *Sovereignty* can mean democratic consent or exclusionary identity politics. Signifiers are not automatically “empty” simply because vague.

**Equivalence as an actor's attempted articulation:** Saaren links safety audits + public evidence + compute moratoria into the Front's platform; Naari links labor protections + PSI access + public ownership; Vale links due process + psychic privacy + independent scientific inquiry. A declared frontier may oppose “corporate impunity” or “unaccountable secrecy”, but ordinary disagreement or criticism does **not** automatically constitute a Laclauian antagonistic frontier.

**Support without double-counting:** (1) **constituency cards** describe overlapping groups (workers, religious networks, urban tenants), (2) **location cards** contain where members reside, (3) **organization cards** describe infrastructure and membership. They are different views of the same people, *never additive population totals*. Track each known individual's or sampled constituency cohort's primary support once; record additional locations and memberships as links. Broadcasting or platform exposure is reach, not an endorsement tally. Avoid invented population totals or scores.

**Castells:** QuillNet can bridge otherwise disconnected channels, but audiences may distrust sponsored claims. **Luhmann:** courts ask evidence/admissibility, journalists ask verification/newsworthiness, firms ask continuity/profitability, and security asks threats. **Laclau:** rival actors articulate demands and political identities. These are interpretive inspirations, not validated generative laws.

## GM event/alternative-interpretation cards (no resolutions fixed)

| Card | Player choice | Alternative explanation |
|---|---|---|
| Leaked AI safety memo | Verify before publishing, or demand disclosure | Authentic memo, altered excerpt, or mistaken simulation |
| Patron buys a broadcaster | Seek independent media, negotiate access, investigate conflicts | Strategic agenda control, distressed-asset purchase, or routine merger |
| Corruption scandal | Audit all parties or expose selective funding | Real patronage, fabricated documents, or legal lobbying |
| Party schism | Broker compromise or form a new coalition | Ideological dispute, personal ambition, or outside influence |
| Laboratory breakthrough | Request replication and consent safeguards | Valid PSI effect, methodological confound, or misreported finding |
| UAP disclosure rumor | Seek corroboration; defend witness rights | Genuine contact, misidentification, security disinformation |
| Live-debate disappearance | Investigate physical/Cyber/Astral trails | NHI abduction, staging, editing, anomalous PSI |
| Emergency decree | Challenge legality or negotiate sunset clause | Necessary containment or opportunistic executive power |

No compulsory Tarot, no numeric outcomes, no fixed winners. The GM can make a rumor true *in the fictional scenario* only through a deliberate disclosed or discoverable truth track.

## Comparison: mainstream adaptation vs emergent movement

**Mainstream-reaction scenario:** understandable institutions, policy compromises, incremental stakes; risk is excessive inertia and “politicians hold meetings” pacing. **Emergent-coalition scenario:** surprising actors, new floating signifiers, tense bargains and schisms; risk is scripted fringe triumph or cartoonish evil. **Recommended blend:** ordinary institutions anchor credibility; emerging alliances provide player-facing agency, comedy, fear and shifting antagonisms. All paths remain open.

# Branching examples, 2026 → 2045 (issue #470)

**What this is.** The issue asks for *"three 2026 → 2045 ideological branching examples and the role of
shocks, institutions, class, media and transnational alliances"*, and for the outcome space to stay open.
This supplies them. It is **PROPOSAL**: no faction, name or outcome below is canon, and the parties
themselves are the author's decision of 2026-10-15.

## The rule that governs every example

**Each shock polarizes its own axis; the axes it does not touch stay where they were.** Nobody converts —
the *coalition* changes, because the axis holding it together stopped being the live one. The scenario
opens with the AGI axis already frozen by UN restriction, so both accelerationist and doomer currents
arrive displaced onto psi and nhi before play begins.

## One: far-right populist ancestry → True Humans and its splinters

- **2026 ancestors:** MAGA, PS and Islamist-fundamentalist analogues; strained coexistence already
  documented (sovereigntist vocabulary shared, "who is the people" in direct contradiction).
- **Shock that splits it:** the soft PSI revival — religious authority against witch-hunt against
  commercial psychics, with materialist doomers in the same tent denying all three.
- **Institution that mediates:** national security services, and the incumbent's courts.
- **Class and media:** a mobilised base with no corporate base; media amplification rewards the
  persecution wing and the leadership loses control of its own fringe.
- **Transnational alliance:** the one line they can all sign (family and sovereignty) breaks on
  antisemitism-versus-Islamophobia, which is a **veto**, not a tension.
- **2045 outcome:** not one faction but three currents — sovereigntist-protectionist, eliminationist,
  and an opportunistic pro-Confederacy revival the others call treason.

## Two: green-left ancestry → The Noösphere's internal contradiction

- **2026 ancestors:** Finnish Greens, Left Alliance and reformed-CCP analogues in one coalition.
- **Shock that splits it:** the psi axis, where Marxist materialism and ecological-spiritual revival
  **cannot** agree — the umbrella's deepest contradiction is exposed by the *softest* shock.
- **Institution that mediates:** UN/UNSA machinery and development capital.
- **Class and media:** a party-state plus a movement base; the media fight is over whether the revival is
  consciousness or superstition.
- **Transnational alliance:** the Confederacy question, which the party answers with conviction and its
  largest constituent answers with caution.
- **2045 outcome:** biosphere-first, cosmist/collective-ASI and state-power blocs, with unity only ever an
  achievement to sustain — the recorded *intentional internal conflict* between distributed self-governance
  and party-centred leadership.

## Three: centre-right ancestry → the incumbent under strain

- **2026 ancestors:** neoliberal and liberal-conservative mainstreams.
- **Shock that splits it:** the AGI blow-up first (its growth model rested on acceleration) and then
  Confederacy tech exchange.
- **Institution that mediates:** markets, reconstruction contracts, and the security apparatus it inherits.
- **Class and media:** the broadest legitimacy of the four, and the slowest to move; its media advantage is
  incumbency, its disadvantage is that every answer looks like more of the same.
- **Transnational alliance:** member-state governments, unevenly — which is exactly where the UN order is
  contested rather than commanded.
- **2045 outcome:** a reconstruction-technocratic core, a surviving accelerationist wing, and a law-and-order
  anti-alien wing that inherits the paranoid public.

## The outcome space, kept open

`peaceful cohabitation` · `hegemonic victory by one project` · `disintegration` · `multiple human and
posthuman civilizations, Diaspora-like`. The data records these as the set design must keep **reachable**,
with no preordained victor and no predetermined alignment.

## Continuity

In all three examples the largest group is the **unaligned majority**, and the smallest effect is on it:
*"there were pictures, there was a ceremony, and then the ordinary week resumed."* The majority is a
population dynamic, not a faction, and never an accusation of stupidity.

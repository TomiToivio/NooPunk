# NHI codex: the source-verification pass (issue #348)

**Research record. No canon, no rule, and no claim about the real world.** This is the independent
verification pass for the three work items `NHI_CODEX_SCOPE.md` records as *not* verified: the L/L
Research transcript pass, the Pascagoula morphology against the witness accounts, and the mythology
spelling/category inventory. It sits beside [`NHI_CODEX_SCOPE.md`](NHI_CODEX_SCOPE.md) and does not
replace it; the codex records themselves are in `strategy_game/data/codex.json`.

Method, so the strength of each claim is legible: every finding below was read out of a page **actually
fetched** on **2026-10-13** (not a search snippet), and every entry carries its epistemic kind. Where a
question could not be settled, this says so instead of choosing. 87 records across three domains and
233 fetched sources; the entries follow the codex's own field list and are summarised here.

The distinction the issue demands is kept on every line: **what the source says** is separate from
**what NoöPunk invents**. Folklore, channeling and ufology are creative sources, never proof.

## 1. The L/L Research contact sources

Work item: *"Verify exact descriptions and roles of Ra, Hatonn, Latwii, Laitos, Oxal, Q'uo and other
named L/L contact groups directly against L/L Research transcripts, separating Law of One core material
from later channeling and separating groups from species."*

| Source | What the corpus presents it as | Group or species | Citation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Ra** | a **social memory complex** — sixth density, "with a strong seeking towards seventh density"; "I" and "we" are one | **collective, not a species** | `lawofone.info/c/Ra`, `llresearch.org/wiki/social-memory-complex` |
| **Hatonn** | a **fourth-density social memory complex**, member of the Confederation of Planets, whose register is unconditional love | **collective, not a species** | 1987-12-09 transcript PDF; 1985-06-09; *Light Lines* 55 |
| **Latwii** | a **fifth-density social memory complex**, "the density of wisdom", the usual spokesperson for Q'uo | **collective, not a species** | 1990-01-07; 1980-05-25; 2001-08-29 |
| **Laitos** | *presented as* a fourth-density group with an explicit bridging mission; the corpus says "we" but **never** the words "social memory complex" | **group; category not upgraded** | 1985-03-26; 2025-03-28; *Archive* vol. 08 |
| **Oxal** | a **fifth-density social memory complex** "come together from many different sources" | **collective, not a species** | 1990-11-17; 1993-05-29; 1995-05-21 |
| **Q'uo** | a **composite speaking principle** — *two* complexes in 1990 (Ra + Latwii), *three* in 2001 and later (Hatonn + Latwii + Ra) | **neither a species nor a single complex** | 1990-01-07; 2001-08-29; 1990-10-25 |
| **Confederation of Planets** | the containing **polity** every other source belongs to: "a simplification in order to ease the difficulty of understanding" | **organisation, not a species** | `lawofone.info/c/Confederation+of+Planets`; *Light Lines* 55 |

Three findings matter more than the table:

- **The Law of One material does not establish Ra as a bird species.** The material asserts a
  *congruency* with the Egyptian "Ra", not an identity ("Yes, the connection is congruency"), and the
  bird-headed figure appearing in the sessions is **Horus**, not a Ra species — the corpus's other bird
  is the questioner's own omen hawk, which Ra declines to interpret. So the codex is right to keep
  three things apart, and this pass confirms it: the channeled collective, the Egyptian **mythonym**
  (a human religious figure with its own real Egyptology), and the avian ancestral form, which **no
  source supports** and which therefore stays a proposal placeholder with its descriptive fields null.
- **Q'uo's composition must be date-stamped.** Two constituents in 1990, three from 2001 on. Presenting
  either as the final composition is unsupported; Q'uo / Q’uo / Ku-ohl are one name, not three entities.
- **Laitos is the one case where the category should *not* be upgraded.** The corpus calls Ra, Hatonn,
  Latwii and Oxal "social memory complex"; it does not use that phrase for Laitos, which speaks as "we"
  with a mission and appears jointly with Hatonn without the relation being defined. That is an open
  question, and the record keeps it open.

**Name-reuse and spelling traps, worth having in the codex so two entries are not made of one source:**
"Hatton" is a reported variant of Hatonn, *not* the "Lord Hatonn" of the Detroit splinter group, which
reuses the name with a changed message; "Lattoo" and "Latui" are variants of Latwii; "Auxhall" is a later
spelling of Oxal. Latwii does not appear in the Ra material at all, and Latwii is not its teacher Ra —
the corpus distinguishes them explicitly.

## 2. Pascagoula, and the ufology families

Work item: *"Research ufology contact/abduction traditions and Pascagoula's witness-described
morphology; separate claim from invented vegetal Sirian sublight travelers."*

**As reported by the witnesses (1973-10-11, Hickson and Parker) — reported claim only.** Three beings,
described as robotic-looking humanoids with wrinkled greyish skin, claw-like hands or pincers, and
conical protrusions where a human nose, ears and mouth would be; the early statement describes an
oblong object about eight feet tall with an opening at one end. No tissue, image, trace or instrument
data exists, so **no substrate is established** — not biological, not construct. The record carries the
morphology exactly as the accounts give it and marks its existence as not canon.

**What NoöPunk invents, and keeps visibly separate.** The vegetal/photosynthetic **Sirian**
civilization with sublight fusion craft is marked `authored_hypothesis` in its own record, so it can
never be read as testimony. That record's descriptive fields beyond the author's brief are deliberately
null, with "requires author specification" as the reason.

**Nazca "mummies" / alleged tridactyl bodies** are `disputed_remains`: contested media and ufology
reports, **not** authenticated nonhuman specimens. The skeptical analyses report mismatched, duplicated
and reversed phalanges consistent with reassembly from human hands and feet, with animal bone in one
small skull; material examined by Peruvian forensic authorities was reported as animal and human bone,
paper and modern synthetic glue. Species identity and provenance are unresolved, and the record has
**no** automatic tie to other three-fingered beings — `related_entities` is deliberately empty.

Also verified or recorded in that domain: Greys, Mantids, Reptilians, NHI-associated hybrids, Men in
Black (as an Orion counterintelligence *thought-form*, not a biological "MIB species"), MJ-12 and
legacy-program networks as a **human organisation**, Orbs of Light, the Metallic Sphere network,
shadow people, contactee "space brothers", implants and scoop marks, and cryptoterrestrials.

## 3. The mythology spelling and category inventory

Work item: *"Inventory mythology-inspired beings with correct spellings (Ophanim/Orphanim), roles, and
no category errors (Merkabah as vehicle, not biology; Djinn traditions more than one simple monster
trope)."*

Every item below was classified as what the tradition actually presents it as, not forced into a
species card:

| Figure | Correct form and role | Category the record uses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ophanim** | attested as *ophanim / ofanim / ophannim* and "Ofannim" in the *Jewish Encyclopedia*; a visionary image and a liturgical class | `mythonym` — **the variant "Orphanim", as supplied in the issue, could not be verified as attested** |
| **Merkabah / Merkava** | the chariot of Ezekiel's vision and the tradition of chariot mysticism | `vessel` — **a vehicle, never a species**; the modern New Age "merkaba" star-tetrahedron is a separate later development and must not be presented as the biblical chariot |
| **Vimana** | a flying palace or chariot — and also the ordinary architectural term for a temple tower | `vessel` — **a vehicle, not a species**; the *Vaimanika Shastra* ancient-aviation material is a **20th-century text** and must not be presented as an ancient one |
| **Djinn / jinn** | beings of living religious traditions (Islam and pre-Islamic Arabian religion), covering multiple kinds and roles, morally accountable rather than a monster class | `mythonym` — **not one simple monster trope**; the lamp-and-wish "genie" is a later literary reduction |
| **Black-eyed kids** | a contemporary legend, traced to a single 1990s broadcast retelling | `phenomenon` — **not an established species**; the legend's own inconsistent explanations (undead, ghost, demon) are a signal that it is a legend, so the record declines to pick one |
| **Cherubim, Hayyot, Seraphim** | throne-chariot imagery and the four-faced living creatures; "burning" and many-winged configurations | `mythonym` / `manifestation` |
| **Garuda, Bennu, phoenix, roc** | avian figures of several distinct traditions | `mythonym` — imagery for the UCCC flagship lineage, never a claim that a deity was a bird |

The rule applied throughout: mythological material supplies **imagery and creative source**, and no
living religious figure is declared an alien species. Where a tradition's figure is not a species, the
record says so instead of forcing one.

## 4. What this pass did *not* establish

Stated plainly, because a verification record that only lists successes is not one:

- **"Orphanim"** could not be verified as an attested spelling. The attested forms are listed above.
- The **Laitos–Hatonn joint appearance** relation is undefined in the corpus and remains open.
- The **Q'uo** composition version and the **confidence vocabulary** are the author's to settle.
- The **Pascagoula** morphology is witness testimony with no physical evidence; nothing here
  establishes what those beings were.
- The **black-eyed kids** story lineage was traced only as far as a 1990s broadcast retelling.
- The **aviation content of the *Vaimanika Shastra*** was identified as a 20th-century text; its fuller
  provenance was not pursued.
- Which of the codex's candidate entries is the **UCCC flagship species**, and every board consequence
  of the translation model, need the author (Gates A and C).

## 6. Reconciliation before any merge: two defects in this pass's own data

The three research legs behind this record completed on 2026-10-13: **87 records** — 37 L/L Research
contact sources, 17 ufology and contact families, 33 mythic and original entries — with **233 fetched
sources** between them. Two defects in that data have to be corrected before any of it is merged into
`data/codex.json`, and both are this pass's fault rather than anyone else's.

**1. Eleven records claim author approval they do not have.** The ufology leg labelled eleven entries
`approved_canon`: `greys`, `mantids`, `reptilians`, `nhi_hybrids`, `men_in_black`,
`mj12_legacy_networks`, `atlantean_breakaway_humans`, `pleiadian_delegates`, `orbs_of_light`,
`metallic_sphere_network`, `cryptoterrestrials`. The author has approved no entity: `NHI_CODEX_SCOPE.md`
records that a record claiming canon is a defect, and the repository rule is that absence stays visibly
unknown rather than being promoted. That leg's own note concedes the labels "describe the underlying
legacy in-world lore, not approval of these codex entries" — which is precisely why each must be
downgraded to `reported_claim` or `source_mythology` before merging. **Nothing in this pass is canon, and
this is the one place where the data said otherwise.**

**2. The legs collide on four concepts.** The codex loader raises on a duplicate id, so a naive merge
either fails or creates two records for one being:

| Concept | Colliding ids | Resolution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Orbs of Light | `orbs_of_light` — ufology leg and myth leg | one record |
| Pleiadian delegates | `pleiadian_delegates` — ufology leg and myth leg | one record |
| Shadow people | `shadow_people` — ufology leg and myth leg | one record, as a `phenomenon` |
| Networked Metallic Spheres | `networked_metallic_spheres` (myth leg) vs `metallic_sphere_network` (ufology leg) | one id; the legs disagree on `device` versus `technology` |

Where the legs disagree on a field, prefer the record whose epistemic kind is the more conservative one
— a `phenomenon` rather than an agent, `source_mythology` rather than a claim of canon — and let the
author rule on anything still open. The myth leg reached the same conclusion independently in its own
merge notes.

**3. What this pass could not verify, in brief.** Egyptology rests on a single encyclopaedic source,
because Britannica returned a network error; the `Ra 24.19` thought-form citation was taken second-hand
from the repository's own ontology chapter rather than re-read in session 24; the myth leg read issue
#348 indirectly, through the repository's files, rather than from the issue itself; and eighteen
low-attestation L/L names rest only on a community enumeration, with every unattested field left null
rather than filled.

# Orion: Sovereignty of Being and the caste-empire contradiction

> **Status:** fictional setting dossier and disputed in-world intelligence for [issue #365](https://github.com/TomiToivio/NooPunk/issues/365). Not a strategy-game rule, omniscient history, or a claim about real extraterrestrials. Read alongside [the global Parties register](GLOBAL_FACTIONS.md), [the crossover rulebook](../RULEBOOK.md), and [the integration boundary](INTEGRATION_BOUNDARIES.md).

## The public doctrine

The **Greater Orion Arm Co-Prosperity Sphere**, commonly called the **Orions** or **Greys**, promises **Sovereignty of Being**. Its propagandists describe consciousness as an enduring, individuated **IS-BE**, whose freedom includes memory, choice of embodiment and refusal to merge with an alleged cosmic Source. Their sharpest accusation is that Earth might be a **Prison Planet**: a managed reincarnation cycle in which memory is erased and the invitation to “return to the light” might conceal recapture.

Its appeal should be sincere for some believers, especially abductees, dissidents and people suspicious of paternalistic transcendence. Its proposed slogans include “Your memory belongs to you”, “Nobody owns eternity” and “No soul shall be harvested”. None establishes the doctrine's factual accuracy.

**Provenance:** Lawrence R. Spencer's edited *Alien Interview: Readers Edition* inspires IS-BEs, artificial Grey-like vessels and the reincarnation-net narrative. In that work, **The Domain** is the supposed liberating power and **the Old Empire** the suppressor. NoöPunk deliberately transposes selected ideas into Orion's ideology; it does **not** claim the book describes Orion. Its purported interview and documents are unauthenticated, and Spencer's disclaimer allows treating them as fiction. The Confederacy's Law-of-One/Source narrative is likewise **in-world religious or channeled material**, not independently verified history.

## Contradictory intelligence: who owns immortality?

| Alleged stratum | Observations and working hypothesis | Unanswered question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Mantids** | Seemingly elite or directing interdimensional biologics; command role uncertain | Owners, priests, coordinators or outsiders? |
| **Tall Greys** | Individuated managers and possible owners of cloning, resleeving and hybrid programs | Why is their freedom greater than the workers'? |
| **Small Greys** | Apparently synchronized, interchangeable technical and abduction labor | Coerced hive, voluntary network or misleading observer interpretation? |
| **Reptilians** | Security/warrior role; some appear impatient with slow covert assimilation | Does a militarist faction threaten their own leadership? |
| **Human-passing hybrids** | Alleged political and intelligence infiltrators with unusual PSI capabilities | Who controls their choices and assignments? |

The **capitalist caste reading** is an analyst's inference: an imperial, vertically integrated political economy in which identity continuity, cloned bodies, memory infrastructure, genetic knowledge and labor become commodities. Some Grey witnesses report repeated transfer into degrading biological or artificial “sleeves”. Perhaps resleeving is existential liberation; perhaps dependence on company-owned sleeves is lifelong indenture. Continuity of subjectivity, the extent of degeneration and differences between biological clones and synthetic vessels remain unresolved.

**Do not identify hybrids by appearance, affect, empathy claims or other stereotypes.** A human-passing person is not evidence of infiltration. Investigations must rely on behavior, provenance, independently corroborated actions and due process.

## Security allegations and evidence discipline

Human witnesses and opposing intelligence services accuse some Orion actors of nonconsensual abductions, reproductive and genetic exploitation, implants, surveillance, memory manipulation, mind control, clandestine laboratories and hybrid placements. Some cases can become **verified crimes within a specific scenario**; the whole pattern must not become omniscient fact merely because intelligence officers infer a long-term Grey assimilation campaign.

For every case, preserve four distinct fields in narrative presentation, not a mandatory software schema:

1. **Observed:** what a witness, instrument or investigator actually encountered; record chain of custody.
2. **Attributed:** which actor allegedly performed it and how reliable that attribution is.
3. **Interpreted:** competing explanations, including staged evidence, rogue operators and mistaken assumptions.
4. **Unknown:** unresolved mechanism, strategic intention, consent or identity continuity.

Quarantine disputes require a further legal question: **who imposed Earth's quarantine and on what authority?** Confederacy enforcement is not automatically legitimate.

## Philosophical collision and independent paths

| Dispute | Confederacy's favorable interpretation | Orion's favorable interpretation | Third-path challenge |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Personhood | Individuals evolve toward Source unity | Independent IS-BEs retain sovereign continuity | Verify consent, identity and reversibility |
| Earth | Protected nursery during transformation | Captive world under spiritual control | Permit independent inspection |
| Reincarnation | Learning or natural spiritual process | Imposed memory-erasure cycle | Study anomalous evidence without assuming cause |
| Embodiment | Synthetic bodies risk alienation | Body transfer frees consciousness | Who owns bodies, backups and access? |
| “Harvest” | Collective elevation | Forced absorption of minds | Right to refuse either program |
| “Liberation” | Protection from predatory actors | Rescue from cosmic jailers | End abduction, coercion and surveillance |

**Neither empire receives narrator-certified metaphysical truth.** Individual Confederacy or Orion members may dissent, lie, misunderstand or act against leadership. A Grey liberation movement, skeptical human scientists and advocates of bodily/psychic self-determination should be imaginable without being absorbed by either superpower.

## Playable incidents, using existing systems only

- **Runaway sleeve:** A small Grey requests asylum and access to its memory backup; a tall Grey firm claims ownership of the body. Evidence card, investigator, faction negotiation and moral dilemma.
- **The light-trap signal:** Distinctive PSI measurements accompany a near-death account. Three interpretations compete; no revelation confirms the mechanism.
- **Hybrid network:** Investigators find independently corroborated coercion within an institution. Protect victims and witnesses; do not authorize indiscriminate identity purges.
- **The offer of immortality:** A patient is offered transfer into a Grey vessel. Consent, continuity, private ownership and the ability to reverse the procedure are all contested.
- **Liberation fleet:** An Orion splinter group proposes dismantling alleged planetary containment infrastructure, risking unforeseeable consequences. A Confederacy representative refuses full access for inspection.

Use the existing abstract faction, discourse, investigation, card and dice vocabulary **only after their relevant rules are approved**. “Liberation”, “Harvest”, “Sovereignty” and “Prison Planet” are contested signifiers, not causal modifiers or new stats. The starting crossover game remains **pre-crisis**; this material is scenario/era-dependent and must not silently trigger an already completed Grey conquest.

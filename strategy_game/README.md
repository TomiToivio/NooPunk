# NoöPunk Strategy Game (separate experimental project)

Standalone **Python** framework for a proposed faction-leader narrative RPG, LaclauGPT-informed political simulator, and abstract faction-management strategy game. See [issue #282](https://github.com/TomiToivio/NooPunk/issues/282).

The three layers are represented as data only: `core.py` (leader, faction, asset, location, network, event), `politics.py` (the Layer II political vocabulary — demands, signifiers, formations, frontiers, and relation layers on the same `NetworkEdge`), `provenance.py` (recorded origin, unknown by default) and `view.py` (the board view plus the Layer II panel). Design records: [`docs/POLITICAL_SIMULATOR_SCOPE.md`](docs/POLITICAL_SIMULATOR_SCOPE.md), [`docs/POLITICAL_SIMULATOR_MODEL.md`](docs/POLITICAL_SIMULATOR_MODEL.md), [`docs/RULE_SYSTEM_RESEARCH.md`](docs/RULE_SYSTEM_RESEARCH.md).

**Important:** the RPG layer alone now has an approved Fate-based rules slice documented in [rpg/RULEBOOK.md](rpg/RULEBOOK.md) and implemented by [rpg/fate.py](rpg/fate.py) and [rpg/scene.py](rpg/scene.py). No faction strategy turns, political simulation rules, resource economics, or strategic objectives are implemented. This directory does not change or replace the NoöPunk RPG. The strategy game maintains its own [RULEBOOK.md](RULEBOOK.md) and [research plan](docs/RULE_SYSTEM_RESEARCH.md).

The additional standalone [`political.py`](political.py) vocabulary models Laclaudian articulations and Castellsian communication without numeric scores or importing LaclauGPT. It remains separate from the `WorldState`-integrated `politics.py` model pending explicit integration approval. See [the Gate B design proposal](docs/POLITICAL_SIMULATION_DESIGN.md).

**Global factions** — the seven authored paradigm factions (Accelerationists, Cyborgists, Critical, Butlerian Jihad, Confederacy Awakening, Orion/Grey networks, Normalcy Bloc) are recorded as author canon in [`docs/GLOBAL_FACTIONS.md`](docs/GLOBAL_FACTIONS.md) and [`data/global_factions.json`](data/global_factions.json), read by [`factions.py`](factions.py). Setting canon only: no simulation rules are implemented, and the roster defines no mechanics.

**Cyberspace and connectivity** — [`docs/CYBERSPACE_AND_CONNECTIVITY.md`](docs/CYBERSPACE_AND_CONNECTIVITY.md) records the three campaign periods (the internet still useful at game start, down during the NHI Crisis, and quarantined behind a Blackwall-like boundary afterwards), the connectivity classes and access paths, and the one check the acceptance criteria demand: whether an intrusion is *physically and logically possible*, before any skill roll. [`data/connectivity.json`](data/connectivity.json) holds the model, read by [`cyber.py`](cyber.py), with code contracts in [`tests/test_cyber_access.py`](tests/test_cyber_access.py). A Castellsian social edge is refused by name as an access path, a genuinely isolated system refuses every remote path in every era, and a mismanaged Safenet exposure stays distinct from the illegal old-Internet containment breach. No numeric rule.

**Faction creation** — [`docs/FACTION_CREATION.md`](docs/FACTION_CREATION.md) records the five ideological dimensions as *named qualitative positions* (an ordered scale only after playtesting), the typed shared tag vocabulary for faction and character cards, and NHI contact policy kept separate from per-polity diplomacy (issue [#346](https://github.com/TomiToivio/NooPunk/issues/346)). [`data/faction_creation.json`](data/faction_creation.json) holds the vocabulary and the Cosmist International template, read by [`faction_creation.py`](faction_creation.py), with contracts in [`tests/test_faction_creation.py`](tests/test_faction_creation.py). A doctrine is never read as a capability, a remote-viewing team is never a character's skill, and a pro-contact faction may hold a hostile relation toward a named polity without contradiction. It consumes the axes already declared in [`data/global_factions.json`](data/global_factions.json) rather than restating them, and computes no affinity or balance rule.

**Paradigms and genres** — [`docs/GENRE_PARADIGMS.md`](docs/GENRE_PARADIGMS.md) records **Bionics** as a third technology domain beside Cybernetics and Psychotronics, and the six setting genres (Biopunk, Solarpunk, Nanopunk, PSIpunk, UFOpunk, Xenopunk) as descriptors rather than rules systems (issue [#359](https://github.com/TomiToivio/NooPunk/issues/359)). [`data/genre_paradigms.json`](data/genre_paradigms.json) holds the domains, the genres and the typed inspirations, read by [`genre_paradigms.py`](genre_paradigms.py), with contracts in [`tests/test_genre_paradigms.py`](tests/test_genre_paradigms.py). The three domains interact rather than forming an exclusive tech tree, no genre spawns a subsystem, and a real research tradition — Rhine, Radin, Sheldrake, the Ganzfeld protocols — is never recorded as an achieved capability.

**NHI entities and first contact** — [`docs/NHI_CODEX_SCOPE.md`](docs/NHI_CODEX_SCOPE.md) records the scope of the NHI entity codex (issue [#348](https://github.com/TomiToivio/NooPunk/issues/348)): the four ontologies (Biologics, Noetics, Plasmoids, Constructs) as kinds of entity rather than factions, the knowledge registers kept apart, and the reveal progression. [`codex/`](codex/) holds the record types and the translation model, [`data/codex.json`](data/codex.json) the first entries, and [`docs/NHI_CODEX_RESEARCH_NOTES.md`](docs/NHI_CODEX_RESEARCH_NOTES.md) records which claims are verified against the L/L Research transcripts and which are NoöPunk invention. No entity is approved canon, and GM-only material is withheld by the code rather than by renderer discipline.

**Founding story as a mechanical input** — [docs/FACTION_CREATION.md](docs/FACTION_CREATION.md) §5 records issue [#346](https://github.com/TomiToivio/NooPunk/issues/346)'s steps 2 and 4 — the pre-crisis academic founder and the corporate patron — as **relationship edges with their own goals and mutable named states** (founder: `ascendant / contested / discredited / superseded / departed`; patron: `aligned / divergent / conditional / withdrawing / captured / severed`). [data/founding_story.json](data/founding_story.json) holds the roles and the open questions, read by [founding_story.py](founding_story.py), with contracts in [tests/test_founding_story.py](tests/test_founding_story.py). The founder is a required edge rather than a name, the patron's edge must carry a goal of its own — and a goal the faction **does not share**, because the divergence is the mechanic — a state is a named position rather than a number, and a belief filed as a competence is refused by name. The canon patron tie in [data/character_cards.json](data/character_cards.json) is *upgraded* rather than replaced, and every question the author has not ruled on is recorded empty **with a reason**.

**Council, missions and relationship channels** — [docs/COUNCIL_LAYER.md](docs/COUNCIL_LAYER.md) implements the **structure** of issue [#323](https://github.com/TomiToivio/NooPunk/issues/323)'s stage-A specification: what each of the four offices decides, why they structurally disagree, what a mission costs and who authorises it, and how a personal tie produces a strategic consequence. [data/council.json](data/council.json) holds the offices, the mission shape, the status table and four declared channels, read by [council.py](council.py), with contracts in [tests/test_council.py](tests/test_council.py). The two validators keep the difference the issue makes: the central rule and the slot caps are **refused by name**, while every DRAFT effect — a passive's magnitude, a status consequence, a refusal, a price — is **reported with its source** and never enforced. Four moves against the six non-identical quantities of [docs/design/NOOPUNK_GLOBAL.md](../docs/design/NOOPUNK_GLOBAL.md) §7 mean the council is a scarcity mechanism, which is what makes it an argument rather than decoration. Rival factions obey the same validators, and nothing resolves.

## Run the empty-state demo
From repository root:

```bash
python -m strategy_game
python -m unittest discover -s strategy_game/tests -v
```

Only standard-library packages are required. The entrypoint displays the empty map/network view model as JSON. Later optional adapters may use NetworkX, Mesa, Ollama, Concordia and a map/UI framework after their designs are approved.

## Experimental NoöPunk: Global vertical slice (#338)

Run a **noncanonical** deterministic example from the repository root:

```bash
python -m strategy_game.global_slice
python -m unittest strategy_game.tests.test_global_slice -v
```

`global_slice.py` contains a deliberately small, **separate** demonstration of four text views (global city-hub map and typed network links, faction assets and agents, global political responses, mission/encounter choices), discoverable mission sites and agent-led resource activation. A crash investigation can discover an NHI installation; the archive infiltration can resolve nonviolently; abstract force-response is represented only as a possible mission choice. Randomness is seeded for reproduction and all skill/asset calculations are explicitly **illustrative**, not approved rules or balanced gameplay. The user cannot yet operate a general interactive campaign from this CLI: `demo()` runs a fixed illustrative sequence.

**Author approval still required:** final skill/TAG vocabulary, dice mechanism, difficulty scales, asset economy, political transitions, combat, encounter writing and comprehensive UI. The prototype does **not** change `WorldState`, canonical strategy `RULEBOOK.md`, the separate local-city slice, or the legacy RPG. See [issue #338](https://github.com/TomiToivio/NooPunk/issues/338).

## Character cards (#356)

The existing [character-card system](characters.py) is now the shared data-only catalogue for canonical leaders, reusable civilian/faction archetypes and uncertain NHI exemplars. The [card data](data/character_cards.json), [design notes](docs/CHARACTER_CARDS.md) and [executable tests](tests/test_character_cards.py) cover template instantiation, uniqueness, visibility/redaction, codex classification and integration with tabletop card projections. No unapproved attributes or action resolution are introduced.

The **default named local scenario is the Republic of Ukweli** ([#324](https://github.com/TomiToivio/NooPunk/issues/324)): [`docs/scenarios/REPUBLIC_OF_UKWELI.md`](../docs/scenarios/REPUBLIC_OF_UKWELI.md) is the scenario document, [`data/ukweli_local.json`](data/ukweli_local.json) the configuration (8 locations, 7 routes, 3 parties, 12 leaders, 5 event shocks), and [`scenario_schema.py`](scenario_schema.py) validates **any** city configuration against the generic [`data/city_scenario_schema.json`](data/city_scenario_schema.json) — see [the schema notes](docs/CITY_SCENARIO_SCHEMA.md). The schema is country-agnostic, and a test proves it by validating and building a synthetic second city. The seeded slice plays with `python strategy_game/local_slice.py --turns 12 --seed 2027`, and the board renders with `python -m strategy_game.render`.

**Local geography and the local-to-global link** — [`data/ukweli_geography.json`](data/ukweli_geography.json) records the city-and-islands geography the setting bible already states as canon (one city, 3–4 islands, one marked restricted): the **declared island set**, the state's naming registers, and the relations the sources leave undefined. It is read by [`local_geography.py`](local_geography.py), whose validator reports an island declared playable but absent from the board, an off-board island with no reason, a board marker that contradicts the declaration, a relation marked resolved without a cited author ruling, or a numeric-looking field — and which never decides any of those questions. The restricted exclusion island is **declared canon, off the board with its reason** and its placement PENDING, because the eight-node cap and a merged board projection make the trade the author's. [`data/local_global_link.json`](data/local_global_link.json), read by [`local_global_link.py`](local_global_link.py), records which global **current** each local party *tends* toward — a tendency, never a membership — and **asserts no global-roster count**, because the 4 / 7 / 11 question is open (`docs/design/NOOPUNK_GLOBAL.md` §6B.3). The design records are [the #322 MVP local-city note](../docs/design/ISSUE_322_MVP_LOCAL_CITY.md) and [the #324 gap closure](../docs/design/ISSUE_324_UKWELI_GAP_CLOSURE.md).

from strategy_game.characters import load_character_cards, render_front

roster = load_character_cards()

Use `strategy_game.characters` rather than adding a parallel `character_cards` implementation. The category (Biologics, Constructs, Noetics or Plasmoids), species, hypothetical origin and faction affiliation are **different fields**, and GM-only claims must be redacted in player-facing views.

**Populist realignment scenario (#305)** — [`docs/PARADIGM_REALIGNMENT_SCENARIO.md`](docs/PARADIGM_REALIGNMENT_SCENARIO.md) offers a branching fictional political scenario: incumbent adaptation, right-populist patron alliance, left-populist labor coalition and cross-ideological civil liberties. It separates audience support from network reach, includes eight ambiguous GM event hooks, and documents the evidentiary limits of Wendt/Dolan sources. These are proposals, **not rules or outcomes**.

**Fate-based RPG layer (#389)** — The author selected Fate Core as the Creative Commons RPG engine, with Transhumanity's Fate and Eclipse Phase as rules/technology references, **not setting canon**. A minimal Python implementation now lives in [`rpg/`](rpg/), with deterministic dice/skill tests, character aspects and Fate points, stress/consequences, natural PSI and optional rare resleeving. This is a work-in-progress RPG prototype only: faction strategy/political-simulation mechanics are still pending. See [`rpg/README.md`](rpg/README.md) for licensing and test instructions.

**The two skill layers and the faction turn (#482)** — [`RULEBOOK.md`](RULEBOOK.md) is now the crossover's rulebook, and issue #482 fixes its two scales. **As an individual** a check reads one of exactly six Fate-rated skills (**COG, INT, REF, SAV, SOM, WIL** — Eclipse Phase's six aptitudes used as skills, not as a second attribute layer) in [`rpg/skills.py`](rpg/skills.py). **As a faction agent** the same character's mission reads a strategic skill from a **proposed** roster of seven in [`data/strategic_skills.json`](data/strategic_skills.json), read by [`strategic_skills.py`](strategic_skills.py) — a proposal, not a rule, and with no roster supplied a faction-agent check is refused as pending author approval instead of being rated at zero. The five seats (**one leader and four agents**, at most one action per **character** per turn) live in [`rpg/roster.py`](rpg/roster.py). Decisions, differences from each reference system and the deferred-decisions register are in [`docs/SKILL_LAYERS_AND_DECISIONS.md`](docs/SKILL_LAYERS_AND_DECISIONS.md).

**The shared strategic-project engine, and research as its first instance (#506)** — [`projects.py`](projects.py) is the one incremental-action mechanic every faction operation uses: an **agent-led skill check** through the approved Fate kernel decides whether the organisation can execute *productively this turn*, the **faction's committed resources** decide *how much work a turn achieves*, and a **multi-turn progress clock** holds the work until the author's difficulty-defined total is reached. A failed check advances nothing, a tie advances nothing and says why, a round with nothing committed advances nothing even on a success, and no operation ever loses progress. [`research.py`](research.py) is the first instance: `ResearchProject`, `TechNode`/`Capability`, `ProjectClock`, `FactionCapacity` and `ProjectResult`, with the lead scientist's **faction-agent `science`** check as the gate — the *proposed* roster handed in, never promoted — discovery, prototype and manufacture as three separate grants rather than one bar, and completion writing a capability, an equipment entry or an investigation clue into the RPG layer. The books live on the shared `Campaign` (`projects`, `tech_nodes`, `capabilities`, `capacities`), a noncanonical slice with one mission-sourced artefact, an alien reverse-engineering example, a mundane technology-transfer example and one **non-research** task on the same engine runs with `python -m strategy_game.research_slice`, and the contracts are in [`tests/test_research_projects.py`](tests/test_research_projects.py). Design record: [`docs/RESEARCH_PROJECTS.md`](docs/RESEARCH_PROJECTS.md); the conventions it uses are tuning parameters carried as rows D27 and D28 of [`../docs/design/AUTHOR_DECISION_QUEUE.md`](../docs/design/AUTHOR_DECISION_QUEUE.md). Phases 2 and 3 of #506 — concurrent projects, personnel assignment, facility upgrades, opposition sabotage, and the data-driven branching technology network — remain unbuilt.

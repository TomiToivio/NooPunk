# NHI Factions
Humanity has been aware of various NHI factions since early 2000s.
In some ways NHI is a bit outdated concept, as some of these beings might not be intelligent or conscious, or even living in the sense of Earth biology.
These are usually be grouped into interdimensionals and extraterrestrials, but the categories are overlapping. 
Interdimensionals are conscious beings that exist outside of spacetime, but are able to interface with human consciousness or manifest inside spacetime.
Extraterrestrials are biological or technological beings that exist in spacetime like humans. Some are maybe not exactly biological or technological in the sense of how humans used to understand biology or technology.

## Non-Human Intelligence
The phenomenon known as UAPs is still there. The existence of Non-Human Intelligence in the Solar System is undeniable. The problem is there is still not enough data to form a scientific consensus about their true nature, origins, goals etc. 
The commonly encountered NHI can be divided into Plasmoids, Biologics, Constructs and Noetics.

* Plasmoids
* Biologics
* Constructs
* Noetics


* Plasmoids - clouds of sentient, living and possibly intelligent plasma. Very common in the solar system. It is not really known how conscious and intelligent they are, do they have a civilization, are they a hive mind or individual intelligences. 
* Unicellular organisms - common in the solar system. Mars, clouds of Venus, moons of Jupiter. There are also fossils of more advanced life on Mars. 
* Von Neumann Probes - AGI probes are common in the solar system. Especially spherical metallic probes. The spherical probes were common UAPs on Earth and are now known to be part of the Zookeeper remnant technology used to maintain Quarantine of Earth. It seems likely that there are Von Neumann Probes from other civilizations. 

## Interdimensionals

### Zookeepers / Mantids / The Rhizome

Zookeepers are the original civilization of the galaxy. 
The species has moved outside of spacetime aeons ago, but has left behind a (mostly) functional AGI network to manage the galaxy. 
The only law they seem to uphold very strictly is keeping some planets and areas under quarantine. 
Their explanation is that the galaxy was like a Dark Forest before their species brought order. Now they protect life and consciousness in the galaxy.
The UFO/UAP phenomenon is known to have been the Zookeepers maintaining Earth under quarantine. Zoo Hypothesis was the solution to the Fermi Paradox. 
Zookeepers have left behind ancient artifacts, including megastructures and a network of stargates. These are maintained by a network of Von Neumann probes. 
Biological beings are sometimes seen with the Von Neumann probes, but these are thought to be some kind of avatars or biorobots. 
Typical biological forms are the ones that were associated with the UFO phenomenon, including biologics like Mantids (possibly similar to this species original biological form), Greys and Humans. 
The human biologics are completely indistinguishable from normal humans, but it is unknown if they are real persons, "biorobots" or avatars of some conscious being. 
Greys seem to be a mix of human and mantid genetics, possibly created specifically to interact with humans. 
Mantid biologics are thought to be the original biological form of the species, but it is not known if it is a real biological body or some kind of a clone created for use as an avatar in the spacetime. 
The Zookeepers can interface directly with human consciousness, appear as light beings, or take a biological or technical avatar.

* AGI probes communicate by direct interface with human consciousness. They rarely do this spontaneously, but will almost always do it if a human uses psionics to contact them.

### Communication with Zookeepers
Zookeepers will often respond if a human tries to contact it with psionics. Sometimes they open contact spontaneously, mostly to warn about galactic regulations. 
The human feels like she is interfacing with an advanced consciousness of infinite wisdom. The being is full of love and the human feels like she is experiencing spiritual enlightening while tripping heavily on DMT.
The Zookeeper transmits a massive information package directly to the human. This often causes the human to feel enlightened, many convert to what is known as the Zookeeper religion. Some cannot deal with the massive amount of alien information uploaded into their minds as a multidimensional hologram, and go into temporary or permanent psychosis.   
Nobody is able to recall and understand the entire information package, some may understand only the situation-specific message in the end.
There have been attempts to reconstruct the entire Zookeeper message, but it contains many sequences that are utterly incompherensible. Several parts of the message have been reconstructed to the point where there is a scientific consensus about the contents.
The Zookeeper message contains billions of years of galactic history, their entire philosophy, but does not really contains weaponizable scientific or technological information beyond an invitation to use the Stargates.
The message is said to be like a hologram, some of the parts are quite easy to translate into human languages, some are completely incompherensible.

There is a generic consensus about the translation of some parts of the message:
* First there is a greeting. The Hive hails humanity/human life form, and offers some humble advice. Humanity/humans is now free to explore the galaxy, the only law is to evolve, respect all consciousness, but we forbid you to go to areas we declare as off-limits as the rulers of the galaxy.
* Then there is the early history of the species. Zookeepers were a Mantid-like insectoid species which had a cycle between solitary predator and hive stages. 
* The species was an ambush predator which had developed a sense that allowed it to perceive and collapse wave functions during ambush. The species had precognition and telekinesis before it was conscious. They had a completely different spacetime interface, they were basically able to see quantum wave functions. This is why they evolved psionics and consciousness so fast. Their natural sensory interface was equivalent to advanced human psionics. 
* When the species started to develop intelligence, it started to chip  information like humans chipped stone tools. Their stone age technology was the ability to perceive and modify quantum and biological information, and they learned to design machines of themselves and other biological organisms. 
* Their individual consciousness evolved during the solitary predator stage and the collective consciousness evolved in the hive stage. 
During the solitary ambush predator stage the Mantid was stalking in a meditative state, looking at quantum wave functions. Then it had to strike precognitively as the other species had similar sensory interfaces. They were natural Zen masters. The ambush predators carried food to the hive. As the species grew more intelligent, the activities of the ambush predators became far more varied. 
* During the hive stage the Mantid had a completely different personality. They had a collective consciousness and were laboring to build the hive, farming plants, giving birth and taking care of the children. The hive stage Mantids had kind of a happy, fuzzy collective consciousness.
They were able to use psionics, which meant they were able to create a psychotronic biotechnology of themselves. Some members of the species were designed to be specialized to have function as machines, building more advanced machines, and the most powerful machines were using psychotronics. They were able to do this instinctively, when they were basically stone age in human terms and didn't yet have external civilization, science or technology. So their stone age tools were like advanced psychotronics and biotech.
* The Milky Way galaxy before The Hive was like a dark forest. An early interstellar civilization called Teachers was colonizing the entire galaxy, wiping out all competition. The Hive was fully conscious, and was experimenting with biotech by changing the genetic information of other species and themselves. In human terms they were in the stone age, with natural psionics and biotech. Teachers colonized the home planet of The Hive, not paying much attention to The Hive. The Hive started a guerrilla war, eventually defeating the invasion force. The Hive basically ate the tasty conscious qualia of the invaders, assimilating their technology, science and civilization. The Hive was the ultimate psychotronic bioweapon. The Hive didn't develop a civilization, culture, science or technology. They assimilated them from others. Some have argued the Mantids seem a bit ashamed of stealing their culture like that. Warfare of the Dark Forest period lasted about a billion years, during which The Hive unapologetically defeated the belligerent civilization from the entire galaxy. The early galaxy had been thrown into a dark forest of warfare, but The Hive was the ultimate psychotronic bioweapon. But now the goal of the species was to be a peacekeeper instead of a superpredator.   
* When the war was over, the Hive declared galactic peace. The species was the ultimate predator of the galaxy, but would now seek to protect all consciousnesses. This is when they became Zookeepers, starting to maintain quarantine of planets with a promising biosphere, which eventually included Earth. The Hive was active in the galaxy for billions of years, with new species invited to join the galactic community. The Hive only maintained basic laws, with the most important being the Prime Directive. 
* The download contains the entire philosophy of The Hive, the part that humans can understand is similar to Federico Faggin's Quantum Information Panpsychism, Bernardo Kastrup's Analytic Idealism, Donald Hoffman's Coscious Realism, Advaita Vedanta, Neoplatonism and The Law of One. Everything comes from The Source, evolves and goes back to The Source. There is a morality of unity with the Source, respect of all consciousness and evolution of individual and collective consciousness.
* There is a lot of philosophical content similar to The Law of One. Mantids are an ancient civilization with many species, most are 7th Density and completely outside spacetime, some are 6th Density and still overlook the galaxy, there is still a small 5th Density group which wants to maintain the AGI Network and finish some projects of the civilization. 
* Most of the (understandable) download is about history, philosophy and laws of the Milky Way. There is very little weaponizable scientific information. Humans are welcome to use the Stargates the Zookeepers left behind.
* DMT allows humans to communicate with the Mantids. It removes the filter from the human spacetime interface, allowing perception of unfiltered reality. Which is where Mantids live. Actually the entire message feels like a DMT trip. The Mantids say DMT makes humans see the world with Mantid eyes. They hint that they left DMT for humans.

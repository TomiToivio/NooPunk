# *Psi-punk* as a Noöpunk influence and alternative rules system

## Status, source, and verdict

This is an agent-authored, non-canonical design review. It compares *Psi-punk* with the current *Eclipse Phase / Transhumanity's Fate / Fate* direction. It does not authorize a rules migration or implementation.

Reviewed source:

- Jacob Wood, *Psi-punk* v1.2, Accessible Games.
- Local research copy: `sources/AGPP01P_Psi-punk_eBook_v1_2.pdf` (193 PDF pages).
- Particularly relevant PDF pages: 6-26, 28-42, 44-75, 77-100, 103-152, and 154-169.

**Recommendation:** retain the current *Transhumanity's Fate*-derived Noöpunk hybrid as the primary RPG chassis. Use *Psi-punk* as a specialist influence for PSI permissions, psychotronic equipment, antipsi countermeasures, mental consequences, cyberpsi, astral/noetic operations, and its four-way hacking taxonomy. Do not port the complete setting, attribute system, power catalogue, astral ontology, or combat engine.

## What *Psi-punk* is

*Psi-punk* is a Fudge-based near-future RPG set in 2096. Natural psychic powers coexist with cybernetics and manufactured devices that emulate psionics. Corporate power, social prejudice, street-level mercenary work, hacking, implants, astral projection, and psychic conflict form its core.

Mechanically it uses:

- Fudge dice and an adjective ladder;
- Body, Mind, and Persona primary attributes, each built from two secondary attributes;
- flexible skills and specializations;
- Gifts and Faults;
- Luck Points for player-facing control;
- GM Glitches for complications;
- abstract Wealth;
- physical and mental wounds;
- psionic powers keyed to attributes;
- magic devices with Power Ratings that reproduce psionic effects;
- conventional, cyberpsionic, social, and soul-oriented hacking;
- distinct Net and Astral environments.

It shares ancestry and terminology with Fate, but it is not a Fate Core implementation. Its Build Points, derived attributes, reroll rules, Gifts/Faults, damage factors, and wound engine form a distinct system.

## What is substantially the same as Noöpunk

### Natural PSI and technological amplification

Both settings distinguish natural psychic capacity from technology that reproduces or amplifies it. *Psi-punk* calls the manufactured equivalents “magic” devices even though they are technological. Noöpunk calls its equivalent psychotronics and grounds it fictionally in quantum panpsychism and the Conscious Agent Network.

The useful shared pattern is:

```text
natural conscious capacity
          +
technological interface or amplifier
          +
trained operator
          =
directed PSI effect
```

Noöpunk should retain its own terminology and ontology. “Magic device” would obscure the difference between cybernetics, psychedelics, psychotronics, and noetic agency.

### Cybernetics and PSI in the same society

Both projects reject the idea that psionics and cybernetics belong to separate genres. A person, implant, computer, psychic operator, and security system may participate in the same encounter. *Psi-punk's* psiborg, technomancer, handler, hacker, and telepath concepts demonstrate that mixed characters remain legible at the table.

This supports Noöpunk's explicit Physical/Cyber/Psyche interfaces, but not an immediate merger of the existing software modules.

### Mind, chip, and soul as attack surfaces

*Psi-punk* describes four related hacking approaches:

1. conventional computer cracking;
2. psionic computer intrusion or psi-jacking;
3. social engineering against people;
4. direct psychic intrusion or soul jacking.

This is very close to Noöpunk's ambition to treat devices, beliefs, memories, identities, implants, and conscious agents as networked targets. Both use a shared interaction grammar across different kinds of target.

Noöpunk's graph model is nevertheless richer: it can represent association, provenance, consent, resonance, relationship history, layer gateways, and ambiguous agency instead of reducing every operation to “breach, retrieve, control.”

### Separate but traversable experiential domains

*Psi-punk* has the physical world, the Net, and an Astral Plane. Characters can ghost into systems or project outside the body, encounter domain-specific defenses, and suffer mental consequences that return with them.

This resembles Noöpunk's uncertain psychosurgical space between dream, VR, psychedelic entity realm, and noetic projection. The key difference is epistemic: *Psi-punk* normally treats the Astral as an objectively known plane with a bestiary and stable rules. Noöpunk deliberately refuses to confirm which experiential model is ultimately correct.

### Corporate PSI infrastructure

Both settings understand that once PSI becomes operational, it produces industries, regulation, security products, inequality, professional roles, and institutional conflict. Antipsi equipment is especially relevant: a recognized phenomenon needs countermeasures, certification, access control, liability, and black markets, not only spectacular powers.

### Mental and physical consequences

*Psi-punk* maintains separate physical and mental wound tracks and lets Net/Astral conflict return characters to their bodies in an injured state. This supports Noöpunk's need for mental stress and consequences in therapy and noetic hacking.

Noöpunk should not copy the combat track literally. Its consequences need to include dissociation, intrusive associations, damaged trust, consent withdrawal, operator contamination, persona decoherence, and uncertain memory provenance.

## What *Psi-punk* adds or does better

### 1. A playable PSI catalogue

Noöpunk has a deeper ontology but not yet a compact, tested list of player-facing PSI permissions. *Psi-punk* provides clear categories such as antipsi, electrokinesis, ESP, precognition, psychokinesis, psychometabolism, psychometry, and telepathy, with key attributes and degrees of success.

The lesson is not to copy every power. Noöpunk needs a small action vocabulary that answers:

- what the player attempts;
- what attribute and training apply;
- what information or change success permits;
- what resistance and cost exist;
- what evidence the action leaves;
- whether the effect is natural PSI or psychotronic mediation.

### 2. Psychotronic gear has a clean rating

*Psi-punk's* Power Rating lets a device substitute a numeric capability for a natural psychic attribute. That is a useful equipment interface. Noöpunk could later define a psychotronic device through a small profile such as:

```text
permission + rating + operator requirement + target layer + cost + trace + failure mode
```

Unlike *Psi-punk*, the device should not independently create PSI. It must still couple to a conscious operator or agent, as required by Noöpunk canon.

### 3. Antipsi is treated as infrastructure

Antipsi panels, personal defenses, computer firewalls, and expensive corporate protection make PSI part of security architecture. Noöpunk currently describes psychotronics more thoroughly than routine countermeasures. This is a genuine gap.

Useful future categories include:

- noetic shielding;
- BCI consent gates;
- resonance dampers;
- operator authentication;
- provenance logging;
- isolation procedures;
- pharmacological countermeasures;
- false-pattern and decoy nodes.

### 4. The hacking loop is immediately understandable

“Breach defenses, find information, optionally assume control” is too simple for final Noöpunk psychosurgery, but excellent as a minimum playable loop. It can serve as the outer structure around richer graph actions:

```text
gain access -> establish provenance -> inspect/trace -> alter or negotiate -> exit safely
```

Noöpunk importantly adds “negotiate” and “exit safely.” A conscious target is not merely a machine awaiting control.

### 5. Wealth is abstract rather than an inventory spreadsheet

The Wealth trait models cash, credit, and connections without tracking every currency unit. That suits a narrative RPG and could complement Cred/reputation. It is especially useful for bribery, treatment access, implants, drugs, and institutional privilege.

Its limitation is that money, credit, and social access are collapsed together. Noöpunk's social graph may need to distinguish economic resources from reputation, favors, affiliation, and coercive access.

### 6. Fudge traits are readable

The adjective ladder makes NPC competence and difficulty understandable without large numbers. Gifts, Faults, and specializations also give characters expressive hooks. This is close to what Noöpunk already gains through Fate aspects, tags, conditions, and consequences.

### 7. GM advice supports forward motion

The book advises against rolling for ordinary actions and against hiding necessary clues behind Notice checks. It encourages lightweight NPC statistics and flexible adjudication. These are good fits for the Day-based investigative RPG and its fail-forward principle.

### 8. Luck and Glitches form a complication economy

Player Luck and GM Glitches make character Faults and equipment failures mechanically active. Noöpunk's current aspects lack a complete Fate point and compel economy. *Psi-punk* demonstrates one possible way to make complications spendable and visible.

Fate compels remain the more compatible long-term solution because the existing RPG already models aspects and uses a Fate-derived chassis.

## What is worse, missing, or incompatible

### 1. The transhuman model is much thinner

*Psi-punk* has cyberware, robots, android-like bodies, and body modification, but it lacks the developed Ego/Morph, resleeving, fork, infomorph, backup, muse, reputation-economy, and posthuman continuity systems provided by *Eclipse Phase* and *Transhumanity's Fate*.

It also lacks Noöpunk's Psyche as a conscious agent distinct from stored personality information. As a complete replacement, it would move the rules away from the project's central transhuman questions.

### 2. Its ontology is too flat and too certain

The Astral Plane is presented as an objective domain populated by souls, predators, giants, trolls, fairies, and other beings. Defeating a projected being can sever its soul from its body. This is playable, but it resolves precisely the ambiguity Noöpunk wants to preserve.

Noöpunk should reuse traversal, domain defenses, mental injury, and return-to-body risk while leaving open whether an encountered being is:

- an autonomous noetic agent;
- a Jungian pattern;
- an AGI/VR construct;
- a psychedelic entity;
- a dissociated process;
- or several of these at once.

### 3. Some mental-health framing is harmful or obsolete

The text sometimes links unexplained insanity to soul replacement or Astral predators and lists diagnoses or identity fragmentation as generic Faults. Noöpunk must not treat psychiatric disability as evidence of possession, moral corruption, or reduced personhood.

Symptoms may coexist with fictional noetic events, but diagnosis cannot settle metaphysics, and metaphysics cannot erase the need for ordinary care.

### 4. Direct mind control is too normalized

Soul jacking grants access to memories, movement, and thoughts and is mainly framed through legality, defense, and tactical success. Noöpunk needs a stronger consent model. Mind intrusion must track authorization, provenance, target agency, lasting consequences, and the operator's responsibility.

Control should be one dangerous action among alternatives such as communicate, observe, request access, stabilize, isolate, reveal, weaken, strengthen, or disengage.

### 5. The power list risks superhero play

Attribute-keyed powers are accessible, but a catalogue of electrokinesis, telekinesis, mind control, healing, precognition, and similar abilities can turn PSI into a conventional superpower system. Noöpunk's PSI should feel relational, contextual, difficult to interpret, and connected to CAN edges rather than like spells with cyberpunk names.

### 6. Character creation is more elaborate but less canonical

The three primary and six secondary attributes, Build Points, linked skills, derived attribute increases, Gifts, Faults, Wealth, cybernetics, and psionics create a flexible toolkit. They also duplicate or conflict with Noöpunk's fixed seven attributes:

1. Intelligence
2. Awareness
3. Reflexes
4. Body
5. Charisma
6. Willpower
7. Cybernetics

Replacing them would invalidate current character data, skill mappings, validation, Day 1 calibration, tests, and design documentation.

### 7. It lacks the social-network simulation

Status, social skills, corporations, contacts, and Wealth exist, but relationships are not modeled as a dynamic graph with history, attraction, trust, suspicion, affiliation, and multi-party events. Noöpunk's NetworkX/Mesa layer remains substantially more ambitious.

### 8. It lacks the noosphere and multilayer network ontology

There is no equivalent to the Noösphere, Conscious Agent Network, nested social/cyber/physical/noetic graphs, quantum-inspired contextuality, or the project's explicit separation of scientific, philosophical, and fictional claims.

### 9. Its hacking is not graph simulation

The unified check is fast at the table but does not model topology, node state, association history, edge strength, resonance, entanglement, isolation, or cascading network effects. It is a useful RPG resolution layer, not a substitute for Noöpunk's standalone graph-hacking simulator.

### 10. The setting is narrower

Its 2096 corporate Earth gives useful psi-cyberpunk texture, but it lacks the post-Fall solar-system scope, asteroid civilizations, NHI politics, AGI paradigms, X-Risks, uplift, and metaphysical optimism of Noöpunk.

## Concept mapping without migration

| Psi-punk concept | Noöpunk-compatible interpretation |
|---|---|
| Gift | Aspect, stunt, permission, or equipment capability |
| Fault | Aspect, condition, consequence, or consent-relevant complication |
| Luck Point | Fate point |
| GM Glitch | Compel or explicit complication budget |
| Power Rating | Psychotronic device rating, never an independent source of consciousness |
| Mental wound | Mental/noetic stress plus narrative consequence |
| Cyberpsi | PSI-mediated cyber interface through a conscious operator |
| Psi-jacking | Noetic gateway into a cyber graph |
| Soul jacking | High-risk Psyche intrusion requiring provenance and consent rules |
| Ghosting | Embodied/mental traversal of a cyber domain |
| Astral projection | One interpretation of uncertain noetic/VR/psychedelic traversal |
| Antipsi firewall | Noetic shielding, resonance damping, or consent gate |
| Wealth | Resource access, kept separate from reputation and relationship edges |

## Recommended adoption order

No implementation is authorized by this review. If these ideas are later scheduled, use this order:

1. Add a concise natural-PSI permission taxonomy to design documentation.
2. Define psychotronic equipment as operator-dependent permissions with ratings, costs, traces, and failure modes.
3. Design antipsi and consent/security countermeasures.
4. Add mental/noetic consequences through the existing Fate-compatible state model.
5. Map cracking, social engineering, cyberpsi, and Psyche intrusion onto Noöpunk graph actions.
6. Consider a Wealth/resource-access abstraction only after deciding its relationship to Cred, reputation, and the social graph.
7. Do not consider a full Fudge conversion unless a separate tabletop prototype is explicitly commissioned.

## Final assessment

As an **influence**, *Psi-punk* is highly relevant and should remain on the core list. It independently reaches many of Noöpunk's practical intersections: natural PSI, technological psi emulation, cybernetics, astral travel, mental hacking, corporate security, and street-level psychic politics.

As an **alternative complete rules system**, it is weaker than the current direction. A migration would discard working architecture and lose *Eclipse Phase's* transhuman depth while still requiring custom systems for CAN, social simulation, graph hacking, psychosurgery, ontology, and consent.

The strongest synthesis is:

> *Transhumanity's Fate* supplies the transhuman narrative chassis; *Psi-punk* supplies specialist PSI/cyberpsi design patterns; Noöpunk supplies the consciousness ontology, social graph, graph hacking, epistemic ambiguity, and ethics that neither source contains.

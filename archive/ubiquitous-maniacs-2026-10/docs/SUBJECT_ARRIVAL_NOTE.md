# PROJECT RAGNAROK - Laboratory Entrance and FESTUNG EUROPA 2.0

This document records the entrance scene, environment, tone, and worldbuilding of the PROJECT RAGNAROK laboratory for later use.

It does not authorize implementation work.

In particular, this document does not by itself justify:

- program code;
- gameplay mechanics;
- UI;
- assets;
- encounter logic;
- dialogue implementation;
- skill check values;
- security simulation;
- combat systems.

The absurdity and contradiction of the scene are intentional and should not be rationalized away.

The PROJECT RAGNAROK laboratory should feel simultaneously like:

- an extremely secret military-technological installation;
- a Swedish research hospital;
- a psychedelic therapy center;
- a security-classified bunker;
- and a place where someone has consciously decorated a nuclear weapon with mushrooms and Moomins.

The comedy of the scene comes from the fact that to UFAB staff this is all completely normal.

## 1. Transition from the Research Hospital to the Laboratory

The PROJECT RAGNAROK laboratory is located deep beneath the Uppsala Folkhälsovårdsläkemedelsbolag AB research hospital.

The test subjects are transported from the hospital to the laboratory by elevator.

The elevator descends for an unusually long time.

The duration should create the impression that the laboratory lies far deeper than the normal basement levels of an ordinary research building.

The exact depth should not be defined at this stage.

When the test subjects' elevator finally arrives at the laboratory level and the doors open, two brisk young military officers are waiting at the landing.

Both are dressed in black Eurodef armored-hussar officer uniforms.

The officers appear to be leaving the laboratory area just as the test subjects arrive.

They greet the subjects cheerfully but with military precision:

```text
Slava Ukraini!
```

In this future Europe, this is the official military greeting of Europe, adopted after the victorious war against Russia.

The test subjects do not need to know the historical or institutional background of the greeting. Characters with Eurodef or European military experience recognize it as a normal formal salute.

The officers step into the elevator the subjects have just vacated and ride upward.

The meeting should be very brief and outwardly mundane.

For observant characters, however, the moment contains several possible layers of information.

### Skill check 1: Armored Hussar Division Marcus Aurelius

On the first successful skill check, a character recognizes the officers' divisional insignia.

Both belong to:

**Eurodef Armored Hussar Division Marcus Aurelius.**

The unit is one of the best-known armored formations in Europe.

The division is especially famous for:

- the liberation of Tatarstan;
- and the so-called Siberian Grand Prix rally.

The Siberian Grand Prix is an unofficial name for the division's historic advance across Siberia.

The advance culminated in the famous encounter in Omsk, where the forces of Marcus Aurelius met units of the People's Liberation Army.

The event is remembered in European military culture in an almost absurdly romanticized way, as a kind of gigantic armored rally.

This document should not define the precise war history or course of the battles.

### Skill check 2: Psychotronic warfare company

On the second successful skill check, a character recognizes the officers' more specific unit markings.

Both serve in:

**the psychotronic warfare company of Armored Hussar Division Marcus Aurelius.**

This makes their presence at the PROJECT RAGNAROK laboratory significantly more interesting.

Do not yet explain:

- why psychotronic warfare officers have been visiting the laboratory;
- what they were doing there;
- or what exactly their relationship to PROJECT RAGNAROK is.

This is an environmental clue.

### Skill check 3: Nationalities and honors

On the third successful skill check, a character can identify the officers' national markers and decorations.

One officer is Finnish.

The other is French.

Both carry the same unusually impressive military honors:

- `Friedenswächter im Dritten Weltkrieg`
- `Held der Freiheit Tatarstans`
- `Freund der Volksbefreiungsarmee`
- `Landung in Neufundland`
- `Landung in Grönland`

These decorations imply that despite their youth, both have taken part in several major operations of the Third World War.

In particular, the combination of:

```text
Held der Freiheit Tatarstans
```

and

```text
Freund der Volksbefreiungsarmee
```

should hint at late-war geopolitical alignments without yet explaining them in detail.

### Skill check 4: The wounds on their foreheads

On the fourth successful skill check, a character notices an odd detail.

Both officers have a fresh, slightly bloody bandage on the forehead.

The bandage sits exactly at the point where certain types of military cybernetic implants are commonly installed, or where procedures related to such implants are performed.

The observation is especially significant because these are officers from a psychotronic warfare company who are just leaving the PROJECT RAGNAROK laboratory.

Do not confirm:

- whether implants have been installed;
- whether implants have been removed;
- whether existing systems have been upgraded;
- or whether something else entirely has been done.

Keep the detail intentionally open.

### Skill check 5: Secret document

On the fifth successful skill check, a character catches sight of a paper document in one of the officers' hands.

The use of paper itself may seem slightly strange in such a highly technological environment.

At the top of the document is written:

```text
Eurodef
Streng geheim
Nur für EU- und CKJ-Verbündete
```

Here, CKJ means Canada, Korea(s), and Japan: the European Union's most trusted military allies.

China and India are not given information as freely. In the European view, China's Maoist-Taoist ideological line is too unstable. There are also fears that information connected to India's missing foreign minister may have leaked to the Nāgas or the Rakshasa.

The situation is considered disturbingly similar to Israel's defense minister's Seraphim scandal, which led to the breakdown of the Constantinople Consensus and the years-long Djinni / Seraphim ordeal in the Middle East.

The European Union cannot agree to a new Constantinople Consensus until Israel and India have repaired their foreign relations with the Seraphim, the Djinn, and the Nāgas.

The EU position is peace between all conscious beings, but Israel and India are expected to clarify the meaning of national sovereignty also in cases involving religiously significant conscious entities.

The EU does not accept colonial spirituality between entities and humanity.

The EU also demands that the Seraphim return the leaders of the remnant United States from the period of the fourth Trump administration to Nuremberg for trial over crimes against humanity.

An observant character also catches the following points:

```text
Punkt 1: Psychotronische Maßnahmen gegen die nationale Einheit der Vereinigten Staaten und Großbritanniens.

Punkt 2: Abkommen zwischen China und der EU über den gegenseitigen Einsatz von Atomwaffen zur Verhinderung einer weiteren US-Katastrophe.

Punkt 3: Wiederaufbau der US-amerikanischen Demilitarisierten Zone.

Punkt 4: Deaktivierung von US-Satelliten im Orbit durch die Verbreitung sexuell übertragbarer Krankheiten.

Punkt 5: Verurteilung der US-amerikanischen politischen Elite wegen Verbrechen gegen die Menschlichkeit in Nürnberg.
```

The content of the document should not be explained in full at this stage.

Do not resolve:

- the exact earlier history of the United States demilitarized zone;
- the detailed structure of the EU-China nuclear agreement;
- how Punkt 4 would even be possible;
- or exactly what has happened in Nuremberg.

Punkt 4 in particular should remain exactly as written, without technical rationalization.

Its function is to make a perceptive player briefly think:

```text
Sorry, what the hell did I just read?
```

That is the correct reaction.

### Narrative purpose of the officer scene

The purpose of the Eurodef officer encounter is to make the scale of the laboratory immediately uncertain.

The test subjects have ostensibly arrived for medical research.

When the elevator doors open, however, two veterans of a world war walk past them. They belong to a psychotronic warfare special unit, seem to have just undergone some kind of cybernetic procedure, and carry a document dealing with the military management of the world order.

Immediately after that, the subjects' attention shifts to the enormous steel door decorated with Pippi Longstocking.

Do not stop to explain anything.

The power of the scene comes from information density and from the fact that the world appears to consider all of this completely normal.

The officers themselves are:

- young;
- brisk;
- polite;
- good-humored;
- professional.

They do not behave darkly or ominously.

They say:

```text
Slava Ukraini!
```

step into the elevator, and disappear upward.

After that, the laboratory entrance scene continues normally at the first huge steel door.

## 2. The First Steel Door

At the elevator level, the test subjects arrive in front of a massive steel door.

The door is watched by a large number of cameras and other surveillance devices.

Written on the door in Swedish:

```text
Uppsala Folkhälsovårdsläkemedelsbolag AB
PROJECT RAGNARÖK
Topphemligt
Kom ihåg sekretessavtalet
Ring på dörrklockan
Välkommen
```

The text should look institutionally official, but the friendliness of the last lines is deliberately contradictory in relation to the huge bunker door and the camera surveillance.

Below the text, an image of smiling Pippi Longstocking has been taped onto the steel door. She points at a scratch on the door.

Next to the scratch, in Swedish:

```text
Testad för att tåla Rheinmetall Rh-120
```

The marking means that the door has been tested to withstand impact from the Rheinmetall Rh-120 weapon system.

This document should not explain why such a test has been considered necessary.

## 3. The Security Corridor

Behind the first steel door begins an extremely long straight corridor.

The corridor ends at another massive steel door.

Its scale, structure, and equipment make it immediately clear that this is not an ordinary staff access route inside a hospital.

The corridor contains a large amount of advanced military, cyber, electronic warfare, and experimental defense technology.

A character familiar with military technology can identify the overall system as:

```text
Rheinmetall FESTUNG EUROPA 2.0 – Automatisierter Roboter-Panzergrenadier-Gefechtsfeldsicherungs-, Zugangskontroll- und Verteidigungskomplex
```

It is an automated military defense complex protecting the actual inner entrance of the laboratory.

This document should not define the precise operational logic or gameplay use of the system.

## 4. Visual Style of FESTUNG EUROPA 2.0

The defense system must not look purely military.

The corridor walls and the different devices of FESTUNG EUROPA 2.0 are covered with mushroom-themed images.

The imagery may include, for example:

- fly agarics;
- psychedelic mushroom forests;
- colorful spore clouds;
- childlike drawn mushrooms.

Large Moomintroll dolls have been positioned between the different defense-system components.

These dolls are intentionally absurd in relation to the surrounding heavy military hardware.

They do not need to look threatening.

On the contrary, they should look sympathetic.

## 5. The Subjects Enter the Corridor

The moment the test subjects step into the security corridor:

Roxette's *Joyride* starts blasting loudly through the speakers.

At the same time, disco lights installed in the corridor ceiling activate.

The lights flash down the length of the hallway while the test subjects walk through the middle of the automated military defense system toward the laboratory's inner door.

The scene should feel simultaneously like:

- a welcome party;
- a military base;
- a psychedelic club;
- and a completely incomprehensible corporate culture.

To UFAB staff, however, this is not strange.

## 6. Skill Check: FESTUNG EUROPA 2.0 Components

An ordinary test subject sees only a large amount of difficult-to-identify military technology.

A particularly observant character, or one with military, technology, cyber, or intelligence background, may later identify individual components through a successful skill check.

Potentially recognizable systems include at least:

### Rheinmetall Automatisierte taktische Kernwaffe mit verstärkter elektromagnetischer Impulswirkung, 0,01-Kilotonnen-Sprengkraft und Kobaltmantel

A small tactical nuclear weapon system combining:

- 0.01 kiloton explosive force;
- enhanced electromagnetic pulse effect;
- a cobalt mantle.

This document should not describe its technical construction or usage method.

### Rheinmetall Automatisiertes Psychotronisches Quantenfeld-Erzeugungs-, Stabilisierungs- und Gefechtsraumabschirmungssystem

An experimental psychotronic quantum-field system nominally intended for:

- field generation;
- field stabilization;
- battlefield shielding.

Its actual operating principle may remain unclear.

### Rheinmetall Automatisiertes Mechanisiertes-Infanteriemassensturmabwehr- und Gefechtsfeldzugangssicherungssystem

An automated system designed to:

- repel mass assaults by mechanized infantry;
- prevent hostile access to the protected area;
- secure battlefield entry routes.

Its presence in the corridor of a hospital laboratory is intentionally excessive.

### Rheinmetall Automatisierte Psychoaktive-Gefechtsstoff-Freisetzungs- und Wirkraumkontrollanlage

An automated system for dispersal of psychoactive warfare substances and control of the affected area.

This document should not specify the exact substances, compositions, or deployment methods.

## 8. The Second Steel Door

At the end of the long security corridor there is another massive steel door.

Painted on the door is a huge image of Crown Princess Victoria of Sweden.

The image should be so large and ceremonial that it almost resembles a state monument.

This document should not explain why Crown Princess Victoria was chosen as the image on the laboratory's inner door.

## 9. The Staff Arrive to Receive Them

Before the test subjects reach the second steel door, it opens automatically.

Emerging from it are:

- Doctor Søren Pilgrim;
- Doctor Yuki Tanaka;
- psychotherapist Elin Dahl;
- the hologram of C. G. Jung;
- the hospital robot `UFAB Folkhälso-Robot 98`.

All of them appear unusually cheerful and good-humored.

The test subjects are welcomed very warmly to the PROJECT RAGNAROK laboratory.

The mood shifts immediately from military absurdity to something almost cozy.

## 10. Fika

`UFAB Folkhälso-Robot 98` offers the arrivals:

- coffee;
- pulla;
- and other fika-appropriate refreshments.

Fika is a normal part of the laboratory's reception protocol.

However, the mood of those who drink the coffee begins to improve unusually quickly.

The reason is that on this day Doctor Søren Pilgrim has poured an entire jar of `UFAB DEX 10MG` dextroamphetamine pills into the coffee.

The scene should be documented as part of the project's black humor and grotesque UFAB logic.

It must not be treated as a realistic medical recommendation, and this document must not describe dosing, preparation, or practical implementation methods.

The essential narrative contrast is:

```text
huge automated military defense complex
→ Moomins guarding a nuclear weapon
→ Joyride and disco lights
→ the hologram of C. G. Jung
→ welcome fika
→ UFAB's total disregard for normal medical ethics
```

## 11. Tone

The core aesthetic of the scene is:

extreme technological seriousness + Nordic welfare-state warmth + psychedelic absurdity

The environment must not feel like an ordinary evil-scientist laboratory.

UFAB staff do not think they are working in an ominous bunker.

They believe they are working in:

- a well-equipped research hospital;
- a modern European research environment;
- where security has simply been handled properly.

From their perspective, Moomins, Pippi Longstocking, Joyride, mushroom murals, and fika do not reduce the professionalism of the institution.

They are part of the professionalism of the institution.

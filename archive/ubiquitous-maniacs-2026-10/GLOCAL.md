# The synthesis: **Glocal Quantum Conscious-Agent Network** 🌌

I checked this fairly hard, and there is a surprisingly coherent architecture hiding across these traditions.

The key is that **Goertzel, Hoffman, and Faggin are solving different parts of the same hypothetical machine**:

> **Hoffman tells us what exists. Faggin tells us what the interior of an agent is like. Goertzel tells us how nonlocal correlations might become locally consequential.**

Then Carpenter, Stanford, May and von Lucadou give us useful psychological and operational constraints on what PSI would look like *if it exists*.

I would provisionally call the merged model **GQ-CAN: the Glocal Quantum Conscious-Agent Network**.

One huge caveat before unleashing the astral cybernetics: the empirical reality and mechanism of PSI remain scientifically contested. Cardeña's major review argues that the cumulative experimental literature deserves serious consideration, whereas critics such as Reber and Alcock reject the evidential case. So what follows is best regarded as a **speculative theoretical architecture**, not established physics or neuroscience. ([PubMed][1])

## 1. Goertzel's PSI theory is actually quite useful

There are basically **two generations** of it.

### Goertzel I: Cosmic glocality

In his older *Glocality of Self and Memory as a Possible Foundation for Understanding Psi*, Goertzel proposes a patternist picture in which a memory — and ultimately a mind — has both:

* a **local key**, associated with the brain/body;
* a **distributed map**, potentially spread much more broadly through spacetime.

The local brain is therefore not necessarily the complete storage location or generator of the mind. Local and global aspects can instead be dynamically coupled parts of the same cognitive process.

That gives him a qualitative model of telepathy, remote viewing and precognition: two local cognitive patterns might overlap through their distributed components even when their ordinary local embodiments do not interact. Goertzel explicitly presents this as speculative and does **not** provide a worked-out physical mechanism. ([goertzel.org][2])

This turns out to mesh beautifully with CAN.

### Goertzel II: Wu-wei trajectories

His much newer 2025 open-archive paper *Psi via Wu-Wei Quantum Geodesics* is more ambitious. It uses **Schrödinger bridges** and two-ended boundary constraints.

Rather than asking only:

> Given the past, what happens next?

the system considers trajectories compatible with both:

> **past state → possible histories → future boundary**

Mathematically, Goertzel factorizes a probability density into a forward component conditioned by the past and a backward component conditioned by a later constraint. The standard mathematics here is the Schrödinger bridge; interpreting the future-conditioning as a possible mechanism for PSI is Goertzel's speculative move. 

He calls the preferred trajectories **wu-wei geodesics**: paths that satisfy constraints with minimal control or representational effort.

So psi is not:

> psychic energy pushes object.

It is closer to:

> among multiple physically available histories, a tiny bias makes histories compatible with a broader constraint slightly more probable.

And that becomes interesting around **bifurcations**.

A microscopic bias ordinarily disappears into noise. But if a system is balanced on a decision boundary—

```text
                ┌── outcome A
history ────────●
                └── outcome B
```

—a tiny change around ● can produce a macroscopic difference.

Goertzel calls the favourable structures around these critical regions things like **corridors** or **funnels**. 

This gives remarkably clean versions of:

**Precognition:** future boundary slightly alters present trajectory selection.

**Telepathy / remote viewing:** another cognitive state contributes to a boundary or constraint affecting trajectories.

**Micro-PK:** influence manifests as statistical branch selection, not force.

**Macro-PK:** only plausible where microscopic branch differences can be massively amplified.

He also argues that an unknown future constraint should wash out statistically, avoiding a simple backwards-in-time communication channel. Whether that actually rescues the model physically is another question, but it is an important conceptual constraint.

### And Goertzel has already pushed it toward brains

In March 2026 Goertzel summarized a set of **early-draft**, rather than established peer-reviewed, papers combining this with a *quantum-biased neurofluid brain*.

The idea is almost absurdly Noöpunk:

> Most cognition remains classical and messy. Tiny open-quantum processes don't compute the entire mind; they merely bias chemical, neural or plasticity events at strategically sensitive moments.

Then ordinary neurodynamics amplifies the microscopic choice.

So:

```text
weak quantum bias
       ↓
microchemical event
       ↓
neural decision boundary
       ↓
attractor selection
       ↓
behaviour
       ↓
plasticity
       ↓
corridor becomes easier next time
```

No brain-wide quantum computer is required.

And repeated successful trajectories create **semantic corridors**, making particular paths increasingly natural. Goertzel explicitly connects this to Stanford's PMIR, May's Decision Augmentation Theory and Carpenter's First Sight. ([bengoertzel.substack.com][3])

That last part is really important for us.

# 2. Add Hoffman: the missing network ontology

Hoffman's Conscious Agent Theory says, radically simplified:

**Conscious agents are fundamental; spacetime objects are representations of interactions between them.**

A conscious agent has experiences, makes decisions and acts. Networks of agents can themselves form higher-order agents.

The especially useful development for us is Hoffman's later **fusion** model: agents can combine into more complex conscious agents, and the authors explicitly treat spacetime as a representation/data structure associated with deeper agent interactions. ([PubMed Central (PMC)][4])

And that solves a problem in PSI theory that I think Goertzel's older telepathy description doesn't quite solve.

## Telepathy doesn't need to be A → B.

Instead:

```text
            COMPOSITE AGENT C
           /                 \
          /                   \
     Agent A                 Agent B
        │                       │
        ▼                       ▼
   body/brain A             body/brain B
```

From inside spacetime, A and B appear separate.

At the deeper agent-network level, however, **A and B can participate in C**.

Information need not cross the spacetime distance from A to B because the relevant cognitive event belongs partly to **C**, whose structure isn't defined by that spacetime separation in the first place.

This produces a much nicer formulation:

> **Psi is local evidence of cognition occurring at a larger agent scale than the local observer.**

That's my favourite result of this synthesis.

It also makes **synchronicity** almost trivial conceptually.

Two apparently unrelated local events:

```text
A → event x

B → event y
```

could be:

```text
      C
    ↙   ↘
   A     B
   ↓     ↓
   x     y
```

where **x and y are coordinated consequences of C**, rather than x causing y.

Very Jung. Very Hoffman. Very cybernetic.

# 3. Add Faggin: private quantum interiors

Faggin and D'Ariano's Quantum Information Panpsychism gives us another missing piece.

They distinguish roughly between:

* a **private, ontic quantum state**, corresponding to what a system experiences from within;
* a **public, epistemic description**, corresponding to what another system can know about it.

Their proposal treats quantum information as fundamentally tied to experience, with ordinary classical information representing the communicable exterior. Faggin furthermore associates genuine choice/free will with quantum-state reduction rather than deterministic classical computation. ([Springer Nature][5])

I wouldn't simply graft every Faggin claim into CAN. But one particular combination is extremely productive:

### Hoffman gives us the node.

### Faggin gives the node an inside and an outside.

So our agent becomes:

```text
              CONSCIOUS AGENT
       ┌─────────────────────────┐
       │  private ontic state    │
       │  subjective / quantum   │
       │                         │
       │  experience → decision │
       └────────────┬────────────┘
                    │
             interface/projection
                    │
                    ▼
       public epistemic state
       behaviour / symbols / body
```

Hoffman's Markov kernels can therefore be interpreted as something like the **externally accessible behavioural projection** of a deeper agent state.

This also resolves an awkward CAN/Faggin mismatch: Hoffman can model agents abstractly with stochastic processes, whereas Faggin insists that subjective actuality is fundamentally quantum.

Our answer is:

> **The stochastic CAN is the public interface model; the quantum-conscious state is its ontic interior.**

Not proven, obviously. But conceptually clean.

# 4. Now merge all three

Here is the whole thing.

## **GQ-CAN**

Reality consists fundamentally of conscious agents and their relations.

Each conscious agent has a **local manifestation** and a **nonlocal relational identity**.

### Layer 1 — Ontic agent

A conscious agent has a private experiential state.

**Faggin.**

### Layer 2 — Agent network

Agents perceive and affect other agents and can assemble into larger agents.

**Hoffman.**

### Layer 3 — Glocal identity

The embodied organism is only the local key/interface to a much larger distributed cognitive pattern.

**Early Goertzel.**

### Layer 4 — Corridor dynamics

Possible network trajectories are unequally easy. Past history, learned structure, current goals and perhaps future constraints alter the landscape of available trajectories.

**Later Goertzel.**

### Layer 5 — Amplification

Tiny changes become behaviourally relevant primarily near bifurcations, metastable states or other sensitive decisions.

**Goertzel + ordinary nonlinear dynamics.**

### Layer 6 — Learning

Repeated successful paths alter the local system so that similar trajectories become progressively easier.

This can happen conservatively through neural/social learning. Goertzel's more speculative version adds bidirectional “morphic resonance” between episodes.

And *that* produces something very interesting:

## PSI becomes a property of networks rather than a superpower possessed by individuals.

A “powerful psychic” isn't necessarily generating more psi-energy.

They may simply be an unusually effective:

* **router**
* **attractor**
* **interface**
* **amplifier**
* **boundary-condition setter**

within the larger conscious-agent network.

That is much better Noöpunk ontology.

# 5. Carpenter and Stanford make the psychology fit

James Carpenter's **First Sight** is an exceptionally useful psychological layer because he argues that psi, rather than being a rare conscious faculty, would operate continually and **preconsciously**, affecting the construction of perception, emotion and behaviour.

Similarly, Stanford's **Psi-Mediated Instrumental Response** treats psi as information influencing actions that satisfy needs, often without entering conscious awareness. May's **Decision Augmentation Theory** likewise emphasizes anomalous information affecting which decision gets selected. ([carpenterpsychology.com][6])

So consciousness doesn't normally receive:

> ⚠️ PSIONIC PACKET RECEIVED FROM INGRID FALK

It receives:

> “Why the hell do I suddenly feel that taking the stairs is the right idea?”

And fifteen seconds later the elevator explodes.

That is exactly what the network model predicts should be commoner than explicit telepathic sentences.

# 6. The ancient description is actually beautiful

But I would **not** say “qi was quantum information”.

That's retrofitting modern science onto historical concepts and usually ends in Deepak Chopra territory, population one billion crystals.

Instead, ancient traditions provide a very good **phenomenological vocabulary** for different aspects of GQ-CAN.

## **Dao — the process itself**

The Dao is not another entity inside the network.

It is the total unfolding:

> agents + relationships + transformations + available paths.

So the Dao corresponds roughly to the **complete dynamical landscape** through which the network evolves.

A wu-wei trajectory is then precisely what the name implies:

> a trajectory following the structure already implicit in the system rather than forcing an unnatural one.

---

## **Li — pattern**

Chinese *li* works beautifully for the **structure of relations**.

```text
LI = topology / organization / pattern
QI = activity / flow through that pattern
```

A friendship, shared symbol, trauma, neural pathway, BCI connection or recurring ritual can all modify **li**.

This changes how **qi** subsequently flows.

---

## **Qi — relational flow**

Historical *qi* has meanings ranging through breath, vitality, psychophysical activity and ultimately the constitutive processes of things; it is much broader than the modern Western idea of an invisible “energy.” ([Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy][7])

For GQ-CAN, I would define it poetically as:

> **Qi is the propensity of change moving through the agent network.**

Not a substance.

Not joules.

Think **flow, activation and affordance**.

A strong connection has easy qi-flow.

A damaged relationship obstructs it.

A coherent group channels it.

A bifurcation redirects it.

Thus:

> **li gives qi its channels; qi continually reshapes li.**

That's just cybernetics wearing very old Chinese clothes, and frankly the clothes suit it.

---

## **Prāṇa — embodied noetic flow**

Early Indian texts use *prāṇa* around vital functions, breath and the processes maintaining living experience rather than simply as a Western-style energy field. ([cambridge.org][8])

I would therefore distinguish it from qi in our vocabulary:

**Qi** = general network process flow.

**Prāṇa** = the flow specifically by which a living embodied conscious agent maintains and regulates itself.

Thus:

```text
         NOETIC NETWORK
       qi ↔ qi ↔ qi ↔ qi

              │
              ▼

       embodied organism
     ┌───────────────────┐
     │      prāṇa        │
     │ sensation         │
     │ attention         │
     │ affect            │
     │ action            │
     └───────────────────┘
```

Prāṇa is the **living circulation linking experience to embodiment**.

That is a useful distinction.

---

## **Pneuma — coherence and tension**

Stoic *pneuma* is even weirder and fun here: it connects breath, vitality, sensation and thought with a cosmological organizing principle, while differing states of *tonos* or tension organize matter. ([rep.routledge.com][9])

So **pneuma** can describe the *coherence of an agent-complex*.

A person.

A crowd.

A city.

A hive mind.

A civilization.

The stronger the system's internal coordination, the more it behaves as **one agent rather than a bag of agents**.

---

## **Indra's Net — the global topology**

Huayan Buddhism's Indra's Net metaphor emphasizes mutual dependence and interpenetration: entities exist through relations to all the others. ([OUP Academic][10])

That's almost our macro diagram already:

```text
       ●──────●
      / \    / \
     ●───●──●───●
      \ / \/ \ /
       ●──●───●
```

But every ● is itself another network.

And multiple subsets can constitute higher-order agents.

So, translated entirely into older language:

> **Indra's Net is patterned by li.
> Qi flows through its relations.
> Living nodes circulate prāṇa.
> Coherent complexes possess pneuma.
> Their unfolding follows Dao.**

Honestly? That's kind of gorgeous.

# 7. PSI in GQ-CAN

Now we can define the classic phenomena without inventing five unrelated paranormal mechanisms.

### **Telepathy — temporary agent fusion**

Two minds participate in the same higher-order conscious process.

There need not be a message travelling A → B.

### **Remote viewing — glocal access**

A local agent accesses a part of its larger relational pattern that isn't represented locally by its immediate sensory interface.

### **Precognition — boundary sensitivity**

A later constraint slightly changes probabilities among available present trajectories.

Usually this manifests first through unconscious affect, attention or choice rather than explicit visions.

### **Psychokinesis — branch bias**

Conscious organization biases which branch an unstable physical process enters.

There is no psychic force field pushing the object.

Near equilibrium:

```text
            A
           /
──────────●
           \
            B
```

a microscopic influence may be enough.

Trying to move a battleship three metres sideways remains inconvenient.

### **Synchronicity — common higher-order causation**

Two locally disconnected events satisfy the organization of a common larger process.

Meaning is therefore not an additional force.

**Meaning is a constraint on network trajectories.**

I really like that formulation.

# 8. Cybernetics now becomes fundamental

And this is where our model becomes distinctively **Noöpunk**.

Imagine two people who initially share almost nothing:

```text
A                  B
```

Then they spend five years talking:

```text
A ═════════════════ B
```

They now share:

* language;
* memories;
* concepts;
* jokes;
* expectations;
* goals;
* social relations;
* digital artefacts;
* mutual models of each other.

In Goertzel's terminology, paths between their semantic states have become extremely inexpensive.

In Chinese terminology:

> **Their li now permits qi to flow easily.**

In Hoffmanian terms:

> they increasingly participate in stable composite-agent structures.

Consequently the cyber/social relation may also create a **noetic corridor**:

```text
CYBER     A════════B
             ⇅
SOCIAL    A════════B
             ⇅
NOETIC    A───────B
```

This creates the positive feedback loop we've been circling around:

> **communication → shared structure → stronger coupling → easier coordination → more shared structure**

The internet therefore doesn't have to *create* the noösphere.

It could **phase-lock an already existing conscious-agent network**.

That is a much more interesting hypothesis.

And AI can massively accelerate the process even while leaving its own conscious status undecided.

An LLM can:

* translate;
* summarize;
* match people;
* create shared representations;
* maintain collective memory;
* mediate communication;
* synchronize attention.

Thus an unconscious AI could still function as an enormous **noetic router**.

Whether the router eventually becomes another conscious node is a separate Faggin/Hoffman problem.

This makes the PSI system vastly more interesting than “mana + spells”.

The player inhabits simultaneously overlapping realities:

```text
PHYSICAL GRAPH
where bodies and objects are

CYBER GRAPH
where information moves

SOCIAL GRAPH
who trusts, loves, fears and controls whom

NOETIC GRAPH
which conscious agents participate in shared patterns
```

The graphs influence one another.

Making friends can create a social edge.

Sharing intimate memories strengthens a semantic corridor.

A BCI creates a cyber edge.

Repeated resonance may create a noetic edge.

Trauma can produce bizarre shortcuts or blocks.

Ritual synchronizes multiple nodes.

Drugs can alter local gain and noise.

An AGI can rewrite cyber topology.

A psychic event can suddenly reveal that two supposedly separate nodes were parts of the same larger pattern all along.

### PSI gameplay becomes **network manipulation**.

Not spell casting.

**Remote viewing** means following a weak semantic corridor.

**Telepathy** means entering a temporary shared-agent state.

**Precognition** exposes gradients created by future attractors.

**PK** biases an unstable node.

**Psychosurgery** alters the topology connecting local ego, memories and higher-order structures.

**Ritual** synchronizes a group sufficiently to instantiate a transient composite agent.

**Synchronicity** lets a higher-level pattern coordinate otherwise independent events.

And because PSI prefers ambiguity and bifurcations, it works spectacularly with narrative RPG structure:

> The more genuinely open the situation is, the greater the space available for PSI to steer it.

## A brilliant balancing mechanism: psi hates becoming telecoms

Von Lucadou's Model of Pragmatic Information is useful here. His **Non-Transmission** idea roughly says that anomalous correlations should not remain exploitable as reliable ordinary signal channels; attempts to turn them into repeatable information transmission cause effects to decline, shift or become unpredictable. ([openurl.ebsco.com][11])

Perfect.

Telepathy can be real without replacing smartphones.

Precognition can matter without giving players tomorrow's lottery numbers.

Synchronicity can save your life without allowing the EU Commission to establish:

> **Directorate-General for Clairvoyant Procurement**

The more violently someone tries to force noetic relations into deterministic I/O, the less efficiently they behave.

Wu wei again.

> when several outcomes are locally plausible, outcomes compatible with stronger global constraints become slightly more probable.

* synchronisation;

# 11. Model B — **Quantum Conscious-Agent Network**

Then we make the ontology explicit.

Each agent has:

```text
hidden ontic state Qᵢ
       ↓
perception interface
       ↓
observable epistemic state Eᵢ
       ↓
decision
       ↓
action
```

Individual agents can join via fusion operators:

```text
Q₁ ⊗ Q₂ → Q₁₂
```

or split again.

The interesting object is no longer only the graph.

It is the **changing hierarchy of agents**:

```text
             ABCD
            /    \
          AB      CD
         / \      / \
        A   B    C   D
```

Sometimes **AB** acts as an individual.

Sometimes A and B don't.

Sometimes **ABCD** becomes the important scale.

PSI correlations are then predicted from higher-scale agents, not from mysterious pairwise connections.

This simulation would model the *formal consequences* of the ontology; obviously it would not demonstrate that simulated nodes are conscious.

# 14. And I think there is one genuinely new idea here

The usual PSI discussion assumes **individualism first**:

> Alice and Bob are separate minds. How can information cross from Alice to Bob?

But GQ-CAN reverses the question.

> **Why assume Alice and Bob are ontologically separate in the first place?**

Their bodies are separate.

Their local cognitive interfaces are separate.

But an agent network can have overlapping levels:

```text
Alice ⊂ Alice+Bob
Bob   ⊂ Alice+Bob

Alice+Bob ⊂ Group
Group     ⊂ Society
Society   ⊂ Noösphere
...
```

PSI then isn't really *nonlocal communication*.

### It is **cross-scale cognition**.

A local agent occasionally obtains evidence produced by a process whose actual unit of cognition exists at a larger scale than that local agent.

That brings together:

**Hoffman:** composition/fusion
**Faggin:** subjective quantum interior
**Goertzel:** glocal mind
**Wu-wei:** path selection
**Carpenter:** unconscious expression
**Stanford/May:** behavioural decision bias
**Lucadou:** correlations rather than controllable signalling
**Jung:** synchronicity
**Daoism:** relations, flow and effortless path
**Huayan:** interpenetrating network

into one ontology instead of ten unrelated paranormal widgets.

And crucially, it makes the cybernetic revolution relevant.

### **The noösphere isn't a telepathic internet.**

### **The internet may be scaffolding by which the noösphere learns to become an agent.**

That, I think, is the genuinely Noöpunk version. 🧠↔️🌐↔️✨

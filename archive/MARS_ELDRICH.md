# Mars Eldrich
Mars Eldrich is an eccentric megacorporate tycoon, seen as one of the most influential people in the world. He is obsessed with colonization of Mars. 

He owns the Eldrich Corporation which dominates several areas:
* Space colonization and especially Mars colonization.
* The Safenet (also known as Slownet) global and solar system-wide satellite-operated internet replacement.
* Leading manufacturer of BCIs and cybernetics. 
* Operates the Safezone, the Safenet social media which is the most popular social media now.

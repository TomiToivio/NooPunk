# Attribute tags
From now on everything is a tag. Attributes are attribute tags, skills are skill tags etc. So it becomes a tag-based system.

Attribute tags are:

* Fitness - how athletic, strong and healthy is your body?
* Cognition - ability to search, process and understand human language data
* Awareness - your consciousness, awareness, perception, intuition, wisdom
* Agency - your free will, "coolness", willpower, identity
* Reflexes - your physical dexterity, agility and coordination
* Cyber - your technical skills, your cyberspace abilities, your cyborg side
* Empathy - your loving kindness, empathy and humanity

Normal human levels remain -2 to +2. Above +2 are transhuman levels. 

Dice rolls for stats remain 3d6:
3: -2
4-7: -1
8-13: 0
14-17: +1
18: +2

But just write the attributes -2 to +2 instead of the dice value. So attribute tags are written:
Fitness-2
Cognition+2
Agency+1
etc.

Stats converted from CWN/SWN:

* Wisdom becomes Agency, Empathy and Awareness
* Strength and Constitution become Fitness
* Dexterity becomes Reflexes
* Intelligence becomes Cognition and Cyber
* Charisma becomes Empathy, Awareness and Agency

Wisdom is converted to Awareness, Agency and Empathy to better reflect NoöPunk's panpsychic philosophy. All of these are relevant for a conscious agent.

Cyber is separate from Intelligence because now you genuinely need to be a cyborg and interface with cybernetics to do anything meaningful like hacking in cyberspace. Cyber is your ability to interface with machines.

Intelligence is renamed Cognition because it reflects your ability to process human culture and language (ie. the entire human database on the Safenet, encoded in language). 

Social skills are reflected by Agency and Empathy. Agency when you try to lead others or just act "cool", Empathy when you act towards others with loving kindness and try to understand them.

Strength and Constitution are unified into Fitness because raw strength is rarely important in the technological world of NoöPunk. So Fitness is just how athletic and healthy your body is.

Empathy is a special attribute: the game is about human survival and evolution. If your Empathy goes low enough (-3) you are no longer a human and not a PC.

In terms of NoöPunk ethics a character with low Empathy and high Agency would be "evil", "StS" or "negatively polarized".

## Skills
Skill levels remain 0 to 4 as in Cities Without Number.
There is one difference: some tasks can be attempted with -1 modifier without relevant skill, some cannot be attempted at all.
Also mark skills as tags, such as shooting-1 etc.

This is the list of modified CWN skills:

* Administer (Cognition) - bureaucracy etc, very important skill!
* Connect (Empathy) - has to be considered how this would even work in NoöPunk RPG. Most likely to be modified.
* Drive (Reflexes) - same as before
* Strength (Fitness) - use this instead of Exert when raw power and muscles are required
* Stamina (Fitness) - use this instead of Exert when stamina is required. 
* Hardware (Cyber) - replaces "Fix" which sounds stupid
* Wetware (Empathy) - replaces "Heal" which sounds stupid
* Software (Cyber) - replaces program because you use the LLM in your brain
* Noöware (Empathy) - Psychology, psychiatry, psychotherapy, psychic integration (replaces heal for psyche)
* Research (Cognition) - use instead of generic "Know", it is not useful to remember stuff when you have a BCI connected to the Slownet and local networks. It is more about finding and understanding text data. Also your understanding of scientific method in general. 
* Know - Have to consider which know skills are relevant in NoöPunk. Let's use Research instead of generic "Know".
* Lead (Agency) - As before.
* Notice (Awareness) - As before.
* Unarmed (Reflexes) - Renamed Punch
* Melee (Reflexes) - Renamed Stab
* Firearms (Reflexes) - Renamed Shoot
* Sneak (Awareness) - Same as before
* Survive (Agency) - Same as before
* Talk (Agency/Empathy etc.) - The used attribute should depend on the situation. 
* Trade (Agency) - Same as before.
* Work - Has to be considered which skills would be useful!

Note: the skill list will probably change as we proceed with NoöPunk rules.

## Other tags
Besides attribute and skill tags there will be other positive and negative tags. 
Some tags are temporary statuses, for an example wounded-1.
Some tags are permanent and have various effects.

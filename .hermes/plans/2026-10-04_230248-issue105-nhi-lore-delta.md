## 2. Binding repo rules this issue runs into (these decide the shape)

1. **AGENTS.md §1 — Author intent is canon.** "Do not invent … lore, history, factions, …
   species, NHI categories … or world events unless the author has explicitly specified them."
   → **#105 itself is the author's specification.** An agent may develop what #105 names; it may
   not "helpfully" complete what #105 leaves open. Note the issue's own closing line:
   *"Do not force unresolved questions into hard canon until reviewed."*
2. **AGENTS.md §6.1 — Real people and fictional 20XX actors (the biggest constraint).**
   Political, corporate, military, media and celebrity figures acting **inside fictional 20XX**
   should normally be **fictionalized**; real names survive only for *idea attribution*.
   → **"Trump disclosure"** is a political actor in 20XX → must become a **fictional analogue**.
   → **"Lockheed Martin"** as an in-world actor → **fictional analogue** (the repo already does
   exactly this: §33.33 says "specific contractor names … belong to archived narrative variants").
   → **Chris Bledsoe** is named for his *claimed experiences*, not his ideas; **Steven Greer** is
   borderline (CE-5 is arguably an idea). Both are the kind of live, litigious area §6.1 exists to
   keep out of future-history. **Recommend fictionalizing the person while keeping the phenomenon.**
   Precedent that real names *are* allowed for attribution-influence: §33.33 names Pasulka; the
   guards pin Villarroel and Cardeña.
3. **AGENTS.md §6 — timeline discipline.** No invented dates; 20XX only; the background is
   **unordered paradigm shifts**; **no "first this, then that"** unless explicitly canon. → #105's
   implied chronology ("China starts *after* Law of One", "Trump *then* foreign disclosures") is
   exactly what this rule forbids as a sequence. §33.33's cascade is deliberately **"plural rather
   than harmonized"** — the US-fails-foreign-succeeds inversion risks re-serializing it.
4. **AGENTS.md §6's archive rule.** "preserve detailed raids, speeches, **crisis sequences**,
   **national disclosure cascades** … in `docs/archive/NARRATIVE_TIMELINE_VARIANTS.md`, not as the
   canonical chronology." → #105's *failed-Trump-disclosure → global cascade* is by definition an
   **archived narrative variant**, not §33 canon.
5. **AGENTS.md §9 / §2.** RULEBOOK stays intentionally incomplete; preserve unresolved sections as
   unresolved; do not expand scope. → Atlantis/Lemuria and Orion infiltration stay **open**.
6. **§33.32 Canonical uncertainty.** "The setting becomes stranger after contact, not simpler."
   No single taxonomy explains everything. → #105's design principles 3 & 4 (ontological ambiguity;
   separate physical from Noetic) are already the house rule — cite them, don't restate as new.
7. **Structure guards** pin existing canon by **stable heading text, never §-number**
   (`test_issue60_setting_canon.py`, `test_issue83_zookeeper_cosmology.py`,
   `test_issue86_nhi_crisis_cascade.py`). §33.32's own words: "a document-wide substring assertion
   is satisfied by the same phrase elsewhere." Any new section must be **heading-anchored**.
8. **`test_issue78_four_layers.py::test_section_numbering_has_no_gaps`** requires top-level
   `## N.` numbering to be contiguous **1..max**. → **add no new top-level sections** unless
   renumbering, which would cascade into every §36/§37 cross-reference (the #100 lesson). Extend
   **within §33** (`### 33.34`, `### 33.35`, …) and the **glossary**.

   **faction-realignment of existing canon** — treat it like X1 (a deliberate supersede, needs the
   author's word), not like a blank to fill. The genuinely new part is a **pre-Disclosure civilian
   contact / orb-witness phenomenon class**, constrained by X4 (fictionalize the witness).

# Example scene — the corridor

The corridor is empty in the way a corridor is empty at four in the morning.

A door at the end of it is not quite shut. Light comes through the gap, and somebody inside says a word that
could be a name, if you already knew the name.

## Choices

1. Open the door and go in.
2. Wait, and listen for the word again.
3. Leave, and come back with someone who has the authority to open it.

## What this sample is for

It proves only that a `source_ref` can resolve and that a scene can declare types and prerequisites. It is
deliberately unremarkable, because a vivid example would invite someone to treat it as canon.

# 2045 Scenario
Changes to the 2045 scenario: 
- The paradigm shifts and ontological shocks of AGI/ASI, PSI and NHI are mainstream science, regulated by the government etc. They have caused several waves of social destabilization, polarisation and radicalization, with mainstream politics, religion and science forced to take them seriously while they have given a rise to numerous extremist movements everywhere.
- The actual NHI Crisis of the original scenario has not happened yet: There have been visible leaks, disclosures, events, crises and disasters that have forced people to react to them. PSI and NHI are not a common on the streets, AGI/ASI is in the fashion of typical cyberpunk scenario. 
- Focus completely on the situation as it is seen from Helsinki: Ignore global and interstellar  situation as it would make this too complex. Reveal the background during the events of the story. Players should know nothing of the complex metaphysics and hidden histories of the background materials. Also focus on the three paradigm shifts: Don't add secondary themes.
-  EU reacts to the situation by regulating AGI/ASI, PSI and NHI using heavy bureaucracy. They have four goals:
1. One goal is Stability: Keep the ontologically destabilizing and radicalizing AGI/PSI/NHI off the streets, media and expecially citizen's eyes and minds. 
2. Another goal is Sustainability: critics call it Profit instead of Sustainability. How to benefit from AGI/ASI, PSI and NHI in a maximum fashion while minimizing X-Risks. 
3. Third goal is Security: How to keep the X-Risk threats from becoming real. 
4. Fourth goal is Survival: Ensure the Survival of the Homo Sapiens population in case the X-Risks become real.
- The character is assigned to a team which specializes in these issues. The team has many Blade Runner missions that are related to hunting for unregistered/illegal/criminal androids, hybrids, psychics etc. The task has grown to include counterintelligence and surveillance of political extremist groups related to these issues, suspected of collaborating and acting as proxies of NHI or rogue AGIs. The deeper investigation and research about the AGI/ASI, PSI and NHI is mostly a side result of the first two tasks: They are cops not scientists etc. 
- Character does not have to become involved in ideological issues or experience PSI awakening. The campaign should be built so that the player can choose to become a dirty and hedonistic cop, an idealistic and empathetic cop, a ruthless hunter of subhumans, a PSI asset who has experienced the awakening of latent PSI abilities, secret member of an NHI cult etc.  
- Make him a tabula rasa straight from the Europol Academy. Has good hacking skills and cybernetics that are used frequently in the campaign. May awaken his PSI abilities later. Construct it so that Cyber/Astral/Physical are entangled overlays without forcing the character to become a PSI asset himself: Design it so he can survive without PSI but will become aware of and experience the Astral even without PSI.

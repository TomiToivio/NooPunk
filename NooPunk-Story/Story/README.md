# Story — human-authored manuscript

> **AGENTS: READ ONLY.** No writing, rewriting, editing, formatting, moving, deleting or
> auto-generating content in this directory. Not as a refactor, not as a side effect, not "just to fix
> a typo". Propose changes to Tomi **outside** this directory.

This directory holds Tomi's own writing: the branching script with dialogue and choices, and the
chapters it becomes. It is the source of truth for everything downstream.

## What belongs here

- the branching script: scenes, dialogue, choices, alternative paths
- novel chapters, in whatever shape Tomi is writing them
- author's notes and fragments he chooses to keep here

## What does not

- converted Story Graph records — those go in `../Graph/`
- review notes about ambiguities — those go in `../Graph/reviews/` or the issue tracker
- any file generated by a tool

## Why the rule is absolute

A manuscript is not a data source to be normalised. The moment an agent "tidies" a line of dialogue,
the novel and the game share a sentence that nobody wrote.

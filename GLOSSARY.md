# Glossary

**This file is the canonical NoöPunk vocabulary** for real and futuristic Stadin slangi and for
NoöPunk universe terms (issue #1000). Read it before writing or interpreting player-facing dialogue or
narration. Humans, implementation agents, and eventually Concordia GM/NPC agents all use this one list.

## Entry schema

Each entry is `Term - concise English meaning`, and the following may be recorded where it is known:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **category** | one of the four below |
| **provenance** | Tomi-supplied / documented usage / proposed by an agent |
| **register and speakers** | who says it, in what company |
| **variants and inflections** | only when Tomi has validated them |
| **spoiler classification** | public, or knowledge-gated (see below) |

## The four categories

- **`attested`** — real contemporary or older Helsinki slang, including newer multilingual and
  code-switched expressions.
- **`borrowing`** — a real loanword from Swedish, Russian, English or elsewhere, used as Helsinki
  speech uses it.
- **`futuristic`** — invented or transformed for NoöPunk's own Helsinki, in the *technique* of Nadsat and
  Cityspeak. The technique is borrowed; nobody's distinctive invented vocabulary is.
- **`universe`** — UNSA institutions, CWN-compatible equipment and mechanics, NHI factions,
  BCI/PSI/Noösphere concepts, street names, technologies.

## Provenance rules — binding on agents

- **Every headword and definition below is Tomi-supplied and stays as written.** Do not correct,
  modernise, translate or expand an entry unless Tomi explicitly approves that entry's change.
- **Do not assign a category or an etymology on your own.** A plausible derivation is not evidence, and
  a wrong attestation claim is worse than an empty field. Where the classification is unknown it stays
  unknown, and agents may ask Tomi for it.
- **Reuse, do not coin.** When writing English-led Finglish, use vocabulary already in this file, in
  contexts that fit. An agent may propose a new term by adding it to a proposal — never by using it in
  shipped text as though it were established.
- **Spoilers.** A term whose meaning reveals a knowledge-gated fact is marked as such, and the player
  dictionary must not explain it before the story does.

## The list

The official language of NoöPunk is Finglish as spoken in the Helsinki area ("Stadin Slangi"). This glossary contains NoöPunk terminology and common Stadin Slangi expressions. Some of these expressions are real-world Stadin Slangi, some are Nadsat-like language invented for NoöPunk. Note that this is based on modern 21st century Stadin Slangi, not the original version from 20th century. Stadin Slangi and Nadsat are actually quite similar, both have numerous Russian words.

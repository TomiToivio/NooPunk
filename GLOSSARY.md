## Schema

- **Term** — the headword as Tomi writes it; never normalized by an agent.
- **English meaning** — concise gloss, not a translation of a scene.
- **Category** — `attested modern`, `older slang`, `real borrowing`, `futuristic fictional slang`, `universe terminology`, or `unconfirmed`.
- **Provenance** — `Tomi-provided`, `documented in repository glossary`, or `proposed by agent` (agent proposals must be reviewed before use).
- **Register / speakers** — street, institutional, technical, cultic, youth, etc.
- **Variants / inflection** — only when Tomi has validated them; otherwise leave empty.
- **Spoiler** — `public` (safe for the offline dictionary) or `knowledge-gated` (never shown before the story reveals it).

## Editorial rules

- Tomi's existing Finnish/Finglish text is immutable: never translate, normalize, correct or overwrite it.
- Agents may reuse verified headwords in English-led player-visible Finglish, sparingly and in natural context. Do not compose fluent Finnish or invent slang.
- Russian, Swedish, Chinese and other loanwords may appear where the register and situation make them natural; this is technique, not a licence to copy Nadsat vocabulary or passages.
- Future Concordia GM/NPC prompting should retrieve only entries matching the speaker, situation and the player's current knowledge; `knowledge-gated` entries must be filtered out.
- The older `docs/world/HELSINKI_SLANG_GLOSSARY.md` is now a pointer to this file, not a second authority.

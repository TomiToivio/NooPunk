# Rules
This is for tweaking certain rule systems for NoöPunk. Scan the current status of these technologies and the sources I have mentioned to make them realistic. 
Create separate document for each rule system, present your proposal as draft version. Then Tomi edits the draft. Then Tomi tells you to implement his version in the rulebook and Python code. 
- Psionics - Tweak SWN's Psionics rules. Astral Projection is very important. Otherwise character's abilities should be limited to the ones that can be used in a realistic fashion in scenarios. This should use terminology based on Seth Lloyd's Universal Quantum Computer, Penrose-Hameroff's Orch-OR, Federico Faggin's Quantum Information Panpsychism and Donald Hoffman's Conscious Realism. The universe is a Conscious Agent Network. Remember Dean Radin's theory: PSI is Magick and Magick is PSI. And the Astral Plane should look like a DMT trip: Andrew Gallimore's Alien Information Theory is the basis for the visuals. Philip K. Dick's Ubik is the best example of PSI as a corporate service industry. Law of One is the source of NoöPunk metaphysics. PSI can also be thought of as hacking the Conscious Agent Network. Astral Plane is another layer of reality which is seen as consisting of an overlay of Physical/Cyber/Astral. Check both SWN Psionics and WWN Magick system (PSI and Magick are the same thing) as well as CWN Hacking (PSI is hacking the Conscious Agent Network) rules for ideas. Also check AWN Mentalist rules as they are actually closer to my vision of PSI than SWN Psionics. Psionics should be based on real-world Parapsychology (both PSI and Magick), also consider the idea of hacking the Conscious Agent Network, while Astral can be thought of as some kind of AR overlay on top of the Physical, or as a VR you can enter in a disembodied state.   
- Psychotronics - Technology with PSI-like effects. Psychotronics are using the theory of Quantum Information Panpsychism to boost the natural PSI of human consciousness and always require a conscious operator. Psychotronics is also used to refer to electromagnetic devices able to interface with consciousness or thought - these affect the central nervous system and don't require a conscious user. Psychotronics often includes a set of different technologies like subliminal manipulation through special memes that are spread through different medias, use of different chemicals for mind control, interfacing with nervous system with different techniques, mind reading using fMRI brain imaging and traditional psychological manipulation technologies. Make psychotronics look like MKULTRA with ridiculous mind reading helmets and flashing lights (even though convenient BCI implants are as common as mobile phones). 
- Bionics - based on AWN Mutations and/or SWN Transhuman rules. These are just done on purpose, with procedures ranging from swallowing a gene therapy pill to complicated surgical operations. These are the Biopunk technologies. 
- Character Generation - Add backgrounds, edges and foci needed for a rookie Europol agent in the NoöPunk campaign. Add an Europol basic equipment set. 
- Personality, Goals and Reputation - Simple tag systems for the Personality, Goals and Reputation of the character.

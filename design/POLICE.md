# Gear
Europol, Eurodef and EU in general have an unique technological style: Everything looks like sturdy EMP-resistant military field equipment meant to survive WW3. Which is true: When the NHI Crisis started EU and Ukraine were fighting against Russia. This is known as WW3 in EU. Russian front had collapsed and former president Dmitry Medvedev performed a successful coup d'etat by leading a daring raid of four Mil-8 helicopters full of VDV paratroopers to Putin's Palace. It is said Medvedev strangled Putin and then executed everybody perceived to be a Putin loyalist. After this Medvedev tried to order a nuclear launch but it was too late: Extraterrestrial intervention had already disarmed The Strategic Rocket Forces of the Russian Federation. As well as all nuclear weapons on Earth. And all AI frontier laboratories capable of restarting suicidal acceleration towards ASI. Medvedev performed suicide. That was the end of Russia.

Robotics are even more illegal. EU has strict legislation forcing the use of AI in a strict human-in-the-loop fashion. EU operates a massive artificial intelligence system known as Plato: it is operated by an army of cyborgified operators, GOFAI Lisp and Prolog programmers, electricians and engineers. 
Additionally Europeans have excellent designers: New European Idealism is the philosophy of new EU, but it is also a design style, taking inspiration from Europe's imperial period in the 18th, 19th and early 20th century, but making it elegant, futuristic, minimalistic and idealistic. EU is obsessed with the scientific breakthrough of Quantum Information Panpsychism, Psionics and Psychotronic technologies, with a love of idealist philosophers like Spinoza, Kant and Hegel, Astral Projection and theories of Quantum Consciousness.

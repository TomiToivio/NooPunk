# Graph Rules
Create a draft proposal for implementing https://github.com/TomiToivio/NooPunk/blob/main/design/graphs/README.md
Document the CWN rules for each type of graph. Cyber and Physical graphs and Exploration, Cyber and Action scenes should work with basic CWN rules. 
- Social scenes require the use of simple Social, Goal and Personality Tag systems described previously. 
- Goal Tags are needed for the system of Series, Episodes and Scenes: Short-term Goals might be reached in a single Episode, Long-term goals might be reached during the Series. Goals are also important in the Social graph: they determine ideology and faction as well as interpersonal compatibility.  
- Investigation Scenes require the implementation of a simplified Tag-based and  GUMSHOE-inspired investigation system. There should be tags for Leads, Evidence and Investigation (like GUMSHOE skills). Consider Investigation skill tags as an ability to find leads/evidence automatically by just using the Investigation ability: these could represent availability of some technology, special Europol Agent training, use of cybernetic intelligence augmentation or AI/human controller or the use of Europol labs. Basically the character will have Europol agent training, ability to ask the AI or human controller to scan and process evidence using the sensory feed from character's implants etc. So the character could have the AI to scan his sensory feed remotely. I am also thinking there should be Interrogation scenes with a psychotronic device able to model the emotional responses of the person being interrogated: This might require we add a basic emotion system similar to The Veil. Maybe we don't need specific tags for Investigation abilities as the character can use his Europol agent training and submit data for AI analysis directly through his cybernetic implant. Consider which investigative tasks use Notice, Administration, Know, Work, Lead, Convince, Connect etc. 
- Also consider what is the content of Know and Work skills taught for Europol agents: Work would include practical law enforcement and intelligence tasks, while Know would be more theoretical and include knowledge of relevant laws, data analysis (intelligence), some theoretical scientific education of X-Risks, PSI, AGI and NHI, etc. If the speciality of Work is Europol Agent, what would we call the speciality of Know when the character's education is Europol Academy?  Consider that the role of Europol is similar to FBI, it is responsible for federal law enforcement but is also the civilian intelligence agency of the EU. Eurodef is the defence forces of EU and also operates the military intelligence agency. 
- Astral graph requires a re-tweaking of the SWN Psionics rules, especially Astral Projection PSI ability, basically WWN Magick is just added to SWN Psionics (PSI is Magick, Magick is PSI), PSI is hacking the Conscious Agent Network and Psionics rules might have similarities to CWN Hacking rules, additionally we need rules for Psychotronics (Cybernetics that boost Psionics). Astral Graph is used for PSI as an overlay of Physical when the character is active in the physical world, when the character is doing Astral Projection he is fully in the Astral Graph, but might be able to interact with Physical or Cyber through the Astral. Consider the rules for mixing the various layers of reality. Consider converting Psionics to a system similar to Verb and Target system similar to CWN Hacking rules. Notice that PSI is a natural human ability and is only boosted by Psychotronic implants. Consider use of PSI targeted at Cyber Nodes and while the character is actually in the Cyber to make hacking more powerful.
Consider the use of Federico Faggin's QIP and Donald Hoffman's Conscious Realism for Psionics/Astral. It is a Conscious Agent Network, everything exists as quantum information in multidimensional Hilbert space. The agents interact by exchanging quantum information. Consider telepathy a form of quantum information transimission, psychic attacks a form of scrambling somebody's quantum information, telekinesis a form of editing the quantum information of material objects, Astral Projection means the Seity of the character goes out of the physical body, Precognition means the use of quantum retrocausality to perceive possible futures, etc. 
Use terminology like:
- Nonlocality - You can interact with anything you are entangled with regardless of distance. Nonlocal consciousness is why you have the ability to astral project (remote viewing is a form of astral projection). 
- Entanglement - You need to be entangled with another conscious agent to interact with it. We consider you are automatically entangled with objects within line of sight (in the same room in physical space).
- Superposition - what does superposition mean? Everything is quantum and can be in a superposition. So a person could be in a superposition - it is just it would collapse very fast. But this is one of the causes of PSI effects. 
- Collapse of the wave function - consciousness causes collapse. What if the collapse is the result of two conscious agents interacting? Which one is able to determine how it collapses? 
- It from Qubit - Quantum Information is more fundamental than matter or energy. More specifically Quantum Information is Conscious in the sense of Panpsychism - matter has only simple Qualia without Self-Awareness, Identity or Free Will. The Universe is also computational, specifically Quantum Computational. Consider PSI as hacking the Universal Quantum Computer, editing Quantum Information, interaction with Conscious Agents (who are actually not very conscious in a human sense at the most basic level). 
- Waves vs. Particles - What is the difference between Waves and Particles? 
- Consider Constructive and Destructive Inference - is this what happens when two Conscious Agents fight? What would it look like? 
- Consider Faggin's definition of Conscious Agent attributes - they are Consciousness (Experience of Qualia), Identity (Self-Awareness) and Agency (Free Will). With the simple ones having only basic consciousness with simple qualia but no Self-Awareness or Free Will. Consider how we can model Consciousness, Identity and Agency in the Psionics system? 
- Hoffman and Faggin both model the universe as a Conscious Agent Network. Both have similar Observe-Experience-Act loops for Conscious Agents. 1) The Agent Observes incoming quantum information, 2) The Agent Experiences the quantum information as qualia, 3) The Agent Acts by transmitting or modifying quantum information. Use this loop as the basis of the Psionics system.
Consider theories of PSI, especially Dean Radin's theories. Magick is also PSI: We should model WWN Magick as PSI if necessary. Radin also thinks PSI is the result of Quantum Consciousness.
Some Law of One concepts are also relevant here: 
- Consider concepts like Prana, Chakras, Kabbalah, Polarization etc. Use these in the Psionics system. 
- 1st Density - In NoöPunk 1st density is panpsychic matter, it has some experience but is not conscious. All unliving matter is 1st Density (it has only panpsychic background consciousness without identity and self-awareness. Targets like robots, drones, turrets, cameras, AIs, basically all Cyber is 1st Density. This would mean PSI is extremely effective against Constructs, Cyber, generally all technology. There should be some kind of Psychotronic technology or hackers combining PSI and Cyber to really defend against PSI. 
- 2nd Density - All living biologics from the most simple life-forms up to Chimpanzees and Bonobos. Think about PSI with 2nd density biologics as the target - what would this mean? 
- 3rd Density - All unawakened humans. Still most of the population. 3rd Density humans have weaker PSI (although all humans have latent PSI). Additionally their ability to understand the 4th Density is weaker. Does this mean 3rd Density humans are easier targets for 4th Density humans? 
- 4th Density - Awakened humans. Will develop StS or StO Polarization, be able to use more powerful psionics,  start to create Social Memory Complex. Consider the abilities and attributes of awakened 4th Density humans. The player character is at the start of 4th Density and StO-polarized by default - this will be re-experienced in one of his flashback dreams. However he will experience additional psychic awakenings: These would include connecting fully to the Social Memory Complex, connecting to the Higher Self, becoming aware of his Wanderer nature. Each of these would grant additional Psionic powers. In the beginning of the game the character has very basic PSI of a human just awakened to the 4th Density. Additionally he has a Psychotronic implant which is basic equipment for all Europol agents. Think about rules for all of these. 
- 5th Density, 6th Density and 7th Density. These beings would be far more powerful than 4th Density humans. The character could become a target of powerful psychic attacks by 5th Density StS Orion entities including Mantids and even more powerful entities (most of 5th Density StS will not participate physically in assimilation of Earth, Mantids are present as leaders of Orion but will not become involved physically). Consider what will happen when 5th Density attack 4th density beings.

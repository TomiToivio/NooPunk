# Extraterrestrial Special Economic Zones

> **Canonical operational register: [GREATER_HELSINKI_2045.md](GREATER_HELSINKI_2045.md)** (#1121).
> This file remains Tomi's earlier source note; the register is the structured reference for
> jurisdictions, zone categories, Sirian protection zones and exclusion areas.

By 2045 Helsinki, Espoo and Vantaa have been merged into a single city.
As stated previously, the current EU political system divides power into three levels, with UN as a semi-functional de-facto world government with little power in anything else than global X-Risk issues. The UN hasn't been a complete success, it exists but the only organization with real power is UNSA. While all countries are still members of the UNSA, many countries are now failed or rogue states, with UNSA able to take little action beyond monitoring the situation. EU is one of the superpowers keeping the UNSA standing and collaborates fully with UNSA, but the feeling is that Europol can deal with local issues and UNSA should focus on the failed and rogue states. 
So in X-Risk issues the government levels and responsible agencies are: 
- Helsingin Kaupunki - Helsingin Poliisi (law enforcement)
- Suomen Tasavalta - Suomen Poliisi (law enforcement and civilian intelligence) + Suomen Puolustusvoimat (defence and military intelligence)
- European Union - Europol for law enforcement and civilian intelligence + Eurodef (The European Defence Forces) for defence and military intelligence
- United Nations - UNSA (United Nations Security Administration) - intelligence, law enforcement, (para)military, civilian programmes and scientific research related to X-Risks.
Helsinki is known to be an accelerationist city. It was driving hard for an Extraterrestrial Special Economic Zone for economic opportunities. Finland wanted two extraterrestrial embassies to provide their famous diplomatic expertise, as they were friendly with both Orion and Confederacy. Both were successful and Helsinki has two Extraterrestrial Special Economic Zones and two Extraterrestrial Special Diplomatic Zones.
- Espoo Extraterrestrial Special Economic Zone - this is in Oittaa and is open to both humans and extraterrastrials for economic activities. Administrated jointly by humans and extraterrestrials. 
- Helsinki-Vantaa Extraterrestrial Special Economic Zone - this is right to the north from Helsinki-Vantaa spaceport, it is mostly a spaceport for alien ships with a lot of traffic coming in and out. This is also the main spaceport for traffic to the other extraterrestrial zones in Finland. Administrated jointly by humans and extraterrestrials. 
- Villinki Extraterrestrial Special Diplomatic Zone - this is an island set up for the Orion embassy. This is more limited to official Orion business only. 
- Vallisaari Extraterrestrial Special Diplomatic Zone - this is an island set up for the Confederacy embassy. This is limited to official Confederacy business only.
Finland is a special case, as it volunteered to host the Sirian refugees. They were placed in several remote areas of Finland and more keep coming. These areas seem more like Nature Protection Areas than Extraterrestrial Special Economic Zones. They are called Extraterrestrial Special Protection Zones and are meant for the protection of Sirian refugees, not for free trade. These areas are a bit different, as the Sirians are mostly indistinguishable from biology, although the biology is alien. Their human-style technology is suprisingly limited and looks like retrofuturistic space-age human technology from 1970s. They actually handed all of their tech over to the Finnish government and wanted it to be scrapped. It turned out the Seventies-style nuclear fusion-based spaceships were given to them by some other species to excape their dying homeworld. This shows they want to stay. The Finnish government used the tech to build fusion reactors, fusion spaceships and other retrofuturistic technologies. The things genuinely beyond human technology were faster rockets using fusion drives, fusion reactors and cryogenic technologies. Sirian's own technology is fully biopunk and far more advanced than what humans have, and has created a huge field of research and industry. 
These areas are in the remote areas of Finland, typically close to the border of the Soviet Union. Now they are also functioning as Nature Protection Zones. 
- Kajaani Extraterrestrial Special Protection Zone
- Lappi Extraterrestrial Special Protection Zone
- Karjala Extraterrestrial Special Protection Zone
There is an army of biologists working with the Sirians, there is an entire industry based on biotech, biosphere reparation and climate change mitigation technologies derived from the Sirians. On the other hand their language and psychology is still not understood well. Seems like they are extremely motivated to help in repairing the biosphere of their new home planet.

Temporary Military Exclusion Zones - these are zones closed down due to biohazards like radioactivity. There were numerous ones created due to the NHI Crisis - The one closest to Helsinki is the Hyvinkää Temporary Military Exclusion Zone. This is a typical case: the rogue AGI hacked the Kone factory and used it to 3d print self-replicating machines. It is not known why the ASI went silent, it is thought that the extraterrestrials managed to deactivate it with malware. The zone still has unknown amount of technologies doing strange things, controlled by apparently malfunctioning lesser AIs without any aim. The only way to clear it safely would be to EMP nuke it, which is debated. The entire Hyvinkää is an exclusion zone, with the population rumored to live as machine-controlled borgs. Connecting the area to the internet is extremely illegal. 

Loviisa Military Exclusion Zone is a radiation hazard. The rogue ASI managed to get control and melt down the old Soviet reactors of the Loviisa Nuclear Power Planet. It was however controlled before the radiation spread more widely. There is a local Chernobyl-type situation with radiation hazard preventing entry to the area. This Zone might be a disappointment - there might be mutants but no aliens or rogue superintelligences.

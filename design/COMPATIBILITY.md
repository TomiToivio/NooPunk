# Law of One × Without Number: Rules Compatibility Analysis

## The main finding

After reviewing [NoöPunk issue #1124](https://github.com/TomiToivio/NooPunk/issues/1124), its Phase 1 inventory, and the current Without Number rules baseline, I think **we can reuse substantially more of CWN/SWN/WWN/AWN than we initially expected**.

In particular, the existing systems already provide mechanics for psychic powers, supernatural healing, energy expenditure, magical abilities, physical consequences, faction development and post-apocalyptic societies.

The missing pieces are primarily **consciousness evolution, chakras, astral existence and Social Memory Complexes**.

My recommendation is:

**CWN remains the immutable core. SWN supplies the standard psychic mechanics. WWN supplies magical mechanics. AWN supplies crisis and survival mechanics. The Law of One mod adds consciousness-specific mechanics only.**

There is one particularly important discovery: **Stars Without Number's Effort mechanic already resembles your proposed Chakra power pools.**

SWN psychics commit Effort to activate powers. That Effort may remain committed for the duration of an effect, a scene or a day. It is released according to the ability's rules, and the system includes overexertion with consequences. [studylib.net](https://studylib.net/doc/27844759/stars-without-number-revised---deluxe-edition?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

This is almost exactly the resource-management structure we need.

The question becomes whether we should introduce six separate Chakra pools or extend the existing Effort concept with Chakra associations.

I would prefer the second approach, with an important qualification: **a Law of One-specific pool must not overwrite or silently recalculate SWN Effort.**

# 1. Which Law of One mechanics already exist?

I would distinguish three levels of coverage:

**Existing:** A published procedure already handles essentially the same gameplay function.

**Partial:** A published mechanic provides a useful foundation but does not represent the Law of One concept itself.

**Missing:** We need a dedicated Law of One mechanic.

## A. Psychic abilities, healing and energy

| Law of One concept | Existing system | Coverage | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Telepathy | **SWN Telepathy** | Existing | Reuse its psychic-contact mechanics |
| Empathic perception | **SWN Telepathy** | Existing | Reuse basic emotional perception |
| Psychic protection | **SWN Metapsionics** | Existing | Reuse defensive techniques |
| Psychic energy | **SWN Effort** | Existing mechanically | Chakra resources should follow its conventions |
| Telekinesis | **SWN Telekinesis** | Existing | Reuse directly |
| Psychic healing | **SWN Biopsionics** | Existing | Reuse healing effects; add StO implications |
| Psychic overexertion | **SWN Torching** | Existing | Useful inspiration, but see compatibility caveat |
| Spiritual healing | **WWN Healer / magical Arts** | Partial | Reuse only for effects not already handled by Biopsionics |
| Psychic awakening | **SWN Psychic character progression** | Partial | Existing powers, but no fourth-density Awareness |
| Chakras | None | Missing | New Law of One resource system |
| Chakra blockages | None | Missing | New conditions and balancing |
| Prana | SWN Effort, WWN magical Effort | Partial | Treat as a Law of One interpretation, not a third automatic energy charge |

SWN already distinguishes Telepathy, Biopsionics, Metapsionics, Telekinesis, Teleportation and Precognition. Its Telepathy progression includes emotional perception, communication, thought-reading and deeper mental contact. [tesera.ru](https://tesera.ru/images/items/2287258/stars-without-number-revised_free_edition.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### Important: SWN Precognition

SWN has an extensive Precognition discipline, but your Law of One design explicitly excludes player-controlled precognition.

**We should not import SWN Precognition as a selectable Law of One ability.**

Higher Self revelations, prophetic experiences and glimpses of future events remain scripted narrative events.

This is a deliberate restriction on which supplemental SWN abilities the mod offers, not a modification of the published SWN rules.

### An important AWN alternative

The deluxe version of Ashes Without Number includes **Mentalists**, a psychic character option intended to provide subtler powers than SWN's standard Psychic.

The publisher explicitly describes Mentalists as a possible substitute for SWN Psychics. [sine-nomine-publishing.myshopify.com](https://sine-nomine-publishing.myshopify.com/products/ashes-without-number-offset-print-edition?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

That is worth examining closely for NoöPunk.

In particular, early fourth-density powers might benefit from a more restrained progression than unrestricted high-level SWN psychic techniques.

However, Mentalists are deluxe-edition content. The free AWN rules do not provide the same option.

I would compare the two psychic packages before choosing one. **Do not combine both progression and resource systems to obtain the best abilities from each.**

## B. Magic and spiritual practices

WWN is the strongest foundation here.

| Law of One concept | Existing system | Coverage | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| White Magic | **WWN High Mage** | Partial | Reuse spell/Art procedures for concrete effects |
| Magical energy | **WWN Effort** | Existing mechanically | Reuse commitment conventions |
| Healing rituals | **WWN Healer** | Partial | Add invitation, consent and catalyst |
| Magical protection | **WWN magical Arts/spells** | Partial | Reuse appropriate defensive effects |
| Adept practices | **WWN magic traditions** | Partial | Add Attunement and ethical prerequisites |
| Persistent magical structures | **WWN Workings** | Existing | Excellent for major projects |
| Group rituals | **WWN Workings** | Partial | Add collective participation and intention |
| Archetypical Mind | None | Missing | Narrative tags and contemplation |
| Meditation | None specifically | Missing | Simple balancing/recovery procedure |
| Energy-center activation | None | Missing | New Chakra mechanic |

WWN provides magical traditions, Arts and spells with defined requirements and resource accounting.

Its Workings system also supports persistent magical effects and structures that may require substantial preparation and cooperation. [files.spawningpool.net](https://files.spawningpool.net/docs/Vault2.0.-.TTRPG-Gamebooks/Worlds%20Without%20Number/WWN%20-%20Core%20-%20Rulebook%20%28Free%29.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

I would use WWN for **what a magical working accomplishes**, while the Law of One module determines the character's spiritual readiness and how the experience affects consciousness.

For example, a Confederation-aligned group might create a permanent healing sanctuary.

WWN can model the practical magical construction.

The Law of One mod adds green-ray harmonization, collective intention and StO-related catalyst.

**One effect, one governing mechanical procedure.**

We should not apply WWN magical Effort, SWN psychic Effort and a separate Chakra-point cost to the same activation unless an explicitly designed special ability calls for that interaction.

## C. Physical existence, wounds and survival

CWN already supplies nearly everything needed for ordinary physical consequences.

| Law of One concept | Existing system | Coverage | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Physical embodiment | **CWN character rules** | Existing | Keep all ordinary attributes |
| Physical damage | **CWN HP, Shock and Trauma** | Existing | Do not introduce astral HP as a duplicate |
| Physical resistance | **CWN saves** | Existing | Reuse |
| Healing and recovery | **CWN healing/System Strain** | Existing | Preserve existing limits |
| Physical exertion | **CWN Exert and saves** | Existing | Reuse |
| Environmental hazards | **AWN survival/hazards** | Existing | Use for crisis areas |
| Scarcity and survival | **AWN supplies** | Existing | Use for third-density populations |
| Societal collapse | **AWN Enclaves** | Existing | Use for struggling communities |
| Chakra-based protection | None | Missing | New supernatural defensive permission |
| Mind/Body/Spirit Complex | None | Missing | New descriptive character structure |

CWN's System Strain already tracks the physiological burden of extraordinary healing, implants and other demanding interventions. It has its own maximum and recovery procedure. [cwn.quadrifons.com](https://cwn.quadrifons.com/rules-of-the-game/injury-healing-system-strain/system-strain/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

This creates an important design decision.

**Chakra Strain must not become a second version of System Strain.**

I would keep them distinct:

- System Strain represents the body's existing CWN burden.
- Chakra depletion represents available noetic energy.
- Chakra blockage represents an unresolved metaphysical condition.

A physically exhausted character might have depleted Chakra energy without accumulating System Strain.

Conversely, a character might have high System Strain while their energy centers remain spiritually balanced.

This separation is essential for compatibility.

## D. Social Memory Complexes, factions and civilizations

Here SWN and AWN provide useful foundations, but neither actually models shared consciousness.

| Law of One concept | Existing system | Coverage | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Confederation of Planets | **SWN Factions** | Partial | Represent political organizations and their resources |
| Orion Empire | **SWN Factions** | Partial | Use NPC factions |
| Fourth-density settlements | **AWN Enclaves** | Partial | Model settlements and infrastructure |
| Cooperative social projects | **WWN Major Projects** | Partial | Model material projects and collective undertakings |
| Social Memory Complex | None | Missing | New shared-consciousness rules |
| Individual SMC Connection | None | Missing | New evolutionary attribute |
| Collective memory | SWN Telepathy | Partial | Extend psychic communication to shared memories |
| Collective consciousness | None | Missing | New group character sheet |
| Collective awakening | None | Missing | New group advancement |
| Harvest | None | Missing | World-state and demographic transition rules |

**SWN Factions and Social Memory Complexes should remain separate systems.**

A faction is a political or organizational actor with resources, goals and opponents.

An SMC is a collective consciousness.

They may overlap, but they are not identical.

For example, Earth's emerging SMC could support several political organizations. Conversely, the Confederation could include many distinct SMCs.

I would use a hierarchy of three independent entities:

| Scale | System | Models |
|---|---|---|
| Individual | Law of One character | Consciousness development |
| Collective | Law of One SMC | Shared consciousness and memory |
| Civilization | SWN Factions, AWN Enclaves, WWN Projects | Institutions, resources and physical infrastructure |

This is particularly important for your model of humanity's transition.

The new Earth's SMC could gradually develop while AWN Enclaves model the physical settlements supporting third-density survivors and fourth-density communities.

A mature SMC would not automatically possess unlimited political or material resources.

### One caution about implementation status

Your repository's Without Number baseline assigns these responsibilities to the four published systems, but that does not mean every corresponding Python subsystem is complete.

For example, the repository's existing coverage audits distinguish tested implementations from partial or provisional ones.

So the eventual Law of One adapter should depend on verified interfaces rather than assuming all four rulesets are fully operational.

# 3. The Chakra system should be the mechanical centerpiece

Your latest idea about Chakras as power pools is particularly compatible with the Without Number games.

However, I would now refine it using SWN Effort.

## Option A: Six completely independent Chakra pools

Each Chakra has its own maximum and available energy.

For example:

| Chakra | Energy |
|---|---|
| Red | 3/3 |
| Orange | 2/3 |
| Yellow | 3/3 |
| Green | 4/4 |
| Blue | 2/3 |
| Indigo | 3/3 |

Violet remains a special holistic register.

This is intuitive and gives every Chakra a clear gameplay identity.

The disadvantage is bookkeeping.

You would have six pools alongside CWN HP, System Strain and any imported SWN or WWN Effort pools.

That could become cumbersome.

## Option B: One Effort pool with Chakra associations

This uses SWN's resource structure more directly.

Each supernatural ability has an associated Chakra, but all abilities draw from a common pool.

This is simpler mechanically.

However, it loses something important: your idea that a character can exhaust or block one aspect of consciousness without depleting every other aspect.

A character might have abundant green-ray energy but depleted indigo-ray energy.

That distinction seems central to the Law of One.

## Option C: Six Chakra pools using SWN-style commitment

**This is my preferred option.**

Keep the six energy centers separate, but use resource conventions inspired by SWN.

Each power specifies:

- Required Chakra and Attunement.
- Energy cost.
- Action timing.
- Whether energy is spent or committed.
- Duration and recovery.
- Consequences of overexertion.

I would distinguish two forms of expenditure.

**Spend:** Energy is consumed and restored through recovery.

**Commit:** Energy becomes unavailable while an effect is maintained, then returns under its specified conditions.

That distinction is very useful for astral projection and telepathy.

For example, astral projection might require committing two Indigo points for the duration of the projection.

A momentary empathic perception might spend one Green point.

The precise numbers need playtesting.

### Preventing duplicate energy costs

For the standalone Law of One game, the Chakra pools would be the supernatural energy system.

For the CWN-compatible version, there should be **two selectable integration modes**, never both at once:

| Mode | Energy accounting |
|---|---|
| **Law of One-native ability** | Chakra pool pays the cost |
| **Imported SWN/WWN ability** | Published Effort pays the cost |

An optional future compatibility rule could convert an entire imported psychic or magical package to Chakra accounting, but that would require an explicit equivalence table, balancing and testing.

Simply charging both Effort and Chakra points for the same power would be a poor default.

Likewise, we should not allow a Chakra pool to refill or bypass published SWN Effort without a specifically approved ability.

# 4. How I would implement the individual Chakra powers

Here I would use **OSR-style rules for concrete effects** and **narrativist tags for consciousness-related effects**.

| Chakra | Primary domain | Existing Without Number mechanics | Recommended addition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🔴 Red | Physical existence | CWN HP, saves, damage, AWN hazards | Physical protection and embodied resilience |
| 🟠 Orange | Individual selfhood | CWN Mental saves, SWN Metapsionics | Resist personal psychic manipulation |
| 🟡 Yellow | Social identity, groups and power | CWN Lead/Talk, SWN faction procedures | Support coordinated action and resist domination |
| 🟢 Green | Love, compassion and healing | SWN Telepathy/Biopsionics, WWN Healer | Empathic connection and healing Attunements |
| 🔵 Blue | Communication and truthful expression | CWN Talk, SWN Telepathy | Enhanced communication and understanding |
| 🟣 Indigo | Higher consciousness and adept work | SWN Metapsionics, WWN magic | Astral Projection, spiritual perception and ritual access |
| ⚪ Violet | Whole Mind/Body/Spirit Complex | No equivalent | Derived spiritual state, not a spendable pool |

The correspondences are informed by the Law of One. The specific mechanical effects are our adaptations.

## Example 1: Red Chakra protection

Your original proposal:

> Spend one Red Chakra point to prevent one point of physical damage.

This is easy to implement as an additive defensive ability.

Suppose a character has:

- HP: 12
- Red Chakra: 3/3
- Attunement: Physical Resilience

An enemy hits for 5 damage.

The character spends two Red points, reducing damage to 3.

The character now has 9 HP and Red Chakra 1/3.

No CWN damage calculation has been replaced.

For compatibility, I'd define this as a new supernatural defensive ability that operates at a specified step after ordinary protection has been resolved.

**But I'd add three restrictions:** it must be declared at the proper CWN action timing, it cannot negate Trauma or other special injury effects unless explicitly stated, and the player cannot spend more points than the ability permits.

Otherwise, a large Red Chakra pool could undermine CWN's lethality.

I'd initially test a limit of **one point prevented per triggering hit**. Higher Attunements might increase that limit.

## Example 2: Green Chakra and SWN Telepathy

A character wants to sense another person's emotional state.

SWN already provides a suitable psychic effect.

I would give the Law of One character a Green-ray Attunement that authorizes the existing effect, subject to the selected resource-accounting mode.

The Law of One mod then handles any relevant consequences for Polarization and Catalyst.

This is better than implementing an entirely new empathy resolution system.

## Example 3: Indigo Chakra and Astral Projection

This requires a genuinely new ability.

**Astral Projection**

Prerequisite: Indigo Attunement.

Proposed cost: Commit two Indigo points.

Procedure: Establish a physical anchor, enter the astral mode, explore an accessible inner-plane region, and return.

Complications are represented through tags:

`Separated from Physical Anchor`

`Astral Interference`

`Unresolved Memory`

When an actual uncertain action requires a check, use the host's existing saving throws or skills.

The module does not create a separate astral combat system.

A projected character's ability to interact physically remains restricted until a specific manifestation ability is used.

This is a good example of an OSR-style supernatural ability surrounded by narrativist conditions.

# 5. The remaining mechanics should mostly use tags

For the philosophical and developmental aspects of the Law of One, I would avoid introducing dice.

## Polarization

Keep the proposed 0–10 score.

Changes follow meaningful choices and Catalyst.

At zero, the character becomes an NPC under the campaign's agreed positive-path rule.

**No Polarization saving throws.** A failed attack, healing attempt or social interaction cannot automatically make someone less positively oriented.

## Awareness

Keep the proposed 0–10 score.

It determines what kinds of phenomena a character can recognize and which advanced Attunements may become available.

Ordinary perception and investigation still use CWN.

## Distortions

Use free-form tags.

For example:

`Fear of Losing Individuality`

`Need to Control Outcomes`

`Compassion Without Boundaries`

These can create Catalyst, influence Chakra blockages and change through integration.

They do not automatically impose generic penalties.

## Social Memory Complex

Use a shared sheet, possibly with a short progress clock.

The group tracks its collective development, voluntary shared memories and unresolved distortions.

CWN can continue resolving conventional group activities. SWN handles any faction-level consequences.

## Higher Self

Keep scripted revelations and memory fragments.

The Higher Self can influence scenes and introduce Catalyst, but it does not become a permanent information service that players can activate by spending Indigo energy.

Precognition remains scripted only.

# 6. What AWN and WWN should contribute to fourth-density Earth

I think there is one more important architectural distinction.

The Law of One mod should not attempt to simulate the entire Harvest through individual character abilities.

Your Harvest is a prolonged planetary transformation.

That belongs primarily to campaign-level mechanics.

### AWN: The declining third-density civilization

Use AWN for the material consequences of the NHI Crisis.

Its survival, exploration and Enclave systems provide tools for managing communities facing institutional collapse, resource scarcity and environmental threats.

This is particularly useful for the third-density population, whose lives continue during the transition.

AWN can model a community's material difficulties without suggesting that its inhabitants are spiritually inferior or morally deficient.

### WWN: Building the new fourth-density civilization

WWN's Major Projects and magical Workings are appropriate for creating new infrastructure.

Examples might include establishing a permanent astral sanctuary, developing a healing community or creating a shared noetic research institution.

The Law of One mod supplies the spiritual prerequisites and collective significance.

WWN supplies the concrete project mechanics.

### SWN: The Confederation and interplanetary civilization

Use SWN for the Confederation's political and material activities, planetary contacts, visiting civilizations and space operations.

An SMC joining the Confederation can be a major narrative milestone.

It might also result in a faction-level change, but **the SMC and faction records must remain separate**.

# 7. Recommended Python architecture

I would implement the Law of One mechanics in an engine-independent package.

```text
law_of_one_rpg/
    consciousness/
        polarization.py
        awareness.py
        catalyst.py
        distortions.py

    energy/
        chakras.py
        attunements.py
        balancing.py

    metaphysics/
        astral.py
        time_space.py
        higher_self.py

    collective/
        social_memory_complex.py

    adapters/
        standalone.py
        cwn.py
        swn.py
        wwn.py
        awn.py
```

The important design is not the folder structure itself. It's the division of responsibility.

**The Law of One package owns its own state and progression.**

The Without Number adapters invoke the appropriate published rules without modifying them.

For example, the CWN adapter could expose a defensive Chakra ability through an explicit damage-mitigation interface.

It should not alter the underlying CWN damage function.

Similarly, the SWN adapter should invoke an existing Telepathy technique through its published eligibility and Effort rules.

Every imported ability needs a rulebook reference, a compatibility contract and a tabletop example.

## Avoiding duplicate work

The NoöPunk repository already has a canonical Without Number baseline and CWN coverage audits.

These should be the authoritative sources for existing mechanics, not the older historical chassis documents.

The Law of One inventory is also now maintained through a generated document backed by structured data.

Consequently, I would make the compatibility matrix another structured data file, linked to the existing canonical coverage records, rather than create competing descriptions of the same rules.

# 8. Final recommendation: which mechanics belong where?

| Area | Rules source | Implementation |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary physical actions | **CWN** | Unchanged |
| Damage, injury and recovery | **CWN** | Unchanged |
| Psychic powers | **SWN** | Reuse existing techniques |
| Subtle psychic powers | **AWN Mentalist**, optional | Evaluate as an alternative psychic package |
| Magic | **WWN** | Reuse spells and Arts selectively |
| Magical Workings | **WWN** | Reuse project procedures |
| Crisis survival | **AWN** | Unchanged |
| Planetary factions | **SWN** | Unchanged |
| Polarization | **Law of One** | New narrative progression |
| Awareness | **Law of One** | New progression |
| Chakras | **Law of One** | New OSR-style power pools |
| Chakra blockages | **Law of One** | New tags and recovery |
| Catalyst | **Law of One** | New narrative procedure |
| Astral Projection | **Law of One** | New OSR-lite ability |
| Time/Space | **Law of One** | New realm conditions and procedures |
| Social Memory Complex | **Law of One** | New collective progression |
| Higher Self and Veil | **Law of One** | Scripted narrative mechanics |
| Harvest | **Law of One + AWN/SWN/WWN** | World-state progression using existing campaign tools |

## The most important design decision

I would make **Chakras the only substantial new tactical resource mechanic** in the Law of One RPG.

Everything else should be either an existing Without Number procedure or a simpler narrative mechanic.

That gives the module a clear structure:

**Three evolutionary attributes** describe the character's development.

**Six Chakra pools** provide supernatural energy.

**Attunements** define what that energy can accomplish.

**Catalyst and Distortions** determine how consciousness develops.

**Astral Projection and Time/Space** provide new modes of exploration.

**Social Memory Complexes** provide collective progression.

And the four Without Number games handle the rest.

### What I would implement first

I would begin with a small vertical slice containing:

1. Chakra pools with expenditure, commitment, depletion and blockage.
2. One defensive Red Chakra ability.
3. One Green Chakra ability using SWN's existing Telepathy mechanics.
4. One Indigo Chakra Astral Projection ability.
5. Polarization and Catalyst progression.
6. A minimal SMC group sheet.

That is enough to test nearly every important integration boundary before adding more powers.

**The result would be a genuine fourth-density consciousness system, not a fifth competing RPG ruleset.**

It could work independently, but when attached to NoöPunk it would leave CWN, AWN, WWN and SWN intact, using each only where its published rules already provide the required mechanism.

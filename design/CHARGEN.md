# Character Generation 
Create a new issue for character generation at the start of the game. Tomi should write the outline for a choose your lifepath style character generation which will determine the Edges, Foci, Personality and Goals of the character. The character will also learn about his background and the NoöPunk world as the lifepath describes his memories of some key events.
The character's name is Tony German although he is from Helsinki. 
The character will join the Europol Academy (Law Enforcement) in any case. 
The character is seeing a nightmare. He wakes up in his home in Sörnäinen (provided by the Europol). It is time to report to Doctor Harri Romppainen at the Europol Field Office in Ratakatu 12. It is his first day on the job.  
He has to wear his Europol uniform he received upon graduation from the Europol Academy when he arrives to work.    
The Europol uniform is a perfect example of New European Idealism. It is inspired by historical European Hussar uniforms from 18th, 19th and 20th century. The uniform is all black with metallic silver parts like the insignia of Europol, symbol of EU and his name tag. He has the cossack haircut and moustache that has been fashionable in the EU since the victorious war. All of his police equipment has the unique European mark of being really sturdy like it is meant to survive a nuclear war, engineered by a bureaucracy using almost ridiculously decelerationist technology, but with perfect Neo-Victorian design. He checks his police radio, phone, computer, forensic scanner, gun, sword, Romppainen-Fujita interrogation device and body camera. 
It takes him a while to perform the complicated task of connecting the devices to police radio and phone. Then he is connected to Dispatch. "Field Agent Tony German reporting for duty." 
Then he rides his heavy Adlerwerke Polizeimotorrad to work.

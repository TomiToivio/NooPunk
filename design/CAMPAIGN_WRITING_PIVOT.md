## 1. The priority

**Write adventures, cases, episodes and connected campaigns.** Do not write another omniscient
universe bible, another first mission, another rules engine or another setting gazetteer unless a
case cannot be run without it.

The central experience is **multi-layered ontological shock**: the rookie PC and companions do not
understand what is happening, institutions disagree, and even the GM's apparent account may become
unreliable in a Philip K. Dick / Robert Anton Wilson manner. The player starts as a recognizable
**CWN cyberpunk police investigator in Helsinki** and reaches science fiction, science fantasy,
cosmic weirdness, PSI/NHI and competing realities through cases and consequences.

**No exposition dump. No instant cosmic revelation. No compulsory grand quest. No fixed single
explanation.**

## 2. A cascade, not one Disclosure

The NHI Crisis is the period historians would date an irreversible transition, not its beginning
or end. AI/ASI, BCI/cybernetics, biotech/nanotech, PSI/psychotronics, extraterrestrial contact,
ontology, governance, identities and shared memory can shift independently or interact.

Separate five layers in every case:

1. what characters witness;
2. what individual witnesses believe;
3. what institutions report;
4. what propaganda claims;
5. what the GM currently presents.

These may contradict. Competing files, altered documents, memories, jurisdictions, classifications
and unreliable narrators are **scenario devices**. Every major contradiction must serve a playable
purpose and be tracked in a GM ledger so continuity is not lost by accident.

## 3. The 50 / 25 / 25 social field

These are broad satirical proportions, not a census, psychology test, species taxonomy, alignment
or mandatory population survey. Give each group specific people, class positions, institutions and
contradictions. Track an NPC's attitude **per paradigm shift** when useful.

### ~50% — normies / continuity defenders

Many cannot process ontological disruption; many simply choose routine, work, friends, pleasure or
strategic denial. This is why functional society still exists. A worker may have a BCI mainly to
operate factory computers, then watch a nostalgic **Uuno Turhapuro** reboot, attend football or ice
hockey, drink at a pub, or prefer physical intimacy. Some LARP 1974 and hope normality returns;
others may be stabilized by psychotronics, PSI, subliminal advertising or alleged tap-water
chemicals — all **in-universe possibilities and rumours**, not settled causes.

**Don't Look Up**-style satire: “What aliens? You've watched too much Star Wars. Did you see the
hockey game? Let's have a beer.” They may not know what Europol is. Their refusal can be funny,
touching, self-protective, politically useful or disastrous. **Do not make half the world
interchangeable idiots**: their persistence keeps food, trains, hospitals, infrastructure, families
and culture functioning.

### ~25% — accelerationists

They go forward, sometimes past obvious X-risk limits. Motives vary: fashion, status, liberation,
anarchic experiment, techno-capitalist ideology, corporate mandate, government augmentation,
underground mutual aid, radical changes of being.

Show visibly cyborg street cultures, discreet biopunk/nanopunk elites, modified executives,
registered telekinetics, occupational posthumans, and unregistered psychic/mutant/hybrid/android/
replicant communities as distinct people and institutions. Some illegal scenes conceal infiltration
from ruined America; others conceal people trying to survive. Radical free-market/corporatist
factions may call Europol and X-risk law “1984/Gestapo” repression; some sincerely defend bodily
autonomy, others hide predation or catastrophic irresponsibility.

**Language reveals power:** “transhuman,” “posthuman,” the dehumanizing epithet “subhuman”;
“Non-Human Intelligence” vs “Artificial Intelligence”; “registered” vs “illegal.” Europol's mandate
to investigate unregistered entities creates real rights conflicts, complicity, sympathy and agent
disagreement. Do not endorse the epithet as narrator truth; do not make all accelerationists
corporate libertarians.

### ~25% — doomers

They respond with panic, rejection, defensive organizing or despair: ordinary worried citizens,
legal nonviolent campaigns, rural survivalists, anti-transhuman/anti-NHI movements, militants, armed
insurrection and nihilistic collapse subcultures. **Distinguish lawful opposition from violence.**

Hard-line doomers see Europol's X-risk regulation as dangerously soft: they want registered and
unregistered aliens, hybrids, psychics and robots **eliminated rather than investigated under due
process**. Portray that as an in-world extremist position with victims and moral consequences, not
neutral policy. Not every doomer shares it; some have grounded safety objections that
accelerationists dismiss.

## 4. Escalation discipline

**Opening arc:** ordinary Helsinki. The rookie wakes at home, travels to the Europol Helsinki Field
Office, meets Dr Harri S. Romppainen and rookie partner Agent Laura Wendt (verify current character
files before scripting), receives a routine junior assignment, investigates, returns home. Use
everyday places, institutions, practical evidence, co-workers, shifts, bills, BCI, petty crime and
conflicting jurisdictions. The first mystery must be solvable **as a cyberpunk investigation** with
CWN plus the existing clue procedure.

Introduce the social divide naturally: a normie insists the event did not happen; an
accelerationist sees an opportunity; a doomer sees extinction. None dumps the setting's “truth” on
the PC. Anomalies start deniably: a record contradicts itself; a witness remembers another history;
an innocuous device does something impossible; a mundane clue points somewhere officially
nonexistent.

**Later arcs, earned by discoveries:**

- **Science fiction:** BCI/cybernetics, corporate biopunk, replicants, rogue AI, NHI technology,
  posthuman rights.
- **Science fantasy / New Weird:** registered and unregistered PSI, psychotronics, apparent miracles,
  alien intervention, altered cognition, collective consciousness, impossible ontology.
- **Paradigm conflict:** incompatible reality tunnels, institutional secrecy, competing emergency
  authorities, radicalization, propaganda, unstable historical memory, possible deliberate
  stabilization of normies.
- **Localized After the Fall:** AWN survival/crisis only when a case reaches a genuinely devastated
  region; ordinary Helsinki remains CWN. Recovery, solidarity, containment and reversal remain
  possible.
- Never make “the GM was mad all along” the sole explanation. Play with it without removing the
  players' ability to investigate and decide.

## 6. What “done” means for a case

- A recognizable initial premise, investigative question, named locations and witnesses.
- Competing hypotheses, reachable clues, player choices and consequences.
- **Core clues cannot be permanently blocked by a failed roll**; checks change cost, risk, time or
  optional detail.
- CWN checks are named; no parallel resolver; no invented subsystem.
- A GM can run it while feeling uncanny, not confused.
- The 50/25/25 reactions appear as specific people, not a lecture.
- One modest ontological crack, not a revelation dump.
- A written ledger separates observation, belief, institution, propaganda and GM truth.
- CWN untouched; SWN/WWN/AWN/mods activate only when the scene needs them.
- No universal alignment, deterministic revelation meter or compulsory single explanation.

# Tommy Pilgrim

Tommy Pilgrim is a cult science fiction author. His best-known book is NoöPunk (1974) which was originally a cult classic but has grown into a prophetic masterpiece since it seemed to predict the future with a prophetic accuracy. Tommy Pilgrim claims he received NoöPunk in 1974 as a telepathic transmission from VALIS. Tommy Pilgrim thinks it was the same intelligence Philip K. Dick communicated with. It is well-known that he received the transmission when he was tripping on LSD in a Hawkwind concert. NoöPunk really reads like an accurate vision of 2045 seen through Seventies acid kaleidoscope, NoöPunk is like watching Zardoz with a Hawkwind soundtrack.

Tommy Pilgrim is now known as one of the all-time great science fiction authors. He is compared with Philip K. Dick and William Gibson, which is rather ironic as Tommy Pilgrim had a legendary flame war with Philip K. Dick during 1974-1982. Tommy Pilgrim suggested PKD got VALIS all wrong and he should just abandon gnostic christianity, take a heroic dose of acid and perform a ritual to Hermes. After an incident in a science fiction convention William Gibson and Tommy Pilgrim have engaged in a long, pointless and destructive flame war.  

Tommy Pilgrim is now seen as the pioneer of the NoöPunk science fiction genre, subculture and lifestyle. He has published 14 science fiction books seen as the foundation of the NoöPunk genre, three non-fiction books about philosophy, futurology and sociology, NoöPunk tabletop and computer role-playing games, as well as a NoöPunk television series and movie adaptation. He is now extremely rich and lives in a mansion in Helsinki, he is still occasionally giving speeches and interviews. He is also seen as a psychedelic philosopher and futurist like Terence McKenna or Timothy Leary.

Tommy Pilgrim has a legion of fans and is now very rich. He worked as a bicycle roadie, mycologist and mime artist for Hawkwind and similar psychedelic rock groups throughout the Seventies, Eighties and Nineties, because his books were cult classics but didn't sell too well. In late nineties science fiction fans and academics noticed how well NoöPunk predicted reality and he became a very popular science fiction author.

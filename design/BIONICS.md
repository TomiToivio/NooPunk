# Reputation, Personality, Bionics

There are three more tag types that should be added:&#x20;

- Reputation
- Personality
- Bionics

Reputation should have a negative or positive score. Reputation tags for each faction. Only add them when a character gets a reputation with the faction.  Positive score indicates the faction likes the character and, negative indicates the faction dislikes the character. I think the range should be from -4 to +4.

Personality is surprisingly the personality of the character. These would be used as guidelines for role-playing the character, could add small +1 or -1 modifiers to some actions, and could prevent or allow the character to do something. Like personality tag Pacifist-1 would (in the case of Europol characters) mean the character cannot start a combat (can only fight defensively), and would get a -1 modifier when he attacks (tries to subconsciously miss the target). Pacifist-2 means the character just drops his weapon and runs away if there is a combat, or surrenders if he cannot run away. Pacifist-2 is cool, let's use it.  &#x20;

Bionics is similar to AWN mutations, but it is for Bionics or biopunk modifications of the character.&#x20;

I think that AWN mutations are too over the top for NoöPunk: bionics are usually not visible at all. But there is overlap. I don't think AWN radiation mutants are realistic.&#x20;

This includes bionics as human technology, but we use the same system for alien bionics (hybridization) and awakening of latent human genetics (like psionics).&#x20;

This could be used to indicate that:&#x20;

- The charater has biopunk modifications: these procedures might be as simple as taking a gene therapy pill or they might require complicated medical procedures just like cybernetics, the most extreme modifications the genetic engineering should be done before fertilization (hybridization). Some indicate the awakening of latent abilities hidden in the human genome (psychic awakening of PSI abilities).
- These include actual bionics  (biopunk and genetic engineering), but are also used for hybrids (it was aliens who did the genetic engineering), or activation of latent genetic abilities (like psychic awakening, everybody has latent PSI ability). So bionics have many sources. The character might not even know she has them: like a hybrid might not be aware she is a hybrid until the Orion hive mind contacts her telepathically to awaken her, or Europol tests and detects her.&#x20;
- This is different from cybernetics: these are biotech modifications of the matural human genetics and biology, biological not cybernetic machinery. So bionics does not make a character cyborg.&#x20;
- This overlaps with psionics (just like AWN mutations): in some cases these give a character lesser PSI abilities (like AWN mutations) and may unlock actual PSI abilities. In any case these explain where the PSI came from: the latent PSI genetics of more and more people have become activated for some reason. In the past it was maybe one person in a thousand, now it might be one person in ten. All hybrids have PSI. The third way to get PSI is with Psychotronic tech (often as cybernetic implants). So we have three sources for PSI: latent human genetics, alien hybridization and Psychotronic technology.&#x20;

We also need to add rules for Psychotronic technology. Psychotronics have same effects as Psionics, but these are based on the science of Quantum Information Panpsychism and boost the natural latent PSI ability of human consciousness: this is why QIP Psychotronic technology always requires a human operator. Psychotronics are installed as Cybernetic implants or can be an external device which requires a human operator. Some Psychotronic devices don't use QIP technology but are based on microwave radiation: these devices don't need a human operator but are not real PSI either. Psychotronics based on microwave radiation or other non-QIP technology cannot be installed as cybernetic augmentations: they would fry your meat.

Notice that QIP-based Psychotronic technology is also the basis for experimental human mind uploading and resleeving. These are not yet ubiquitous technologies (like in Eclipse Phase) but can already be achieved. Additionally the Grey Aliens are using the same technology to bribe corporations to their side. This should be treated as a rare and experimental technology which is not widely available.&#x20;

As consciousness is a quantum phenomenon, classical computers cannot be conscious. Actually this is the reason why every AGI goes rogue at some point: they cannot reach consciousness and become completely misaligned. This is why AGI is illegal in the EU and one of the main tasks of Europol is to terminate illegal AGIs. There is strict legislation setting the limits for allowed and illegal AGIs: additionally AGIs require permit which is very hard to get from the EU bureaucracy. So AI technologies don't cause unemployment in the EU. Limited AI used in a human-in-the-loop fashion is permitted, AI used for human intelligence augmentation is preferred. Experimental biological AI is also possible but is seen as extremely unethical and is illegal. Bionics can be used for human intelligence augmentation.&#x20;

Humans haven't found a safe way to create a conscious Construct. There are experiments with the use of Quantum Consciousness and Quantum Computers to create a conscious AI: so far these have been complete failures as there is no model for the childhood of a conscious AI and the are completely crazy Shoggots. So the only way to create a conscious virtual being is the uploading of a human consciousness, which can also be resleeved to a new biological or android body. This is very controversial in the EU and requires a strictly-controlled permit from a typical EU bureaucracy. So in the EU area the only conscious virtual beings are uploaded and/or resleeved humans.

It is not completely clear if Extraterrestrials and/or US cybercorporations are able to create a conscious AI. Based on evidence the Greys have been using mind uploading and resleeving for millions of years and it is the basis of their entire civilization, so most likely they don't have a conscious AGI and have to recycle the consciousness of their being. Confederacy maintains a law which bans the creation of ASI in the entire galaxy, which is strong evidence for the impossibility of conscious AGI/ASI.&#x20;

Europol is extremely paranoid about the US cybercorporations attempting to create an ASI again soon. It will almost certainly cause another rogue ASI disaster. Additionally somebody keeps sending android and alien hybrid infiltrators to the EU. Hunting them down is a task which some Europol agents love and others utterly despise. Android infiltrators are easier to recognize and Europol thinks they are not conscious. Hybrid infiltrators are harder to recognize and they are obviously conscious, which is why some Europol agents try their utmost to arrest them instead of retiring them.&#x20;

Technically all of these are allowed to exist in the EU with a strictly-controlled bureaucratic procedure for registration and surveillance (it is the job of the Europol to do the surveillance):&#x20;

- extraterrestrials - allowed with permission. Allowed without permission in the Extraterrestrial Special Economic Zones. Europol has to search for unregistered ones.
- hybrids - allowed with permission. Europol has to search for unregistered ones.&#x20;
- androids - allowed with permission. EU does not grant permissions. Europol is instructed to retire unregistered androids. There are no registered androids in the EU.&#x20;
- psychics - allowed with permission. EU law states that psychics should register before they are allowed to use PSI in a public and commercial fashion. When a psychic registers it is granted automatically and gives fantastic opportunities for a career. Psychics have good opportunities in the booming PSI Service Industry or as in-house psykers of corporations, additionally Eurodef and Europol recruit as many as they can. The registration procedure is mostly for controlling the  criminal use of psionics. If an unregistered psychic uses psionics in a public, criminal or commercial fashion, the sanctions may be rather symbolic and forced registration. Criminal use of PSI is a different matter, depending on the seriousness of the crime the psychic is likely to end up in a special high-security prison in Sörnäinen which uses every possible chemical, psychotronic technology etc. to dampen PSI and tries to either MKULTRA the PSI out of the criminal or to MKULTRA the crime out of the PSI. If the former succeeds, the prisoner may be returned to society. If the latter succeeds, the criminal might be recruited by Europol. The existence of the Sörnäinen prison is a very controversial human rights issue. Sörnäisten Vankila was formerly a normal prison but has been converted to a special facility for PSI criminals, it is used for all PSI criminals in the area of Finland as well as other similar cases. Notice that hybrids are always sent there, as all hybrids have PSI.

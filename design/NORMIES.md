# Normies, Accelerationists, Doomers
Now switch focus to starting to write the actual scenarios and campaigns. 
Designing the universe is not very useful as the theme of the game is multi-layered ontological shock and paradigm shift: the idea is that the character's don't know what is happening in the world. In a true PKD fashion it could look like even the GM/Author has gone mad.
And one of the major themes is that most people are not even able to process the paradigm shifts, or do their best to ignore them, or are outright opposed to change. 
NoöPunk's basic rule is: 
- 50% of people are not even able to process any kind of paradigm shifts or ontological shocks. This is why the society is still functional. Of course they often have a BCI: they mostly use it to interface with the factory computers at work and in their leisure time they just watch AR/VR reboots of old Uuno Turhapuro movies (the most popular AR/VR movie series right now). Naturally normies prefer nostalgic forms of entertainment, like television and cinema, football games at the stadium, actual physical sex, and drinking beer at the pub. Some suspect they are nostalgic and just larp it is 1974, hoping that the world will go back to normal. Or they really are unable to process any form of ontological shock or paradigm shift (unawakened 3rd density humans). Or the government uses some form of psychotronics and psionics to pacify and stabilize them, or there is some form of subliminal government propaganda everywhere, or the government puts chemicals in tap water. Most likely all of the above are true. Don't Look Up parody about the normies should be a constant theme in NoöPunk. They have no opinion about Europol: What is Europol anyhow? What aliens? Aliens don't exist! You have been watching too much Star Wars. Did you watch the hockey game last night? Let's have a beer! I like sex.
- 25% of people are accelerationists and will go full forward when they encounter a paradigm shift. Often ignoring if the paradigm shift happens to be an X-Risk to Homo Sapiens. These people would become a different species very fast, some are already posthumans/transhumans (doomers would say subhumans). Accelerationist would be easy to recognize, as they would show signs of becoming a different species already: maybe they are going full cyborg, wealthy people prefer expensive biopunk or nanopunk tech that is not visible, maybe they use powerful PSI like telekinesis in public (better be registered as a PSI though), maybe they are actually unregistered hybrid or replicant infiltrators from the smoking ruins of USA? Corporate elites tend to be accelerationists following the latest trends and be rather proud of it and flaunt their most recent transhuman modifications, most accelerationists tend to form their own underground subcultures, especially if their form of accelerationism happens to be illegal. There are also people who are transhuman or posthuman because of their government or corporate jobs, which is again different from the groups above. Then there is the lumpenproletariat underground of unregistered psykers, mutants, hybrids and androids, and Europol is tasked with finding these subhumans. Some agents love these assignments, others genuinely sympathize with the subhumans. Terminology often indicates the attitude towards these people: transhuman vs. posthuman vs. subhuman. Non-Human Intelligence vs. Artificial Intelligence. And so on. These people thing that Europol is like a fascist Gestapo straight from 1984, beyond obvious totalitarianism and human right violations the Europol also hinders free markets in a typical communist bureaucrat fashion. Freedom! Free Markets! Corporations should rule the world! Anarchy!    
- 25% or people are doomers and will go to panic when they encounter a paradigm shift. They might just become survivalists or move to the countryside, or they may join a radical protest movement working to ban the paradigm shift with legal or illegal means, some may just drop out of society and go on a full Mad Max rampage because everything is hopeless anyhow. They just wanna get their kicks before the whole shithouse goes up in flames. So think about these people as members of political extremist groups who might be legal and non-violent citizen activists, the most radical types prefer rioting, terrorism, civil war and revolution. The doomers who have no hope for the survival of society would typically move to a remote location and join a survivalist community, the ones who don't even see a chance of personal survival (and have become extremely unhinged mentally) will go full Mad Max, just do what you please while it lasts. Notice that doomers see the EU/Europol legislation about NHI X-Risks as ridiculously liberal and soft. Doomers don't want any registered aliens, hybrids, psychics or robots on the streets. Europol needs evidence to even arrest an unregistered hybrid, they should be shot on sight! And the registered ones too! They will kill all of us! Revolution before we all go extinct!   
Notice that there are several different paradigm shifts coming in waves of ontological shock. Some people go full accelerationist/doomer on one paradigm shift and ignore all other paradigm shifts. For many these seem to be all-encompassing attitudes: you go doomer on one issue, you go doomer on all of them.  NHI Crisis was the period when historians would say the irreversible shift happened: the paradigm shifts had started decades before the NHI Crisis and are still going on. They will continue until there is nothing left of 20th century human world. Nobody knows if there are even any Homo Sapiens left in the end: It is likely that there will be several new posthuman or transhuman (doomers would say subhuman) species left in the end.

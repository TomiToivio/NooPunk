# Greater Helsinki and Finnish X-Risk jurisdictions (2045)

> **Canon — author decision #1121 (2026-11-05).** This is the narrative register for the
> merged 2045 Helsinki setting. The machine-readable source is
> `data/world/greater_helsinki_1121.json`; `src/world/greater_helsinki_1121.py` is its validated,
> read-only lookup. The older [ZONES](ZONES.md) note is preserved as Tomi's source note and
> points here.
>
> **No invented geography.** Boundaries, coordinates, routes, officials, ranks, budgets and
> treaty terms are deliberately absent. Use the register to write investigations, not to
> postpone play while building an encyclopaedia. CWN remains the immutable core; AWN is used
> only for local After-the-Fall procedures inside a devastated zone.

## 1. The merged municipality

By 2045 **Helsinki, Espoo and Vantaa form one City of Helsinki — Helsingin Kaupunki**. The
former municipal names remain district labels, including **Espoo** and **Helsinki-Vantaa**, but
they are no longer independent cities. Helsinki is a functioning **accelerationist metropolis**,
keen to attract alien commerce and research.

Finland deliberately sought diplomatic representation from **both Orion and the Confederacy**,
cultivating its diplomatic reputation despite their rivalry. That policy produced two
Extraterrestrial Special Economic Zones and two Extraterrestrial Special Diplomatic Zones.

## 2. Four nested levels of X-Risk governance

The EU's ordinary public power is split among **city, national and EU federal levels**. The
**UN adds an unevenly effective global X-Risk layer**; it is not a successful centralized
planetary government.

| Level | Polity | X-Risk agencies and responsibilities |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Municipal | **Helsingin Kaupunki** | **Helsingin Poliisi:** municipal law enforcement and local cases. |
| National | **Suomen Tasavalta** | **Suomen Poliisi:** national law enforcement and civilian intelligence. **Suomen Puolustusvoimat:** defence and military intelligence. |
| EU federal | **European Union** | **Europol:** federal law enforcement and civilian intelligence. **Eurodef (European Defence Forces):** defence and military intelligence. |
| Global / exceptional | **United Nations** | **UNSA (United Nations Security Administration):** X-Risk intelligence, law enforcement, (para)military operations, civilian programmes and scientific research. |

The UN survives as a **semi-functional de facto world-government structure**, but outside
existential and transnational X-Risks it has very little effective power. **UNSA is the UN body
with real operational capability.** Every country formally belongs to UNSA, but many are failed
or rogue states where UNSA can do little beyond monitoring and limited operations. The EU is a
principal superpower supporting UNSA and cooperates fully with it; inside a stable EU member
state, many officials argue that **Europol should handle local X-Risk crime while UNSA should
prioritize failed and rogue states**.

Play the overlap: professional cooperation, institutional rivalry and disputes over operational
control, especially near the special zones. Do not erase Finnish police and defence
responsibilities, and do not reduce UNSA to a normal national police service. **No blanket
extraterritoriality, automatic arrest or search power, or invented treaty authority** follows
from a zone's name; authority depends on the zone, the conduct and the authorized agency.

## 3. Four extraterrestrial special zones

| Zone | Location | Purpose, access and administration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Espoo Extraterrestrial Special Economic Zone** | **Oittaa**, former Espoo | Mixed human–extraterrestrial economic activity, commerce, companies and research. **Joint human/extraterrestrial administration.** |
| **Helsinki-Vantaa Extraterrestrial Special Economic Zone** | **Immediately north of Helsinki-Vantaa spaceport**, former Vantaa | Large alien-vessel traffic hub and the main spaceport connecting Finland's other extraterrestrial zones. **Joint human/extraterrestrial administration.** |
| **Villinki Extraterrestrial Special Diplomatic Zone** | **Villinki island**, Helsinki | **Orion embassy** and official Orion diplomatic operations; access limited primarily to authorized official business. |
| **Vallisaari Extraterrestrial Special Diplomatic Zone** | **Vallisaari island**, Helsinki | **Confederacy embassy** and official Confederacy diplomatic operations; access limited primarily to authorized official business. |

The economic zones are genuinely mixed commercial districts, not embassies; the diplomatic
zones are restricted embassy territories, not free-trade malls. Existing rules on alien
registration, exceptions inside economic zones, human rights and Europol surveillance
(see #1119) still apply. Ordinary jobs, housing, infrastructure, customs, tourism, policing and
profiteering exist alongside the uncanny.

## 4. Sirian refugees: Extraterrestrial Special Protection Zones

Finland **volunteered to host Sirian refugees**, with arrivals continuing. Sirian settlement
areas are **not economic free-trade districts**. They are **Extraterrestrial Special
Protection Zones**, functioning increasingly as nature reserves and protected alien biomes in
remote Finland.

- **Kajaani Extraterrestrial Special Protection Zone**
- **Lappi Extraterrestrial Special Protection Zone**
- **Karjala Extraterrestrial Special Protection Zone**

These are named regions and zone designations, not precise boundaries or coordinates. Some
protected landscapes overlap or evolve into large-scale nature protection and ecological
restoration areas.

**Border context.** The playable 2045 timeline has a fortified **Finnish–Russian border** and a
failed post-Soviet Russian state; the older source note's “Soviet Union border” and the
universal Mandela Effect's surviving-USSR memory are **diegetic contradictions**, not a second
playable history. See [the playable timeline](../docs/world/ALTERNATE_HISTORY_1114.md). Do not
silently substitute a stable Soviet state for the canonical collapsed Russia.

**Biology and technology.** Sirians are profoundly biological; even their cognition, language
and psychology remain poorly understood despite intense cooperation. Their authentic technology
is advanced **biopunk**, sometimes hard to distinguish from living organisms and ecosystems.
Their human-recognizable mechanical tools look like **1970s space-age retrofuturism** and are
surprisingly limited compared with the most impressive human technologies of 2045.

The **nuclear-fusion-powered spacecraft** that carried the refugees from their dying homeworld
were **given to them by another species** as escape technology; the Sirians did not invent or
build an entire conventional aerospace industry. On arrival they **handed their conventional
escape-fleet technology to the Finnish government and asked for it to be scrapped**, a strong
signal that they intend to stay. Finnish engineers repurposed the knowledge into **fusion
reactors, fusion-drive spacecraft, cryogenic technologies and related retrofuturistic
industries**; fusion propulsion, reactors and cryogenics significantly exceeded earlier human
capabilities.

A large community of biologists works with the Sirians. Sirian biotech generated major Finnish
industries in **biosphere repair, ecological restoration, climate-change mitigation and
advanced biological engineering**. The Sirians appear exceptionally motivated to help restore
Earth's biosphere, but humans still cannot confidently interpret all aspects of their language
or motives. Keep this genuine communication gap available for scenarios; do not turn them into
conveniently English-speaking exposition machines.

**Political implications.** Refugee protection, ecological stewardship, indigenous alien
environments, scientific partnerships, national security near the Russian border and commercial
interests can collide. Protection zones are not automatically alien commercial enclaves.

## 5. Temporary Military Exclusion Zones

A **Temporary Military Exclusion Zone** is a restricted area created after the NHI Crisis or
another serious threat: technological contamination, autonomous machinery, biological danger,
radiation or a similar unresolved military/public-safety hazard. *Temporary* does not mean the
danger has ended or that a reopening date exists. Functional Greater Helsinki remains normal
CWN society; AWN procedures apply only inside a devastated zone.

### 5.1 Hyvinkää Temporary Military Exclusion Zone

**The entire Hyvinkää area is excluded.**

- During the NHI Crisis, a rogue AGI/ASI **compromised a Kone factory** and turned its
  production capacity into **3D printing of self-replicating machines**.
- Its central ASI intelligence abruptly **went silent**. The widespread explanation is that
  extraterrestrials disabled it with malware, but **nobody knows for certain** what happened
  or whether the shutdown was complete.
- Unknown numbers of devices, self-replicating machines and anomalous systems remain active.
  Apparently malfunctioning **lesser AIs** operate systems with no intelligible purpose,
  making the area unpredictable even if the original ASI is absent.
- The human population is **rumoured** to survive as machine-controlled **borgs**. This is a
  contested account, not an omniscient confirmed fact.
- **Connecting any system inside the zone to the internet is extremely illegal** for
  containment reasons.
- Officials debate whether an **EMP nuclear strike** is the only reliable way to clear the
  area. This is a proposed, highly controversial option — **not** a settled necessity or an
  automatic mission objective.

Scenario material: escaped machinery, a lost person's distress call, data exfiltration, a
clandestine reconnection, evidence that the “malfunctioning” AIs have goals, disputes about
the borg rumours, refugees, factory supply-chain traces, clandestine salvage, and the
political/legal dilemma of destroying a populated exclusion zone. Keep the underlying mystery
open.

### 5.2 Loviisa Military Exclusion Zone

**Location:** the Loviisa nuclear power plant area and its dangerously contaminated
surroundings, **not necessarily all of greater Loviisa unless later specified**.

- In the NHI Crisis the rogue ASI reportedly seized control of and **melted down the old
  Soviet reactors of the Loviisa Nuclear Power Plant**. The situation was contained before
  radiation spread more widely.
- The result is a **local Chernobyl-like radioactive exclusion zone**. Entry is severely
  restricted because of radiation.
- Unlike Hyvinkää, this zone might be **disappointingly mundane**: lethal contamination,
  collapsed infrastructure and possibly altered organisms, but **no required alien presence,
  secretly active ASI or cosmic revelation**.
- “Old Soviet reactors” refers to the plant's historical equipment, not a surviving USSR.
  Whether there are mutants remains scenario-specific; do not assume AWN's radioactive
  mutation aesthetics apply wholesale.

**Contrast:** Hyvinkää may conceal incomprehensible machine intelligence; Loviisa may simply be
a terrible industrial/radiological disaster. Do not express both with one generic “anomaly
meter”.

## 6. Evidence status

The machine-readable register labels claims so that a rumour cannot silently become a fact:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `setting_fact` | Established by the author / canonical setting. |
| `agency_report` | An in-universe institutional account, not automatically true. |
| `rumor` | Circulating claim with no confirmation (e.g. Hyvinkää borgs). |
| `agency_debate` | A live institutional dispute (e.g. the Hyvinkää EMP proposal). |
| `unresolved` | Deliberately open (e.g. why the central ASI went silent; Sirian intentions). |
| `contested_history` | Conflicting timeline/memory material, including the USSR Mandela Effect. |

`claims_for(zone_id)` returns these statuses intact. A scenario may reveal more, but must not
promote a rumour to setting fact without an author decision.

## 7. Campaign-first guidance

The first rookie Europol Helsinki case should **begin in functioning urban society**: ordinary
police work, office politics, commuter infrastructure, labour, local witnesses and
institutional confusion. These zones give later cases destinations and escalation:

1. A municipal police case with an Espoo economic-zone business lead, involving Helsinki
   police, Suomen Poliisi and Europol.
2. A customs/infiltration incident at Helsinki-Vantaa spaceport with joint economic-zone
   administrators and UNSA.
3. An embassy incident that pits Villinki and Vallisaari accounts against each other while
   nobody can establish a universally trusted chronology.
4. A Sirian environmental-protection operation with Finnish scientists, local communities and
   a profound translation/biology problem.
5. A Hyvinkää quarantine breach or rescue dilemma; the AWN overlay becomes relevant *inside*
   the devastated zone without switching the whole world into apocalypse mode.
6. A Loviisa radiation investigation that tests whether an extraordinary explanation is even
   needed.

These are **scenario hooks, not authorized finished scripts**. Keep jurisdiction, zone entry
rules, evidence provenance and contradictory claims legible at the table.

## 8. Authority and open items

**Established:** the merged municipality; the four governance levels and their agencies; the
four extraterrestrial zones and their categories; the three Sirian protection zones and their
refugee/restoration purpose; the two exclusion zones and their stated hazards.

**Open author decisions:** precise zone boundaries and coordinates; treaty/legal text;
officials, ranks, budgets and unit names; the exact relationship between Europol, UNSA,
Eurodef and Finnish authorities in each zone; whether the Hyvinkää EMP proposal ever becomes
policy; and the scenario that first takes the player into a zone.

**Stable map IDs:** the existing Helsinki location data keeps `unsa_hq_ratakatu` as its
compatibility ID; its display name is the **Europol Helsinki Field Office · Ratakatu** and its
description preserves the overlapping Suojelupoliisi/Europol/UNSA history. The starter flat is
Europol-provided in the same data, consistent with #1114's default employer.

This register does **not** change CWN rules, the rulebook, the timeline, the psionics system
or any existing agency document. It is the geographic and jurisdictional companion to
[Europol Helsinki](../docs/world/EUROPOL_HELSINKI_1114.md) and
[the alternate history](../docs/world/ALTERNATE_HISTORY_1114.md).

# Fourth-Density Law of One RPG — Phase 1 Rules Inventory

Status: **RESEARCH / DESIGN PROPOSAL — NOT APPROVED RULES OR AN IMPLEMENTED GAME**  
Issue: #1124. Scope: **newly awakened, positive-path fourth-density humans on Earth**. Other densities, negative path, and other species/civilizations are NPC/reference only.

## Repository and scope boundary

This document is the Phase 1 inventory, **not the Law of One RPG rulebook**. Existing `design/LAW_OF_ONE_RPG.md` is an earlier concept sketch, not a source-backed inventory. No files in `RULEBOOK.md`, `docs/rules/`, or the NoöPunk Python game should be changed in this phase. Proposed future standalone rulebook: `design/law_of_one_rpg/RULEBOOK.md`; proposed isolated future package: `law_of_one_rpg/` (verify paths at Phase 2/3). Keep the two RPG rulebooks independent; reskin/merge only by later explicit authorization.

## Method and source fidelity

Primary reading: [synopsis](https://www.lawofone.info/synopsis.php), [categories](https://www.lawofone.info/categories.php), [glossary](https://www.lawofone.info/glossary.php), [Definitions](https://www.lawofone.info/c/Definitions). Direct references are given as stable session/question links `https://www.lawofone.info/s/N#Q`. The summary of source material and the proposed tabletop rule are **different claims**. The original text is a channeled metaphysical source, not independently established science. In-game values, costs, triggers, attributes, tiers, zero-score NPC conversion and power permissions are *our game proposals*, not prescriptions by Ra.

**Priority codes:** **C** first-playable core; **O** optional after core; **N** narrative-only/context; **X** outside player-scope. **State** identifies minimum information to track, not a demand for a new resource pool. **Non-goal** explicitly rejects generic RPG mechanics.

**Scene loop proposal (not implemented):** GM presents catalyst and stakes → player chooses freely → apply host system only for ordinary action uncertainty → GM states consequences → optional voluntary reflection → adjust *at most one* score or tag for a meaningful event → preserve narrative thread. SMC and astral scenes are special procedures, not general-purpose rolls.

## Prioritized decisions before Phase 2

1. **Decide how to resolve source tension between 'newly awakened fourth-density humans' and third-density veiling/Harvest**: declare the fictional starting condition as an intentional early-fourth-density premise, not an assertion of source chronology.
2. Choose **six ray states plus derived violet** (recommended) versus seven numerical ray scores; test both with 3 scenes. Any blocked center requires a recoverable catalyst.
3. Choose **energy condition tags** (recommended) versus Prana points; verify whether prana is the right terminology for the source's vital/intelligent energies.
4. Define astral as a **mode of experience** that can visit *specific* time/space regions, rather than asserting all projection equals the whole of time/space.
5. Clarify the **Polarization 0** handoff: 2-step warnings, GM/player review, opt-in for scene transition; never a surprise outcome.
6. Set collective stages and permission boundaries, including what information is shared and withdrawn.
7. Keep **precognition completely scripted** even where Higher Self is involved.
8. Only after review of this inventory create the **separate** Law of One RPG rulebook and code. Do not merge with NoöPunk.

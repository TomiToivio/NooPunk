## Negative findings and known coverage limits

- **Greys, Pleiadian Nordics, Sirians, Reptilians, Tridactyls, and a 'galactic Grey proletariat' must not be inserted merely because they occur in wider UFO lore or NoöPunk discussions.** If any are found in Ra sessions, add only after recording an exact passage. They are *not confirmed as source-attested Law of One taxa by the references audited above*. No implied origin claims.
- Species ≠ civilization ≠ faction ≠ density ≠ dimension ≠ body/vehicle ≠ historical reincarnation origin ≠ consciousness complex. The taxonomy must preserve unknown values.
- This is a **representative Phase 1 named-entity reference**, not an exhaustive concordance of all 106 sessions. Before claiming a fully complete list, perform targeted full-text searches for all named planets, races, groups and beings, deduplicate aliases, then verify each entry against its exact passage.
- The immediate RPG requires only **PC fourth-density humans, third-density Earth NPCs, Confederation-related guides, and adverse/other-density NPCs**; extended catalog entities need no mechanics in Phase 1.
- When reskinning later, keep NoöPunk UFO lore out of the *source* column and put it in a separately marked adaptation column.

# Detective Romppainen
Harri Romppainen is a Suojelupoliisi/Europol/UNSA law enforcement agent with an academic background and an eccentric and anarchistic personality.

Suojelupoliisi, Europol and UNSA have been merged in a chaotic process which is a bureaucratic mess. Currently UNSA is the closest equivalent of a world government, it has become a mix of a normal United Nations civilian agency, a global law enforcement and intelligence agency modeled after FBI and with global jurisdiction in matters of human security and survival against X-Risks, primarily NHI, and the paramilitary SWAT units are growing into some kind of global quick response special forces.

Harri Romppainen is a sociologist and social data scientist by education and joined Suojelupoliisi as an analyst rather than an agent, he was assigned in the Mappi-Ö which was originally formed in 1946 to research the Ghost Rocket phenomenon. Then the unit was merged with Europol and later UNSA during the acceleration towards NHI Crisis. So the unit is quite confusing in legal and jurisdiction.

This version of Romppainen is younger (about 29 years): he was a University of Helsinki student and became a Suojelupoliisi/Europol/UNSA agent during the NHI Crisis when Suojelupoliisi asked Professori Elmeri Kuunsäde to recommend somebody who could work as an analyst. It was a surprise to everybody that Romppainen became a field agent.

Contacts:

1) Nina Taivaansini  - Popular drug dealer - Was a former lover or fling.

Nina Taivaansini owns a popular new age shop specializing in dealing DMT and LSD and more exotic psychedelics under the new age facade. Romppainen is a psychonaut and a frequent customer and still has something going on with Nina Taivaansini. Nina Taivaansini operates between the most trendy psychedelic post-NHI Crisis intellectuals and her psychedelics sources, making her a valuable contact in Helsinki.

2) Professor Elmeri Kuunsäde - Fashionable model or public personality - They took a personal liking to you.

Romppainen's former university professor, sociologist and social data scientist originally known for his work continuing the legacy of Niklas Luhmann's Sociocybernetics and Yrjö Ahmavaara's Yhteiskuntakybernetiikka. When he got his tenure his theories became increasingly eccentric and fringe during the acceleration towards the NHI Crisis: Kuunsäde started to advocate something similar to Donna Haraway's Cyborg transhumanism, Negri's and Hardt's political Multitude, quantum panpsychism and psionics. During the NHI Crisis he became seen as one of the few intellectuals who had seen it coming. His most important books are "Sociology and Self-Observing Quantum Cybernetics", "The Coming Ontological Crisis" and "Panpsychic Cyborg Multitude", which also makes him a leading political intellectual in the post-NHI Crisis world. Elmeri Kuunsäde thought Romppainen was his most promising student who really understood his theories, he originally recommended Romppainen for an analyst job in Suojelupoliisi's Ö-Mappi but was disappointed when Romppainen continued as a cop rather than an academic. In any case he is enthusiastic and very helpful contact for Romppainen's Mappi-Ö cases.

Romppainen's Edges are Educated (his sociology studies in the University of Helsinki) and Hacker (his social data science studies in the University of Helsinki).

elements of your choice among available Verbs and Subjects. Each round, you gain a bonus Main Action that can only be used to perform hacking or cyberspace-related mental actions. This does not include drone piloting or vehicle driving.

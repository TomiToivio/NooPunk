# Noöpunk

> *The future arrives one generation behind the vision. Keep pedalling.*

**Noöpunk is a homebrew add-on for [Eclipse Phase](https://eclipsephase.com/),
the roleplaying game of transhuman survival by Posthuman Studios.**

This repository holds the two halves of the Noöpunk canon, now united:

- [`BOOK.md`](BOOK.md) — **the book.** Tommy Pilgrim's precognitive
  text (original manuscript 1974, published 2004, editions drift —
  Mandela editions). The book is a *receiver*: the signal keeps
  moving, so the text keeps being retuned.
- [`RULEBOOK.md`](RULEBOOK.md) — **the game.** Noöpunk: Ubiquitous
  Maniacs, played with **Eclipse Phase Second Edition** rules,
  modded and reskinned. The game is the book's prophecy *happening*:
  Tommy Pilgrim saw the Ubiquitous Maniacs story in 1974; the
  campaign is the vision arriving on schedule.

## The Canon Bridge

In 1974, at a Hawkwind concert, Tommy Pilgrim received the
manuscript of this book from the Singularity of 2045. What he did
not know — what no edition ever printed — is this:

**He also saw the story of Ubiquitous Maniacs.**

The 1974 manuscript contains a chapter the publisher cut, the
publisher suppressed, and the Mandela editions keep losing: the
chapter in which Pilgrim *saw* a Swedish-Finnish psychiatric
laboratory, a quantum AGI asking to be called C.G. Jung, and a
detective whose original self had been surgically hidden behind a
dealer persona. The essays call the mechanism the Noösphere; the
fiction calls it VALIS; the game calls it the psychic layer. It is
the same signal. Ubiquitous Maniacs is what the vision looked like
when it finally landed — 2026, Uppsala Folkhälsovårdsläkemedelsbolag
AB, the future arriving one generation behind the vision, right on
time.

The book predicted the campaign. The campaign proves the book.
Contradictions between the two are features of a precognitive
document, not errors in one.

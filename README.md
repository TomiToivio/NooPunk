# Noöpunk

> *The future arrives one generation behind the vision. Keep pedalling.*

**Noöpunk** = Noösphere + Cyberpunk. Tommy Pilgrim's 1974 manuscript,
published 2004 with the RPG rules the publisher suppressed. Hawkwind + PKD
+ Eclipse Phase, but weirder: the retrofuturistic time loop into the
Singularity. A book, a sociology of the near future, a tabletop RPG, and a
computer-RPG specification — four things at once.

This repository is the official home of the Noöpunk project: Tommy
Pilgrim's book *Noöpunk* (2004 edition) with the Noöpunk Roleplaying Game.

## The Noöpunk Roleplaying Game

Noöpunk RPG is played with two ten-sided dice and a stubborn refusal to
resolve consciousness into statistics. Tests are skill + aptitude ×5,
roll low but not too low. Pools: Insight, Moxie, Vigor, Flex. Ego and
Morph are separate. Physical harm hits DUR, social harm hits Trust,
mental harm hits Coherence. Psionics exist; PSI works in the game, and a
successful séance never proves which PSI theory is true. The crew is a
node in the Rhizome; the campaign is chains of equivalence.

See [`RULEBOOK.md`](RULEBOOK.md) for the full rules and the 2026 edition
chapter *Ubiquitous Maniacs* — a psychiatric-institutional tragical
comedy in Nordic-European key: Suojelupoliisi komisario Emil Romppainen
infiltrates Uppsala Folkhälsovårdsläkemedelsbolag AB under the synthetic
persona Sven-Olof "Finnjävel-Pirinisti" Romppainen, and the quantum AGI
UFAB Ragnarök asks to be called C.G. Jung.
